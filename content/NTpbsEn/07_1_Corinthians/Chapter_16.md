<span class="v-num">1</span> Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I directed the churches of Galatia, so also do you.

<span class="v-num">2</span> On <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of the week let each of you put <span class="added-text">(something)</span> <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=set aside)</span> for himself, storing up whatever he may be prospered <span class="added-text">(in)</span>, so that when I come, then collections may not be made.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And when I arrive, whomever you approve, these I will send through letters to carry your <span class="alt-label">gift</span><span class="alt-text">(=grace)</span> to Jerusalem.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And if it is fitting for me also to go, they will go with me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And I will come to you, when I pass through Macedonia; for I am passing through Macedonia.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And with you perhaps I will stay, or even winter, so that you may send me forward wherever I may go.[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For I do not want to see you now in passing; but I hope to remain some time with you, if the Lord permits.[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> But I will remain in Ephesus until Pentecost;

<span class="v-num">9</span> for a great and effective door has been opened to me, and <span class="added-text">(there are)</span> many opposing <span class="added-text">(me)</span>.[^6]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now if Timothy comes, see that he may be with you without fear; for he works the work of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, as I also <span class="added-text">(do)</span>.[^7]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Therefore let no one despise him; but send him forward in peace, so that he may come to me; for I am <span class="alt-label">waiting</span><span class="alt-text">(=expecting)</span> for him with the brothers.[^8]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now concerning Apollos the brother, I urged him much that he might come to you with the brothers; and it was not at all <span class="added-text">(his)</span> will that he come now, but he will come when he has opportunity.[^9]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Watch, stand firm in the faith, be <span class="alt-label">men</span><span class="alt-text">(=act courageously)</span>, be strong.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Let all your <span class="added-text">(things)</span> be <span class="alt-label">done</span><span class="alt-text">(=let all of you happen)</span> in love.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Now I urge you, brothers - you know the household of Stephanas, that it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first fruit of Achaia, and they have appointed themselves to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> service of the saints -

<span class="v-num">16</span> that you also submit to such <span class="added-text">(people)</span>, and to everyone working together and laboring <span class="added-text">(with them)</span>.[^10]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And I rejoice at the <span class="alt-label">presence</span><span class="alt-text">(=coming)</span> of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus, because these have supplied your lack;

<span class="v-num">18</span> for they refreshed my spirit and yours. Therefore acknowledge such <span class="added-text">(people)</span>.

<span class="v-num">19</span> The churches of Asia greet you. Aquila and Priscilla greet you much in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, with the church in their house.

<span class="v-num">20</span> All the brothers greet you. Greet one another with a holy kiss.

<span class="v-num">21</span> The greeting with my own hand, of Paul.

<span class="v-num">22</span> If anyone does not love the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be <span class="alt-label">anathema</span><span class="alt-text">(=accursed)</span>. <span class="added-text">(O Lord,)</span> come![^11]

<span class="v-num">23</span> The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you.

<span class="v-num">24</span> My love <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you in Christ Jesus. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> first <span class="added-text">(letter)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Corinthians was written from Philippi <span class="alt-label">through</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus and Timothy.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(γίνωνται) 부정 목적절로, '연보를 그때 가서 하지 않도록' 미리 준비하게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다. 즉 사도가 도착한 후 급하게 헌금을 모으는 일이 없도록 미리 대비시키려는 의도이다.
[^2]: οὓς ἐὰν δοκιμάσητε는 부정관계대명사와 ἐάν+가정법이 결합된 불특정 조건절로 '너희가 누구를 인정하든지'라는 뜻이며, 아직 확정되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~라면'이라는 불확실한 조건을 제시한다. 바울이 자신의 동행 여부를 확정하지 않고 상황에 따라 결정될 것임을 보여준다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(προπέμψητε)은 바울이 머물거나 겨울을 나려는 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다. 성도들의 배웅(후원)을 받아 다음 사역지로 가려는 실제적 의도를 보여준다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법(ἐπιτρέπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 바울은 자신의 계획을 주님의 허락이라는 조건 아래 겸손하게 두고 있다.
[^6]: ἐνεργής(효과적인)는 단순히 열린 문이 아니라 실제 사역의 열매가 나타나는 활동적인 기회임을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(ἔλθῃ)은 디모데의 방문이 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 이어지는 ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)은 '그가 두려움 없이 있게 하려는' 목적을 나타낸다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔλθῃ)은 목적절로, '그를 평안히 보내어 그가 내게 오도록 하기 위함'이라는 의도를 나타낸다. 디모데를 배웅하는 행동의 목적이 바울에게 도착하게 하는 데 있음을 강조한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔλθῃ)은 파레칼레사(권하다)의 목적/내용절로 '가도록' 권한 내용을 나타내며, 두 번째 ἵνα νῦν ἔλθῃ도 동일하게 '지금 오는 것'이라는 목적/내용을 표현한다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(ὑποτάσσησθε)으로 목적절을 이끌며, 이러한 자들에게 복종해야 할 이유·목적을 제시한다.
[^11]: μαρὰν ἀθά는 아람어 그대로 남긴 감탄사로 '우리 주여 오시옵소서'라는 초대교회의 재림 기도문이다.
