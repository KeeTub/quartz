<span class="v-num">1</span> And I, brothers, having come to you, came not according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> excellence of word or of wisdom, proclaiming to you the testimony of God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> For I decided not to know anything among you except Jesus Christ, and this <span class="added-text">(one)</span> having been crucified.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I was among you in weakness and in fear and in much trembling,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and my word and my proclamation <span class="added-text">(were)</span> not in persuasive words of human wisdom, but in demonstration of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit and of power,

<span class="v-num">5</span> so that your faith might not be in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wisdom of men, but in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But we speak wisdom among the mature <span class="added-text">(ones)</span>; yet wisdom not of this age, nor of the rulers of this age, the <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> being brought to nothing,[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> but we speak <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wisdom of God in a mystery, the <span class="added-text">(wisdom)</span> having been hidden, which God foreordained before the ages for our glory,[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> which none of the rulers of this age has known; for if they had known, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.[^6]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But as it is written, 'What eye has not seen, and ear has not heard, and has not entered into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heart of man, <span class="added-text">(these things)</span> which God prepared for the ones loving him' —[^7]

<span class="v-num">10</span> but God has revealed <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to us through his Spirit; for the Spirit searches all things, even the deep things of God.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For who among men knows the things of a man, except the spirit of the man which is in him? So also no one knows the things of God, except the Spirit of God.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But we have received not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit which <span class="added-text">(is)</span> from God, so that we might know the things freely given to us by God.[^8]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Which things also we speak, not in words taught by human wisdom, but in <span class="added-text">(words)</span> taught by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, comparing spiritual things with spiritual <span class="added-text">(words)</span>.[^9]

<span class="v-num">14</span> But <span class="added-text">(the)</span> natural man does not receive the things of the Spirit of God, for they are foolishness to him, and he is not able to know <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, because they are spiritually discerned.[^10]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But the spiritual <span class="added-text">(man)</span> discerns all things, yet he himself is discerned by no one.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For who has known <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mind of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, that he will instruct him? But we have <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mind of Christ.[^11]

[^1]: ὑπεροχή(뛰어남)는 당대 수사학·철학적 화려함을 가리키며, 바울은 이를 의도적으로 배제했음을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἐσταυρωμένον은 완료분사로, 십자가 사건의 결과가 지속됨을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ)은 목적절로, 믿음의 근거가 하나님의 능력에만 있게 하려는 의도를 나타낸다.
[^4]: τελείοις(온전한 자들)는 영적으로 성숙한 신자를 가리키며, 세상 지혜와 대조된다.
[^5]: μυστήριον은 이전에는 감추였으나 이제 계시된 하나님의 구원계획을 뜻한다.
[^6]: εἰ+직설법 과거(반사실적 조건문)로, 실제로는 그들이 몰랐고 십자가에 못박은 사실이 전제된다.
[^7]: 이사야 64:4 등 구약 본문을 자유롭게 인용하여 계시의 초월성을 강조한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(εἰδῶμεν)은 목적절로, 성령을 받은 목적이 하나님의 은혜를 아는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^9]: συγκρίνοντες는 영적 진리를 영적 언어로 해석·전달하는 방식을 가리킨다.
[^10]: Ψυχικός(육에 속한)는 성령 없이 자연적 지각에 의존하는 인간을 가리킨다.
[^11]: 이사야 40:13 인용으로, 하나님의 뜻을 아는 자가 없음을 전제하고 그리스도의 마음을 받은 신자의 특권을 대조한다.
