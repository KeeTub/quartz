# 1 Corinthians 10

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that our fathers were all under the cloud, and all passed through the sea,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and all were baptized into Moses in the cloud and in the sea,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and all ate the same spiritual food,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and all drank the same spiritual drink, for they drank from a spiritual rock following <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; and the rock was Christ.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But God was not pleased with the majority of them, for they were struck down in the wilderness.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now these things became examples for us, to the end that we should not be desirers of evil things, as even they desired.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Neither become idolaters, as some of them <span class="added-text">(did)</span>; as it is written, 'The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Neither let us commit sexual immorality, as some of them committed sexual immorality, and twenty-three thousand fell in one day.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Neither let us test Christ, as even some of them tested <span class="added-text">(Him)</span>, and were destroyed by the serpents.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Neither grumble, as even some of them grumbled, and were destroyed by the destroyer.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now all these things happened to them as examples, and were written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the ages have come.[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> So then, let the one who thinks he stands take heed lest he fall.

<span class="v-num">13</span> No temptation has overtaken you except <span class="added-text">(what is)</span> common to man; but God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able <span class="added-text">(to bear)</span>, but will make with the temptation also the way of escape, that you may be able to bear <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Therefore, my beloved, flee from idolatry.

<span class="v-num">15</span> I speak as to wise men; you judge what I say.

<span class="v-num">16</span> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> communion of the body of Christ?[^6]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Because <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> one bread, we the many are one body, for we all partake of the one bread.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Look at Israel according to the flesh: are not those eating the sacrifices participants of the altar?

<span class="v-num">19</span> What then do I say? That an idol is anything? Or that a thing sacrificed to idols is anything?

<span class="v-num">20</span> But that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to demons, and not to God; and I do not want you to become participants with demons.

<span class="v-num">21</span> You cannot drink the cup of the Lord and the cup of demons; you cannot partake of the table of the Lord and of the table of demons.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Or do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than He?

<span class="v-num">23</span> All things are lawful for me, but not all things are profitable; all things are lawful for me, but not all things build up.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Let no one seek his own <span class="added-text">(good)</span>, but each one that of the other.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Eat everything sold in the meat market, asking no questions for conscience' sake;

<span class="v-num">26</span> for the earth <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Lord's, and the fullness of it.[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> But if any of the unbelievers invites you, and you want to go, eat everything set before you, asking no questions for conscience' sake.

<span class="v-num">28</span> But if anyone says to you, 'This is a thing sacrificed to idols,' do not eat, for the sake of the one who informed <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, and for conscience' sake; for the earth <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Lord's, and the fullness of it.[^8]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Now conscience, I say, not one's own, but that of the other. For why is my freedom judged by another's conscience?

<span class="v-num">30</span> But if I partake by grace, why am I evil spoken of for that for which I give thanks?

<span class="v-num">31</span> Whether then you eat, or drink, or whatever you do, do all things to the glory of God.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Be without offense both to Jews and to Greeks and to the church of God;

<span class="v-num">33</span> even as I also please all <span class="added-text">(men)</span> in all things, not seeking my own profit, but the <span class="added-text">(profit)</span> of the many, that they may be saved.[^9]

[^1]: 바위가 그리스도를 가리킴은 광야 여정 내내 이스라엘과 함께하며 물을 공급하신 그리스도의 선재적 임재를 뜻한다.
[^2]: εἰς τὸ μὴ εἶναι는 목적 부정사구문으로 '~하지 않게 하려 함'이라는 결과적 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 출애굽기 32:6 금송아지 사건의 인용으로, 우상숭배의 전형적 장면을 상기시킨다.
[^4]: 출애굽기 광야 사건 전체를 신약 교회를 향한 유형(τύποι)으로 해석하는 구약 암시 구절이다.
[^5]: τοῦ δύνασθαι는 τοῦ+부정사로 이루어진 목적구문으로 '능히 감당하게 하려 함'이라는 결과적 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: κοινωνία는 단순한 상징이 아니라 실제적 '참여·연합'을 뜻하며, 성찬이 그리스도와의 실질적 교제임을 강조한다.
[^7]: 시편 24:1의 인용으로, 만물이 주님께 속함을 근거로 자유로운 식사를 정당화한다.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(εἴπῃ)으로 이루어진 3급 조건문으로, 아직 일어나지 않았으나 충분히 있을 수 있는 상황을 가정한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(σωθῶσι)으로 이루어진 목적절로, 바울이 자기 유익을 포기하는 궁극적 목적이 타인의 구원임을 밝힌다.
