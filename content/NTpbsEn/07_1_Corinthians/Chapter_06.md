# 1 Corinthians 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> Does anyone of you, having a matter against the other, dare to be judged before the unrighteous, and not before the saints?

<span class="v-num">2</span> Do you not know that the saints will judge the world? And if the world is judged <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> you, are you unworthy of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> smallest <span class="alt-label">matters</span><span class="alt-text">(=courts)</span>?

<span class="v-num">3</span> Do you not know that we will judge angels? How much more matters of this life?

<span class="v-num">4</span> If then you have <span class="alt-label">courts</span><span class="alt-text">(=lawsuits)</span> concerning matters of this life, do you set these ones, the ones despised in the church, <span class="added-text">(to judge)</span>?[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> I speak <span class="added-text">(this)</span> to your shame. Is it so, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> there is not among you a wise man, not even one, who will be able to judge between his brother?

<span class="v-num">6</span> But brother goes to law with brother, and that before unbelievers?

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now therefore it is altogether a <span class="alt-label">defeat</span><span class="alt-text">(=loss)</span> among you, that you have lawsuits with one another. Why do you not rather suffer wrong? Why do you not rather be defrauded?

<span class="v-num">8</span> But you yourselves do wrong and defraud, and that <span class="added-text">(to your)</span> brothers.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Or do you not know that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> unrighteous will not inherit <span class="added-text">(the)</span> kingdom of God? Do not be deceived: neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor effeminate, nor abusers of themselves with mankind,

<span class="v-num">10</span> nor thieves, nor covetous ones, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor extortioners, will inherit <span class="added-text">(the)</span> kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And such were some of you: but you were washed, but you were sanctified, but you were justified in the name of the Lord Jesus, and <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> the Spirit of our God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> All things are lawful to me, but not all things are profitable: all things are lawful to me, but I will not be brought under the power of any.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Foods <span class="added-text">(are)</span> for the belly, and the belly for foods: but God will abolish both it and them. But the body <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not for fornication, but for the Lord; and the Lord for the body:[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And God has both raised up the Lord, and will raise us up through his power.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Do you not know that your bodies are members of Christ? Shall I then take away the members of Christ, and make <span class="added-text">(them)</span> members of a harlot? Let it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=God forbid)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Or do you not know that he who is joined to a harlot is one body <span class="added-text">(with her)</span>? For, he says, 'the two shall become one flesh.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> But he who is joined to the Lord is one spirit.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Flee fornication. Every sin that a man does is outside the body; but he who commits fornication sins against his own body.[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Or do you not know that your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit in you, whom you have from God, and you are not your own?[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For you were bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God's.[^7]

---

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ἔχητε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만약 ~하게 되면'이라는 뜻이다. 일상의 소송이 실제로 일어날 수 있는 상황을 가정하며 이어지는 책망의 전제를 마련한다.
[^2]: 세 동사(씻다·거룩하게 하다·의롭게 하다)는 모두 부정과거 수동태로, 과거에 단회적으로 완료된 구원 사건을 강조한다.
[^3]: σῶμα(몸)는 단순한 육체가 아니라 부활과 연합될 인격 전체를 가리키는 신학적 용어다.
[^4]: ‘둘이 한 육체가 되리라’는 창세기 2:24 인용으로, 부부의 결합을 그리스도와의 연합의 원리로 확장 적용한다.
[^5]: ὃ ἐὰν ποιήσῃ는 부정관계대명사와 가정법이 결합된 구문으로, '사람이 무엇을 범하든지'라는 불특정 조건을 나타낸다. 특정 상황을 지시하는 것이 아니라 일반적 가능성을 열어두는 표현이다.
[^6]: ναός(성전)는 예루살렘 성전 본체를 가리키는 단어로, 성도의 몸이 하나님의 임재 처소임을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἠγοράσθητε(값 주고 사다)는 노예 시장의 속량 은유로, 성도의 몸의 소유권이 하나님께 있음을 뜻한다.
