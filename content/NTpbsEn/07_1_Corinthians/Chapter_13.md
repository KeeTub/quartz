# 1 Corinthians 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> If I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, but do not have love, I have become sounding brass or a <span class="alt-label">clanging</span><span class="alt-text">(=noisy)</span> cymbal.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And if I have prophecy, and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith, so as to <span class="alt-label">remove</span><span class="alt-text">(=move)</span> mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And if I give <span class="alt-label">away</span><span class="alt-text">(=feed)</span> all my possessions, and if I deliver up my body that I may be burned, but do not have love, it profits me nothing.[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Love suffers long, is kind; love does not envy; love does not <span class="alt-label">boast</span><span class="alt-text">(=brag)</span>, is not puffed up,

<span class="v-num">5</span> does not behave <span class="alt-label">unseemly</span><span class="alt-text">(=rudely)</span>, does not seek its own, is not <span class="alt-label">provoked</span><span class="alt-text">(=irritated)</span>, does not <span class="alt-label">reckon</span><span class="alt-text">(=take into account)</span> evil,

<span class="v-num">6</span> does not rejoice <span class="alt-label">at</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> unrighteousness, but rejoices <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> the truth,

<span class="v-num">7</span> <span class="alt-label">bears</span><span class="alt-text">(=endures)</span> all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Love never <span class="alt-label">fails</span><span class="alt-text">(=falls away)</span>. But whether <span class="added-text">(there are)</span> prophecies, they will be done away; whether tongues, they will cease; whether knowledge, it will be done away.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For we know in part, and we prophesy in part;

<span class="v-num">10</span> But when the <span class="alt-label">perfect</span><span class="alt-text">(=complete)</span> comes, then that which is in part will be done away.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> When I was a <span class="alt-label">child</span><span class="alt-text">(=infant)</span>, I spoke as a child, I thought as a child, I reasoned as a child; but when I became a <span class="alt-label">man</span><span class="alt-text">(=husband, i.e. an adult)</span>, I did away with the things of the child.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For now we see through a <span class="alt-label">mirror</span><span class="alt-text">(=glass)</span>, in an <span class="alt-label">enigma</span><span class="alt-text">(=dimly)</span>, but then face <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=towards)</span> face; now I know in part, but then I will know <span class="alt-label">fully</span><span class="alt-text">(=know even as I am fully known)</span>, even as also I have been fully known.[^6]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But now faith, hope, love, these three remain; but the greatest of these <span class="added-text">(is)</span> love.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(λαλῶ, ἔχω)은 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 상황을 가정하는 3류 조건문으로, '설령 ~하더라도'라는 양보적 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 아무리 뛰어난 은사라도 사랑이 없으면 무가치함을 강조하는 논리적 전제절이다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법은 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 미래 조건을 나타낸다. 예언, 지식, 믿음이 아무리 크다고 가정해도 사랑이 없으면 무익하다는 극단적 조건을 강조하는 수사법이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(καυθήσωμαι)은 몸을 내주는 목적이 '불태워짐'에 있음을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^4]: τὸ τέλειον(온전한 것)은 단순한 도덕적 완전이 아니라 부분적 지식·예언이 끝나는 종말론적 완성 상태를 가리킨다.
[^5]: ἀνήρ는 통상 '남편'을 뜻하나 여기서는 '어린아이'와 대조되는 '장성한 사람'을 가리키는 문맥적 용례다.
[^6]: ἐπιγνώσομαι는 γινώσκω에 ἐπι-가 더해져 '단편적 앎'과 대조되는 '온전하고 철저한 앎'을 강조한다.
