<span class="v-num">1</span> Now concerning the spiritual <span class="added-text">(gifts)</span>, brothers, I do not want you to be ignorant.

<span class="v-num">2</span> You know that you were <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">nations</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gentiles)</span>, being led away to the mute idols, <span class="alt-label">however</span><span class="alt-text">(=as)</span> you were led.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Therefore I make known to you that no one speaking in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of God calls Jesus <span class="alt-label">accursed</span><span class="alt-text">(=anathema)</span>, and no one is able to say <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Jesus <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Lord, except in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Now there are varieties of gifts, but the same Spirit.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And there are varieties of <span class="alt-label">ministries</span><span class="alt-text">(=services)</span>, and the same Lord.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And there are varieties of workings, but <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> the same God who works all things in all <span class="added-text">(people)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> But to each one is given the manifestation of the Spirit toward the <span class="alt-label">profit</span><span class="alt-text">(=benefit)</span> <span class="added-text">(of all)</span>.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For to one indeed through the Spirit is given <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of wisdom, and to another <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of knowledge according to the same Spirit;

<span class="v-num">9</span> and to another faith by the same Spirit; and to another gifts of healings by the same Spirit;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and to another workings of <span class="alt-label">miracles</span><span class="alt-text">(=powers)</span>; and to another prophecy; and to another <span class="alt-label">discernings</span><span class="alt-text">(=distinguishings)</span> of spirits; to another kinds of tongues; and to another interpretation of tongues.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But all these things <span class="added-text">(the)</span> one and the same Spirit works, dividing to each one individually as he wills.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For just as the body is one and has many members, and all the members of the body, being many, are one body, so also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Christ.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For indeed <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> one Spirit we all were baptized into one body, whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free; and all were made to drink <span class="alt-label">into</span><span class="alt-text">(=of)</span> one Spirit.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For indeed the body is not one member, but many.

<span class="v-num">15</span> If the foot should say, "Because I am not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hand, I am not of the body," it is not for this reason not of the body.[^1]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And if the ear should say, "Because I am not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> eye, I am not of the body," it is not for this reason not of the body.[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> If the whole body <span class="added-text">(were)</span> an eye, where <span class="added-text">(would be)</span> the hearing? If the whole <span class="added-text">(were)</span> hearing, where <span class="added-text">(would be)</span> the smelling?

<span class="v-num">18</span> But now God has set the members, each one of them, in the body, just as he willed.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But if all were one member, where <span class="added-text">(would be)</span> the body?

<span class="v-num">20</span> But now <span class="added-text">(there are)</span> indeed many members, yet one body.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the eye is not able to say to the hand, "I have no need of you"; or again the head to the feet, "I have no need of you."

<span class="v-num">22</span> But much more, the members of the body which seem to be weaker are necessary;

<span class="v-num">23</span> and those <span class="added-text">(parts)</span> of the body which we think to be less honorable, on these we put more abundant honor; and our <span class="alt-label">unpresentable</span><span class="alt-text">(=less presentable)</span> <span class="added-text">(parts)</span> have more abundant <span class="alt-label">presentability</span><span class="alt-text">(=comeliness)</span>;

<span class="v-num">24</span> but our <span class="alt-label">presentable</span><span class="alt-text">(=comely)</span> <span class="added-text">(parts)</span> have no need. But God has <span class="alt-label">tempered</span><span class="alt-text">(=combined)</span> the body together, having given more abundant honor to the <span class="added-text">(part)</span> lacking,

<span class="v-num">25</span> that there might be no <span class="alt-label">division</span><span class="alt-text">(=schism)</span> in the body, but that the members might care the same for one another.[^3]

<span class="v-num">26</span> And whether one member suffers, all the members suffer with <span class="added-text">(it)</span>; or whether one member is glorified, all the members rejoice with <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">27</span> Now you are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> body of Christ, and members <span class="alt-label">individually</span><span class="alt-text">(=in part)</span>.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And God set some in the church, first apostles, second prophets, third teachers, then <span class="added-text">(workers of)</span> miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governings, kinds of tongues.

<span class="v-num">29</span> <span class="added-text">(Are)</span> all apostles? <span class="added-text">(Are)</span> all prophets? <span class="added-text">(Are)</span> all teachers? <span class="added-text">(Do)</span> all <span class="added-text">(work)</span> miracles?

<span class="v-num">30</span> Do all have gifts of healings? Do all speak with tongues? Do all interpret?

<span class="v-num">31</span> But earnestly desire the <span class="alt-label">greater</span><span class="alt-text">(=better)</span> gifts; and yet I show to you a way according to <span class="alt-label">excellence</span><span class="alt-text">(=a most excellent way)</span>.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 상황을 가정하는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 ~라고 말한다면'이라는 미래적 가능성을 나타낸다. 발이 실제로 그렇게 말하든 안 하든, 그런 주장이 있을 수 있음을 가정하여 논증을 전개하는 수사적 조건절이다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 상황을 가정하는 조건절로, '만일 ~라고 말한다 해도'라는 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 실제 그런 말을 했다는 뜻이 아니라 논증을 위한 가정임을 보여준다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ, μεριμνῶσι) 목적절로, 하나님이 몸을 그렇게 구성하신 목적을 나타낸다. 분쟁이 없고 서로 돌보게 하시려는 하나님의 설계 의도를 강조한다.
