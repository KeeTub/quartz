# 1 Corinthians 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, a called apostle of Jesus Christ, through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, and Sosthenes the brother,

<span class="v-num">2</span> to the church of God which is in Corinth, to those having been sanctified in Christ Jesus, called saints, with all who call upon the name of our Lord Jesus Christ in every place, both theirs and ours:

<span class="v-num">3</span> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">4</span> I give thanks to my God always concerning you, for the grace of God which was given to you in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">5</span> that in everything you were enriched in him, in all speech and all knowledge,

<span class="v-num">6</span> even as the testimony of Christ was confirmed in you,

<span class="v-num">7</span> so that you are lacking in no gift, eagerly awaiting the revelation of our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">8</span> who will also confirm you until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end, blameless in the day of our Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">9</span> God is faithful, through whom you were called into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fellowship of his Son Jesus Christ our Lord.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now I exhort you, brothers, through the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that you all say the same thing, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> there be no divisions among you, but that you be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.[^1]

<span class="v-num">11</span> For it was made known to me concerning you, my brothers, by those of Chloe's <span class="added-text">(household)</span>, that there are contentions among you.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now I say this, that each of you says, "I indeed am of Paul," and "I of Apollos," and "I of Cephas," and "I of Christ."

<span class="v-num">13</span> Has Christ been divided? Was Paul crucified for you? Or were you baptized into the name of Paul?

<span class="v-num">14</span> I give thanks to God that I baptized none of you, except Crispus and Gaius,

<span class="v-num">15</span> lest anyone should say that I baptized into my own name.[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And I also baptized the household of Stephanas; beyond that I do not know if I baptized any other.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For Christ did not send me to baptize, but to preach the gospel, not in wisdom of <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=speech)</span>, lest the cross of Christ be made void.[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For the word of the cross is to those perishing foolishness, but to us who are being saved it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For it is written, 'I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of the understanding ones I will set aside.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Where is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wise? Where is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> scribe? Where is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> debater of this age? Has not God made foolish the wisdom of this world?

<span class="v-num">21</span> For since, in the wisdom of God, the world through wisdom did not know God, God was pleased through the foolishness of the preaching to save those believing;

<span class="v-num">22</span> since indeed Jews ask for a sign, and Greeks seek wisdom,

<span class="v-num">23</span> but we preach Christ crucified, to Jews indeed a stumbling block, and to Greeks foolishness,

<span class="v-num">24</span> but to those who are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wisdom of God.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men, and the weakness of God is stronger than men.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For behold your calling, brothers, that not many wise according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, not many mighty, not many noble <span class="added-text">(are called)</span>;

<span class="v-num">27</span> but God chose the foolish things of the world, that he might put to shame the wise; and God chose the weak things of the world, that he might put to shame the strong things;[^5]

<span class="v-num">28</span> and the base things of the world, and the despised things, God chose, and the things that are not, that he might bring to nothing the things that are;[^6]

<span class="v-num">29</span> so that no flesh should boast before him.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But of him you are in Christ Jesus, who became for us wisdom from God, and righteousness and sanctification and redemption,

<span class="v-num">31</span> that, as it is written, 'He who boasts, let him boast in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.'[^7]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(λέγητε, ᾖ, ἦτε)은 권면(παρακαλῶ)의 내용이자 목적을 나타내는 절로, '~하도록/~하라고' 권한다는 뜻이다. 명령이 아닌 간접적 권고의 형태로 성도의 일치를 목적으로 제시한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(εἴπῃ)으로 목적/결과를 나타내는 부정 목적절이다. 바울이 세례를 거의 베풀지 않은 이유, 곧 사람들이 자신의 이름을 내세우지 못하게 하려는 의도를 밝힌다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(κενωθῇ) 목적절로, 말의 지혜로 전하지 않는 이유(목적)를 나타낸다. 십자가의 능력이 인간의 화려한 수사로 인해 무력화되지 않게 하려는 의도를 강조한다.
[^4]: 사 29:14 인용.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(καταισχύνῃ)은 목적절로, 하나님이 미련하고 약한 것들을 택하신 '의도'를 나타낸다. 지혜자와 강한 자를 부끄럽게 하시려는 하나님의 역설적 목적을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(καταργήσῃ)은 목적절로, 하나님이 없는 것들을 택하신 '목적'이 있는 것들을 폐하시려는 데 있음을 나타낸다. 하나님의 역설적 선택 방식의 의도를 강조하는 구문이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 하나님께서 이렇게 하신 목적(사람이 자기 앞에서 자랑하지 못하게 하심, 앞 문맥과 연결)을 나타낸다. 그 결과 자랑은 오직 주 안에서만 가능하다는 뉘앙스를 담는다.
