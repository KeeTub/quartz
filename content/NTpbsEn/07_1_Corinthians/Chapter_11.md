# 1 Corinthians 11

<span class="v-num">1</span> Be imitators of me, as I also <span class="added-text">(am an imitator)</span> of Christ.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Now I praise you, brothers, that you remember me in all things, and hold fast the traditions, just as I delivered <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to you.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But I want you to know that the head of every man is Christ, and the head of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> woman <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the man, and the head of Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Every man praying or prophesying, having <span class="added-text">(something)</span> down over <span class="added-text">(his)</span> head, dishonors his head.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But every woman praying or prophesying with <span class="added-text">(her)</span> head uncovered dishonors her <span class="added-text">(own)</span> head, for it is one and the same as <span class="added-text">(if)</span> she were shaved.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For if a woman is not covered, let her also be shorn; but if it is shameful for a woman to be shorn or shaved, let her be covered.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For a man ought not to cover <span class="added-text">(his)</span> head, being <span class="added-text">(the)</span> image and glory of God; but woman is the glory of man.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For man is not from woman, but woman from man.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For also man was not created for the sake of the woman, but woman for the sake of the man.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For this reason the woman ought to have <span class="added-text">(a symbol of)</span> authority upon <span class="added-text">(her)</span> head, because of the angels.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Nevertheless, neither is man without woman, nor woman without man, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For as the woman <span class="added-text">(is)</span> from the man, so also the man <span class="added-text">(is)</span> through the woman, but all things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> from God.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Judge among yourselves: is it proper for a woman to pray to God uncovered?

<span class="v-num">14</span> Or does not even nature itself teach you that if a man has long hair, it is a dishonor to him?[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But if a woman has long hair, it is a glory to her? For <span class="added-text">(her)</span> hair has been given to her instead of a covering.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But if anyone seems to be contentious, we have no such custom, nor <span class="added-text">(do)</span> the churches of God.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Now in giving this instruction I do not praise <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, because you come together not for the better but for the worse.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For first of all, when you come together in the church, I hear <span class="added-text">(that there)</span> are divisions among you, and I partly believe it.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For there must also be factions among you, so that those who are approved may become evident among you.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> When you come together therefore into one place, it is not to eat <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord's supper.

<span class="v-num">21</span> For each one takes his own supper first in eating, and one is hungry, and another is drunk.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For do you not have houses to eat and drink in? Or do you despise the church of God and shame those who have nothing? What shall I say to you? Shall I praise you in this? I do not praise <span class="added-text">(you)</span>.

<span class="v-num">23</span> For I received from the Lord that which I also delivered to you, that the Lord Jesus, in the night in which he was <span class="alt-label">betrayed</span><span class="alt-text">(=delivered up)</span>, took bread,[^6]

<span class="v-num">24</span> and having given thanks, he broke <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and said, "Take, eat; this is my body which is broken for you; do this in remembrance of me."

<span class="v-num">25</span> In the same way also the cup, after having supped, saying, "This cup is the new covenant in my blood; do this, as often as you drink <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, in remembrance of me."

<span class="v-num">26</span> For as often as you eat this bread, and drink this cup, you proclaim the Lord's death, until he comes.[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> So then whoever eats this bread or drinks the cup of the Lord unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.

<span class="v-num">28</span> But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of the bread, and drink of the cup.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For he who eats and drinks unworthily, eats and drinks judgment to himself, not discerning the Lord's body.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For this reason many among you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> weak and sick, and many <span class="alt-label">sleep</span><span class="alt-text">(=have died)</span>.

<span class="v-num">31</span> For if we judged ourselves, we would not be judged.

<span class="v-num">32</span> But being judged, we are chastened by the Lord, so that we may not be condemned with the world.[^8]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Therefore, my brothers, when you come together to eat, wait for one another.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But if anyone is hungry, let him eat at home, so that you do not come together unto judgment. And the rest I will set in order whenever I come.[^9]

[^1]: κεφαλή(머리)는 단순한 신체 부위가 아니라 '근원·권위'의 의미를 함께 지니는 신학적 용어로, 남자-여자-그리스도-하나님으로 이어지는 위계적 질서를 표현한다.
[^2]: ἐξουσία(권세)는 여기서 복종의 표가 아니라, 오히려 여자가 스스로 행사하는 '권리·표지'로서 머리 덮개를 가리키는 것으로 이해된다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(κομᾷ)은 '만일 ~라면'이라는 미래 가능성을 전제한 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않았으나 실제로 일어날 수 있는 상황을 가정한다. 남자가 머리를 길게 기르는 구체적 사례를 가정하여 그 결과(수치)를 이끌어낸다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(κομᾷ)은 아직 미정인 일반적 조건을 나타내는 3형식 조건절로 '만일 (실제로) 머리를 기른다면'이라는 열린 가능성을 표현한다. 즉 여자가 머리를 기르는 것을 전제로 그 결과(영광)를 선언하는 구조다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(γένωνται) 목적절로, 분파의 존재조차 인정받은 자를 드러내시려는 하나님의 섭리적 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: παρεδίδοτο는 미완료 수동태로서 '넘겨지고 계시던' 그 밤의 지속적·현재진행적 배경을 강조하나, 본문은 개역체 과거형으로 통일하여 옮겼다.
[^7]: ἄχρις οὗ ἂν ἔλθῃ는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 재림을 가리키는 가정법 구문으로, 주의 만찬이 재림 때까지 지속될 선포 행위임을 나타낸다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(κατακριθῶμεν) 목적절로, 주님의 징계가 성도를 세상과 함께 정죄받지 않게 하려는 구속적 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^9]: ἵνα μὴ...συνέρχησθε는 가정법 목적절로, 성찬 참여가 도리어 심판에 이르지 않게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
