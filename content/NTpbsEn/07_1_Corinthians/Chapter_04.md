# 1 Corinthians 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> Let a man so account us, as servants of Christ, and stewards of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mysteries of God.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Here, moreover, what is required in stewards is that one be found faithful.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> But to me it is a very small thing that I should be judged by you, or by <span class="added-text">(man's)</span> <span class="alt-label">day</span><span class="alt-text">(=a human court)</span>; <span class="added-text">(indeed)</span> I do not even judge myself.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> For I know nothing against myself; yet I am not justified by this: but he who judges me is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">5</span> So then, do not judge anything before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> time, until the Lord comes, who will both bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts; and then shall each man have praise from God.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now these things, brothers, I have applied to myself and Apollos for your sakes, that in us you might learn not to think <span class="added-text">(of men)</span> above what is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against the other.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For who makes you to differ <span class="added-text">(from another)</span>? And what do you have that you did not receive? But if you also received <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, why do you boast as <span class="added-text">(if)</span> not having received <span class="added-text">(it)</span>?

<span class="v-num">8</span> Now you are full, now you are rich, you have reigned as kings without us: and I would indeed that you did reign, that we also might reign with you.[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For I think that God has set forth us the apostles last, as men appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle to the world, and to angels, and to men.

<span class="v-num">10</span> We <span class="added-text">(are)</span> fools for Christ's sake, but you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> wise in Christ; we <span class="added-text">(are)</span> weak, but you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> strong; you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> honorable, but we <span class="added-text">(are)</span> despised.

<span class="v-num">11</span> To the present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwelling place,

<span class="v-num">12</span> And labor, working with our own hands: being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we endure it:

<span class="v-num">13</span> Being defamed, we entreat: we are made as the filth of the world, <span class="added-text">(and are)</span> the offscouring of all things to this day.

<span class="v-num">14</span> I do not write these things to shame you, but as my beloved children I warn <span class="added-text">(you)</span>.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For though you have ten thousand instructors in Christ, yet you do not have many fathers: for in Christ Jesus I have begotten you through the gospel.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> I urge you therefore, be followers of me.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For this cause I have sent to you Timothy, who is my beloved and faithful child in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, who shall bring you into remembrance of my ways which are in Christ, as I teach everywhere in every church.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now some are puffed up, as though I were not coming to you.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But I will come to you shortly, if the Lord wills, and I will know, not the speech of them who are puffed up, but the power.[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For the kingdom of God is not in word, but in power.

<span class="v-num">21</span> What do you desire? Shall I come to you with a rod, or in love, and <span class="added-text">(in)</span> a spirit of gentleness?

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(εὑρεθῇ)이 ζητεῖται의 목적/내용을 나타냄: 청지기에게 요구되는 바가 '신실한 자로 발견되는 것'이라는 목표를 제시한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀνακριθῶ)은 앞의 ἐλάχιστον과 연결되어 '~에 의해 심판받는 것'이라는 내용을 설명하는 목적/내용절로, 바울에게 그것이 얼마나 사소한 일인지를 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μάθητε, φυσιοῦσθε)이 이끄는 두 개의 목적절로, 바울이 자신과 아볼로를 예로 든 궁극적 의도(목적)를 밝힌다. '기록된 것 이상으로 생각하지 않기를 배우게 하려' 그리고 '교만하여 서로 대적하지 않게 하려'는 두 가지 목적이 병렬로 제시된다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(συμβασιλεύσωμεν)이 목적절을 이끌어 '너희가 참으로 왕 노릇했다면'의 결과로 바라던 목적(우리도 함께 왕 노릇함)을 나타낸다. 바울의 반어법적 탄식 속에서 아직 이루어지지 않은 소망을 드러낸다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(ἔχητε)은 아직 미확정인 가상의 상황을 가정하는 3류 조건문으로, '설령 ~일지라도'라는 양보적 뉘앙스를 담고 있다. 바울은 실제 가능성을 인정하면서도 자신의 영적 부성의 유일함을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐὰν+가정법(θελήσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '주께서 원하시면'이라는 뜻이다. 바울은 자신의 계획을 주님의 뜻에 전적으로 맡기는 겸손한 태도를 보여준다.
