# 1 Corinthians 8

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now concerning the <span class="alt-label">idol-sacrifices</span><span class="alt-text">(=food offered to idols)</span>, we know that we all have knowledge. Knowledge puffs up, but love builds up.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> But if anyone thinks he knows something, he has not yet known as it is necessary to know;

<span class="v-num">3</span> But if anyone loves God, this one has been known by him.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Concerning the eating then of idol-sacrifices, we know that an idol <span class="added-text">(is)</span> nothing in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, and that <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> no other God except one.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For even if indeed there are <span class="added-text">(those)</span> called gods, whether in heaven or on the earth—as there are many gods and many lords—

<span class="v-num">6</span> yet to us <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> one God the Father, from whom <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all things, and we <span class="added-text">(are)</span> <span class="alt-label">unto</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, through whom <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all things, and we <span class="added-text">(are)</span> through him.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But knowledge <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not in all; but some, by the conscience of the idol until now, eat as <span class="added-text">(if it were)</span> idol-sacrifice, and their conscience being weak is defiled.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But food does not set us <span class="alt-label">before</span><span class="alt-text">(=commend us to)</span> God; for neither if we eat do we <span class="alt-label">abound</span><span class="alt-text">(=have more)</span>, nor if we do not eat are we <span class="alt-label">lacking</span><span class="alt-text">(=have less)</span>.[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But watch lest somehow this <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=right/freedom)</span> of yours becomes a stumbling block to those who are weak.[^5]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For if anyone sees you, the one having knowledge, <span class="alt-label">reclining</span><span class="alt-text">(=sitting at table)</span> in an idol's temple, will not his conscience, being weak, be built <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=emboldened)</span> to eat the idol-sacrifices?[^6]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the weak brother will perish by your knowledge, the brother for whom Christ died.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But thus sinning against the brothers, and <span class="alt-label">wounding</span><span class="alt-text">(=striking)</span> their weak conscience, you sin against Christ.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore if food causes my brother to stumble, I will never eat meat <span class="alt-label">forever</span><span class="alt-text">(=for ever)</span>, so that I might not cause my brother to stumble.[^7]

[^1]: γνῶσις(지식)와 ἀγάπη(사랑)의 대조는 바울 윤리의 핵심 주제로, 지식만으로는 공동체를 세우지 못함을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἔγνωσται는 완료수동으로, 하나님께서 그를 이미 아신 상태가 지속됨을 나타내어 인간의 '앎'보다 하나님의 앎이 우선함을 보인다.
[^3]: 아버지(ἐξ οὗ)와 아들(δι᾿ οὗ)에 대응하는 전치사 구조가 대구를 이루어 하나님과 그리스도의 창조·구속 사역을 병렬로 고백한다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(φάγωμεν)은 아직 결정되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '먹든지 안 먹든지'라는 상황을 가정한다. 먹는 행위 자체가 하나님과의 관계에 영향을 주지 못함을 강조하는 수사적 대구 구문이다.
[^5]: ἐξουσία는 여기서 '권세'보다 '자유·권리'의 의미로, 지식 있는 자가 지닌 행동의 자유를 가리킨다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἴδῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 상황을 가정하는 3类 조건문이다. '누가 보게 된다면'이라는 실제로 일어날 수 있는 가능성을 전제로 그 결과(약한 자의 양심에 미치는 영향)를 경고하는 구조다.
[^7]: ἵνα μὴ σκανδαλίσω는 가정법 목적절로, 바울 자신의 결단(고기를 먹지 않음)의 목적이 형제를 넘어지지 않게 함에 있음을 밝힌다.
