# 1 Corinthians 15

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now I make known to you, brothers, the gospel which I proclaimed to you, which also you received, in which also you stand,

<span class="v-num">2</span> through which also you are being saved, by what word I proclaimed <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to you, if you hold fast, unless you believed in vain.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For I delivered to you among <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first things, what also I received, that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and that he was buried, and that he has been raised on the third day according to the scriptures,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and that he appeared to Cephas, then to the twelve.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Then he appeared to above five hundred brothers at once, of whom the greater part remain until now, but some also have fallen asleep.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then he appeared to James, then to all the apostles.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And last of all, as to <span class="added-text">(one)</span> untimely born, he appeared to me also.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For I am the least of the apostles, who am not <span class="alt-label">sufficient</span><span class="alt-text">(=worthy)</span> to be called an apostle, because I persecuted the church of God.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> grace of God I am what I am, and his grace toward me was not in vain, but I labored more abundantly than all of them; yet not I, but the grace of God with me.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Therefore whether I, or they, so we preach, and so you believed.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now if Christ is preached, that he has been raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, how do some among you say that there is no resurrection of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead?

<span class="v-num">13</span> But if there is no resurrection of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, neither has Christ been raised;

<span class="v-num">14</span> And if Christ has not been raised, then our preaching <span class="added-text">(is)</span> vain, and your faith also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> vain.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And we are found even false witnesses of God, because we testified <span class="alt-label">against</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> God that he raised up Christ, whom he did not raise up, if indeed <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead are not raised;

<span class="v-num">16</span> For if <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead are not raised, neither has Christ been raised;

<span class="v-num">17</span> And if Christ has not been raised, your faith <span class="added-text">(is)</span> vain; you are still in your sins.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Then also those who fell asleep in Christ have perished.

<span class="v-num">19</span> If in this life only we have hoped in Christ, we are of all men most <span class="alt-label">pitiable</span><span class="alt-text">(=to be pitied)</span>.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But now Christ has been raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, and has become <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstfruits of those who have fallen asleep.[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> For since through man <span class="added-text">(came)</span> death, through man also <span class="added-text">(came the)</span> resurrection of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all will be made alive.[^3]

<span class="v-num">23</span> But each in his own order: Christ <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstfruits, then those of Christ at his coming.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Then <span class="added-text">(comes)</span> the end, when he delivers up the kingdom to God, even the Father, when he abolishes all rule and all authority and power;

<span class="v-num">25</span> For he must reign, until he puts all <span class="added-text">(his)</span> enemies under his feet.

<span class="v-num">26</span> The last enemy to be abolished <span class="added-text">(is)</span> death.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For 'he put all things in subjection under his feet.' But when he says that 'all things have been put in subjection,' it is evident that <span class="added-text">(he is)</span> excepted who put all things in subjection to him.[^4]

<span class="v-num">28</span> And when all things are subjected to him, then the Son himself also will be subjected to him who put all things in subjection to him, that God may be all in all.[^5]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Otherwise what will they do who are baptized for the dead, if the dead are not raised at all? Why also are they baptized for the dead?

<span class="v-num">30</span> Why also do we stand in jeopardy every hour?

<span class="v-num">31</span> I die daily, <span class="added-text">(I swear)</span> by our boasting which I have in Christ Jesus our Lord.

<span class="v-num">32</span> If according to man I fought with beasts in Ephesus, what advantage <span class="added-text">(is it)</span> to me, if <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead are not raised? Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Do not be deceived: 'evil companionships corrupt good morals.'[^6]

<span class="v-num">34</span> Awake to righteousness, and do not sin; for some have <span class="added-text">(no)</span> knowledge of God. I speak <span class="added-text">(this)</span> to your shame.

<span class="v-num">35</span> But someone will say, "How are the dead raised? And with what body do they come?"

<span class="v-num">36</span> Foolish one, what you sow is not made alive, unless it dies;[^7]

<span class="v-num">37</span> And what you sow, you do not sow the body that will be, but a bare grain, it may chance, of wheat or of some of the rest;

<span class="v-num">38</span> But God gives it a body as he willed, and to each of the seeds its own body.

<span class="v-num">39</span> Not all flesh <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the same flesh; but one is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of men, another <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of beasts, another of fish, another of birds.

<span class="v-num">40</span> There are also heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies; but the glory of the heavenly <span class="added-text">(is)</span> one, and <span class="added-text">(the glory)</span> of the earthly <span class="added-text">(is)</span> another.

<span class="v-num">41</span> There is one glory of the sun, and another glory of the moon, and another glory of the stars; for star differs from star in glory.

<span class="v-num">42</span> So also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the resurrection of the dead. It is sown in corruption, it is raised in incorruption;

<span class="v-num">43</span> It is sown in dishonor, it is raised in glory; it is sown in weakness, it is raised in power;

<span class="v-num">44</span> It is sown a natural body, it is raised a spiritual body. There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body.[^8]

<span class="v-num">45</span> So also it is written, 'The first man Adam became a living soul'; the last Adam <span class="added-text">(became)</span> a life-giving spirit.[^9]

<span class="v-num">46</span> But not first <span class="added-text">(was)</span> the spiritual, but the natural, then the spiritual.

<span class="v-num">47</span> The first man <span class="added-text">(was)</span> out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> earth, earthy; the second man <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Lord out of heaven.

<span class="v-num">48</span> As is the earthy, such also <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the earthy; and as is the heavenly, such also <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the heavenly.

<span class="v-num">49</span> And as we have borne the image of the earthy, we will also bear the image of the heavenly.

<span class="v-num">50</span> Now this I say, brothers, that flesh and blood cannot inherit <span class="added-text">(the)</span> kingdom of God, neither does corruption inherit incorruption.

<span class="v-num">51</span> Behold, I tell you a mystery: We will not all sleep, but we will all be changed,[^10]

<span class="v-num">52</span> In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet; for the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we will be changed.

<span class="v-num">53</span> For this corruptible must put on incorruption, and this mortal <span class="added-text">(must)</span> put on immortality.

<span class="v-num">54</span> And when this corruptible has put on incorruption, and this mortal has put on immortality, then will come to pass the saying that is written, 'Death is swallowed up in victory.'[^11]

<span class="v-num">55</span> 'O death, where <span class="added-text">(is)</span> your sting? O <span class="alt-label">Hades</span><span class="alt-text">(=hell)</span>, where <span class="added-text">(is)</span> your victory?'[^12]

<span class="v-num">56</span> Now the sting of death <span class="added-text">(is)</span> sin; and the power of sin <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the law.

<span class="v-num">57</span> But thanks <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">58</span> Therefore, my beloved brothers, be steadfast, unmovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your labor is not in vain in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

[^1]: ἔκτρωμα(조산아)는 자격 없이 갑작스럽게 사도가 된 바울 자신의 겸손한 자기 인식을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἀπαρχή(첫 열매)는 뒤따를 전체 추수(성도의 부활)를 보증하는 표상이다.
[^3]: ζωοποιηθήσονται는 미래수동형으로, 아직 오지 않은 종말의 부활을 확실한 미래 사건으로 선언한다.
[^4]: 시편 8:6의 인용으로, 만물을 다스리는 그리스도의 권세를 뒷받침한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ)은 종말의 궁극 목적, 곧 하나님이 만유 안에 만유가 되시는 것을 나타낸다.
[^6]: 헬라 극작가 메난드로스의 격언을 인용해 나쁜 교제의 해악을 경고한다.
[^7]: ἐὰν μὴ ἀποθάνῃ는 3급 조건문으로, 씨앗의 죽음이 부활의 필연적 전제임을 보여준다.
[^8]: 관사 없이 술어로 쓰인 σῶμα ψυχικόν/πνευματικόν은 몸의 종류(속성)를 규정하는 표현이다.
[^9]: 창세기 2:7의 인용으로, 첫 아담과 마지막 아담(그리스도)을 대조한다.
[^10]: μυστήριον은 이전에 감추였다가 계시로 드러난 종말의 진리, 곧 성도의 순간적 변화를 가리킨다.
[^11]: 이사야 25:8의 암시로, 종말의 부활 때 사망이 완전히 정복됨을 선언한다.
[^12]: 호세아 13:14의 인용으로, 사망을 향한 승리의 조롱을 표현한다.
