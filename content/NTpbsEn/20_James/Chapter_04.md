<span class="v-num">1</span> From where <span class="added-text">(come)</span> wars and fights among you? <span class="added-text">(Is it)</span> not from <span class="alt-label">here</span><span class="alt-text">(=this)</span>, from your pleasures warring in your members?

<span class="v-num">2</span> You desire, and do not have; you murder and envy, and are not able to obtain; you fight and war, yet do not have, because you do not ask.

<span class="v-num">3</span> You ask, and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, that you may spend <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on your pleasures.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Adulterers and adulteresses, do you not know that friendship of the world is enmity <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> God? Whoever therefore wishes to be a friend of the world is <span class="alt-label">established</span><span class="alt-text">(=becomes)</span> an enemy of God.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Or do you think that the scripture speaks in vain? Does the spirit that dwelt in us long unto envy?[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But he gives greater grace; therefore he says, 'God resists <span class="added-text">(the)</span> proud, but gives grace to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> humble.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Submit therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you. Cleanse <span class="added-text">(your)</span> hands, <span class="added-text">(you)</span> sinners, and purify <span class="added-text">(your)</span> hearts, <span class="added-text">(you)</span> double-minded.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning, and <span class="added-text">(your)</span> joy to gloom.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Humble yourselves before the Lord, and he will lift you <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=exalt you)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Do not speak against one another, brothers. He who speaks against a brother, and judges his brother, speaks against the law, and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law, but a judge.

<span class="v-num">12</span> There is one lawgiver, who is able to save and to <span class="alt-label">destroy</span><span class="alt-text">(=kill)</span>. Who are you who judges the <span class="alt-label">other</span><span class="alt-text">(=your neighbor)</span>?

<span class="v-num">13</span> <span class="alt-label">Come</span><span class="alt-text">(=Now)</span> then, you who say, "Today and tomorrow we will go into this city, and we will spend a year there, and we will trade, and we will gain <span class="added-text">(profit)</span>";

<span class="v-num">14</span> You who do not know the <span class="added-text">(things)</span> of tomorrow. For what is your life? For it is a <span class="alt-label">vapor</span><span class="alt-text">(=mist)</span> that appears for a little <span class="added-text">(while)</span>, and then vanishes.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Instead you should say, "If the Lord wills, we will both live, and do this or that."[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But now you boast in your <span class="alt-label">arrogance</span><span class="alt-text">(=boastings)</span>; all such boasting is evil.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Therefore, to him who knows <span class="added-text">(how)</span> to do good, and does not do <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, it is sin to him.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 '정욕에 쓰기 위해'라는 잘못된 구함의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: τὸ πνεῦμα는 성령 또는 인간의 영으로 해석이 갈리는 난해구절로, 여기서는 하나님이 우리 안의 영을 질투하시리만큼 사모하신다는 뜻으로 이해된다.
[^3]: 잠언 3:34 인용.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법의 3급 조건문으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성('주의 뜻이면')을 나타낸다.
