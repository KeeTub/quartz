# James 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> Be not many teachers, my brothers, knowing that we shall receive greater judgment.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For in many things we all stumble. If anyone does not stumble in word, this <span class="added-text">(one is)</span> a perfect man, able also to bridle the whole body.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Behold, we put the bits into the mouths of the horses, that they may obey us, and we turn about their whole body.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Behold also the ships, being so great, and driven by fierce winds, are turned about by a very small rudder, wherever the impulse of the one steering wills.

<span class="v-num">5</span> So also the tongue is a small member, and boasts greatly. Behold, how small a fire kindles how great a <span class="alt-label">forest</span><span class="alt-text">(=wood)</span>!

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the tongue <span class="added-text">(is)</span> a fire, the world of unrighteousness. So the tongue is set among our members, defiling the whole body, and setting on fire the <span class="alt-label">course</span><span class="alt-text">(=wheel)</span> of <span class="alt-label">nature</span><span class="alt-text">(=life)</span>, and being set on fire by <span class="alt-label">hell</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gehenna)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For every <span class="alt-label">kind</span><span class="alt-text">(=nature)</span> of beasts and birds, of reptiles and sea creatures, is tamed and has been tamed by <span class="alt-label">mankind</span><span class="alt-text">(=human nature)</span>,

<span class="v-num">8</span> but the tongue no one of men is able to tame; <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> an unrestrainable evil, full of deadly poison.

<span class="v-num">9</span> With it we bless God, even <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, and with it we curse men, who have been made according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> likeness of God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Out of the same mouth comes forth blessing and cursing. My brothers, these things ought not to be so.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Does a spring send forth from the same opening the sweet and the bitter?

<span class="v-num">12</span> Can a fig tree, my brothers, produce olives, or a vine figs? So no spring <span class="added-text">(can)</span> make salt water and sweet.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> wise and knowledgeable among you? Let him show out of <span class="added-text">(his)</span> good conduct his works in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> meekness of wisdom.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But if you have bitter <span class="alt-label">envy</span><span class="alt-text">(=jealousy)</span> and <span class="alt-label">self-seeking</span><span class="alt-text">(=strife)</span> in your heart, do not boast and lie against the truth.

<span class="v-num">15</span> This wisdom is not <span class="added-text">(one)</span> coming down from above, but <span class="added-text">(is)</span> earthly, <span class="alt-label">sensual</span><span class="alt-text">(=natural)</span>, demonic.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For where <span class="alt-label">envy</span><span class="alt-text">(=jealousy)</span> and <span class="alt-label">self-seeking</span><span class="alt-text">(=strife)</span> <span class="added-text">(are)</span>, there <span class="added-text">(is)</span> confusion and every worthless <span class="alt-label">deed</span><span class="alt-text">(=thing)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, easily <span class="alt-label">obeyed</span><span class="alt-text">(=compliant)</span>, full of mercy and good fruits, without <span class="alt-label">partiality</span><span class="alt-text">(=impartial)</span> and without hypocrisy.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fruit of righteousness is sown in peace by those <span class="alt-label">making</span><span class="alt-text">(=who make)</span> peace.

[^1]: 'τὸν τροχὸν τῆς γενέσεως'(삶의 수레바퀴)는 인생 전체의 진행 과정을 가리키는 관용적 표현으로, 혀가 삶 전체를 불태우는 파괴력을 지녔음을 강조한다.
[^2]: '하나님의 모양대로'(καθ᾿ ὁμοίωσιν Θεοῦ)는 창세기 1:26의 인간 창조 기사를 반영하여, 사람을 저주하는 것이 하나님의 형상을 저주하는 것임을 부각시킨다.
