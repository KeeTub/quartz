# James 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> My brothers, do not have the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, <span class="added-text">(the Lord)</span> of glory, with <span class="alt-label">partiality</span><span class="alt-text">(=favoritism)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> For if there comes into your synagogue a man with a gold ring, in bright clothing, and there comes in also a poor <span class="added-text">(man)</span> in filthy clothing,[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> and you look upon the one wearing the bright clothing and say to him, 'You sit here <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=in a good place)</span>,' and say to the poor <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, 'You stand there, or sit here under my footstool,'

<span class="v-num">4</span> have you not made distinctions among yourselves, and become judges with evil thoughts?

<span class="v-num">5</span> Listen, my beloved brothers: did not God choose the poor of this world, rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom which He promised to those who love Him?

<span class="v-num">6</span> But you have dishonored the poor <span class="added-text">(man)</span>. Do not the rich oppress you, and themselves drag you to <span class="alt-label">courts</span><span class="alt-text">(=tribunals)</span>?

<span class="v-num">7</span> Do they not blaspheme the good name that was called upon you?

<span class="v-num">8</span> If indeed you fulfill the royal law, according to the Scripture, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself,' you do well;[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> but if you show partiality, you <span class="alt-label">commit</span><span class="alt-text">(=work)</span> sin, being convicted by the law as transgressors.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For whoever shall keep the whole law, but stumble in one <span class="added-text">(point)</span>, has become guilty of all.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For He who said, 'Do not commit adultery,' also said, 'Do not murder.' Now if you do not commit adultery, but you murder, you have become a transgressor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law.[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> So speak and so act, as <span class="added-text">(those)</span> about to be judged by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law of liberty;

<span class="v-num">13</span> for judgment <span class="added-text">(is)</span> merciless to the one who has not shown mercy; and mercy boasts <span class="alt-label">against</span><span class="alt-text">(=triumphs over)</span> judgment.

<span class="v-num">14</span> What <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the profit, my brothers, if someone says he has faith, but does not have works? Can <span class="added-text">(his)</span> faith save him?[^5]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And if a brother or sister is <span class="added-text">(found)</span> naked and lacking daily food,[^6]

<span class="v-num">16</span> and someone of you says to them, 'Go in peace, be warmed and be filled,' but does not give them the things needful for the body, what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the profit?

<span class="v-num">17</span> So also faith, if it does not have works, is dead by itself.[^7]

<span class="v-num">18</span> But someone will say, 'You have faith, and I have works'; show me your faith <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=apart from)</span> your works, and I will show you <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> my works my faith.

<span class="v-num">19</span> You believe that God is one; you do well. The demons also believe, and shudder.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But do you want to know, O <span class="alt-label">empty</span><span class="alt-text">(=foolish)</span> man, that faith without works is dead?

<span class="v-num">21</span> Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he offered up Isaac his son upon the altar?[^8]

<span class="v-num">22</span> You see that faith worked together with his works, and by works faith was <span class="alt-label">perfected</span><span class="alt-text">(=made complete)</span>?

<span class="v-num">23</span> And the Scripture was fulfilled which says, 'And Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him for righteousness,' and he was called '<span class="added-text">(the)</span> friend of God.'[^9]

<span class="v-num">24</span> You see then that a man is justified by works, and not by faith alone.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And likewise was not Rahab the harlot also justified by works, when she received the messengers, and sent <span class="added-text">(them)</span> out by another way?

<span class="v-num">26</span> For as the body without <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit is dead, so also faith without works is dead.

[^1]: '영광의 주'라는 칭호는 예수 그리스도의 신적 영광을 가리키는 표현이다.
[^2]: ἐὰν+가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 상황을 전제하는 조건절이다.
[^3]: 레위기 19:18 인용이다.
[^4]: 출애굽기 20:13-14(십계명) 인용이다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법 조건절로 실제 있을 수 있는 상황을 가정한다.
[^6]: ἐὰν+가정법 조건절이다.
[^7]: ἐὰν+가정법 조건절이다.
[^8]: 여기서 '옳다 인정받다(δικαιόω)'는 바울의 칭의 용법과 달리 행위로 드러나 확증되는 의를 가리킨다.
[^9]: 창세기 15:6 인용이다.
