<span class="v-num">1</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> elders among you I exhort, <span class="added-text">(I)</span> the fellow elder and witness of the sufferings of Christ, <del class="deleted-text">[who am]</del> also a <span class="alt-label">partaker</span><span class="alt-text">(=companion)</span> of the glory about to be revealed,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Shepherd the flock of God among you, exercising oversight not by compulsion but <span class="alt-label">willingly</span><span class="alt-text">(=of your own will)</span>; not for shameful gain but eagerly;

<span class="v-num">3</span> nor as lording it over those allotted <span class="added-text">(to you)</span>, but becoming examples to the flock.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when the chief shepherd is manifested, you will receive the unfading crown of glory.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Likewise, younger ones, be subject to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elders; and all <span class="added-text">(of you)</span>, being subject to one another, clothe yourselves with humility, because 'God resists <span class="added-text">(the)</span> proud, but gives grace to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> humble.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, so that he may exalt you in <span class="added-text">(due)</span> time,[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> casting all your anxiety upon him, because he <span class="alt-label">cares</span><span class="alt-text">(=is concerned)</span> concerning you.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Be sober, be watchful, because your adversary <span class="added-text">(the)</span> devil walks about as a roaring lion, seeking someone he may devour;

<span class="v-num">9</span> resist him, firm in the faith, knowing the same sufferings are being accomplished in your brotherhood in the world.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the God of all grace, who called us into his eternal glory in Christ Jesus, <del class="deleted-text">[after you]</del> having suffered a little while, he himself will perfect you, establish, strengthen, <del class="deleted-text">[and]</del> settle <span class="added-text">(you)</span>;[^5]

<span class="v-num">11</span> To him <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory and the power (= dominion) forever and ever. Amen.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Through Silvanus, the faithful brother to you, as I reckon, I have written briefly, exhorting and testifying this to be <span class="added-text">(the)</span> true grace of God in which you stand.

<span class="v-num">13</span> She who is co-elect in Babylon greets you, and Mark my son.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Greet one another with a kiss of love. Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you all who are in Christ Jesus. Amen.

[^1]: κοινωνός는 단순한 목격자가 아니라 '함께 나누는 자'로서, 베드로가 그리스도의 고난뿐 아니라 장차 나타날 영광에도 동참할 자임을 강조한다.
[^2]: κλῆρος는 '제비뽑기로 얻은 몫'에서 유래하여 장로들에게 맡겨진 성도들을 뜻하며, 소유물이 아니라 위탁받은 자들임을 함의한다.
[^3]: 이는 잠언 3:34(LXX)의 인용으로, 겸손의 권면을 성경 권위로 뒷받침한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ὑψώσῃ)은 목적절로, 낮춤이 하나님께서 때가 이르면 높이시는 목적으로 이어짐을 나타낸다.
[^5]: παθόντας는 부정과거 분사로, 본동사(온전케 하다 등)보다 앞선 시점의 짧은 고난을 나타내어 '잠시 고난받은 후에'로 옮겼다.
