<span class="v-num">1</span> Christ therefore having suffered for us in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, arm yourselves also with the same <span class="alt-label">thought</span><span class="alt-text">(=resolve)</span>; because the one having suffered in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh has ceased from sin<span class="added-text">(s)</span>.

<span class="v-num">2</span> <span class="added-text">(so as)</span> to live the remaining time in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh no longer by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lusts of men, but by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> For sufficient to us <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the past time of <span class="added-text">(our)</span> life to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, having walked in licentiousness, lusts, drunkenness, revelings, drinking parties, and abominable idolatries;

<span class="v-num">4</span> in which they think it strange that you do not run with <span class="added-text">(them)</span> into the same <span class="alt-label">excess</span><span class="alt-text">(=flood)</span> of debauchery, blaspheming <span class="added-text">(you)</span>;

<span class="v-num">5</span> who will give account to the one ready to judge <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For this reason the gospel was preached to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead also, that they might be judged according to men in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, but might live according to God in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But the end of all things has drawn near. Be sober-minded therefore, and <span class="alt-label">watch</span><span class="alt-text">(=be sober)</span> unto prayers;

<span class="v-num">8</span> But above all things, having fervent love toward one <span class="alt-label">another</span><span class="alt-text">(=yourselves)</span>, because love will cover a multitude of sins.

<span class="v-num">9</span> <span class="added-text">(Be)</span> hospitable to one another, without murmurings;

<span class="v-num">10</span> As each <span class="added-text">(one)</span> has received a <span class="alt-label">gift</span><span class="alt-text">(=grace-gift)</span>, <span class="added-text">(be)</span> ministering it to one another, as good stewards of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> manifold grace of God;

<span class="v-num">14</span> If you are reproached in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> name of Christ, blessed <span class="added-text">(are you)</span>; because the <span class="added-text">(Spirit)</span> of glory and the Spirit of God rests upon you: on their part he is blasphemed, but on your part he is glorified.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For let none of you suffer as a murderer, or thief, or evildoer, or as a meddler in other men's <span class="alt-label">matters</span><span class="alt-text">(=busybody in other people's affairs)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For the time <span class="added-text">(has come)</span> for the judgment to begin from the house of God; and if <span class="added-text">(it begins)</span> first from us, what <span class="added-text">(will be)</span> the end of those disobeying the gospel of God?

<span class="v-num">18</span> "And if the righteous is <span class="alt-label">scarcely</span><span class="alt-text">(=with difficulty)</span> saved, where will the ungodly and sinner appear?"[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> So then let those also who suffer according to the will of God commit their souls in <span class="alt-label">well-doing</span><span class="alt-text">(=doing good)</span> to <span class="added-text">(a/the)</span> faithful Creator.
