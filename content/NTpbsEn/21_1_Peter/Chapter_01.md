# 1 Peter 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elect sojourners of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dispersion <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

<span class="v-num">2</span> according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foreknowledge of God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, in sanctification of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of Jesus Christ: grace to you and peace be multiplied.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to his abundant mercy has caused us to be born again unto a living hope through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> resurrection of Jesus Christ from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead,

<span class="v-num">4</span> unto an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and unfading, kept in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens for us,

<span class="v-num">5</span> who are being guarded by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God through faith unto a salvation ready to be revealed in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> last time;

<span class="v-num">6</span> in <span class="alt-label">which</span><span class="alt-text">(=this)</span> you rejoice greatly, though now for a little while, if it must be, having been grieved by various trials,

<span class="v-num">7</span> so that the <span class="alt-label">testing</span><span class="alt-text">(=proven genuineness)</span> of your faith, much more precious than gold that perishes, though tested by fire, may be found unto praise and honor and glory at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> revelation of Jesus Christ:[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> whom, not having seen, you love; in whom, now not seeing but believing, you rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory,

<span class="v-num">9</span> receiving the <span class="alt-label">end</span><span class="alt-text">(=outcome)</span> of your faith, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> salvation of <span class="added-text">(your)</span> souls;

<span class="v-num">10</span> concerning which salvation the prophets sought out and searched diligently, who prophesied concerning the grace <span class="added-text">(that would come)</span> to you,

<span class="v-num">11</span> searching what or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ in them was signifying, testifying beforehand the sufferings <span class="added-text">(destined)</span> for Christ, and the glories after these things;

<span class="v-num">12</span> to whom it was revealed that not to themselves, but to us, they were ministering these things, which now have been announced to you through those who preached <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gospel to you by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit sent from heaven — things which angels desire to look into.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore, having girded up the loins of your mind, being sober, hope perfectly upon the grace being brought to you at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> revelation of Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">14</span> As children of obedience, not conforming yourselves to the former lusts <span class="added-text">(that were yours)</span> in your ignorance,

<span class="v-num">15</span> but according to the <span class="added-text">(One)</span> who called you, holy, you yourselves also become holy in all <span class="added-text">(your)</span> conduct;

<span class="v-num">16</span> because it is written, 'Be holy, for I am holy.'

<span class="v-num">17</span> And if you call upon <span class="added-text">(as)</span> Father the <span class="added-text">(One)</span> who judges impartially according to each one's work, conduct yourselves in fear during the time of your sojourning;[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> knowing that you were redeemed from your futile conduct handed down from <span class="added-text">(your)</span> fathers, not with perishable things, <span class="added-text">(like)</span> silver or gold,

<span class="v-num">19</span> but with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot:

<span class="v-num">20</span> foreknown indeed before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, but manifested in these last times for your sake,[^4]

<span class="v-num">21</span> who through him believe in God, who raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead and gave him glory, so that your faith and hope might be in God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Having purified your souls in the obedience of the truth through the Spirit unto unfeigned brotherly love, love one another fervently from a pure heart:

<span class="v-num">23</span> having been born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of God living and abiding forever.

<span class="v-num">24</span> because 'all flesh <span class="added-text">(is)</span> as grass, and all <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of man as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flower of grass. The grass withered, and its flower fell off;[^5]

<span class="v-num">25</span> but the word of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord abides forever.' And this is the word which was preached as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gospel to you.

[^1]: πληθυνθείη는 소원을 나타내는 희구법으로, 서신 서두에 쓰이는 축복 기원의 관용구이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(εὑρεθῇ)은 목적절로, 믿음의 연단이 그리스도 재림 때 나타날 결과(칭찬·존귀·영광)를 지향함을 보여준다.
[^3]: ἀπροσωπολήπτως(공평하게)는 하나님의 심판이 사람의 외모나 신분에 좌우되지 않음을 강조하는 핵심 용어이다.
[^4]: 완료분사 προεγνωσμένου(미리 알려짐)와 부정과거분사 φανερωθέντος(나타나심)의 대조는 그리스도의 선재와 성육신을 대비시킨다.
[^5]: 이사야 40:6-8의 인용으로, 인생의 무상함과 하나님 말씀의 영원함을 대조한다.
