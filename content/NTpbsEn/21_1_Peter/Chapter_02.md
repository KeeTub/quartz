<span class="v-num">1</span> Having therefore put off all malice, and all deceit, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil-speakings,

<span class="v-num">2</span> as newborn babes, desire the <span class="alt-label">reasonable</span><span class="alt-text">(=spiritual)</span>, pure milk, that by it you may grow,[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> if indeed you tasted that the Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> <span class="alt-label">good</span><span class="alt-text">(=kind)</span>.

<span class="v-num">4</span> To whom coming, <span class="added-text">(a)</span> living stone, rejected indeed by men, but with God chosen, precious,

<span class="v-num">5</span> you also, as living stones, are built up, a spiritual house, a holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices acceptable to God through Jesus Christ.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Therefore also it is contained in the Scripture, 'Behold, I lay in Zion a chief cornerstone, chosen, precious; and the one believing on him shall by no means be put to shame.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> To you therefore who believe <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the <span class="alt-label">honor</span><span class="alt-text">(=preciousness)</span>; but to <span class="added-text">(those)</span> disobeying, 'the stone which the builders rejected, this became <span class="added-text">(the)</span> head of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> corner,'[^4]

<span class="v-num">8</span> and 'a stone of stumbling, and a rock of offense'; who stumble at the word, being disobedient, unto which also they were appointed.[^5]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation, a people for <span class="added-text">(his)</span> possession, that you may proclaim the excellencies of him who called you out of darkness into his marvelous light;[^6]

<span class="v-num">10</span> who once <span class="added-text">(were)</span> not a people, but now <span class="added-text">(are)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> people of God; who had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Beloved, I urge <span class="added-text">(you)</span> as sojourners and pilgrims, to abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul;

<span class="v-num">12</span> having your conduct among the Gentiles good, that in that in which they speak against you as evildoers, having observed <span class="added-text">(your)</span> good works, they may glorify God in the day of visitation.[^7]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Submit yourselves therefore to every human <span class="alt-label">institution</span><span class="alt-text">(=ordinance)</span>, for the Lord's sake: whether to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> king, as supreme;

<span class="v-num">14</span> or to governors, as sent through him for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> punishment indeed of evildoers, but <span class="added-text">(for the)</span> praise of those doing good;

<span class="v-num">15</span> because so is the will of God, that by doing good you should silence the ignorance of foolish men.

<span class="v-num">16</span> as free, and not as having <span class="added-text">(your)</span> freedom <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a cloak of malice, but as servants of God.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Honor all, love the brotherhood, fear God, honor the king.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Servants, submitting yourselves in all fear to <span class="added-text">(your)</span> masters, not only to the good and gentle, but also to the <span class="alt-label">perverse</span><span class="alt-text">(=crooked)</span>.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> <span class="alt-label">grace</span><span class="alt-text">(=commendable)</span>, if because of conscience toward God someone endures sorrows, suffering unjustly.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For what credit <span class="added-text">(is it)</span>, if sinning and being buffeted, you shall endure <span class="added-text">(it)</span>? But if, doing good and suffering, you shall endure <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> <span class="alt-label">grace</span><span class="alt-text">(=favor)</span> with God.

<span class="v-num">21</span> For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that you should follow his footsteps;[^8]

<span class="v-num">22</span> who did not commit sin, nor was deceit found in his mouth;

<span class="v-num">23</span> who, being reviled, did not revile in return; suffering, did not threaten; but committed <span class="added-text">(himself)</span> to him who judges righteously;

<span class="v-num">24</span> who himself bore our sins in his body on the tree, that we, having died to sins, might live to righteousness; by whose stripe you were healed.[^9]

<span class="v-num">25</span> For you were like sheep going astray; but you have now returned to the Shepherd and <span class="alt-label">Overseer</span><span class="alt-text">(=Bishop)</span> of your souls.

[^1]: ἵνα+αὐξηθῆτε(가정법)는 목적절로, 순전한 젖을 사모하는 목적이 신앙의 성장에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἀνενέγκαι(부정사)는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문으로, 신령한 집으로 지어지는 목적이 신령한 제사를 드리는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^3]: 이사야 28:16의 인용으로, 그리스도를 시온의 모퉁잇돌로 제시한다.
[^4]: 시편 118:22의 인용이다.
[^5]: 이사야 8:14의 인용이다.
[^6]: ὅπως+ἐξαγγείλητε(가정법)는 목적절로, 하나님의 백성으로 택하심을 받은 목적이 그의 덕을 선포하는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἵνα+δοξάσωσι(가정법)는 목적절로, 선한 행실의 목적이 이방인들이 하나님께 영광 돌리게 하는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: ἵνα+ἐπακολουθήσητε(가정법)는 목적절로, 그리스도의 고난과 본을 남기심의 목적이 그 발자취를 따르게 하는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^9]: ἵνα+ζήσωμεν(가정법)은 목적절로, 그리스도께서 죄를 짊어지신 목적이 우리가 의에 대해 살게 하는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
