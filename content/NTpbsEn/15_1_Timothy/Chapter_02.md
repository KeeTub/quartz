<span class="v-num">1</span> I exhort, therefore, first of all, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> petitions, prayers, <span class="alt-label">intercessions</span><span class="alt-text">(=petitions)</span>, <span class="added-text">(and)</span> thanksgivings be made for all men,

<span class="v-num">2</span> for kings and all those being in high position, that we might live a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and gravity.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> For this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> good and acceptable before God our Savior,

<span class="v-num">4</span> who wants all men to be saved and to come to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> truth.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> one God, and one mediator of God and men, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> man Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">6</span> who gave himself <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a ransom for all, the testimony in its <span class="alt-label">own</span><span class="alt-text">(=proper)</span> times,[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> for which I was appointed a herald and apostle <span class="added-text">(I speak (the)</span> truth in Christ, I do not lie), a teacher of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles in faith and truth.

<span class="v-num">8</span> I want, therefore, the men to pray in every place, lifting up holy hands without wrath and <span class="alt-label">disputing</span><span class="alt-text">(=doubting)</span>;

<span class="v-num">9</span> likewise also the women <span class="added-text">(are)</span> to adorn themselves in <span class="alt-label">respectable</span><span class="alt-text">(=modest)</span> apparel, with <span class="alt-label">shame</span><span class="alt-text">(=modesty)</span> and <span class="alt-label">self-control</span><span class="alt-text">(=sound mind)</span>, not with braided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly clothing,

<span class="v-num">10</span> but with what is fitting for women professing godliness, through good works.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Let a woman learn in quietness, in all submission.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But I do not permit a woman to teach, nor to have <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=dominion)</span> over a man, but to be in quietness.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For Adam was formed first, then Eve.

<span class="v-num">14</span> and Adam was not deceived, but the woman, having been deceived, came to be in transgression;

<span class="v-num">15</span> But she will be saved through childbearing, if they continue in faith and love and holiness with <span class="alt-label">self-control</span><span class="alt-text">(=sound mind)</span>.[^4]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 기도가 지향하는 목적(평온하고 경건한 삶)을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἀντίλυτρον은 신약에서 이곳에만 나오는 강조형으로, 단순한 대가가 아니라 값을 치른 대속의 완전성을 부각한다.
[^3]: αὐθεντεῖν은 신약 유일 용례로 단순한 가르침의 권위가 아니라 '주관하다·지배하다'의 강한 뉘앙스를 지닌다.
[^4]: ἐὰν+가정법의 3급 조건문으로 아직 성취되지 않은 가능성을 나타내며, 주어가 단수(여자)에서 복수(그들)로 전환되어 여성 전체를 가리킨다.
