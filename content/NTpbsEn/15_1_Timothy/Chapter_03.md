<span class="v-num">1</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the word: if anyone desires <span class="added-text">(the)</span> office of overseer, he desires a good work.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Therefore it is necessary <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the overseer to be blameless, husband of one wife, temperate, sober-minded, orderly, hospitable, apt to teach;

<span class="v-num">3</span> not given to wine, not a striker, not greedy of filthy lucre, but gentle, not a <span class="alt-label">brawler</span><span class="alt-text">(=not quarrelsome)</span>, not a lover of money;

<span class="v-num">4</span> ruling well his own house, having <span class="added-text">(his)</span> children in subjection with all gravity;

<span class="v-num">5</span> But if anyone does not know <span class="added-text">(how)</span> to rule his own house, how will he take care of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> church of God?

<span class="v-num">6</span> not a novice, lest being puffed up <span class="added-text">(with pride)</span> he fall into the judgment of the devil.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And he must also have a good testimony from those <span class="alt-label">outside</span><span class="alt-text">(=outsiders)</span>, lest he fall into reproach and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> snare of the devil.[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Deacons likewise <span class="added-text">(must be)</span> <span class="alt-label">grave</span><span class="alt-text">(=dignified)</span>, not double-tongued, not given to much wine, not greedy of filthy lucre;

<span class="v-num">9</span> holding the mystery of the faith in a pure conscience.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And let these also first be tested, then let them serve <span class="added-text">(as deacons)</span>, being blameless.

<span class="v-num">11</span> women likewise <span class="added-text">(must be)</span> <span class="alt-label">grave</span><span class="alt-text">(=dignified)</span>, not slanderers, temperate, faithful in all things.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Let deacons be husbands of one wife, ruling well <span class="added-text">(their)</span> children and their own houses.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For those who have served well purchase to themselves a good <span class="alt-label">standing</span><span class="alt-text">(=degree)</span>, and much <span class="alt-label">boldness</span><span class="alt-text">(=confidence)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith which is in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">14</span> These things I write to you, hoping to come to you shortly;

<span class="v-num">15</span> but if I delay, <span class="added-text">(I write)</span> that you may know how it is necessary to conduct <span class="alt-label">yourself</span><span class="alt-text">(=behave)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house of God, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> church of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> pillar and ground of the truth.[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And confessedly great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifested in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, was justified in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, was seen by angels, was preached among <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, was believed on in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, was received up in glory.[^4]

[^1]: ἵνα μὴ...ἐμπέσῃ는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 절로, 새신자를 감독으로 세우지 말아야 할 이유(목적)를 밝힌다.
[^2]: ἵνα μὴ...ἐμπέσῃ 역시 목적의 가정법 절로 앞 절의 구조를 반복한다.
[^3]: ἐὰν βραδύνω는 미실현 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건문이며, ἵνα εἰδῇς는 그 조건 속에서 편지를 쓰는 목적을 밝히는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^4]: TR은 '하나님께서(Θεός)'로 읽어 성육신하신 하나님을 명시하는 반면 일부 사본은 '그가(ὅς)'로 읽는다; 이 여섯 동사는 그리스도의 지상 사역 전체를 요약하는 초대교회 찬송으로 이해된다.
