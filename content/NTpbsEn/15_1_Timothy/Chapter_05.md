# 1 Timothy 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> Do not rebuke an <span class="alt-label">elder</span><span class="alt-text">(=older man)</span>, but exhort <span class="added-text">(him)</span> as a father; younger men, as brothers;[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Older women, as mothers; younger <span class="added-text">(women)</span>, as sisters, in all purity.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Honor widows, those who are widows indeed.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But if any widow has children or grandchildren, let them learn first to show piety toward their own <span class="alt-label">house</span><span class="alt-text">(=household)</span>, and to repay a return to their forebears; for this is good and acceptable before God.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But she who is a widow indeed, and left alone, has hoped upon God, and continues in supplications and prayers night and day;

<span class="v-num">6</span> But she who lives in <span class="alt-label">pleasure</span><span class="alt-text">(=self-indulgence)</span> is dead while living.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And command these things, that they may be blameless.[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> But if anyone does not provide for his own, and especially for <span class="added-text">(those of his)</span> household, he has denied the faith, and is worse than an unbeliever.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Let a widow be enrolled not less than sixty years <span class="added-text">(old)</span>, having been <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wife of one husband,

<span class="v-num">10</span> Being witnessed to in good works: if she has brought up children, if she has entertained strangers, if she has washed <span class="added-text">(the)</span> saints' feet, if she has helped <span class="added-text">(the)</span> afflicted, if she has followed after every good work.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But refuse younger widows; for when they grow wanton against Christ, they desire to marry,

<span class="v-num">12</span> Having condemnation, because they have rejected their first faith;

<span class="v-num">13</span> And at the same time they also learn <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> idle, going about the houses; and not only idle, but also gossips and busybodies, speaking the things they ought not.

<span class="v-num">14</span> I want therefore younger <span class="added-text">(widows)</span> to marry, bear children, manage the house, give no occasion to the adversary for reviling's sake.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For already some have turned aside after Satan.

<span class="v-num">16</span> If any believing man or believing woman has widows, let them help them, and let not the church be burdened; that it may help those who are widows indeed.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Let the elders who rule well be counted worthy of double honor, especially those who labor in the word and teaching.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For the scripture says, "You shall not muzzle an ox threshing <span class="added-text">(out the grain)</span>;" and, "The laborer is worthy of his wage."[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Against an elder do not receive an accusation, except upon <span class="added-text">(the testimony of)</span> two or three witnesses.[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Those who sin, rebuke before all, that the rest also may have fear.[^7]

<span class="v-num">21</span> I solemnly charge <span class="added-text">(you)</span> before God and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ and the elect angels, that you keep these things without prejudice, doing nothing by partiality.[^8]

<span class="v-num">22</span> Lay hands quickly on no one, neither be partaker of other men's sins. Keep yourself pure.

<span class="v-num">23</span> No longer drink only water, but use a little wine, because of your stomach and your frequent infirmities.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Some men's sins are evident beforehand, going before to judgment; but some also follow after.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Likewise also the good works are evident beforehand; and those that are otherwise cannot be hidden.

[^1]: 여기 πρεσβυτέρῳ는 17절의 직분으로서의 '장로'가 아니라 단순히 나이 많은 남자를 가리킨다.
[^2]: ζῶσα(현재분사, 살아 있으면서)와 τέθνηκε(완료형, 죽어버렸다)의 대조로 역설적 상태를 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ὦσιν) 목적절로, 명령의 목적('책잡힐 것이 없게 하려고')을 나타냄. 명령을 지키는 결과가 아니라 의도된 목표를 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπαρκέσῃ)은 목적절로, 교회가 짐을 지지 않게 하는 행위의 목적(참 과부를 돕게 하려 함)을 나타낸다. 교회 구제의 자원이 진짜 도움이 필요한 이들에게 집중되도록 하는 의도를 강조한다.
[^5]: 앞 인용은 신명기 25:4, 뒤 인용은 누가복음 10:7과 연결되어 사도의 가르침을 성경으로 확증한다.
[^6]: 두세 증인의 원칙은 신명기 19:15을 따른 것으로, 장로 재판의 절차적 보호를 강조한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχωσι) 목적절로 '나머지 사람들도 두려움을 갖게 하려고'라는 책망의 목적을 나타낸다. 공개적 책망의 의도가 개인 징계를 넘어 공동체 전체의 경각심 형성에 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(φυλάξῃς)이 '지키라'는 명령의 내용을 목적절로 제시함. 엄중한 명령(디아마르튀로마이)이 지향하는 실천적 목표를 나타낸다.
