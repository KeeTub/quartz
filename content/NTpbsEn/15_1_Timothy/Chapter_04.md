# 1 Timothy 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> But the Spirit says expressly, that in later times some will depart from the faith, giving heed to deceiving spirits and teachings of demons,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hypocrisy of liars, having been seared in their own conscience,

<span class="v-num">3</span> forbidding to marry, <span class="added-text">(and commanding)</span> to abstain from foods, which God created for reception with thanksgiving to the ones believing and having <span class="alt-label">known</span><span class="alt-text">(=knowing)</span> the truth.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For every creature of God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> good, and nothing <span class="added-text">(is)</span> to be rejected, being received with thanksgiving;

<span class="v-num">5</span> for it is sanctified through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of God and prayer.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Putting these things before the brothers, you will be a good servant of Jesus Christ, being nourished <span class="added-text">(in)</span> the words of the faith and of the good teaching which you have followed.

<span class="v-num">7</span> But refuse the profane and old-womanish <span class="alt-label">myths</span><span class="alt-text">(=fables)</span>; and <span class="alt-label">exercise</span><span class="alt-text">(=train)</span> yourself unto godliness.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For bodily exercise is profitable for a little; but godliness is profitable for all things, having <span class="added-text">(the)</span> promise of the life now and of the <span class="added-text">(life)</span> about to come.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the word, and worthy of all acceptance.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For to this end we both labor and are reproached, because we have hoped in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God, who is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Savior of all men, especially of believers.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Command and teach these things.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Let no one despise your youth, but be<span class="added-text">(come)</span> <span class="added-text">(a)</span> <span class="alt-label">pattern</span><span class="alt-text">(=example)</span> of the believers in word, in conduct, in love, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Until I come, give attention to the reading, to the exhortation, to the teaching.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Do not neglect the gift in you, which was given to you through prophecy with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> laying on of the hands of the <span class="alt-label">presbytery</span><span class="alt-text">(=eldership)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Practice these things; be in these things; that your progress may be manifest <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> all.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Take heed to yourself and to the teaching; continue in them; for doing this, you will save both yourself and the ones hearing you.

[^1]: ἀποστήσονταί는 미래시제로서 장차 있을 배교를 예언적으로 선언하는 표현이다.
[^2]: σωτὴρ πάντων ἀνθρώπων은 만인을 향한 하나님의 보편적 구원의 뜻과, 믿는 자에게 실제로 임하는 구원을 대조하는 표현이다.
[^3]: προφητείας와 ἐπιθέσεως τῶν χειρῶν은 초대교회에서 은사 수여와 직분 임명이 예언적 지시와 장로들의 안수를 통해 이루어졌음을 보여준다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ)이 이끄는 목적절로, 디모데의 헌신이 낳을 결과(진보의 드러남)를 목적으로 제시한다.
