<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> command of God our Savior, and of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ our hope,

<span class="v-num">2</span> To Timothy, <span class="added-text">(my)</span> true child in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith: Grace, mercy, peace from God our Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

<span class="v-num">3</span> As I <span class="alt-label">urged</span><span class="alt-text">(=exhorted)</span> you to remain in Ephesus, <del class="deleted-text">[while]</del> going to Macedonia, that you might charge some not to teach different <span class="added-text">(doctrine)</span>,[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> nor to give heed to myths and endless genealogies, which cause <span class="alt-label">disputes</span><span class="alt-text">(=questionings)</span> rather than <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">administration</span><span class="alt-text">(=stewardship)</span> of God which is in faith;

<span class="v-num">5</span> But the <span class="alt-label">end</span><span class="alt-text">(=goal)</span> of the command is love out of a pure heart and a good conscience and faith <span class="alt-label">unfeigned</span><span class="alt-text">(=sincere)</span>,

<span class="v-num">6</span> from which some, having missed the <span class="alt-label">mark</span><span class="alt-text">(=swerved)</span>, have turned aside to vain <span class="alt-label">talking</span><span class="alt-text">(=empty discourse)</span>,

<span class="v-num">7</span> wanting to be teachers of the law, understanding neither what they say nor concerning what things they confidently affirm.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But we know that the law <span class="added-text">(is)</span> good, if anyone uses it <span class="alt-label">lawfully</span><span class="alt-text">(=rightly)</span>,[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> knowing this, that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law is not laid down for a righteous <span class="added-text">(person)</span>, but for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lawless and <span class="alt-label">unruly</span><span class="alt-text">(=insubordinate)</span>, for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ungodly and sinners, for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> unholy and profane, for those who strike <span class="added-text">(their)</span> father and those who strike <span class="added-text">(their)</span> mother, for murderers,

<span class="v-num">10</span> for fornicators, for those who lie with <span class="alt-label">men</span><span class="alt-text">(=homosexuals)</span>, for <span class="alt-label">slave-traders</span><span class="alt-text">(=kidnappers)</span>, for liars, for perjurers, and if there is any other thing that is opposed to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sound doctrine,

<span class="v-num">11</span> according to the gospel of the glory of the blessed God, which I was entrusted <span class="added-text">(with)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And I have <span class="alt-label">thanks</span><span class="alt-text">(=gratitude)</span> to Christ Jesus our Lord, who <span class="alt-label">enabled</span><span class="alt-text">(=empowered)</span> me, that he considered me faithful, putting <span class="added-text">(me)</span> into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">ministry</span><span class="alt-text">(=service)</span>,

<span class="v-num">13</span> <span class="added-text">(I,)</span> who was formerly a blasphemer and a persecutor and an insolent <span class="added-text">(man)</span>; but I was shown mercy, because I did <span class="added-text">(it)</span> ignorantly, in unbelief;

<span class="v-num">14</span> and the grace of our Lord abounded <span class="alt-label">exceedingly</span><span class="alt-text">(=overflowed)</span> with faith and love that <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the saying and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am <span class="alt-label">first</span><span class="alt-text">(=chief)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But for this reason I was shown mercy, that in me first Jesus Christ might show forth <span class="added-text">(his)</span> all <span class="alt-label">patience</span><span class="alt-text">(=longsuffering)</span>, for a <span class="alt-label">pattern</span><span class="alt-text">(=example)</span> to those who are about to believe on him unto life eternal.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Now to the King of the <span class="alt-label">ages</span><span class="alt-text">(=eternal)</span>, incorruptible, invisible, the only wise God, <span class="added-text">(be)</span> honor and glory forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of ages)</span>. Amen.

<span class="v-num">18</span> This command I <span class="alt-label">entrust</span><span class="alt-text">(=commit)</span> to you, child Timothy, according to the prophecies <span class="added-text">(which)</span> went <span class="alt-label">before</span><span class="alt-text">(=were previously given)</span> <span class="alt-label">concerning</span><span class="alt-text">(=upon)</span> you, that by them you might <span class="alt-label">war</span><span class="alt-text">(=wage)</span> the good warfare,[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> having faith and a good conscience, which some having rejected, concerning the faith have made shipwreck;

<span class="v-num">20</span> of whom is Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I delivered to Satan, that they might be <span class="alt-label">taught</span><span class="alt-text">(=disciplined)</span> not to blaspheme.[^6]

---

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(παραγγείλῃς)은 목적절로, 디모데를 에베소에 머물게 한 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(χρῆται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 3급 조건문으로, 율법을 바르게 쓰는 경우를 가정한다.
[^3]: '미쁘다 이 말이여'(πιστὸς ὁ λόγος)는 목회서신에 반복되는 신뢰할 만한 격언 도입 공식이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐνδείξηται)은 바울에게 긍휼이 임한 목적, 곧 후대 믿는 자들의 본보기가 되게 하려 함을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(στρατεύῃ)은 앞선 예언들을 따라 디모데가 선한 싸움을 싸우게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(παιδευθῶσι)은 사탄에게 내어준 목적, 곧 훈계를 받아 비방하지 않게 함을 나타낸다.
