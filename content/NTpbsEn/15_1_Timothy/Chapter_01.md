<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> command of God our Savior and of Christ Jesus our hope,

<span class="v-num">2</span> To Timothy <span class="added-text">(my)</span> true child in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith. Grace, mercy and peace from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

<span class="v-num">3</span> As I exhorted you to remain in Ephesus, <span class="alt-label">going</span><span class="alt-text">(=when I went)</span> into Macedonia, so that you might charge some not to teach differently,

<span class="v-num">4</span> nor <span class="alt-label">to give their mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=to occupy themselves)</span> <span class="added-text">(to)</span> myths and endless genealogies, which promote speculations rather than dispensation of God <span class="added-text">(which is)</span> in faith.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the goal of the charge is love from a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith,

<span class="v-num">6</span> from which some, having missed goal, turned aside to senseless talk,

<span class="v-num">7</span> desiring to be teachers of the law, not understanding either what they are saying or <span class="added-text">(the things)</span> about which they are asserting.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And we know that the law <span class="added-text">(is)</span> good, if anyone <span class="alt-label">may use</span><span class="alt-text">(=uses)</span> it lawfully,

<span class="v-num">14</span> And the grace of our Lord overflowed <span class="added-text">(for me)</span> with faith and love in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">15</span> The saying <span class="added-text">(is)</span> faithful and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners; of whom I am chief,
