# 1 Timothy 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> As many as are slaves under a yoke, let them regard their own masters <span class="added-text">(as)</span> worthy of all honor, so that the name of God and the teaching may not be blasphemed.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> But those having believing masters, let them not despise <span class="added-text">(them)</span> because they are brothers; but rather let them serve <span class="added-text">(them)</span> more, because those partaking of the benefit are believing and beloved. Teach and exhort these things.

<span class="v-num">3</span> If anyone teaches differently, and does not agree to sound words, those of our Lord Jesus Christ, and to the teaching according to godliness,

<span class="v-num">4</span> he is puffed up, knowing nothing, but being sick about disputes and word-battles, from which come envy, strife, blasphemies, evil suspicions,

<span class="v-num">5</span> constant wranglings of men corrupted in mind and deprived of the truth, supposing godliness to be a means of gain. Withdraw from such <span class="added-text">(people)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But godliness with contentment is great gain.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For we brought nothing into the world, <span class="added-text">(and it is)</span> evident that neither can we carry anything out.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But having food and coverings, we will be content with these.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But those wishing to be rich fall into temptation and a snare and many foolish and harmful desires, which plunge men into ruin and destruction.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For the love of money is a root of all evils; which some, coveting, were led astray from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many pains.[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> But you, O man of God, flee these things; but pursue righteousness, godliness, faith, love, patience, meekness.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Fight the good fight of faith; take hold of the eternal life, to which you were also called, and confessed the good confession before many witnesses.

<span class="v-num">13</span> I charge you before God, the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> giving life to all things, and Christ Jesus, the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> having testified the good confession before Pontius Pilate,

<span class="v-num">14</span> <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you keep the commandment spotless, blameless, until the appearing of our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">15</span> which He will show in His own times, the blessed and only Sovereign, the King of those reigning, and Lord of those ruling as lord,

<span class="v-num">16</span> who alone has immortality, dwelling in unapproachable light, whom no man has seen, nor can see, to whom <span class="added-text">(be)</span> honor and eternal might. Amen.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Charge those rich in the present age not to be highminded, nor to have hoped in the uncertainty of riches, but in God, the living <span class="added-text">(one)</span>, who gives us all things richly for enjoyment;

<span class="v-num">18</span> <span class="added-text">(that they)</span> do good, be rich in good works, be ready to distribute, willing to share,

<span class="v-num">19</span> storing up for themselves a good foundation for the future, so that they may take hold of the eternal life.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> O Timothy, guard the thing entrusted <span class="added-text">(to you)</span>, turning away from the profane empty talkings and oppositions of the falsely named knowledge,

<span class="v-num">21</span> which some, professing, have missed the mark concerning the faith. Grace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> first <span class="added-text">(letter)</span> to Timothy was written from Laodicea, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> chief city of Phrygia Pacatiana.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(βλασφημῆται)은 목적절로, 종들의 존경이 하나님의 이름과 교훈이 비방받지 않게 하려는 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^2]: αὐτάρκεια(자족)는 외적 환경에 좌우되지 않는 내적 만족을 뜻하며, 바울은 이를 경건과 결합시켜 참된 유익의 기준으로 제시한다.
[^3]: φιλαργυρία(돈을 사랑함)가 악의 뿌리라는 것은 돈 자체가 아니라 돈에 대한 탐욕이 모든 죄의 근원임을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^4]: ἀπρόσιτος(다가갈 수 없는)는 하나님의 초월적 거룩함을 나타내며, 인간이 스스로는 결코 도달할 수 없는 신적 영광을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιλάβωνται)은 목적절로, 선한 일을 쌓아둠이 영원한 생명을 붙잡으려는 목적임을 보여준다.
