# Titus 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, a servant of God, and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith of God's elect and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of truth, the <span class="added-text">(truth)</span> according to godliness,

<span class="v-num">2</span> upon <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hope of eternal life, which the God who cannot lie promised before eternal <span class="alt-label">times</span><span class="alt-text">(=ages)</span>,

<span class="v-num">3</span> but manifested in his own seasons his word, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> proclamation with which I was entrusted according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> commandment of God our Savior,

<span class="v-num">4</span> to Titus, <span class="added-text">(my)</span> genuine child according to <span class="added-text">(our)</span> common faith: grace, mercy, peace from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ our Savior.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For this reason I left you in Crete, that you might set in order the things that are lacking, and appoint elders in every city, as I directed you—[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> if anyone is blameless, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> husband of one wife, having faithful children not under accusation of dissipation or unruly.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For the overseer must be blameless, as God's steward; not self-willed, not quick-tempered, not given to wine, not a striker, not greedy of shameful gain,

<span class="v-num">8</span> but hospitable, a lover of good, sensible, just, holy, self-controlled,

<span class="v-num">9</span> holding fast the faithful word which is according to the teaching, that he may be able both to exhort in the sound doctrine, and to convict the ones who contradict.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For there are many unruly ones, both vain talkers and deceivers, especially those of the circumcision,

<span class="v-num">11</span> whose mouths must be stopped, who overturn whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for the sake of shameful gain.

<span class="v-num">12</span> One of them, their own prophet, said, 'Cretans <span class="added-text">(are)</span> always liars, evil beasts, idle bellies.'

<span class="v-num">13</span> This testimony is true. For which cause rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith,[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> not giving heed to Jewish myths, and commandments of men who turn away from the truth.

<span class="v-num">15</span> To the pure all things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> pure, but to the defiled and unbelieving nothing <span class="added-text">(is)</span> pure, but even their mind and conscience are defiled.

<span class="v-num">16</span> They profess to know God, but in works they deny <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, being abominable, and disobedient, and unto every good work reprobate.
