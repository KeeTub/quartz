# Titus 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> Remind them to be subject to rulers and authorities, to obey, to be ready for every good work,

<span class="v-num">2</span> to slander no one, to be uncontentious, gentle, showing all meekness toward all men.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For we ourselves also were once foolish, disobedient, being deceived, serving various desires and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, hating one another;

<span class="v-num">4</span> But when the kindness and the love of <span class="alt-label">mankind</span><span class="alt-text">(=philanthropy)</span> of our Savior God appeared,

<span class="v-num">5</span> not <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> works in righteousness which we did, but according to his mercy he saved us, through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> washing of regeneration, and renewal of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit,

<span class="v-num">6</span> whom he poured out on us richly, through Jesus Christ our Savior,

<span class="v-num">7</span> so that having been justified by his grace, we might become heirs according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hope of eternal life.[^1]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the saying, and concerning these things I want you to affirm confidently, so that those having believed God may be careful to maintain good works. These things are good and profitable to men.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But avoid foolish questionings and genealogies and strifes and legal <span class="alt-label">fightings</span><span class="alt-text">(=quarrels about the law)</span>; for they are unprofitable and vain.

<span class="v-num">10</span> A <span class="alt-label">heretical</span><span class="alt-text">(=factious)</span> man after <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first and second admonition, reject,

<span class="v-num">11</span> knowing that such a one has been perverted, and sins, being self-condemned.

<span class="v-num">12</span> When I send Artemas to you, or Tychicus, be diligent to come to me at Nicopolis; for I have decided to winter there.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Send forward Zenas the lawyer and Apollos diligently, so that nothing may be lacking to them.[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And let our <span class="added-text">(people)</span> also learn to maintain good works for necessary needs, so that they may not be unfruitful.[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> All those with me greet you. Greet those loving us in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith. Grace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen. To Titus, having been appointed <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first bishop of the church of the Cretans, written from Nicopolis of Macedonia.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(γενώμεθα)은 목적절로 하나님의 구원 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(φροντίζωσι)은 목적절로, 믿는 자들이 선행에 힘쓰도록 하는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(λείπῃ)은 목적절로, 그들의 여행에 부족함이 없도록 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ὦσιν)은 목적절로, 열매 없는 자가 되지 않도록 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
