# Titus 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> But you, speak the things that are fitting to the sound teaching.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Old men <span class="added-text">(are)</span> to be sober, dignified, sound-minded, sound in faith, in love, in patience;

<span class="v-num">3</span> old women likewise <span class="added-text">(are)</span> to be reverent in behavior, not slanderers, not enslaved to much wine, teachers of what is good;

<span class="v-num">4</span> that they may train the young women to be lovers of <span class="added-text">(their)</span> husbands, lovers of <span class="added-text">(their)</span> children,[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> sound-minded, pure, keepers at home, good, submitting to their own husbands, that the word of God may not be blasphemed.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Likewise <span class="alt-label">urge</span><span class="alt-text">(=exhort)</span> the younger men to be sound-minded;

<span class="v-num">7</span> in all things showing yourself <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> an <span class="alt-label">example</span><span class="alt-text">(=pattern)</span> of good works, in the teaching <span class="added-text">(showing)</span> integrity, dignity, incorruption,

<span class="v-num">8</span> sound speech that cannot be condemned, that the one from the opposing side may be put to shame, having nothing evil to say concerning you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> <span class="added-text">(Exhort)</span> servants to be subject to their own masters, to be well-pleasing in all things, not talking back,

<span class="v-num">10</span> not pilfering, but showing all good fidelity, that they may adorn the teaching of God our Savior in all things.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> For the grace of God, the saving <span class="added-text">(grace)</span>, appeared to all men,

<span class="v-num">12</span> training us that, having denied ungodliness and worldly desires, we should live sound-mindedly and righteously and godly in the present age,[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> awaiting the blessed hope and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ,[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all lawlessness, and purify for himself a people <span class="added-text">(for his own)</span> possession, zealous of good works.[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Speak these things, and exhort, and rebuke with all authority. Let no one <span class="alt-label">disregard</span><span class="alt-text">(=despise)</span> you.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(σωφρονίζωσι): 늙은 여인들이 젊은 여인들을 훈련시키는 목적절.
[^2]: ἵνα μὴ+가정법(βλασφημῆται): 순종의 목적이 하나님 말씀이 비방받지 않게 함임을 보여줌.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐντραπῇ): 디도의 선한 행실의 목적이 반대자를 부끄럽게 함에 있음을 보여줌.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(κοσμῶσιν): 종들의 신실함의 목적이 하나님의 가르침을 빛나게 함에 있음을 보여줌.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ζήσωμεν): 은혜가 우리를 훈육하는 목적이 현세에서 경건한 삶을 살게 함에 있음을 보여줌.
[^6]: 11절의 Ἐπεφάνη(은혜의 나타나심-초림)와 13절의 ἐπιφάνειαν(영광의 나타나심-재림)이 인클루지오를 이루어 그 사이 그리스도인의 삶(12절)을 감싸고 있음을 보여줌.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(λυτρώσηται, καθαρίσῃ): 그리스도의 자기희생의 이중 목적—속량과 정결케 함.
