# 2 Thessalonians 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> For the rest, brothers, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may run and be glorified, just as also with you,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> and that we may be delivered from unreasonable and evil men, for the faith <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not <span class="added-text">(the possession)</span> of all.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But the Lord is faithful, who will establish you and guard <span class="added-text">(you)</span> from the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And we have confidence in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord concerning you, that <span class="added-text">(the things)</span> which we command you, both you do and you will do.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And may the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God, and into the patience of Christ.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now we command you, brothers, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you withdraw yourselves from every brother walking disorderly, and not according to the tradition which he received from us.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For you yourselves know how it is necessary to imitate us, because we did not behave disorderly among you,

<span class="v-num">8</span> nor did we eat bread as a gift from anyone, but in labor and toil, working night and day, so as not to burden any of you,[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> not because we do not have authority, but that we might give ourselves <span class="added-text">(as)</span> an example to you, for you to imitate us.[^5]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if anyone does not want to work, neither let him eat.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For we hear <span class="added-text">(that)</span> some among you are walking disorderly, working nothing, but being busybodies.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now such ones we command and exhort through our Lord Jesus Christ, that working with quietness, they eat their own bread.[^6]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But you, brothers, do not lose heart <span class="added-text">(in)</span> doing good.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And if anyone does not obey our word through the letter, note this one, and do not associate with him, that he may be ashamed;[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Yet do not regard <span class="added-text">(him)</span> as an enemy, but admonish <span class="added-text">(him)</span> as a brother.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Now may the Lord of peace himself give you peace always in every way. <span class="added-text">(May)</span> the Lord <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen.[^8]

<span class="v-num">17</span> The greeting <span class="added-text">(is)</span> by my hand, <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Paul, which is a sign in every letter; so I write.

<span class="v-num">18</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> second <span class="added-text">(letter)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Thessalonians was written from Athens. In 106 lines.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(τρέχῃ, δοξάζηται)은 기도의 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^2]: πεποίθαμεν은 완료시제로 이미 확립된 지속적 확신 상태를 나타낸다.
[^3]: κατευθύναι는 희구법(optative)으로 기원·소원을 나타낸다.
[^4]: πρὸς τὸ μὴ ἐπιβαρῆσαι는 πρός+τό 부정사 목적구문으로 '~하지 않기 위하여'를 뜻한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(δῶμεν)과 εἰς τό+부정사(μιμεῖσθαι)가 함께 목적을 이중으로 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐσθίωσιν)은 명령·권면의 내용을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐντραπῇ)은 목적을 나타내는 절이다.
[^8]: δῴη는 희구법으로 기원을 나타낸다.
