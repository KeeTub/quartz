# 2 Thessalonians 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now we ask you, brothers, concerning the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ and our gathering together to him,

<span class="v-num">2</span> that you may not be quickly shaken in mind or troubled, either through spirit, or through word, or through letter as if <span class="added-text">(it were)</span> from us, as though the day of Christ has come.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Let no one deceive you in any way; for <span class="added-text">(that day will not come)</span> unless the apostasy comes first, and the man of sin (=lawlessness), the son of destruction, is revealed,[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> the one opposing and exalting himself above every one called God or <span class="added-text">(object of)</span> worship, so that he sits in the temple of God as God, showing himself that he is God.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Do you not remember that, while I was still with you, I was telling you these things?

<span class="v-num">6</span> And now you know the <span class="added-text">(thing)</span> restraining, so that he may be revealed in his own time.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For the mystery of lawlessness is already at work; only <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> the one restraining now, until he is taken out of the midst.[^4]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And then the lawless one will be revealed, whom the Lord will consume with the breath of his mouth, and will destroy with the appearance of his coming;

<span class="v-num">9</span> whose coming is according to the working of Satan, in all power and signs and wonders of falsehood,[^5]

<span class="v-num">10</span> and in all deceit of unrighteousness among those who are perishing, because they did not receive the love of the truth, that they might be saved.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And for this reason God will send them a working of <span class="alt-label">deception</span><span class="alt-text">(=delusion)</span>, for them to believe the lie,[^6]

<span class="v-num">12</span> that all who did not believe the truth, but delighted in unrighteousness, may be judged.[^7]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But we ought to give thanks to God always concerning you, brothers loved by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, because God chose you from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning unto salvation, in sanctification of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit and belief of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> truth,[^8]

<span class="v-num">14</span> unto which he called you through our gospel, unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> obtaining of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of our Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">15</span> So then, brothers, stand firm, and hold the traditions which you were taught, whether by word or by our letter.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Now our Lord Jesus Christ himself, and God our Father, who loved us and gave <span class="added-text">(us)</span> eternal comfort and good hope in grace,

<span class="v-num">17</span> comfort your hearts, and establish you in every good word and work.

[^1]: εἰς τὸ + 부정사(σαλευθῆναι)는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문이다.
[^2]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 조건을 나타낸다. TR은 '죄의 사람'(ἁμαρτίας)을 읽어 일부 사본의 '불법의 사람'(ἀνομίας)과 다르다.
[^3]: κατέχον(막는 것)은 종말의 불법한 자의 출현을 억제하는 실체를 가리키는 이 서신 특유의 용어이다.
[^4]: ἐνεργεῖται(현재형)는 불법의 역사가 바울 당시 이미 진행 중임을 강조한다.
[^5]: κατ᾿ ἐνέργειαν(~의 역사를 따라)은 불법한 자의 활동 근원이 사탄에게 있음을 나타낸다.
[^6]: εἰς τὸ πιστεῦσαι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문으로 미혹의 결과를 설명한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(κριθῶσι)은 심판이라는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^8]: εἵλετο(택하다, 부정과거)는 하나님의 영원한 선택을 가리키는 신학적 핵심 동사이다.
