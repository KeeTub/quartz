<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore you are without excuse, O man, everyone who judges; for in that which you judge the other, you condemn yourself, for you who judge practice the same things.

<span class="v-num">2</span> But we know that the judgment of God is according to truth upon those practicing such things.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But do you reckon this, O man who judges those practicing such things and yet does the same, that you will escape the judgment of God?

<span class="v-num">4</span> Or do you despise the riches of his kindness and forbearance and longsuffering, not knowing that the kindness of God leads you to repentance?[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But according to your hardness and unrepentant heart you are storing up for yourself wrath in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of wrath and revelation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteous judgment of God,[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> who will render to each according to his works:[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> to those who by patience in good work seek glory and honor and incorruption, eternal life;

<span class="v-num">8</span> but to those who are of selfish ambition, and disobey the truth but obey unrighteousness, <span class="added-text">(there will be)</span> wrath and indignation,

<span class="v-num">9</span> tribulation and anguish upon every soul of man that works evil, of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jew first and also of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Greek;

<span class="v-num">10</span> but glory and honor and peace to everyone who works the good, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jew first and also to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Greek;

<span class="v-num">11</span> for there is no partiality with God.[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For as many as sinned without law will also perish without law, and as many as sinned in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law will be judged through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law;

<span class="v-num">13</span> for not the hearers of the law <span class="added-text">(are)</span> righteous before God, but the doers of the law will be justified.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> For when Gentiles who do not have <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law do by nature the things of the law, these, not having <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, are a law to themselves;[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness together, and their thoughts between one another accusing or even defending <span class="added-text">(them)</span>,

<span class="v-num">16</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day when God will judge the hidden things of men, according to my gospel, through Jesus Christ.[^7]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Behold, you are called a Jew, and rest upon the law, and boast in God,

<span class="v-num">18</span> and know <span class="added-text">(his)</span> will, and <span class="alt-label">approve</span><span class="alt-text">(=discern)</span> the things that <span class="alt-label">differ</span><span class="alt-text">(=are more excellent)</span>, being instructed out of the law;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and are confident that you yourself are a guide of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blind, a light of those in darkness,

<span class="v-num">20</span> an instructor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foolish, a teacher of babes, having the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law;

<span class="v-num">21</span> You then who teach another, do you not teach yourself? You who preach not to steal, do you steal?

<span class="v-num">22</span> You who say not to commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples?

<span class="v-num">23</span> You who boast in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, through the transgression of the law do you dishonor God?

<span class="v-num">24</span> "For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you," just as it is written.[^8]

<span class="v-num">25</span> For circumcision indeed profits if you practice <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law; but if you are a transgressor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, your circumcision has become uncircumcision.[^9]

<span class="v-num">26</span> If then the uncircumcision keeps the righteous requirements of the law, will not his uncircumcision be reckoned for circumcision?[^10]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And will not the uncircumcision which is by nature, fulfilling the law, judge you who, through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> letter and circumcision, are a transgressor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law?

<span class="v-num">28</span> For he is not a Jew who is one outwardly, nor is that circumcision which is outward in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh;[^11]

<span class="v-num">29</span> but he is a Jew who is one inwardly, and circumcision is of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heart, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit, not in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> letter; whose praise is not from men, but from God.[^12]

[^1]: χρηστότης(인자하심)가 회개로 인도한다는 것은, 하나님의 심판의 지연이 정죄가 아니라 은혜의 기회임을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^2]: '진노의 날'은 최후 심판이라는 종말론적 사건을 가리키며, 현재의 완악함이 미래의 심판을 위해 진노를 축적하는 것으로 그려진다.
[^3]: '행위대로 갚으심'은 최후 심판의 원리를 말하는 것으로, 이후 3장의 믿음에 의한 칭의 교리와 모순되지 않고 믿음의 열매인 삶 전체를 심판의 근거로 삼는 것이다.
[^4]: προσωποληψία(편애)는 유대인과 이방인을 차별 없이 동일한 기준으로 심판하시는 하나님의 공의를 강조하는 핵심어이다.
[^5]: δικαιωθήσονται(의롭다 함을 얻으리라)는 미래형으로, 최후 심판 때 행위로 드러나는 의를 말하며 3장의 믿음으로 말미암은 칭의와 대조가 아니라 보완적 관계이다.
[^6]: νόμον이 관사 없이 쓰여 모세의 율법 자체가 아니라 율법이 요구하는 도덕적 원리를 가리키며, 이는 양심을 통한 자연법 개념을 뒷받침한다.
[^7]: διὰ Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ(예수 그리스도로 말미암아)는 최후 심판이 그리스도를 통해 집행됨을 나타내는 매개적 전치사구이다.
[^8]: 이 인용은 이사야 52:5(또는 겔 36:20)의 칠십인역 표현을 반영한 구약 인용으로, 이스라엘의 불순종이 이방인 가운데 하나님의 이름을 욕되게 함을 지적한다.
[^9]: ἐάν+가정법(πράσσῃς, ᾖς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '네가 실제로 율법을 행하느냐 범하느냐'에 따라 할례의 가치가 결정됨을 강조한다. 즉 할례라는 외적 표지가 아니라 실천 여부가 관건임을 조건문 형식으로 보여준다.
[^10]: ἐάν+가정법(φυλάσσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 3급 조건문으로, '만약 ~한다면'이라는 열린 가정을 나타낸다. 무할례자가 실제로 율법을 지킬 가능성을 상정하며 논증을 전개하는 수사적 조건절이다.
[^11]: 28-29절은 '드러난/은밀한', '육체/영', '유대인/할례'를 교차 배열하는 키아즘 구조로, 외적 형식보다 내적 실체가 참됨을 강조한다.
[^12]: '마음의 할례'는 신명기 30:6의 암시로, 율법조문(γράμμα)이 아닌 성령 안에서의 내적 변화가 참된 언약 백성의 표지임을 말한다.
