<span class="v-num">1</span> What then <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the advantage of the Jew, or what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the benefit of circumcision?

<span class="v-num">2</span> Much in every way: for first, that they were entrusted <span class="added-text">(with)</span> the <span class="alt-label">oracles</span><span class="alt-text">(=words)</span> of God.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For what if some did not believe? Will their unbelief nullify the <span class="alt-label">faith</span><span class="alt-text">(=faithfulness)</span> of God?[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> <span class="added-text">(May it)</span> never be! Let God be true, but every man a liar, as it has been written, 'That you might be justified in your words, and might overcome when you are judged.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But if our unrighteousness <span class="alt-label">commends</span><span class="alt-text">(=establishes)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteousness of God, what shall we say? <span class="added-text">(Is)</span> God unrighteous who inflicts <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wrath? I speak according to <span class="alt-label">man</span><span class="alt-text">(=in human terms)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> <span class="added-text">(May it)</span> never be! Since then how shall God judge the world?

<span class="v-num">7</span> For if the truth of God abounded through my lie to his glory, why am I still also judged as a sinner?

<span class="v-num">8</span> And <span class="added-text">(why)</span> not <span class="added-text">(say)</span>, as we are slandered, and as some affirm that we say, 'Let us do evil things that good things may come'? Their condemnation is just.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> What then? Are we <span class="alt-label">better</span><span class="alt-text">(=do we excel)</span>? Not at all: for we have already charged both Jews and Greeks, that they are all under sin,

<span class="v-num">10</span> as it has been written, "There is none righteous, not even one:[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> there is none who understands, there is none who seeks after God.

<span class="v-num">12</span> They have all turned aside, together they have become unprofitable; there is none who does good, there is not even one.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Their throat <span class="added-text">(is)</span> an opened grave; with their tongues they have used deceit; <span class="added-text">(the)</span> poison of asps <span class="added-text">(is)</span> under their lips:

<span class="v-num">14</span> whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness:

<span class="v-num">15</span> their feet <span class="added-text">(are)</span> swift to shed blood:

<span class="v-num">16</span> destruction and misery <span class="added-text">(are)</span> in their ways:

<span class="v-num">17</span> and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way of peace they have not known:

<span class="v-num">18</span> there is no fear of God before their eyes."

<span class="v-num">19</span> Now we know that whatever the law says, it speaks to those in the law, so that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Because by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> works of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law no flesh will be justified in his sight: for through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> knowledge of sin.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But now, apart from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteousness of God has been manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets;[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> even <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteousness of God through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> Jesus Christ, to all and upon all who believe: for there is no distinction:[^8]

<span class="v-num">23</span> for all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God;

<span class="v-num">24</span> being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:

<span class="v-num">25</span> whom God set forth <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a <span class="alt-label">propitiation</span><span class="alt-text">(=mercy seat)</span> through faith in his blood, for a demonstration of his righteousness, because of the passing <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=overlooking)</span> of sins previously committed,[^9]

<span class="v-num">26</span> in the forbearance of God, for the demonstration of his righteousness at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> present time, that he might be just and the justifier of the one who is of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith of Jesus.[^10]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Where then <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the boasting? It is excluded. By what law? Of works? No, but by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law of faith.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Therefore we <span class="alt-label">conclude</span><span class="alt-text">(=reckon)</span> that a man is justified by faith, without <span class="added-text">(the)</span> works of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law.

<span class="v-num">29</span> Or <span class="added-text">(is he)</span> the God of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jews only? <span class="added-text">(Is he)</span> not also <span class="added-text">(the God)</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles? Yes, of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles also:

<span class="v-num">30</span> since <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> one God, who will justify <span class="added-text">(the)</span> circumcision by faith, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> uncircumcision through faith.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Do we then nullify <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law through faith? <span class="added-text">(May it)</span> never be! On the contrary, we establish <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law.

[^1]: πίστιν τοῦ Θεοῦ는 여기서 사람의 '믿음'이 아니라 하나님의 신실하심(faithfulness)을 가리킨다.
[^2]: 시편 51:4(LXX)의 인용이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔλθῃ) 목적절로 '선한 것이 오게 하려고'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 바울이 반박하는 자들의 왜곡된 논리를 인용한 것으로, 악을 행하는 목적이 선을 이루려 함이라는 궤변을 소개하는 구문이다.
[^4]: 시편 14:1-3, 53:1-3 등을 모아 엮은 인용(catena)이 10-18절까지 이어진다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 율법이 죄를 드러내어 온 세상을 하나님 앞에 정죄하려는 궁극 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἐπίγνωσις ἁμαρτίας는 율법의 기능이 의롭게 함이 아니라 죄를 깨닫게 하는 것임을 강조한다.
[^7]: πεφανέρωται는 완료시제로, 하나님의 의가 나타난 사건의 지속적 결과를 강조한다.
[^8]: πίστεως Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ는 여기서 '예수 그리스도를 믿는 믿음'(목적격적 소유격)으로 이해된다.
[^9]: ἱλαστήριον은 속죄소(출 25장)와 화목제물의 이미지를 함께 담은 핵심 신학 용어다.
[^10]: εἰς τὸ εἶναι는 부정사 목적구문으로, 하나님이 의로우시며 동시에 의롭다 하시는 분이 되려는 목적을 나타낸다.
