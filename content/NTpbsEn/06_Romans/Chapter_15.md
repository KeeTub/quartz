# Romans 15

<span class="v-num">1</span> But we the strong ought to bear the weaknesses of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For let each of us please <span class="added-text">(his)</span> neighbor for <span class="added-text">(his)</span> good, toward building up (=edification).

<span class="v-num">3</span> For even Christ did not please himself, but, just as it is written, 'The reproaches of the ones reproaching you fell upon me.'

<span class="v-num">4</span> For as many things as were written before, were written before for our teaching, so that through the patience and the comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Now may the God of patience and comfort grant you to be of the same mind with one another according to Christ Jesus,[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> so that with one accord, with one mouth, you may glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Therefore receive one another, just as Christ also received us, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of God.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Now I say, Jesus Christ has become a servant of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> circumcision for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> truth of God, to confirm the promises <span class="added-text">(given)</span> to the fathers,[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> the Gentiles <span class="added-text">(should)</span> glorify God for <span class="added-text">(his)</span> mercy, just as it is written, 'Because of this I will confess to you among <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, and sing to your name.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And again he says, 'Rejoice, <span class="added-text">(you)</span> Gentiles, with his people.'

<span class="v-num">11</span> And again, 'Praise the Lord all <span class="added-text">(you)</span> Gentiles, and praise him all <span class="added-text">(you)</span> peoples.'

<span class="v-num">12</span> And again Isaiah says, 'There will be the root of Jesse, and the one rising up to rule <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles; in him <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles will hope.'

<span class="v-num">13</span> Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that you may abound in hope, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> But I myself also am convinced concerning you, my brothers, that you yourselves also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But I have written to you, brothers, the more boldly in part, as reminding you again, because of the grace given to me by God,

<span class="v-num">16</span> for me to be a minister of Jesus Christ to the Gentiles, ministering <span class="added-text">(as priest)</span> the gospel of God, so that the offering of the Gentiles might become acceptable, having been sanctified in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.[^7]

<span class="v-num">17</span> I have, then, <span class="added-text">(a)</span> boast in Christ Jesus in the things pertaining to God;

<span class="v-num">18</span> for I will not dare to speak of anything which Christ did not work through me, for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> obedience of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, by word and deed,

<span class="v-num">19</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of signs and wonders, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of God; so that from Jerusalem and around as far as Illyricum I have fully preached the gospel of Christ;

<span class="v-num">20</span> and thus being eager to preach the gospel, not where Christ was named, so that I might not build upon another's foundation;[^8]

<span class="v-num">21</span> but, just as it is written, 'To whom it was not announced concerning him, they will see; and they who have not heard, will understand.'

<span class="v-num">22</span> Therefore I was also hindered many times from coming to you.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But now, no longer having a place in these regions, and having a longing to come to you for many years,

<span class="v-num">24</span> whenever I journey to Spain, I will come to you; for I hope, passing through, to see you, and to be sent forward there by you, if first I may be filled (=satisfied) in part with your <span class="added-text">(company)</span>.[^9]

<span class="v-num">25</span> But now I am going to Jerusalem, ministering to the saints.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For Macedonia and Achaia were pleased to make some contribution for the poor of the saints who are in Jerusalem;

<span class="v-num">27</span> for they were pleased, and they are debtors to them. For if the Gentiles have shared in their spiritual things, they ought also to minister to them in fleshly things.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Having therefore completed this, and sealed to them this fruit, I will go on by way of you into Spain.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And I know that when I come to you, I will come in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fullness of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blessing of the gospel of Christ.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Now I urge you, brothers, through our Lord Jesus Christ, and through the love of the Spirit, to strive together with me in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> prayers to God on my behalf;

<span class="v-num">31</span> so that I may be delivered from the ones disobeying in Judea, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> my service to Jerusalem may become acceptable to the saints;[^10]

<span class="v-num">32</span> so that I may come to you in joy through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, and may be refreshed together with you.[^11]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Now the God of peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 성경의 인내와 위로가 소망을 낳는 목적을 나타냄.
[^2]: δῴη는 희구법으로 축원·소원을 나타내는 기도체 표현이다.
[^3]: ἵνα절이 5절의 기도의 목적(하나님께 영광 돌림)을 밝힌다.
[^4]: εἰς τὸ βεβαιῶσαι는 부정사 목적구문으로 그리스도의 사역의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: 시편 인용으로 이방인의 찬양이 구약에서 예언되었음을 보여준다.
[^6]: εἰς τὸ περισσεύειν은 부정사 목적구문으로 성령의 능력으로 소망이 넘치는 결과·목적을 나타낸다.
[^7]: εἰς τὸ εἶναι와 ἵνα γένηται가 이어져 바울의 사역과 이방인 제물의 목적을 이중으로 밝힌다.
[^8]: ἵνα μὴ οἰκοδομῶ는 부정 목적절로 바울의 선교 원칙(남의 터에 세우지 않음)의 이유를 밝힌다.
[^9]: ἐὰν+가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성(3급 조건문)을 나타내며 바울의 여행 계획의 조건을 표현한다.
[^10]: 두 개의 ἵνα절이 바울의 기도 요청 목적(구원과 봉사의 열납)을 이중으로 나타낸다.
[^11]: ἵνα절이 30-31절 기도 요청의 궁극 목적(기쁨의 만남과 안식)을 밝힌다.
