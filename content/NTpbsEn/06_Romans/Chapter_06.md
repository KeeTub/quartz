<span class="v-num">1</span> What then shall we say? Shall we continue in sin, that grace may abound?[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> May it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=Let it not be)</span>. We who died to sin, how shall we still live in it?

<span class="v-num">3</span> Or do you not know that as many <span class="added-text">(of us)</span> as were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into his death?

<span class="v-num">4</span> We were buried therefore with him through baptism into death, in order that just as Christ was raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead through the glory of the Father, so we also might walk in newness of life.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> For if we have become <span class="alt-label">united</span><span class="alt-text">(=grown together)</span> with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> likeness of his death, <del class="deleted-text">[but]</del> we shall also be <span class="added-text">(united with the likeness)</span> of his resurrection.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> knowing this, that our old man was crucified with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, in order that the body of sin might be done away, so that we should no longer serve sin.[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For the one who died has been justified from sin.[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> But if we died with Christ, we believe that we shall also live with him,

<span class="v-num">9</span> knowing that Christ, having been raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, dies no more; death no more has dominion over him.[^6]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For that which he died, he died to sin once for all. But that which he lives, he lives to God.[^7]

<span class="v-num">11</span> So also you, reckon yourselves to be dead indeed to sin, but alive to God, in Christ Jesus our Lord.[^8]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Let not sin therefore reign in your mortal body, so as to obey it in its lusts;[^9]

<span class="v-num">13</span> neither present your members <span class="added-text">(as)</span> <span class="alt-label">instruments</span><span class="alt-text">(=weapons)</span> of unrighteousness to sin, but present yourselves to God as alive from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, and your members <span class="added-text">(as)</span> <span class="alt-label">instruments</span><span class="alt-text">(=weapons)</span> of righteousness to God.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For sin shall not have dominion over you, for you are not under law, but under grace.[^10]

<span class="v-num">15</span> What then? Shall we sin, because we are not under law but under grace? May it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=Let it not be)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Do you not know that to whom you present yourselves <span class="added-text">(as)</span> servants for obedience, servants you are of whom you obey, whether of sin unto death, or of obedience unto righteousness?

<span class="v-num">17</span> But thanks <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to God, that you were servants of sin, but you obeyed from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heart that form of teaching to which you were delivered.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And having been freed from sin, you became <span class="alt-label">servants</span><span class="alt-text">(=were enslaved)</span> to righteousness.

<span class="v-num">19</span> I speak <span class="added-text">(in)</span> human <span class="added-text">(terms)</span> because of the weakness of your flesh. For just as you presented your members <span class="added-text">(as)</span> servants to uncleanness and to lawlessness unto lawlessness, so now present your members <span class="added-text">(as)</span> servants to righteousness unto holiness.[^11]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For when you were servants of sin, you were free in regard <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> righteousness.

<span class="v-num">21</span> What fruit then did you have then, in the things of which you are now ashamed? For the end of those <span class="added-text">(things is)</span> death.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But now, having been freed from sin, and having become <span class="alt-label">servants</span><span class="alt-text">(=enslaved)</span> to God, you have your fruit unto holiness, and the end, everlasting life.

<span class="v-num">23</span> For the wages of sin <span class="added-text">(is)</span> death, but the gift of God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> everlasting life, in Christ Jesus our Lord.[^12]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(πλεονάσῃ) 목적절로 '은혜가 더하게 하려고'라는 반문적 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(περιπατήσωμεν) 목적절로 새 생명 가운데 행하게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: σύμφυτοι(연합한)는 나무 접붙임처럼 그리스도의 죽음·부활과 하나 되었음을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(καταργηθῇ) 목적절과 τοῦ+부정사(δουλεύειν) 목적구문이 함께 죄의 몸이 폐하여지는 목적을 이중으로 강조한다.
[^5]: δεδικαίωται는 완료시제로 죽음으로 인해 죄로부터 벗어난 지속적 상태를 나타낸다.
[^6]: οὐκ ἔτι ἀποθνήσκει, οὐκ ἔτι κυριεύει는 현재시제로 부활 이후 영원히 지속되는 상태를 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἐφάπαξ(단번에)는 그리스도의 죽으심이 반복되지 않는 단회적 사건임을 강조하는 핵심 용어다.
[^8]: λογίζεσθε(여기라)는 회계 용어로, 사실에 근거해 스스로를 그렇게 계산하고 인정하라는 명령이다.
[^9]: εἰς τό+부정사(ὑπακούειν) 구문으로 죄가 왕 노릇할 때 나타나는 결과(순종)를 목적처럼 나타낸다.
[^10]: ὑπὸ νόμον/ὑπὸ χάριν의 ὑπό는 '지배 아래'라는 뜻으로 율법과 은혜라는 두 통치 원리를 대조한다.
[^11]: ἀνθρώπινον λέγω는 '사람의 방식대로 말한다'는 관용구로, 종살이 비유를 통해 영적 진리를 쉽게 설명하려는 의도다.
[^12]: ὀψώνια(삯)는 본래 군인에게 지급하는 급료를 가리키는 단어로, 죄를 섬긴 대가가 사망임을 생생하게 나타낸다.
