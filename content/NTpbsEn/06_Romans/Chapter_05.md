<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore, having been justified by faith, we have peace toward God through our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">2</span> through whom also we have obtained <span class="added-text">(the)</span> access by faith into this grace in which we stand, and we boast in hope of the glory of God.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And not only <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, but we also boast in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribulations, knowing that tribulation produces endurance,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and endurance, proven <span class="alt-label">character</span><span class="alt-text">(=tested character)</span>, and proven character, hope;

<span class="v-num">5</span> and hope does not put to <span class="alt-label">shame</span><span class="alt-text">(=disappoint)</span>, because the love of God has been poured out in our hearts through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit given to us.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For while we were still weak, Christ, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> appointed time, died for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ungodly.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For scarcely will anyone die for a righteous <span class="added-text">(man)</span>; for perhaps for the good <span class="added-text">(man)</span> someone might even dare to die.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But God <span class="alt-label">demonstrates</span><span class="alt-text">(=demonstrated)</span> His own love toward us, in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Much more then, having now been justified by His blood, we shall be saved through Him from the wrath.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For if, being enemies, we were reconciled to God through the death of His Son, much more, having been reconciled, we shall be saved by His life;

<span class="v-num">11</span> And not only <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, but also <span class="alt-label">boasting</span><span class="alt-text">(=rejoicing)</span> in God through our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom we have now received the reconciliation.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore, just as through one man sin entered into the world, and through sin, death, and so death passed <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> all men, because all sinned;

<span class="v-num">13</span> For until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, sin was in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world; but sin is not imputed when there is no law;

<span class="v-num">14</span> But death reigned from Adam until Moses, even over those who had not sinned after the likeness of Adam's transgression, who is a type of the one to come.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But not as the trespass, so also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the gift. For if by the trespass of the one, the many died, much more the grace of God and the gift in grace, that of the one man Jesus Christ, abounded to the many.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And not as through one who sinned, <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the gift; for the judgment <span class="added-text">(was)</span> from one to condemnation, but the gift <span class="added-text">(is)</span> from many trespasses to justification.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For if by the trespass of the one, death reigned through the one, much more those who receive the abundance of grace and of the gift of righteousness will reign in life through the one, Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">18</span> So then, as through one trespass <span class="added-text">(the judgment came)</span> to all men to condemnation, so also through one righteous act <span class="added-text">(the gift came)</span> to all men to justification of life.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For as through the disobedience of the one man the many were <span class="alt-label">made</span><span class="alt-text">(=constituted)</span> sinners, so also through the obedience of the one the many will be <span class="alt-label">made</span><span class="alt-text">(=constituted)</span> righteous.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law came in besides, so that the trespass might increase. But where sin increased, grace abounded much more;[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> so that, as sin reigned in death, so also grace might reign through righteousness to eternal life, through Jesus Christ our Lord.[^3]

[^1]: συνίστησι는 현재시제(역사적 현재)이나 개역체 서사 관습에 따라 과거형 '드러내셨느니라'로 옮겼다.
[^2]: ἵνα πλεονάσῃ는 목적절로, 율법이 들어온 목적이 범죄를 드러내어 더하게 함에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα βασιλεύσῃ는 목적절로, 20절의 은혜의 넘침이 궁극적으로 영생에 이르는 통치를 목적함을 보여준다.
