# Romans 1

  <span class="legend-item"><span class="dot alt"></span>(=alt.word)</span>

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, a servant of Jesus Christ, a called apostle, set apart (=separated) for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gospel of God,

<span class="v-num">2</span> which he promised beforehand through his prophets in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> holy scriptures,

<span class="v-num">3</span> concerning his Son, who came into being from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> seed of David according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh,

<span class="v-num">4</span> who was declared <span class="added-text">(to be the)</span> Son of God in power, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of holiness, from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> resurrection of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, Jesus Christ our Lord,[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> through whom we received grace and apostleship for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> obedience of faith among all the nations, for his name's sake,

<span class="v-num">6</span> among whom you also are, called of Jesus Christ:

<span class="v-num">7</span> to all who are in Rome, beloved of God, called saints: grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">8</span> First indeed I thank my God through Jesus Christ for all of you, because your faith is proclaimed in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> whole world;

<span class="v-num">9</span> for God is my witness, whom I serve in my spirit in the gospel of his Son, how unceasingly I make mention of you,

<span class="v-num">10</span> always in my prayers, begging that now at last I might by the will of God be given a way to come to you;

<span class="v-num">11</span> for I long to see you, that I might impart some spiritual gift to you, for the strengthening of you,[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> that is, that I may be comforted together with you through each other's faith, both yours and mine.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, that I often planned to come to you, and was hindered until now, that I might have some fruit among you also, just as among the rest of the nations also.[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> I am a debtor both to Greeks and to barbarians, both to wise and to foolish;

<span class="v-num">15</span> so, as much as is in me, <span class="added-text">(I am)</span> eager to preach the gospel to you also who are in Rome.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God unto salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and <span class="added-text">(also)</span> to the Greek.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteousness of God is revealed in it from faith to faith, as it is written, 'But the righteous shall live by faith.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wrath of God is revealed from heaven upon all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who suppress the truth in unrighteousness.

<span class="v-num">19</span> because that which is known of God is manifest <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> them, for God manifested <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to them.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For his invisible things from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> creation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, both his eternal power and <span class="alt-label">Godhead</span><span class="alt-text">(=divinity)</span>, so that they are without excuse.[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> because, having known God, they did not glorify <span class="added-text">(him)</span> as God, nor gave thanks, but became vain in their reasonings, and their foolish heart was darkened.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Claiming to be wise, they became fools,

<span class="v-num">23</span> and exchanged the glory of the incorruptible God for the likeness of an image of corruptible man, and of birds, and of four-footed beasts, and of creeping things.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Therefore God also gave them up in the lusts of their hearts to uncleanness, to dishonor their bodies among themselves;

<span class="v-num">25</span> who exchanged the truth of God for the lie, and worshiped and served the creation rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For this cause God gave them up to (=vile) passions of dishonor; for even their females exchanged the natural use for that which is against nature;

<span class="v-num">27</span> and likewise also the males, having left the natural use of the female, burned in their lust toward one another, males with males working out shamefulness, and receiving in themselves the recompense which was fitting for their error.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And as they did not approve to have God in <span class="added-text">(their)</span> full knowledge, God gave them up to a debased mind, to do the things not fitting,

<span class="v-num">29</span> being filled with all unrighteousness, sexual immorality, wickedness, greed, malice; full of envy, murder, strife, deceit, malignity; whisperers,

<span class="v-num">30</span> backbiters, haters of God, insolent, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,

<span class="v-num">31</span> without understanding, covenant-breakers, without natural affection, <span class="alt-label">unforgiving</span><span class="alt-text">(=implacable)</span>, unmerciful:

<span class="v-num">32</span> who, having known the righteous decree of God, that those who practice such things are worthy of death, not only do the same, but also approve <span class="added-text">(of)</span> those who practice <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

[^1]: ὁρισθέντος(선포된)는 부활 사건을 통해 예수께서 하나님의 아들로 공적으로 확정되셨음을 나타내는 아오리스트 수동 분사이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(μεταδῶ)은 바울이 로마 성도를 방문하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(σχῶ)은 바울이 열매를 얻으려는 목적절이다.
[^4]: 하박국 2:4 인용으로, 이신칭의 교리의 핵심 근거 구절이다.
[^5]: εἰς τό+부정사(εἶναι)는 사람들이 변명할 수 없게 된 목적(또는 결과)을 나타낸다.
