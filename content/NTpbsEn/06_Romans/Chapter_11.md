<span class="v-num">1</span> I say then, Did God push away His people? May it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=Far from it)</span>! For I also am an Israelite, from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> seed of Abraham, of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Benjamin.

<span class="v-num">2</span> God did not push away His people, whom He foreknew. Or do you not know what the scripture says <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> Elijah? How he pleads with God against Israel, saying,

<span class="v-num">3</span> "Lord, they killed your prophets, and they dug down your altars; and I alone was left, and they seek my life."

<span class="v-num">4</span> But what does the divine answer say to him? "I left for myself seven thousand men, who did not bow <span class="added-text">(the)</span> knee to Baal."

<span class="v-num">5</span> So then in the present time also there has come to be a remnant according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> election of grace.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But if by grace, <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> no longer of works; otherwise grace no longer becomes grace. But if of works, it is no longer grace; otherwise the work is no longer work.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> What then? What Israel seeks for, this it did not obtain, but the election obtained <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and the rest were hardened,

<span class="v-num">8</span> as it is written, "God gave them a spirit of <span class="alt-label">numbness</span><span class="alt-text">(=stupor)</span>, eyes not to see, and ears not to hear," until this very day.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And David says, "Let their table become a snare and a trap, and a stumbling block and a retribution to them;"

<span class="v-num">10</span> "Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, and bend their back always."

<span class="v-num">11</span> I say then, Did they stumble that they might fall? May it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=Far from it)</span>! But by their trespass salvation <span class="added-text">(came)</span> to the Gentiles, to provoke them to jealousy.[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> But if their trespass <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> riches of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, and their failure <span class="added-text">(the)</span> riches of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, how much more their fullness?

<span class="v-num">13</span> For I speak to you, the Gentiles; inasmuch as I am indeed an apostle of Gentiles, I glorify my ministry,

<span class="v-num">14</span> if by any means I might provoke to jealousy my flesh, and might save some of them.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For if their casting away <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> reconciliation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, what <span class="added-text">(will)</span> the receiving <span class="added-text">(be)</span>, if not life from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead?

<span class="v-num">16</span> And if the firstfruit <span class="added-text">(is)</span> holy, so <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the lump; and if the root <span class="added-text">(is)</span> holy, so <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the branches.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, being a wild olive tree, were grafted in among them, and became a fellow-partaker of the root and of the fatness of the olive tree,

<span class="v-num">18</span> do not boast against the branches; but if you boast, you do not bear the root, but the root <span class="added-text">(bears)</span> you.

<span class="v-num">19</span> You will say then, "The branches were broken off, that I might be grafted in."[^3]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Well; they were broken off by unbelief, and you stand by faith. Do not be highminded, but fear;

<span class="v-num">21</span> for if God did not spare the natural branches, <span class="added-text">(take heed)</span> lest He also not spare you.

<span class="v-num">22</span> <span class="alt-label">See</span><span class="alt-text">(=Behold)</span> then <span class="added-text">(the)</span> kindness and severity of God; upon those who fell, severity; but upon you, kindness, if you continue in the kindness; otherwise you also will be cut off.[^4]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And they also, if they do not continue in unbelief, will be grafted in; for God is able to graft them in again.[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> For if you were cut out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wild olive tree by nature, and contrary to nature were grafted into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> good olive tree, how much more will these, who are the natural <span class="added-text">(branches)</span>, be grafted into their own olive tree?

<span class="v-num">25</span> For I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, of this mystery, lest you be wise in yourselves, that hardening in part has happened to Israel, until the fullness of the Gentiles comes in;[^6]

<span class="v-num">26</span> And so all Israel will be saved, as it is written, "The Deliverer will come out of Zion, and will turn away ungodliness from Jacob;"

<span class="v-num">27</span> "And this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> my covenant <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> them, when I take away their sins."

<span class="v-num">28</span> Concerning the gospel, <span class="added-text">(they are)</span> enemies for your sake; but concerning the election, <span class="added-text">(they are)</span> beloved for the fathers' sake.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For the gifts and the calling of God <span class="added-text">(are)</span> without repentance.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For as you also once did not obey God, but now have obtained mercy through their disobedience,

<span class="v-num">31</span> so these also have now not obeyed, that <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> the mercy shown to you they also may obtain mercy.[^7]

<span class="v-num">32</span> For God has shut up all together in disobedience, that He might have mercy on all.[^8]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Oh, the depth of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable <span class="added-text">(are)</span> His judgments, and untraceable His ways!

<span class="v-num">34</span> For who has known <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mind of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord? Or who became His counselor?

<span class="v-num">35</span> Or who has first given to Him, and it will be repaid to him?

<span class="v-num">36</span> For from Him and through Him and to Him <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all things; to Him <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory forever. Amen.

[^1]: 후반부("만일 행위로 말미암으면...")는 비잔틴/TR 사본 전통에는 있으나 대다수 비평본(NA/UBS)에는 없는 구절로, 은혜와 행위의 대조를 강조하는 TR 특유의 확장이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(πέσωσι)은 목적절로, '그들이 완전히 넘어지려고 실족했느냐'는 의도를 묻는 구문이다. 바울은 이 목적을 강하게 부정(μὴ γένοιτο)하며, 이스라엘의 실족이 완전한 파멸이 아닌 이방인 구원의 통로임을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐγκεντρισθῶ)은 목적절로, 가지들이 꺾인 목적/의도가 '내가 접붙임을 받는 것'임을 나타낸다. 하나님의 섭리적 목적을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(ἐπιμείνῃς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 조건을 나타낸다: '네가 만일 인자하심에 계속 머무른다면'이라는 뜻으로, 하나님의 인자하심 안에 거함이 지속적 조건임을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἐὰν μὴ ἐπιμείνωσι는 3인칭 복수 부정과거 가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다(제3형 조건문). 이스라엘이 불신앙에 계속 머물지 않는다면 접붙임을 받게 된다는 열려있는 가능성을 제시하며, 회복의 조건이 그들의 불신앙 지속 여부에 달려있음을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἦτε)은 목적절로, '너희가 스스로 지혜 있다고 여기지 않게 하려고'라는 바울의 의도를 나타낸다. 이 신비를 알리는 목적이 청중의 교만을 막기 위함임을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐλεηθῶσι)은 목적절로, 이스라엘의 불순종이 결국 긍휼을 얻게 하시려는 하나님의 목적/의도임을 보여준다. 인간의 실패조차 하나님의 구원 계획 안에 있음을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐλεήσῃ)은 목적절로, 하나님이 모든 사람을 순종치 않음에 가두신 목적이 바로 모든 사람에게 긍휼을 베푸시려는 것임을 나타낸다. 심판이 아니라 구원을 향한 하나님의 의도된 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
