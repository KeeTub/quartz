# Romans 12

<span class="v-num">1</span> I exhort you therefore, brothers, through the mercies of God, to present your bodies <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a living sacrifice, holy, well-pleasing to God, <span class="added-text">(which is)</span> your <span class="alt-label">reasonable</span><span class="alt-text">(=spiritual)</span> service.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, so that you <span class="added-text">(may)</span> discern what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the good and well-pleasing and perfect will of God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> For I say, through the grace given to me, to everyone being among you, not to think <span class="added-text">(of yourself)</span> more highly than one ought to think, but to think so as to think soberly, each as God has apportioned a measure of faith.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For just as we have many members in one body, but all the members do not have the same function,

<span class="v-num">5</span> so we, the many, are one body in Christ, and individually members of one another.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But having gifts differing according to the grace given to us; whether prophecy, according to the proportion of faith;

<span class="v-num">7</span> or <span class="alt-label">ministry</span><span class="alt-text">(=service)</span>, in service; or the one teaching, in teaching;

<span class="v-num">8</span> or the one exhorting, in exhortation; the one <span class="alt-label">giving</span><span class="alt-text">(=sharing)</span>, in <span class="alt-label">simplicity</span><span class="alt-text">(=sincerity)</span>; the one leading, with diligence; the one showing mercy, with cheerfulness.

<span class="v-num">9</span> <span class="added-text">(Let)</span> love <span class="added-text">(be)</span> without hypocrisy. Abhorring the evil, cleaving to the good;

<span class="v-num">10</span> In brotherly love, <span class="added-text">(be)</span> affectionate to one another; in honor, <span class="alt-label">preferring</span><span class="alt-text">(=going before)</span> one another;

<span class="v-num">11</span> Not slothful in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the <span class="alt-label">time</span><span class="alt-text">(=opportunity)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Rejoicing in hope, enduring in tribulation, persevering in prayer;

<span class="v-num">13</span> Sharing in the needs of the saints, pursuing hospitality.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Bless those who persecute you; bless, and do not curse.

<span class="v-num">15</span> <span class="added-text">(Rejoice)</span> with the ones rejoicing, and weep with the ones weeping.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Being of the same mind toward one another; not minding high things, but associating with the lowly. Do not be wise in your own <span class="alt-label">estimation</span><span class="alt-text">(=in yourselves)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Repaying no one evil for evil. Taking thought <span class="added-text">(for)</span> good things before all men;

<span class="v-num">18</span> If possible, as much as <span class="added-text">(depends)</span> on you, being at peace with all men.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Not avenging yourselves, beloved, but give place to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wrath. For it is written, "Vengeance <span class="added-text">(is)</span> mine, I will repay, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord."[^3]

<span class="v-num">20</span> If therefore your enemy hungers, feed him; if he thirsts, give him drink; for in doing this, you will heap coals of fire upon his head.[^4]

<span class="v-num">21</span> Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good.

[^1]: εἰς τὸ δοκιμάζειν은 εἰς τό+부정사로 목적을 나타내는 구문이다.
[^2]: TR 사본 일부는 '주를(Κυρίῳ)' 대신 '때를(καιρῷ)' 섬기라고 읽어 '기회를 붙잡으라'는 뜻으로 이해된다.
[^3]: 신 32:35의 인용으로, 원수 갚는 권한이 오직 하나님께 있음을 근거로 개인적 보복을 금한다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(πεινᾷ, διψᾷ)은 '만일 ~하거든'이라는 미래에 실현될 수 있는 조건을 나타낸다. 원수가 실제로 주리거나 목마를 가능성을 전제하고 그에 대한 실천적 대응(먹이고 마시게 함)을 명령형으로 제시하는 구조다.
