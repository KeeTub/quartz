<span class="v-num">1</span> There is therefore now no condemnation to the ones in Christ Jesus, <span class="added-text">(who are)</span> not walking according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, but according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus has freed me from the law of sin and of death.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For the impossible <span class="added-text">(thing)</span> of the law, in which it was weak through the flesh, God, having sent his own Son in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> likeness of sinful flesh and concerning <span class="alt-label">sin</span><span class="alt-text">(=as a sin offering)</span>, condemned sin in the flesh,

<span class="v-num">4</span> so that the righteous requirement of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh but according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> For the ones being according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh mind the things of the flesh, but the ones <span class="added-text">(being)</span> according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, the things of the Spirit.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For the <span class="alt-label">mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=mindset)</span> of the flesh <span class="added-text">(is)</span> death, but the <span class="alt-label">mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=mindset)</span> of the Spirit <span class="added-text">(is)</span> life and peace.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Because the <span class="alt-label">mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=mindset)</span> of the flesh <span class="added-text">(is)</span> enmity toward God; for it does not subject itself to the law of God, for neither can it;

<span class="v-num">8</span> and the ones being in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh are not able to please God.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But you are not in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh but in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, if indeed <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of God dwells in you. But if anyone does not have <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of Christ, this one is not his.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But if Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in you, the body <span class="added-text">(is)</span> indeed dead because of sin, but the Spirit <span class="added-text">(is)</span> life because of righteousness.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But if the Spirit of the one having raised Jesus from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead dwells in you, the one having raised Christ from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead will also give life to your mortal bodies, on account <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> his Spirit dwelling in you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> So then, brothers, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh;

<span class="v-num">13</span> for if you live according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, you are about to die; but if by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit you put to death the deeds of the body, you will live.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For as many as are led by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of God, these are sons of God.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For you did not receive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit of slavery again to fear, but you received <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of adoption, in whom we cry, "Abba, Father."

<span class="v-num">16</span> The Spirit himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And if children, also heirs; heirs indeed of God, and joint-heirs <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> Christ; if indeed we suffer with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, so that we may also be glorified with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.[^4]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For I consider that the sufferings of the present time <span class="added-text">(are)</span> not worthy <span class="added-text">(to be compared)</span> with the glory about to be revealed <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> us.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For the earnest expectation of the creation eagerly awaits the revealing of the sons of God.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For the creation was subjected to futility, not willingly, but because of the one having subjected <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, in hope,

<span class="v-num">21</span> that the creation itself also will be freed from the slavery of corruption into the freedom of the glory of the children of God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For we know that all the creation groans together and travails together until now;

<span class="v-num">23</span> and not only <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, but also we ourselves, having the firstfruit of the Spirit, we ourselves also groan within ourselves, eagerly awaiting adoption, the redemption of our body.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> hope we were saved. But hope <span class="added-text">(that is)</span> seen is not hope; for what one sees, why does he also hope for it?

<span class="v-num">25</span> But if we hope for what we do not see, we eagerly await <span class="added-text">(it)</span> through patience.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Likewise also the Spirit helps our weaknesses; for we do not know what we should pray for as we ought, but the Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings unutterable;

<span class="v-num">27</span> and the one searching the hearts knows what the <span class="alt-label">mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=mindset)</span> of the Spirit <span class="added-text">(is)</span>, because he intercedes for the saints according to God.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And we know that to the ones loving God all things work together for good, to the ones being called according to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> purpose.

<span class="v-num">29</span> because whom he foreknew, he also predestined <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> conformed to the image of his Son, so that he might be <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstborn among many brothers;[^5]

<span class="v-num">30</span> and whom he predestined, these he also called; and whom he called, these he also justified; and whom he justified, these he also glorified.

<span class="v-num">31</span> What then shall we say to these things? If God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> for us, who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> against us?

<span class="v-num">32</span> He who indeed did not spare his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how will he not also with him freely give us all things?

<span class="v-num">33</span> Who will bring a charge against God's elect? <span class="added-text">(It is)</span> God who justifies;

<span class="v-num">34</span> who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one condemning? Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one having died, and rather also having been raised, who is also at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of God, who also intercedes for us.

<span class="v-num">35</span> Who will separate us from the love of Christ? Tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or danger, or sword?

<span class="v-num">36</span> As it is written, "For your sake we are killed all the day long; we were reckoned as sheep for slaughter."[^6]

<span class="v-num">37</span> But in all these things we are more than conquerors through the one having loved us.

<span class="v-num">38</span> For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present nor things to come,

<span class="v-num">39</span> nor height nor depth, nor any other creation will be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

[^1]: '육신을 따라... 영을 따라'(μὴ κατὰ σάρκα...ἀλλὰ κατὰ πνεῦμα) 구절은 TR(비잔틴 전통)에는 있으나 대다수 현대 비평본에는 1절에서 빠져 있다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῇ) 목적절로, 율법의 요구가 성령을 따르는 자 안에서 성취되는 것이 하나님의 의도임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: TR은 '디아 토 에노이쿤 아우투 프뉴마'(목적격, ~때문에)로 읽으나 다수 비평본은 속격 '디아 투 에노이쿤토스...프뉴마토스'(~을 통하여)로 읽어 뉘앙스에 차이가 있다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(συνδοξασθῶμεν) 목적절로, 함께 고난받음이 함께 영광 받게 하려는 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^5]: εἰς τό+부정사(εἶναι) 목적구문으로, 아들이 많은 형제 중 맏아들 되게 하려는 하나님의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: 시편 44:22(70인역 43:22)의 인용으로, 성도가 겪는 환난이 옛적부터 예견된 하나님 백성의 정체성임을 뒷받침한다.
