<span class="v-num">1</span> Now I recommend to you Phoebe our sister, who is a servant of the church that is in Cenchrea,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> that you may receive her in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord worthily of the saints, and may stand <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=assist)</span> her in whatever matter she may need <span class="added-text">(of)</span> you, for she herself also became a <span class="alt-label">helper</span><span class="alt-text">(=patron)</span> of many, and of me myself.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Greet Priscilla and Aquila, my fellow workers in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">4</span> who for my life laid down their own neck, to whom not I alone give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and <span class="added-text">(greet)</span> the church that is in their house. Greet Epaenetus my beloved, who is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first fruit of Achaia <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> Christ.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Greet Mary, who labored much <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> us.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners, who are of note among the apostles, who also have been in Christ before me.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Greet Amplias my beloved in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Greet Urbanus our fellow worker in Christ, and Stachys my beloved.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Greet Apelles, the approved in Christ. Greet those of <span class="added-text">(the household of)</span> Aristobulus.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Greet Herodion my kinsman. Greet those of <span class="added-text">(the household of)</span> Narcissus, who are in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Greet Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labor in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord. Greet Persis the beloved, who labored much in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Greet Rufus, the chosen in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, and his mother and mine.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Greet Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes, and the brothers with them.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Greet Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympas, and all the saints with them.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Greet one another with a holy kiss. The churches of Christ greet you.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Now I urge you, brothers, to watch <span class="added-text">(out for)</span> those <span class="alt-label">making</span><span class="alt-text">(=causing)</span> the divisions and the stumbling blocks, contrary to the teaching which you learned; and turn away from them.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For such <span class="added-text">(people)</span> do not serve our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and through smooth speech and flattering <span class="alt-label">words</span><span class="alt-text">(=blessing)</span> they deceive the hearts of the guileless.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For your obedience reached to all; I rejoice therefore <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> you; but I want you to be wise as to the good, and innocent as to the evil.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And the God of peace will crush Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Timothy my fellow worker greets you, and Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen.

<span class="v-num">22</span> I Tertius, who wrote this epistle, greet you in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Gaius, my host and <span class="added-text">(host)</span> of the whole church, greets you. Erastus the steward of the city greets you, and <span class="added-text">(our)</span> brother Quartus.

<span class="v-num">24</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen.[^3]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Now to him who is able to establish you according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> revelation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mystery that has been kept secret in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> times <span class="alt-label">eternal</span><span class="alt-text">(=through long ages)</span>,

<span class="v-num">26</span> but now has been made manifest, and through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> prophetic scriptures, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> commandment of the eternal God, made known <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> all the nations for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> obedience of faith,

<span class="v-num">27</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> only wise God, through Jesus Christ, to whom <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory into the ages. Amen. <span class="added-text">(Written)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Romans <span class="added-text">(it)</span> was written from Corinth through Phoebe the servant of the church that is in Cenchrea.
