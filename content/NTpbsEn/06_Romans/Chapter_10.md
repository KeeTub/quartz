# Romans 10

<span class="v-num">1</span> Brothers, my heart's <span class="alt-label">desire</span><span class="alt-text">(=good pleasure)</span> indeed, and <span class="added-text">(my)</span> supplication to God on behalf of Israel, is for salvation.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For I testify to them that they have a zeal of God, but not according to full knowledge.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For being ignorant of the righteousness of God, and seeking to establish their own righteousness, they did not submit to the righteousness of God.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For Christ is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law for righteousness to everyone who believes.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> For Moses writes concerning the righteousness which is of the law, 'The man who does these things shall live by them.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But the righteousness which is of faith speaks thus, "Do not say in your heart, 'Who will ascend into heaven?'" that is, to bring Christ down;[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> or, "Who will descend into the abyss?" that is, to bring Christ up from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But what does it say? "The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart"; that is, the word of faith which we preach;

<span class="v-num">9</span> that if you confess with your mouth <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus, and believe in your heart that God raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, you will be saved.[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heart one believes unto righteousness, and with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth confession is made unto salvation.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For the scripture says, 'Everyone who believes on him shall not be put to shame.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For there is no distinction between Jew and Greek: for the same <span class="added-text">(Lord is)</span> Lord of all, being rich toward all who call upon him.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For 'everyone who calls upon the name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord shall be saved.'[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> How then shall they call upon <span class="added-text">(him)</span> in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe <span class="added-text">(him)</span> of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without <span class="added-text">(one)</span> preaching?

<span class="v-num">15</span> And how shall they preach unless they are sent? As it is written, 'How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the gospel of peace, of those who preach the gospel of good things!'[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But not all obeyed the gospel; for Isaiah says, 'Lord, who has believed our <span class="alt-label">report</span><span class="alt-text">(=our message)</span>?'[^8]

<span class="v-num">17</span> So then faith <span class="added-text">(comes)</span> by hearing, and hearing by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of God.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But I say, Have they not heard? Indeed, 'their sound went out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world.'[^9]

<span class="v-num">19</span> But I say, Did Israel not know? First Moses says, 'I will provoke you to jealousy by <span class="added-text">(those who are)</span> not a nation, by a nation without understanding I will anger you.'[^10]

<span class="v-num">20</span> But Isaiah is very bold and says, 'I was found by those not seeking me, I became manifest to those not asking for me.'[^11]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But to Israel he says, 'All day long I stretched out my hands to a people disobeying and contradicting.'[^12]

[^1]: τέλος는 단순히 '종결'만이 아니라 율법이 지향하던 '목표·완성'을 뜻해, 그리스도께서 율법의 성취이자 종착점이 되심을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 레위기 18:5 인용으로, 율법의 의는 행함을 요구함을 보여준다.
[^3]: 신명기 30:12-13을 바울이 그리스도 중심으로 재해석하여 적용한 것이다.
[^4]: ἐὰν+가정법으로 이루어진 3급 조건문으로, 시인과 믿음이라는 조건이 충족될 때 구원이라는 결과가 따름을 나타낸다.
[^5]: 이사야 28:16 인용으로, 믿음의 보편적 유효성을 강조한다.
[^6]: 요엘 2:32 인용으로, 앞 절의 유대인·헬라인 구별 없음을 뒷받침한다.
[^7]: ἐὰν μὴ ἀποσταλῶσι는 아오리스트 수동 가정법으로, '보내심을 받지 않는다면'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 조건을 나타낸다. 보냄(파송)이 전파의 필수 전제 조건임을 강조하는 3류 조건문이다.
[^8]: 이사야 53:1 인용으로, 복음에 대한 불신앙을 예언한 것으로 본다.
[^9]: 시편 19:4 인용으로, 복음의 소리가 이미 온 세상에 퍼졌음을 논증한다.
[^10]: 신명기 32:21 인용으로, 이방인을 통해 이스라엘이 시기하게 될 것을 예고한다.
[^11]: 이사야 65:1 인용으로, 이방인이 복음을 받아들일 것을 예언한 것이다.
[^12]: 이사야 65:2 인용으로, 이스라엘의 완악함과 하나님의 오래 참으심을 대조한다.
