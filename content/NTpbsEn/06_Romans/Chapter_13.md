# Romans 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> Let every soul be subject to the governing authorities. For there is no authority except from God, and the authorities that exist have been appointed by God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> So that the one opposing the authority has resisted the ordinance of God, and the ones having resisted will receive judgment to themselves.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For rulers are not a fear to the good works, but to the evil. Do you wish not to fear the authority? Do the good, and you will have praise from it,

<span class="v-num">4</span> For he is God's servant to you for good. But if you do the evil, be afraid, for he does not bear the sword in vain; for he is God's servant, an avenger for wrath to the one practicing evil.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Therefore it is necessary to be subject, not only because of the wrath, but also because of the conscience.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For because of this you pay taxes also; for they are God's ministers, attending continually to this very thing.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Therefore render to all their dues: to whom <span class="added-text">(is due)</span> tax, tax; to whom <span class="added-text">(is due)</span> custom, custom; to whom <span class="added-text">(is due)</span> fear, fear; to whom <span class="added-text">(is due)</span> honor, honor.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Owe nothing to anyone, except to love one another; for the one loving the other has fulfilled the law.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For <span class="added-text">(the commandment)</span>, 'You shall not commit adultery, you shall not murder, you shall not steal, you shall not bear false witness, you shall not covet,' and if there is any other commandment, it is summed up in this word, in, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Love does not work evil to the neighbor; therefore love is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fulfillment of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And this, knowing the time, that <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> already <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hour for us to be awakened out of sleep; for now our salvation <span class="added-text">(is)</span> nearer than when we believed.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> The night has advanced, and the day has drawn near; let us therefore put off the works of darkness, and let us put on the <span class="alt-label">armor</span><span class="alt-text">(=weapons)</span> of light.

<span class="v-num">13</span> As in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day, let us walk decently, not in revelings and drunkenness, not in sexual <span class="alt-label">immorality</span><span class="alt-text">(=beds)</span> and licentiousness, not in strife and envy,

<span class="v-num">14</span> But put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, to <span class="added-text">(fulfill its)</span> lusts.

[^1]: ἐξουσία(권세)는 개별 통치자가 아니라 하나님이 세우신 통치 질서 자체를 가리키는 문맥적 의미로 읽어야 한다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(ποιῇς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '혹시라도 악을 행한다면'이라는 뉘앙스이다. 이는 청중의 실제 행위 여부에 따라 두려움의 결과가 좌우됨을 경고하는 실제적 조건문이다.
[^3]: πεπλήρωκε는 완료시제로, 사랑함이 율법을 이미 온전히 이루어 놓은 지속적 결과임을 강조한다.
[^4]: 출애굽기 20장과 레위기 19:18의 인용으로, 십계명이 사랑의 계명 안에 요약됨을 보여준다.
[^5]: ἐγερθῆναι는 부정과거 부정사로, 잠에서 깨어나는 것을 단회적·긴급한 사건으로 묘사한다.
