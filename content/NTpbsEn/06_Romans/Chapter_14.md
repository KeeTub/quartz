<span class="v-num">1</span> Receive the one being weak in faith, not to <span class="added-text">(disputes of)</span> <span class="alt-label">reasonings</span><span class="alt-text">(=doubtful disputations)</span>.

<span class="v-num">2</span> One <span class="added-text">(person)</span> indeed believes to eat all things, but the one being weak eats vegetables.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Let the one eating not despise the one not eating, and let the one not eating not judge the one eating, for God received him.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Who are you, the one judging another's servant? To his own master he stands or falls. But he will stand, for God is able to make him stand.

<span class="v-num">5</span> One <span class="added-text">(person)</span> indeed judges <span class="added-text">(one)</span> day <span class="added-text">(above)</span> <span class="added-text">(another)</span> day, but another judges every day <span class="added-text">(alike)</span>. Let each be fully convinced in his own mind.

<span class="v-num">6</span> The one regarding the day, regards <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord. And the one not regarding the day, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord he does not regard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>. The one eating, eats to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, for he gives thanks to God; and the one not eating, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord he does not eat, and gives thanks to God.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For none of us lives to himself, and none dies to himself.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For if we live, we live to the Lord; and if we die, we die to the Lord. Therefore whether we live or whether we die, we are the Lord's.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For to this <span class="added-text">(end)</span> Christ both died and rose and lived <span class="added-text">(again)</span>, that he might be Lord both of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead and of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But you, why do you judge your brother? Or you also, why do you despise your brother? For we shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For it is written, 'As I live, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall confess to God.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> So then each of us will give <span class="alt-label">account</span><span class="alt-text">(=word)</span> concerning himself to God.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore let us no longer judge one another, but rather judge this, not to put a stumbling block or a snare before the brother.[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> I know and am persuaded in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus that nothing <span class="added-text">(is)</span> unclean of itself; except to the one reckoning anything to be unclean, to that one <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> unclean.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But if because of food your brother is grieved, you are no longer walking according to love. Do not by your food destroy that one, for whom Christ died.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Therefore let not your good <span class="added-text">(thing)</span> be <span class="alt-label">blasphemed</span><span class="alt-text">(=spoken evil of)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For the kingdom of God is not food and drink, but righteousness and peace and joy in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For the one serving Christ in these things is well-pleasing to God, and approved by men.

<span class="v-num">19</span> So then let us pursue the things of peace, and the things of edification <span class="added-text">(which are)</span> toward one another.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Do not for the sake of food destroy the work of God. All things indeed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> clean, but <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> evil to the man who eats <span class="alt-label">through</span><span class="alt-text">(=by causing)</span> stumbling.

<span class="v-num">21</span> It is good not to eat meat, nor to drink wine, nor <span class="added-text">(to do anything)</span> in which your brother stumbles or is offended or is weak.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Do you have faith? Have <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to yourself before God. Blessed is the one not judging himself in what he <span class="alt-label">approves</span><span class="alt-text">(=has determined right)</span>.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But the one who <span class="alt-label">doubts</span><span class="alt-text">(=is divided in judgment)</span>, if he eats, has been condemned, because <span class="added-text">(he did)</span> not <span class="added-text">(act)</span> from faith; and whatever is not from faith is sin.[^5]

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법이 반복되어 '살든지 죽든지'라는 양보적 조건절을 이룬다. 삶과 죽음 어느 경우든 상관없이 그 결과(주께 속함)가 성립함을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 그리스도의 죽음과 부활의 목적(주 되심)을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 이사야 45:23 인용으로, 만물이 하나님께 굴복하고 고백할 것을 확증한다.
[^4]: τὸ μὴ τιθέναι는 목적 부정사구문으로 '걸림돌을 놓지 않는 것'을 판단의 목적으로 제시한다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 상황을 가정하는 3급 조건문이다.
