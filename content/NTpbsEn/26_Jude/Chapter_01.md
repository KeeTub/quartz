<span class="v-num">1</span> Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> brother of James, to the <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> having been sanctified in God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and kept <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> Jesus Christ, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> called:[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Mercy to you, and peace, and love, be multiplied.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Beloved, making all diligence to write to you concerning the common salvation, I had <span class="added-text">(a)</span> necessity to write to you, exhorting <span class="added-text">(you)</span> to contend earnestly for the faith once delivered to the saints.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> For certain men crept in <span class="alt-label">unnoticed</span><span class="alt-text">(=stealthily)</span>, the ones having been written <span class="alt-label">before</span><span class="alt-text">(=beforehand)</span> of old unto this judgment, ungodly <span class="added-text">(ones)</span>, turning the grace of our God into <span class="alt-label">licentiousness</span><span class="alt-text">(=sensuality)</span>, and denying the only Master, God, and our Lord Jesus Christ.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But I want to remind you, you knowing this once <span class="added-text">(for all)</span>, that the Lord, having saved <span class="added-text">(a/the)</span> people out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Egypt, <span class="alt-label">afterward</span><span class="alt-text">(=the second time)</span> destroyed the ones not believing.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And <span class="added-text">(the)</span> angels, the ones not having kept their own first <span class="alt-label">estate</span><span class="alt-text">(=position)</span>, but having left their own dwelling place, He has kept in eternal chains under darkness unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judgment of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> great day.

<span class="v-num">7</span> As Sodom and Gomorrah, and the cities around them, having in like manner to these fornicated, and having gone after <span class="alt-label">other</span><span class="alt-text">(=strange)</span> flesh, are set forth <span class="added-text">(as)</span> an example, undergoing <span class="added-text">(the)</span> punishment of eternal fire.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Likewise nevertheless these also, dreaming, defile <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh indeed, and <span class="alt-label">reject</span><span class="alt-text">(=set aside)</span> <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=lordship)</span>, and speak evil <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=blaspheme)</span> <span class="alt-label">glories</span><span class="alt-text">(=dignities)</span>.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But Michael the archangel, when contending with the devil he disputed about <span class="added-text">(the)</span> body of Moses, did not dare to bring a <span class="alt-label">railing</span><span class="alt-text">(=blasphemous)</span> judgment <span class="added-text">(against him)</span>, but said, "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> Lord rebuke you."[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But these <span class="added-text">(men)</span> speak evil of as many things as they do not know; but as many things as they understand naturally, like the irrational <span class="alt-label">animals</span><span class="alt-text">(=creatures)</span>, in these they are <span class="alt-label">corrupted</span><span class="alt-text">(=destroyed)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Woe to them! For they have gone <span class="added-text">(in)</span> the way of Cain, and have <span class="alt-label">rushed</span><span class="alt-text">(=poured out)</span> for <span class="alt-label">reward</span><span class="alt-text">(=hire)</span> in the error of Balaam, and perished in the <span class="alt-label">rebellion</span><span class="alt-text">(=gainsaying)</span> of Korah.

<span class="v-num">12</span> These are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hidden <span class="alt-label">reefs</span><span class="alt-text">(=spots)</span> in your love <span class="alt-label">feasts</span><span class="alt-text">(=agape-feasts)</span>, feasting together <span class="added-text">(with you)</span> without fear, feeding themselves; clouds without water, carried about by winds; trees withered in late <span class="alt-label">autumn</span><span class="alt-text">(=autumn trees)</span>, without fruit, twice dead, plucked up by the roots.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Wild waves of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sea, foaming out their own shames; wandering stars, for whom the <span class="alt-label">blackness</span><span class="alt-text">(=gloom)</span> of darkness has been reserved for <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=into the age)</span>.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Enoch also, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, "Behold, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord came with <span class="added-text">(His)</span> holy <span class="alt-label">myriads</span><span class="alt-text">(=ten thousands)</span>,[^5]

<span class="v-num">15</span> to execute judgment against all, and to convict all the ungodly among them concerning all their ungodly <span class="alt-label">deeds</span><span class="alt-text">(=works)</span> which they have committed in an ungodly way, and concerning all the harsh things which ungodly sinners have spoken against Him."

<span class="v-num">16</span> These are murmurers, complainers, walking according to their own lusts; and their mouth speaks great swelling <span class="alt-label">words</span><span class="alt-text">(=arrogant things)</span>, admiring <span class="alt-label">persons</span><span class="alt-text">(=flattering people)</span> for the sake of <span class="alt-label">advantage</span><span class="alt-text">(=profit)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But you, beloved, remember the words spoken before by the apostles of our Lord Jesus Christ;

<span class="v-num">18</span> that they told you that in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> last time there will be mockers, walking according to their own lusts of ungodliness.

<span class="v-num">19</span> These are the ones causing <span class="alt-label">divisions</span><span class="alt-text">(=separating themselves)</span>, <span class="alt-label">sensual</span><span class="alt-text">(=natural, soulish)</span>, not having <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But you, beloved, building up yourselves on your most holy faith, praying in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit,

<span class="v-num">21</span> keep yourselves in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> love of God, waiting for the mercy of our Lord Jesus Christ unto eternal life.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And on some have mercy, making a <span class="alt-label">distinction</span><span class="alt-text">(=who are doubting)</span>,

<span class="v-num">23</span> but others save with fear, snatching <span class="added-text">(them)</span> out of the fire, hating even the <span class="alt-label">garment</span><span class="alt-text">(=tunic)</span> <span class="alt-label">stained</span><span class="alt-text">(=defiled)</span> by the flesh.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Now to Him being able to keep <span class="alt-label">them</span><span class="alt-text">(=you)</span> without stumbling, and to present <span class="added-text">(them)</span> faultless before the presence of His glory in exceeding <span class="alt-label">joy</span><span class="alt-text">(=gladness)</span>,

<span class="v-num">25</span> to the only wise God our Savior, <span class="added-text">(be)</span> glory and majesty, <span class="alt-label">dominion</span><span class="alt-text">(=power)</span> and <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=power)</span>, both now and unto all the ages. Amen.

[^1]: 완료 수동분사 ἡγιασμένοις·τετηρημένοις는 과거에 이루어져 지금까지 지속되는 상태를 나타내니, 이미 거룩하게 되고 보존된 신자의 신분을 강조한다.
[^2]: τῇ...πίστει에 관사가 붙어 막연한 '믿음 일반'이 아니라 사도들에게서 성도들에게 전해진 특정한 신앙의 내용(교리체계)을 가리킨다.
[^3]: τὸν μόνον δεσπότην Θεὸν καὶ Κύριον ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦν Χριστόν은 관사가 한 번만 쓰여 두 칭호가 한 대상을 가리킬 가능성도 있으나, 전통적으로는 하나님과 그리스도 두 인격을 지칭하는 것으로 번역된다.
[^4]: 모세의 시신을 둘러싼 미가엘과 마귀의 다툼은 정경 밖 문헌(모세의 승천기 등)의 전승을 배경으로 한다.
[^5]: 에녹서(1 Enoch 1:9)의 인용으로, 정경 밖 문헌을 권위 있는 예언으로 인용한 사례이다.
