# 2 Corinthians 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> This <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> third <span class="added-text">(time)</span> I am coming to you. On <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth of two witnesses and three every <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=matter)</span> shall be <span class="alt-label">established</span><span class="alt-text">(=stand)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> I have said before and I say beforehand, as <span class="added-text">(when I was)</span> present the second <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, and being absent now I write to the ones having sinned before, and to all the rest, that if I come again, I will not spare.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Since you seek a proof of the Christ speaking in me, who toward you is not weak, but is powerful <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> For even if He was crucified from weakness, yet He lives from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God; for we also are weak in Him, but we shall live together with Him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God toward you.[^4]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Test yourselves whether you are in the faith, prove yourselves. Or do you not recognize yourselves, that Jesus Christ is in you? unless you are <span class="added-text">(in some way)</span> disqualified.[^5]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But I hope that you will know that we are not disqualified.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now I pray to God that you do no evil <span class="added-text">(thing)</span>, not that we may appear approved, but that you may do the good, even though we may be as disqualified.[^6]

<span class="v-num">8</span> For we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For we rejoice when we are weak, but you are strong; and this we also pray for, your <span class="alt-label">perfecting</span><span class="alt-text">(=being made complete)</span>.[^7]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Because of this I write these things being absent, that being present I may not use sharpness, according to the authority which the Lord gave me for building up, and not for tearing down.[^8]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Finally, brothers, rejoice, be made complete, be comforted, think the same thing, be at peace; and the God of love and peace will be with you.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Greet one another with a holy kiss.

<span class="v-num">13</span> All the saints greet you. The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> second <span class="added-text">(epistle)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Corinthians was written from Philippi of Macedonia, through Titus and Luke.

[^1]: 신 19:15을 인용하여 재판의 원칙을 교회 권징에 적용한다.
[^2]: ἐὰν ἔλθω(가정법)는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '내가 다시 갈 경우에는'이라는 뜻이다. 바울이 재방문을 아직 확정하지 않았지만 그 가능성을 전제로 경고하는 것이다.
[^3]: δοκιμή은 재판정에서의 '증거·검증'을 뜻하며, 고린도인들이 바울의 사도권을 시험하려 함을 가리킨다.
[^4]: ζῇ(현재)는 그리스도의 지속적 부활 생명을, ζήσομεθα(미래)는 성도의 장차 나타날 능력을 대조한다.
[^5]: πειράζετε(시험하다)와 δοκιμάζετε(분별하다)는 동의어 반복으로 자기 점검의 철저함을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα φανῶμεν와 ἵνα ποιῆτε는 목적절로, 바울 자신의 인정보다 고린도인의 선행을 우선하는 목적을 드러낸다.
[^7]: κατάρτισις는 뼈를 맞추듯 온전히 회복시킨다는 뜻으로, 교회의 성숙을 향한 목회적 소원을 담는다.
[^8]: ἵνα χρήσωμαι는 목적절이며, εἰς οἰκοδομὴν/εἰς καθαίρεσιν의 대조는 사도권의 본래 목적이 세움에 있음을 강조한다.
