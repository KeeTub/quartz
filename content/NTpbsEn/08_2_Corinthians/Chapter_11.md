# 2 Corinthians 11

<span class="v-num">1</span> Would that you endured with me a little <span class="added-text">(in my)</span> foolishness; but indeed do endure me.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For I am jealous over you with God's jealousy; for I betrothed you to one husband, to present <span class="added-text">(you as)</span> a pure virgin to Christ.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But I fear lest, as the serpent deceived Eve by his craftiness, so your thoughts should be corrupted from the <span class="alt-label">simplicity</span><span class="alt-text">(=sincerity)</span> that is toward Christ.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> For if indeed the one coming preaches another Jesus whom we did not preach, or you receive a different spirit which you did not receive, or a different gospel which you did not accept, you might well bear <span class="added-text">(with it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For I reckon <span class="added-text">(myself)</span> to have fallen short in nothing of the most <span class="alt-label">eminent</span><span class="alt-text">(=super)</span> apostles.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But even if <span class="added-text">(I am)</span> <span class="alt-label">unskilled</span><span class="alt-text">(=untrained)</span> in speech, yet not in knowledge; but in everything we have been made manifest among all <span class="added-text">(people)</span> toward you.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Or did I commit sin, humbling myself that you might be exalted, because I preached God's gospel to you <span class="alt-label">freely</span><span class="alt-text">(=without charge)</span>?[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> I robbed other churches, taking <span class="alt-label">wages</span><span class="alt-text">(=support)</span> for <span class="added-text">(my)</span> service to you;

<span class="v-num">9</span> And being present with you and lacking, I burdened no one; for the brothers who came from Macedonia supplied my lack; and in everything I kept myself from being a burden to you, and I will keep <span class="added-text">(myself so)</span>.

<span class="v-num">10</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> truth of Christ is in me, that this boasting shall not be <span class="alt-label">stopped</span><span class="alt-text">(=silenced)</span> in regard to me in the regions of Achaia.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Why? Because I do not love you? God knows.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But what I do, I will also do, that I may cut off the <span class="alt-label">occasion</span><span class="alt-text">(=opportunity)</span> from those desiring an occasion, that in what they boast, they may be found just as we <span class="added-text">(are)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For such <span class="added-text">(men are)</span> false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into apostles of Christ.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> no wonder; for Satan himself transforms himself into an angel of light.

<span class="v-num">15</span> <span class="added-text">(It is)</span> no great thing then if his ministers also transform themselves as ministers of righteousness, whose end shall be according to their works.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Again I say, let no one think me to be foolish; but if otherwise, receive me even as foolish, that I too may boast a little.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> What I speak, I do not speak according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, but as in foolishness, in this <span class="alt-label">confidence</span><span class="alt-text">(=basis)</span> of boasting.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Since many boast according to the flesh, I also will boast.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For you gladly bear with the foolish, being wise <span class="added-text">(yourselves)</span>.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For you bear with <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, if anyone enslaves you, if anyone devours <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, if anyone takes <span class="added-text">(from you)</span>, if anyone exalts himself, if anyone strikes you on the face.

<span class="v-num">21</span> I speak with regard to <span class="alt-label">dishonor</span><span class="alt-text">(=to my shame)</span>, as though we had been weak; but in whatever anyone dares <span class="added-text">(to boast)</span>—I speak in foolishness—I dare also.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Are they Hebrews? I also <span class="added-text">(am)</span>. Are they Israelites? I also <span class="added-text">(am)</span>. Are they Abraham's seed? I also <span class="added-text">(am)</span>.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Are they ministers of Christ? I speak as one out of my mind—I am more <span class="added-text">(so)</span>; in labors more abundantly, in beatings beyond measure, in imprisonments more frequently, in near-deaths often.

<span class="v-num">24</span> From <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jews five times I received forty <span class="added-text">(lashes)</span> less one,

<span class="v-num">25</span> Three times I was beaten with rods, once I was stoned, three times I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I have spent in the deep;

<span class="v-num">26</span> in journeys often; in dangers of rivers, in dangers of robbers, in dangers from <span class="added-text">(my own)</span> race, in dangers from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, in dangers in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city, in dangers in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wilderness, in dangers in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sea, in dangers among false brothers;

<span class="v-num">27</span> in labor and hardship, in sleeplessness often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness;

<span class="v-num">28</span> Besides the things that are <span class="alt-label">outside</span><span class="alt-text">(=external)</span>, there is <span class="added-text">(that)</span> daily <span class="alt-label">pressure</span><span class="alt-text">(=anxiety)</span> upon me, the care for all the churches.

<span class="v-num">29</span> Who is weak, and I am not weak? Who is made to stumble, and I do not burn?

<span class="v-num">30</span> If I must boast, I will boast of the things of my weakness.

<span class="v-num">31</span> The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who is blessed forever, knows that I do not lie.

<span class="v-num">32</span> In Damascus the <span class="alt-label">governor</span><span class="alt-text">(=ethnarch)</span> under Aretas the king was guarding the city of the Damascenes, wanting to seize me;

<span class="v-num">33</span> and through a window in a basket I was let down through the wall, and I escaped his hands.

[^1]: μή πως ... φθαρῇ는 부정적 결과를 염려하는 가정법 구문으로 '~할까 두렵다'는 뜻을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα ὑψωθῆτε는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로 '너희를 높이려 함'의 뜻이다.
[^3]: 두 개의 ἵνα절(ἐκκόψω, εὑρεθῶσι)이 모두 가정법 목적절로 저자의 의도를 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα καυχήσωμαι는 가정법 목적절로 '내가 자랑하게 하려 함'을 나타낸다.
