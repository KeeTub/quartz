# 2 Corinthians 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> And working together <span class="added-text">(with him)</span>, we also exhort <span class="added-text">(you)</span> not to receive the grace of God in vain.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For he says, "In an acceptable time I listened to you, and in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of salvation I helped you"; behold, now <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> acceptable time; behold, now <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> day of salvation.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> giving no occasion of stumbling in anything, so that the ministry may not be blamed,[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> but in everything commending ourselves as servants of God, in much endurance, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses,

<span class="v-num">5</span> in stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labors, in watchings, in fastings,

<span class="v-num">6</span> in purity, in knowledge, in longsuffering, in kindness, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, in love unfeigned,

<span class="v-num">7</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of truth, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God; through the weapons of righteousness on the right hand and the left,

<span class="v-num">8</span> through glory and dishonor, through evil report and good report; as deceivers, and <span class="added-text">(yet)</span> true;

<span class="v-num">9</span> as unknown, and <span class="added-text">(yet)</span> well known; as dying, and behold, we live; as being chastened, and not put to death;

<span class="v-num">10</span> as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and <span class="added-text">(yet)</span> possessing all things.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Our mouth has been opened to you, Corinthians, our heart has been widened.

<span class="v-num">12</span> You are not straitened in us, but you are straitened in your own affections.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Now for the same recompense—I speak as to children—you also be widened.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Do not become unequally yoked with unbelievers; for what partnership <span class="added-text">(has)</span> righteousness with lawlessness? And what fellowship <span class="added-text">(has)</span> light with darkness?[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And what harmony <span class="added-text">(has)</span> Christ with Belial? Or what portion <span class="added-text">(has)</span> a believer with an unbeliever?

<span class="v-num">16</span> And what agreement <span class="added-text">(has the)</span> temple of God with idols? For you are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> temple of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God, just as God said, "I will dwell in them, and walk among <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; and I will be their God, and they will be my people.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Therefore come out from the midst of them, and be separated, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, and touch not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> unclean thing; and I will receive you,[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> and I will be to you a Father, and you will be to me sons and daughters," says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Almighty.[^6]

[^1]: 이사야 49:8 인용으로, 종말론적 '이제'를 강조하여 구원의 때가 현재 임하였음을 선포한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(μωμηθῇ) 목적절: 거리낌을 주지 않는 목적/의도가 '이 직분이 비방받지 않게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않은 결과를 막으려는 의도를 표현한다.
[^3]: κοινωνία(교제)는 단순 친분이 아니라 근본적 연합·나눔의 관계를 뜻하므로, 빛과 어둠처럼 본질이 다른 자와의 결합 불가능성을 강조한다.
[^4]: 레위기 26:12/에스겔 37:27 인용; ναός(성전)는 하나님의 임재 처소를 뜻하며 여기서 신자 공동체가 그 성전이 됨을 선언한다.
[^5]: 이사야 52:11 인용이 이어짐; '구별되라'는 명령은 우상 및 부정한 것과의 결별을 요구한다.
[^6]: 사무엘하 7:14 계열 인용이 마무리됨; 하나님과 백성 사이의 부자(父子) 언약 관계로 결론짓는다.
