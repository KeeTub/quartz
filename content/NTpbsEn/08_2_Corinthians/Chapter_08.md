# 2 Corinthians 8

<span class="v-num">1</span> We make known to you, brothers, the grace of God that has been given in the churches of Macedonia,

<span class="v-num">2</span> that in much trial of affliction, the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded to the riches of their <span class="alt-label">liberality</span><span class="alt-text">(=simplicity)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For according to <span class="added-text">(their)</span> power, I testify, and beyond <span class="added-text">(their)</span> power, <span class="added-text">(they were)</span> willing of themselves,

<span class="v-num">4</span> with much entreaty begging us that we would receive the grace and the fellowship of the ministry which is toward the saints;

<span class="v-num">5</span> and not as we hoped, but they gave themselves first to the Lord, and to us through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God.

<span class="v-num">6</span> so that we urged Titus, that as he had begun before, so also he would complete in you this grace also.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But as you abound in everything—in faith, and word, and knowledge, and all diligence, and in the love from you in us—see that you abound in this grace also.[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> I do not speak by way of commandment, but through the diligence of others, proving also the genuineness of your love.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For you know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that though he was rich, for your sakes he became poor, so that you through his poverty might become rich.[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And in this I give <span class="added-text">(my)</span> judgment, for this is profitable for you, who not only began to do <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, but also to desire <span class="added-text">(it)</span> since last year.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now then also complete the doing <span class="added-text">(of it)</span>, so that just as <span class="added-text">(there was)</span> the readiness to desire <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, so also <span class="added-text">(there may be)</span> the completion out of what you have.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For if the readiness is present, it is acceptable according to what one has, not according to what he does not have.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> not that others <span class="added-text">(may have)</span> ease, and you <span class="added-text">(have)</span> affliction, but by equality—in the present time your abundance <span class="added-text">(supplying)</span> for their lack,[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> that also their abundance may be for your lack, so that there may be equality,[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> as it is written, 'He who <span class="added-text">(gathered)</span> much did not have too much, and he who <span class="added-text">(gathered)</span> little did not have too little.'[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But thanks <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to God who puts the same earnestness for you into the heart of Titus,

<span class="v-num">17</span> for he indeed accepted the exhortation, but being more earnest, he went out to you of his own accord.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And we sent with him the brother whose praise in the gospel <span class="added-text">(is)</span> through all the churches;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and not only <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, but he was also chosen by the churches to travel with us with this grace which is administered by us to the glory of the Lord himself, and <span class="added-text">(to show)</span> your readiness;[^8]

<span class="v-num">20</span> avoiding this, that anyone should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us,

<span class="v-num">21</span> providing for good things not only before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, but also before men.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And we sent with them our brother whom we have often proved diligent in many things, but now much more diligent, because of the great confidence which he has in you.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Whether <span class="added-text">(it is asked)</span> concerning Titus, he is my partner and fellow worker toward you; or <span class="added-text">(concerning)</span> our brothers, they are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> apostles of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> churches, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of Christ.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Therefore show to them, and before the face of the churches, the proof of your love, and of our boasting on your behalf.

[^1]: ἵνα...ἐπιτελέσῃ는 목적절(가정법)로, 디도에게 완성을 권한 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(περισσεύητε)은 목적절로, 앞서 언급한 여러 은사들처럼 '이 은혜'(연보)에도 풍성해지도록 하려는 의도를 나타낸다. 권면의 목표를 강조하는 구문이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(πλουτήσητε)이 이끄는 목적절로, 그리스도께서 가난하게 되신 목적을 나타낸다. 즉 그의 가난을 통해 우리가 부요하게 되는 것이 하나님의 의도된 계획임을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἐὰν ἔχῃ τις는 아직 실현 여부가 불확실한 3급 조건문으로, 가진 것에 따른 헌신의 원리를 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(생략된 동사 함축) 목적절로, '다른 이들은 편하고 너희는 어렵게 하려는' 의도가 아님을 부정하는 구문이다. 뒤에 이어지는 '균등함으로' 부분과 대조되어 바울의 진짜 목적을 밝히는 데 쓰인다.
[^6]: ἵνα γένηται는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 상호 나눔을 통한 균등이라는 목표를 강조한다.
[^7]: 출애굽기 16:18(만나 사건)을 인용하여 하나님이 정하신 균등의 원리를 예증한다.
[^8]: χειροτονηθεὶς는 본래 '손을 들어 선출하다'는 뜻으로, 이 형제가 교회들의 공적 인준을 받아 파송되었음을 나타낸다.
