# 2 Corinthians 1

<div class="bible-legend">
  <span class="legend-item"><span class="dot alt"></span>(=alt.word)</span>

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, <span class="added-text">(an)</span> apostle of Jesus Christ through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, and Timothy the brother, to the church of God being in Corinth, with all the saints being in all Achaia:

<span class="v-num">2</span> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort,

<span class="v-num">4</span> who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those in any affliction, through the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God;[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> because as the sufferings of Christ abound toward us, so through Christ our comfort also abounds.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And whether we are afflicted, <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> for your comfort and salvation, which is worked out in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> endurance of the same sufferings which we also suffer; or whether we are comforted, <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> for your comfort and salvation; and our hope for you <span class="added-text">(is)</span> firm,

<span class="v-num">7</span> knowing that as you are partakers of the sufferings, so also <span class="added-text">(you are partakers)</span> of the comfort.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For we do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning our affliction which happened to us in Asia, that we were weighed down exceedingly beyond <span class="added-text">(our)</span> power, so that we despaired even of living;

<span class="v-num">9</span> But we ourselves have had the <span class="alt-label">sentence</span><span class="alt-text">(=judgment)</span> of death in ourselves, so that we would not trust in ourselves, but in God who raises the dead;[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> who delivered us from so great a death, and <span class="added-text">(still)</span> delivers, in whom we have hoped that he will yet also deliver <span class="added-text">(us)</span>,

<span class="v-num">11</span> you also joining to help for us by <span class="added-text">(your)</span> prayer, so that thanks may be given through many, for the gift given to us from many <span class="alt-label">persons</span><span class="alt-text">(=on our behalf)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For our boasting is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and sincerity of God, not in fleshly wisdom, but in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> grace of God, we conducted ourselves in the world, and more abundantly toward you.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For we write no other things to you than what you read or even fully know, and I hope that you will fully know <span class="added-text">(them)</span> even to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end,

<span class="v-num">14</span> as also you have known us in part, that we are your boast, just as you also <span class="added-text">(are)</span> ours, in the day of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And in this confidence I intended to come to you before, so that you might have a second grace;[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> and through you to pass into Macedonia, and again from Macedonia to come to you, and by you to be sent forward to Judea.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Therefore, purposing this, did I then use lightness? Or the things which I plan, do I plan according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, so that with me there should be yes yes, and no no?[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> But God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> faithful, that our word to you was not yes and no.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For the Son of God, Jesus Christ, who was preached among you through us, through me and Silvanus and Timothy, was not yes and no, but in him it has become yes.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For as many as <span class="added-text">(are the)</span> promises of God, in him <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the yes, and in him <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the amen, to God for glory through us.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Now he who establishes us with you in Christ, and anointed us, <span class="added-text">(is)</span> God;

<span class="v-num">22</span> who also sealed us, and gave the guarantee of the Spirit in our hearts.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But I call God <span class="added-text">(as)</span> witness upon my soul, that <span class="added-text">(it was)</span> to spare you <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I did not come again to Corinth.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Not that we lord it over your faith, but we are fellow workers of your joy; for by faith you stand.

[^1]: εἰς τὸ δύνασθαι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문으로, 하나님의 위로를 받는 목적이 타인을 위로함에 있음을 보여준다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ὦμεν)은 목적절로, 죽음의 판결을 겪은 목적이 자기 신뢰에서 하나님 신뢰로의 전환에 있음을 보여준다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(εὐχαριστηθῇ)은 목적절로, 성도들의 기도가 많은 이들의 감사로 이어지는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχητε)은 목적절로, 바울의 방문 계획이 성도들에게 두 번째 은혜를 주려는 목적임을 밝힌다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ) 목적절로, 바울이 '예'와 '아니오'를 동시에 말하는 이중적 태도를 보이려는 의도가 있었는지 반문하는 수사적 목적을 나타낸다.
