<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore, having these promises, beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fear of God.

<span class="v-num">2</span> <span class="alt-label">Receive</span><span class="alt-text">(=Make room for)</span> us; we wronged no one, we corrupted no one, we exploited no one.

<span class="v-num">3</span> I do not say <span class="added-text">(this)</span> toward condemnation; for I have said before that you are in our hearts, to die together and to live together.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Great <del class="deleted-text">[is]</del> my boldness toward you, great <del class="deleted-text">[is]</del> my boasting on behalf of you; I have been filled with comfort, I overflow with joy <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=upon)</span> all our affliction.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For indeed, when we had come into Macedonia, our flesh had no rest, but we were afflicted in every way—outside, fightings; inside, fears.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But God, who comforts the lowly, comforted us <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> the coming of Titus,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and not only by his coming, but also by the comfort with which he was comforted <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> you, reporting to us your longing, your mourning, your zeal for me, so that I rejoiced even more.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For even if I grieved you <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> the letter, I do not regret <del class="deleted-text">[it]</del>, even if I did regret <del class="deleted-text">[it]</del>; for I see that that letter, even if only for an <span class="alt-label">hour</span><span class="alt-text">(=a while)</span>, grieved you.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Now I rejoice, not that you were grieved, but that you were grieved unto repentance; for you were grieved according to God, that in nothing you might suffer loss <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> us.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For godly <span class="alt-label">grief</span><span class="alt-text">(=grief according to God)</span> produces repentance unto salvation not to be regretted; but the grief of the world produces death.[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> For behold, this very thing, that you were grieved according to God, what earnestness it produced in you—indeed, what defense, what indignation, what fear, what longing, what zeal, what avenging! In everything you have proven yourselves to be pure in the matter.

<span class="v-num">12</span> So then, even if I wrote to you, <del class="deleted-text">[it was]</del> not for the sake of the one who did the wrong, nor for the sake of the one who suffered the wrong, but for the sake of your earnestness on our behalf being made known to you before God.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For this reason we have been comforted <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=at)</span> your comfort; and we rejoiced even more abundantly at the joy of Titus, because his spirit has been refreshed <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> all of you.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For if in anything I have boasted to him on your behalf, I was not put to shame; but as we spoke all things to you in truth, so also our boasting before Titus was found <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> truth.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And his affection is more abundantly toward you, as he remembers the obedience of you all, how with fear and trembling you received him.

<span class="v-num">16</span> I rejoice that in everything I have confidence in you.

[^1]: εἰς τὸ συναποθανεῖν καὶ συζῆν은 εἰς τό+부정사로 나타내는 목적 부정사구문으로, 성도들이 바울의 마음속에 있는 목적(함께 죽고 살기 위함)을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ζημιωθῆτε) 목적절로, 하나님의 뜻대로 하는 근심의 궁극 목적(해를 받지 않게 함)을 나타낸다.
[^3]: κατὰ Θεὸν λύπη(하나님의 뜻대로 하는 근심)와 τοῦ κόσμου λύπη(세상의 근심)가 대조되어, 근심 자체가 아니라 그 근심이 향하는 방향과 결과가 핵심임을 보여준다.
[^4]: εἵνεκεν τοῦ φανερωθῆναι는 τοῦ+부정사로 나타내는 목적 구문으로, 편지를 쓴 목적이 성도들의 간절함이 하나님 앞에 드러나게 하려는 것임을 밝힌다.
