# 2 Corinthians 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> But I decided this for myself, not to come again to you in grief.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For if I grieve you, then who is the one gladdening me, except the one being grieved <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> me?

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I wrote this same thing to you, that when I came I might not have grief from those from whom I ought to rejoice—being confident about all of you, that my joy is <span class="added-text">(the joy)</span> of you all.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote to you through many tears, not that you might be grieved, but that you might know the love which I have more abundantly toward you.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But if anyone has caused grief, he has not grieved me, but in part—not to say too much—<span class="added-text">(he has grieved)</span> all of you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Sufficient for such a one <span class="added-text">(is)</span> this punishment which <span class="added-text">(was)</span> by the <span class="alt-label">majority</span><span class="alt-text">(=more)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> So that on the contrary you should rather forgive and comfort <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, lest somehow such a one should be swallowed up by excessive grief.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore I urge you to confirm love toward him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For to this end I also wrote, that I might know the proof of you, whether you are obedient in all things.[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But whom you forgive anything, I also <span class="added-text">(forgive)</span>; for indeed if I have forgiven anything, to whom I have forgiven <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, <span class="added-text">(I did it)</span> for your sake in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> presence of Christ,

<span class="v-num">11</span> lest we should be taken advantage of by Satan; for we are not ignorant of his devices.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now when I came to Troas for the gospel of Christ, and a door having been opened to me in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord,

<span class="v-num">13</span> I had no rest in my spirit, because I did not find Titus my brother; but taking leave of them, I went out to Macedonia.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But thanks <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to God, who always leads us in triumph in Christ, and manifests through us the fragrance of the knowledge of him in every place.[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> For we are a fragrance of Christ to God, among those being saved and among those perishing;[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> to the one a fragrance from death to death, and to the other a fragrance from life to life. And who is sufficient for these things?

<span class="v-num">17</span> For we are not as the many, peddling the word of God; but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the presence of God, we speak in Christ.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχω) 목적절로, 바울이 편지를 쓴 목적(근심을 피하기 위함)을 나타낸다. 미래에 실현되길 바라는 의도를 표현하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(λυπηθῆτε, γνῶτε)이 이중으로 쓰여 편지를 쓴 목적을 밝힘: 근심하게 하려는 것이 아니라 넘치는 사랑을 알게 하려는 목적절.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιβαρῶ) 목적절로, 바울이 말을 절제하려는 의도를 밝힌다. '내가 너희 모두에게 지나친 부담을 주지 않으려고'라는 뜻으로, 앞선 완곡한 표현('어느 정도는')의 이유를 설명한다.
[^4]: ἵνα γνῶ는 목적을 나타내는 가정법으로, 바울이 편지를 쓴 실제 의도를 밝힌다.
[^5]: ἵνα μὴ πλεονεκτηθῶμεν은 목적을 나타내는 수동태 가정법으로, 용서의 실천적 이유가 사탄의 술책 차단에 있음을 보여준다.
[^6]: θριαμβεύω는 로마 개선장군이 포로를 이끌고 행진하던 이미지로, 바울은 자신을 그리스도께 사로잡혀 그분의 승리를 드러내는 자로 묘사한다.
[^7]: εὐωδία는 구약의 제사 때 드리는 '향기로운 냄새'(창 8:21 등)의 언어를 반영하며, 신자의 삶 자체가 하나님께 드려지는 제물로 그려진다.
