<span class="v-num">1</span> For concerning the ministry that <span class="added-text">(is)</span> <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> the saints, it is <span class="alt-label">superfluous</span><span class="alt-text">(=unnecessary)</span> for me to write to you.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For I know your eagerness, concerning which I boast to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Macedonians on your behalf, that Achaia has been prepared from last year; and the zeal from you stirred up the <span class="alt-label">majority</span><span class="alt-text">(=most of them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But I sent the brothers, so that our boasting on your behalf might not be made empty in this respect; so that, just as I was saying, you might be prepared,[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> lest somehow, if <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Macedonians come with me and find you unprepared, we <span class="added-text">(not to say you)</span> should be put to shame in this confidence of boasting.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Therefore I considered <span class="added-text">(it)</span> necessary to urge the brothers, that they should go before to you and prepare beforehand your previously announced <span class="alt-label">blessing</span><span class="alt-text">(=bountiful gift)</span>, that it might be ready thus as a blessing, and not as <span class="added-text">(something extorted by)</span> <span class="alt-label">covetousness</span><span class="alt-text">(=greediness)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But this <span class="added-text">(I say)</span>: the one sowing sparingly will also reap sparingly; and the one sowing <span class="alt-label">on</span><span class="alt-text">(=with a view to)</span> <span class="alt-label">blessings</span><span class="alt-text">(=bountifully)</span> will also reap <span class="alt-label">on</span><span class="alt-text">(=with a view to)</span> <span class="alt-label">blessings</span><span class="alt-text">(=bountifully)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Each one <span class="added-text">(should give)</span> just as he purposes in <span class="added-text">(his)</span> heart, not out of <span class="alt-label">grief</span><span class="alt-text">(=reluctance)</span> or out of necessity, for God loves a cheerful giver.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> able to make all grace abound <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> you, so that, having all sufficiency in everything always, you may abound <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> every good work,

<span class="v-num">9</span> as it is written, 'He scattered abroad, he gave to the poor; his righteousness remains forever.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now may the one supplying seed to the sower, and bread for food, supply and multiply your seed <span class="added-text">(sown)</span>, and increase the <span class="alt-label">fruits</span><span class="alt-text">(=produce)</span> of your righteousness,[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> because the ministry of this <span class="alt-label">service</span><span class="alt-text">(=liturgy)</span> is not only supplying fully the things lacking of the saints, but also abounding through many thanksgivings to God;

<span class="v-num">13</span> through the proof of this ministry, glorifying God for the submission of your confession <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=unto)</span> the gospel of Christ, and for the <span class="alt-label">generosity</span><span class="alt-text">(=liberality)</span> of <span class="added-text">(your)</span> fellowship toward them and toward all,

<span class="v-num">15</span> Now thanks <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to God for his indescribable gift.
