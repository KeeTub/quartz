<span class="v-num">1</span> But know this, that in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> last days difficult times will come upon <span class="added-text">(us)</span>.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For men will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,

<span class="v-num">3</span> without natural affection, unappeasable, slanderers, without self-control, savage, haters of good,

<span class="v-num">4</span> traitors, reckless, puffed up, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God,

<span class="v-num">5</span> having a form of godliness but having denied the power of it. And from these turn away.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For of these are the ones creeping into the houses, and taking captive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foolish women heaped up with sins, led by various lusts,

<span class="v-num">7</span> always learning, and never able to come to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> truth.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Now in the manner <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Jannes and Jambres withstood Moses, so also these withstand the truth, men corrupted in the mind, disqualified concerning the faith.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But they will not advance further, for their folly will be plain to all, as also that of those <span class="added-text">(men)</span> became.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But you have followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love, endurance,

<span class="v-num">11</span> persecutions, sufferings, such as happened to me in Antioch, in Iconium, in Lystra; what persecutions I endured, and out of them all the Lord delivered me.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And indeed all who wish to live godly in Christ Jesus will be persecuted;

<span class="v-num">13</span> but evil men and imposters will advance to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> worse, deceiving and being deceived.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But you, remain in the things you learned and were assured of, knowing from whom you learned <span class="added-text">(them)</span>,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and that from <span class="added-text">(a)</span> babe you have known the sacred writings, which are able to make you wise unto salvation, through faith which is in Christ Jesus.[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> All Scripture <span class="added-text">(is)</span> God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, for instruction which is in righteousness,[^3]

<span class="v-num">17</span> so that the man of God may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.[^4]

---

[^1]: 얀네와 얌브레는 출애굽기 애굽 술사들에 대한 유대 전통(구약 암시)으로, 모세를 대적한 사건을 진리를 대적하는 자들의 원형으로 제시한다.
[^2]: ἱερὰ γράμματα(거룩한 글들)는 구약 성경 전체를 가리키는 유대적 표현으로, 디모데가 어릴 때부터 익힌 구약이 그리스도 안의 믿음을 통해 구원에 이르는 지혜를 준다는 것을 강조한다.
[^3]: θεόπνευστος(하나님의 감동으로 된)는 성경 전체의 신적 기원과 권위를 단언하는 핵심 신학 용어로, 성경의 실천적 유익(가르침·책망·교정·훈육)의 근거가 된다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ)의 목적절로, 성경의 유익(16절)이 지향하는 궁극 목적, 곧 하나님의 사람이 온전히 갖추어져 선한 일을 행하게 됨을 나타낸다.
