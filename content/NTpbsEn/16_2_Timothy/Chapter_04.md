# 2 Timothy 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> I therefore charge <span class="added-text">(you)</span> before God and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ, the one about to judge <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead according to his appearing and his kingdom,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Preach the word, be <span class="alt-label">ready</span><span class="alt-text">(=stand ready)</span> in season, out of <span class="alt-label">season</span><span class="alt-text">(=whether it is timely or not)</span>, reprove, rebuke, exhort, with all patience and teaching.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For there will be a time when they will not endure sound teaching, but according to their own desires they will heap up for themselves teachers, having <span class="added-text">(their)</span> ears itching,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and they will turn away <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hearing from the truth, and will be turned aside to <span class="alt-label">fables</span><span class="alt-text">(=myths)</span>.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But you, be sober in all things, endure hardship, do <span class="added-text">(the)</span> work of an evangelist, fulfill your <span class="alt-label">ministry</span><span class="alt-text">(=service)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For I am already being poured out <span class="added-text">(as a drink offering)</span>, and the time of my departure has come <span class="alt-label">near</span><span class="alt-text">(=is at hand)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> I have fought the good fight, I have finished the course, I have kept the faith;[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Henceforth, there is laid up for me the crown of righteousness, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give to me in that day; and not only to me, but also to all the ones having loved his appearing.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Be diligent to come to me quickly.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For Demas has forsaken me, having loved this present age, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus to Dalmatia.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Luke alone is with me. Having taken up Mark, bring <span class="added-text">(him)</span> with yourself, for he is useful to me for <span class="alt-label">ministry</span><span class="alt-text">(=service)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But Tychicus I sent to Ephesus.

<span class="v-num">13</span> The cloak which I left in Troas with Carpus, when coming, bring <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and the books, especially the parchments.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Alexander the coppersmith <span class="alt-label">showed</span><span class="alt-text">(=did)</span> many evil things to me; may the Lord repay him according to his works,[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> of whom you also beware, for he has greatly withstood our <span class="alt-label">words</span><span class="alt-text">(=message)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> At my first defense no one stood <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=supported)</span> me, but all forsook me; may it not be counted against them.[^5]

<span class="v-num">17</span> But the Lord stood by me, and strengthened me, that through me the <span class="alt-label">proclamation</span><span class="alt-text">(=preaching)</span> might be fully accomplished, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> all the nations might hear; and I was delivered out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lion.[^6]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work, and will save <span class="added-text">(me)</span> into his heavenly kingdom; to whom <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory into the ages of the ages. Amen.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the household of Onesiphorus.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Erastus remained in Corinth; but Trophimus I left in Miletus, being sick.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus greets you, and Pudens, and Linus, and Claudia, and all the brothers.

<span class="v-num">22</span> The Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with your spirit. Grace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you<span class="added-text">(pl)</span>. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> second <span class="added-text">(epistle)</span> to Timothy, having been appointed <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first bishop of the church of the Ephesians, was written from Rome, when Paul appeared before Caesar Nero a second time.

[^1]: ἐπιφάνειαν(나타나심)은 그리스도의 재림을 가리키는 핵심 종말론 용어로, 심판의 기준점이 됨을 강조한다.
[^2]: σπένδομαι는 현재 수동태로, 제사에서 전제(奠祭)로 부어지는 진행 중인 상태를 그려 순교가 임박했음을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 세 동사 모두 완료시제로, 과거의 행위가 현재까지 그 결과로 남아있는 확고한 상태를 강조한다.
[^4]: ἀποδῴη는 기원법(希求法)으로, 단정적 예언이 아니라 하나님의 공의로운 갚으심을 바라는 소원을 나타낸다.
[^5]: λογισθείη는 기원법으로, 바울이 자신을 버린 이들에게 그 죄가 전가되지 않기를 바라는 용서의 기도를 담는다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(πληροφορηθῇ, ἀκούσῃ)은 주께서 힘을 주신 목적, 곧 복음의 완전한 선포와 이방인들의 들음을 나타낸다.
