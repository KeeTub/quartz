# 2 Timothy 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And what you heard from me through many witnesses, these things entrust to faithful men, who will be competent to teach others also.

<span class="v-num">3</span> You therefore endure hardship, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">4</span> No one serving as a soldier gets entangled in the affairs of <span class="added-text">(this)</span> life, that he may please the one who enlisted <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And if anyone also competes <span class="added-text">(in games)</span>, he is not crowned unless he competes lawfully.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> The farmer who labors must first partake of the fruits.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Consider what I say; for the Lord will give you understanding in all things.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Remember Jesus Christ, raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">seed</span><span class="alt-text">(=offspring)</span> of David, according to my gospel,[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> in which I suffer hardship to the point of bonds, as an evildoer; but the word of God is not bound.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Because of this I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation that is in Christ Jesus, with eternal glory.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the saying: for if we died together <span class="added-text">(with him)</span>, we will also live together <span class="added-text">(with him)</span>;

<span class="v-num">12</span> if we endure, we will also reign together <span class="added-text">(with him)</span>; if we deny <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, he also will deny us;

<span class="v-num">13</span> if we are unfaithful, he remains faithful; he cannot deny himself.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Remind <span class="added-text">(them)</span> of these things, solemnly testifying before the Lord not to fight about words, to no profit, <span class="added-text">(but)</span> to the ruin of the hearers.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Be diligent to present yourself approved to God, a workman not ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But avoid profane, empty talk; for they will proceed further in ungodliness,

<span class="v-num">17</span> and their word will spread like gangrene, of whom are Hymenaeus and Philetus,

<span class="v-num">18</span> who have gone astray concerning the truth, saying that the resurrection has already happened, and they overturn the faith of some.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Nevertheless the solid foundation of God stands, having this seal, 'The Lord knew those who are his,' and, 'Let everyone who names the name of Christ depart from unrighteousness.'[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and silver, but also of wood and of clay, and some for honor, and some for dishonor.

<span class="v-num">21</span> If therefore anyone cleanses himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, and useful to the master, prepared for every good work.[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> But flee youthful lusts; and pursue righteousness, faith, love, peace, with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But refuse foolish and uninstructed disputes, knowing that they produce fights;

<span class="v-num">24</span> And a servant of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord must not fight, but be gentle toward all, apt to teach, patient <span class="added-text">(of wrongs)</span>,

<span class="v-num">25</span> in meekness instructing those who oppose <span class="added-text">(themselves)</span>; <span class="added-text">(if perhaps)</span> God may grant them repentance to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> truth,[^8]

<span class="v-num">26</span> and they may return to soberness out of the snare of the devil, having been captured by him unto his will.[^9]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀρέσῃ)으로 목적을 나타내는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἐὰν μὴ νομίμως ἀθλήσῃ는 ἐάν+가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 규칙을 따르지 않을 경우를 가정하여 면류관을 얻지 못하는 결과를 말한다.
[^3]: ἐγηγερμένον은 완료분사로 부활이 이미 이루어져 지속되는 상태임을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(τύχωσι)으로 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ὀρθοτομοῦντα는 '똑바로 자르다'는 뜻으로 진리의 말씀을 왜곡 없이 바르게 다루는 사역자의 태도를 가리킨다.
[^6]: 앞 인용은 민수기 16:5 LXX을 반영하며, 하나님이 자기 백성을 아신다는 구약적 확신을 이어받는다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(ἐκκαθάρῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 제3형 조건문으로, '누구든지 스스로 깨끗케 하면'이라는 열려있는 조건을 제시한다. 이는 각 신자의 자발적 결단에 따라 귀히 쓰는 그릇이 될 수 있음을 강조한다.
[^8]: μήποτε δῷ는 목적/가능성을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 회개를 주실지 여부가 하나님께 달려 있음을 암시한다.
[^9]: ἀνανήψωσιν은 앞 절의 μήποτε δῷ와 연결된 가정법으로 회개의 목적(정신을 차림)을 이어서 나타낸다.
