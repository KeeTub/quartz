# 2 Timothy 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> promise of life, the <span class="added-text">(life)</span> in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">2</span> To Timothy, <span class="added-text">(my)</span> beloved child: Grace, mercy, peace from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

<span class="v-num">3</span> I have thanks to God, whom I serve from <span class="added-text">(my)</span> forefathers with a clean conscience, as I have unceasing remembrance concerning you in my prayers night and day,

<span class="v-num">4</span> longing to see you, remembering your tears, so that I may be filled with joy;[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> receiving remembrance of the unfeigned faith in you, which dwelt first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice, and I am persuaded that <span class="added-text">(it dwells)</span> in you also.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For which cause I remind you to stir up (=rekindle) the gift of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands;

<span class="v-num">7</span> For God did not give us a spirit of fear (=cowardice), but of power and love and self-control (=sound mind).[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore do not be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor <span class="added-text">(of)</span> me his prisoner, but suffer hardship with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gospel according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of God,

<span class="v-num">9</span> who saved us and called <span class="added-text">(us)</span> with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to His own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Christ Jesus before eternal times (=ages),

<span class="v-num">10</span> but now has been manifested through the appearing of our Savior Jesus Christ, who indeed abolished death, but illuminated life and incorruption through the gospel,[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> for which I was appointed a preacher and apostle and teacher of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles;

<span class="v-num">12</span> For which cause I also suffer these things, but I am not ashamed; for I know whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that he is able to guard my deposit (=that which was entrusted to me) unto that day.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Have (=Hold) a pattern of sound words, which you heard from me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Guard the good deposit through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit who dwells in us.

<span class="v-num">15</span> You know this, that all who are in Asia turned away from me, of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.

<span class="v-num">16</span> May the Lord give mercy to the house of Onesiphorus, because he often refreshed me and was not ashamed of my chain,

<span class="v-num">17</span> but having come to Rome, he sought me out very diligently and found <span class="added-text">(me)</span>;

<span class="v-num">18</span> may the Lord grant him to find mercy from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord in that day. And how many things he served in Ephesus, you know very well.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῶ) 목적절로, 만남의 목적이 '기쁨의 충만'임을 나타낸다.
[^2]: δειλία(두려움)와 δύναμις·ἀγάπη·σωφρονισμός(능력·사랑·절제)가 대조를 이루어, 하나님이 주신 영의 성격을 규정한다.
[^3]: καταργήσαντος·φωτίσαντος는 부정과거 분사로, 그리스도의 나타나심을 통한 결정적 완결 행위(사망 폐함·생명 비춤)를 나타낸다.
[^4]: παραθήκη는 법률 용어로 '맡겨진 예치물'을 뜻하며, 바울이 하나님께 맡긴 것(복음·자신의 생애)이 그 날까지 안전히 지켜질 것을 확신함을 나타낸다.
