# 2 John 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> The elder to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elect lady and to her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only, but also all the ones who have known the truth,

<span class="v-num">2</span> because of the truth which <span class="alt-label">abides</span><span class="alt-text">(=remains)</span> in us, and it will be with us into the <span class="alt-label">age</span><span class="alt-text">(=forever)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Grace, mercy, <span class="added-text">(and)</span> peace will be with us from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, and from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of the Father, in truth and love.

<span class="v-num">4</span> I rejoiced greatly that I have found <span class="added-text">(some)</span> of your children walking in truth, just as we received <span class="added-text">(the)</span> commandment from the Father.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And now I ask you, lady, not as writing to you a new commandment, but the one which we had from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning, that we love one another.[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And this is love, that we walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, just as you heard from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning, that you should walk in it.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Because many <span class="alt-label">deceivers</span><span class="alt-text">(=deceiving ones)</span> have entered into the world, the ones not confessing Jesus Christ coming in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh. This one is the deceiver and the antichrist.[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Watch yourselves, that we may not lose <span class="added-text">(the things)</span> which we worked <span class="added-text">(for)</span>, but that we may receive a full reward.[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Everyone <span class="alt-label">transgressing</span><span class="alt-text">(=going beyond)</span> and not abiding in the teaching of Christ does not have God. The one abiding in the teaching of Christ, this one has both the Father and the Son.

<span class="v-num">10</span> If anyone comes to you and does not bring this teaching, do not receive him into <span class="added-text">(your)</span> house, and do not say to him, <span class="alt-label">"Greetings</span><span class="alt-text">(=Rejoice)</span>."

<span class="v-num">11</span> For the one saying to him, <span class="alt-label">"Greetings</span><span class="alt-text">(=Rejoice)</span>," shares in his evil works.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Having many things to write to you, I did not want <span class="added-text">(to write them)</span> through paper and ink, but I hope to come to you and to speak mouth to mouth, that our joy may be <span class="alt-label">full</span><span class="alt-text">(=fulfilled)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> The children of your elect sister greet you. Amen.

[^1]: ἵνα ἀγαπῶμεν은 목적을 나타내는 부정사적 가정법절로, '구하는' 목적이 서로 사랑함에 있음을 보여준다.
[^2]: 두 ἵνα절 모두 '사랑'과 '계명'의 내용(=목적)을 풀어 설명하는 동격적 목적절이다.
[^3]: πλάνος와 ἀντίχριστος는 동일 인물(들)을 가리키며, 그리스도의 성육신 부인이 곧 적그리스도의 표지임을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα μὴ...ἀλλὰ 구문은 부정과 긍정의 목적을 대조하여 '잃지 않고 오히려 받는' 결과를 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα ᾖ πεπληρωμένη는 목적절로, 직접 대면하여 말하려는 이유가 '기쁨의 충만'에 있음을 보여준다.
