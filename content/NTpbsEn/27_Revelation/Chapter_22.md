<span class="v-num">1</span> And he showed me a pure river of water of life, bright as crystal, proceeding out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> throne of God and of the Lamb.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> In <span class="added-text">(the)</span> middle of its <span class="alt-label">street</span><span class="alt-text">(=broad way)</span>, and of the river, on this side and on that, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tree of life, producing twelve fruits, yielding its fruit according to each month; and the leaves of the tree <span class="added-text">(were)</span> for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> healing of the nations.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And no curse shall be anymore; and the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him;

<span class="v-num">4</span> and they shall see his face, and his name <span class="added-text">(shall be)</span> upon their foreheads.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And night shall not be there, and they have no need of a lamp and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> light of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sun, because <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord God gives them light; and they shall reign forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of the ages)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And he said to me, "These words <span class="added-text">(are)</span> faithful and true; and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord God of the holy prophets sent his angel to show to his servants the things which must come to pass shortly.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Behold, I come quickly. Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one keeping the words of the prophecy of this book."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And I, John, <span class="added-text">(am)</span> the one seeing these things and hearing <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; and when I heard and saw, I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel showing me these things.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he said to me, "See <span class="added-text">(that you do)</span> not <span class="added-text">(do that)</span>; for I am a fellow servant of you, and of your brothers the prophets, and of those keeping the words of this book; worship God."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And he said to me, "Do not seal the words of the prophecy of this book; for the time is near.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Let the one doing unrighteousness do unrighteousness still, and let the filthy be filthy still; and let the righteous be righteous still; and let the holy be holy still.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And behold, I come quickly, and my reward <span class="added-text">(is)</span> with me, to render to each as his work shall be.

<span class="v-num">13</span> I am the Alpha and the Omega, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end, the first and the last.[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> those doing his commandments, that their <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=right)</span> shall be <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> the tree of life, and they may enter into the city by the gates.[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But outside <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the dogs, and the sorcerers, and the fornicators, and the murderers, and the idolaters, and everyone loving and doing a lie.

<span class="v-num">16</span> I Jesus sent my angel to testify to you these things <span class="alt-label">among</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, the bright and morning star."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And the Spirit and the bride say, "Come." And let the one hearing say, "Come." And let the one thirsting come; and let the one willing take the water of life freely.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For I testify jointly to everyone hearing the words of the prophecy of this book: if anyone adds to these things, God shall add to him the plagues written in this book;[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> and if anyone takes away from the words of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> book of life, and out of the holy city, and <span class="added-text">(from)</span> the things written in this book.[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> He who testifies these things says, "Yes, I come quickly. Amen." Yes, come, Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">21</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen.

[^1]: 에스겔 47장의 성전에서 흘러나오는 생명수 강 환상을 배경으로 한 구약 암시이다.
[^2]: 창세기 2-3장의 생명나무와 에스겔 47장의 치료하는 나무 잎사귀 이미지가 결합된 구약 암시이다.
[^3]: 알파와 오메가는 헬라어 자모의 처음과 끝으로, 그리스도의 영원하심과 만유의 주권을 나타내는 자기 선언이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+미래/가정법 구문으로 생명나무에 나아가고 도시에 들어가는 것이 계명을 행하는 결과이자 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(ἐπιτιθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '누구든지 만약 더하면'이라는 열린 가정을 나타낸다. 이는 실제로 그런 일이 일어날 수도 있다는 경고성 뉘앙스를 담고 있다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀφαιρῇ)은 아직 일어나지 않은 미래의 가능한 상황을 가정하는 제3조건문이다. 실제로 일어날지 불확실하지만, 만약 그런 일이 있다면 반드시 그 결과(생명책에서 제거됨)가 뒤따른다는 엄중한 경고의 뉘앙스를 지닌다.
