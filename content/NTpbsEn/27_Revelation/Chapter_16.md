# Revelation 16

<span class="v-num">1</span> And I heard a great voice out of the temple, saying to the seven angels, "Go, and pour out the bowls of the wrath of God into the earth."

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the first went away, and poured out his bowl upon the earth; and there came a foul and evil sore upon the men having the mark of the beast, and the ones worshipping his image.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And the second angel poured out his bowl into the sea; and it became blood as of a dead <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and every living soul died in the sea.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And the third angel poured out his bowl into the rivers and into the springs of the waters; and it became blood.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And I heard the angel of the waters, saying, "You are righteous, O Lord, the one who is and who was, the Holy One, because you have judged these things;

<span class="v-num">6</span> for they poured out the blood of saints and prophets, and you have given them blood to drink; for they are worthy."

<span class="v-num">7</span> And I heard another out of the altar, saying, "Yes, O Lord God Almighty, true and righteous <span class="added-text">(are)</span> your judgments."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the fourth angel poured out his bowl upon the sun; and it was given to it to scorch men with fire;

<span class="v-num">9</span> and men were scorched with great heat, and they blasphemed the name of God, who has authority over these plagues, and they did not repent to give him glory.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the fifth angel poured out his bowl upon the throne of the beast; and his kingdom became darkened; and they gnawed their tongues from the pain,

<span class="v-num">11</span> and they blasphemed the God of heaven because of their pains and because of their sores, and did not repent of their works.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And the sixth angel poured out his bowl upon the great river Euphrates; and its water was dried up, so that the way of the kings from the rising of the sun might be prepared.[^1]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And I saw out of the mouth of the dragon, and out of the mouth of the beast, and out of the mouth of the false prophet, three unclean spirits like frogs;

<span class="v-num">14</span> for they are spirits of demons, performing signs, to go out to the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the war of that great day of God Almighty.[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> "Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one who watches, and keeps his garments, so that he may not walk naked, and they see his shame."[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he gathered them to the place called in Hebrew Armageddon.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And the seventh angel poured out his bowl into the air; and there came out a great voice from the temple of heaven, from the throne, saying, "It is done."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And there were voices, and thunders, and lightnings; and there was a great earthquake, such as had not occurred since men were upon the earth, so mighty an earthquake, so great.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the great city was split into three parts, and the cities of the nations fell; and Babylon the great was remembered before God, to give to her the cup of the wine of the fury of his wrath.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And every island fled, and mountains were not found.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And great hail, about the weight of a talent, came down out of heaven upon men; and men blasphemed God because of the plague of the hail; for its plague was exceedingly great.[^4]

---

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἑτοιμασθῇ)은 물이 마른 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^2]: ἐκπορεύεσθαι와 συναγαγεῖν은 귀신들의 표적 행함의 목적을 나타내는 부정사구문이다.
[^3]: ἵνα μὴ...περιπατῇ, βλέπωσι는 옷을 지키는 목적(부끄러움을 보이지 않기 위함)을 나타내는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^4]: καταβαίνει는 역사적 현재이나 개역체 서사 관행에 따라 과거형으로 옮겼다.
