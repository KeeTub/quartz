<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things I heard a great voice of much people in heaven, saying, "Hallelujah; the salvation and the glory and the honor and the power <span class="added-text">(belong)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord our God,

<span class="v-num">2</span> for true and righteous <span class="added-text">(are)</span> his judgments; for he judged the great harlot, who corrupted the earth with her fornication, and he avenged the blood of his servants <span class="alt-label">at</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> her hand."

<span class="v-num">3</span> And a second time they said, "Hallelujah; and her smoke goes up into the ages of the ages."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And the twenty and four elders and the four living creatures fell down and worshiped God, the one sitting on the throne, saying, "Amen; Hallelujah."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And a voice came out from the throne, saying, "Praise our God, all <span class="added-text">(you)</span> his servants, and the ones fearing him, both the small and the great."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And I heard as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of much people, and as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of many waters, and as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of mighty thunders, saying, "Hallelujah; for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord God the Almighty <span class="alt-label">reigned</span><span class="alt-text">(=has begun to reign)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Let us rejoice and be glad, and let us give the glory to him; for the marriage of the Lamb came, and his wife prepared herself."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And it was given to her that she should be clothed <span class="added-text">(in)</span> fine linen, clean and bright; for the fine linen is the righteous acts of the saints.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he said to me, "Write, Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the ones called to the supper of the marriage of the Lamb." And he said to me, "These <span class="added-text">(are the)</span> true words of God."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And I fell before his feet to worship him. And he said to me, "See <span class="added-text">(that you do)</span> not <span class="added-text">(do that)</span>; I am a fellow servant of you and of your brothers, the ones having the testimony of Jesus; worship God; for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy."[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And I saw the heaven opened, and behold, a white horse, and the one sitting on it, called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he judges and makes war.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And his eyes <span class="added-text">(were)</span> as a flame of fire, and on his head <span class="added-text">(were)</span> many diadems; having a name written, which no one knows except himself;

<span class="v-num">13</span> and <span class="added-text">(he was)</span> clothed with a garment dipped in blood; and his name is called "The Word of God."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And the armies in heaven followed him on white horses, clothed in fine linen, white and clean.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And out of his mouth goes a sharp sword, that with it he should strike the nations; and he will shepherd them with a rod of iron; and he treads the winepress of the wine of the wrath and of the anger of God the Almighty.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he has on <span class="added-text">(his)</span> garment and on his thigh a name written, "King of kings, and Lord of lords."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And I saw one angel standing in the sun; and he cried with a great voice, saying to all the birds flying in mid-heaven, "Come and gather together to the supper of the great God,

<span class="v-num">18</span> that you may eat <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of kings, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of captains, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of mighty <span class="added-text">(men)</span>, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of horses and of the ones sitting on them, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh of all, both free and bond, and small and great."[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> And I saw the beast, and the kings of the earth, and their armies, gathered together to make war with the one sitting on the horse, and with his army.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And the beast was seized, and with him the false prophet, the one having done the signs before him, by which he deceived the ones having received the mark of the beast, and the ones worshiping his image; the two were cast alive into the lake of fire, the one burning with brimstone.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the rest were killed with the sword of the one sitting on the horse, the <span class="added-text">(sword)</span> proceeding out of his mouth; and all the birds were filled from their flesh.

[^1]: ἐβασίλευσε는 부정과거로서 통치의 시작(즉위)을 강조하는 표현이다(=이제 왕이 되어 다스리기 시작하셨다).
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(περιβάληται)은 목적절로, 신부에게 세마포를 입도록 허락하신 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 예수의 증언'은 예언의 영이니, 참된 예언 사역의 핵심 내용과 능력이 예수 그리스도를 증거하는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(πατάσσῃ)은 목적절로, 검이 나오는 목적(이방을 치기 위함)을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(φάγητε)은 목적절로, 큰 잔치(초대)의 목적을 나타낸다. 즉 새들을 불러 모으는 행위의 의도된 결과가 '먹는 것'임을 강조한다.
