# Revelation 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> And I saw when the Lamb opened one of the seals, and I heard one of the four living creatures saying, as with a voice of thunder, "Come and see."

<span class="v-num">2</span> And I saw, and behold a white horse, and the one sitting on it having a bow; and a crown was given to him, and he went out conquering, and that he might conquer.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And when he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature saying, "Come and see."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And another horse went out, red; and to the one sitting on it was given to take <span class="added-text">(away)</span> peace from the earth, and that they should kill one another; and a great sword was given to him.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And when he opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature saying, "Come and see." And I saw, and behold a black horse, and the one sitting on it having a balance in his hand.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And I heard a voice in the midst of the four living creatures saying, "A choenix of wheat for a denarius, and three choenixes of barley for a denarius; and the oil and the wine do not harm."

<span class="v-num">7</span> And when he opened the fourth seal, I heard a voice of the fourth living creature saying, "Come and see."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And I saw, and behold a pale <span class="added-text">(green)</span> horse, and the one sitting on it, his name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Death, and Hades followed with him; and authority was given to them over the fourth <span class="added-text">(part)</span> of the earth, to kill with sword and with famine and with death, and by the beasts of the earth.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And when he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of the ones having been slain because of the word of God, and because of the testimony which they held,[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> and they cried with a loud voice, saying, "How long, O holy and true Master, do you not judge and avenge our blood on those dwelling on the earth?"[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And white robes were given to each of them, and it was said to them that they should rest yet a little time, until also their fellow servants and their brothers, who were about to be killed as they also <span class="added-text">(were)</span>, should be fulfilled (=complete their number).[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And I saw when he opened the sixth seal; and behold, a great earthquake occurred, and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and the stars of heaven fell to the earth, as a fig tree, shaken by a great wind, casts its unripe figs;

<span class="v-num">14</span> and heaven departed (=split apart) as a scroll being rolled up, and every mountain and island were moved out of their places;

<span class="v-num">15</span> And the kings of the earth, and the great men, and the rich, and the <span class="alt-label">chiliarchs</span><span class="alt-text">(=commanders)</span>, and the mighty, and every slave and every free <span class="added-text">(man)</span> hid themselves in the caves and in the rocks of the mountains,

<span class="v-num">16</span> and they said to the mountains and to the rocks, "Fall on us, and hide us from the face of the one sitting on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb,

<span class="v-num">17</span> for the great day of his wrath has come, and who is able to stand?"

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(νικήσῃ)은 목적절로, '이기기 위하여/이기려고'라는 뜻을 나타낸다. 그가 나아간 목적이 정복에 있음을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(σφάξωσι)은 목적절로, 붉은 말 탄 자에게 권세가 주어진 목적이 사람들로 서로 죽이게 하려는 것임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: μαρτυρίαν은 단순 '증거'가 아니라 순교로 이어진 신앙 고백을 뜻하며, 계시록 전체에서 순교자의 증언이라는 신학적 함의를 지닌다.
[^4]: δεσπότης는 κύριος보다 강한 절대적 통치자·소유주의 뉘앙스로, 순교자들이 부르짖는 대상이 만유의 절대 주권자임을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα ἀναπαύσωνται는 목적/명령 내용을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 순교자들의 안식이 정해진 기간과 정해진 수의 완성에 달려 있음을 보여준다.
