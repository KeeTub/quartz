<span class="v-num">1</span> A great sign was seen in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heaven, a woman clothed <span class="added-text">(with)</span> the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and being with child, she cried out, travailing and being in pain to give birth.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And another sign was seen in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heaven, and behold, a great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and upon his heads seven diadems;

<span class="v-num">4</span> And his tail <span class="alt-label">drew</span><span class="alt-text">(=dragged)</span> the third of the stars of the heaven, and cast them to the earth. And the dragon stood before the woman who was about to give birth, so that when she gave birth, he might devour her child.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And she gave birth <span class="added-text">(to)</span> a male son, who is about to <span class="alt-label">shepherd</span><span class="alt-text">(=rule)</span> all the nations with a rod of iron; and her child was caught up to God and to His throne.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she has a place prepared from God, so that there they might feed her a thousand two hundred <span class="added-text">(and)</span> sixty days.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And there was war in the heaven: Michael and his angels made war against the dragon; and the dragon made war, and his angels,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and they did not prevail, neither was their place found any longer in the heaven.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the great dragon was cast <span class="added-text">(down)</span>, the ancient serpent, the one called Devil, and Satan, the one deceiving the whole world; he was cast to the earth, and his angels were cast down with him.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And I heard a great voice saying in the heaven, "Now the salvation and the power and the kingdom of our God, and the authority of His Christ, has come; for the accuser of our brothers has been cast down, the one accusing them before our God day and night.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And they overcame him because of the blood of the Lamb, and because of the word of their testimony, and they did not love their <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span> unto death.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore rejoice, <span class="added-text">(you)</span> heavens, and the ones dwelling in them. Woe to the ones dwelling <span class="added-text">(on)</span> the earth and the sea, for the devil has come down to you, having great wrath, knowing that he has <span class="added-text">(only)</span> a short time."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And when the dragon saw that he was cast to the earth, he persecuted the woman who gave birth <span class="added-text">(to)</span> the male <span class="added-text">(child)</span>.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And two wings of the great eagle were given to the woman, so that she might fly into the wilderness, to her place, where she is fed there a time, and times, and half a time, from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of the serpent.[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And the serpent cast <span class="added-text">(out)</span> water like a river out of his mouth after the woman, so that he might cause her to be carried away by the river.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the earth helped the woman, and the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed up the river which the dragon cast out of his mouth.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And the dragon was angry at the woman, and went away to make war with the rest of her seed, the ones keeping the commandments of God, and having the testimony of Jesus Christ. And I stood upon the sand of the sea.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(καταφάγῃ) 목적절로, 용이 여자 앞에 서 있는 목적을 나타낸다. 아이를 삼키려는 의도된 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(τρέφωσιν)은 목적절로, 하나님이 그 곳을 예비하신 목적이 여자를 1260일 동안 먹이시기 위함임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(πέτηται)은 목적절로, 두 날개가 주어진 목적(광야로 날아가 보호받기 위함)을 나타낸다. 하나님의 섭리적 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιήσῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, 뱀이 물을 토해낸 '의도·목적'을 나타낸다. 즉 여자를 물에 휩쓸리게 하려는 사탄의 계획된 목적을 강조한다.
