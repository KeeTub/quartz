<span class="v-num">1</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave to him, to show to his servants the things which must come to pass <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> <span class="alt-label">speed</span><span class="alt-text">(=soon)</span>, and having sent through his angel he signified <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to his servant John,

<span class="v-num">2</span> who testified <span class="added-text">(to)</span> the word of God and the testimony of Jesus Christ, even as many things as he saw.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one reading, and the ones hearing the words of the prophecy, and keeping the things written in it, for the time <span class="added-text">(is)</span> near.

<span class="v-num">4</span> John, to the seven churches which are in Asia: Grace to you and peace from the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> who is and who was and who is coming, and from the seven Spirits which are before his throne,[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn from the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. To him who loved us and washed us from our sins <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> his blood,[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> and made us kings and priests to his God and Father, to him <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory and the <span class="alt-label">might</span><span class="alt-text">(=power)</span> into the ages of the ages. Amen.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Behold, he comes with the clouds, and every eye will see him, even those who pierced him, and all the tribes of the earth will mourn over him. Yes, Amen.[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> "I am the Alpha and the Omega, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end," says the Lord, "the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> who is and who was and who is coming, the <span class="alt-label">Almighty</span><span class="alt-text">(=All-powerful one)</span>."[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> I, John, your brother and fellow partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and <span class="alt-label">patience</span><span class="alt-text">(=endurance)</span> of Jesus Christ, was in the island called Patmos, because of the word of God and because of the testimony of Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">10</span> I was in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit on the Lord's day, and I heard behind me a great voice <span class="alt-label">like</span><span class="alt-text">(=as)</span> of a trumpet,

<span class="v-num">11</span> saying, "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the first and the last," and "What you see, write in a book, and send <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the seven churches which are in Asia: to Ephesus, and to Smyrna, and to Pergamos, and to Thyatira, and to Sardis, and to Philadelphia, and to Laodicea."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And I turned to see the voice which spoke with me, and having turned, I saw seven golden lampstands,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of the seven lampstands <span class="added-text">(one)</span> like <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of Man, clothed with a garment reaching to the feet, and girded about the <span class="alt-label">breasts</span><span class="alt-text">(=chest)</span> with a golden belt;[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And his head and hairs <span class="added-text">(were)</span> white like white wool, as snow, and his eyes <span class="added-text">(were)</span> as a flame of fire;

<span class="v-num">15</span> and his feet <span class="added-text">(were)</span> like fine <span class="alt-label">bronze</span><span class="alt-text">(=burnished brass)</span>, as having been refined in a furnace, and his voice <span class="added-text">(was)</span> as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of many waters;

<span class="v-num">16</span> and having in his right hand seven stars, and out of his mouth a sharp two-edged sword going forth, and his face <span class="added-text">(was)</span> as the sun shines in its <span class="alt-label">power</span><span class="alt-text">(=strength)</span>.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And when I saw him, I fell at his feet as <span class="added-text">(one)</span> dead. And he laid his right hand on me, saying to me, "Do not fear. I am the first and the last,

<span class="v-num">18</span> and the living one; and I became dead, and behold, I am living into the ages of the ages. Amen. And I have the keys of <span class="alt-label">Hades</span><span class="alt-text">(=hell)</span> and of death.[^6]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Write the things which you saw, and the things which are, and the things which are about to come to pass after these things;

<span class="v-num">20</span> The mystery of the seven stars which you saw upon my right <span class="added-text">(hand)</span>, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands which you saw are seven churches."

[^1]: '있는 이, 계셨던 이, 오실 이'는 출애굽기 3:14의 '나는 스스로 있는 자'를 반영하는 하나님의 이름 표현이다.
[^2]: πρωτότοκος(장자)는 출생 순서가 아니라 부활을 통해 얻으신 으뜸·상속의 지위를 가리킨다.
[^3]: 다니엘 7:13(구름 타고 오심)과 스가랴 12:10(그들이 찌른 자를 봄)을 결합한 인유이다.
[^4]: 알파와 오메가는 헬라어 자모의 처음과 끝으로, 만유의 시작과 끝 되심을 나타내는 메리즘(포괄 표현)이다.
[^5]: '사람의 아들 같은 이'는 다니엘 7:13의 인자(人子) 형상을 인유하여 그리스도의 신적 권위를 나타낸다.
[^6]: 음부와 사망의 열쇠는 죽음과 그 너머의 영역을 다스리는 부활하신 그리스도의 절대적 권세를 뜻한다.
