<span class="v-num">1</span> And I saw another strong angel coming down out of heaven, clothed with a cloud, and a rainbow <span class="added-text">(was)</span> upon his head, and his face <span class="added-text">(was)</span> as the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire;

<span class="v-num">2</span> and he had in his hand a little scroll opened; and he set his right foot upon the sea, and the left upon the earth,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and he cried with a great voice, as a lion roars; and when he cried, the seven thunders uttered their <span class="added-text">(own)</span> voices.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when the seven thunders had uttered their <span class="added-text">(own)</span> voices, I was about to write <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; and I heard a voice from heaven saying to me, "Seal the things which the seven thunders spoke, and do not write these things."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the angel, whom I saw standing upon the sea and upon the earth, lifted up his hand to heaven,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and swore by him who lives forever and ever, who created heaven and the things in it, and the earth and the things in it, and the sea and the things in it, "That there should be time no longer;[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> but in the days of the voice of the seventh angel, when he is about to sound the trumpet, the mystery of God should be <span class="alt-label">finished</span><span class="alt-text">(=completed)</span>, as he announced the gospel to his <span class="added-text">(own)</span> servants the prophets."[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the voice which I heard from heaven <span class="added-text">(spoke)</span> again with me, and said, "Go, take the little scroll opened in the hand of the angel standing upon the sea and upon the earth."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And I went to the angel, saying to him, "Give me the little scroll." And he said to me, "Take <span class="added-text">(it)</span> and eat it up; and it will make your belly bitter, but in your mouth it will be sweet as honey."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And I took the little scroll out of the hand of the angel, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth as honey, sweet; and when I had eaten it, my belly was made bitter.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And he said to me, "You must prophesy again <span class="alt-label">concerning</span><span class="alt-text">(=about)</span> many peoples and nations and tongues and kings."
