<span class="v-num">1</span> And the fifth angel sounded <span class="added-text">(the)</span> trumpet, and I saw a star fallen from heaven to the earth, and <span class="added-text">(to)</span> it was given the key of the well of the abyss.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And he opened the well of the abyss, and smoke went up out of the well like <span class="added-text">(the)</span> smoke of a great furnace, and the sun and the air were darkened <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> the smoke of the well.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And out of the smoke came forth locusts into the earth, and power was given to them, as the scorpions of the earth have power;

<span class="v-num">4</span> and it was said to them that they should not harm the grass of the earth, nor any green thing, nor any tree, but only the men who do not have the seal of God on their foreheads.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And it was given to them that they should not kill them, but that they should be tormented five months; and their torment <span class="added-text">(was)</span> as the torment of a scorpion, when it strikes a man.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And in those days men will seek death, and will not find it; and they will desire to die, and death will flee from them.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And the shapes of the locusts <span class="added-text">(were)</span> like horses prepared for war, and on their heads <span class="added-text">(were)</span> as it were crowns like gold, and their faces <span class="added-text">(were)</span> as the faces of men;

<span class="v-num">8</span> and they had hair like the hair of women, and their teeth were as <span class="added-text">(the teeth)</span> of lions;

<span class="v-num">9</span> and they had breastplates like breastplates of iron, and the sound of their wings <span class="added-text">(was)</span> as the sound of chariots of many horses running to war.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And they have tails like scorpions, and <span class="alt-label">stings</span><span class="alt-text">(=goads)</span> were in their tails; and their power <span class="added-text">(was)</span> to harm men five months.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And they have over them <span class="added-text">(as)</span> king the angel of the abyss; his name in Hebrew <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Abaddon, and in the Greek he has <span class="added-text">(the)</span> name Apollyon.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> The first woe has passed; behold, two woes are still coming after these things.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And the sixth angel sounded <span class="added-text">(the)</span> trumpet, and I heard one voice from the four horns of the golden altar which <span class="added-text">(is)</span> before God,

<span class="v-num">14</span> saying to the sixth angel who had the trumpet, "Loose the four angels who are bound at the great river Euphrates."

<span class="v-num">15</span> And the four angels were loosed, who had been prepared for the hour and day and month and year, to kill the third <span class="alt-label"><span class="added-text">(part)</span></span><span class="alt-text">(=one third)</span> of men.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the number of the armies of the horsemen <span class="added-text">(was)</span> two myriads of myriads; and I heard their number.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And thus I saw the horses in the vision, and those sitting on them, having breastplates of fire and <span class="alt-label">hyacinth</span><span class="alt-text">(=dark blue)</span> and <span class="alt-label">brimstone</span><span class="alt-text">(=sulfur)</span>; and the heads of the horses <span class="added-text">(were)</span> as the heads of lions, and out of their mouths proceeded fire and smoke and brimstone.

<span class="v-num">18</span> By these three <span class="added-text">(plagues)</span> the third <span class="alt-label"><span class="added-text">(part)</span></span><span class="alt-text">(=one third)</span> of men were killed, by the fire and by the smoke and by the brimstone, which proceeded out of their mouths.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For their power is in their mouth; for their tails <span class="added-text">(are)</span> like serpents, having heads, and with them they harm.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And the rest of men, who were not killed by these plagues, did not repent of the works of their hands, so as not to worship demons, and idols of gold and of silver and of brass and of stone and of wood, which can neither see, nor hear, nor walk;[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> and they did not repent of their murders, nor of their <span class="alt-label">sorceries</span><span class="alt-text">(=magic)</span>, nor of their fornication, nor of their thefts.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀδικήσωσι)은 명령의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, '~하지 않도록'이라는 금지의 목적을 표현한다. 메뚜기들에게 주어진 제한 명령의 실제 내용을 이 절이 담고 있다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀποκτείνωσιν, βασανισθῶσι) 목적절로, 메뚜기들에게 주어진 권세의 목적/한계를 밝힘: 죽이는 것이 아니라 다섯 달간 괴롭히는 것이 하나님이 허락하신 목적임을 보여준다.
[^3]: 아바돈(히브리어)과 아볼루온(헬라어)은 둘 다 '파괴자'라는 뜻으로, 무저갱에서 나온 세력의 본질이 파괴임을 드러낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα ἀποκτείνωσι는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 미리 정해진 때에 이루어질 파괴의 목적을 밝힌다.
[^5]: οὔτε μετενόησαν...ἵνα μὴ προσκυνήσωσι는 이중 부정 목적절로, '회개하지 않아 (결국) 경배하기를 그치지 않았다'는 뜻이 되어 우상숭배의 지속을 강조한다.
