# Revelation 17

<span class="v-num">1</span> And one of the seven angels having the seven bowls came and spoke with me, saying to me, "Come here, I will show you the judgment of the great harlot, the one sitting upon the many waters,

<span class="v-num">2</span> with whom the kings of the earth committed fornication, and those dwelling on the earth were made drunk from the wine of her fornication."

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he carried me away into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wilderness in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">Spirit</span><span class="alt-text">(=spirit)</span>; and I saw a woman sitting upon a scarlet beast, full of names of blasphemy, having seven heads and ten horns.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And the woman was clothed with purple and scarlet, and adorned with gold and precious stone and pearls, having a golden cup in her hand, full of abominations and the uncleanness of her fornication,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and upon her forehead a name written, "Mystery, Babylon the Great, the Mother of Harlots and of the Abominations of the Earth."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And I saw the woman drunk from the blood of the saints, and from the blood of the martyrs of Jesus; and I marveled, having seen her, with great marvel.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And the angel said to me, "Why did you marvel? I will tell you the mystery of the woman, and of the beast carrying her, the one having the seven heads and the ten horns.

<span class="v-num">8</span> The beast which you saw, was, and is not, and is about to come up out of the abyss, and to go into destruction; and those dwelling upon the earth, whose names have not been written on the book of life from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, will marvel, seeing the beast, that it was, and is not, and yet <span class="alt-label">is</span><span class="alt-text">(=will be present)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Here <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the mind having wisdom. The seven heads are seven mountains, where the woman sits upon them.

<span class="v-num">10</span> and they are seven kings: five have fallen, and one is, the other has not yet come; and when he comes, he must remain a little while.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the beast which was, and is not, is himself an eighth <span class="added-text">(king)</span>, and is of the seven, and goes into destruction.[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And the ten horns which you saw are ten kings, who have not yet received a kingdom, but receive authority as kings for one hour with the beast.

<span class="v-num">13</span> These have one mind, and give their power and authority to the beast.

<span class="v-num">14</span> These will make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb will overcome them, for he is Lord of lords and King of kings, and those with him are called and chosen and faithful."

<span class="v-num">15</span> And he says to me, "The waters which you saw, where the harlot sits, are peoples and multitudes, and nations and tongues.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the ten horns which you saw upon the beast, these will hate the harlot, and will make her desolate and naked, and will eat her flesh, and will burn her up with fire.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For God gave into their hearts to perform his purpose, and to perform one purpose, and to give their kingdom to the beast, until the words of God should be fulfilled.[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the woman whom you saw is the great city, which has (=holds) dominion over the kings of the earth."

[^1]: ἦν(과거)-οὐκ ἔστι(현재)-καίπερ ἔστιν(현재)의 시제 대조는 짐승의 존재-부재-재출현을 그리스도의 영원한 존재(1:4,8)와 대조적으로 패러디한 표현이다.
[^2]: 여덟째 왕(ὄγδοος)이 일곱 중 하나로도 불리는 것은 그가 이전 일곱 왕의 계열에 속하면서도 종말론적으로 완결된(8은 새 시작·완결의 수) 존재임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἄχρι τελεσθῇ(가정법)는 아직 성취되지 않은 미래 사건이 반드시 이루어질 때까지라는 확실한 목적·기한을 나타낸다.
