<span class="v-num">1</span> And I saw a beast coming up out of the sea, having seven heads and ten horns, and on its horns ten <span class="alt-label">crowns</span><span class="alt-text">(=diadems)</span>, and on its heads a name of blasphemy.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the beast which I saw was like a leopard, and its feet as <span class="added-text">(feet)</span> of a bear, and its mouth as a mouth of a lion. And the dragon gave to it his power, and his throne, and great authority.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I saw one of its heads as having been slain to death; and the <span class="alt-label">plague</span><span class="alt-text">(=wound)</span> of its death was healed, and the whole earth marveled <span class="alt-label">after</span><span class="alt-text">(=followed after)</span> the beast.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And they worshiped the dragon who gave authority to the beast, and they worshiped the beast, saying, "Who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> like the beast? Who is able to make war with it?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> And there was given to it a mouth speaking great things and blasphemies; and authority was given to it to <span class="alt-label">act</span><span class="alt-text">(=continue)</span> forty-two months.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And it opened its mouth for blasphemy toward God, to blaspheme his name, and his tabernacle, and those dwelling in heaven.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And it was given to it to make war with the saints and to overcome them; and authority was given to it over every tribe and tongue and nation.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And all who dwell on the earth will worship it, whose names have not been written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> If anyone has an ear, let him hear.

<span class="v-num">10</span> If anyone gathers <span class="added-text">(into)</span> captivity, he goes into captivity; if anyone kills with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sword, he must be killed with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And I saw another beast coming up out of the earth, and it had two horns like a lamb, and it spoke as a dragon.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And it exercised all the authority of the first beast in its <span class="alt-label">presence</span><span class="alt-text">(=before it)</span>; and it made the earth and those dwelling in it worship the first beast, whose <span class="alt-label">plague</span><span class="alt-text">(=wound)</span> of death had been healed.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And it worked great signs, so that it even made fire come down from heaven to the earth in the sight of men.[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And it deceived those dwelling on the earth, because of the signs which were given to it to do in the sight of the beast, saying to those dwelling on the earth to make an image to the beast which <span class="alt-label">has</span><span class="alt-text">(=had)</span> the wound of the sword and lived.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And it was given to it to give <span class="alt-label">breath</span><span class="alt-text">(=spirit)</span> to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast should even speak, and should cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And it caused all, the small and the great, and the rich and the poor, and the free and the <span class="alt-label">slaves</span><span class="alt-text">(=bondmen)</span>, that they should give them a mark on their right hand, or on their foreheads,[^6]

<span class="v-num">17</span> and that no one should be able to buy or sell, except the one having the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of its name.[^7]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Here is wisdom. Let the one having <span class="alt-label">understanding</span><span class="alt-text">(=mind)</span> calculate the number of the beast; for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> number of a man, and his number is <span class="alt-label">666</span><span class="alt-text">(=χξς)</span>.[^8]

[^1]: '창세로부터'(ἀπὸ καταβολῆς κόσμου)는 '죽임당한'을 수식할 수도, '기록되지 못한'을 수식할 수도 있어 그리스도의 예정된 죽음 또는 예정된 택함 두 해석이 모두 가능하다.
[^2]: 포로와 칼에 관한 표현은 구약의 심판 선언(예: 렘 15:2, 마 26:52)을 반영한 것으로 보인다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(προσκυνήσωσι)은 목적절로, 땅과 그 거민들을 강권하는 행위의 목적/의도가 첫째 짐승 숭배임을 나타낸다. 둘째 짐승의 활동 전체가 이 우상숭배를 이끌어내려는 뚜렷한 목적성을 지님을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῇ)은 목적절로, 큰 이적을 행하는 목적/결과가 사람들 앞에서 불을 하늘에서 내려오게 하는 데 있음을 보여준다. 거짓 표적의 의도된 효과를 강조하는 구문이다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(λαλήσῃ, ποιήσῃ, ἀποκτανθῶσι)이 이끄는 목적절로, 짐승의 형상에게 생기를 부여한 목적(말하게 함)과 그 결과로 의도된 목적(경배하지 않는 자를 죽게 함)을 나타낸다. 이는 사탄적 권세가 추구하는 구체적 의도를 강조하는 표현이다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(δώσῃ) 목적절로, 짐승이 모든 사람에게 표를 받게 하려는 의도를 나타낸다. 강제적 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(δύνηται)은 앞선 행위(표를 받게 함)의 목적을 나타내는 목적절로, '아무도 매매할 수 없게 하려는' 의도를 강조한다.
[^8]: 고대에는 문자가 숫자로도 쓰였으므로(게마트리아), 666은 특정 인물의 이름을 문자수로 환산한 수를 가리키는 것으로 이해된다.
