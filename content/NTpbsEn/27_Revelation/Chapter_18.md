<span class="v-num">2</span> And he cried out with might, with a great voice, saying, "Fallen, fallen <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Babylon the great, and she has become a dwelling place of demons, and a <span class="alt-label">haunt</span><span class="alt-text">(=prison)</span> of every unclean spirit, and a <span class="alt-label">haunt</span><span class="alt-text">(=prison)</span> of every unclean and hated bird,

<span class="v-num">7</span> As much as she glorified herself and lived <span class="alt-label">luxuriously</span><span class="alt-text">(=sensuously)</span>, give her that much torment and <span class="alt-label">grief</span><span class="alt-text">(=mourning)</span>; for she says in her heart, 'I sit <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a queen, and I am not a widow, and I will never see <span class="alt-label">grief</span><span class="alt-text">(=mourning)</span>.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore in one day her plagues will come, death and mourning and famine; and she will be burned up with fire, for strong <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> Lord God who judges her.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the kings of the earth, who committed fornication and lived luxuriously with her, will weep and <span class="alt-label">mourn</span><span class="alt-text">(=beat their breasts)</span> over her, when they see the smoke of her burning,

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the merchants of the earth weep and mourn over her, because no one buys their <span class="alt-label">cargo</span><span class="alt-text">(=merchandise)</span> anymore,

<span class="v-num">12</span> <span class="alt-label">cargo</span><span class="alt-text">(=merchandise)</span> of gold, and silver, and precious stone, and pearl, and fine linen, and purple, and silk, and scarlet; and every <span class="added-text">(kind of)</span> thyine wood, and every vessel of ivory, and every vessel of most precious wood, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> bronze, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> iron, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> marble,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and cinnamon, and incense, and <span class="alt-label">myrrh</span><span class="alt-text">(=perfume)</span>, and frankincense, and wine, and oil, and fine flour, and wheat, and cattle, and sheep, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> horses, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> chariots, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> <span class="alt-label">bodies</span><span class="alt-text">(=slaves)</span>, and souls of men.

<span class="v-num">20</span> "Rejoice over her, <span class="added-text">(O)</span> heaven, and <span class="added-text">(you)</span> holy apostles and prophets, for God has judged your <span class="alt-label">judgment</span><span class="alt-text">(=case)</span> <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=against)</span> her."[^2]
