<span class="v-num">1</span> And I saw on the right <span class="added-text">(hand)</span> of the One sitting on the throne a <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span> written inside and on the <span class="alt-label">back</span><span class="alt-text">(=outside)</span>, sealed with seven seals.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a great voice, "Who is worthy to open the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, and to loose its seals?"[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And no one was able, in the heaven, nor on the earth, nor under the earth, to open the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, nor to look on it.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And I wept much, because no one worthy was found to open and to read the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, nor to look on it.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And one of the elders said to me, "Do not weep; behold, the Lion who is of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has overcome, to open the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, and to loose its seven seals."[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And I saw, and behold, in the midst of the throne and of the four living creatures, and in the midst of the elders, a Lamb standing as <span class="alt-label">slain</span><span class="alt-text">(=having been slain)</span>, having seven horns and seven eyes, which are the seven Spirits of God sent forth into all the earth.[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And He came, and took the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span> out of the right <span class="added-text">(hand)</span> of the One sitting on the throne.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And when He took the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, the four living creatures and the twenty-four elders fell down before the Lamb, each having harps, and golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And they sang a new song, saying, "You are worthy to take the <span class="alt-label">book</span><span class="alt-text">(=scroll)</span>, and to open its seals, because You were slain, and purchased us for God <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> Your blood, out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation,[^5]

<span class="v-num">10</span> and made us <span class="alt-label">kings</span><span class="alt-text">(=a kingdom)</span> and priests to our God; and we shall reign on the earth."[^6]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And I saw, and I heard <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of many angels around the throne and the living creatures and the elders; and <span class="added-text">(their)</span> number was ten thousands of ten thousands, and thousands of thousands,

<span class="v-num">12</span> saying with a great voice, "Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive the power, and riches, and wisdom, and <span class="alt-label">might</span><span class="alt-text">(=strength)</span>, and honor, and glory, and blessing."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And every creature which is in heaven, and on the earth, and under the earth, and <span class="added-text">(those)</span> which are on the sea, and all things in them, I heard saying, "To the One sitting on the throne and to the Lamb <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the blessing, and the honor, and the glory, and the <span class="alt-label">might</span><span class="alt-text">(=dominion)</span>, forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of the ages)</span>."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And the four living creatures said, "Amen," and the twenty-four elders fell down and worshiped Him who lives forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of the ages)</span>.

[^1]: βιβλίον(책/두루마리)이 안팎으로 기록되고 일곱 인으로 봉인된 것은 하나님의 온전한 구속 계획이 감추어져 있음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἄξιος(합당한)는 이후 어린 양의 자격을 대조적으로 부각시키는 핵심어이다.
[^3]: ἐνίκησεν(이기셨다)이 부정과거로 쓰여 이미 확정된 승리를 나타내며, 이는 이어지는 어린 양의 자격 부여의 근거가 된다.
[^4]: ἑστηκὸς(서 있는, 완료 분사)와 ἐσφαγμένον(죽임 당한, 완료 분사)이 함께 쓰여, 죽임 당하였으나 살아 서 있는 어린 양의 역설적 승리를 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἐσφάγης(죽임 당하셨다, 부정과거)는 십자가 사건의 역사적 확실성을, ᾠδὴν καινὴν(새 노래)은 구속의 새로움을 강조한다.
[^6]: βασιλεύσομεν(다스릴 것이다)은 TR 사본에 따라 1인칭 복수 미래로 읽어 '우리가 다스리리라'로 옮겼으며, 일부 사본은 3인칭 복수(그들이 다스리리라)로 읽는다.
