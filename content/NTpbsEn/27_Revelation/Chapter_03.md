# Revelation 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> And to the angel of the church in Sardis write, 'These things says the one having the spirits of God and the seven stars: "I know your works, that you have the name that you live, and you are dead."'

<span class="v-num">2</span> Be watching, and strengthen the remaining things which are about to die; for I have not found your works fulfilled before God.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Remember therefore how you have received and heard, and keep <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and repent. If therefore you do not watch, I will come upon you as a thief, and you will not know what hour I will come upon you.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> You have a few names even in Sardis, who did not defile their garments; and they will walk with me in white, for they are worthy.

<span class="v-num">5</span> The one who overcomes, this one will be clothed in white garments; and I will not blot out his name from the book of life, and I will confess his name before my Father and before his angels.

<span class="v-num">6</span> The one having an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And to the angel of the church in Philadelphia write, 'These things says the holy one, the true one, the one having the key of David, the one who opens and no one shuts, and shuts and no one opens:'

<span class="v-num">8</span> I know your works; behold, I have given before you an opened door, and no one is able to shut it; for you have a little power, and have kept my word, and have not denied my name.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Behold, I give <span class="added-text">(some)</span> from the synagogue of Satan, of those saying themselves to be Jews, and they are not, but lie. Behold, I will make them come and worship before your feet, and know that I have loved you.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Because you have kept the word of my patience, I also will keep you from the hour of trial that is about to come upon the whole world, to try those dwelling upon the earth.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Behold, I come quickly; hold fast what you have, that no one take your crown.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> The one who overcomes, I will make him a pillar in the temple of my God, and he shall no more go out; and I will write upon him the name of my God, and the name of the city of my God, the new Jerusalem, which comes down out of heaven from my God, and my new name.

<span class="v-num">13</span> The one having an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And to the angel of the church of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Laodiceans write, 'These things says the Amen, the faithful and true witness, the beginning of the creation of God:'

<span class="v-num">15</span> I know your works, that you are neither cold nor hot. I would that you were cold or hot.

<span class="v-num">16</span> So then because you are lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I am about to vomit you out of my mouth.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Because you say, 'I am rich, and have become wealthy, and have need of nothing,' and do not know that you are the wretched one and pitiable, and poor and blind and naked;

<span class="v-num">18</span> I counsel you to buy from me gold refined by fire, that you may be rich, and white garments, that you may be clothed, and that the shame of your nakedness may not be revealed; and anoint your eyes with eye salve, that you may see.[^4]

<span class="v-num">19</span> As many as I love, I rebuke and chasten. Be zealous therefore, and repent.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Behold, I stand at the door and knock; if anyone hears my voice, and opens the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> The one who overcomes, I will give to him to sit with me on my throne, as I also overcame, and sat down with my Father on his throne.

<span class="v-num">22</span> The one having an ear, let him hear what the Spirit says to the churches.

[^1]: ἐὰν+가정법(3급 조건문)으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다: 깨어 있지 않으면 도둑같이 임하리라는 경고.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절: 그들로 하여금 와서 경배하게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절: 면류관을 빼앗기지 않게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절이 세 번 이어져 부요함·옷 입음·봄이라는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법(φιλῶ)이 관계대명사 ὅσους와 결합되어 '내가 사랑하는 자는 누구든지'라는 불특정 다수를 포괄하는 조건적 관계절을 이룬다. 미래에 반복적으로 실현될 일반적 사실을 나타내며, 예외 없이 모든 사랑받는 자에게 적용되는 원리임을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(3급 조건문)으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성—문을 열면 그리스도께서 들어가시리라는 조건—을 나타낸다.
