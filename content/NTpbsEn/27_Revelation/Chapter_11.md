<span class="v-num">1</span> And there was given to me a reed like a rod, saying, "Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and the ones worshiping in it,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and the court that is within the temple, cast <span class="added-text">(it)</span> out, and do not measure it, because it was given to the <span class="alt-label">nations</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gentiles)</span>, and they will tread the holy city <span class="added-text">(for)</span> forty-two months.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I will give <span class="added-text">(authority)</span> to my two witnesses, and they will prophesy a thousand two hundred <span class="added-text">(and)</span> sixty days, clothed in sackcloth."

<span class="v-num">4</span> These are the two olive trees and the two lampstands standing before the God of the earth.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And if anyone <span class="alt-label">wants</span><span class="alt-text">(=desires)</span> to harm them, fire goes out of their mouth and devours their enemies; and if anyone wants to harm them, thus it is necessary <span class="added-text">(for)</span> him to be killed.

<span class="v-num">6</span> These have <span class="alt-label">power</span><span class="alt-text">(=authority)</span> to shut the heaven, so that rain would not fall in the days of their prophecy; and they have power over the waters, to turn them into blood, and to strike the earth with every plague, as often as they might <span class="alt-label">want</span><span class="alt-text">(=desire)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And when they finish their testimony, the beast that comes up out of the abyss will make war with them, and will overcome them, and will kill them.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And their corpses <span class="added-text">(will lie)</span> on the <span class="alt-label">street</span><span class="alt-text">(=broad way)</span> of the great city, which is called spiritually Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And <span class="added-text">(some)</span> from the peoples and tribes and tongues and nations will see their corpses three days and a half, and they will not allow their corpses to be put into tombs.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the ones dwelling on the earth will rejoice over them, and will be glad, and will send gifts to one another, because these two prophets tormented the ones dwelling on the earth.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And after the three days and a half, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood on their feet, and great fear fell upon the ones <span class="alt-label">watching</span><span class="alt-text">(=beholding)</span> them.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And they heard a great voice from heaven saying to them, "Come up here." And they went up into heaven in the cloud, and their enemies <span class="alt-label">watched</span><span class="alt-text">(=beheld)</span> them.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And in that hour there was a great earthquake, and the tenth <span class="added-text">(part)</span> of the city fell, and in the earthquake seven thousand names of men were killed; and the rest became afraid, and gave glory to the God of heaven.

<span class="v-num">14</span> The second woe has passed; behold, the third woe comes quickly.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And the seventh angel sounded <span class="added-text">(the trumpet)</span>, and there were great voices in heaven, saying, "The kingdoms of the world have become <span class="added-text">(the kingdom)</span> of our Lord, and of his Christ, and he will reign into the ages of the ages."[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the twenty-four elders sitting before God on their thrones fell on their faces, and worshiped God,

<span class="v-num">17</span> saying, "We give thanks to you, Lord God Almighty, the one who is and who was and who is coming, because you have taken your great power, and reigned.

<span class="v-num">18</span> and the nations were <span class="alt-label">angry</span><span class="alt-text">(=enraged)</span>, and your wrath came, and the time of the dead to be judged, and to give the reward to your servants the prophets and the saints and the ones fearing your name, the small and the great, and to destroy the ones destroying the earth."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the temple of God in heaven was opened, and the ark of his covenant appeared in his temple; and there were lightnings, and voices, and thunders, and an earthquake, and great hail.
