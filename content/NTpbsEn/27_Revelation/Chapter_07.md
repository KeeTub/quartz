<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, so that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wind might not blow upon the earth, nor upon the sea, nor upon any tree.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And I saw another angel coming up from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> rising of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sun, having <span class="added-text">(the)</span> seal of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God; and he cried with a great voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to harm the earth and the sea,

<span class="v-num">3</span> saying, "Do not harm the earth, nor the sea, nor the trees, until we <span class="alt-label">seal</span><span class="alt-text">(=have sealed)</span> the servants of our God upon their foreheads."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And I heard the number of those having been sealed: a hundred <span class="added-text">(and)</span> forty-four thousand, having been sealed out of every tribe of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sons of Israel.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Judah, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Reuben, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Gad, twelve thousand having been sealed;

<span class="v-num">6</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Asher, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Naphtali, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Manasseh, twelve thousand having been sealed;

<span class="v-num">7</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Simeon, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Levi, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Issachar, twelve thousand having been sealed;

<span class="v-num">8</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Zebulun, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Joseph, twelve thousand having been sealed; of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Benjamin, twelve thousand having been sealed.

<span class="v-num">9</span> After these things I saw, and behold, a great multitude which no one was able to number, out of every nation and tribes and peoples and tongues, standing before the throne and before the Lamb, clothed <span class="added-text">(in)</span> white robes, and palm branches in their hands;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and crying with a great voice, saying, "The salvation <span class="added-text">(belongs)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> one sitting upon the throne of our God, and to the Lamb."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And all the angels stood around the throne and the elders and the four living creatures, and they fell before the throne upon their faces, and worshiped God,

<span class="v-num">12</span> saying, "Amen: the blessing and the glory and the wisdom and the thanksgiving and the honor and the power and the strength <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to our God forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of the ages)</span>. Amen."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And one of the elders answered, saying to me, "These, the ones clothed <span class="added-text">(in)</span> the white robes, who are they, and from where did they come?"

<span class="v-num">14</span> And I have said to him, "My lord, you know." And he said to me, "These are the ones coming out of the great tribulation, and they washed their robes, and made their robes white in the blood of the Lamb.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Because of this they are before the throne of God, and they serve him day and night in his temple; and the one sitting upon the throne will spread <span class="added-text">(his)</span> tabernacle over them.

<span class="v-num">16</span> They will not hunger anymore, nor will they thirst anymore, nor will the sun fall upon them at all, nor any heat;

<span class="v-num">17</span> because the Lamb <span class="added-text">(who is)</span> in the midst of the throne will shepherd them, and will guide them to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living fountains of waters; and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes."
