# 2 Peter 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> This already, beloved, <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> second letter I write to you, in which I stir up your sincere mind by way of reminder,

<span class="v-num">2</span> to remember the words spoken before by the holy prophets, and the commandment of us the apostles, of the Lord and Savior:

<span class="v-num">3</span> knowing this first, that scoffers will come in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> last of the days, walking according to their own lusts,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and saying, "Where is the promise of his coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all things continue thus from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning of creation."

<span class="v-num">5</span> For this they willingly are ignorant of, that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens existed long ago, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> earth having been formed out of water and through water, by the word of God,

<span class="v-num">6</span> through which the world that then existed, having been flooded with water, perished;[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> but the heavens and the earth that now exist are stored up by the same word, kept for fire unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But let not this one thing escape you, beloved, that one day with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> The Lord is not slow concerning his promise, as some count slowness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But the day of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord will come as a thief in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> night, in which the heavens will pass away with a roar, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elements being burned will be dissolved, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> earth and the works in it will be burned up.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Since then all these things are to be dissolved, what manner <span class="added-text">(of persons)</span> ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness,

<span class="v-num">12</span> looking for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens being on fire will be dissolved, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elements burning will melt?

<span class="v-num">13</span> But according to his promise we look for new heavens and a new earth, in which righteousness dwells.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Therefore, beloved, looking for these things, be diligent to be found by him in peace, without spot and blameless,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and count the longsuffering of our Lord <span class="added-text">(as)</span> salvation, even as also our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given to him, wrote to you,

<span class="v-num">16</span> as also in all <span class="added-text">(his)</span> letters, speaking in them concerning these things, in which are some things hard to understand, which the unlearned and unstable twist, as <span class="added-text">(they do)</span> also the rest of the scriptures, to their own destruction.

<span class="v-num">17</span> You therefore, beloved, knowing <span class="added-text">(this)</span> beforehand, watch, lest being carried away together with the error of the lawless, you fall from your own steadfastness;[^6]

<span class="v-num">18</span> but grow in grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To him <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory both now and to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of eternity. Amen.

[^1]: 창세기 대홍수 사건에 대한 암시로, 노아 시대 심판을 종말 심판의 예표로 제시한다.
[^2]: 시편 90:4을 인용하여 주(님)의 시간 개념이 인간의 시간과 다름을 논증한다.
[^3]: βραδύνει는 현재시제로 지속적 상태를 나타내어 주(님)의 성품이 변함없이 더디지 않으심을 강조한다.
[^4]: TR은 κατακαήσεται(불타 없어지리라)로 읽어 세상의 완전한 소멸을 강조하며, 이는 후대 비평본의 εὑρεθήσεται(드러나리라)와 다른 종말론적 뉘앙스를 지닌다.
[^5]: 이사야 65:17, 66:22을 배경으로 하여 종말론적 새 창조의 소망을 제시한다.
[^6]: ἵνα μὴ + 가정법(συναπαχθέντες...ἐκπέσητε)은 부정 목적절로, '떨어져 나가지 않도록'이라는 경계의 목적을 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않은 위험을 미리 대비하라는 권면의 뉘앙스이다.
