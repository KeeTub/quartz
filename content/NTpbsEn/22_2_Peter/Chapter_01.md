# 2 Peter 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Simeon Peter, a servant and apostle of Jesus Christ, to those who have obtained a faith equally precious as ours in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteousness of our God and Savior Jesus Christ:

<span class="v-num">2</span> Grace to you and peace be multiplied in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of God, and of Jesus our Lord.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> As His divine power has granted to us all things pertaining to life and godliness, through the full knowledge of the one who called us through glory and <span class="alt-label">virtue</span><span class="alt-text">(=excellence)</span>,[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> through which he has granted to us the greatest and precious promises, that through these you may become partakers of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And for this very <span class="added-text">(reason)</span>, having added all diligence, supply in your faith virtue, and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> virtue knowledge,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> knowledge self-control, and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> self-control <span class="alt-label">patience</span><span class="alt-text">(=endurance)</span>, and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> patience godliness,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> godliness brotherly love, and in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> brotherly love love.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For these things being present in you and abounding, render <span class="added-text">(you)</span> neither idle nor unfruitful unto the full knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ;

<span class="v-num">9</span> For <span class="added-text">(he)</span> in whom these things are not present is blind, being <span class="alt-label">nearsighted</span><span class="alt-text">(=short-sighted)</span>, having forgotten the purification of his <span class="alt-label">old</span><span class="alt-text">(=former)</span> sins.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Therefore, brothers, be the more diligent to make your calling and election sure; for doing these things, you will never stumble at any time.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For so, an entrance into the eternal kingdom of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ will be richly supplied to you.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore I will not be negligent to always remind you concerning these things, though you know <span class="added-text">(them)</span> and have been established in the present truth.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And I think <span class="added-text">(it)</span> right, as long as I am in this <span class="alt-label">tabernacle</span><span class="alt-text">(=body)</span>, to stir you up by <span class="added-text">(way of)</span> reminder,

<span class="v-num">14</span> knowing that the putting off of my <span class="alt-label">tabernacle</span><span class="alt-text">(=body)</span> is <span class="alt-label">imminent</span><span class="alt-text">(=swift)</span>, even as our Lord Jesus Christ made clear to me.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And I will also be diligent that you may be able, at every time after my <span class="alt-label">departure</span><span class="alt-text">(=death)</span>, to make <span class="added-text">(a)</span> remembrance of these things.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For we did not follow cunningly <span class="alt-label">devised</span><span class="alt-text">(=cleverly crafted)</span> myths when we made known to you the power and coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, but we were eyewitnesses of his majesty.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For he received honor and glory from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, when such a voice was brought to him by the majestic glory, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And we heard this voice brought from heaven, when we were with him on the holy mountain.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And we have the more sure prophetic word, to which you do well to give attention, as to a lamp shining in a <span class="alt-label">dark</span><span class="alt-text">(=dry, murky)</span> place, until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day dawns and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> morning <span class="alt-label">star</span><span class="alt-text">(=day star)</span> rises in your hearts;[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> knowing this first, that no prophecy of scripture comes of <span class="added-text">(its)</span> <span class="alt-label">own</span><span class="alt-text">(=private)</span> interpretation.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> For prophecy was never brought by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of man, but holy men of God spoke, being carried along by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.[^7]

[^1]: πληθυνθείη는 희구법(옵타티브)으로 축원·소원을 나타낸다.
[^2]: δεδωρημένης는 완료분사로, 이미 온전히 이루어진 은혜의 결과가 지금도 유효함을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(γένησθε)은 목적절로, 신적 성품에 참여하게 하시려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἐπόπται는 단순 목격이 아니라 직접 눈으로 본 증인이라는 법정적 뉘앙스를 지닌다.
[^5]: φωσφόρος(샛별)는 그리스도의 재림 때 임할 온전한 빛을 예표하는 표현이다.
[^6]: ἰδίας ἐπιλύσεως는 예언자 개인의 자의적 해석이 아니라 성령의 계시에서 비롯됨을 강조한다.
[^7]: φερόμενοι(이끌려)는 항해 용어로, 성령이 인간 저자를 온전히 주도하여 성경 영감을 이루셨음을 나타낸다.
