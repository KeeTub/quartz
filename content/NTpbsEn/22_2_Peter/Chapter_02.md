<span class="v-num">1</span> But there were also false prophets among the people, as also among you there will be false teachers, who will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the Master having bought them, bringing upon themselves swift destruction;

<span class="v-num">2</span> And many will follow their destructive ways, because of whom the way of the truth will be blasphemed;

<span class="v-num">3</span> And in covetousness, with fabricated words, they will exploit you for profit; for whom the judgment from long ago is not idle, and their destruction does not slumber.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For if God did not spare <span class="added-text">(the)</span> angels having sinned, but having cast <span class="added-text">(them)</span> into hell with chains of darkness, delivered <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to be kept for judgment;[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> and did not spare <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ancient world, but preserved Noah, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> eighth <span class="added-text">(person)</span>, a preacher of righteousness, having brought a flood upon <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ungodly;

<span class="v-num">6</span> and having turned to ashes the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah, condemned <span class="added-text">(them)</span> with an overthrow, having made <span class="added-text">(them)</span> an example to those who are about to live ungodly;

<span class="v-num">7</span> and rescued righteous Lot, being oppressed by the licentious behavior of the lawless,

<span class="v-num">8</span> <span class="added-text">(for that righteous man, dwelling among them, was tormenting (his)</span> righteous soul day by day, by <span class="added-text">(their)</span> unlawful deeds, in seeing and hearing);

<span class="v-num">9</span> the Lord knows how to rescue <span class="added-text">(the)</span> godly out of temptation, and to keep <span class="added-text">(the)</span> unrighteous under punishment for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment,

<span class="v-num">10</span> but especially those going after <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lust of defilement, and despising <span class="alt-label">lordship</span><span class="alt-text">(=dominion)</span>. <span class="added-text">(They are)</span> presumptuous, self-willed; they do not tremble <span class="added-text">(at)</span> glorious <span class="alt-label">ones</span><span class="alt-text">(=dignities)</span>, blaspheming <span class="added-text">(them)</span>;

<span class="v-num">11</span> whereas angels, being greater in strength and power, do not bring against them a blasphemous judgment before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But these, like irrational animals born by nature for capture and destruction, blaspheming in the things which they do not understand, will be destroyed in their own destruction,

<span class="v-num">13</span> receiving <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wages of unrighteousness, counting <span class="added-text">(as)</span> pleasure the luxury <span class="added-text">(of living)</span> in the daytime; <span class="added-text">(they are)</span> spots and blemishes, reveling in their deceits while feasting together with you,

<span class="v-num">14</span> having eyes full of adultery and unceasing sin, enticing unstable souls, having a heart trained in covetousness, children of a curse;

<span class="v-num">15</span> having forsaken the straight way, they went astray, having followed the way of Balaam the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Bosor, who loved <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wages of unrighteousness,

<span class="v-num">16</span> but he had a rebuke for his own transgression: a speechless donkey, speaking with a man's voice, restrained the madness of the prophet.

<span class="v-num">17</span> These are wells without water, clouds driven by a storm, for whom the blackness of darkness is kept forever.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For, speaking great swelling words of vanity, they entice, through the lusts of the flesh, through licentiousness, those who have actually escaped from those living in error,

<span class="v-num">19</span> promising them liberty, while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by whom a man is overcome, by this he is also enslaved.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For if, having escaped the defilements of the world through the full knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, and again having been entangled in these, they are overcome, the last <span class="added-text">(state)</span> has become worse for them than the first.[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> For it was better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than having known <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, to turn back from the holy commandment delivered to them.[^3]

<span class="v-num">22</span> But it has happened to them according to the true proverb, 'A dog returning to his own vomit,' and, 'A sow, having washed, to <span class="added-text">(her)</span> wallowing in mire.'[^4]

[^1]: εἰ로 시작하는 조건절이 4~8절에 걸쳐 이어지다가 9절 '주께서 아시나니'에서 귀결절로 마무리되는 긴 조건구문이다.
[^2]: ἀποφυγόντες(피한)와 ἐμπλακέντες(얽매인)의 부정과거 분사가 대조되어, 한때 벗어났던 자가 다시 얽매이는 상태 변화를 강조한다.
[^3]: μὴ ἐπεγνωκέναι(온전히 알지 못함)와 ἐπιγνοῦσιν(온전히 알고서)의 대조는 단순한 지식이 아니라 인격적·체험적 앎을 가리키는 완료·부정과거의 뉘앙스를 담고 있다.
[^4]: 잠언 26:11의 인용으로, 거짓 교사들의 배교가 짐승이 본성으로 돌아가는 것과 같음을 비유한다.
