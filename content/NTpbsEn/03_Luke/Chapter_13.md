<span class="v-num">1</span> Now some were present at that very time, reporting to him about the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mixed with their sacrifices.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Jesus, answering, said to them, "Do you think that these Galileans became sinners beyond all the Galileans, because they suffered such things?"

<span class="v-num">3</span> "No, I say to you; but unless you repent, you will all likewise perish."[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Or those eighteen, on whom the tower in Siloam fell and killed them, do you think that they became <span class="alt-label">debtors</span><span class="alt-text">(=sinners)</span> beyond all the men dwelling in Jerusalem?

<span class="v-num">5</span> "No, I say to you; but unless you repent, you will all <span class="alt-label">likewise</span><span class="alt-text">(=in the same way)</span> perish."[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And he spoke this parable: "A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard; and he came seeking fruit on it, and did not find <span class="added-text">(any)</span>."

<span class="v-num">7</span> So he said to the vinedresser, 'Behold, three years I have come seeking fruit on this fig tree, and I do not find <span class="added-text">(any)</span>; cut it down; why does it even make the ground useless?'

<span class="v-num">8</span> But he, answering, said to him, 'Lord, leave it this year also, until I dig around it and put in <span class="alt-label">manure</span><span class="alt-text">(=fertilizer)</span>;'

<span class="v-num">9</span> 'And if indeed it bears fruit <span class="added-text">(well)</span> — but if not, in the <span class="alt-label">future</span><span class="alt-text">(=coming year)</span> you shall cut it down.'"[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now he was teaching in one of the synagogues on the Sabbath<span class="added-text">(s)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And behold, there was a woman having a spirit of <span class="alt-label">infirmity</span><span class="alt-text">(=weakness)</span> eighteen years, and she was bent over, and not able to straighten up <span class="alt-label">completely</span><span class="alt-text">(=at all)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And Jesus, seeing her, called <span class="added-text">(her)</span> and said to her, "Woman, you are <span class="alt-label">released</span><span class="alt-text">(=have been freed)</span> from your infirmity."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he laid <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands on her; and immediately she was made <span class="alt-label">straight</span><span class="alt-text">(=upright)</span>, and glorified God.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But the ruler of the synagogue, answering, being indignant that Jesus healed on the Sabbath, said to the crowd, "There are six days in which it is necessary to work; therefore come and be healed on these <span class="added-text">(days)</span>, and not on the day of the Sabbath."

<span class="v-num">15</span> Then the Lord answered him and said, "Hypocrite, does not each of you on the Sabbath loose his ox or <span class="added-text">(his)</span> donkey from the manger, and leading <span class="added-text">(it)</span> away, give it <span class="alt-label">water</span><span class="alt-text">(=water it)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">16</span> But this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, whom Satan bound, behold, eighteen years, was it not necessary <span class="added-text">(for her)</span> to be loosed from this bond on the day of the Sabbath?"

<span class="v-num">17</span> And as he said these things, all those opposing him were put to shame; and all the crowd rejoiced at all the glorious things being done by him.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Then he said, "What is the kingdom of God like? And to what shall I compare it?"

<span class="v-num">19</span> It is like a grain of mustard seed, which a man took and <span class="alt-label">threw</span><span class="alt-text">(=cast)</span> into his <span class="added-text">(own)</span> garden; and it grew, and became a large tree, and the birds of the <span class="alt-label">air</span><span class="alt-text">(=heaven)</span> <span class="alt-label">nested</span><span class="alt-text">(=dwelt)</span> in its branches."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And again he said, "To what shall I compare the kingdom of God?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> It is like leaven, which a woman took and <span class="alt-label">hid</span><span class="alt-text">(=mixed in)</span> in three <span class="alt-label">measures</span><span class="alt-text">(=satons)</span> of flour, until <span class="alt-label">all</span><span class="alt-text">(=the whole)</span> was leavened."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And he went through the cities and villages, teaching, and making <span class="added-text">(his)</span> journey <span class="alt-label">toward</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Then someone said to him, "Lord, are <span class="added-text">(there)</span> few who are being saved?" And he said to them,

<span class="v-num">24</span> <span class="alt-label">"Strive</span><span class="alt-text">(=Fight to enter)</span> to enter through the narrow <span class="alt-label">door</span><span class="alt-text">(=gate)</span>; for many, I say to you, will seek to enter, and will not be able.[^4]

<span class="v-num">25</span> <span class="alt-label">Once</span><span class="alt-text">(=From when)</span> the master of the house has risen up and shut the door, and you begin to stand outside and knock <span class="added-text">(on)</span> the door, saying, 'Lord, Lord, open to us,' then, answering, he will say to you, 'I do not know you, where you are from.'

<span class="v-num">26</span> Then you will begin to say, 'We ate and drank in your presence, and you taught in our streets.'

<span class="v-num">27</span> And he will say, 'I say to you, I do not know you, where you are from; depart from me, all <span class="added-text">(you)</span> workers of unrighteousness.'

<span class="v-num">28</span> There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth, when you see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets in the kingdom of God, but yourselves being thrust out.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And they will come from east and west, and from north and south, and will <span class="alt-label">recline</span><span class="alt-text">(=sit down together)</span> in the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And behold, there are last <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> who will be first, and there are first <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> who will be last."

<span class="v-num">31</span> On that very day some Pharisees came to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, saying to him, "Go out and depart from here, for Herod wants to kill you."

<span class="v-num">32</span> "And he said to them, "Go and tell that fox, 'Behold, I cast out demons and <span class="alt-label">perform</span><span class="alt-text">(=complete)</span> healings today and tomorrow, and on the third <span class="added-text">(day)</span> I am <span class="alt-label">finished</span><span class="alt-text">(=perfected)</span>.'

<span class="v-num">33</span> But I must journey today and tomorrow and the <span class="added-text">(day)</span> following; for it is not possible for a prophet to perish outside Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">34</span> "Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> killing the prophets and stoning those sent to her, how often I wanted to gather your children together, in the <span class="alt-label">way</span><span class="alt-text">(=manner)</span> a hen <span class="added-text">(gathers)</span> her own brood under <span class="added-text">(her)</span> wings, and you were not willing!

<span class="v-num">35</span> Behold, your house is <span class="alt-label">left</span><span class="alt-text">(=abandoned)</span> to you desolate; and truly I say to you, you will not see me until <span class="added-text">(the time)</span> comes when you say, 'Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one coming in the name of the Lord.'"

[^1]: ἐὰν μὴ μετανοῆτε는 아직 실현되지 않은 회개 여부에 따른 결과를 경고하는 가정법 조건절이다.
[^2]: ἐὰν μὴ μετανοῆτε는 가정법 현재를 사용한 3류 조건문으로, 아직 결정되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다. 회개하지 않는 상태가 지속될 경우 반드시 멸망이 따른다는 강한 경고의 뉘앙스를 담고 있다.
[^3]: κἂν ποιήσῃ는 가정법으로 표현된 조건절로, 아직 판가름 나지 않은 열매 맺음의 가능성을 나타낸다.
[^4]: εἰσελθεῖν διὰ τῆς στενῆς πύλης는 목적을 나타내는 부정사구로, 힘써 싸워야 할 목표를 가리킨다.
