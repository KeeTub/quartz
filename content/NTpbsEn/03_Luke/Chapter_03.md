<span class="v-num">1</span> Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate being governor of Judea, and Herod being tetrarch of Galilee, and his brother Philip being tetrarch of the region of Iturea and Trachonitis, and Lysanias being tetrarch of Abilene,

<span class="v-num">2</span> Annas and Caiaphas being high priests, the word of God came upon John the son of Zacharias in the wilderness.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he came into all the region around the Jordan, preaching a baptism of repentance for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> forgiveness of sins,

<span class="v-num">4</span> as it is written in the book of the words of Isaiah the prophet, saying, "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Prepare the way of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, make his paths straight.'[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Every valley will be filled, and every mountain and hill will be made low, and the crooked <span class="added-text">(places)</span> will become straight, and the rough ways <span class="added-text">(will become)</span> smooth <span class="added-text">(paths)</span>;

<span class="v-num">6</span> and all flesh will see the salvation of God."

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then he said to the crowds coming out to be baptized by him, "<span class="added-text">(You)</span> offspring of vipers, who warned you to flee from the coming wrath?

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore bring forth fruits worthy of repentance, and do not begin to say within yourselves, 'We have Abraham <span class="added-text">(as our)</span> father'; for I say to you that God is able from these stones to raise up children to Abraham.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And even now the axe is laid to the root of the trees; therefore every tree not producing good fruit is cut down and thrown into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fire."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the crowds asked him, saying, "What then shall we do?"

<span class="v-num">11</span> And answering, he said to them, "The one having two tunics, let him share with the one not having <span class="added-text">(any)</span>; and the one having food, let him do likewise."

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then tax collectors also came to be baptized, and said to him, "Teacher, what shall we do?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he said to them, "Collect nothing more than what has been appointed to you."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And soldiers also asked him, saying, "And what shall we do?" And he said to them, "Do not extort anyone, nor accuse <span class="added-text">(anyone)</span> falsely, and be content with your wages."

<span class="v-num">15</span> Now as the people were in expectation, and all were reasoning in their hearts concerning John, whether he might be the Christ,

<span class="v-num">16</span> John answered, saying to all, "I indeed baptize you with water; but <span class="added-text">(One)</span> mightier than I is coming, of whom I am not worthy to loose the strap of his sandals; he will baptize you with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit and fire;[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> whose winnowing fork <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in his hand, and he will thoroughly cleanse his threshing floor, and will gather the wheat into his barn; but the chaff he will burn up with unquenchable fire."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And with many other <span class="added-text">(exhortations)</span> indeed he exhorted, proclaiming good news to the people.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Herod the tetrarch, being reproved by him concerning Herodias his brother Philip's wife, and concerning all the evil things which Herod had done,

<span class="v-num">20</span> added this also to them all, that he shut up John in prison.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Now it came to pass, when all the people were baptized, that Jesus also having been baptized, and praying, the heaven was opened,[^3]

<span class="v-num">22</span> and the Holy Spirit descended in bodily form like a dove upon him, and a voice came from heaven, saying, "You are my beloved Son; in you I am well pleased."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And Jesus himself began <span class="added-text">(his ministry)</span> being about thirty years <span class="added-text">(of age)</span>, being, as was supposed, the son of Joseph, the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Heli,[^4]

<span class="v-num">24</span> the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Matthat, the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Levi, the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Melchi, the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Janna, the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Joseph,

[^5]: 족보가 하나님으로 끝나 아담이 하나님의 아들임을 나타내며 인류의 기원을 하나님께 둔다.
