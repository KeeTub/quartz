<span class="v-num">1</span> And Jesus, full of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, returned from the Jordan, and was led <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> the Spirit into the wilderness,

<span class="v-num">2</span> being tempted forty days by the devil. And he ate nothing in those days, and when they were ended, afterward he was hungry.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And the devil said to him, "If you are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God, speak to this stone that it may become bread."[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Jesus answered him, saying, "It is written, 'Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.'"[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the devil, having led him up into a high mountain, showed him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time;

<span class="v-num">6</span> and the devil said to him, "I will give you all this authority and their glory, because it has been delivered to me, and I give it to whomever I wish;[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> You therefore, if you will worship before me, all <span class="added-text">(things)</span> shall be yours."[^4]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Jesus answered and said to him, "Get behind me, Satan; for it is written, 'You shall worship <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God, and him only shall you serve.'"[^5]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he led him to Jerusalem, and set him on the pinnacle of the temple, and said to him, "If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down from here;

<span class="v-num">10</span> for it is written, 'He will command his angels concerning you, to keep you;'[^6]

<span class="v-num">11</span> and, 'they shall bear you up on <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hands, lest you strike your foot against a stone.'"[^7]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And Jesus answered and said to him, "It is said, 'You shall not tempt <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God.'"[^8]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And when the devil had ended every temptation, he departed from him until an opportune time.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee, and a report went out concerning him through the whole surrounding region.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And he taught in their synagogues, being glorified by all.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up, and he went in, according to his custom, on the day of the Sabbaths, into the synagogue, and stood up to read.[^9]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And there was given to him <span class="added-text">(the)</span> book of Isaiah the prophet. And having unrolled the book, he found the place where it was written,

<span class="v-num">18</span> '<span class="added-text">(The)</span> Spirit of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> upon me, because he anointed me to preach good <span class="alt-label">news</span><span class="alt-text">(=the gospel)</span> to the poor; he has sent me to heal the <span class="alt-label">brokenhearted</span><span class="alt-text">(=those crushed in heart)</span>, to proclaim <span class="alt-label">release</span><span class="alt-text">(=forgiveness)</span> to captives, and recovery of sight to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blind, to send forth <span class="added-text">(the)</span> oppressed in <span class="alt-label">release</span><span class="alt-text">(=freedom)</span>;[^10]

<span class="v-num">19</span> to proclaim <span class="added-text">(the)</span> acceptable year of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.'

<span class="v-num">20</span> And having rolled up the book, having given <span class="added-text">(it)</span> back to the attendant, he sat down; and the eyes of all in the synagogue were fixed on him.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And he began to say to them, "Today this scripture is fulfilled in your ears."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And all bore witness to him, and marveled at the words of grace proceeding out of his mouth, and said, "Is not this the son of Joseph?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> And he said to them, "Surely you will say to me this <span class="alt-label">parable</span><span class="alt-text">(=proverb)</span>, 'Physician, heal yourself; whatever we heard was done in Capernaum, do also here in your hometown.'"

<span class="v-num">24</span> And he said, "Truly I say to you, no prophet is accepted in his own hometown.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But truly I say to you, many widows were in Israel in the days of Elijah, when the heaven was shut up three years and six months, when a great famine came upon all the land;[^11]

<span class="v-num">26</span> and Elijah was sent to none of them, except to Sarepta of Sidon, to a woman <span class="added-text">(who was)</span> a widow.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And many lepers were in Israel in the time of Elisha the prophet, and none of them was cleansed, except Naaman the Syrian."[^12]

<span class="v-num">28</span> And all in the synagogue were filled with wrath, hearing these things,

<span class="v-num">29</span> and rising up, they cast him out of the city, and led him to the brow of the hill on which their city was built, so as to throw him down;[^13]

<span class="v-num">30</span> but he, passing through the midst of them, went his way.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And he went down to Capernaum, a city of Galilee, and was teaching them on the Sabbaths.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And they were astonished at his teaching, because his word was with authority.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And in the synagogue there was a man having a spirit of an unclean demon, and he cried out with a loud voice,

<span class="v-num">34</span> saying, "Ah! What <span class="added-text">(is there)</span> between us and <span class="alt-label">you</span><span class="alt-text">(=what have we to do with you)</span>, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know you, who you are, the Holy One of God."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be silent, and come out of him." And when the demon had thrown him down in the midst, it came out of him, having hurt him <span class="alt-label">nothing</span><span class="alt-text">(=not at all)</span>.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And amazement came upon all, and they spoke together with one another, saying, "What <span class="added-text">(is)</span> this <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=matter)</span>, that with authority and power he commands the unclean spirits, and they come out?"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And a report about him went out into every place of the surrounding region.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And having risen up out of the synagogue, he entered into the house of Simon; and Simon's mother-in-law was held <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=gripped by)</span> a great fever, and they asked him concerning her.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And standing over her, he rebuked the fever, and it left her; and immediately, having risen up, she served them.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And when the sun was setting, all as many as had <span class="added-text">(any)</span> sick with various diseases brought them to him; and he, laying his hands on each one of them, healed them.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And demons also came out from many, crying out and saying, "You are the Christ, the Son of God." And rebuking <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, he did not permit them to speak, because they knew him to be the Christ.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And when day came, having gone out, he went into a deserted place; and the crowds sought him, and came to him, and held him back, <span class="added-text">(so)</span> that he would not go away from them.

<span class="v-num">43</span> But he said to them, "I must preach the good <span class="alt-label">news</span><span class="alt-text">(=gospel)</span> of the kingdom of God to the other cities also; for I was sent for this <span class="added-text">(purpose)</span>."

<span class="v-num">44</span> And he was preaching in the synagogues of Galilee.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)은 명령의 목적/내용을 나타내는 절로, '돌에게 명하여 (그 결과) 떡이 되게 하라'는 의도를 표현한다. 즉 마귀가 요구하는 명령의 목표를 가정법으로 제시한 것이다.
[^2]: 신명기 8:3 인용; 사람의 생명이 떡보다 하나님의 말씀에 근거함을 강조한다.
[^3]: ᾧ ἐὰν θέλω는 '내가 원하는 자에게는 누구든지'라는 뜻으로, 관계대명사+ἐάν+가정법이 결합되어 불특정 대상을 가리키는 부정 관계절(일반화된 조건)을 이룬다. 마귀가 임의로 누구에게나 권세를 줄 수 있다는 자의성을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(προσκυνήσῃς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 조건을 나타낸다. '만일 네가 경배하면'이라는 조건이 충족될 때 비로소 결과(모든 것이 네 것이 됨)가 성립되는 구조로, 사탄의 시험적 제안임을 보여준다.
[^5]: 신명기 6:13 인용; 경배와 섬김의 대상이 오직 하나님뿐임을 선언한다.
[^6]: τοῦ διαφυλάξαι는 목적 부정사구문(τοῦ+부정사)이며, 시편 91:11 인용이다.
[^7]: 시편 91:12 인용; 사탄이 성경을 오용하여 예수를 시험한다.
[^8]: 신명기 6:16 인용; 하나님을 시험하지 말라는 명령으로 사탄의 시험을 물리친다.
[^9]: ἀναγνῶναι는 부정사로 ἀνέστη의 목적(읽기 위하여)을 나타낸다.
[^10]: 이사야 61:1-2 인용(70인역 근거); 메시아의 사역을 예언한 본문을 예수께서 자신에게 적용하신다.
[^11]: 열왕기상 17장을 배경으로 엘리야 시대의 기근을 예로 든 구약 암시이다.
[^12]: 열왕기하 5장 나아만의 치유 사건을 언급한 구약 암시이다.
[^13]: εἰς τὸ κατακρημνίσαι는 목적 부정사구문(εἰς τό+부정사)으로 '밀쳐 떨어뜨리려는' 목적을 나타낸다.
