# Luke 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> And it happened, as the crowd was pressing upon him to hear the word of God, that he was standing by the lake <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Gennesaret;

<span class="v-num">2</span> and he saw two boats standing by the lake, but the fishermen, having gotten out from them, were washing their nets.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And having gotten into one of the boats, which was Simon's, he asked him to put out a little from the land; and having sat down, he taught the crowds out of the boat.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when he stopped speaking, he said to Simon, "Put out into the deep, and let down your nets for a catch."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Simon answering said to him, "Master, having labored through the whole night, we took nothing; but at your word I will let down the net."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And having done this, they enclosed a great multitude of fish, and their net was breaking;

<span class="v-num">7</span> and they signaled to the partners in the other boat, to come and help them; and they came and filled both boats, so that they began to sink.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But Simon Peter, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, fell down at the knees of Jesus, saying, "Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, O Lord."

<span class="v-num">9</span> For amazement had seized him and all those with him, at the catch of fish which they had taken;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and likewise also James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were partners with Simon. And Jesus said to Simon, "Do not be afraid; from now on you will be catching men."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And having brought the boats to land, having left all things, they followed him.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And it happened, while he was in one of the cities, that behold, a man full of leprosy <span class="added-text">(was there)</span>; and having seen Jesus, having fallen on <span class="added-text">(his)</span> face, he begged him, saying, "Lord, if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me."[^1]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And having stretched out <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand, he touched him, saying, "I am willing, be cleansed." And immediately the leprosy departed from him.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he commanded him to tell no one; "but having gone, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing, just as Moses commanded, for a testimony to them."

<span class="v-num">15</span> But the word concerning him spread abroad even more; and many crowds gathered together to hear, and to be healed by him from their sicknesses;

<span class="v-num">16</span> but he was withdrawing into the deserted places, and praying.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And it happened on one of the days, that he was teaching; and there were sitting <span class="added-text">(there)</span> Pharisees and teachers of the law, who had come from every village of Galilee and Judea and Jerusalem; and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord was <span class="added-text">(present)</span> for healing them.

<span class="v-num">18</span> and behold, men carrying on a bed a man who was paralyzed, and they were seeking to bring him in and to lay <span class="added-text">(him)</span> before him;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and not finding by what <span class="added-text">(way)</span> they might bring him in because of the crowd, having gone up onto the roof, they let him down through the tiles with <span class="added-text">(his)</span> small bed into the middle, before Jesus.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And having seen their faith, he said to him, "Man, your sins are (=have been) forgiven you."[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the scribes and the Pharisees began to reason, saying, "Who is this who speaks blasphemies? Who can forgive sins, except God alone?"

<span class="v-num">22</span> But Jesus, having perceived their reasonings, answering said to them, "Why are you reasoning in your hearts?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> "Which is easier, to say, 'Your sins are forgiven you,' or to say, 'Rise up and walk'?"

<span class="v-num">24</span> "But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on the earth to forgive sins," he said to the paralyzed man, "I say to you, rise up, and having taken up your small bed, go to your house."[^3]

<span class="v-num">25</span> And immediately, having risen up before them, having taken up <span class="added-text">(that)</span> on which he was lying, he went away to his house, glorifying God.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And amazement seized all <span class="added-text">(of them)</span>, and they glorified God, and they were filled with fear, saying, "We have seen extraordinary things today."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And after these things he went out, and saw a tax collector named Levi, sitting at the tax office, and said to him, "Follow me."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And having left all things, having risen up, he followed him.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And Levi made a great feast for him in his house; and there was a great crowd of tax collectors and others who were reclining with them.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And their scribes and the Pharisees grumbled at his disciples, saying, "Why do you eat and drink with tax collectors and sinners?"

<span class="v-num">31</span> And Jesus answering said to them, "Those who are healthy have no need of a physician, but those who are sick.

<span class="v-num">32</span> I have not come to call <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteous, but sinners to repentance."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And they said to him, "Why do the disciples of John fast often, and make prayers, and likewise also <span class="added-text">(the disciples)</span> of the Pharisees; but yours eat and drink?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> But he said to them, "Can you make the sons of the bridal chamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them?

<span class="v-num">35</span> But days will come, and when the bridegroom is taken away from them, then they will fast in those days."

<span class="v-num">36</span> And he also spoke a parable to them, "No one puts a patch of a new garment on an old garment; but if he does, he both tears the new, and the patch from the new does not match the old.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And no one puts new wine into old wineskins; but if he does, the new wine will burst the skins, and it will be spilled out, and the skins will be destroyed;

<span class="v-num">38</span> but new wine must be put into new wineskins, and both are preserved.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And no one, having drunk old <span class="added-text">(wine)</span>, immediately desires new; for he says, 'The old is better.'"

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(θέλῃς)으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 조건으로 제시함. 나병환자가 예수의 의지 여부에 자신의 치유를 전적으로 맡기는 겸손한 신뢰의 태도를 보여줌.
[^2]: ἀφέωνταί는 현재완료 수동태로, 과거에 이루어진 용서가 지금까지 지속되는 상태(이미 용서받아 있음)를 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα εἰδῆτε는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 절로, 이어지는 치유 행위의 신학적 근거(인자의 죄 사함 권세)를 밝히려는 목적을 담고 있다.
