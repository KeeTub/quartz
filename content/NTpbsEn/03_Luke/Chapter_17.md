<span class="v-num">1</span> He said to the disciples, "It is impossible for the stumbling blocks not to come, but woe <span class="added-text">(is)</span> to <span class="added-text">(him)</span> through whom it comes.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> <span class="added-text">(It)</span> is profitable for him if a <span class="alt-label">millstone</span><span class="alt-text">(=donkey-turned millstone)</span> is hung around his neck, and he is thrown into the sea, rather than that he should cause one of these little ones to stumble.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Take heed to yourselves. And if your brother sins against you, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him.[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and turns to you seven times in a day, saying, 'I repent,' you shall forgive him."[^4]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the apostles said to the Lord, "Add to us faith."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the Lord said, "If you had faith as a grain of mustard seed, you would say to this mulberry tree, 'Be uprooted, and be planted in the sea,' and it would obey you.[^5]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But which of you, having a servant plowing or tending sheep, will say to him immediately when he comes in from the field, 'Come and recline <span class="added-text">(to eat)</span>'?

<span class="v-num">8</span> But will he not rather say to him, 'Prepare what I may eat for supper, and having girded yourself, serve me until I eat and drink; and afterward you shall eat and drink'?

<span class="v-num">9</span> Does he have thanks to that servant, because he did the things commanded him? I think not.

<span class="v-num">10</span> So you also, when you have done all the things commanded you, say, 'We are unprofitable servants; we have done what we ought to have done.'"[^6]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And it came to pass, as he was going to Jerusalem, that he passed through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of Samaria and Galilee.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And as he entered into a certain village, ten leprous men met him, who stood far off,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and they lifted up <span class="added-text">(their)</span> voice, saying, "Jesus, Master, have mercy on us."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And when he saw <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, he said to them, "Go, show yourselves to the priests." And it came to pass, as they went, they were cleansed.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And one of them, seeing that he was healed, returned, glorifying God with a loud voice,

<span class="v-num">16</span> and fell on <span class="added-text">(his)</span> face at his feet, giving him thanks; and he was a Samaritan.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus answered and said, "Were not the ten cleansed? But where <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the nine?

<span class="v-num">18</span> Were none found who returned to give glory to God, except this foreigner?"

<span class="v-num">19</span> And he said to him, "Arise, go your way; your faith has saved you."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And being asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God comes, he answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation;

<span class="v-num">21</span> nor will they say, 'Behold, here <span class="added-text">(it is)</span>,' or, 'Behold, there <span class="added-text">(it is)</span>'; for behold, the kingdom of God is <span class="alt-label">within</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> you."[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And he said to the disciples, "Days will come, when you will desire to see one of the days of the Son of Man, and you will not see <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And they will say to you, 'Behold, here <span class="added-text">(he is)</span>,' or, 'Behold, there <span class="added-text">(he is)</span>'; do not go out, nor follow <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For just as the lightning, flashing out of the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> part under heaven, shines to the <span class="added-text">(other)</span> part under heaven, so also will the Son of Man be in his day.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But first he must suffer many things, and be rejected by this generation.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And just as it happened in the days of Noah, so also will it be in the days of the Son of Man.

<span class="v-num">27</span> They were eating, drinking, marrying, and being given in marriage, until the day that Noah entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Likewise also as it happened in the days of Lot: they were eating, drinking, buying, selling, planting, building;

<span class="v-num">29</span> but on the day that Lot went out from Sodom, it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all;

<span class="v-num">30</span> According to these things it will be in the day the Son of Man is revealed.

<span class="v-num">31</span> In that day, he who is on the housetop, with his goods in the house, let him not come down to take them away; and likewise he who is in the field, let him not turn back.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Remember Lot's wife.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Whoever seeks to save his life will lose it; and whoever loses it will <span class="alt-label">preserve</span><span class="alt-text">(=keep alive)</span> it.[^8]

<span class="v-num">34</span> I say to you, in that night there will be two on one bed; the one will be taken, and the other will be left.

<span class="v-num">35</span> Two <span class="added-text">(women)</span> will be grinding together; the one will be taken, and the other will be left."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And answering they say to him, "Where, Lord?" And he said to them, "Where the body <span class="added-text">(is)</span>, there the eagles will be gathered together."

[^1]: Ἀνένδεκτόν(불가피한)은 이중부정 구문 안에서 '오지 않는 것이 불가능하다' 즉 걸림돌의 도래가 필연적임을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(σκανδαλίσῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, '~하게 하는 것' 즉 결과적으로 지향되는 행동을 가리킨다. 여기서는 비교 구문 속에서 '작은 자를 실족시키는 것'이라는 목적/결과가 얼마나 심각한지를 강조하는 뉘앙스다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(ἁμάρτῃ, μετανοήσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~하거든'이라는 뜻이다. 형제의 범죄와 회개가 실제 일어날 수 있는 상황으로 전제되며, 그에 따른 대응(책망, 용서)이 명령형으로 이어진다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(ἁμάρτῃ, ἐπιστρέψῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~하거든'의 뜻이다. 반복되는 죄와 회개라는 가정 상황에 대해 용서를 명령하는 구조다.
[^5]: εἰ+미완료(εἴχετε)와 ἂν+미완료(ἐλέγετε, ὑπήκουσεν)는 현재사실에 반하는 가정(2급 조건문)으로, '너희에게 믿음이 없다'는 전제를 함축한다.
[^6]: ἀχρεῖοι(무익한)는 종의 공로를 내세울 수 없음을 뜻하며, 은혜에 근거한 섬김의 태도를 강조한다.
[^7]: ἐντὸς(안에, 가운데)는 개인 내면의 임재와 공동체 가운데의 임재 양쪽으로 해석될 수 있어 문맥상 애매하나, 바리새인들을 향한 발언임을 고려하면 '너희 가운데'(=사람들 사이)로 이해함이 자연스럽다.
[^8]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 다수를 향한 열린 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다. 목숨을 구하려는 시도와 잃는 상황 모두에 적용되는 보편적 원리를 제시한다.
