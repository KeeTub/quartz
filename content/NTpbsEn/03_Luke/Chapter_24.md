<span class="v-num">1</span> Now on the first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of the week, at deep dawn, they came to the tomb, bringing the spices which they had prepared, and some <span class="added-text">(women)</span> with them.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And they found the stone rolled away from the tomb,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and having entered, they did not find the body of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And it came to pass, as they were perplexed about this, behold, two men stood by them in shining garments.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And they became frightened and bowed <span class="added-text">(their)</span> face<span class="added-text">(s)</span> to the ground, and <span class="added-text">(the men)</span> said to them, "Why do you seek the living among the dead?

<span class="v-num">6</span> He is not here, but has risen; remember how he spoke to you while he was still in Galilee,[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> saying, 'The Son of man must be delivered into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hands of sinful men, and be crucified, and on the third day rise again.'"

<span class="v-num">8</span> And they remembered his words,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and having returned from the tomb, they reported all these things to the eleven and to all the rest.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now they were Mary Magdalene, and Joanna, and Mary <span class="added-text">(the mother)</span> of James, and the other <span class="added-text">(women)</span> with them, who told these things to the apostles.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And their words appeared before them like nonsense, and they did not believe them.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But Peter arose and ran to the tomb, and having stooped down, he saw the linen cloths lying by themselves; and he went away, wondering to himself at what had happened.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And behold, two of them were going that very day to a village named Emmaus, which was sixty stadia from Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And they were talking with each other about all these things that had happened.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And it came to pass, as they were talking and questioning together, that Jesus himself drew near and went with them,

<span class="v-num">16</span> but their eyes were held so that they did not recognize him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And he said to them, "What are these words that you are exchanging with each other as you walk, and are <span class="added-text">(you)</span> sad?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> And one of them, named Cleopas, answered and said to him, "Are you alone a stranger in Jerusalem, and do you not know the things that have happened in it in these days?"

<span class="v-num">19</span> And he said to them, "What things?" And they said to him, "The things concerning Jesus the Nazarene, who was a man, a prophet, mighty in deed and word before God and all the people,

<span class="v-num">20</span> and how the chief priests and our rulers delivered him up to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judgment of death, and crucified him;

<span class="v-num">21</span> but we were hoping that he was the one who was about to redeem Israel; but indeed, with all these things, today is the third day since these things happened.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Moreover, some women of our company also amazed us, having been at the tomb early;

<span class="v-num">23</span> and not finding his body, they came, saying that they had also seen a vision of angels, who say that he is alive.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And some of those with us went to the tomb, and found it just as the women also said; but him they did not see."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And he said to them, "O foolish ones, and slow of heart to believe in all that the prophets have spoken!

<span class="v-num">26</span> Was it not necessary for the Christ to suffer these things, and to enter into his glory?"[^2]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And beginning from Moses and from all the prophets, he interpreted to them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And they drew near to the village where they were going, and he acted as though he would go further.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And they urged him, saying, "Stay with us, for it is toward evening, and the day has declined." And he went in to stay with them.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And it came to pass, as he reclined with them, that he took the bread and blessed it, and having broken it, gave it to them.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And their eyes were opened, and they recognized him; and he became invisible <span class="added-text">(and departed)</span> from them.[^3]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And they said to each other, "Was not our heart burning within us, while he spoke to us on the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?"

<span class="v-num">33</span> And they rose up that very hour, and returned to Jerusalem, and found the eleven gathered together, and those with them,

<span class="v-num">34</span> saying, "The Lord has indeed risen, and has appeared to Simon."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And they related the things that happened on the road, and how he was known to them in the breaking of the bread.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And as they were saying these things, Jesus himself stood in the midst of them, and said to them, "Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you."

<span class="v-num">37</span> But they were terrified and became frightened, and thought they were seeing a spirit.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he said to them, "Why are you troubled? And why do thoughts arise in your hearts?

<span class="v-num">39</span> See my hands and my feet, that it is I myself; touch me and see, for a spirit does not have flesh and bones, as you see me having."[^4]

<span class="v-num">40</span> And having said this, he showed them <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands and feet.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And while they still did not believe for joy, and were amazed, he said to them, "Do you have any food here?"

<span class="v-num">42</span> And they gave him a piece of broiled fish, and <span class="added-text">(some)</span> of a honeycomb.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And having taken <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, he ate before them.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And he said to them, "These are the words which I spoke to you while I was still with you, that all things must be fulfilled which are written in the Law of Moses, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Prophets, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Psalms, concerning me."

<span class="v-num">45</span> Then he opened their mind, to understand the scriptures,[^5]

<span class="v-num">46</span> and said to them, "Thus it is written, and thus it was necessary for the Christ to suffer, and to rise from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead on the third day,

<span class="v-num">47</span> and that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all the nations, beginning from Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">48</span> And you are witnesses of these things.

<span class="v-num">49</span> And behold, I send the promise of my Father upon you; but you, stay in the city of Jerusalem, until you are clothed with power from on high."[^6]

<span class="v-num">50</span> And he led them out as far as Bethany, and having lifted up his hands, he blessed them.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And it came to pass, as he was blessing them, that he was parted from them, and was carried up into heaven.

<span class="v-num">52</span> And they worshiped him, and returned to Jerusalem with great joy;

<span class="v-num">53</span> and were continually in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen.

[^1]: ἠγέρθη는 아오리스트 수동태로, 부활이 하나님께서 친히 행하신 완결된 사건임을 나타내는 신적 수동태이다.
[^2]: ἔδει(반드시 ~해야 했다)는 그리스도의 고난이 하나님의 뜻에 따른 필연적 사건임을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^3]: διηνοίχθησαν은 아오리스트 수동태로, 제자들의 눈이 열린 것이 하나님(그리스도)의 행하신 일임을 암시하는 신적 수동태이다.
[^4]: 살과 뼈(σάρκα καὶ ὀστέα)는 부활하신 몸이 영이 아니라 실제 육체임을 강조하는 표현이다.
[^5]: διήνοιξεν은 능동태로, 31절의 수동태(눈이 열림)와 대조되어 그리스도께서 친히 제자들의 마음을 여신 것을 나타낸다.
[^6]: δύναμιν ἐξ ὕψους(높은 곳으로부터 오는 능력)는 오순절 성령 강림을 예고하는 표현이다.
