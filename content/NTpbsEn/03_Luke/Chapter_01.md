<span class="v-num">1</span> Since many have undertaken to compose an account concerning the matters that have been fully accomplished among us,

<span class="v-num">2</span> even as those who from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning were eyewitnesses and became servants of the word delivered <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to us,

<span class="v-num">3</span> it seemed good to me also, having followed all things closely from the <span class="alt-label">beginning</span><span class="alt-text">(=from above)</span>, to write to you in order, most excellent Theophilus,

<span class="v-num">4</span> that you may know the certainty concerning the words in which you were instructed.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the division of Abijah; and his wife <span class="added-text">(was)</span> of the daughters of Aaron, and her name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Elizabeth.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Both were righteous before God, walking blameless in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And they had no child, because Elizabeth was barren, and both were advanced in their days.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Now it happened, while he was serving as priest before God in the order of his division,

<span class="v-num">9</span> according to the custom of the priesthood, he was chosen by lot to enter into the temple of the Lord and burn incense.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the whole multitude of the people was praying outside at the hour of the incense <span class="added-text">(offering)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And there appeared to him an angel of the Lord, standing at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right <span class="added-text">(side)</span> of the altar of incense.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And Zacharias was greatly startled when he saw <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, and fear fell upon him.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But the angel said to him, "Do not be afraid, Zacharias, for your prayer has been heard, and your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And there will be joy and gladness to you, and many will rejoice at his birth.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For he will be great before the Lord, and shall drink no wine nor strong drink, and he will be filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit even from his mother's womb.[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he will turn many of the sons of Israel to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord their God.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And he will go before Him in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit and power of Elijah, to turn <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hearts of fathers to <span class="added-text">(their)</span> children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the righteous, to prepare for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord a people made ready."[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Zacharias said to the angel, "By what shall I know this? For I am an old man, and my wife is advanced in her days."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the angel answered and said to him, "I am Gabriel, who stands before God; and I was sent to speak to you, and to bring you these good tidings.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And behold, you will be silent and unable to speak, until the day these things take place, because you did not believe my words, which will be fulfilled in their time."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the people were waiting for Zacharias, and they marveled at his delaying in the temple.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But when he came out he could not speak to them; and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple; and he kept making signs to them, and remained mute.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And it happened, when the days of his service were fulfilled, he departed to his house.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Now after these days his wife Elizabeth conceived, and she hid herself five months, saying,

<span class="v-num">25</span> "Thus the Lord has done for me in the days in which He looked upon <span class="added-text">(me)</span>, to take away my reproach among men."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Now in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God to a city of Galilee named Nazareth,

<span class="v-num">27</span> to a virgin betrothed to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Mary.[^4]

<span class="v-num">28</span> And the angel came in to her and said, <span class="alt-label">"Rejoice</span><span class="alt-text">(=Peace)</span>, highly favored one; the Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> with you; blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> you among women."

<span class="v-num">29</span> But when she saw <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, she was greatly troubled at his word, and considered what kind of greeting this might be.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And the angel said to her, "Do not be afraid, Mary, for you have found favor with God.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And behold, you will conceive in <span class="added-text">(your)</span> womb and bring forth a son, and you shall call his name Jesus.[^5]

<span class="v-num">32</span> He will be great, and will be called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Highest; and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord God will give Him the throne of David his father,[^6]

<span class="v-num">33</span> and He will reign over the house of Jacob forever, and of His kingdom there will be no end."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And Mary said to the angel, "How will this be, since I do not know a man?"

<span class="v-num">35</span> And the angel answered and said to her, "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> Holy Spirit will come upon you, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Highest will overshadow you; therefore also the holy One being born will be called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God.[^7]

<span class="v-num">36</span> And behold, Elizabeth your relative has also conceived a son in her old age; and this is the sixth month for her who was called barren.

<span class="v-num">37</span> For with God nothing will be impossible."[^8]

<span class="v-num">38</span> And Mary said, "Behold, the <span class="alt-label">servant</span><span class="alt-text">(=handmaid)</span> of the Lord; let it be to me according to your word." And the angel departed from her.

<span class="v-num">39</span> Now Mary arose in these days and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Judah,

<span class="v-num">40</span> and entered into the house of Zacharias, and greeted Elizabeth.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And it happened, when Elizabeth heard the greeting of Mary, the babe leaped in her womb; and Elizabeth was filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit,

<span class="v-num">42</span> and she cried out with a loud voice, and said, "Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> you among women, and blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the fruit of your womb.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And why is this granted to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?[^9]

<span class="v-num">44</span> For behold, as soon as the voice of your greeting came into my ears, the babe leaped in my womb for joy.

<span class="v-num">45</span> And blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> she who believed, for there will be a fulfillment of the things spoken to her from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord."

<span class="v-num">46</span> And Mary said, "My soul magnifies the Lord,[^10]

<span class="v-num">47</span> and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior.

<span class="v-num">48</span> For He has looked upon the low estate of His handmaid; for behold, from now on all generations will call me blessed.

<span class="v-num">49</span> For He who is mighty has done great things for me, and holy <span class="added-text">(is)</span> His name.

<span class="v-num">50</span> And His mercy <span class="added-text">(is)</span> to generations of generations toward those who fear Him.

<span class="v-num">51</span> He has done mighty deeds with His arm; He has scattered the proud in the thoughts of their hearts.

<span class="v-num">52</span> He has put down the <span class="alt-label">mighty</span><span class="alt-text">(=rulers)</span> from <span class="added-text">(their)</span> thrones, and exalted the lowly.

<span class="v-num">53</span> He has filled the hungry with good things, and the rich He has sent away empty.

<span class="v-num">54</span> He has helped His servant Israel, in remembrance of <span class="added-text">(His)</span> mercy,

<span class="v-num">55</span> as He spoke to our fathers, to Abraham and to his seed forever."

<span class="v-num">56</span> And Mary remained with her about three months, and returned to her house.

<span class="v-num">57</span> Now the time came for Elizabeth to give birth, and she brought forth a son.

<span class="v-num">58</span> And her neighbors and relatives heard that the Lord had magnified His mercy <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> her, and they rejoiced with her.

<span class="v-num">59</span> And it happened on the eighth day, they came to circumcise the child; and they were calling him by the name of his father, Zacharias.

<span class="v-num">60</span> But his mother answered and said, "No, but he shall be called John."

<span class="v-num">61</span> And they said to her, "There is no one among your relatives who is called by this name."

<span class="v-num">62</span> So they made signs to his father, as to what he would want him called.

<span class="v-num">63</span> And he asked for a writing tablet, and wrote, saying, "His name is John." And they all marveled.

<span class="v-num">64</span> And immediately his mouth was opened and his tongue <span class="added-text">(loosed)</span>, and he spoke, blessing God.

<span class="v-num">65</span> And fear came upon all who dwelt around them; and all these sayings were talked about throughout all the hill country of Judea.

<span class="v-num">66</span> And all who heard them laid them up in their hearts, saying, "What then will this child be?" And the hand of the Lord was with him.

<span class="v-num">67</span> And his father Zacharias was filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and prophesied, saying,

<span class="v-num">68</span> "Blessed <span class="added-text">(be)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord God of Israel, for He has visited and accomplished redemption for His people,[^11]

<span class="v-num">69</span> and has raised up a horn of salvation for us in the house of David His servant,[^12]

<span class="v-num">70</span> <span class="added-text">(as He spoke through the mouth of His holy prophets who have been since (the)</span> <span class="alt-label">age</span><span class="alt-text">(=of old)</span>)

<span class="v-num">71</span> salvation from our enemies, and from the hand of all who hate us;

<span class="v-num">72</span> to show mercy <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> our fathers, and to remember His holy covenant,

<span class="v-num">73</span> the oath which He swore to Abraham our father,[^13]

<span class="v-num">74</span> to grant to us, that being delivered from the hand of our enemies, we might serve Him without fear,

<span class="v-num">75</span> in holiness and righteousness before Him all the days of our life.

<span class="v-num">76</span> And you, child, will be called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> prophet of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Highest; for you will go before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord to prepare His ways,[^14]

<span class="v-num">77</span> to give knowledge of salvation to His people by the <span class="alt-label">remission</span><span class="alt-text">(=forgiveness)</span> of their sins,

<span class="v-num">78</span> through the tender <span class="alt-label">mercy</span><span class="alt-text">(=affection)</span> of our God, with which the <span class="alt-label">Dayspring</span><span class="alt-text">(=Rising Sun)</span> from on high has visited us,[^15]

<span class="v-num">79</span> to give light to those sitting in darkness and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> shadow of death, to guide our feet into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way of peace."[^16]

<span class="v-num">80</span> And the child grew and became strong in spirit, and was in the deserts until the day of his <span class="alt-label">appearing</span><span class="alt-text">(=manifestation)</span> to Israel.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιγνῷς) 목적절로, 누가가 데오빌로에게 글을 쓴 목적을 밝힌다. '~하게 하려 함이라'는 뜻으로 이미 배운 것의 확실함을 재확인시키려는 저술 의도를 나타낸다.
[^2]: 미래수동태 '충만하게 될 것이다'는 태어나기 전부터 이미 성령의 역사가 임함을 강조한다.
[^3]: '엘리야의 영과 능력'은 말라기 4:5-6의 예언을 배경으로 요한을 엘리야적 선구자로 제시한다.
[^4]: παρθένος(처녀)는 이사야 7:14 예언 성취의 핵심 단어로 동정녀 잉태를 명시한다.
[^5]: '잉태하여 아들을 낳으리니 이름을 부르라'는 이사야 7:14 칠십인역의 표현을 그대로 반영한다.
[^6]: '지극히 높으신 이의 아들'은 다윗 언약(삼하 7:12-16)의 성취로서 메시아의 신적 아들 됨을 가리킨다.
[^7]: '덮을 것이다(ἐπισκιάσει)'는 출애굽기 40:34-35에서 하나님의 임재를 나타내던 표현으로, 성령의 초자연적 잉태 사역을 가리킨다.
[^8]: '하나님께는 불가능한 것이 없다'는 창세기 18:14 사라의 잉태 약속을 반영한 표현이다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔλθῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, '내 주의 어머니가 내게 오는 것'이 바로 그 놀라운 사건의 내용이자 목적을 밝힘. 여기서는 결과적 의미(~하는 일이)로도 읽혀 엘리사벳의 놀람과 겸손을 강조함.
[^10]: 마리아의 찬가는 한나의 기도(삼상 2:1-10)의 구조와 표현을 따른다.
[^11]: '돌보다'와 '속량하다'는 출애굽 구원 전승의 언어로, 메시아의 오심을 새로운 출애굽으로 묘사한다.
[^12]: '구원의 뿔'은 시편 18:2과 사무엘상 2:10에서 온 표현으로 왕적 능력과 구원을 상징한다.
[^13]: '맹세'는 창세기 22:16-17에서 하나님이 아브라함에게 하신 언약적 맹세를 가리킨다.
[^14]: '주님 앞에 앞서 가서 길을 예비하다'는 말라기 3:1과 이사야 40:3을 결합한 표현으로 세례 요한의 사역을 예언한다.
[^15]: ἀνατολή(돋는 해)는 칠십인역에서 메시아를 가리키는 '싹'(슥 3:8, 6:12)의 번역어로 쓰이는 메시아적 용어다.
[^16]: '어둠과 죽음의 그늘에 앉은 자들'은 이사야 9:2을 인용해 메시아의 빛의 사역을 예고한다.
