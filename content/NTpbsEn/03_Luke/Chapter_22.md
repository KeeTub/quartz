<span class="v-num">1</span> Now the feast of unleavened bread, which is called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Passover, was drawing near.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the chief priests and the scribes were seeking how they might kill him, for they feared the people.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Then Satan entered into Judas, who was called Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And he went away and discussed with the chief priests and the captains how he might betray him to them.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And they rejoiced, and agreed to give him money.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And he consented, and sought an opportunity to betray him to them without a crowd <span class="added-text">(present)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then came the day of unleavened bread, on which it was necessary <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the Passover <span class="added-text">(lamb)</span> to be sacrificed.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And he sent Peter and John, saying, "Go and prepare the Passover for us, that we may eat."[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And they said to him, "Where do you want <span class="added-text">(us)</span> to prepare <span class="added-text">(it)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> He said to them, "Behold, when you have entered into the city, a man carrying a jar of water will meet you; follow him into the house where he enters,

<span class="v-num">11</span> and you shall say to the master of the house, 'The teacher says to you, "Where is the guest room, where I may eat the Passover with my disciples?"'

<span class="v-num">12</span> And he will show you a large upper room, furnished; prepare <span class="added-text">(it)</span> there."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And having gone, they found <span class="added-text">(it)</span> just as he had told them, and they prepared the Passover.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And when the hour came, he sat down <span class="added-text">(at the table)</span>, and the twelve apostles with him.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And he said to them, "I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer,

<span class="v-num">16</span> for I say to you, I will no longer eat of it at all, until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And having received a cup, having given thanks, he said, "Take this, and divide <span class="added-text">(it)</span> among yourselves;

<span class="v-num">18</span> for I say to you, I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, until the kingdom of God comes."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And having taken bread, having given thanks, he broke <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and gave <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to them, saying, "This is my body, which is given for you; do this in remembrance of me.[^2]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Likewise also the cup after supper, saying, "This cup <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the new covenant in my blood, which is poured out for you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But behold, the hand of the one betraying me is with me on the table.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the Son of man indeed goes as it has been determined; but woe to that man, through whom he is betrayed."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And they began to question among themselves, who then of them it might be who was about to do this.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And there was also a dispute among them, as to which of them was considered to be greater.

<span class="v-num">25</span> He said to them, "The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those exercising authority over them are called benefactors.

<span class="v-num">26</span> But you <span class="added-text">(shall)</span> not <span class="added-text">(be)</span> so; rather let the greater among you become as the younger, and the one leading as the one serving.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For who is greater, the one reclining <span class="added-text">(at table)</span>, or the one serving? Is it not the one reclining? But I am among you as the one who serves.

<span class="v-num">28</span> But you are the ones who have continued with me in my trials;

<span class="v-num">29</span> and I <span class="alt-label">appoint</span><span class="alt-text">(=covenant)</span> to you, just as my Father appointed to me, a kingdom,

<span class="v-num">30</span> that you may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel."[^4]

<span class="v-num">31</span> And the Lord said, "Simon, Simon, behold, Satan has asked for you, to sift <span class="added-text">(you)</span> as wheat;

<span class="v-num">32</span> but I have prayed for you, that your faith may not fail; and you, when you have turned again, strengthen your brothers."[^5]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And he said to him, "Lord, I am ready to go with you both to prison and to death."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And he said, "I say to you, Peter, the rooster will not crow today, before you deny three times that you know me."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And he said to them, "When I sent you out without purse and bag and sandals, did you lack anything?" And they said, "Nothing."

<span class="v-num">36</span> So he said to them, "But now, let the one who has a purse take <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, likewise also a bag; and the one who has none, let him sell his garment and buy a sword.

<span class="v-num">37</span> for I say to you, that this which is written must yet be fulfilled in me, 'And he was reckoned with the lawless'; for indeed the things concerning me have <span class="added-text">(their)</span> fulfillment."[^6]

<span class="v-num">38</span> And they said, "Lord, behold, here <span class="added-text">(are)</span> two swords." And he said to them, "It is enough."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And he went out, and proceeded, according to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> custom, to the Mount of Olives; and his disciples also followed him.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And when he came to the place, he said to them, "Pray that you may not enter into temptation."

<span class="v-num">41</span> And he was withdrawn from them about a stone's throw, and having knelt down, he prayed,

<span class="v-num">42</span> saying, "Father, if you are willing, remove this cup from me; nevertheless not my will, but yours be done."

<span class="v-num">43</span> And an angel from heaven appeared to him, strengthening him.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And being in agony, he prayed more earnestly; and his sweat became as it were drops of blood falling down to the ground.

<span class="v-num">45</span> And having risen up from prayer, and having come to the disciples, he found them sleeping from sorrow,

<span class="v-num">46</span> And he said to them, "Why do you sleep? Rise and pray, that you may not enter into temptation."[^7]

<span class="v-num">47</span> And while he was still speaking, behold, a crowd, and the one called Judas, one of the twelve, went before them, and drew near to Jesus to kiss him.

<span class="v-num">48</span> But Jesus said to him, "Judas, do you betray the Son of man with a kiss?"

<span class="v-num">49</span> And those around him, seeing what was about to happen, said to him, "Lord, shall we strike with the sword?"

<span class="v-num">50</span> And a certain one of them struck the servant of the high priest, and cut off his right ear.

<span class="v-num">51</span> But Jesus answered and said, "Permit <span class="added-text">(this)</span> far enough." And having touched his ear, he healed him.

<span class="v-num">52</span> Then Jesus said to the chief priests and captains of the temple and elders who had come upon him, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs?

<span class="v-num">53</span> When I was with you daily in the temple, you did not stretch out <span class="added-text">(your)</span> hands against me. But this is your hour, and the power of darkness."

<span class="v-num">54</span> Then having seized him, they led <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away, and brought him into the house of the high priest. But Peter followed at a distance.

<span class="v-num">55</span> And when they had kindled a fire in the middle of the courtyard, and had sat down together, Peter sat down among them.

<span class="v-num">56</span> And a certain servant girl, seeing him sitting by the light, and looking intently at him, said, "This <span class="added-text">(man)</span> also was with him."

<span class="v-num">57</span> But he denied him, saying, "Woman, I do not know him."

<span class="v-num">58</span> And after a little while another <span class="added-text">(person)</span>, having seen him, said, "You also are of them." But Peter said, "Man, I am not."

<span class="v-num">59</span> And after about one hour had passed, another <span class="added-text">(person)</span> kept insisting, saying, "Truly this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> also was with him, for indeed he is a Galilean."

<span class="v-num">60</span> But Peter said, "Man, I do not know what you are saying." And immediately, while he was still speaking, the rooster crowed.

<span class="v-num">61</span> And the Lord turned and looked directly at Peter; and Peter remembered the word of the Lord, how he had said to him, "Before the rooster crows, you will deny me three times."

<span class="v-num">62</span> And Peter went out, and wept bitterly.

<span class="v-num">63</span> And the men who were holding Jesus mocked him, beating <span class="added-text">(him)</span>;

<span class="v-num">64</span> and having blindfolded him, they struck his face, and asked him, saying, "Prophesy! Who is the one who struck you?"

<span class="v-num">65</span> And many other things, blaspheming, they spoke against him.

<span class="v-num">66</span> And as day came, the elders of the people, both chief priests and scribes, gathered together, and led him up into their council, saying,

<span class="v-num">67</span> "If you are the Christ, tell us." But he said to them, "If I tell you, you will not believe;[^8]

<span class="v-num">68</span> and if I also ask <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, you will not answer me, or let <span class="added-text">(me)</span> go.[^9]

<span class="v-num">69</span> From now on the Son of man will be sitting at the right hand of the power of God."

<span class="v-num">70</span> And they all said, "Are you then the Son of God?" And he said to them, "You say <span class="added-text">(rightly)</span>, that I am."

<span class="v-num">71</span> And they said, "What further need do we have of testimony? For we ourselves have heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span> from his own mouth."

[^1]: ἵνα φάγωμεν은 목적절로, 유월절을 준비시키는 목적(우리가 먹기 위함)을 나타낸다. 가정법 현재는 아직 이루어지지 않은 의도된 결과를 가리킨다.
[^2]: σῶμα(몸)와 ἀνάμνησις(기념)는 성찬 제정의 핵심어로, 그리스도의 대속적 죽음을 반복하여 기억하게 하는 명령이다.
[^3]: διαθήκη(언약)는 옛 언약(출 24장)을 배경으로 하며, 그리스도의 피로 세워지는 새 언약을 가리킨다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 나라 안에서 함께 다스리는 특권을 목적으로 제시한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법으로 믿음이 떨어지지 않기를 구하는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: 이사야 53:12 인용으로, 그리스도의 수난이 죄인의 반열에 헤아려짐으로 성취됨을 보여준다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 시험에 들지 않도록 기도할 것을 명한다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법 3급 조건문으로 아직 실현되지 않은 가정을 나타낸다.
[^9]: ἐάν+가정법 조건문이 반복되어 청중의 완고함을 강조한다.
