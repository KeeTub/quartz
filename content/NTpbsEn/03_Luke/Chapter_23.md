<span class="v-num">1</span> The whole multitude of them, having risen up, led him to Pilate.

<span class="v-num">2</span> They began to accuse him, saying, "We found this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> perverting our nation, and forbidding to give tribute to Caesar, saying himself to be Christ a king."

<span class="v-num">3</span> Pilate asked him, saying, "Are you the King of the Jews?" And he, answering him, said, "You say <span class="added-text">(so)</span>."

<span class="v-num">4</span> Pilate said to the chief priests and the crowds, "I find no <span class="alt-label">fault</span><span class="alt-text">(=guilt)</span> in this man."

<span class="v-num">5</span> But they insisted more strongly, saying, "He stirs up the people, teaching throughout all Judea, beginning from Galilee even to here."

<span class="v-num">6</span> Pilate, hearing Galilee, asked whether the man was a Galilean.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Having known that he was of Herod's jurisdiction, he sent him up to Herod, who himself also was in Jerusalem in these days.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Herod, seeing Jesus, rejoiced greatly; for he was wanting to see him for a long <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, because of hearing many things about him; and he was hoping to see some sign done by him.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> He questioned him with many words; but he (=Jesus) answered him nothing.

<span class="v-num">10</span> The chief priests and the scribes stood, vehemently accusing him.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Herod, having set him at <span class="alt-label">nought</span><span class="alt-text">(=despised him)</span> with his soldiers, and having mocked <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, having arrayed him in a splendid robe, sent him back to Pilate.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Both Pilate and Herod became friends with one another on that very day; for previously they had been at enmity with each other.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Pilate, having called together the chief priests and the rulers and the people,

<span class="v-num">14</span> said to them, "You brought to me this man, as one <span class="alt-label">perverting</span><span class="alt-text">(=misleading)</span> the people; and behold, having examined <span class="added-text">(him)</span> before you, I found no fault in this man of those things which you accuse him;

<span class="v-num">15</span> "nor did Herod; for I sent you to him, and behold, nothing worthy of death has been done by him.

<span class="v-num">16</span> "Having therefore chastised him, I will release <span class="added-text">(him)</span>."

<span class="v-num">17</span> Now he had <span class="added-text">(a)</span> necessity to release to them one at the feast.[^2]

<span class="v-num">18</span> They all cried out together, saying, "Away with this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and release to us Barabbas!"

<span class="v-num">19</span> who had been thrown into prison for a certain insurrection made in the city, and for murder.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Pilate therefore, wanting to release Jesus, spoke again to them.

<span class="v-num">21</span> But they shouted out, saying, "Crucify, crucify him!"

<span class="v-num">22</span> He said to them the third <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, "Why, what evil did this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> do? I found no cause of death in him; having therefore chastised him, I will release <span class="added-text">(him)</span>."

<span class="v-num">23</span> They pressed with loud voices, demanding him to be crucified; and their voices and <span class="added-text">(those)</span> of the chief priests prevailed.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Pilate decided that their demand should be granted;

<span class="v-num">25</span> he released to them the one thrown into prison for insurrection and murder, whom they asked for; but Jesus he delivered to their will.

<span class="v-num">26</span> As they led him away, having laid hold of a certain Simon a Cyrenian coming from the <span class="alt-label">field</span><span class="alt-text">(=country)</span>, they laid on him the cross, to carry <span class="added-text">(it)</span> behind Jesus.

<span class="v-num">27</span> A great multitude of the people followed him, and <span class="added-text">(of)</span> women who were also beating <span class="added-text">(their breasts)</span> and lamenting him.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Jesus, turning to them, said, "Daughters of Jerusalem, do not weep for me, but weep for yourselves and for your children.

<span class="v-num">29</span> "For behold, days are coming in which they will say, 'Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the barren, and the wombs that did not bear, and the breasts that did not nurse.'

<span class="v-num">30</span> "Then they will begin to say to the mountains, 'Fall on us,' and to the hills, 'Cover us.'

<span class="v-num">31</span> "For if they do these things in the green tree, what will be done in the dry?"

<span class="v-num">32</span> And two others also, <span class="alt-label">criminals</span><span class="alt-text">(=evildoers)</span>, were led with him to be put to death.

<span class="v-num">33</span> When they came to the place called <span class="added-text">(The)</span> Skull, there they crucified him, and the criminals, one on the right and one on the left.

<span class="v-num">34</span> Jesus said, "Father, forgive them; for they do not know what they are doing." And dividing his garments, they cast lots.[^3]

<span class="v-num">35</span> The people stood watching. And the rulers also, with them, sneered, saying, "He saved others; let him save himself, if this is the Christ, the chosen of God."

<span class="v-num">36</span> The soldiers also mocked him, coming and offering him vinegar,

<span class="v-num">37</span> and saying, "If you are the King of the Jews, save yourself."

<span class="v-num">38</span> There was also an inscription written over him in Greek and Roman and Hebrew letters, "This is the King of the Jews."

<span class="v-num">39</span> One of the criminals who were hanged blasphemed him, saying, "If you are the Christ, save yourself and us."

<span class="v-num">40</span> But the other, answering, rebuked him, saying, "Do you not even fear God, since you are in the same judgment?

<span class="v-num">41</span> "And we indeed justly; for we receive the due reward of what we did; but this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> did nothing wrong."

<span class="v-num">42</span> He said to Jesus, "Remember me, Lord, when you come in your kingdom."

<span class="v-num">43</span> Jesus said to him, "Truly I say to you, today you will be with me in paradise."[^4]

<span class="v-num">44</span> It was about the sixth hour, and darkness came over all the <span class="alt-label">land</span><span class="alt-text">(=earth)</span>, until the ninth hour.

<span class="v-num">45</span> The sun was darkened, and the veil of the temple was torn in the middle.[^5]

<span class="v-num">46</span> Jesus, crying with a loud voice, said, "Father, into your hands I commit my spirit." And having said this, he breathed his <span class="alt-label">last</span><span class="alt-text">(=gave up the spirit)</span>.[^6]

<span class="v-num">47</span> The centurion, seeing what happened, glorified God, saying, "Truly this man was <span class="alt-label">righteous</span><span class="alt-text">(=innocent)</span>."[^7]

<span class="v-num">48</span> All the crowds that had come together for this spectacle, seeing the things that happened, returned, beating their breasts.

<span class="v-num">49</span> All his acquaintances stood at a distance, and the women who had followed him from Galilee, watching these things.

<span class="v-num">50</span> And behold, a man named Joseph, being a councilor, a good and righteous man,

<span class="v-num">51</span> <span class="added-text">(this man had not consented to their counsel and deed,)</span> from Arimathea, a city of the Jews, who himself also was waiting for the kingdom of God,

<span class="v-num">52</span> this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, having gone to Pilate, asked for the body of Jesus.

<span class="v-num">53</span> And having taken it down, he wrapped it in fine linen, and laid it in a tomb cut in rock, where no one had ever yet been laid.

<span class="v-num">54</span> And the day was the preparation, and the Sabbath was drawing <span class="alt-label">on</span><span class="alt-text">(=dawning)</span>.

<span class="v-num">55</span> The women also, who had come with him from Galilee, having followed after, saw the tomb, and how his body was laid.

[^1]: ἤλπιζέ는 미완료 시제로, 헤롯이 오래전부터 지속적으로 기대해 왔음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 이 절은 마태·마가·요한과의 병행을 위해 TR(다수사본)에 포함된 구절로, 일부 고대 사본에는 없다. TR 전통을 따라 본문에 포함한다.
[^3]: "아버지여 그들을 사하여 주옵소서"는 일부 초기 사본(바티칸 사본 등)에는 없으나 TR(다수사본) 전통을 따라 본문에 포함한다.
[^4]: 파라다이스(낙원)는 헬라어로 본래 '동산'을 뜻하며, 여기서는 죽은 자가 그리스도와 함께 있는 복된 안식의 상태를 가리킨다.
[^5]: 성전 휘장은 지성소를 가리던 것으로, 그 찢어짐은 하나님께 나아가는 길이 그리스도의 죽음으로 열렸음을 상징한다.
[^6]: ἐξέπνευσεν은 문자적으로 '숨을 내쉬다'라는 뜻으로, 예수께서 스스로 자신의 영을 내어주셨음을 강조한다.
[^7]: δίκαιος는 '의로운'과 함께 법정적으로 '죄 없는'이라는 뜻도 포함하여, 백부장이 예수의 무죄를 인정한 것으로도 읽힌다.
