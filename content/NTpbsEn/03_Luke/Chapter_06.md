<span class="v-num">1</span> Now it happened on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> second-first Sabbath, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he passed through the grainfields, and his disciples plucked the heads <span class="added-text">(of grain)</span> and ate, rubbing <span class="added-text">(them)</span> with <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hands.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> But some of the Pharisees said to them, "Why do you do what is not lawful to do on the Sabbaths?"

<span class="v-num">3</span> And Jesus, answering, said to them, "Have you not read even this, what David did, when he was hungry, he and those who were with him,[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> how he entered into the house of God, and took the loaves of the <span class="alt-label">presentation</span><span class="alt-text">(=showbread)</span>, and ate, and gave also to those with him, which is not lawful to eat except for the priests alone?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> And he said to them, "The Son of Man is Lord also of the Sabbath."[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now it happened also on another Sabbath, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he entered into the synagogue and taught; and there was a man there, and his right hand was withered.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And the scribes and the Pharisees watched him closely, whether he would heal on the Sabbath, so that they might find <span class="added-text">(an)</span> accusation against him.[^4]

<span class="v-num">8</span> But he knew their thoughts, and said to the man having the withered hand, "Rise, and stand in the midst." And he, rising up, stood.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Then Jesus said to them, "I will ask you, what is lawful on the Sabbaths: to do good, or to do evil? To save a <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span>, or to destroy <span class="added-text">(it)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> And looking around at them all, he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." And he did so, and his hand was restored, <span class="alt-label">healthy</span><span class="alt-text">(=whole)</span> as the other.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But they were filled with <span class="alt-label">rage</span><span class="alt-text">(=folly)</span>, and discussed with one another what they might do to Jesus.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now it happened in these days, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he went out to the mountain to pray, and he spent the whole night in prayer to God.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And when it was day, he called his disciples, and having chosen twelve from them, whom he also named apostles:

<span class="v-num">14</span> Simon, whom he also named Peter, and Andrew his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,

<span class="v-num">15</span> Matthew and Thomas, James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Alphaeus and Simon called the Zealot,

<span class="v-num">16</span> Judas <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of James, and Judas Iscariot, who also became <span class="added-text">(the)</span> traitor.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And coming down with them, he stood on a level place, with a crowd of his disciples, and a great multitude of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and <span class="added-text">(from)</span> the coastal <span class="added-text">(region)</span> of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him and to be healed of their diseases,

<span class="v-num">18</span> and those troubled by unclean spirits were healed.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the whole crowd sought to touch him, because power went out from him and healed them all.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he, lifting up his eyes on his disciples, said, "Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the poor, for yours is the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> those who hunger now, for you will be filled. Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> those who weep now, for you will laugh.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Blessed are you, when men hate you, and when they exclude you, and reproach <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, and cast out your name as evil, for the sake of the Son of Man.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Rejoice in that day, and leap <span class="added-text">(for joy)</span>, for behold, your reward <span class="added-text">(is)</span> great in heaven, for their fathers did the same things to the prophets.[^6]

<span class="v-num">24</span> But woe to you who are rich, for you have received your consolation.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Woe to you who are full, for you will hunger. Woe to you who laugh now, for you will mourn and weep.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Woe to you, when all men speak well of you, for their fathers did the same things to the false prophets.

<span class="v-num">27</span> "But I say to you who hear, 'Love your enemies, do good to those who hate you,'

<span class="v-num">28</span> bless those who curse you, and pray for those who mistreat you,

<span class="v-num">29</span> To the one striking you on the cheek, offer also the other; and from the one taking away your cloak, do not withhold <span class="added-text">(your)</span> tunic also,

<span class="v-num">30</span> Give to everyone who asks you; and from the one taking away your things, do not demand <span class="added-text">(them)</span> back,

<span class="v-num">31</span> And just as you want men to do to you, you also do to them likewise,[^7]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And if you love those who love you, what <span class="alt-label">credit</span><span class="alt-text">(=grace)</span> is <span class="added-text">(that)</span> to you? For even sinners love those who love them,

<span class="v-num">33</span> And if you do good to those who do good to you, what credit is <span class="added-text">(that)</span> to you? For even sinners do the same,[^8]

<span class="v-num">34</span> And if you lend to those from whom you hope to receive back, what credit is <span class="added-text">(that)</span> to you? For even sinners lend to sinners, so that they may receive back the same <span class="added-text">(amount)</span>,[^9]

<span class="v-num">35</span> But love your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing back; and your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the Most High, for he is kind to the ungrateful and evil,"

<span class="v-num">36</span> Be therefore merciful, just as your Father also is merciful.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And do not judge, and you will not be judged; do not condemn, and you will not be condemned; forgive, and you will be forgiven,

<span class="v-num">38</span> Give, and it will be given to you: good measure, pressed down and shaken together and running over, they will give into your bosom. For with the same measure that you measure, it will be measured back to you."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And he spoke a parable to them: "Can a blind <span class="added-text">(man)</span> lead a blind <span class="added-text">(man)</span>? Will not both fall into a pit?

<span class="v-num">40</span> A disciple is not above his teacher, but everyone who is fully <span class="alt-label">trained</span><span class="alt-text">(=perfected)</span> will be like his teacher.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And why do you see the speck that is in your brother's eye, but do not perceive the beam that is in your own eye?

<span class="v-num">42</span> Or how can you say to your brother, 'Brother, let me cast out the speck that is in your eye,' when you yourself do not see the beam that is in your own eye? Hypocrite, cast out first the beam from your own eye, and then you will see clearly to cast out the speck that is in your brother's eye.

<span class="v-num">43</span> For there is no good tree that bears bad fruit, nor a bad tree that bears good fruit,

<span class="v-num">44</span> for each tree is known by its own fruit; for they do not gather figs from thorns, nor do they gather a grape cluster from a bramble bush,

<span class="v-num">45</span> The good man, out of the good treasure of his heart, brings forth what is good; and the evil man, out of the evil treasure of his heart, brings forth what is evil; for out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaks."

<span class="v-num">46</span> "But why do you call me, 'Lord, Lord,' and do not do the things I say?

<span class="v-num">47</span> Everyone who comes to me, and hears my words, and does them, I will show you whom he is like.

<span class="v-num">48</span> He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation on the rock; and when a flood came, the river burst against that house, and could not shake it, for it had been founded on the rock.[^10]

<span class="v-num">49</span> But he who heard and did not do <span class="added-text">(them)</span> is like a man who built a house on the ground without a foundation, against which the river burst, and immediately it fell, and the <span class="alt-label">ruin</span><span class="alt-text">(=collapse)</span> of that house was great."

[^1]: '둘째-첫 안식일'(δευτεροπρώτῳ)은 신약 전체에 유일하게 등장하는 표현으로 정확한 의미가 불확실하며, 유월절 주간과 관련된 특정 안식일을 가리킨 것으로 추정된다.
[^2]: 다윗이 진설병을 먹은 사건(삼상 21:1-6)을 인용하여 안식일 규례보다 인간의 필요가 우선함을 논증하신다.
[^3]: '안식일의 주인'이라는 선언은 예수께서 안식일 규례 자체보다 높은 권위를 지니신 분임을 밝히는 그리스도론적 주장이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(εὕρωσι)은 서기관들과 바리새인들이 감시한 목적을 나타낸다: 예수를 고발할 구실을 찾으려는 의도적이고 적대적인 목적절이다.
[^5]: ἄνοια는 단순한 어리석음이 아니라 격렬한 분노·광기를 뜻하며, 예수께 적대하는 자들의 격분을 나타낸다.
[^6]: '그들의 조상들이 ~에게도 이와 같이 행하였느니라'라는 후렴구가 23절과 26절에서 각각 선지자와 거짓 선지자에 대해 대구를 이루며 복과 화의 구조를 감싼다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῶσιν)이 θέλετε의 목적어절 역할을 하며 '~하기를 원하는 것'의 내용(목적)을 나타낸다. 사람들에게 바라는 바를 먼저 내가 행하라는 황금률의 핵심 구문이다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀγαθοποιῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~한다면'의 뜻이다. 여기서는 되갚음의 선행이 특별한 은혜가 아님을 논증하기 위한 가정적 상황을 제시한다.
[^9]: ἐὰν+가정법(δανείζητε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절('꾸어준다면')이다. ἵνα+가정법(ἀπολάβωσι)은 죄인들이 꾸어주는 행위의 목적('같은 것을 받고자')을 나타낸다.
[^10]: 48절의 반석 위에 세운 집과 49절의 기초 없는 집이 대구를 이루어 듣고 행함과 듣고 행하지 않음의 결과를 감싸는 구조를 이룬다.
