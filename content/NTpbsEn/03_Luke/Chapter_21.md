# Luke 21

<span class="v-num">1</span> Having looked up, he saw the rich <span class="added-text">(people)</span> casting their gifts into the treasury.

<span class="v-num">2</span> He saw also a certain poor widow casting there two mites,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and said, "Truly I say to you, that this poor widow cast in more than all;

<span class="v-num">4</span> for all these out of their abundance cast into the gifts of God, but she out of her lack cast in all the living that she had."

<span class="v-num">5</span> As some were speaking about the temple, that it was adorned with beautiful stones and offerings, he said,

<span class="v-num">6</span> "<span class="added-text">(As for)</span> these things which you behold, days will come in which there will not be left <span class="added-text">(a)</span> stone upon <span class="added-text">(a)</span> stone that will not be thrown down."

<span class="v-num">7</span> They asked him, saying, "Teacher, when then will these things be? And what <span class="added-text">(will be)</span> the sign, when these things are about to happen?"

<span class="v-num">8</span> He said, "See that you are not led astray; for many will come in my name, saying, 'I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>', and, 'The time has drawn near.' Therefore do not go after them.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> When you hear of wars and disturbances, do not be terrified; for these things must happen first, but the end <span class="added-text">(will)</span> not <span class="added-text">(come)</span> immediately."

<span class="v-num">10</span> Then he said to them, "Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom;

<span class="v-num">11</span> there will be great earthquakes in various places, and famines and pestilences; and there will be terrors and great signs from heaven.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and persecute <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, delivering <span class="added-text">(you)</span> up to synagogues and prisons, bringing <span class="added-text">(you)</span> before kings and governors for my name's sake.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And it will turn out for you as a testimony.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Settle it therefore in your hearts, not to premeditate how to answer;

<span class="v-num">15</span> for I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries will not be able to refute or resist.

<span class="v-num">16</span> You will be betrayed even by parents and brothers and relatives and friends, and they will put <span class="added-text">(some)</span> of you to death;

<span class="v-num">17</span> and you will be hated by all because of my name.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But not a hair of your head shall by any means perish.

<span class="v-num">19</span> By your endurance possess your souls.

<span class="v-num">20</span> When you see Jerusalem surrounded by armies, then know that its desolation has drawn near.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Then let those in Judea flee to the mountains, and let those in the midst of her depart, and let not those in the countryside enter into her.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For these are days of vengeance, that all the things that are written may be fulfilled.[^2]

<span class="v-num">23</span> But woe to those who are with child and to those who nurse <span class="added-text">(infants)</span> in those days! For there will be great distress upon the land, and wrath upon this people.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And they will fall by the edge of the sword, and will be led captive into all the nations; and Jerusalem will be trampled down by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles until the times of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles are fulfilled.[^3]

<span class="v-num">25</span> And there will be signs in sun and moon and stars, and upon the earth distress of nations, in perplexity, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sea and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> waves roaring,

<span class="v-num">26</span> men fainting from fear and expectation of the things coming upon the <span class="alt-label">world</span><span class="alt-text">(=inhabited earth)</span>; for the powers of the heavens will be shaken.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And then they will see the Son of Man coming in a cloud with power and great glory.[^4]

<span class="v-num">28</span> But when these things begin to happen, stand <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=look up)</span> and lift up your heads, because your redemption draws near."

<span class="v-num">29</span> And he told them a parable: "Look at the fig tree, and all the trees.

<span class="v-num">30</span> When they now put forth <span class="added-text">(leaves)</span>, you see <span class="added-text">(it)</span> and know of yourselves that summer is now near.

<span class="v-num">31</span> So also you, when you see these things happening, know that the kingdom of God is near.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Truly I say to you, that this generation will not pass away until all things happen.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not by any means pass away.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But take heed to yourselves, lest your hearts be weighed down with dissipation and drunkenness and cares of <span class="added-text">(this)</span> life, and that day come upon you suddenly.

<span class="v-num">35</span> For it will come as a snare upon all those sitting upon the face of all the earth.

<span class="v-num">36</span> Therefore watch at every time, praying that you may be counted worthy to escape all these things that are about to happen, and to stand before the Son of Man."[^5]

<span class="v-num">37</span> And he was teaching in the temple in the daytime, but at night he went out and lodged on the mountain called <span class="added-text">(the Mount)</span> of Olives.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And all the people came early in the morning to him in the temple, to hear him.

[^1]: 많은 자들이 '내가 그리스도라' 자칭하며 미혹할 것을 경고하신 것으로, 거짓 메시아 주장을 가리킨다.
[^2]: '보복의 날들'은 호세아 9:7, 예레미야 5:29 등 구약의 심판 예언을 반영한 표현이다.
[^3]: '이방인의 때'는 예루살렘 멸망 이후 이방인이 지배하는 종말론적 기간을 가리킨다.
[^4]: '구름을 타고 오는 인자'는 다니엘 7:13의 인자 환상을 암시한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(καταξιωθῆτε)은 '~하도록'이라는 목적절로, 깨어 기도하는 행위의 목적을 나타낸다. 장차 있을 환난을 피하고 인자 앞에 설 자격을 얻는 것이 기도의 궁극적 목표임을 강조한다.
