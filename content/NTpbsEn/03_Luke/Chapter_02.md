<span class="v-num">1</span> Now it happened in those days, a decree went out from Caesar Augustus, to <span class="alt-label">register</span><span class="alt-text">(=to be registered)</span> all the inhabited <span class="alt-label">world</span><span class="alt-text">(=empire)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> This registration first took place while Cyrenius was governing Syria.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And all were going to be registered, each to his own city.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Joseph also went up from Galilee, out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city of Nazareth, into Judea, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city of David which is called Bethlehem, because of his being of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house and lineage of David,

<span class="v-num">5</span> to be registered with Mary, his betrothed wife, she being with child.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And it happened, while they were there, the days for her to give birth were fulfilled;

<span class="v-num">7</span> and she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in the manger, because there was no place for them in the inn.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And there were shepherds in the same region, staying in the field and keeping watch by night over their flock.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And behold, an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord stood by them, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord shone around them; and they feared a great fear.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the angel said to them, "Do not fear; for behold, I bring good news to you <span class="added-text">(of)</span> great joy which will be to all the people,

<span class="v-num">11</span> because there was born to you today a Savior, who is Christ <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city of David.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the sign to you: you will find a babe wrapped in swaddling clothes, lying in a manger."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And suddenly there was with the angel a multitude of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavenly host, praising God and saying,

<span class="v-num">14</span> "Glory in the highest to God, and on earth peace, among men good will."[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And it happened, as the angels went away from them into heaven, that the men, the shepherds, said to one another, "Let us indeed go through to Bethlehem, and see this thing that has happened, which the Lord made known to us."

<span class="v-num">16</span> And they came, having hurried, and found both Mary and Joseph, and the babe lying in the manger.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they made widely known concerning the word spoken to them about this child.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And all who heard marveled concerning the things spoken to them by the shepherds.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Mary kept all these sayings, pondering <span class="added-text">(them)</span> in her heart.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And the shepherds returned, glorifying and praising God for all the things they heard and saw, just as it was told to them.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And when eight days were fulfilled for circumcising the child, his name was called Jesus, the name called by the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And when the days of their purification were fulfilled, according to the law of Moses, they brought him up to Jerusalem, to present <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to the Lord,[^3]

<span class="v-num">23</span> just as it is written in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, "Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the Lord,"[^4]

<span class="v-num">24</span> and to give a sacrifice, according to what is said in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, "A pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons."[^5]

<span class="v-num">25</span> And behold, there was a man in Jerusalem whose name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Simeon, and this man <span class="added-text">(was)</span> righteous and devout, waiting for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> consolation of Israel, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit was upon him.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And it had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit, that he should not see death before he should see the Christ of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.[^6]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And he came in the Spirit into the temple; and when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him according to the custom of the law,[^7]

<span class="v-num">28</span> then he himself received him into his arms, and blessed God, and said,

<span class="v-num">29</span> "Now You are releasing your servant, Master, according to your word, in peace;[^8]

<span class="v-num">30</span> for my eyes have seen your salvation,

<span class="v-num">31</span> which you prepared before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of all the peoples;

<span class="v-num">32</span> a light for revelation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of your people Israel."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And Joseph and his mother were marveling at the things being spoken about him.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And Simeon blessed them, and said to Mary his mother, "Behold, this <span class="added-text">(child)</span> is appointed for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fall and rising again of many in Israel, and for a sign spoken against,

<span class="v-num">35</span> <span class="added-text">(yes, a sword shall pierce through your own soul also,)</span> that the thoughts of many hearts may be revealed."[^9]

<span class="v-num">36</span> And there was Anna, a prophetess, daughter of Phanuel, of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tribe of Asher; she was advanced in many days, having lived with a husband seven years from her virginity;

<span class="v-num">37</span> and she <span class="added-text">(was)</span> a widow of about eighty-four years, who did not depart from the temple, serving <span class="added-text">(God)</span> with fastings and prayers night and day.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And she, coming up at that very hour, gave thanks likewise to the Lord, and spoke of him to all those waiting for redemption in Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And when they had finished all things according to the law of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, they returned into Galilee, to their own city Nazareth.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And the child grew, and was strengthened in spirit, being filled with wisdom; and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> grace of God was upon him.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And his parents went yearly to Jerusalem at the feast of the Passover.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And when he became twelve years old, they having gone up to Jerusalem according to the custom of the feast,

<span class="v-num">43</span> and having finished the days, as they returned, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem; and Joseph and his mother did not know <span class="added-text">(it)</span>,

<span class="v-num">44</span> but supposing him to be in the company, they went a day's journey, and sought him among the relatives and acquaintances;

<span class="v-num">45</span> and not having found him, they returned to Jerusalem, seeking him.

<span class="v-num">46</span> And it happened, after three days, they found him in the temple, sitting in the midst of the teachers, both hearing them and questioning them.

<span class="v-num">47</span> And all who heard him were amazed at his understanding and his answers.

<span class="v-num">48</span> And having seen him, they were astonished; and his mother said to him, "Child, why did you do this to us? Behold, your father and I have been seeking you, being troubled."

<span class="v-num">49</span> And he said to them, "Why <span class="added-text">(is it)</span> that you sought me? Did you not know that I must be in the things (=house) of my Father?"[^10]

<span class="v-num">50</span> And they did not understand the saying which he spoke to them.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And he went down with them, and came to Nazareth, and was subject to them; and his mother kept all these sayings in her heart.

<span class="v-num">52</span> And Jesus advanced in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.

[^1]: ἀπογράφεσθαι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사로, 칙령이 발한 목적(호적 등록)을 가리킨다.
[^2]: TR은 εὐδοκία를 주격으로 읽어 '사람들에게 (하나님의) 기뻐하심'으로 옮기니, 이는 '땅의 평화·사람에 대한 은총'이라는 전통적 이해를 지지한다.
[^3]: παραστῆσαι는 목적 부정사로, 예루살렘으로 올라간 목적(주께 드림)을 나타낸다.
[^4]: 출애굽기 13:2, 12의 인용으로, 처음 난 것을 하나님께 드리는 규례를 가리킨다.
[^5]: τοῦ δοῦναι는 목적 부정사이며, 레위기 12:8의 인용으로 가난한 자를 위한 규례를 가리킨다.
[^6]: πρὶν ἢ ἴδῃ는 가정법을 취하여 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 사건을 나타낸다.
[^7]: τοῦ ποιῆσαι는 목적 부정사로, 부모가 성전에 들어온 목적을 나타낸다.
[^8]: δέσποτα는 절대적 주권자에 대한 호칭으로, 시므온이 하나님을 자신의 생사를 주관하시는 분으로 부르는 표현이다.
[^9]: ὅπως ἂν + 가정법은 목적절로, 마음의 생각들이 드러나게 될 목적을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἐν τοῖς τοῦ πατρός μου는 관사와 소유격만으로 '아버지의 집(일)'을 함축하는 관용 표현이다.
