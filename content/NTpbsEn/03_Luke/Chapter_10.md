<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things the Lord also appointed another seventy, and sent them two by two before his <span class="alt-label">face</span><span class="alt-text">(=ahead of him)</span>, into every city and place where he himself was about to go.

<span class="v-num">2</span> So he said to them, "The harvest <span class="added-text">(is)</span> indeed plentiful, but the workers <span class="added-text">(are)</span> few; therefore pray <span class="added-text">(to)</span> the lord of the harvest, that he would send out workers into his harvest.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Go; behold, I send you as lambs in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of wolves.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Do not carry a purse, nor a bag, nor sandals; and greet no one along the way.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And into whatever house you enter, first say, 'Peace to this house.'

<span class="v-num">6</span> And if indeed a son of peace is there, your peace will rest upon him; but if not, it will return to you.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And remain in that same house, eating and drinking the things from them; for the worker is worthy of his wage. Do not move from house to house.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And into whatever city you enter, and they receive you, eat the things set before you,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and heal the sick <span class="added-text">(who are)</span> in it, and say to them, 'The kingdom of God has come near to you.'

<span class="v-num">10</span> But into whatever city you enter, and they do not receive you, having gone out into its streets, say,

<span class="v-num">11</span> 'Even the dust that clung to us from your city we wipe off against you; nevertheless know this, that the kingdom of God has come near.'

<span class="v-num">12</span> But I say to you that it will be more tolerable for Sodom in that day than for that city.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the mighty works that were done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in the judgment than for you.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And you, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, will be brought down to Hades.

<span class="v-num">16</span> The one who hears you hears me; and the one who rejects you rejects me; and the one who rejects me rejects the one who sent me."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And the seventy returned with joy, saying, "Lord, even the demons are subject to us in your name."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And he said to them, "I was watching Satan fall like lightning from heaven.[^2]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Behold, I give you the authority to tread upon serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy; and nothing shall by any means hurt you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Nevertheless do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you; but rather rejoice that your names have been written in the heavens."[^4]

<span class="v-num">21</span> In that same hour Jesus rejoiced greatly in the spirit, and said, "I <span class="alt-label">thank</span><span class="alt-text">(=confess)</span> you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you have hidden these things from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wise and understanding, and have revealed them to infants; yes, Father, for so it was well-pleasing before you."[^5]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And having turned to the disciples he said, "All things have been delivered to me by my Father; and no one knows who the Son is except the Father, and who the Father is except the Son, and <span class="added-text">(he)</span> to whom the Son wills to reveal <span class="added-text">(him)</span>."[^6]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having turned to the disciples privately he said, "Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the eyes that see the things you see.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For I say to you that many prophets and kings desired to see the things you see, and did not see <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; and to hear the things you hear, and did not hear <span class="added-text">(them)</span>."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And behold, a certain lawyer stood up, testing him, and saying, "Teacher, what shall I do to inherit eternal life?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> And he said to him, "What is written in the law? How do you read <span class="added-text">(it)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">27</span> And he answering said, "You shall love <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your strength, and with all your mind; and your neighbor as yourself."[^7]

<span class="v-num">28</span> And he said to him, "You have answered rightly; do this, and you will live."

<span class="v-num">29</span> But he, wishing to justify himself, said to Jesus, "And who is my neighbor?"[^8]

<span class="v-num">30</span> And Jesus, taking up <span class="added-text">(the question)</span>, said, "A certain man was going down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell among robbers, who both stripped him and inflicted wounds and went away, leaving <span class="added-text">(him)</span> half dead.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And by coincidence a certain priest was going down on that road, and having seen him, passed by on the other side.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And likewise a Levite also, having come to the place, having come and seen <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, passed by on the other side.

<span class="v-num">33</span> But a certain Samaritan, journeying, came to where he was, and having seen him, was moved with compassion,

<span class="v-num">34</span> and having come to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, bound up his wounds, pouring on oil and wine; and having set him on his own animal, brought him to an inn, and took care of him.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And on the next day, when he departed, he took out two denarii and gave <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to the innkeeper, and said to him, 'Take care of him; and whatever you spend more, I, when I return, will repay you.'

<span class="v-num">36</span> Which of these three, then, does it seem to you, became a neighbor to the one who fell among the robbers?"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And he said, "The one who showed mercy <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> him." Then Jesus said to him, "Go, and you do likewise."[^9]

<span class="v-num">38</span> And it came to pass, as they were going, that he entered into a certain village; and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And this woman had a sister called Mary, who also, sitting beside the feet of Jesus, was hearing his word.

<span class="v-num">40</span> But Martha was distracted about much serving; and having come up, she said, "Lord, do you not care that my sister has left me alone to serve? Tell her then to help me."[^10]

<span class="v-num">41</span> And Jesus answering said to her, "Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things;

<span class="v-num">42</span> but one thing is needed; and Mary has chosen the good part, which will not be taken away from her."[^11]

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~라면'의 뜻으로 실제 그럴지 불확실한 상황을 가정한다. 평화의 아들이 있을지 없을지에 따라 결과가 달라짐을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἐθεώρουν은 미완료로, 순간적 사건이 아니라 지속적으로 지켜본 환상적 장면을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἐξουσίαν(권세)은 τοῦ ἐχθροῦ의 δύναμιν(능력)을 압도하는 권세로, 제자들의 사역이 사탄 권세를 이기는 것임을 보여준다.
[^4]: ἐγράφη(기록되었다)는 부정과거로, 이미 확정되어 남아 있는 결과를 강조한다.
[^5]: ἐξομολογοῦμαι는 단순한 감사를 넘어 '공개적으로 시인하며 찬양함'의 뜻을 담아 예수의 기도가 아버지의 뜻에 대한 확증임을 보인다.
[^6]: ᾧ ἐὰν βούληται는 '아들이 계시하고자 하는 자는 누구든지'라는 뜻으로, ἐάν+가정법이 관계대명사와 결합해 불특정 대상을 가리키는 조건적 관계절을 이룬다. 아들의 자유로운 계시 의지에 달린 개방적 가능성을 강조한다.
[^7]: 신명기 6:5과 레위기 19:18을 결합 인용한 것으로, 율법의 핵심을 하나님 사랑과 이웃 사랑으로 요약한다.
[^8]: δικαιοῦν ἑαυτόν은 '자기를 의롭다고 내세우려' 함을 뜻하며, 이어지는 비유의 배경이 된다.
[^9]: 율법사가 처음 물었던 '누가 내 이웃인가'라는 질문이 '누가 이웃이 되어 주었는가'로 전환되어, 사랑의 실천이 이웃 관계를 규정함을 보인다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(συναντιλάβηται)은 εἰπέ의 목적/내용절로, '~하도록 말해달라'는 명령의 내용을 나타낸다. 마르다가 예수께 바라는 구체적 목적(동생이 돕게 되는 것)을 표현하는 구문이다.
[^11]: μερίδα(몫, 분깃)는 식사의 몫을 넘어 하나님 앞에서의 영적 분깃을 가리키며, 마리아의 선택이 영구히 지속됨을 강조한다.
