<span class="v-num">1</span> And it came to pass afterward, that he himself went about through <span class="added-text">(every)</span> city and village, preaching and proclaiming the good news <span class="added-text">(of)</span> the kingdom of God; and the twelve <span class="added-text">(were)</span> with him,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and certain women who had been healed from evil spirits and infirmities, Mary called Magdalene, from whom seven demons had gone out,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and Joanna <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wife of Chuza Herod's steward, and Susanna, and many other <span class="added-text">(women)</span>, who ministered to him from their possessions.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when a great crowd was gathering, and those from <span class="added-text">(every)</span> city were coming to him, he spoke by <span class="added-text">(a)</span> parable,

<span class="v-num">5</span> "The sower went out to sow his seed; and as he sowed, some fell beside the road, and was trodden down, and the birds of the heaven devoured it.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell on the rock, and having sprung up, it withered, because it had no moisture.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell in the midst of the thorns, and the thorns grew up with it and choked it.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell on the good ground, and having sprung up, it made fruit a hundredfold." Saying these things he called out, "He who has ears to hear, let him hear."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And his disciples asked him, saying, "What might this parable be?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> And he said, "To you it has been given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to the rest <span class="added-text">(it is given)</span> in parables, that seeing they may not see, and hearing they may not understand.[^1]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now this is the parable: the seed is the word of God.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And those beside the road are those who hear; then the devil comes and takes away the word from their heart, lest believing they should be saved.[^2]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And those on the rock are they who, when they hear, receive the word with joy; and these have no root, who believe for a time, and in time of testing fall away.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And that which fell into the thorns, these are they who have heard, and going on their way are choked by anxieties and riches and pleasures of life, and bring no fruit to maturity.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And that on the good ground, these are they who, having heard the word with a noble and good heart, hold it fast, and bear fruit with patience."

<span class="v-num">16</span> "But no one, having lit a lamp, covers it with a vessel, or puts <span class="added-text">(it)</span> under a bed; but he puts <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on a lampstand, that those who come in may see the light.[^3]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For there is nothing hidden that will not become manifest; nor anything concealed that will not be known and come to light.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Take heed therefore how you hear; for whoever has, to him will be given; and whoever does not have, even what he seems to have will be taken from him."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And his mother and his brothers came to him, and could not reach him because of the crowd.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And it was told him, some saying, "Your mother and your brothers stand outside, wishing to see you."

<span class="v-num">21</span> But he answered and said to them, "My mother and my brothers are these, who hear the word of God and do it."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And it came to pass, on one of the days, that he got into a boat with his disciples, and he said to them, "Let us go over to the other side of the lake." And they set out.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But as they sailed he fell asleep. And a storm of wind came down on the lake; and they were being filled <span class="added-text">(with water)</span>, and were in danger.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And they came to him and woke him, saying, "Master, Master, we are perishing!" But he arose and rebuked the wind and the raging of the water; and they ceased, and there was a calm.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And he said to them, "Where is your faith?" And being afraid they marveled, saying to one another, "Who then is this, that he commands even the winds and the water, and they obey him?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> And they sailed to the country of the Gadarenes, which is opposite Galilee.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And when he went out onto the land, a certain man from the city met him, who had demons for a long time, and wore no clothing, and did not stay in a house, but among the tombs.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And when he saw Jesus, and cried out, he fell down before him, and said with a loud voice, "What <span class="added-text">(do I have)</span> to do with you, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I beg you, do not torment me."

<span class="v-num">29</span> For he was commanding the unclean spirit to come out of the man. For it had seized him many times; and he was bound with chains and shackles, being kept, and breaking the bonds he was driven by the demon into the deserts.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And Jesus asked him, saying, "What is your name?" And he said, "Legion," because many demons had entered into him.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And he begged him that he would not command them to go away into the abyss.[^4]

<span class="v-num">32</span> Now there was there a herd of many pigs feeding on the mountain; and they begged him that he would permit them to enter into those. And he permitted them.[^5]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And the demons went out from the man and entered into the pigs; and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the lake, and was choked.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And when those who fed <span class="added-text">(them)</span> saw what had happened, they fled, and going away told <span class="added-text">(it)</span> in the city and in the country.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And they went out to see what had happened; and they came to Jesus, and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting, clothed and in his right mind, at the feet of Jesus; and they were afraid.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And those who had seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span> told them how the demon-possessed man had been saved.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And the whole multitude of the region of the Gadarenes around asked him to depart from them, for they were seized with great fear. And he got into the boat and returned.

<span class="v-num">38</span> But the man from whom the demons had gone out begged him that he might be with him. But Jesus sent him away, saying,

<span class="v-num">39</span> "Return to your house, and relate how much God has done for you." And he went away, proclaiming through the whole city how much Jesus had done for him.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And it came to pass, when Jesus returned, the crowd welcomed him; for they were all waiting for him.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And behold, there came a man whose name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Jairus, and he himself was a ruler of the synagogue; and falling at the feet of Jesus, he begged him to come into his house;

<span class="v-num">42</span> for he had an only daughter, about twelve years <span class="added-text">(old)</span>, and she was dying. But as he went, the crowds pressed upon him.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And a woman having a flow of blood for twelve years, who had spent all her living on physicians, and could not be healed by any,

<span class="v-num">44</span> came behind <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, and touched the border of his garment; and immediately the flow of her blood stopped.

<span class="v-num">45</span> And Jesus said, "Who touched me?" And when all denied <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, Peter and those with him said, "Master, the crowds surround you and press upon <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, and you say, 'Who touched me?'"

<span class="v-num">46</span> But Jesus said, "Someone touched me; for I perceived power going out from me."

<span class="v-num">47</span> And when the woman saw that she was not hidden, she came trembling, and falling down before him, declared to him before all the people for what cause she had touched him, and how she was healed immediately.

<span class="v-num">48</span> And he said to her, "Take courage, daughter; your faith has saved you; go into peace."

<span class="v-num">49</span> While he was still speaking, someone comes from the ruler of the synagogue's <span class="added-text">(house)</span>, saying to him, "Your daughter has died; do not trouble the teacher."

<span class="v-num">50</span> But Jesus, hearing <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, answered him, saying, "Do not fear; only believe, and she will be saved."

<span class="v-num">51</span> And when he came into the house, he permitted no one to go in, except Peter, and James, and John, and the father of the child and the mother.

<span class="v-num">52</span> And all were weeping and mourning for her; but he said, "Do not weep; she is not dead, but sleeps."

<span class="v-num">53</span> And they laughed him to scorn, knowing that she had died.

<span class="v-num">54</span> But he, putting them all outside, and taking hold of her hand, called, saying, "Child, arise."

<span class="v-num">55</span> And her spirit returned, and she arose immediately; and he directed <span class="added-text">(that)</span> something be given her to eat.

<span class="v-num">56</span> And her parents were astonished; but he charged them to tell no one what had happened.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(βλέπωσι, συνιῶσι) 목적절로, 비유로 말씀하시는 목적(의도)을 나타낸다. 단순한 결과가 아니라 하나님의 주권적 섭리 안에서 듣는 자의 반응에 따라 깨달음이 좌우됨을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(σωθῶσιν) 목적절로, 마귀가 말씀을 빼앗는 행위의 목적(의도)을 나타낸다. 마귀의 궁극적 목적은 사람들이 믿어 구원받지 못하게 막는 것임을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(βλέπωσι)으로 등불을 등잔대 위에 두는 목적을 나타낸다. 즉 '들어가는 자들이 빛을 보게 하려고'라는 의도된 결과를 강조하는 목적절이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιτάξῃ)으로 간청의 내용(목적절)을 나타냄. 예수께 무저갱으로 보내지 말아 달라고 요청하는 실제 바람을 표현한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιτρέψῃ)은 간청의 내용(목적)을 나타내는 목적절로, '허락해 주시기를' 간구하는 행위의 구체적 내용을 이끈다.
