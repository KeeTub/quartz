<span class="v-num">1</span> And having entered, he was passing through Jericho.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And behold, a man named <span class="added-text">(and)</span> called Zacchaeus—he himself was a chief tax collector, and this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> was rich.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he was seeking to see Jesus, who he was, and was not able because of the crowd, for he was small in stature.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And having run ahead in front, he went up on a sycamore tree so that (=in order to) he might see him, for he was about to pass through that <span class="added-text">(way)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And as he came to the place, Jesus, having looked up, saw him, and said to him, "Zacchaeus, having hastened, come down, for today I must stay in your house."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And having hastened, he came down, and received him, rejoicing.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, all were murmuring, saying, "He has entered to lodge with a sinful man."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Zacchaeus, having stood, said to the Lord, "Behold, Lord, the half of my possessions I <span class="alt-label">give</span><span class="alt-text">(=am giving)</span> to the poor, and if I have extorted anything from anyone, I restore <span class="added-text">(it)</span> fourfold."[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And Jesus said to him, "Today salvation has come to this house, because he himself also is a son of Abraham."

<span class="v-num">10</span> For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> having been lost.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And as they were hearing these things, having added, he spoke a parable, because he was near Jerusalem, and <span class="added-text">(because)</span> they supposed that the kingdom of God was about to appear immediately.

<span class="v-num">12</span> He said therefore, "A certain nobleman went into a far country, to receive for himself a kingdom, and to return."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And having called ten servants of his own, he gave them ten minas, and said to them, 'Do business until I come.'

<span class="v-num">14</span> But his citizens hated him, and sent a delegation after him, saying, 'We do not want this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> to reign over us.'

<span class="v-num">15</span> And it came to pass, when he returned, having received the kingdom, that he said the servants to be called to him, to whom he had given the money, so that (=in order that) he might know what each had gained by trading.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the first came, saying, 'Lord, your mina has gained ten minas.'

<span class="v-num">17</span> And he said to him, "Well <span class="added-text">(done)</span>, good servant, because you have been faithful in a very little, be having authority over ten cities."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the second came, saying, 'Lord, your mina has made five minas.'

<span class="v-num">19</span> And to this one also he said, "You also, be over five cities."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And another came, saying, 'Lord, behold, your mina, which I kept laid up in a handkerchief.'

<span class="v-num">21</span> For I feared you, because you are a <span class="alt-label">strict</span><span class="alt-text">(=austere)</span> man; you take up what you did not lay down, and reap what you did not sow.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And he said to him, "Out of your own mouth I will judge you, wicked servant. You knew that I am a <span class="alt-label">strict</span><span class="alt-text">(=austere)</span> man, taking up what I did not lay down, and reaping what I did not sow;"

<span class="v-num">23</span> then why did you not give my money to the <span class="alt-label">bank</span><span class="alt-text">(=table)</span>, and I, having come, would have collected it with interest?"

<span class="v-num">24</span> And to those standing by he said, "Take from him the mina, and give <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the one having the ten minas."

<span class="v-num">25</span> <span class="added-text">(And they said to him, 'Lord, he has ten minas.')</span>

<span class="v-num">26</span> "For I say to you, that to everyone having, it will be given; but from the one not having, even what he has will be taken away from him."

<span class="v-num">27</span> But those enemies of mine, the ones not having wanted me to reign over them, bring <span class="added-text">(them)</span> here, and slay <span class="added-text">(them)</span> before me."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And having said these things, he went on ahead, going up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And it came to pass, as he came near to Bethphage and Bethany, at the mountain called <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Olives, he sent two of his disciples,

<span class="v-num">30</span> saying, "Go into the village opposite <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, in which, entering, you will find a colt having been tied, on which no one of men has ever yet sat; having loosed it, bring <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">31</span> And if anyone asks you, 'Why are you loosing <span class="added-text">(it)</span>?' thus you shall say to him, 'The Lord has need of it.'"[^6]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And those having been sent, having gone, found <span class="added-text">(it)</span> just as he had told them.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And as they were loosing the colt, its owners said to them, "Why are you loosing the colt?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> And they said, "The Lord has need of it."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And they brought it to Jesus; and having thrown their own garments on the colt, they set Jesus on <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And as he was going, they were spreading their garments in the way.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And as he was already drawing near, at the descent of the Mount of Olives, the whole multitude of the disciples began, rejoicing, to praise God with a loud voice concerning all the mighty works which they had seen,

<span class="v-num">38</span> saying, "Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the King who <span class="alt-label">comes</span><span class="alt-text">(=coming)</span> in the name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord; peace in heaven, and glory in the highest."[^7]

<span class="v-num">39</span> And some of the Pharisees from the crowd said to him, "Teacher, rebuke your disciples."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And having answered, he said to them, "I say to you, that if these should be silent, the stones will cry out."[^8]

<span class="v-num">41</span> And as he drew near, having seen the city, he wept over it,

<span class="v-num">42</span> saying, "If you also had known, even you, at least in this your day, the things toward <span class="added-text">(your)</span> peace!—but now they are hidden from your eyes."

<span class="v-num">43</span> For days will come upon you, and your enemies will cast up a <span class="alt-label">rampart</span><span class="alt-text">(=siege wall)</span> around you, and will surround you, and will hem you in on every side,

<span class="v-num">44</span> and will level you to the ground, and your children within you, and will not leave in you stone upon stone; because you did not know the time of your visitation."[^9]

<span class="v-num">45</span> And having entered into the temple, he began to cast out those selling in it, and <span class="added-text">(those)</span> buying,

<span class="v-num">46</span> saying to them, "It is written, 'My house is a house of prayer'; but you have made it a den of robbers."[^10]

<span class="v-num">47</span> And he was teaching daily in the temple; but the chief priests and the scribes were seeking to destroy him, and the leading <span class="added-text">(men)</span> of the people;

<span class="v-num">48</span> and they were not finding what they might do, for all the people were hanging upon him, listening.

[^1]: 미완료 διήρχετο는 지나가는 과정이 계속되었음을 나타내나 개역체 서사는 과거형으로 옮김
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδῃ) 목적절로, 삭개오가 나무에 오른 목적이 예수를 보기 위함임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 현재 시제 δίδωμι, ἀποδίδωμι는 이미 행하던 습관인지 지금 서원하는 결단인지 논란이 있으나, 문맥상 지금 하는 서원의 선언으로 봄이 자연스럽다.
[^4]: 인자가 오신 목적을 요약하는 핵심 구절로, '잃어버린 자'(τὸ ἀπολωλός)는 죄로 인해 하나님과의 관계를 잃은 인류를 가리킨다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(γνῷ)은 목적절로, 종들을 부르라 명한 목적이 '각자 어떻게 장사했는지 알기 위함'임을 나타낸다. 왕의 의도적이고 확실한 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἐρωτᾷ)으로 아직 일어나지 않았지만 충분히 일어날 수 있는 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절이다. '만일 누가 물으면(실제로 그럴 수 있으니)'이라는 뉘앙스로, 제자들에게 예상되는 상황에 대한 구체적 대응 지침을 준다.
[^7]: 시편 118:26을 인용하되 '왕'을 덧붙여 메시아적으로 적용함.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(σιωπήσωσιν)의 아직 실현되지 않은 조건으로, 제자들이 잠잠할 경우 돌들이 외치리라는 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다.
[^9]: '돌보아 주시는 때'(ἐπισκοπή)는 하나님께서 메시아를 통해 자기 백성을 찾아오신 은혜의 때를 가리킨다.
[^10]: 이사야 56:7과 예레미야 7:11을 결합하여 인용함.
