<span class="v-num">1</span> And it happened, while he was praying in a certain place, when he ceased, one of his disciples said to him, "Lord, teach us to pray, just as John also taught his disciples."

<span class="v-num">2</span> But he said to them, "When you pray, say, Our Father who <span class="added-text">(art)</span> in the heavens, hallowed be your name. Your kingdom come. Your will be done, as in heaven, also on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> earth.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Give us our <span class="alt-label">daily</span><span class="alt-text">(=needed)</span> bread each day;

<span class="v-num">4</span> and forgive us our sins, for we ourselves also forgive everyone indebted to us; and do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>."

<span class="v-num">5</span> But he said to them again, "Which of you shall have a friend, and go to him at midnight, and say to him, 'Friend, lend me three loaves,

<span class="v-num">6</span> for a friend of mine has come to me from a journey, and I have nothing to set before him,' saying,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and he from within answering shall say, 'Do not cause me troubles; the door is already shut, and my children are with me in bed; I am not able, having risen, to give <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to you'?

<span class="v-num">8</span> I say to you, even if he will not, having risen, give <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to him because of his being his friend, yet because of his persistence, having risen, he will give him as much as he needs.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And I say to you, 'Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.'

<span class="v-num">10</span> For everyone asking receives; and the one seeking finds; and to the one knocking it will be opened.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But of which of you, being a father, if the son asks <span class="added-text">(for)</span> bread, will he give him a stone? Or if also a fish, will he instead of a fish give him a serpent?

<span class="v-num">12</span> Or also if he should ask <span class="added-text">(for)</span> an egg, will he give him a scorpion?[^2]

<span class="v-num">13</span> If therefore you, being evil, know <span class="added-text">(how)</span> to give good gifts to your children, how much more shall the Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> from heaven give <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit to those asking him?"

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he was casting out a demon, and it was mute. And it happened, when the demon had gone out, the mute <span class="added-text">(man)</span> spoke; and the crowds marveled.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But some of them said, "By Beelzebul, the ruler of the demons, he casts out demons."

<span class="v-num">16</span> And others, testing <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, were seeking from him a sign from heaven.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But he, knowing their thoughts, said to them, "Every kingdom divided against itself is made desolate; and a house <span class="added-text">(divided)</span> against a house falls.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And if Satan also is divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand? Because you say <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I cast out demons by Beelzebul.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But if I by Beelzebul cast out demons, by whom do your sons cast <span class="added-text">(them)</span> out? Because of this they themselves shall be your judges.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But if I by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> finger of God cast out demons, then the kingdom of God has come upon you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">21</span> When the strong <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, having been armed, guards his own <span class="alt-label">courtyard</span><span class="alt-text">(=palace)</span>, his possessions are in peace;

<span class="v-num">22</span> but when <span class="added-text">(one)</span> stronger than he, having come upon <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, overcomes him, he takes away his armor in which he trusted, and distributes his spoils.

<span class="v-num">23</span> The one not being with me is against me; and the one not gathering with me scatters.

<span class="v-num">24</span> When the unclean spirit goes out from the man, it passes through waterless places, seeking rest; and not finding <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, it says, 'I will return to my house from where I came out.'

<span class="v-num">25</span> And having come, it finds <span class="added-text">(it)</span> swept and adorned.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Then it goes and takes with <span class="added-text">(it)</span> seven other spirits more evil than itself, and having entered, they dwell there; and the last <span class="added-text">(state)</span> of that man becomes worse than the first."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And it happened, while he was saying these things, a certain woman from the crowd, having raised <span class="added-text">(her)</span> voice, said to him, "Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the womb that bore you, and the breasts which you sucked."

<span class="v-num">28</span> But he said, "Rather, blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> those hearing the word of God and keeping it."

<span class="v-num">29</span> But as the crowds were gathering together, he began to say, "This generation is an evil generation; it seeks a sign, and a sign shall not be given to it except the sign of Jonah the prophet.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For just as Jonah became a sign to the Ninevites, so also will the Son of man be to this generation.

<span class="v-num">31</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> queen of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> south will rise up in the judgment with the men of this generation, and will condemn them; for she came from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and behold, a greater <span class="added-text">(one)</span> than Solomon <span class="added-text">(is)</span> here.

<span class="v-num">32</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> men of Nineveh will rise up in the judgment with this generation, and will condemn it; for they repented at the preaching of Jonah, and behold, a greater <span class="added-text">(one)</span> than Jonah <span class="added-text">(is)</span> here.

<span class="v-num">33</span> No one, having lit a lamp, puts <span class="added-text">(it)</span> in a hidden place, nor under the measuring basket, but on the lampstand, so that those entering may see the light.[^4]

<span class="v-num">34</span> The lamp of the body is the eye; therefore when your eye is <span class="alt-label">single</span><span class="alt-text">(=clear)</span>, your whole body also is full of light; but when it is evil, your body also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> dark.

<span class="v-num">35</span> Watch therefore, lest the light that is in you is darkness.

<span class="v-num">36</span> If therefore your whole body <span class="added-text">(is)</span> full of light, having no part dark, it will be full of light, as when the lamp with <span class="added-text">(its)</span> bright shining gives you light."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And as he spoke, a certain Pharisee asked him that he might dine with him; and having entered, he sat down.

<span class="v-num">38</span> But the Pharisee, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, marveled that he had not first washed before the meal.

<span class="v-num">39</span> But the Lord said to him, "Now you Pharisees cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish; but your inward part is full of greed and wickedness.

<span class="v-num">40</span> Fools! Did not he who made the outside also make the inside?

<span class="v-num">41</span> But give as alms the things that are within, and behold, all things are clean to you.

<span class="v-num">42</span> But woe to you, Pharisees! For you tithe mint and rue and every herb, and pass over justice and the love of God; these you ought to have done, and not to have left the others undone.

<span class="v-num">43</span> Woe to you, Pharisees! For you love the chief seat in the synagogues, and the greetings in the marketplaces.

<span class="v-num">44</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are as the unseen tombs, and the men walking over <span class="added-text">(them)</span> do not know <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">45</span> And one of the lawyers answering said to him, "Teacher, saying these things you insult us also."

<span class="v-num">46</span> And he said, "Woe to you also, lawyers! For you burden men with burdens hard to bear, and you yourselves do not touch the burdens with one of your fingers.

<span class="v-num">47</span> Woe to you! For you build the tombs of the prophets, and your fathers killed them.

<span class="v-num">48</span> So you bear witness and consent to the works of your fathers; for they indeed killed them, and you build their tombs.

<span class="v-num">49</span> Because of this also the wisdom of God said, 'I will send to them prophets and apostles, and <span class="added-text">(some)</span> of them they will kill and persecute,'

<span class="v-num">50</span> so that the blood of all the prophets, shed from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, may be required of this generation,[^5]

<span class="v-num">51</span> from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of Abel to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of Zechariah, who perished between the altar and the <span class="alt-label">house</span><span class="alt-text">(=temple)</span>. Yes, I say to you, it shall be required of this generation.[^6]

<span class="v-num">52</span> Woe to you, lawyers! For you took away the key of knowledge; you yourselves did not enter in, and those entering in you hindered."

<span class="v-num">53</span> And as he said these things to them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to press <span class="added-text">(him)</span> vehemently, and to question him closely about many things,

<span class="v-num">54</span> lying in wait for him, and seeking to catch something out of his mouth, that they might accuse him.[^7]

[^1]: TR(Scrivener)은 마태복음 6:9과 조화되어 '하늘에 계신 우리 아버지여'라는 긴 형태를 채택했으나, 다른 사본 전통은 누가복음에서 단순히 '아버지여'로만 되어 있다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(αἰτήσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로 '혹시 ~을 구한다면'의 뜻이다. 앞선 떡과 뱀의 비유와 짝을 이루어 자녀의 요청에 대한 아버지의 반응을 예로 드는 수사적 조건문이다.
[^3]: '하나님의 손가락'은 마 12:28의 '하나님의 성령'과 병행되는 표현으로, 하나님의 직접적인 능력과 통치의 임재를 가리킨다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 등불을 등잔대 위에 두는 목적이 '들어가는 자들이 빛을 보게 함'임을 밝힌다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 이 세대가 창세로부터 흘린 모든 선지자의 피를 담당하게 되는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: 아벨(창세기)부터 스가랴(역대하, 히브리 성경 정경의 마지막 책)까지는 구약 정경 전체에 걸친 순교자의 피를 포괄적으로 가리키는 표현이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(κατηγορήσωσιν)은 목적절로, 그들이 예수의 말을 트집 잡아 고발하려는 의도를 나타낸다. 즉 앞선 행동(엿보고 노림)의 목적이 바로 이 고발에 있음을 강조한다.
