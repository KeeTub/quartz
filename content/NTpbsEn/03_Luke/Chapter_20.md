<span class="v-num">1</span> And it happened on one of those days, as he was teaching the people in the temple and preaching the gospel, the chief priests and the scribes came upon <span class="added-text">(him)</span> together with the elders,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and they spoke to him, saying, "Tell us, by what authority you do these things, or who is the one having given you this authority?"

<span class="v-num">3</span> But answering he said to them, "I also will ask you one <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=question)</span>, and tell me:

<span class="v-num">4</span> The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or from men?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> But they reasoned among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Why then did you not believe him?'[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> but if we say, 'From men,' all the people will stone us, for they are persuaded <span class="added-text">(that)</span> John was a prophet."[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And they answered that they did not know from where.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Jesus said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he began to speak to the people this parable: "A certain man planted a vineyard, and let it out to farmers, and went away to a foreign land for a long <span class="alt-label">time</span><span class="alt-text">(=for many seasons)</span>.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And at <span class="added-text">(the proper)</span> time he sent a servant to the farmers, that they might give him from the fruit of the vineyard; but the farmers, beating him, sent <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away empty.[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And he added to send another servant; but they, beating him also and dishonoring <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, sent <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away empty.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And he added to send a third; but they, wounding him also, cast <span class="added-text">(him)</span> out.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Then the lord of the vineyard said, 'What shall I do? I will send my beloved son; perhaps, seeing this one, they will respect <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.'

<span class="v-num">14</span> But the farmers, seeing him, reasoned among themselves, saying, 'This is the heir; come, let us kill him, that the inheritance may become ours.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And casting him out of the vineyard, they killed <span class="added-text">(him)</span>. What then will the lord of the vineyard do to them?

<span class="v-num">16</span> He will come and destroy these farmers, and give the vineyard to others." But hearing <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, they said, "May it never be."

<span class="v-num">17</span> But he, looking upon them, said, "What then is this that is written, 'The stone which the builders rejected, this one became <span class="added-text">(the)</span> head of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> corner'?

<span class="v-num">18</span> Everyone falling upon that stone will be broken to pieces; but on whomever it falls, it will grind him to powder."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the chief priests and the scribes sought to lay hands on him in that very hour, but they feared the people; for they knew that he spoke this parable against them.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And watching <span class="added-text">(him)</span> closely, they sent spies, pretending themselves to be righteous, that they might catch him in <span class="added-text">(his)</span> word, in order to deliver him to the rule and to the authority of the governor.[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they asked him, saying, "Teacher, we know that you say and teach rightly, and you do not accept <span class="added-text">(a)</span> <span class="alt-label">face</span><span class="alt-text">(=show partiality)</span>, but in truth teach the way of God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Is it lawful for us to give tribute to Caesar, or not?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> But perceiving their craftiness, he said to them, "Why do you test me?

<span class="v-num">24</span> Show me a denarius; whose image and inscription does it have?" And answering they said, "Caesar's."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And he said to them, "Then render the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And they were not able to catch him in a word before the people; and marveling at his answer, they were silent.

<span class="v-num">27</span> Then some of the Sadducees, who deny <span class="added-text">(that)</span> there is a resurrection, came to him and asked him,

<span class="v-num">28</span> saying, "Teacher, Moses wrote for us, if anyone's brother, having a wife, should die, and this one should die childless, that his brother should take the wife, and raise up seed for his brother.[^6]

<span class="v-num">29</span> There were then seven brothers; and the first, taking a wife, died childless.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And the second took the wife, and he died childless.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And the third took her; and likewise also the seven left no children, and died.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Last of all the woman also died.

<span class="v-num">33</span> In the resurrection therefore, whose wife does she become? For the seven had her as wife."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And answering, Jesus said to them, "The sons of this age marry and are given in marriage;

<span class="v-num">35</span> but those having been counted worthy to attain that age, and the resurrection from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, neither marry nor are given in marriage;

<span class="v-num">36</span> for neither can they die anymore; for they are equal to angels, and are sons of God, being sons of the resurrection.

<span class="v-num">37</span> But that the dead are raised, even Moses showed at the bush, as he calls <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.

<span class="v-num">38</span> But God is not <span class="added-text">(the God)</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, but of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living; for all live to him."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And answering, some of the scribes said, "Teacher, you have said well."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And they no longer dared to ask him anything.

<span class="v-num">41</span> But he said to them, "How do they say the Christ is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of David?

<span class="v-num">42</span> And David himself says in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> book of Psalms, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand,

<span class="v-num">43</span> until I put your enemies <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a footstool of your feet."'

<span class="v-num">44</span> David therefore calls him Lord, and how is he his son?"

<span class="v-num">45</span> And while all the people were listening, he said to his disciples,

<span class="v-num">46</span> "Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk about in long robes, and love greetings in the marketplaces, and the chief seats in the synagogues, and the chief places at the feasts,

<span class="v-num">47</span> who devour the houses of widows, and for a pretense make long prayers. These will receive greater condemnation."

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 아직 결정되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3형 조건문으로, '만일 우리가 말한다면'이라는 가상의 상황을 전제한다. 이는 종교 지도자들이 곤란한 딜레마 앞에서 계산적으로 대응을 저울질하는 모습을 보여준다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 아직 결정되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 우리가 ~라고 말한다면'이라는 열린 가정을 뜻한다. 종교 지도자들이 답변의 결과를 두려워하며 계산하는 심리를 보여준다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(δῶσιν)은 종을 보낸 목적을 나타냅니다: '열매를 받아오게 하려고'. 주인의 정당한 요구와 기대를 강조하는 목적절입니다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)은 목적절로, 농부들이 상속자를 죽이려는 '의도·목적'을 나타낸다. 즉 '유업을 차지하려는 목적으로' 죽이자는 계획된 악한 의도를 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιλάβωνται)은 첩자들을 보낸 목적을 나타낸다: 예수님의 말씀에서 트집을 잡아 총독에게 넘기려는 의도적 함정이었음을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀποθάνῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절('만일 ~하면')이며, 뒤이은 ἵνα+가정법(λάβῃ)은 모세 율법이 규정하는 목적('~하도록')을 나타낸다.
