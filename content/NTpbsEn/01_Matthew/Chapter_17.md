<span class="v-num">1</span> And after six days, Jesus took Peter and James and John his brother, and brought them up into a high mountain by <span class="alt-label">themselves</span><span class="alt-text">(=privately)</span>.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And he was transfigured before them, and his face shone as the sun, and his garments became white as the light.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And behold, Moses and Elijah appeared to them, talking with him.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Peter answered and said to Jesus, "Lord, it is good for us to be here; if you <span class="alt-label">want</span><span class="alt-text">(=are willing)</span>, let us make here three tabernacles, one for you, and one for Moses, and one for Elijah."

<span class="v-num">5</span> While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them; and behold, a voice out of the cloud, saying, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear him."[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And when the disciples heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they fell on their face, and were exceedingly afraid.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And Jesus came and touched them, and said, "Rise, and do not be afraid."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And when they lifted up their eyes, they saw no one, except Jesus only.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And as they were coming down from the mountain, Jesus commanded them, saying, "Tell no one the vision, until the Son of Man is risen from the dead."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And his disciples asked him, saying, "Why then do the scribes say that Elijah must come first?"

<span class="v-num">11</span> And Jesus answered and said to them, "Elijah indeed comes first, and will restore all things;

<span class="v-num">12</span> but I say to you, that Elijah has already come, and they did not recognize him, but did to him whatever they <span class="alt-label">wanted</span><span class="alt-text">(=wished)</span>; thus also the Son of Man is about to suffer by them."[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Then the disciples understood that he spoke to them concerning John the Baptist.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And when they came to the crowd, a man came to him, kneeling to him, and saying,

<span class="v-num">15</span> "Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is <span class="alt-label">epileptic</span><span class="alt-text">(=lunatic)</span> and suffers grievously; for often he falls into the fire, and often into the water.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And I brought him to your disciples, and they could not heal him."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus answered and said, "O unbelieving and perverted generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I bear with you? Bring him here to me."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Jesus rebuked <span class="alt-label">it</span><span class="alt-text">(=him)</span>, and the demon went out from him, and the <span class="alt-label">boy</span><span class="alt-text">(=child)</span> was healed from that hour.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Then the disciples came to Jesus privately, and said, "Why could we not cast it out?"

<span class="v-num">20</span> And Jesus said to them, "Because of your unbelief. For truly I say to you, if you have faith as a grain of mustard seed, you shall say to this mountain, 'Move from here to there,' and it shall move; and nothing shall be impossible to you.[^4]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But this kind does not go out, except by prayer and fasting."[^5]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And while they were <span class="alt-label">staying</span><span class="alt-text">(=abiding)</span> in Galilee, Jesus said to them, "The Son of Man is about to be delivered into the hands of men,

<span class="v-num">23</span> and they will kill him, and on the third day he will be raised." And they were exceedingly sorrowful.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And when they came to Capernaum, those who received the <span class="alt-label">half-shekel</span><span class="alt-text">(=temple tax)</span> came to Peter, and said, "Does your teacher not pay the <span class="alt-label">half-shekel</span><span class="alt-text">(=temple tax)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">25</span> He said, "Yes." And when he entered into the house, Jesus <span class="alt-label">anticipated</span><span class="alt-text">(=spoke first to)</span> him, saying, "What do you think, Simon? From whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? From their sons, or from <span class="alt-label">strangers</span><span class="alt-text">(=others)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> Peter said to him, "From <span class="alt-label">strangers</span><span class="alt-text">(=others)</span>." Jesus said to him, "Then the sons are free.

<span class="v-num">27</span> But that we may not offend <span class="alt-label">them</span><span class="alt-text">(=cause them to stumble)</span>, go to the sea, and cast a hook, and take up the first fish that comes up; and when you have opened its mouth, you will find a <span class="alt-label">stater</span><span class="alt-text">(=coin)</span>; take that, and give it to them <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=instead of)</span> me and you."[^6]

[^1]: μετεμορφώθη(변형되다)는 신적 영광이 겉으로 드러나는 순간을 나타내는 부정과거 수동태로, 예수의 본래 영광이 잠시 나타난 것을 가리킨다.
[^2]: 하늘의 음성은 시편 2:7과 이사야 42:1을 배경으로 하여 예수를 메시아 왕이자 여호와의 종으로 선포한다.
[^3]: 여기서 엘리야는 세례 요한을 가리키는 것으로, 예수께서 요한의 사역을 엘리야적 사역의 성취로 해석하신다.
[^4]: ἐὰν ἔχητε(만일 너희가 가지면)는 3급 조건문으로, 아직 실현되지 않았으나 가능한 믿음의 조건을 가정한다.
[^5]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 주요 사본(시내사본, 바티칸사본 등)에 없다는 사유로 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^6]: ἵνα μὴ σκανδαλίσωμεν(우리가 실족시키지 않기 위하여)은 목적을 나타내는 ἵνα+가정법 구문이다.
