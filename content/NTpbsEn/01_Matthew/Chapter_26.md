<span class="v-num">1</span> And it happened, when Jesus finished all these <span class="alt-label">words</span><span class="alt-text">(=sayings)</span>, he said to his disciples,

<span class="v-num">2</span> "You know that after two days the Passover takes place, and the Son of Man is delivered up to be crucified."

<span class="v-num">3</span> Then the chief priests and the scribes and the elders of the people gathered together to the <span class="alt-label">courtyard</span><span class="alt-text">(=palace)</span> of the high priest called Caiaphas,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and took counsel together that they might seize Jesus by deceit and kill <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But they were saying, "Not during the feast, lest there be an uproar among the people."[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now when Jesus was in Bethany, in the house of Simon the leper,

<span class="v-num">7</span> a woman having an alabaster flask of very costly ointment came to him, and poured <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on his head as he reclined <span class="added-text">(at table)</span>.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But his disciples, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, were indignant, saying, "To what <span class="added-text">(purpose is)</span> this waste?

<span class="v-num">9</span> For this ointment could have been sold for much, and given to the poor."

<span class="v-num">10</span> But Jesus, knowing <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said to them, "Why do you trouble the woman? For she has worked a good work toward me.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For you always have the poor with yourselves, but me you do not always have.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For she, in pouring this ointment on my body, did <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to prepare me for burial.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Truly I say to you, wherever this gospel is proclaimed in the whole world, what she has done will also be told, for a memorial of her."[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Then one of the twelve, called Judas Iscariot, having gone to the chief priests,

<span class="v-num">15</span> said, "What are you willing to give me, and I will deliver him to you?" And they weighed out to him thirty pieces of silver.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And from that time he was seeking an opportunity that he might deliver him <span class="added-text">(up)</span>.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Now on the first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of Unleavened Bread the disciples came to Jesus, saying to him, "Where do you want us to prepare for you to eat the Passover?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> And he said, "Go into the city to a certain <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and say to him, 'The Teacher says, My time is near; I will keep the Passover at your house with my disciples.'"

<span class="v-num">19</span> And the disciples did as Jesus directed them, and prepared the Passover.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now evening having come, he reclined <span class="added-text">(at table)</span> with the twelve.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And as they were eating, he said, "Truly I say to you that one of you will deliver me up."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And being exceedingly sorrowful, each of them began to say to him, "Surely it is not I, Lord?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> But he answered and said, "The one who dipped his hand with me in the dish, this one will deliver me up.

<span class="v-num">24</span> The Son of Man indeed goes, just as it is written concerning him; but woe to that man through whom the Son of Man is delivered up! It would have been good for him if that man had not been born."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And Judas, who was delivering him up, answered and said, "Surely it is not I, Rabbi?" He said to him, "You have said <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Now as they were eating, Jesus, having taken the bread, and having blessed <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, broke <span class="added-text">(it)</span> and gave <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the disciples, and said, "Take, eat; this is my body."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And having taken the cup, and having given thanks, he gave <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to them, saying, "Drink from it, all of you;

<span class="v-num">28</span> for this is my blood of the new covenant, which is poured out <span class="alt-label">concerning</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> many for the forgiveness of sins.[^5]

<span class="v-num">29</span> But I say to you, I will not drink from now <span class="added-text">(on)</span> of this fruit of the vine, until that day when I drink it new with you in my Father's kingdom."

<span class="v-num">30</span> And having sung a hymn, they went out to the Mount of Olives.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Then Jesus said to them, "All you will be made to stumble because of me on this night. For it is written, 'I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep of the flock will be scattered.'

<span class="v-num">32</span> But after I am raised, I will go before you into Galilee."

<span class="v-num">33</span> But Peter answered and said to him, "Even if all will be made to stumble because of you, I will never be made to stumble."

<span class="v-num">34</span> Jesus said to him, "Truly I say to you that on this <span class="added-text">(very)</span> night, before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times."

<span class="v-num">35</span> Peter said to him, "Even if it is necessary for me to die with you, I will not deny you." Likewise also all the disciples said.

<span class="v-num">36</span> Then Jesus came with them to a place called Gethsemane, and said to the disciples, "Sit here, while I go over there and pray."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And having taken with <span class="added-text">(him)</span> Peter and the two sons of Zebedee, he began to be sorrowful and deeply distressed.

<span class="v-num">38</span> Then he said to them, "My soul is deeply grieved, to <span class="added-text">(the point of)</span> death; remain here and watch with me."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And having gone forward a little, he fell on his face, praying, and saying, "My Father, if it is possible, let this cup pass from me; yet not as I will, but as you <span class="added-text">(will)</span>."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And he came to the disciples, and found them sleeping, and said to Peter, "So, were you not able to watch with me one hour?

<span class="v-num">41</span> Watch and pray, that you may not enter into temptation. The spirit indeed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> willing, but the flesh <span class="added-text">(is)</span> weak."[^6]

<span class="v-num">42</span> Again, a second time, having gone away, he prayed, saying, "My Father, if this cup cannot pass from me unless I drink it, your will be done."[^7]

<span class="v-num">43</span> And having come, he found them sleeping again; for their eyes were <span class="alt-label">heavy</span><span class="alt-text">(=weighed down)</span>.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And having left them, having gone away again, he prayed a third time, saying the same word.

<span class="v-num">45</span> Then he came to his disciples, and said to them, "Sleep on now, and rest; behold, the hour has drawn near, and the Son of Man is delivered up into the hands of sinners.

<span class="v-num">46</span> Rise, let us go. Behold, the one delivering me up has drawn near."

<span class="v-num">47</span> And while he was still speaking, behold, Judas, one of the twelve, came, and with him a great crowd with swords and clubs, from the chief priests and elders of the people.

<span class="v-num">48</span> Now the one delivering him up had given them a sign, saying, "Whomever I kiss, he is <span class="added-text">(the one)</span>; seize him."

<span class="v-num">49</span> And immediately, having come to Jesus, he said, "Hail, Rabbi," and kissed him earnestly.

<span class="v-num">50</span> But Jesus said to him, "Friend, for what <span class="added-text">(purpose)</span> have you come?" Then they came and laid hands on Jesus, and seized him.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And behold, one of those with Jesus, having stretched out his hand, drew his sword, and having struck the servant of the high priest, cut off his ear.

<span class="v-num">52</span> Then Jesus said to him, "Return your sword to its place; for all who take <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sword will perish by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sword.

<span class="v-num">53</span> Or do you think that I cannot now call upon my Father, and he will <span class="alt-label">provide</span><span class="alt-text">(=set beside)</span> me more than twelve legions of angels?

<span class="v-num">54</span> How then should the scriptures be fulfilled, that it must happen thus?"

<span class="v-num">55</span> In that hour Jesus said to the crowds, "Have you come out as against a robber, with swords and clubs to seize me? Daily I sat <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> you teaching in the temple, and you did not seize me.

<span class="v-num">56</span> But all this has happened, that the scriptures of the prophets might be fulfilled." Then all the disciples left him and fled.[^8]

<span class="v-num">57</span> Now those who had seized Jesus led <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away to Caiaphas the high priest, where the scribes and the elders had gathered together.

<span class="v-num">58</span> But Peter followed him from a distance, as far as the courtyard of the high priest; and having gone in, he sat inside with the officers, to see the end.

<span class="v-num">59</span> Now the chief priests and the elders and the whole council were seeking false testimony against Jesus, that they might put him to death,[^9]

<span class="v-num">60</span> and they found none; and <span class="added-text">(though)</span> many false witnesses came forward, they found none. But afterward two false witnesses came forward

<span class="v-num">61</span> and said, "This man said, 'I am able to destroy the temple of God, and to build it in three days.'"

<span class="v-num">62</span> And the high priest stood up and said to him, "Do you answer nothing? What is it <span class="added-text">(that)</span> these <span class="added-text">(men)</span> testify against you?"

<span class="v-num">63</span> But Jesus kept silent. And the high priest answered and said to him, "I put you under oath by the living God, that you tell us if you are the Christ, the Son of God."[^10]

<span class="v-num">64</span> Jesus said to him, "You have said <span class="added-text">(it)</span>. Nevertheless I say to you, from now <span class="added-text">(on)</span> you will see the Son of Man sitting at the right hand of Power, and coming upon the clouds of heaven."[^11]

<span class="v-num">65</span> Then the high priest tore his garments, saying, "He has blasphemed; what further need do we have of witnesses? Behold, now you have heard his blasphemy.

<span class="v-num">66</span> What does it seem to you?" And they answered and said, "He is <span class="alt-label">deserving</span><span class="alt-text">(=liable)</span> of death."

<span class="v-num">67</span> Then they spat in his face, and struck him with their fists; and some slapped <span class="added-text">(him)</span>,

<span class="v-num">68</span> saying, "Prophesy to us, Christ, who is the one who struck you?"

<span class="v-num">69</span> Now Peter was sitting outside in the courtyard, and a servant girl came to him, saying, "You also were with Jesus the Galilean."

<span class="v-num">70</span> But he denied <span class="added-text">(it)</span> before all <span class="added-text">(of them)</span>, saying, "I do not know what you are saying."

<span class="v-num">71</span> And when he had gone out to the gateway, another <span class="added-text">(girl)</span> saw him, and said to those <span class="added-text">(who were)</span> there, "This <span class="added-text">(man)</span> also was with Jesus the Nazarene."

<span class="v-num">72</span> And again he denied <span class="added-text">(it)</span> with an oath, "I do not know the man."

<span class="v-num">73</span> And after a little while, those standing <span class="added-text">(there)</span> came and said to Peter, "Truly you also are <span class="added-text">(one)</span> of them; for even your <span class="alt-label">speech</span><span class="alt-text">(=accent)</span> makes you evident."

<span class="v-num">74</span> Then he began to curse and to swear, "I do not know the man." And immediately a rooster crowed.

<span class="v-num">75</span> And Peter remembered the word of Jesus who had said to him, "Before a rooster crows, you will deny me three times"; and he went out<span class="added-text">(side)</span> and wept bitterly.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절: 궤계로 잡아 죽이려는 목적을 나타냄.
[^2]: ἵνα μὴ+가정법: 소동이 일어나지 않게 하려는 목적절.
[^3]: ὅπου ἐὰν+가정법(κηρυχθῇ)은 '어디서든지 ~하는 곳마다'라는 뜻으로, 장소를 특정하지 않은 채 미래의 모든 가능성을 포괄하는 부정관계 조건절이다. 복음 전파의 보편성과 그에 따른 필연적 결과(여자의 행위가 함께 전해짐)를 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(παραδῷ): 유다가 예수를 넘겨줄 목적으로 기회를 찾음.
[^5]: '새 언약의 피'는 출애굽기 언약의 피(출24:8)와 예레미야 31장의 새 언약 예언을 배경으로 함.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(εἰσέλθητε)은 '~하지 않도록'이라는 목적절로, 깨어 기도하는 행위의 목적을 나타낸다. 시험에 빠지지 않기 위해 미리 깨어 기도해야 함을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^7]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법: 마시지 않고는 잔이 지나갈 수 없다는 조건을 나타냄.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῶσιν): 선지자들의 성경이 이루어지려는 목적을 나타냄.
[^9]: ὅπως+가정법(θανατώσωσι): 죽이려는 목적절.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(εἴπῃς)은 여기서 '말하라'는 명령의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, ἐξορκίζω(맹세시키다)의 목적어 역할을 한다. 즉 '네가 말하도록 내가 맹세시킨다'는 뜻으로, 대제사장이 예수께 강력히 답변을 요구하는 뉘앙스를 담고 있다.
[^11]: '권능의 우편에 앉음'은 시편 110:1, '구름을 타고 옴'은 다니엘 7:13을 인용한 것으로 예수의 메시아 되심과 재림을 선언함.
