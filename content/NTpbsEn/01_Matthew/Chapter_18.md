<span class="v-num">1</span> In that hour the disciples came to Jesus, saying, "Who then is greater in the kingdom of the heavens?"

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Jesus, having called a little child, set it in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of them,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and said, "Truly I say to you, unless you turn and become as the little children, you shall not enter into the kingdom of the heavens.[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Therefore whoever humbles himself as this little child, this one is the greater in the kingdom of the heavens.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And whoever receives one such little child <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=on)</span> my name receives me;[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> but whoever causes one of these little ones believing in me to stumble, it is profitable for him that a donkey's millstone be hung on his neck, and that he be sunk in the depth of the sea.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Woe to the world from the stumbling blocks! For it is necessary <span class="added-text">(that)</span> the stumbling blocks come; but woe to that man through whom the stumbling block comes!

<span class="v-num">8</span> But if your hand or your foot causes you to stumble, cut it off and cast <span class="added-text">(it)</span> from you; it is good for you to enter into life lame or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into the eternal fire.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And if your eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and cast <span class="added-text">(it)</span> from you; it is good for you to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into the <span class="alt-label">hell</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gehenna)</span> of fire.

<span class="v-num">10</span> See that you do not despise one of these little ones; for I say to you, that their angels in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens always see the face of my Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For the Son of Man came to save that which was lost.[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> What do you think? If a certain man has a hundred sheep, and one of them goes astray, does he not leave the ninety-nine, and going to the mountains, seek the one that is straying?[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And if he happens to find it, truly I say to you, that he rejoices over it more than over the ninety-nine which have not strayed.[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Thus it is not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will before your Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens, that one of these little ones should perish.[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But if your brother sins against you, go and reprove him between you and him alone. If he listens to you, you have gained your brother.[^8]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But if he does not listen, take with you one or two more, so that by the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.[^9]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And if he refuses to hear them, tell <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the church; and if he refuses to hear the church also, let him be to you as the Gentile and the tax collector.[^10]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Truly I say to you, whatever you bind on earth shall be bound in heaven; and whatever you loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.[^11]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Again I say to you, that if two of you agree on earth concerning any matter, whatever they ask, it shall be done for them by my Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.[^12]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there I am in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of them."

<span class="v-num">21</span> Then Peter, having come to him, said, "Lord, how many times shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Up to seven times?"

<span class="v-num">22</span> Jesus said to him, "I do not say to you up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven.[^13]

<span class="v-num">23</span> Therefore the kingdom of the heavens is likened to a certain king <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, who wanted to settle accounts with his servants.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And when he began to settle <span class="added-text">(accounts)</span>, one debtor of ten thousand talents was brought to him.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But he not having <span class="added-text">(anything)</span> to repay, his lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife and children, and all that he had, and repayment to be made.

<span class="v-num">26</span> The servant then, falling down, worshiped him, saying, 'Lord, have patience with me, and I will repay you all.'

<span class="v-num">27</span> And the lord of that servant, moved with compassion, released him, and forgave him the debt.

<span class="v-num">28</span> But that servant, having gone out, found one of his fellow servants, who owed him a hundred denarii; and having seized him, he choked <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, saying, 'Pay me what you owe.'

<span class="v-num">29</span> His fellow servant then, falling at his feet, begged him, saying, 'Have patience with me, and I will repay you all.'

<span class="v-num">30</span> But he was not willing, but went and cast him into prison, until he should repay what was owed.

<span class="v-num">31</span> So when his fellow servants saw the things that happened, they were greatly grieved, and came and reported to their lord all the things that had happened.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Then his lord, having called him, said to him, 'Wicked servant, I forgave you all that debt, because you begged me.'

<span class="v-num">33</span> Should you not also have had mercy on your fellow servant, as I also had mercy on you?'

<span class="v-num">34</span> And his lord, being angry, delivered him to the tormentors, until he should repay all that was owed to him.

<span class="v-num">35</span> So also my heavenly Father will do to you, if you do not forgive from your hearts each one his brother his trespasses."[^14]

[^1]: ἐὰν μὴ στραφῆτε는 3급 조건문(가정법)으로 아직 이루어지지 않은 회심의 필연성을 강조한다.
[^2]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법(δέξηται)은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 조건절로, 미래에 실현 가능한 상황을 가정한다. 아직 일어나지 않았지만 언제든 실현될 수 있는 일반적 조건을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(κρεμασθῇ, καταποντισθῇ)이 συμφέρει의 내용을 풀어주는 목적절로, '차라리 ~하는 것이 낫다'는 결과적 목적을 나타낸다. 실족케 하는 죄의 심각성을 강조하는 수사적 표현이다.
[^4]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 눅19:10과의 병행 구절에서 유래한 후대 삽입으로 보아 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(γένηται, πλανηθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 상황을 가정하는 조건절이다. '만일 ~라면'이라는 열린 가능성을 전제로 목자의 반응을 이끌어내는 수사적 질문의 조건 배경이 된다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(γένηται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '혹시라도 찾게 되면'이라는 불확실하지만 실제로 일어날 수 있는 상황을 전제한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπόληται)은 목적절로, 하나님의 뜻의 내용/지향점을 나타낸다. 즉 '작은 자 하나라도 잃는 것'이 하나님 아버지의 뜻이 결코 아님을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '혹시 ~한다면'이라는 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 형제의 범죄와 그 반응(들음)을 모두 실제로 일어날 수도 있는 상황으로 가정하며 권면의 근거를 제시한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 증인 제도를 통해 사실 확증의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἐάν+가정법은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~하거든/한다면'의 뜻이다. 형제가 듣지 않을 경우를 가정하여 그다음 단계의 권징 절차를 제시한다.
[^11]: ὅσα ἐὰν+가정법은 '무엇이든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내며 어떤 경우에도 해당됨을 강조한다.
[^12]: ἐὰν+가정법(συμφωνήσωσιν)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로 '만일 ~한다면'의 뜻이다. 합심 기도의 조건이 충족될 때 응답이 따른다는 논리적 귀결을 강조한다.
[^13]: 일흔 번씩 일곱 번은 문자적 횟수 계산이 아니라 용서에 한계가 없음을 뜻하는 관용적 과장 표현이다.
[^14]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법은 '만일 ~하지 않으면'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 조건절로, 용서 여부에 따라 하나님의 대응이 결정됨을 경고한다. 각 사람의 실제 순종 여부가 관건임을 강조하는 뉘앙스다.
