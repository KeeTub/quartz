<span class="v-num">1</span> And when they came near to Jerusalem and came to Bethphage, to the Mount of Olives, then Jesus sent two disciples,

<span class="v-num">2</span> saying to them, "Go into the village opposite you, and immediately you will find a donkey tied, and a colt with her; having loosed <span class="added-text">(them,)</span> bring <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to me.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And if anyone says anything to you, you shall say, 'The Lord has need of them,' and immediately he will send them."[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Now this whole <span class="added-text">(thing)</span> happened, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying,[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> 'Say to the daughter of Zion, "Behold, your king comes to you, meek and mounted on a donkey, and a colt <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foal of a beast of burden."'[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the disciples having gone and having done as Jesus commanded them,

<span class="v-num">7</span> brought the donkey and the colt, and put on them their garments, and he sat on them.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the very great crowd spread their own garments on the road; and others cut branches from the trees and spread <span class="added-text">(them)</span> on the road.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the crowds going before and the ones following cried out, saying, "Hosanna to the son of David! Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> he who comes in the name of the Lord! Hosanna in the highest!"

<span class="v-num">10</span> And when he entered into Jerusalem, all the city was shaken, saying, "Who is this?"

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the crowds said, "This is Jesus the prophet, from Nazareth of Galilee."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And Jesus entered into the temple of God, and cast out all those selling and buying in the temple, and overturned the tables of the money changers, and the seats of those selling doves.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he said to them, "It is written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer'; but you have made it a den of robbers."[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And blind and lame <span class="added-text">(people)</span> came to him in the temple, and he healed them.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But the chief priests and the scribes, having seen the wonderful things which he did, and the children crying out in the temple and saying, "Hosanna to the son of David!" were indignant,

<span class="v-num">16</span> and said to him, "Do you hear what these are saying?" And Jesus said to them, "Yes; have you never read, 'Out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth of infants and nursing <span class="added-text">(babes)</span> you have prepared praise'?"[^5]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And having left them, he went out of the city to Bethany, and lodged there.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now in the morning, returning to the city, he was hungry;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and seeing one fig tree by the road, he went to it, and found nothing on it except leaves only; and he said to it, "Let no fruit come from you forevermore." And the fig tree withered at once.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And the disciples, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, marveled, saying, "How did the fig tree wither at once?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> But Jesus answered and said to them, "Truly I say to you, if you have faith and do not doubt, not only will you do what <span class="added-text">(was done)</span> to the fig tree, but even if you say to this mountain, 'Be taken up and cast into the sea,' it will happen;[^6]

<span class="v-num">22</span> and all things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And when he had come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came to him as he was teaching, saying, "By what authority do you do these things? And who gave you this authority?"

<span class="v-num">24</span> But Jesus answered and said to them, "I also will ask you one thing, which if you tell me, I also will tell you by what authority I do these things.[^7]

<span class="v-num">25</span> The baptism of John, where was it from? From heaven or from men?" And they reasoned among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say to us, 'Why then did you not believe him?'[^8]

<span class="v-num">26</span> But if we say, 'From men,' we fear the crowd, for all hold John as a prophet."[^9]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And answering Jesus, they said, "We do not know." He also said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things.

<span class="v-num">28</span> But what do you think? A man had two children, and coming to the first he said, 'Child, go, work today in my vineyard.'

<span class="v-num">29</span> And he answered and said, 'I will not'; but afterward he repented and went.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And coming to the second he said likewise. And he answered and said, 'I <span class="added-text">(will go,)</span> lord'; and he did not go.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Which of the two did the will of the father?" They said to him, "The first." Jesus said to them, "Truly I say to you, that the tax collectors and the harlots go before you into the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">32</span> For John came to you in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way of righteousness, and you did not believe him, but the tax collectors and the harlots believed him; and you, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, did not repent afterward to believe him.

<span class="v-num">33</span> "Hear another parable. There was a certain man, a master of a house, who planted a vineyard, and put a fence around it, and dug a winepress in it, and built a tower, and let it out to farmers, and went away <span class="added-text">(to another country)</span>.[^10]

<span class="v-num">34</span> And when the time of the fruits drew near, he sent his servants to the farmers, to receive his fruits;

<span class="v-num">35</span> and the farmers, having taken his servants, beat one, and killed another, and stoned another.

<span class="v-num">36</span> Again he sent other servants more than the first, and they did to them likewise.

<span class="v-num">37</span> But afterward he sent to them his son, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

<span class="v-num">38</span> But the farmers, seeing the son, said among themselves, 'This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize his inheritance.'

<span class="v-num">39</span> And having taken him, they cast <span class="added-text">(him)</span> out of the vineyard, and killed <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.

<span class="v-num">40</span> When therefore the lord of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those farmers?"

<span class="v-num">41</span> They said to him, "He will destroy those wicked <span class="added-text">(men)</span> wickedly, and will let out the vineyard to other farmers, who will give him the fruits in their seasons."

<span class="v-num">42</span> Jesus said to them, "Have you never read in the Scriptures, 'The stone which the builders rejected, this has become the head of the corner; this was from the Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes'?"[^11]

<span class="v-num">43</span> Therefore I say to you, that the kingdom of God will be taken from you, and will be given to a nation producing its fruits.[^12]

<span class="v-num">44</span> And he who falls on this stone will be broken; but on whomever it falls, it will grind him to powder."

<span class="v-num">45</span> And when the chief priests and the Pharisees heard his parables, they knew that he was speaking about them;

<span class="v-num">46</span> and seeking to seize him, they feared the crowds, since they held him as a prophet.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ)은 아직 일어나지 않았지만 실제로 일어날 가능성이 있는 미래 조건을 나타낸다. '만일 누가 묻거든'이라는 가정 상황을 전제로 그에 따른 대응 지침을 제시하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῇ)은 목적절로, 이 사건이 일어난 목적/의도가 예언 성취에 있음을 나타낸다. 하나님의 섭리적 계획을 강조하는 신학적 목적절이다.
[^3]: 슥 9:9(사 62:11 결합)의 인용으로, 왕의 겸손한 임재를 나타낸다.
[^4]: 사 56:7과 렘 7:11을 결합 인용하여 성전의 본래 목적과 그 타락을 대조한다.
[^5]: 시 8:2의 인용으로, 어린아이들의 찬양을 하나님의 예정된 계획으로 정당화하신다.
[^6]: ἐὰν ἔχητε는 3급 조건문으로, 아직 실현되지 않았으나 가능한 믿음의 상태를 가정한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπητέ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '너희가 (만약) 대답한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 예수님의 대답 여부가 상대방의 응답에 달려 있음을 강조한다.
[^8]: ἐὰν εἴπωμεν은 3급 조건문으로, 대답에 따른 곤란한 결과를 미리 계산하는 그들의 논리를 보여준다.
[^9]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 아직 결정되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '만일 우리가 ~라고 말한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 종교 지도자들이 대답을 놓고 고민하는 딜레마 상황을 생생하게 보여준다.
[^10]: 사 5:1-2의 포도원 비유를 배경으로 하며, 하나님과 이스라엘의 관계를 그린다.
[^11]: 시 118:22-23의 인용으로, 버림받은 그리스도가 도리어 구원의 기초가 되심을 예언적으로 확증한다.
[^12]: βασιλεία τοῦ Θεοῦ(하나님의 나라)가 이스라엘 지도층에게서 옮겨져 열매 맺는 (새) 백성에게 주어짐을 선언하는 심판적 문맥이다.
