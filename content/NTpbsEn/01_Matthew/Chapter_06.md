# Matthew 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> Take heed <span class="added-text">(not)</span> to do your alms before men, to be seen by them; otherwise you have no reward from your Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in the heavens.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Therefore when you do alms, do not sound a trumpet before you, as the hypocrites do in the synagogues and in the streets, that they may be glorified by men. Truly I say to you, they have their reward.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But when you do alms, let not your left <span class="added-text">(hand)</span> know what your right <span class="added-text">(hand)</span> does,

<span class="v-num">4</span> so that your alms may be in secret; and your Father who sees in secret, he himself will reward you <span class="added-text">(in the open)</span>.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And when you pray, you shall not be as the hypocrites, for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and in the corners of the streets, that they may appear to men. Truly I say to you, that they have their reward.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But you, when you pray, enter into your inner room, and having shut your door, pray to your Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you <span class="added-text">(in the open)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And when praying, do not use vain repetitions, as the Gentiles <span class="added-text">(do)</span>; for they think that they will be heard for their much speaking.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore do not be like them; for your Father knows what things you have need of, before you ask him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Therefore pray in this <span class="alt-label">way</span><span class="alt-text">(=thus)</span>, you: "Our Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in the heavens, hallowed be your name.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Your kingdom come. Your will be done, as in heaven, <span class="added-text">(so)</span> also on the earth.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Give us today our daily bread,[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> and forgive us our debts, as we also forgive our debtors,

<span class="v-num">13</span> And do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>. For yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever. Amen."[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you;[^5]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.[^6]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Moreover when you fast, do not be as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance; for they disfigure their faces, that they may appear to men to be fasting. Truly I say to you, that they have their reward.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But you, when you fast, anoint your head, and wash your face,

<span class="v-num">18</span> so that you do not appear to men to be fasting, but to your Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in secret; and your Father who sees in secret will reward you <span class="added-text">(in the open)</span>.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on the earth, where moth and rust destroy, and where thieves break through and steal;

<span class="v-num">20</span> But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust destroys, and where thieves do not break through nor steal;

<span class="v-num">21</span> For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.

<span class="v-num">22</span> The lamp of the body is the eye; if therefore your eye is <span class="alt-label">single</span><span class="alt-text">(=sound)</span>, your whole body will be full of light.[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> But if your eye is bad, your whole body will be dark. If therefore the light that is in you is darkness, how great <span class="added-text">(is)</span> that darkness![^8]

<span class="v-num">24</span> No one can serve two masters; for either he will hate the one, and love the other, or he will hold to the one, and despise the other. You cannot serve God and <span class="alt-label">mammon</span><span class="alt-text">(=riches)</span>.[^9]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Therefore I say to you, do not be anxious for your <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span>, what you shall eat, or what you shall drink; nor for your body, what you shall put on. Is not life more than food, and the body more than clothing?

<span class="v-num">26</span> Look at the birds of the air, for they do not sow, nor reap, nor gather into barns, and your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not much more valuable than they?

<span class="v-num">27</span> But which of you by being anxious can add one cubit to his stature?

<span class="v-num">28</span> And why are you anxious about clothing? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they do not labor, nor spin;

<span class="v-num">29</span> yet I say to you, that not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But if God so clothes the grass of the field, which today exists, and tomorrow is thrown into the oven, <span class="added-text">(will he)</span> not much more <span class="added-text">(clothe)</span> you, O you of little faith?

<span class="v-num">31</span> Therefore do not be anxious, saying, 'What shall we eat?' or 'What shall we drink?' or 'What shall we wear?'

<span class="v-num">32</span> For the Gentiles seek after all these things; for your heavenly Father knows that you have need of all these things.

<span class="v-num">33</span> But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you.[^10]

<span class="v-num">34</span> Therefore do not be anxious for tomorrow, for tomorrow will be anxious for its own things. Sufficient for the day <span class="added-text">(is)</span> its own trouble.

[^1]: πρὸς τὸ θεαθῆναι는 πρὸς τό+부정사로 목적을 나타내는 구문으로 '그들에게 보이려는' 의도를 강조한다.
[^2]: 이 기도의 서두 호칭으로, 이하 절들(9~13절)이 하나의 기도문(인용문)으로 이어진다.
[^3]: ἐπιούσιον은 헬라어 문헌에서 매우 드물게 쓰이는 단어로 '필요한', '오늘의', '다가오는'등 뜻이 불확실하나 전통적으로 '일용할'로 번역된다.
[^4]: 송영(頌榮) 부분('나라와 권능과 영광이...')은 초기 사본들에는 없고 후대 전례 관습에서 유래하여 비잔틴/공인본문(TR) 전통에 포함된 것으로 본다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀφῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 사람을 용서하는 것이 하늘 아버지의 용서를 받는 조건으로 제시된다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀφῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만약 너희가 용서하지 않으면'이라는 열린 조건을 제시한다. 실제 삶에서 결정될 문제이기에 청중에게 실천적 결단을 촉구하는 경고성 조건절이다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만약 네 눈이 성하다면'이라는 열린 조건을 제시한다. 이는 눈의 상태에 따라 온몸의 밝음 여부가 결정되는 잠재적 결과를 강조한다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '만약 ~하다면'이라는 가정을 나타낸다. 눈의 상태에 따라 온 몸의 상태가 결정된다는 실제적 가능성을 강조한다.
[^9]: μαμμωνᾷ(맘몬)는 아람어에서 유래한 단어로 재물을 의인화하여 하나님과 대립되는 섬김의 대상으로 제시한다.
[^10]: 하나님의 나라를 최우선으로 구하라는 명령으로, 그 나라의 통치와 의가 그리스도인의 최고 관심사임을 나타낸다.
