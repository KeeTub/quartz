<span class="v-num">1</span> And Jesus, answering, spoke to them again in parables, saying,

<span class="v-num">2</span> "The kingdom of the heavens has been compared to a man, a king, who made a wedding feast for his son,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and sent his servants to call those having been invited to the wedding feast, and they were not willing to come.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Again he sent other servants, saying, 'Tell those having been invited, "Behold, I have prepared my feast, my oxen and the fatted beasts have been slain, and all things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> ready; come to the wedding feast."'

<span class="v-num">5</span> But they, having disregarded <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, went away, one to his own field, another to his business,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and the rest, having seized his servants, treated <span class="added-text">(them)</span> shamefully and killed <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> But the king, having heard, was angered, and having sent his armies, destroyed those murderers and burned their city.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Then he said to his servants, 'The wedding feast indeed is ready, but those having been invited were not worthy.'

<span class="v-num">9</span> 'Go therefore to the crossroads of the streets, and as many as you find, invite <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to the wedding feast.'

<span class="v-num">10</span> And those servants, having gone out into the streets, gathered together all as many as they found, both evil and good; and the wedding feast was filled with those reclining.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But the king, having come in to see those reclining, saw there a man not clothed <span class="added-text">(with)</span> a wedding garment,

<span class="v-num">12</span> and he said to him, 'Friend, how did you come in here not having a wedding garment?' But he was silenced.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Then the king said to the servants, 'Having bound his feet and hands, take him away and cast <span class="added-text">(him)</span> into the outer darkness; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.'

<span class="v-num">14</span> For many are called, but few <span class="added-text">(are)</span> chosen."

<span class="v-num">15</span> Then the Pharisees, having gone, took counsel how they might trap him in a word.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And they sent to him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, "Teacher, we know that you are true, and teach the way of God in truth, and it does not matter to you about anyone; for you do not look at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of men.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Tell us therefore, what does it seem to you? Is it lawful to give tax to Caesar, or not?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> But Jesus, having known their wickedness, said, "Why do you test me, hypocrites?

<span class="v-num">19</span> Show me the coin of the tax." And they brought to him a denarius.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he said to them, "Whose <span class="added-text">(is)</span> this image and inscription?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> They said to him, "Caesar's." Then he said to them, "Render therefore the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And having heard, they marveled; and having left him, they went away.

<span class="v-num">23</span> On that day Sadducees, those saying there is no resurrection, came to him and questioned him,

<span class="v-num">24</span> saying, "Teacher, Moses said, 'If anyone dies not having children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed to his brother.'[^1]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Now there were with us seven brothers; and the first, having married, died, and not having seed, left his wife to his brother.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Likewise the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And last of all the woman died also.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Therefore in the resurrection, whose wife of the seven will she be? For all had her."

<span class="v-num">29</span> But Jesus, answering, said to them, "You are <span class="alt-label">mistaken</span><span class="alt-text">(=deceived)</span>, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> angels of God in heaven.

<span class="v-num">31</span> But concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken to you by God, saying,

<span class="v-num">32</span> 'I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob'? God is not the God of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, but of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And the crowds, having heard <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, were astonished at his teaching.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But the Pharisees, having heard that he had silenced the Sadducees, gathered together at the same <span class="added-text">(place)</span>,

<span class="v-num">35</span> and one of them, a lawyer, questioned <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, testing him, and saying,

<span class="v-num">36</span> "Teacher, which <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> great commandment in the law?"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And Jesus said to him, "'You shall love <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul, and with all your mind.'

<span class="v-num">38</span> This is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first and great commandment.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And <span class="added-text">(the)</span> second is like it, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'

<span class="v-num">40</span> On these two commandments the whole law and the prophets hang."

<span class="v-num">41</span> Now while the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus questioned them,

<span class="v-num">42</span> saying, "What does it seem to you concerning the Christ? Whose son is he?" They said to him, "<span class="added-text">(The son)</span> of David."

<span class="v-num">43</span> He said to them, "How then does David in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit call him Lord, saying,

<span class="v-num">44</span> 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand, until I put your enemies <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a footstool of your feet"'?

<span class="v-num">45</span> If then David calls him Lord, how is he his son?"

<span class="v-num">46</span> And no one was able to answer him a word, nor did anyone dare from that day <span class="added-text">(on)</span> to question him anymore.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀποθάνῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 일반적 가능성을 가리키는 조건절로 '누구든지 자식 없이 죽으면'이라는 미래의 불특정 상황을 전제한다. 모세 율법의 일반 규정을 진술할 때 쓰는 전형적 어법이다.
