# Matthew 7

<div class="bible-legend">
  <span class="legend-item"><span class="dot alt"></span>(=alt.word)</span>
  <span class="legend-item"><span class="dot added"></span>(added)</span>
  <span class="legend-item"><span class="dot deleted"></span>[omit]</span>

<span class="v-num">1</span> Do not judge, that you may not be judged,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> for with what judgment you judge, you will be judged, and with what measure you measure, it will be measured back to you.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And why do you see the speck that <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in your brother's eye, but do not notice the beam that <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in your own eye?

<span class="v-num">4</span> Or how will you say to your brother, 'Let me cast out the speck from your eye,' and behold, the beam <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in your own eye?

<span class="v-num">5</span> Hypocrite, cast out first the beam from your own eye, and then you will see clearly to cast out the speck from your brother's eye.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Do not give the holy <span class="added-text">(thing)</span> to the dogs, nor cast your pearls before the swine, lest they trample them with their feet, and turning, tear you.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For everyone who asks receives, and the one who seeks finds, and to the one who knocks it will be opened.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Or what man is there of you, whom if his son asks for bread, will he give him a stone?[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And if he asks for a fish, will he give him a serpent?[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> If therefore you, being evil, know <span class="added-text">(how)</span> to give good gifts to your children, how much more will your Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in the heavens give good things to those who ask him?

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore all things whatever you would that men should do to you, do you even so to them, for this is the law and the prophets.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Enter through the narrow gate, for wide <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the gate, and broad <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the way that leads to destruction, and many are those who enter through it.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For narrow <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the gate, and constricted <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the way that leads to life, and few are those who find it.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But beware of the false prophets, who come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly are ravening wolves.

<span class="v-num">16</span> By their fruits you will know them. Do <span class="added-text">(men)</span> gather a grape cluster from thorns, or figs from thistles?

<span class="v-num">17</span> So every good tree produces good fruits, but the bad tree produces evil fruits.

<span class="v-num">18</span> A good tree cannot produce evil fruits, nor can a bad tree produce good fruits.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Every tree not producing good fruit is cut down and cast into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fire.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Therefore by their fruits you will know them.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Not everyone who says to me, 'Lord, Lord,' will enter into the kingdom of the heavens, but the one who does the will of my Father who <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Many will say to me in that day, 'Lord, Lord, did we not prophesy in your name, and in your name cast out demons, and in your name do many mighty works?'

<span class="v-num">23</span> And then I will declare to them, 'I never knew you; depart from me, you who work lawlessness.'

<span class="v-num">24</span> Everyone therefore who hears these my words, and does them, I will liken him to a wise man, who built his house upon the rock,

<span class="v-num">25</span> and the rain came down, and the rivers came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house, and it did not fall, for it had been founded upon the rock.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And everyone who hears these my words and does not do them will be likened to a foolish man, who built his house upon the sand,

<span class="v-num">27</span> and the rain came down, and the rivers came, and the winds blew, and struck against that house, and it fell, and its fall was great.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these words, the crowds were astonished at his teaching,

<span class="v-num">29</span> for he was teaching them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(κριθῆτε)은 목적절로, '판단받지 않기 위하여'라는 뜻입니다. 판단하지 말라는 명령의 목적(결과)이 판단받지 않는 것임을 보여줍니다.
[^2]: ἐὰν+가정법(αἰτήσῃ)은 '누가 언제 요청하든지'라는 미정 미래 조건을 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않았지만 실제로 일어날 가능성이 있는 일반적 조건절로, 아버지의 선한 반응이 조건 충족 시 당연히 뒤따름을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(αἰτήσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 가상의 상황을 설정한다. 이는 하나님 아버지께 무엇을 구하든지 좋은 것을 주신다는 원리를 예화로 보여주는 조건 구문이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῶσιν)이 θέλητε의 목적어절 역할을 하여 '~해 주기를 원하다'는 바람의 내용을 나타내는 목적절이다. 사람들이 바라는 것이 무엇인지를 구체화한다.
