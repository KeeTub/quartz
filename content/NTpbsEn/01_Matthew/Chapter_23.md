# Matthew 23

<span class="v-num">1</span> Then Jesus spoke to the crowds and to his disciples,

<span class="v-num">2</span> saying, "Upon the seat of Moses the scribes and the Pharisees have sat down."

<span class="v-num">3</span> Therefore, all things—as many as they tell you to keep—keep and do, but do not <span class="added-text">(do)</span> according to their works, for they say and do not do.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For they bind heavy and hard-to-bear burdens and lay <span class="added-text">(them)</span> upon the shoulders of men, but they themselves are not willing to move them with their finger.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And they do all their works in order to be seen by men. They widen their <span class="alt-label">phylacteries</span><span class="alt-text">(=prayer bands)</span> and enlarge the fringes of their garments,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and they love the first <span class="alt-label">place</span><span class="alt-text">(=chief seat)</span> at feasts, and the first seats in the synagogues,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and greetings in the marketplaces, and to be called by men, 'Rabbi, Rabbi.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> But you, do not be called 'Rabbi,' for one is your teacher, the Christ, and you are all brothers.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And do not call <span class="added-text">(anyone)</span> your father on the earth, for one is your Father, the <span class="added-text">(one)</span> in the heavens.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Neither be called <span class="alt-label">teachers</span><span class="alt-text">(=leaders)</span>, for one is your <span class="alt-label">teacher</span><span class="alt-text">(=leader)</span>, the Christ.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But the greater among you shall be your servant.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And whoever exalts himself shall be humbled, and whoever humbles himself shall be exalted.[^1]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you devour the houses of widows, and for a pretense pray at length; because of this you will receive greater judgment.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you shut the kingdom of the heavens before men; for you yourselves do not enter, nor do you allow those entering to enter.[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you go around the sea and the dry land to make one proselyte, and when he becomes <span class="added-text">(one)</span>, you make him twice as much a son of <span class="alt-label">hell</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gehenna)</span> as yourselves.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Woe to you, blind guides, who say, 'Whoever swears by the temple, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gold of the temple, he is obligated.'

<span class="v-num">17</span> Fools and blind <span class="added-text">(men)</span>! For which is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifies the gold?

<span class="v-num">18</span> And, 'Whoever swears by the altar, it is nothing; but whoever swears by the gift that is upon it, he is obligated.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Fools and blind <span class="added-text">(men)</span>! For which <span class="added-text">(is)</span> greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifies the gift?

<span class="v-num">20</span> Therefore, the one who swears by the altar swears by it and by all things upon it;

<span class="v-num">21</span> and the one who swears by the temple swears by it and by the one dwelling in it;

<span class="v-num">22</span> and the one who swears by heaven swears by the throne of God and by the one sitting upon it.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you tithe mint and dill and cumin, and have left undone the weightier matters of the law: judgment and mercy and faith. These you ought to have done, and not to have left the others undone.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Blind guides, who strain out a gnat but swallow a camel.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you cleanse the outside of the cup and of the dish, but inside they are full of greed and self-indulgence.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the dish, so that <span class="added-text">(ἵνα)</span> the outside of them may become clean also.[^4]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you are like whitewashed tombs, which outwardly indeed appear beautiful, but inside are full of dead men's bones and all uncleanness.

<span class="v-num">28</span> So you also outwardly indeed appear righteous to men, but inwardly you are full of hypocrisy and lawlessness.

<span class="v-num">29</span> Woe to you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! For you build the tombs of the prophets and adorn the monuments of the righteous,

<span class="v-num">30</span> and say, 'If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.'

<span class="v-num">31</span> So then you testify <span class="alt-label">against</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> yourselves that you are sons of those who murdered the prophets.

<span class="v-num">32</span> You also, fill up the measure of your fathers.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Serpents, offspring of vipers! How shall you escape from the judgment of <span class="alt-label">hell</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gehenna)</span>?

<span class="v-num">34</span> Therefore, behold, I send to you prophets and wise men and scribes; and some of them you will kill and crucify, and some of them you will scourge in your synagogues and persecute from city to city,

<span class="v-num">35</span> so that <span class="added-text">(ὅπως)</span> upon you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel to the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom you murdered between the temple and the altar.[^5]

<span class="v-num">36</span> Truly I say to you, all these things will come upon this generation.

<span class="v-num">37</span> Jerusalem, Jerusalem, who kills the prophets and stones those sent to her! How often I wanted to gather your children together, in the way a hen gathers her own chicks under <span class="added-text">(her)</span> wings, and you were not willing!

<span class="v-num">38</span> Behold, your house is left to you desolate.

<span class="v-num">39</span> For I say to you, you shall not see me from now <span class="added-text">(on)</span>, until you say, 'Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the one coming in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.'

[^1]: 높임-낮춤, 낮춤-높임의 교차대구(키아즘) 구조로 자기를 높이려는 자와 낮추려는 자의 결과를 대조하여 강조한다.
[^2]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 13절과 내용이 중복되고 병행 본문(막12:40, 눅20:47)에서 전이된 후대 삽입으로 보아 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^3]: ὃς ἐὰν(ἄν)+가정법은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다. 서기관들의 자의적 맹세 규정을 예시로 들어 반박하는 조건문이다.
[^4]: ἵνα γένηται는 목적절로, 안을 먼저 깨끗이 하는 것이 겉까지 깨끗해지는 목적이자 결과임을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ὅπως ἔλθῃ는 목적절로, 앞선 34절의 박해 행위가 결국 온 세대에 그 죄값이 임하게 되는 목적/결과로 연결됨을 보여준다.
