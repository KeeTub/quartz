# Matthew 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> Then Jesus was led up into the wilderness by the Spirit, to be tempted by the devil.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having fasted forty days and forty nights, afterward he was hungry.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And the tempter, having come to him, said, "If you are the Son of God, command that these stones become loaves of bread."[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> But he, answering, said, "It is written, 'Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word proceeding through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth of God.'"[^4]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Then the devil took him <span class="added-text">(along)</span> into the holy city, and set him on the <span class="alt-label">pinnacle</span><span class="alt-text">(=top)</span> of the temple,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and said to him, "If you are the Son of God, throw yourself down; for it is written, 'He will command his angels concerning you, and on <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hands they will bear you up, lest you strike your foot against a stone.'"[^5]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Jesus said to him, "Again it is written, 'You shall not tempt <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God.'"[^6]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Again the devil took him <span class="added-text">(along)</span> to a very high mountain, and showed him all the kingdoms of the world and their glory,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and said to him, "All these I will give you, if you will fall down and worship me."[^7]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Then Jesus said to him, "Go away, Satan; for it is written, 'You shall worship <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord your God, and him only you shall serve.'"[^8]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Then the devil left him; and behold, angels came and ministered to him.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Now Jesus, having heard that John was delivered <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=arrested)</span>, withdrew into Galilee;

<span class="v-num">13</span> and having left Nazareth, having come, he dwelt in Capernaum by the sea, in the borders of Zebulun and Naphtali,

<span class="v-num">14</span> so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,[^9]

<span class="v-num">15</span> "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> land of Zebulun and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Naphtali, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way of the sea, beyond the Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles,

<span class="v-num">16</span> the people sitting in darkness saw a great light, and to those sitting in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> region and shadow of death, light dawned on them."[^10]

<span class="v-num">17</span> From then, Jesus began to preach and to say, "Repent, for the kingdom of the heavens has drawn near."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Jesus, walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brothers, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.

<span class="v-num">19</span> and he said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you fishers of men."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And they immediately left the nets and followed him.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And having gone on from there, he saw another two brothers, James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Zebedee and John his brother, in the boat with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and he called them.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And they immediately left the boat and their father and followed him.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And his report went out into all Syria; and they brought to him all who were sick, afflicted with various diseases and torments, and those possessed by demons, and epileptics, and paralytics; and he healed them.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And great crowds followed him from Galilee and Decapolis and Jerusalem and Judea, and beyond the Jordan.

[^1]: πειρασθῆναι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문으로, 성령께서 예수를 광야로 이끄신 목적이 마귀에게 시험받으심에 있었음을 보여준다.
[^2]: νηστεύσας(부정과거 분사)는 금식이라는 완결된 행위를 나타내고, 그 뒤에 굶주림이 뒤따랐음을 시간 순서로 보여준다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(γένωνται)은 '~하도록/~되게'라는 목적(명령의 내용/결과)을 나타내는 목적절로, 명령의 의도를 표현한다.
[^4]: γέγραπται(완료시제)는 기록된 말씀이 지금도 살아 유효함을 뜻하며, 신명기 8:3 인용이다.
[^5]: 마귀도 시편 91:11-12을 인용하나 문맥을 왜곡하여 하나님을 시험하도록 유도하는 점에서 말씀의 오용을 보여준다.
[^6]: 신명기 6:16 인용으로, 마귀의 왜곡된 인용에 대해 다른 말씀으로 균형을 잡아 대응하신다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(πεσὼν προσκυνήσῃς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '만일 네가 엎드려 경배한다면'이라는 시험적 제안을 나타낸다. 마귀가 예수님께 실제로 일어날 수 있는 선택으로 유혹을 제시하는 구조다.
[^8]: 신명기 6:13 인용으로 세 번째 시험에 대한 응답이며, 경배와 섬김의 대상이 오직 하나님이심을 선언한다.
[^9]: ἵνα πληρωθῇ는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 예수의 가버나움 정착이 이사야 예언 성취라는 하나님의 계획 안에 있음을 보여준다.
[^10]: 이사야 9:1-2 인용으로, 갈릴리에서의 예수의 사역이 어둠 가운데 빛의 도래라는 구약 예언의 성취임을 밝힌다.
