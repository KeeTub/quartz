<span class="v-num">1</span> And it happened, when Jesus finished commanding his twelve disciples, he departed from there to teach and to preach in their cities.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Now John, having heard in the prison the works of the Christ, having sent two of his disciples,

<span class="v-num">3</span> said to him, "Are you the one coming, or should we look for another?"

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Jesus answering said to them, "Having gone, report to John the things you hear and see:

<span class="v-num">5</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> blind receive sight, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lame walk; lepers are cleansed, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> deaf hear; <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead are raised, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> poor have good news preached <span class="added-text">(to them)</span>;

<span class="v-num">6</span> and blessed is he, whoever is not offended because of me."[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now as these were going away, Jesus began to say to the crowds concerning John, "What did you go out into the wilderness to see? A reed shaken by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wind?

<span class="v-num">8</span> But what did you go out to see? A man clothed in soft garments? Behold, those wearing soft <span class="added-text">(garments)</span> are in the houses of kings.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But what did you go out to see? A prophet? Yes, I say to you, and more than a prophet.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For this is he concerning whom it is written, 'Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Truly I say to you, among those born of women there has not arisen <span class="added-text">(one)</span> greater than John the Baptist; but the least in the kingdom of the heavens is greater than he.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And from the days of John the Baptist until now, the kingdom of the heavens suffers <span class="alt-label">violence</span><span class="alt-text">(=is forcefully advanced)</span>, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> violent take it by force.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For all the prophets and the law prophesied until John;

<span class="v-num">14</span> and if you are willing to receive <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, he is Elijah who is to come.

<span class="v-num">15</span> He who has ears to hear, let him hear.

<span class="v-num">16</span> But to what shall I liken this generation? It is like children sitting in the marketplaces, calling to their companions,

<span class="v-num">17</span> and saying, 'We piped for you, and you did not dance; we mourned for you, and you did not beat <span class="added-text">(your breasts)</span>.'

<span class="v-num">18</span> For John came neither eating nor drinking, and they say, 'He has a demon.'

<span class="v-num">19</span> The Son of Man came eating and drinking, and they say, 'Behold, a man <span class="added-text">(who is)</span> a glutton and a wine-drinker, a friend of tax collectors and sinners.' But wisdom was justified <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> her children."

<span class="v-num">20</span> Then he began to reproach the cities in which most of his mighty works were done, because they did not repent.

<span class="v-num">21</span> "Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to you, Bethsaida! For if the mighty works which were done in you had been done in Tyre and Sidon, they would have repented long ago in sackcloth and ashes.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But I say to you, it will be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment, than for you.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And you, Capernaum, who are exalted to heaven, will be brought down to Hades; for if the mighty works which were done in you had been done in Sodom, it would have remained until this day.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But I say to you, that it will be more tolerable for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Sodom in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment, than for you."

<span class="v-num">25</span> At that time Jesus answering said, "I <span class="alt-label">praise</span><span class="alt-text">(=confess/thank)</span> you, Father, Lord of heaven and earth, that you hid these things from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wise and understanding, and revealed them to <span class="alt-label">infants</span><span class="alt-text">(=little children)</span>.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Yes, Father, for so it was well-pleasing <span class="alt-label">before</span><span class="alt-text">(=in your sight)</span> you.

<span class="v-num">27</span> All things were delivered to me by my Father; and no one knows the Son except the Father; nor does anyone know the Father except the Son, and <span class="added-text">(he)</span> to whomever the Son wills to reveal <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.[^4]

<span class="v-num">28</span> Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.

<span class="v-num">29</span> Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart; and you will find rest for your souls.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light."

[^1]: ὃς ἐὰν μὴ σκανδαλισθῇ는 부정관계사+ἐάν+가정법으로 '누구든지 실족하지 않는다면'이라는 미래의 불확정 조건을 나타낸다. 아직 실현되지 않은 가능성을 전제로 한 일반적 조건절이다.
[^2]: 말라기 3:1을 인용하여 세례 요한을 메시아의 선구자로 규정한다.
[^3]: βιάζεται는 능동/수동 해석이 갈리는 난해구절로, 나라의 강력한 도래와 그것을 얻으려는 열심 두 뜻이 함께 들린다.
[^4]: ᾧ ἐὰν βούληται는 관계대명사+ἐάν+가정법 구조로 '아들이 원하는 자에게는 누구든지'라는 뜻의 부정관계 조건절이다. 특정 대상을 미리 정하지 않고 아들의 자유로운 선택에 달려 있음을 강조한다.
