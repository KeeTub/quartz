<span class="v-num">1</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> book of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> genealogy of Jesus Christ, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of David, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of Abraham.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Abraham begot Isaac, and Isaac begot Jacob, and Jacob begot Judah and his brothers,

<span class="v-num">3</span> and Judah begot Perez and Zerah from Tamar, and Perez begot Hezron, and Hezron begot Ram,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and Ram begot Amminadab, and Amminadab begot Nahshon, and Nahshon begot Salmon,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and Salmon begot Boaz from Rahab, and Boaz begot Obed from Ruth, and Obed begot Jesse,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and Jesse begot David the king. And David the king begot Solomon from her <span class="added-text">(who had been the wife)</span> of Uriah,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and Solomon begot Rehoboam, and Rehoboam begot Abijah, and Abijah begot Asa,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and Asa begot Jehoshaphat, and Jehoshaphat begot Joram, and Joram begot Uzziah,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and Uzziah begot Jotham, and Jotham begot Ahaz, and Ahaz begot Hezekiah,

<span class="v-num">10</span> and Hezekiah begot Manasseh, and Manasseh begot Amon, and Amon begot Josiah,

<span class="v-num">11</span> and Josiah begot Jechoniah and his brothers, at the time of the deportation <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=of)</span> Babylon.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And after the deportation to Babylon, Jechoniah begot Shealtiel, and Shealtiel begot Zerubbabel,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and Zerubbabel begot Abiud, and Abiud begot Eliakim, and Eliakim begot Azor,

<span class="v-num">14</span> and Azor begot Zadok, and Zadok begot Achim, and Achim begot Eliud,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and Eliud begot Eleazar, and Eleazar begot Matthan, and Matthan begot Jacob,

<span class="v-num">16</span> and Jacob begot Joseph the husband of Mary, from whom Jesus, the one called Christ, was born.[^1]

<span class="v-num">17</span> So all the generations from Abraham to David <span class="added-text">(were)</span> fourteen generations, and from David until the deportation to Babylon <span class="added-text">(were)</span> fourteen generations, and from the deportation to Babylon until the Christ <span class="added-text">(were)</span> fourteen generations.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now the birth of Jesus Christ was thus: His mother Mary having been betrothed to Joseph, before they came together, she was found having in <span class="alt-label">womb</span><span class="alt-text">(=to be with child)</span> <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Joseph her husband, being righteous, and not wanting to make her a public example, resolved to put her away secretly.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But as he was pondering these things, behold, an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord appeared to him in a dream, saying, "Joseph, son of David, do not fear to take Mary your wife, for that which is begotten in her is of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And she will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins."[^3]

<span class="v-num">22</span> Now all this happened so that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> thing spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying,[^4]

<span class="v-num">23</span> "Behold, the virgin will have in womb and will bear a son, and they will call his name Emmanuel," which is, being translated, God with us.[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> And Joseph, having been awakened from sleep, did as the angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord commanded him, and took his wife,

<span class="v-num">25</span> and did not know her until she brought forth her firstborn son; and he called his name Jesus.[^6]

[^1]: 앞선 조상들에는 능동태 '낳고'(ἐγέννησε)가 반복되나 예수께는 수동태 ἐγεννήθη가 쓰여 요셉이 육신의 아버지가 아님을 문법적으로 드러낸다.
[^2]: γεννηθέν(수동 분사)이 이미 이루어진 잉태를 가리키며, ἐστιν(현재)은 그 잉태의 근원이 지금도 성령께 있음을 강조한다.
[^3]: '예수'(Ἰησοῦς)는 히브리어 '여호와는 구원이시다'에서 온 이름으로, σώσει(구원하다)와 어원적으로 연결되어 이름 자체가 사역을 예고한다.
[^4]: ἵνα πληρωθῇ(가정법)는 목적절로, 그리스도의 탄생이 예언 성취라는 신적 의도를 나타낸다.
[^5]: 이사야 7장 14절 인용으로, παρθένος(처녀)가 마태에 의해 메시아의 동정녀 탄생 성취로 적용된다.
[^6]: ἐγίνωσκεν(미완료)은 출산 시까지 지속된 부부관계의 부재를 강조하는 표현이다.
