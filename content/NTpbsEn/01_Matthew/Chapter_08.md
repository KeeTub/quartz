<span class="v-num">1</span> And as he came down from the mountain, many crowds followed him.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And behold, a leper, having come, worshiped him, saying, "Lord, if you are willing, you are able to cleanse me."[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And Jesus, having stretched out <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand, touched him, saying, "I am willing, be cleansed." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Jesus said to him, "See <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you tell no one, but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, for a testimony to them."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And when Jesus had entered into Capernaum, a centurion came to him, entreating him,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and saying, "Lord, my servant lies in the house paralyzed, grievously tormented."

<span class="v-num">7</span> And Jesus said to him, "Having come, I will heal him."

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the centurion, answering, said, "Lord, I am not worthy that you should enter under my roof; but only speak a word, and my servant will be healed.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For I also am a man under authority, having under myself soldiers; and I say to this one, 'Go,' and he goes; and to another, 'Come,' and he comes; and to my servant, 'Do this,' and he does <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And Jesus, having heard, marveled, and said to those following, "Truly I say to you, not even in Israel have I found so great faith.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And I say to you, that many will come from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> east and west, and will recline <span class="added-text">(at table)</span> with Abraham and Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of the heavens;

<span class="v-num">12</span> but the sons of the kingdom will be cast out into the outer darkness; there will be the weeping and the gnashing of teeth."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And Jesus said to the centurion, "Go, and as you have believed, let it be done to you." And his servant was healed in that hour.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus, having come into Peter's house, saw his mother-in-law lying <span class="added-text">(sick)</span> and burning with fever,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and he touched her hand, and the fever left her; and she arose, and served them.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And when evening had come, they brought to him many who were demon-possessed; and he cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were <span class="alt-label">sick</span><span class="alt-text">(=ill)</span>,

<span class="v-num">17</span> so that what was spoken through Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, 'He himself took our infirmities, and bore our diseases.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Jesus, seeing many crowds around him, commanded <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to depart to the other side.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And a certain scribe, having come near, said to him, "Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go."[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> And Jesus said to him, "The foxes have holes, and the birds of the sky <span class="added-text">(have)</span> nests; but the Son of Man has nowhere to lay <span class="added-text">(his)</span> head."[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And another of his disciples said to him, "Lord, permit me first to go and bury my father."

<span class="v-num">22</span> But Jesus said to him, "Follow me, and let the dead bury their own dead."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And when he had boarded the boat, his disciples followed him.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And behold, a great storm arose in the sea, so that the boat was being covered by the waves; but he was sleeping.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And his disciples, having come to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, woke him, saying, "Lord, save us, we are perishing."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And he said to them, "Why are you afraid, O you of little faith?" Then, having risen, he rebuked the winds and the sea, and there was a great calm.[^6]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And the men marveled, saying, "What sort <span class="added-text">(of man)</span> is this, that even the winds and the sea obey him?"

<span class="v-num">28</span> And when he had come to the other side, into the country of the Gergesenes, two demon-possessed <span class="added-text">(men)</span> met him, coming out from the tombs, exceedingly fierce, so that no one was able to pass by that way.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And behold, they cried out, saying, "What <span class="added-text">(is there)</span> to us and to you, Jesus, Son of God? Have you come here before the time to torment us?"

<span class="v-num">30</span> Now there was, far from them, a herd of many swine feeding.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And the demons entreated him, saying, "If you cast us out, permit us to go away into the herd of swine."

<span class="v-num">32</span> And he said to them, "Go." And having come out, they went away into the herd of swine; and behold, the whole herd of swine rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and died in the waters.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And those who fed <span class="added-text">(them)</span> fled, and having gone away into the city, reported everything, and <span class="added-text">(the events)</span> concerning those who had been demon-possessed.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And behold, the whole city went out to meet Jesus; and having seen him, they begged <span class="added-text">(him)</span> that he would depart from their region.

[^1]: ἐὰν θέλῃς는 ἐάν+가정법으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 나병환자는 주님의 능력을 확신하면서도 그분의 뜻(의지)에 전적으로 결과를 맡기는 겸손한 태도를 보인다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(εἰσέλθῃς)이 '~하기에' 합당함의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, 백부장의 겸손한 자기 낮춤을 표현한다.
[^3]: 이사야 53:4의 인용으로, 마태는 이를 예수의 치유 사역이 종의 노래를 성취한 것으로 해석한다.
[^4]: ὅπου ἐὰν ἀπέρχῃ는 'ἐάν+가정법'으로 '어디로 가시든지'라는 뜻의 부정관계 조건절이며, 장소를 특정하지 않은 채 미래의 모든 가능성을 포괄하는 표현이다. 서기관의 헌신 다짐이 조건에 관계없이 무조건적임을 강조한다.
[^5]: '인자'(υἱὸς τοῦ ἀνθρώπου)는 예수께서 자신을 지칭하실 때 즐겨 쓰신 칭호로, 여기서는 낮아지심과 정처 없음을 강조한다.
[^6]: '믿음이 적은 자'(ὀλιγόπιστος)는 마태복음에만 나오는 특유 표현으로, 믿음의 부재가 아니라 믿음의 부족을 꾸짖는 표현이다.
