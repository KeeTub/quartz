# Matthew 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> And on that day, Jesus, having gone out from the house, sat by the sea.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And many crowds gathered to him, so that he got into the boat and sat, and all the crowd stood on the shore.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he spoke many things to them in parables, saying, "Behold, the sower went out to sow.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And as he sowed, some <span class="added-text">(seeds)</span> fell beside the road, and the birds came and devoured them,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and others fell on the rocky <span class="added-text">(places)</span>, where they did not have much earth, and immediately they sprang up, because they had no depth of earth,

<span class="v-num">6</span> but when the sun rose, they were scorched, and because they had no root, they withered,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and others fell upon the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked them,

<span class="v-num">8</span> but others fell upon the good ground, and were yielding fruit, one a hundredfold, one sixty, one thirty.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> He who has ears to hear, let him hear."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the disciples came and said to him, "Why do you speak to them in parables?"

<span class="v-num">11</span> And answering, he said to them, "Because it has been given to you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of the heavens, but to them it has not been given.[^2]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For whoever has, to him will be given, and he will have abundance; but whoever does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore I speak to them in parables, because seeing they do not see, and hearing they do not hear, nor do they understand.[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And in them the prophecy of Isaiah is fulfilled, which says, ‘Hearing you will hear, and will not understand at all; and seeing you will see, and will not perceive at all.[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> For the heart of this people has grown dull, and with their ears they have heard heavily, and their eyes they have closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and should turn, and I should heal them.’

<span class="v-num">16</span> But blessed are your eyes, because they see; and your ears, because they hear.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For truly I say to you, that many prophets and righteous <span class="added-text">(men)</span> desired to see what you see, and did not see <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and to hear what you hear, and did not hear <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">18</span> You, therefore, hear the parable of the sower.

<span class="v-num">19</span> When anyone hears the word of the kingdom and does not understand <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, the evil one comes and snatches away what was sown in his heart; this is he who was sown beside the road.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But he who was sown on the rocky <span class="added-text">(places)</span>, this is he who hears the word, and immediately receives it with joy;

<span class="v-num">21</span> yet he has no root in himself, but is <span class="alt-label">temporary</span><span class="alt-text">(=lasts only for a time)</span>; and when tribulation or persecution arises because of the word, immediately he stumbles.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And he who was sown among the thorns, this is he who hears the word, and the care of this age and the deceit of riches choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But he who was sown on the good ground, this is he who hears the word and understands <span class="added-text">(it)</span>; who indeed bears fruit, and produces, one a hundredfold, one sixty, one thirty."

<span class="v-num">24</span> He set another parable before them, saying, "The kingdom of the heavens is likened to a man sowing good seed in his field;

<span class="v-num">25</span> but while the men were sleeping, his enemy came and sowed tares in the midst of the wheat, and went away.

<span class="v-num">26</span> But when the blade sprang up, and produced fruit, then the tares appeared also.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And the servants of the master of the house came and said to him, <span class="alt-label">‘Sir</span><span class="alt-text">(=Lord)</span>, did you not sow good seed in your field? Where then does it have tares?’

<span class="v-num">28</span> And he said to them, ‘An enemy man has done this.’ And the servants said to him, ‘Do you want us, then, to go and gather them up?’

<span class="v-num">29</span> But he said, ‘No, lest while gathering the tares you root up the wheat together with them.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Let both grow together until the harvest, and in the time of the harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather the tares first, and bind them into bundles to burn them, but gather the wheat into my barn.’"

<span class="v-num">31</span> He set another parable before them, saying, "The kingdom of the heavens is like a grain of mustard seed, which a man took and sowed in his field;

<span class="v-num">32</span> which indeed is smaller than all the seeds; but when it is grown, it is greater than the herbs, and becomes a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in its branches."

<span class="v-num">33</span> He spoke another parable to them: "The kingdom of the heavens is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of flour, until it was all leavened."

<span class="v-num">34</span> All these things Jesus spoke to the crowds in parables, and without a parable he did not speak to them,

<span class="v-num">35</span> so that it might be fulfilled which was spoken through the prophet, saying, ‘I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things hidden from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world.’[^5]

<span class="v-num">36</span> Then, having sent away the crowds, Jesus went into the house; and his disciples came to him, saying, "Explain to us the parable of the tares of the field."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And answering, he said to them, "He who sows the good seed is the Son of Man;

<span class="v-num">38</span> and the field is the world; and the good seed, these are the sons of the kingdom; but the tares are the sons of the evil one;

<span class="v-num">39</span> and the enemy who sowed them is the devil; and the harvest is the end of the age; and the reapers are angels.

<span class="v-num">40</span> As therefore the tares are gathered and burned in fire, so it will be in the end of this age.

<span class="v-num">41</span> The Son of Man will send his angels, and they will gather out of his kingdom all the <span class="alt-label">offenses</span><span class="alt-text">(=stumbling-blocks)</span>, and those who practice lawlessness,

<span class="v-num">42</span> and will cast them into the furnace of fire; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

<span class="v-num">43</span> Then the righteous will shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. He who has ears to hear, let him hear.

<span class="v-num">44</span> Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like treasure hidden in a field, which a man found and hid; and out of his joy he goes and sells all that he has, and buys that field.

<span class="v-num">45</span> Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a merchant man seeking good pearls,

<span class="v-num">46</span> who, having found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.

<span class="v-num">47</span> Again, the kingdom of the heavens is like a net that was cast into the sea, and gathered <span class="added-text">(fish)</span> of every kind;

<span class="v-num">48</span> which, when it was filled, they drew up on the shore, and sitting down, gathered the good into containers, but threw the bad out.

<span class="v-num">49</span> So it will be at the end of the age: the angels will go out, and separate the wicked from among the righteous,

<span class="v-num">50</span> and will cast them into the furnace of fire; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

<span class="v-num">51</span> Jesus said to them, "Have you understood all these things?" They said to him, "Yes, Lord."

<span class="v-num">52</span> And he said to them, "Therefore every scribe discipled into the kingdom of the heavens is like a man, a master of a house, who brings out of his treasure things new and old."[^6]

<span class="v-num">53</span> And it came to pass, when Jesus had finished these parables, he departed from there;

<span class="v-num">54</span> and having come into his own country, he was teaching them in their synagogue, so that they were astonished, and said, "Where did this man <span class="added-text">(get)</span> this wisdom, and these mighty works?

<span class="v-num">55</span> Is not this the carpenter's son? Is not his mother called Mary, and his brothers James and Joses and Simon and Judas?

<span class="v-num">56</span> And his sisters, are they not all with us? Where then did this man get all these things?"

<span class="v-num">57</span> And they were offended in him. But Jesus said to them, "A prophet is not without honor, except in his own country, and in his own house."

<span class="v-num">58</span> And he did not do many mighty works there, because of their unbelief.[^7]

[^1]: ἐδίδου는 미완료 시제로, 좋은 땅에서 열매가 지속적·반복적으로 맺혀감을 나타낸다.
[^2]: μυστήρια(비밀)는 인간 이성으로는 알 수 없고 오직 계시로만 주어지는 하나님 나라의 진리를 가리킨다.
[^3]: 비유는 완악한 마음에 대한 심판적 감춤과 동시에, 듣고자 하는 자에게는 계시의 통로가 되는 이중 기능을 한다.
[^4]: 이사야 6:9-10 인용으로, 완악한 백성에게 임하는 심판적 소경됨을 예언한 말씀이다.
[^5]: 시편 78:2 인용으로, 예수의 비유 사역이 옛적부터 감춰진 하나님의 경륜을 드러내는 것임을 보여준다.
[^6]: 새 것과 옛 것을 함께 내어옴은 그리스도 안에서 옛 언약의 계시와 새 나라의 가르침이 하나로 연결됨을 보여준다.
[^7]: ἀπιστίαν(믿지 않음)은 단순한 지적 회의가 아니라 예수의 권위 자체를 거부하는 마음의 완악함을 가리키며, 이는 이적의 제한과 직결된다.
