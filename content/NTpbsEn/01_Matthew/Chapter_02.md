# Matthew 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now Jesus having been born in Bethlehem of Judea, in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> days of Herod the king, behold, magi from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> east arrived at Jerusalem,

<span class="v-num">2</span> saying, "Where is he who was born king of the Jews? For we saw his star in the east, and we came to worship him."

<span class="v-num">3</span> And Herod the king having heard <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him;

<span class="v-num">4</span> and having gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people, he inquired from them where the Christ is to be born.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And they said to him, "In Bethlehem of Judea; for so it has been written through the prophet,

<span class="v-num">6</span> 'And you, Bethlehem, land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you shall come forth a ruler, who will shepherd my people Israel.'"

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then Herod, having secretly called the magi, inquired precisely from them the time of the appearing star,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and having sent them to Bethlehem, he said, "Having gone, search carefully concerning the child; and when you find <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, report to me, so that I also, having gone, may worship him."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And they, having heard the king, went; and behold, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them, until it came <span class="added-text">(and)</span> stood over where the child was.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And having seen the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy;

<span class="v-num">11</span> And having come into the house, they found the child with Mary his mother, and having fallen down, they worshiped him; and having opened their treasures, they offered to him gifts, gold and frankincense and myrrh.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And having been divinely warned in a dream not to return to Herod, they departed into their own country by another way.[^1]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And after they had departed, behold, an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord appeared to Joseph in a dream, saying, "Having risen, take the child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and remain there until I tell you; for Herod is about to seek the child, to destroy him."[^2]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he, having risen, took the child and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and was there until the death of Herod, so that what was spoken by the Lord through the prophet might be fulfilled, saying, 'Out of Egypt I called my son.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Then Herod, having seen that he had been mocked by the magi, was greatly enraged, and having sent <span class="added-text">(men)</span>, he killed all the male children in Bethlehem and in all its districts, from two years old and under, according to the time which he had precisely learned from the magi.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Then was fulfilled what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying,

<span class="v-num">18</span> 'A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation and weeping and great mourning, Rachel weeping <span class="added-text">(for)</span> her children, and she would not be comforted, because they are not.'

<span class="v-num">19</span> And after Herod had died, behold, an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> saying, "Having risen, take the child and his mother, and go into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Israel; for those seeking the child's life have died."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And he, having risen, took the child and his mother, and came into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Israel.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But having heard that Archelaus was reigning over Judea in place of his father Herod, he was afraid to go there; and having been divinely warned in a dream, he withdrew into the region of Galilee,

<span class="v-num">23</span> and having come, he dwelt in a city called Nazareth, so that what was spoken through the prophets might be fulfilled, that he would be called a Nazarene.[^5]

[^1]: χρηματισθέντες는 단순 통보가 아니라 신적 계시를 통해 지시받음을 뜻하는 전문 용어로, 본문에서 하나님의 직접적 인도하심을 강조한다.
[^2]: φαίνεται는 역사적 현재형이나 개역체 서사 흐름을 따라 과거형 '나타나'로 옮겼다.
[^3]: ἵνα πληρωθῇ는 목적절로, 요셉의 애굽 피신이 호세아의 예언을 이루기 위한 하나님의 섭리였음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: φαίνεται 역시 역사적 현재형이나 서사체 통일을 위해 과거형으로 옮겼다.
[^5]: ὅπως πληρωθῇ는 목적절로, 나사렛 정착이 선지자들의 예언 성취를 위한 하나님의 계획이었음을 보여준다.
