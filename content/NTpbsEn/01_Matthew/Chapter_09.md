<span class="v-num">1</span> And having embarked into the boat, <span class="added-text">(Jesus)</span> crossed over and came into his own city.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And behold, they brought to him a paralytic having been laid on a bed; and Jesus, having seen their faith, said to the paralytic, "Take courage, child, your sins have been forgiven you."[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And behold, some of the scribes said within themselves, "This one blasphemes."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Jesus, having seen their thoughts, said, "Why do you think evil things in your hearts?

<span class="v-num">5</span> For which is easier, to say, 'Your sins have been forgiven,' or to say, 'Rise and walk'?

<span class="v-num">6</span> But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on the earth to forgive sins" — then he said to the paralytic — "Arise, take up your bed, and go to your house."[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having arisen, he went away to his house.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the crowds, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, marveled, and glorified God, who gave such authority to men.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And Jesus, passing by from there, saw a man sitting at the tax office, called Matthew, and said to him, "Follow me." And having risen, he followed him.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And it came to pass, as he sat at table in the house, that behold, many tax collectors and sinners came and sat down with Jesus and his disciples.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the Pharisees, having seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said to his disciples, "Why does your teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?"

<span class="v-num">12</span> But Jesus, having heard, said to them, "The healthy have no need of a physician, but those being sick <span class="added-text">(do)</span>.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But go and learn what this is: 'I desire mercy, and not sacrifice.' For I did not come to call <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteous, but sinners to repentance."[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Then the disciples of John came to him, saying, "Why do we and the Pharisees fast much, but your disciples do not fast?"

<span class="v-num">15</span> And Jesus said to them, "Can the sons of the bridechamber mourn, as long as the bridegroom is with them? But days will come when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then they will fast.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And no one puts a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; for its <span class="alt-label">patch</span><span class="alt-text">(=filling)</span> takes away from the garment, and a worse tear results.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Nor do they put new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the skins burst, and the wine pours out, and the skins are ruined. But they put new wine into new wineskins, and both are preserved."

<span class="v-num">18</span> As he spoke these things to them, behold, a ruler came and worshiped him, saying, "My daughter has just died; but come and lay your hand on her, and she will live."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And Jesus arose and followed him, and his disciples <span class="added-text">(did)</span> also.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And behold, a woman having a flow of blood for twelve years, having come up behind, touched the fringe of his garment.

<span class="v-num">21</span> For she said within herself, "If I only touch his garment, I will be made well."[^4]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And Jesus, having turned and seen her, said, "Take courage, daughter; your faith has made you well." And the woman was made well from that hour.[^5]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And Jesus, having come into the ruler's house, and having seen the flute players and the crowd making a commotion,

<span class="v-num">24</span> said to them, "Depart, for the girl has not died, but is sleeping." And they laughed at him.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But when the crowd had been put out, he went in and took hold of her hand, and the girl arose.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And this report went out into all that land.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And as Jesus passed by from there, two blind men followed him, crying out and saying, "Have mercy on us, Son of David."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And when he had come into the house, the blind men came to him, and Jesus said to them, "Do you believe that I am able to do this?" They said to him, "Yes, Lord."

<span class="v-num">29</span> Then he touched their eyes, saying, "According to your faith be it done to you."

<span class="v-num">30</span> And their eyes were opened; and Jesus sternly warned them, saying, "See that no one knows <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">31</span> But they, having gone out, spread his fame in all that land.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And as they went out, behold, they brought to him a mute man possessed by a demon.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And when the demon had been cast out, the mute man spoke; and the crowds marveled, saying, "It has never appeared this way in Israel."

<span class="v-num">34</span> But the Pharisees said, "By the ruler of the demons he casts out the demons."[^6]

<span class="v-num">35</span> And Jesus went about all the cities and the villages, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and every sickness among the people.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And having seen the crowds, he was moved with compassion for them, because they were weary and scattered like sheep having no shepherd.

<span class="v-num">37</span> Then he said to his disciples, "The harvest indeed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> plentiful, but the laborers <span class="added-text">(are)</span> few;

<span class="v-num">38</span> therefore pray to the Lord of the harvest, that he may send out laborers into his harvest."

[^1]: ἀφέωνταί는 완료수동태로 죄 사함이 이미 완결되어 그 효력이 지속됨을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα εἰδῆτε는 목적절(‘알게 하려’)이며, τότε λέγει의 현재시제는 역사적 현재로서 본문에서는 과거형으로 옮겼다.
[^3]: 호세아 6:6 인용으로 하나님이 원하시는 것은 형식적 제사가 아니라 자비임을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(ἅψωμαι)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '혹시라도 만지기만 하면'이라는 여인의 확신 어린 기대와 소망을 생생하게 표현한다.
[^5]: σέσωκέ는 완료시상으로 믿음으로 인한 구원의 지속적 효력을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐν τῷ ἄρχοντι의 ἐν은 도구/권능의 의미로 ‘~의 힘으로’를 나타낸다.
