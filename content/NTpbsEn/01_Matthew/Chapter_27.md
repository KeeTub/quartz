<span class="v-num">1</span> Now morning having come, all the chief priests and the elders of the people took counsel against Jesus, so as to put him to death.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having bound him, they led him away and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Then Judas, the one who betrayed him, having seen that he was condemned, having <span class="alt-label">repented</span><span class="alt-text">(=felt remorse)</span>, returned the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and elders,

<span class="v-num">4</span> saying, "I sinned, having betrayed innocent blood." But they said, "What <span class="added-text">(is that)</span> to us? You shall see <span class="added-text">(to it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And having thrown the pieces of silver into the temple, he departed; and having gone away, he hanged himself.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But the chief priests, having taken the pieces of silver, said, "It is not lawful to put them into the <span class="alt-label">treasury</span><span class="alt-text">(=korban)</span>, since it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> price of blood."

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having taken counsel, they bought with them the potter's field, for a burial place for strangers.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore that field was called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> field of blood, until this day.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet, saying, 'And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him who was priced, whom they of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sons of Israel priced,[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> and gave them for the potter's field, as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord directed me.'

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now Jesus stood before the governor; and the governor questioned him, saying, "Are you the king of the Jews?" And Jesus said to him, "You say <span class="added-text">(so)</span>."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And while he was being accused by the chief priests and the elders, he answered nothing.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Then Pilate said to him, "Do you not hear how many things they testify against you?"

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he did not answer him to even one word, so that the governor marveled greatly.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Now at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> feast the governor was accustomed to release to the crowd one prisoner, whom they wanted.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And they had then a notorious prisoner, called Barabbas.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Therefore when they were gathered together, Pilate said to them, "Whom do you want <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I release to you? Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> For he knew that because of envy they had delivered him.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And while he was sitting on the judgment seat, his wife sent to him, saying, "<span class="added-text">(Have)</span> nothing to do with that righteous man; for I suffered many things today in a dream because of him."

<span class="v-num">20</span> But the chief priests and the elders persuaded the crowds that they should ask for Barabbas, and destroy Jesus.[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the governor answered and said to them, "Which of the two do you want <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I release to you?" And they said, "Barabbas."

<span class="v-num">22</span> Pilate said to them, "What then shall I do <span class="added-text">(with)</span> Jesus who is called Christ?" They all said to him, "Let him be crucified."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And the governor said, "Why, what evil has he done?" But they cried out exceedingly, saying, "Let him be crucified."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And Pilate, seeing that nothing was <span class="alt-label">gained</span><span class="alt-text">(=availed)</span>, but rather a tumult was arising, having taken water, washed <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands before the crowd, saying, "I am innocent of the blood of this righteous <span class="added-text">(man)</span>; you shall see <span class="added-text">(to it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And all the people answered and said, "His blood <span class="added-text">(be)</span> upon us and upon our children."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Then he released to them Barabbas; but Jesus, having scourged <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, he delivered <span class="added-text">(him)</span> that he might be crucified.[^3]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Then the soldiers of the governor, having taken Jesus into the praetorium, gathered against him the whole <span class="alt-label">cohort</span><span class="alt-text">(=band)</span>.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And having stripped him, they put on him a scarlet robe;

<span class="v-num">29</span> and having plaited a crown of thorns, they put <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on his head, and a reed in his right <span class="added-text">(hand)</span>; and having knelt before him, they mocked him, saying, "Hail, king of the Jews!"

<span class="v-num">30</span> And having spit on him, they took the reed and struck <span class="added-text">(him)</span> on his head.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And when they had mocked him, they took off the robe from him, and put on him his own garments, and led him away to crucify <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And as they went out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon; him they compelled that he might carry his cross.[^4]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And having come to a place called Golgotha, which is called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> place of a skull,

<span class="v-num">34</span> they gave him to drink vinegar mixed with gall; and having tasted <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, he would not drink.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And having crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled that which was spoken by the prophet, 'They divided my garments among themselves, and for my clothing they cast lots.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">36</span> And sitting down, they kept watch over him there.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And they put up over his head his accusation written, "This is Jesus the king of the Jews."

<span class="v-num">38</span> Then were crucified with him two robbers, one on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand and one on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> left.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And those passing by reviled him, wagging their heads,

<span class="v-num">40</span> and saying, "You who destroy the temple and build <span class="added-text">(it)</span> in three days, save yourself; if you are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God, come down from the cross."

<span class="v-num">41</span> Likewise also the chief priests, mocking with the scribes and elders, said,

<span class="v-num">42</span> "He saved others; himself he cannot save. If he is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> king of Israel, let him come down now from the cross, and we will believe him.

<span class="v-num">43</span> He trusts in God; let him deliver him now, if he wants him. For he said, 'I am <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God.'"[^6]

<span class="v-num">44</span> And the robbers also, who were crucified with him, reproached him with the same thing.

<span class="v-num">45</span> Now from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sixth hour, darkness came over all the land until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ninth hour.

<span class="v-num">46</span> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, "Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani?" that is, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?"[^7]

<span class="v-num">47</span> And some of those standing there, having heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said, "This <span class="added-text">(man)</span> is calling Elijah."

<span class="v-num">48</span> And immediately one of them ran, and took a sponge, and having filled <span class="added-text">(it)</span> with vinegar, and having put <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on a reed, gave him <span class="added-text">(to)</span> drink.

<span class="v-num">49</span> But the rest said, "Let <span class="added-text">(it)</span> be, let us see if Elijah comes to save him."

<span class="v-num">50</span> And Jesus, having cried out again with a loud voice, yielded up <span class="added-text">(his)</span> spirit.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And behold, the veil of the temple was torn in two from top to bottom; and the earth was shaken, and the rocks were split;

<span class="v-num">52</span> and the tombs were opened, and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised,

<span class="v-num">53</span> and having come out of the tombs after his resurrection, they went into the holy city, and appeared to many.

<span class="v-num">54</span> Now the centurion, and those with him keeping watch over Jesus, having seen the earthquake and the things that happened, feared exceedingly, saying, "Truly this was <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God."[^8]

<span class="v-num">55</span> And many women were there, watching from afar, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering to him;

<span class="v-num">56</span> among whom was Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James and Joses, and the mother of the sons of Zebedee.

<span class="v-num">57</span> And when evening had come, there came a rich man from Arimathea, named Joseph, who himself also was a disciple of Jesus.

<span class="v-num">58</span> This man, having gone to Pilate, asked for the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded the body to be given <span class="added-text">(to him)</span>.

<span class="v-num">59</span> And Joseph, having taken the body, wrapped it in clean linen,

<span class="v-num">60</span> and laid it in his new tomb, which he had hewn out in the rock; and having rolled a great stone to the door of the tomb, he went away.

<span class="v-num">61</span> And Mary Magdalene was there, and the other Mary, sitting opposite the grave.

<span class="v-num">62</span> Now on the next day, which is after the preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered together to Pilate,

<span class="v-num">63</span> saying, "Sir, we remembered that that deceiver said while yet alive, 'After three days I will rise <span class="added-text">(again)</span>.'"

<span class="v-num">64</span> "Command therefore that the tomb be made secure until the third day, lest his disciples come by night <span class="added-text">(and)</span> steal him, and say to the people, 'He is risen from the dead'; and the last deception will be worse than the first."

<span class="v-num">65</span> Pilate said to them, "You have a guard; go, make <span class="added-text">(it)</span> as secure as you know <span class="added-text">(how)</span>."

<span class="v-num">66</span> And they went and made the tomb secure, sealing the stone, with the guard <span class="added-text">(posted)</span>.

[^1]: 인용은 형식상 예레미야로 되어 있으나 내용은 스가랴 11:12-13과 예레미야의 이미지가 혼합된 구약 인용이다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(αἰτήσωνται, ἀπολέσωσιν) 목적절로, 대제사장들과 장로들이 무리를 설득한 목적(바라바는 놓아주고 예수는 죽이려는 의도)을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(σταυρωθῇ)은 파레도켄('넘겨주었다')의 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다. 즉 예수를 넘겨준 것은 다름 아닌 십자가에 못박히게 하려는 의도된 목적이었음을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἄρῃ)은 목적절로, 시몬을 강제로 동원한 목적이 '십자가를 지게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῇ) 목적절로, 예언 성취라는 하나님의 의도를 나타낸다. 십자가 사건이 우연이 아니라 성경 성취를 위한 목적임을 강조한다.
[^6]: 시편 22:8의 조롱 어구를 그대로 반영한 것으로, 십자가의 조롱이 시편 22편의 성취임을 다시 보인다.
[^7]: 시편 22:1의 아람어 음역으로, 십자가 위의 절규가 시편 22편 전체를 배경으로 하고 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: 헬라어 원문 "Θεοῦ υἱὸς"에 관사가 없어 '하나님의 아들'이라는 신적 정체성 고백을 이방인 백부장의 입을 통해 강조하는 효과가 있다.
