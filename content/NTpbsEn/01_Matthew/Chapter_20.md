# Matthew 20

<span class="v-num">1</span> For the kingdom of the heavens is like a man, a housemaster, who went out at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> same early <span class="alt-label"><span class="added-text">(hour)</span></span><span class="alt-text">(=at dawn)</span> to hire workers into his vineyard.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having agreed with the workers for a denarius <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the day, he sent them into his vineyard.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And having gone out about the third hour, he saw others standing idle in the marketplace,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and to those also he said, 'Go, you also, into the vineyard, and whatever is right I will give you.' And they went.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Again, having gone out about <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sixth and ninth hour, he did likewise.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And about the eleventh hour, having gone out, he found others standing idle, and he said to them, 'Why do you stand here idle the whole day?'

<span class="v-num">7</span> They say to him, 'Because no one hired us.' He says to them, 'Go, you also, into the vineyard, and whatever is right you will receive.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And evening having come, the lord of the vineyard says to his steward, 'Call the workers and give them the wage, beginning from the last unto the first.'

<span class="v-num">9</span> And having come, those about the eleventh hour received each a denarius.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But having come, the first <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> supposed that they would receive more; and they also received each a denarius.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And having received <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they murmured against the housemaster,

<span class="v-num">12</span> saying, 'These last <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> worked <span class="added-text">(only)</span> one hour, and you have made them equal to us who bore the burden of the day and the scorching heat.'

<span class="v-num">13</span> But he, answering, said to one of them, 'Friend, I do you no wrong; did you not agree with me for a denarius?'

<span class="v-num">14</span> Take <span class="added-text">(what is)</span> yours and go. But I will to give to this last <span class="added-text">(one)</span> as also to you.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Or is it not lawful for me to do what I will with <span class="added-text">(what is)</span> mine? Is your eye evil because I am good?

<span class="v-num">16</span> So the last shall be first, and the first last; for many are called, but few chosen.[^3]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus, going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples aside on the way, and said to them,

<span class="v-num">18</span> "Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man will be delivered up to the chief priests and scribes; and they will condemn him to death,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and they will deliver him to the nations (=Gentiles) to mock and to scourge and to crucify <span class="added-text">(him)</span>; and on the third day he will rise."[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Then the mother of the sons of Zebedee came to him with her sons, worshiping and asking something from him.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And he said to her, "What do you desire?" She says to him, "Say that these my two sons may sit, one on your right hand and one on <span class="added-text">(your)</span> left, in your kingdom."[^5]

<span class="v-num">22</span> But Jesus, answering, said, "You do not know what you ask. Are you able to drink the cup that I am about to drink, and to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with?" They say to him, "We are able."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And he says to them, "You will indeed drink my cup, and be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with; but to sit on my right hand and on <span class="added-text">(my)</span> left is not mine to give, but <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> for those for whom it has been prepared by my Father."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And the ten, having heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, were indignant concerning the two brothers.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But Jesus, having called them to him, said, "You know that the rulers of the nations lord it over them, and the great ones exercise authority over them.

<span class="v-num">26</span> It will not be so among you; but whoever wills to become great among you, let him be your servant,[^6]

<span class="v-num">27</span> and whoever wills to be first among you, let him be your slave,[^7]

<span class="v-num">28</span> just as the Son of man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a ransom instead of many."[^8]

<span class="v-num">29</span> And as they went out from Jericho, a great crowd followed him.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And behold, two blind <span class="added-text">(men)</span> sitting by the way, having heard that Jesus was passing by, cried out, saying, "Have mercy on us, Lord, Son of David."

<span class="v-num">31</span> But the crowd rebuked them, that they should be silent; but they cried out <span class="added-text">(the)</span> more, saying, "Have mercy on us, Lord, Son of David."[^9]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And Jesus, having stood still, called them, and said, "What do you will <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I should do for you?"

<span class="v-num">33</span> They say to him, "Lord, that our eyes may be opened."[^10]

<span class="v-num">34</span> And Jesus, moved with compassion, touched their eyes; and immediately their eyes received sight, and they followed him.

[^1]: ὃ ἐὰν ᾖ δίκαιον은 '무엇이든 정당한 것이면'이라는 뜻으로, ἐάν+가정법이 아직 확정되지 않은 미래의 조건(불특정 관계절)을 나타낸다. 주인의 약속이 구체적 액수가 아니라 그의 정의로운 판단에 맡겨져 있음을 강조한다.
[^2]: ὃ ἐὰν ᾖ δίκαιον은 ἐάν+가정법 구문으로 '무엇이든지 의로운 것이면'이라는 미확정 조건절이다. 구체적 액수를 정하지 않고 주인의 선한 판단에 맡기는 불확실한 미래 상황을 나타낸다.
[^3]: 이 절은 19:30(“먼저 된 자로서 나중 되고...”)과 어순을 뒤집어 반복하는 인클루지오(inclusio)로 비유 전체를 감싸는 틀이 된다.
[^4]: εἰς τό + 부정사(ἐμπαῖξαι, μαστιγῶσαι, σταυρῶσαι)는 이방인들에게 넘겨주는 목적을 나타내는 부정사구문이다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(καθίσωσιν)이 εἰπέ의 목적어 역할을 하는 목적절로, '~하도록 말씀해 달라'는 청원의 내용을 나타낸다. 명령의 구체적 내용을 ἵνα절로 풀어 미래에 이루어지길 바라는 뉘앙스를 담는다.
[^6]: ὃς ἐὰν θέλῃ는 '누구든지 원하면'이라는 뜻의 부정관계 조건절로, 가정법을 사용해 특정 개인이 아닌 불특정 다수 중 누구에게나 해당되는 일반적 조건을 나타낸다. 미래에 실현 가능한 상황을 전제하는 열린 조건이다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(θέλῃ)은 '누구든지 원하면'이라는 불특정 대상을 포함한 조건절로, 미래에 실현 가능한 일반 조건을 나타낸다. 으뜸이 되고자 하는 자는 누구나 예외 없이 종의 자리를 택해야 함을 강조한다.
[^8]: λύτρον(대속물)은 노예나 포로를 값을 치르고 자유케 하는 몸값을 뜻하며, ἀντὶ πολλῶν은 많은 사람을 대신하는 대속적 죽음을 가리킨다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(σιωπήσωσιν)은 목적절로, 무리가 꾸짖은 '의도'를 나타낸다 – 그들을 잠잠하게 하려는 목적.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀνοιχθῶσιν)이 목적절로 쓰였으나 여기서는 소원/요청의 내용을 나타내는 독립적 용법(명령문 대용)이다. '우리 눈을 뜨게 해주소서'라는 간청의 목적을 표현한다.
