<span class="v-num">1</span> Then the scribes and Pharisees from Jerusalem came to Jesus, saying,

<span class="v-num">2</span> "Why do your disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? For they do not wash their hands when they eat bread."

<span class="v-num">3</span> But he, answering, said to them, "Why do you also transgress the commandment of God because of your tradition?

<span class="v-num">4</span> For God commanded, saying, 'Honor your father and mother,' and, 'The one speaking evil of father or mother, let him die (=let him surely die).'

<span class="v-num">5</span> but you say, 'Whoever says to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> father or mother, "<span class="added-text">(It is)</span> a gift, whatever you might have been profited from me,"'[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> then he shall by no means honor his father or his mother; and you have made void the commandment of God because of your tradition.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Hypocrites, well did Isaiah prophesy concerning you, saying,

<span class="v-num">8</span> 'This people draws near to me with their mouth, and honors me with <span class="added-text">(their)</span> lips, but their heart is far away from me.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But in vain do they worship me, teaching <span class="added-text">(as)</span> doctrines the commandments of men.'"

<span class="v-num">10</span> And having called the crowd to <span class="added-text">(himself)</span>, he said to them, "Hear and understand.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Not the thing entering into the mouth defiles the man; but the thing going out of the mouth, this defiles the man."

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then his disciples, having come to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, said to him, "Do you know that the Pharisees, having heard the saying, were offended?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> But he, answering, said, "Every plant which my heavenly Father did not plant shall be rooted up.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Leave them; they are blind guides of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blind. And if a blind <span class="added-text">(man)</span> leads a blind <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, both will fall into a ditch."[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But Peter, answering, said to him, "Explain to us this parable."

<span class="v-num">16</span> But Jesus said, "Are you also still without understanding?

<span class="v-num">17</span> Do you not yet perceive that everything entering into the mouth goes into the belly, and is cast out into the <span class="alt-label">drain</span><span class="alt-text">(=latrine)</span>?

<span class="v-num">18</span> But the things going out of the mouth come out from the heart, and those defile the man.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For out of the heart come forth evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false witnesses, blasphemies.

<span class="v-num">20</span> These are the things defiling the man; but to eat with unwashed hands does not defile the man."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And Jesus, having gone out from there, withdrew into the parts of Tyre and Sidon.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And behold, a Canaanite woman, having come out from those borders, cried out to him, saying, "Have mercy on me, Lord, Son of David; my daughter is badly demon-possessed."

<span class="v-num">23</span> But he answered her not a word. And his disciples, having come to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, asked him, saying, "Send her away, for she cries out after us."

<span class="v-num">24</span> But he, answering, said, "I was not sent except to the lost sheep of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house of Israel."

<span class="v-num">25</span> But she, having come, worshiped him, saying, "Lord, help me."

<span class="v-num">26</span> But he, answering, said, "It is not good to take the children's bread and to throw <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the little dogs."

<span class="v-num">27</span> But she said, "Yes, Lord; for even the little dogs eat from the crumbs falling from their masters' table."

<span class="v-num">28</span> Then Jesus, answering, said to her, "O woman, great <span class="added-text">(is)</span> your faith; let it be to you as you will." And her daughter was healed from that hour.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And Jesus, having departed from there, came beside the sea of Galilee; and having gone up onto the mountain, he sat there.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And many crowds came to him, having with themselves lame, blind, mute, maimed, and many others, and they laid them down beside the feet of Jesus, and he healed them,

<span class="v-num">31</span> so that the crowds marveled, seeing <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mute speaking, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> maimed whole, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lame walking, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blind seeing; and they glorified the God of Israel.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And Jesus, having called his disciples to <span class="added-text">(himself)</span>, said, "I have compassion on the crowd, because they have already remained with me three days, and they do not have <span class="added-text">(anything)</span> that they might eat. And I do not wish to send them away <span class="alt-label">hungry</span><span class="alt-text">(=fasting)</span>, lest they faint in the way."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And his disciples say to him, "From where <span class="added-text">(would we get)</span> for us in a desert so many loaves, so as to satisfy so great a crowd?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> And Jesus says to them, "How many loaves do you have?" And they said, "Seven, and a few small fish."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And he commanded the crowds to recline upon the ground;

<span class="v-num">36</span> and having taken the seven loaves and the fish, having given thanks, he broke <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and gave to his disciples, and the disciples <span class="added-text">(gave)</span> to the crowd.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And they all ate and were satisfied; and they took up that which remained over of the fragments, seven baskets full.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And those who ate were four thousand men, besides women and children.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And having sent away the crowds, he got into the boat, and came into the borders of Magdala.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ὠφεληθῇς)은 '네가 내게서 유익을 얻을 만한 것이 무엇이든지'라는 미확정 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다. 바리새인들이 편법으로 부모 부양을 회피하는 상황을 가정적으로 제시하는 구문이다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(ὁδηγῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 가정을 표현한다. 눈먼 자가 눈먼 자를 인도하는 상황을 가정하여 그 필연적 결과(둘 다 구덩이에 빠짐)를 경고한다.
