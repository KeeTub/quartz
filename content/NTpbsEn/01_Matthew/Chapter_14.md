# Matthew 14

<span class="v-num">1</span> At that time <span class="added-text">(the)</span> tetrarch Herod heard the report of Jesus,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and said to his servants, "This is John the Baptist; he was raised from the dead, and because of this the powers work in him."

<span class="v-num">3</span> For Herod, having seized John, bound him and put <span class="added-text">(him)</span> in prison, because of Herodias his brother Philip's wife.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For John said to him, "It is not lawful for you to have her."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And wanting to kill him, he feared the crowd, because they held him as a prophet.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But when Herod's birthday feast was being held, the daughter of Herodias danced in the midst and pleased Herod;

<span class="v-num">7</span> whereupon he promised with an oath to give her whatever she might ask.[^1]

<span class="v-num">8</span> But she, having been instructed by her mother, said, "Give me here on a platter the head of John the Baptist."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the king was grieved, but because of the oaths and those reclining with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, he commanded <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to be given;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and having sent, he beheaded John in the prison.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And his head was brought on a platter, and was given to the girl; and she brought <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to her mother.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And his disciples came and took up the body, and buried it; and having come, they reported <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to Jesus.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And Jesus, having heard, withdrew from there in a boat to a deserted place by himself. And the crowds, having heard, followed him on foot from the cities.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus, having gone out, saw a great crowd, and was moved with compassion on them, and healed their sick.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And when evening had come, his disciples came to him, saying, "The place is deserted, and the hour is already past; send away the crowds, that having gone into the villages they may buy food for themselves."[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But Jesus said to them, "They have no need to go away; you give them <span class="added-text">(something)</span> to eat."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And they say to him, "We have nothing here except five loaves and two fish."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And he said, "Bring them here to me."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And having commanded the crowds to recline on the grass, and having taken the five loaves and the two fish, having looked up to heaven, he blessed; and having broken <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, he gave the loaves to the disciples, and the disciples to the crowds.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And they all ate, and were satisfied; and they took up what remained of the fragments, twelve baskets full.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And those eating were about five thousand men, besides women and children.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And immediately Jesus made his disciples get into the boat, and go before him to the other side, until he should send away the crowds.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having sent away the crowds, he went up into the mountain by himself to pray. And when evening had come, he was there alone.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But the boat was already in the middle of the sea, being tossed by the waves; for the wind was contrary.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And in the fourth watch of the night Jesus went away to them, walking on the sea.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And the disciples, having seen him walking on the sea, were troubled, saying, "It is a ghost;" and they cried out from fear.

<span class="v-num">27</span> But immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying, "Take courage; it is I; do not be afraid."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And Peter answered him and said, "Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the waters."

<span class="v-num">29</span> And he said, "Come." And Peter, having gone down from the boat, walked on the waters, to come to Jesus.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But seeing the wind strong, he was afraid; and beginning to sink he cried out, saying, "Lord, save me."

<span class="v-num">31</span> And immediately Jesus, having stretched out <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand, took hold of him, and said to him, "<span class="added-text">(You)</span> of little faith, why did you doubt?"

<span class="v-num">32</span> And when they had gotten into the boat, the wind ceased.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And those in the boat came and worshiped him, saying, "Truly you are <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And having crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And the men of that place, having recognized him, sent into all that surrounding region, and brought to him all who were sick;

<span class="v-num">36</span> and they begged him that they might only touch the fringe of his garment; and as many as touched <span class="added-text">(it)</span> were made completely well.[^3]

[^1]: ὃ ἐὰν αἰτήσηται는 '무엇이든 요청하는 것'이라는 부정관계절로, ἐάν+가정법이 쓰여 아직 정해지지 않은 미래의 요청 내용을 포괄적으로 나타낸다. 헤롯의 맹세가 조건 없이 무엇이든 다 주겠다는 무모한 약속임을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπελθόντες...ἀγοράσωσιν)은 '무리를 보내는' 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다. 즉 '가서 사 먹게 하려고'라는 의도된 결과를 표현한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἅψωνται)은 '만지게 해달라'는 간청의 내용(목적)을 나타낸다. 아직 이루어지지 않은 소망을 가정법으로 표현하여 겸손하고 간절한 요청의 뉘앙스를 담는다.
