<span class="v-num">1</span> And the Pharisees and Sadducees, having come, testing <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, asked him to show them a sign from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heaven.

<span class="v-num">2</span> But he, answering, said to them, "When evening has come, you say, 'Fair weather, for the sky is red.'

<span class="v-num">3</span> And in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> morning, 'Today <span class="added-text">(there will be a)</span> storm, for the sky is red, being gloomy.' Hypocrites, you know how to discern the face of the sky, but the signs of the times you cannot <span class="added-text">(discern)</span>?

<span class="v-num">4</span> A wicked and adulterous generation seeks after a sign, and a sign will not be given to it, except the sign of Jonah the prophet." And leaving them, he went away.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And his disciples, having come to the other side, forgot to take bread<span class="added-text">(s)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But Jesus said to them, "See and take heed from the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees."

<span class="v-num">7</span> But they reasoned among themselves, saying, "<span class="added-text">(It is)</span> because we did not take bread<span class="added-text">(s)</span>."

<span class="v-num">8</span> But Jesus, knowing <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said to them, "Why do you reason among yourselves, <span class="added-text">(you of)</span> little faith, that you did not take bread<span class="added-text">(s)</span>?

<span class="v-num">9</span> Do you not yet perceive, nor remember the five loaves of the five thousand, and how many baskets you took up?

<span class="v-num">10</span> Nor the seven loaves of the four thousand, and how many baskets you took up?

<span class="v-num">11</span> How do you not perceive that I did not speak to you concerning bread, <span class="added-text">(but)</span> to take heed from the leaven of the Pharisees and Sadducees?"

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then they understood that he did not say to take heed from the leaven of bread, but from the teaching of the Pharisees and Sadducees.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And Jesus, having come into the parts of Caesarea of Philip, asked his disciples, saying, "Who do the men say <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I am, the Son of Man?"

<span class="v-num">14</span> And they said, "Some <span class="added-text">(say)</span> John the Baptist, and others Elijah, and others Jeremiah, or one of the prophets."

<span class="v-num">15</span> He said to them, "But you, who do you say <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I am?"

<span class="v-num">16</span> And Simon Peter, answering, said, "You are the Christ, the Son of the living God."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus, answering, said to him, "Blessed are you, Simon Bar-Jonah, because flesh and blood did not reveal <span class="added-text">(this)</span> to you, but my Father who is in the heavens.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And I also say to you, that you are Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gates of Hades will not prevail against it.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And I will give you the keys of the kingdom of the heavens, and whatever you shall bind on the earth shall <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=will have been)</span> bound in the heavens, and whatever you shall loose on the earth shall <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=will have been)</span> loosed in the heavens."[^1]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Then he charged his disciples that they should tell no one that he was Jesus the Christ.[^2]

<span class="v-num">21</span> From then Jesus began to show his disciples that he must go away to Jerusalem, and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised <span class="added-text">(on)</span> the third day.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And Peter, having taken him aside, began to rebuke him, saying, "<span class="added-text">(Be it)</span> far from you, Lord; this shall never be to you."

<span class="v-num">23</span> But he, turning, said to Peter, "Get behind me, Satan; you are a stumbling-block to me, because you are not minding the things of God, but the things of men."

<span class="v-num">24</span> Then Jesus said to his disciples, "If anyone wants to come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.

<span class="v-num">25</span> For whoever wants to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For what is a man profited, if he gains the whole world, but loses his own <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span>? Or what shall a man give <span class="added-text">(in)</span> exchange for his <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span>?[^3]

<span class="v-num">27</span> For the Son of Man is about to come in the glory of his Father with his angels, and then he will render to each according to his deeds.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Truly I say to you, there are some of those standing here who shall not taste of death until they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom."

[^1]: ὃ ἐάν+가정법 구문으로 '무엇이든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 조건절이다. 미래에 실현될 가능성을 열어두는 표현으로, 땅에서의 결정이 하늘의 결정과 상응함을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(εἴπωσιν)은 명령의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, '아무에게도 말하지 못하도록' 엄히 지시하신 의도를 강조한다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(κερδήσῃ, ζημιωθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 가정하는 조건절로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 세상을 다 얻는 것과 영혼을 잃는 것을 대조하여 영혼의 가치가 무한함을 강조하는 수사적 조건문이다.
