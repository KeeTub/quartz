<span class="v-num">1</span> And having called together his twelve disciples, <span class="added-text">(Jesus)</span> gave them authority <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=against)</span> unclean spirits, so as to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every sickness.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Now the names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Zebedee, and John his brother;

<span class="v-num">3</span> Philip, and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the tax collector; James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Alphaeus, and Lebbaeus who was called by the surname Thaddaeus;

<span class="v-num">4</span> Simon the Canaanite, and Judas Iscariot, who also <span class="alt-label">betrayed</span><span class="alt-text">(=delivered up)</span> him.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Jesus sent out these twelve, having commanded them, saying, "Do not go into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, and do not enter into a city of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Samaritans,

<span class="v-num">6</span> but go rather to the lost sheep of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house of Israel.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And as you go, preach, saying, 'The kingdom of the heavens has come near.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> Heal the sick, cleanse <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lepers, raise <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, cast out demons. Freely you received, freely give.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Do not <span class="alt-label">acquire</span><span class="alt-text">(=possess)</span> gold, nor silver, nor copper in your <span class="alt-label">belts</span><span class="alt-text">(=money belts)</span>,

<span class="v-num">10</span> nor a <span class="alt-label">bag</span><span class="alt-text">(=knapsack)</span> for the way, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the worker is worthy of his food.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And into whatever city or village you enter, find out who in it is worthy; and stay there until you depart.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And as you enter into the house, greet it.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And if the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you.[^1]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And whoever does not receive you, nor hear your words, as you go out of that house or that city, shake off the dust of your feet.[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Truly I say to you, it will be more tolerable for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Sodom and Gomorrah in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment, than for that city.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Behold, I send you out as sheep in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of wolves; be therefore wise as serpents, and <span class="alt-label">innocent</span><span class="alt-text">(=harmless)</span> as doves.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But beware of men, for they will deliver you up to councils, and in their synagogues they will scourge you;

<span class="v-num">18</span> and you will be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them and to the Gentiles.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But when they deliver you up, do not be anxious how or what you should speak; for it will be given to you in that hour what you should speak.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father speaking in you.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And brother will deliver up brother to death, and father <span class="added-text">(his)</span> child; and children will rise up against parents, and cause them to be put to death.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And you will be hated by all for my name's sake; but the one who endures to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> end, this one will be saved.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But when they persecute you in this city, flee into another. For truly I say to you, you will not finish <span class="added-text">(going through)</span> the cities of Israel, until the Son of Man comes.

<span class="v-num">24</span> A disciple is not above <span class="added-text">(his)</span> teacher, nor a servant above his lord.

<span class="v-num">25</span> It is enough for the disciple that he become as his teacher, and the servant as his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebul, how much more those of his household?[^3]

<span class="v-num">26</span> Therefore do not fear them; for there is nothing covered that will not be revealed, and hidden that will not be known.

<span class="v-num">27</span> What I tell you in the darkness, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, proclaim on the housetops.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And do not fear those who kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul; but rather fear him who is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.[^4]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Are not two sparrows sold for an assarion? And not one of them will fall to the ground without your Father<span class="added-text">('s will)</span>.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But even the hairs of your head are all numbered.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Therefore do not fear; you are of more value than many sparrows.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Therefore whoever will confess me before men, I also will confess him before my Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.

<span class="v-num">33</span> But whoever will deny me before men, I also will deny him before my Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.

<span class="v-num">34</span> Do not think that I came to <span class="alt-label">bring</span><span class="alt-text">(=cast)</span> peace on the earth; I did not come to bring peace, but a sword.

<span class="v-num">35</span> For I came to set a man <span class="alt-label">against</span><span class="alt-text">(=to divide against)</span> his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law;

<span class="v-num">36</span> And a man's enemies <span class="added-text">(will be)</span> those of his own household.

<span class="v-num">37</span> The one who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me; and the one who loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me;

<span class="v-num">38</span> And whoever does not take his cross and follow after me is not worthy of me.

<span class="v-num">39</span> The one who finds his life will lose it; and the one who loses his life for my sake will find it.[^5]

<span class="v-num">40</span> The one who receives you receives me; and the one who receives me receives him who sent me.

<span class="v-num">41</span> The one who receives a prophet in the name of a prophet will receive a prophet's reward; and the one who receives a righteous man in the name of a righteous man will receive a righteous man's reward.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And whoever gives one of these little ones only a cup of cold <span class="added-text">(water)</span> to drink in the name of a disciple, truly I say to you, he will by no means lose his reward."[^6]

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 결과가 정해지지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 ~라면'의 뜻이다. 집의 합당 여부에 따라 평화의 임함과 돌아옴이 결정되는 실제적 조건을 표현한다.
[^2]: ἐὰν+가정법(μὴ δέξηται, ἀκούσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건절로, '누구든지 영접하지 않는 경우에는'이라는 열린 가정을 표현한다. 각 마을·집마다 실제로 벌어질 수 있는 상황을 전제로 제자들에게 행동 지침을 주는 구조다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)은 목적절이지만 여기서는 결과/내용절처럼 '~하는 것으로 족하다'는 의미를 나타낸다. 제자가 선생과 같이 되는 것이 마땅한 목표임을 강조한다.
[^4]: γέεννα(게헨나)는 힌놈의 골짜기에서 유래한 최후 심판의 장소를 가리키는 표현으로, 육체의 죽음을 넘어서는 궁극적 두려움의 대상을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ψυχή는 '목숨'과 '생명 전체'를 함께 뜻하며, 자기 생명을 지키려는 태도와 그리스도를 위해 내어주는 태도가 대조된다.
[^6]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법(ποτίσῃ)은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다. 작은 선행 하나라도 반드시 보상받는다는 확실성을 강조하는 구문이다.
