# Matthew 25

<span class="v-num">1</span> Then the kingdom of the <span class="alt-label">heavens</span><span class="alt-text">(=heaven)</span> will be likened to ten virgins, who having taken their lamps went out to meet the bridegroom.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And five of them were wise, and the five <span class="added-text">(were)</span> foolish.

<span class="v-num">3</span> The foolish ones, having taken their lamps, did not take oil with themselves,

<span class="v-num">4</span> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the bridegroom delaying, they all grew drowsy and slept.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And at midnight there was a cry, 'Behold, the bridegroom comes; go out to meet him.'

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then all those virgins rose and trimmed their lamps.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the foolish said to the wise, 'Give us of your oil, for our lamps are going out.'

<span class="v-num">9</span> But the wise answered, saying, 'Lest there not be enough for us and you; but go rather to the ones selling, and buy for yourselves.'

<span class="v-num">10</span> And while they were going away to buy, the bridegroom came; and the ones ready went in with him to the wedding feast, and the door was shut.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And afterward the rest of the virgins also came, saying, 'Lord, Lord, open to us.'

<span class="v-num">12</span> But he answering said, 'Truly I say to you, I do not know you.'

<span class="v-num">13</span> Watch therefore, for you do not know the day nor the hour in which the Son of Man comes.[^1]

<span class="v-num">14</span> For <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> as a man going abroad <span class="added-text">(who)</span> called his own servants, and delivered to them his possessions;

<span class="v-num">15</span> and to one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one, to each according to his own ability; and immediately he went abroad.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he who had received the five talents, having gone, worked with them, and made another five talents.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Likewise also he who <span class="added-text">(had received)</span> the two gained another two also.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But he who had received the one, having gone away, dug in the earth, and hid his lord's money.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And after a long time the lord of those servants came, and settled accounts with them.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he who had received the five talents came and brought another five talents, saying, 'Lord, you delivered to me five talents; behold, I have gained another five talents besides them.'

<span class="v-num">21</span> His lord said to him, 'Well <span class="added-text">(done)</span>, good and faithful servant; you were faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things; enter into the joy of your lord.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And he who had received the two talents also came and said, 'Lord, you delivered to me two talents; behold, I have gained another two talents besides them.'

<span class="v-num">23</span> His lord said to him, 'Well <span class="added-text">(done)</span>, good and faithful servant; you were faithful over a few things, I will set you over many things; enter into the joy of your lord.'

<span class="v-num">24</span> And he who had received the one talent also came and said, "Lord, I knew you, that you are a hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering where you did not scatter;

<span class="v-num">25</span> and being afraid, I went away and hid your talent in the earth; behold, you have what is yours."

<span class="v-num">26</span> But his lord answered and said to him, "Evil and slothful servant, you knew that I reap where I did not sow, and gather where I did not scatter;

<span class="v-num">27</span> you ought therefore to have put my money with the bankers, and <span class="added-text">(at)</span> coming I would have received back my own with interest.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Take therefore the talent from him, and give <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the one having the ten talents.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For to everyone who has, it will be given, and he will have abundance; but from him who does not have, even what he has will be taken from him.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And cast out the useless servant into the outer darkness; there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth."

<span class="v-num">31</span> And when the Son of Man comes in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then he will sit on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> throne of his glory,[^3]

<span class="v-num">32</span> and all the nations will be gathered before him, and he will separate them from one another, as the shepherd separates the sheep from the goats,

<span class="v-num">33</span> and he will set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.

<span class="v-num">34</span> Then the King will say to those on his right hand, "Come, you blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world.[^4]

<span class="v-num">35</span> For I was hungry, and you gave me to eat; I was thirsty, and you gave me drink; I was a stranger, and you took me in;

<span class="v-num">36</span> naked, and you clothed me; I was sick, and you visited me; I was in prison, and you came to me."

<span class="v-num">37</span> Then the righteous will answer him, saying, "Lord, when did we see you hungry, and fed <span class="added-text">(you)</span>? or thirsty, and gave <span class="added-text">(you)</span> drink?

<span class="v-num">38</span> And when did we see you a stranger, and took <span class="added-text">(you)</span> in? or naked, and clothed <span class="added-text">(you)</span>?

<span class="v-num">39</span> And when did we see you sick, or in prison, and came to you?"

<span class="v-num">40</span> And the King answering will say to them, "Truly I say to you, inasmuch as you did <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to one of the least of these my brothers, you did <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to me."

<span class="v-num">41</span> Then he will say also to those on the left, "Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire, prepared for the devil and his angels.[^5]

<span class="v-num">42</span> For I was hungry, and you did not give me to eat; I was thirsty, and you did not give me drink;

<span class="v-num">43</span> I was a stranger, and you did not take me in; naked, and you did not clothe me; sick, and in prison, and you did not visit me."

<span class="v-num">44</span> Then they also will answer him, saying, "Lord, when did we see you hungry, or thirsty, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister to you?"

<span class="v-num">45</span> Then he will answer them, saying, "Truly I say to you, inasmuch as you did not do <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to one of the least of these, neither did you do <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to me."

<span class="v-num">46</span> And these will go away into eternal punishment; but the righteous into eternal life.[^6]

[^1]: γρηγορεῖτε는 현재 명령형으로 일회적 각성이 아니라 지속적으로 깨어 있는 상태를 요구한다.
[^2]: πιστός(충성된)는 액수의 크기가 아니라 맡겨진 것에 대한 신실함 자체를 평가 기준으로 삼는다.
[^3]: '자기 영광의 보좌'라는 표현은 24장의 재림 예언과 연결되어 인자의 신적 심판권을 나타낸다.
[^4]: κληρονομήσατε(상속받으라)는 노력의 대가가 아니라 창세 전부터 예비된 것을 유업으로 받는 은혜의 성격을 강조한다.
[^5]: 이 영원한 불은 본래 사람이 아니라 마귀와 그 사자들을 위해 예비된 것임을 명시한다.
[^6]: 같은 αἰώνιος(영원한)가 형벌과 생명 모두에 동일하게 쓰여, 형벌의 지속성이 생명의 지속성과 같음을 나타낸다.
