<span class="v-num">1</span> Having seen the crowds, he went up to the mountain; and when he had sat down, his disciples came to him.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having opened his mouth, he taught them, saying,

<span class="v-num">3</span> "Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the poor <span class="added-text">(in)</span> spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens,[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the ones mourning, for they will be comforted,

<span class="v-num">5</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the meek, for they will inherit the earth,

<span class="v-num">6</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the ones hungering and thirsting <span class="added-text">(for)</span> righteousness, for they will be filled,

<span class="v-num">7</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the merciful, for they will receive mercy,

<span class="v-num">8</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the pure in heart, for they will see God,

<span class="v-num">9</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the peacemakers, for they will be called sons of God,

<span class="v-num">10</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the ones having been persecuted for the sake of righteousness, for theirs is the kingdom of the heavens.

<span class="v-num">11</span> You are blessed whenever they revile you and persecute <span class="added-text">(you)</span>, and say every evil word against you, lying, for my sake.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Rejoice and be exceedingly glad, for your reward <span class="added-text">(is)</span> great in the heavens; for so they persecuted the prophets who <span class="added-text">(were)</span> before you.

<span class="v-num">13</span> You are the salt of the earth; but if the salt loses its <span class="alt-label">savor</span><span class="alt-text">(=becomes tasteless)</span>, with what will it be salted? It is no longer good for anything, except to be thrown out and to be trampled under <span class="added-text">(foot)</span> by men.[^2]

<span class="v-num">14</span> You are the light of the world; a city set on a <span class="alt-label">hill</span><span class="alt-text">(=mountain)</span> cannot be hidden.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Nor do they light a lamp and put it under a <span class="alt-label">bushel</span><span class="alt-text">(=measuring basket)</span>, but on the lampstand, and it shines on all who are in the house.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Let your light so shine before men, so that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father who is in the heavens.[^3]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Do not think that I came to <span class="alt-label">destroy</span><span class="alt-text">(=abolish)</span> the law or the prophets; I did not come to <span class="alt-label">destroy</span><span class="alt-text">(=abolish)</span>, but to fulfill.[^4]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For truly I say to you, until heaven and earth pass away, one iota or one <span class="alt-label">stroke</span><span class="alt-text">(=point)</span> will by no means pass from the law, until all things come to <span class="alt-label">pass</span><span class="alt-text">(=be fulfilled)</span>.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Whoever therefore breaks one of these least commandments, and teaches men so, will be called least in the kingdom of the heavens; but whoever does and teaches <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, this one will be called great in the kingdom of the heavens.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For I say to you, that unless your righteousness exceeds <span class="added-text">(that)</span> of the scribes and Pharisees, you will by no means enter into the kingdom of the heavens.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> You have heard that it was said to the <span class="alt-label">ancients</span><span class="alt-text">(=those of old)</span>, 'You shall not murder; and whoever murders will be liable to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judgment.'

<span class="v-num">22</span> But I say to you, that everyone who is angry with his brother without a cause will be liable to the judgment; and whoever says to his brother, 'Raca,' will be liable to the <span class="alt-label">council</span><span class="alt-text">(=Sanhedrin)</span>; and whoever says, 'You fool,' will be liable to the <span class="alt-label">hell</span><span class="alt-text">(=Gehenna)</span> of fire.[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> Therefore if you bring your gift to the altar, and there you remember that your brother has something against you,[^8]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Leave your gift there before the altar, and go; first be reconciled to your brother, and then come <span class="added-text">(and)</span> offer your gift.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Be <span class="alt-label">well-disposed</span><span class="alt-text">(=agree)</span> with your <span class="alt-label">accuser</span><span class="alt-text">(=adversary)</span> quickly, while you are on the way with him, lest the <span class="alt-label">accuser</span><span class="alt-text">(=adversary)</span> deliver you to the judge, and the judge deliver you to the officer, and you be thrown into prison.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Truly I say to you, you will by no means come out from there, until you have paid the last <span class="alt-label">quadrans</span><span class="alt-text">(=penny)</span>.

<span class="v-num">27</span> You have heard that it was said to the <span class="alt-label">ancients</span><span class="alt-text">(=those of old)</span>, 'You shall not commit adultery;'

<span class="v-num">28</span> But I say to you, that everyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

<span class="v-num">29</span> But if your right eye causes you to stumble, pluck it out and throw <span class="added-text">(it)</span> from you; for it is <span class="alt-label">better</span><span class="alt-text">(=profitable)</span> for you that one of your members perish, and not <span class="added-text">(that)</span> your whole body be thrown into hell.[^9]

<span class="v-num">30</span> And if your right hand causes you to stumble, cut it off and throw <span class="added-text">(it)</span> from you; for it is <span class="alt-label">better</span><span class="alt-text">(=profitable)</span> for you that one of your members perish, and not <span class="added-text">(that)</span> your whole body be thrown into hell.[^10]

<span class="v-num">31</span> And it was said, 'Whoever divorces his wife, let him give her a certificate of divorce;'

<span class="v-num">32</span> But I say to you, that whoever divorces his wife, except for the <span class="alt-label">reason</span><span class="alt-text">(=cause)</span> of fornication, makes her commit adultery; and whoever marries a divorced woman commits adultery.[^11]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Again you have heard that it was said to the <span class="alt-label">ancients</span><span class="alt-text">(=those of old)</span>, 'You shall not swear falsely, but shall perform to the Lord your oaths;'

<span class="v-num">34</span> But I say to you, do not swear at all; neither by heaven, for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> throne of God;

<span class="v-num">35</span> nor by the earth, for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> footstool of his feet; nor by Jerusalem, for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city of the great King;

<span class="v-num">36</span> Nor shall you swear by your head, because you cannot make one hair white or black.

<span class="v-num">37</span> But let your <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=speech)</span> be, 'Yes, yes; no, no;' for whatever is more than these is from the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>.

<span class="v-num">38</span> You have heard that it was said, 'Eye for eye, and tooth for tooth;'

<span class="v-num">39</span> But I say to you, do not resist the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>; but whoever slaps you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also;

<span class="v-num">40</span> And to the one wanting to sue you and take your tunic, let him have your cloak also;

<span class="v-num">41</span> And whoever compels you <span class="added-text">(to go)</span> one mile, go with him two.

<span class="v-num">42</span> Give to the one asking you, and do not turn away from the one wanting to borrow from you.

<span class="v-num">43</span> You have heard that it was said, 'You shall love your neighbor, and hate your enemy;'

<span class="v-num">44</span> But I say to you, love your enemies, bless the ones cursing you, do good to the ones hating you, and pray for the ones mistreating you and persecuting you;

<span class="v-num">45</span> so that you may become sons of your Father who is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens; for he makes his sun rise on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> evil and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> good, and sends rain on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteous and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> unrighteous.[^12]

<span class="v-num">46</span> For if you love the ones loving you, what reward do you have? Do not even the tax collectors do the same?[^13]

<span class="v-num">47</span> And if you greet only your brothers, what more do you do? Do not even the tax collectors do so?[^14]

<span class="v-num">48</span> Therefore you shall be perfect, as your Father who is in the heavens is perfect."

[^1]: '하늘들의 나라'(하늘나라)는 마태복음 특유의 표현으로 '하나님의 나라'와 같되 유대적 어법상 하나님 이름을 직접 부르길 꺼려 대체한 표현이다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(μωρανθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 소금이 그 맛을 잃는다면'이라는 가정을 표현한다. 실제로 일어날 수도 있는 상황을 경고하는 뉘앙스이다.
[^3]: ὅπως+가정법(ἴδωσιν, δοξάσωσι)은 목적절로, 선행을 보이는 목적이 하나님께 영광 돌리는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: πληρῶσαι(완전하게 하다/성취하다)는 단순 폐지의 반대가 아니라 율법의 참뜻을 온전히 이루신다는 그리스도의 사역을 가리킨다.
[^5]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 대상을 가리키는 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 일반적 가능성을 나타낸다. 계명을 대하는 태도에 따라 하늘나라에서의 지위가 결정됨을 일반화하여 보편적 원리로 제시한다.
[^6]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법(περισσεύσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 조건을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 ~하지 않으면'이라는 실현 가능성이 열린 부정 조건을 제시한다. 의가 능가하지 않으면 천국에 들어갈 수 없다는 강한 필요조건을 강조한다.
[^7]: γέεννα(지옥)는 예루살렘 남쪽 힌놈 골짜기 이름에서 유래하여 종말의 형벌 장소를 가리키는 신학적 용어로 굳어졌다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(προσφέρῃς, μνησθῇς)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 상황을 가정하는 조건절이다. '혹시라도 예물을 드리다가 생각나거든'이라는 뉘앙스로, 실제로 얼마든지 일어날 수 있는 상황을 전제한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπόληται)은 결과적 목적절로, 몸 전체가 망하는 것보다 지체 하나를 잃는 편이 유익하다는 뜻을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπόληται)이 συμφέρει의 내용절로 쓰여 '~하는 것이 유익하다'는 의미상 목적/결과를 나타낸다. 지체 하나를 잃는 것이 온 몸이 지옥에 던져지는 것보다 낫다는 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^11]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법(γαμήσῃ)은 '누구든지 ~한다면'이라는 불특정 대상의 미래 가능 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않았으나 실제로 일어날 수 있는 일반적 상황을 가정한다. 이혼당한 여자와 결혼하는 모든 경우에 예외 없이 적용되는 원칙을 강조한다.
[^12]: ὅπως+가정법(γένησθε)은 목적절로, 원수 사랑과 기도가 하나님 아버지의 성품을 닮는 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^13]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀγαπήσητε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절(3형 조건문)로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 가정적 상황을 설정한다. 세리도 하는 수준의 사랑으로는 특별한 상(보상)이 없음을 논증하는 전제로 쓰였다.
[^14]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀσπάσησθε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 ~한다면'의 의미다. 형제만 사랑하는 것이 특별할 것 없는 세상 수준의 사랑임을 지적하는 가정적 예시로 쓰였다.
