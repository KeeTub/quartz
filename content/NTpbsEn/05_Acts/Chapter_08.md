<span class="v-num">1</span> Now Saul was consenting to <span class="alt-label">his</span><span class="alt-text">(=Stephen's)</span> <span class="alt-label">killing</span><span class="alt-text">(=death)</span>. And on that day a great persecution arose against the church in Jerusalem, and all were scattered throughout the regions of Judea and Samaria, except the apostles.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And devout men buried Stephen, and made great lamentation over him.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But Saul was ravaging the church, entering into houses, and dragging off men and women, delivered <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to prison.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Therefore those who were scattered went about, proclaiming the word <span class="added-text">(as good news)</span>.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Philip, having gone down to a city of Samaria, proclaimed to them the Christ.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the crowds gave attention with one accord to the things spoken by Philip, as they heard and saw the signs which he did.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For <span class="added-text">(from)</span> many of those having unclean spirits, <span class="added-text">(the spirits)</span> came out, crying with a loud voice; and many who were paralyzed and lame were healed.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And there was great joy in that city.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But a certain man named Simon previously existed in the city, practicing magic and amazing the nation of Samaria, saying himself to be someone great,

<span class="v-num">10</span> to whom all gave attention, from small to great, saying, "This one is the power of God, the great <span class="added-text">(one)</span>."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And they gave attention to him, because for a considerable time he had amazed them with <span class="added-text">(his)</span> magic <span class="added-text">(arts)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But when they believed Philip, proclaiming as good news the things concerning the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And Simon himself also believed, and having been baptized, he was continuing with Philip; and beholding signs and great <span class="alt-label">miracles</span><span class="alt-text">(=powers)</span> happening, he was amazed.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Now the apostles in Jerusalem, having heard that Samaria had received the word of God, sent to them Peter and John,

<span class="v-num">15</span> who, having gone down, prayed concerning them, that they might receive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For not yet had <span class="added-text">(it)</span> fallen upon any of them, but only they existed having been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Then they laid <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hands upon them, and they received <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Simon, having seen that through the laying on of the apostles' hands the Holy Spirit was given, brought to them money,

<span class="v-num">19</span> saying, "Give to me also this authority, that on whomever I lay hands, he may receive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit."[^1]

<span class="v-num">20</span> "But Peter said to him, 'Your silver <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you unto destruction, because you thought to obtain the gift of God through money.'

<span class="v-num">21</span> There is no part nor lot for you in this matter, for your heart is not right before God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Repent therefore from this wickedness of yours, and beseech God, if perhaps the thought of your heart will be forgiven you.

<span class="v-num">23</span> For I see you to be in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gall of bitterness and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> bond of unrighteousness."

<span class="v-num">24</span> But Simon, answering, said, "You <span class="added-text">(yourselves)</span> beseech the Lord for me, that nothing of what you have said may come upon me."

<span class="v-num">25</span> So then, having testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, and proclaimed the good news to many villages of the Samaritans.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord spoke to Philip, saying, "Arise and go toward <span class="added-text">(the)</span> south, on the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza" - this is <span class="added-text">(a)</span> desert <span class="added-text">(road)</span>.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And having risen up, he went; and behold, an Ethiopian man, a eunuch, a court <span class="alt-label">official</span><span class="alt-text">(=potentate)</span> of Candace the queen of the Ethiopians, who was over all her treasure, who had come to Jerusalem to worship,

<span class="v-num">28</span> was returning, and sitting on his chariot, and was reading the prophet Isaiah.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And the Spirit said to Philip, "Go near and join yourself to this chariot."

<span class="v-num">30</span> And Philip, having run up, heard him reading the prophet Isaiah, and said, "Do you indeed understand what you are reading?"

<span class="v-num">31</span> And he said, "For how could I be able <span class="added-text">(to understand)</span>, unless someone should guide me?" And he besought Philip, having come up, to sit with him.[^2]

<span class="v-num">32</span> Now the passage of the Scripture which he was reading was this: "He was led as a sheep to slaughter, and as a lamb before the one shearing it <span class="added-text">(is)</span> <span class="alt-label">silent</span><span class="alt-text">(=voiceless)</span>, so he does not open his mouth.

<span class="v-num">33</span> In his humiliation his judgment was taken away; and who will declare his generation? For his life is taken from the earth."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And the eunuch, answering Philip, said, "I beseech you, concerning whom does the prophet say this? Concerning himself, or concerning some other one?"

<span class="v-num">35</span> And Philip, having opened his mouth, and beginning from this Scripture, proclaimed to him Jesus <span class="added-text">(as good news)</span>.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And as they were going along the road, they came upon some water; and the eunuch said, "Behold, water; what prevents me from being baptized?"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And Philip said, "If you believe with <span class="added-text">(your)</span> whole heart, it is permitted." And he, answering, said, "I believe Jesus Christ to be the Son of God."[^3]

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he commanded the chariot to stand still; and they went down both into the water, both Philip and the eunuch; and he baptized him.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And when they came up out of the water, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord snatched away Philip; and the eunuch saw him no more, for he went his way rejoicing.

<span class="v-num">40</span> But Philip was found at Azotus; and passing through, he was proclaiming the good news to all the cities, until he came to Caesarea.

[^2]: ἐὰν μή τις ὁδηγήσῃ (가정법)는 '누군가 인도해 주지 않는다면'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 조건을 나타내며, 안내자가 없이는 이해가 불가능함을 강조한다.
