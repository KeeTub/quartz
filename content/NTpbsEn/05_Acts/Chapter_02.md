<span class="v-num">1</span> And when the day of Pentecost was being <span class="alt-label">fulfilled</span><span class="alt-text">(=had come)</span>, they were all together with one accord in one place.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And suddenly there came from heaven a sound like a rushing mighty wind <span class="added-text">(being borne along)</span>, and it filled the whole house where they were sitting.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And there appeared to them tongues <span class="added-text">(as)</span> divided, like fire, and it sat upon each one of them,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and they were all filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them to utter.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Now there were dwelling in Jerusalem Jews, devout men, from every nation under heaven.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And when this sound occurred, the multitude came together and were confounded, because each one heard them speaking in his own dialect.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And they were all amazed and marveled, saying to one another, "Behold, are not all these who speak Galileans?

<span class="v-num">8</span> And how do we hear, each in our own dialect in which we were born?

<span class="v-num">9</span> Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and those dwelling in Mesopotamia, Judea and Cappadocia, Pontus and Asia,

<span class="v-num">10</span> Phrygia and Pamphylia, Egypt and the parts of Libya around Cyrene, and the visiting Romans, both Jews and proselytes,

<span class="v-num">11</span> Cretans and Arabians—we hear them speaking in our <span class="added-text">(own)</span> tongues the great things of God."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And they were all amazed and perplexed, saying to one another, "What could this mean?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> But others, mocking, said, "They are full of new wine."

<span class="v-num">14</span> But Peter, standing with the eleven, lifted up his voice and declared to them, "Men of Judea, and all you dwelling in Jerusalem, let this be known to you, and give ear to my words.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For these are not drunk, as you suppose, for it is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> third hour of the day.

<span class="v-num">16</span> But this is that which was spoken through the prophet Joel,

<span class="v-num">17</span> 'And it shall be in the last days, says God, I will pour out from my Spirit upon all flesh, and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams;[^1]

<span class="v-num">18</span> and indeed upon my servants and upon my handmaidens in those days I will pour out from my Spirit, and they shall prophesy;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and I will show wonders in heaven above, and signs on earth beneath, blood, and fire, and vapor of smoke;

<span class="v-num">20</span> the sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before the great and glorious day of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord comes;

<span class="v-num">21</span> and it shall be, whoever calls upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">22</span> Men of Israel, hear these words: Jesus the Nazarene, a man attested to you from God by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did through him in your midst, as you yourselves also know,

<span class="v-num">23</span> him, being delivered up by the determined counsel and foreknowledge of God, you took, and by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hands of lawless men, having nailed him <span class="added-text">(to the cross)</span>, killed;

<span class="v-num">24</span> whom God raised up, having loosed the pains of death, because it was not possible for him to be held by it.

<span class="v-num">25</span> For David says concerning him, 'I foresaw the Lord always before me, because he is at my right hand, that I should not be shaken;[^3]

<span class="v-num">26</span> therefore my heart was glad, and my tongue rejoiced; moreover my flesh also shall dwell in hope;

<span class="v-num">27</span> because you will not leave my soul in Hades, nor allow your Holy One to see corruption.

<span class="v-num">28</span> You have made known to me the ways of life; you will fill me with joy with your <span class="alt-label">presence</span><span class="alt-text">(=face)</span>.'

<span class="v-num">29</span> Men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, it is permitted to speak with confidence to you concerning the patriarch David, that he both died and was buried, and his tomb is with us until this day.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Being therefore a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn to him with an oath, that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up the Christ to sit on his throne,

<span class="v-num">31</span> he, foreseeing this, spoke concerning the resurrection of the Christ, that his soul was not left in Hades, nor did his flesh see corruption.

<span class="v-num">32</span> This Jesus God raised up, of which we all are witnesses.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Therefore, having been exalted to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of God, and having received the promise of the Holy Spirit from the Father, he poured out this which you now see and hear.[^4]

<span class="v-num">34</span> For David did not ascend into the heavens, but he himself says, 'The Lord said to my Lord, "Sit at my right hand,[^5]

<span class="v-num">35</span> until I make your enemies <span class="added-text">(your)</span> footstool."'

<span class="v-num">36</span> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God has made him both Lord and Christ, this Jesus whom you crucified."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And when they heard this, they were pierced (=cut) to the heart, and said to Peter and the rest of the apostles, "Men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, what shall we do?"

<span class="v-num">38</span> Peter said to them, "Repent, and let each of you be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> forgiveness of sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.[^6]

<span class="v-num">39</span> For the promise is to you and to your children, and to all who are far off, as many as the Lord our God shall call."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And with many other words he testified and exhorted, saying, "Be saved from this crooked generation."

<span class="v-num">41</span> So then, those who gladly received his word were baptized; and there were added that day about three thousand souls.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And they were continuing steadfastly in the apostles' teaching and <span class="added-text">(in)</span> fellowship, and in the breaking of bread, and in prayers.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And fear came upon every soul, and many wonders and signs were done through the apostles.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And all who believed were together, and had all things in common,

<span class="v-num">45</span> and they sold <span class="added-text">(their)</span> possessions and property, and divided them to all, according as anyone had need;

<span class="v-num">46</span> And continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, they partook of food with gladness and singleness of heart,

<span class="v-num">47</span> praising God, and having favor with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily those who were being saved.

---

[^1]: 욜 2:28-32 인용(70인역과 유사)으로 '마지막 날들'은 성령 부어주심으로 시작된 메시아 시대를 가리킴.
[^2]: 욜 2:32 인용의 결론부로, 구원의 보편적 조건인 '주의 이름을 부름'을 제시함.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(σαλευθῶ)은 목적절로, 주께서 내 오른편에 계신 목적이자 결과가 '내가 흔들리지 않는 것'임을 나타낸다. 즉 하나님의 임재가 성도의 견고함의 근거임을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^4]: 부정과거 분사 ὑψωθείς는 주동사 ἐξέχεε보다 시간상 선행하는 완료된 사건, 곧 예수의 승귀를 가리킴.
[^5]: 시 110:1(LXX 109편) 인용으로, 예수의 승천과 높아지심을 증명하는 근거로 사용됨.
[^6]: εἰς ἄφεσιν('~을 위하여')의 뉘앙스가 논쟁적이나, 문맥상 회개와 세례가 죄 사함에 이르는 목적을 가리키는 것으로 봄이 자연스러움.
