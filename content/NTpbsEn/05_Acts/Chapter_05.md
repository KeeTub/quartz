<span class="v-num">1</span> But a certain man named Ananias, with Sapphira his wife, sold a <span class="alt-label">possession</span><span class="alt-text">(=piece of land)</span>,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> and kept <span class="alt-label">back</span><span class="alt-text">(=embezzled)</span> part of the price, his wife also being aware <span class="added-text">(of it)</span>, and having brought a certain part, laid <span class="added-text">(it)</span> at the feet of the apostles.

<span class="v-num">3</span> But Peter said, "Ananias, why has Satan filled your heart, for you to lie <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=deceive)</span> the Holy Spirit, and to keep back part of the price of the land?

<span class="v-num">4</span> While it remained, did it not remain your own? And after it was sold, was it not in your <span class="added-text">(own)</span> authority? Why <span class="added-text">(is it)</span> that you have put this thing in your heart? You have not lied to men, but to God."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Ananias, hearing these words, fell down and breathed his last; and great fear came upon all those hearing these things.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the younger men rose up and wrapped him up, and carrying him out, buried <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now it happened, about an interval of three hours, that his wife, not knowing what had happened, came in.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Peter answered her, "Tell me, did you sell the land for so <span class="alt-label">much</span><span class="alt-text">(=this price)</span>?" And she said, "Yes, for so much."

<span class="v-num">9</span> Then Peter said to her, "How is it that you have agreed together to test the Spirit of the Lord? Behold, the feet of those who buried your husband <span class="added-text">(are)</span> at the door, and they will carry you out."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And immediately she fell down at his feet, and breathed her last; and the young men, coming in, found her dead, and carrying <span class="added-text">(her)</span> out, buried <span class="added-text">(her)</span> beside her husband.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And great fear came upon all the church, and upon all those hearing these things.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And through the hands of the apostles many signs and wonders were done among the people; and they were all with one accord in Solomon's porch.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But of the rest, no one dared to <span class="alt-label">join</span><span class="alt-text">(=cling)</span> to them; but the people magnified them.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And more <span class="added-text">(were)</span> added believing on the Lord, multitudes both of men and women;

<span class="v-num">15</span> so that they even carried out the sick into the streets, and laid <span class="added-text">(them)</span> on beds and mats, so that <span class="added-text">(as)</span> Peter came, at least <span class="added-text">(his)</span> shadow might overshadow some of them.[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the multitude also of the surrounding cities came together to Jerusalem, bringing sick <span class="added-text">(people)</span>, and those who were troubled by unclean spirits, who were all healed.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Then the high priest rose up, and all those with him <span class="added-text">(who were of)</span> the sect of the Sadducees, and were filled with jealousy,

<span class="v-num">18</span> and laid their hands on the apostles, and put them in public custody.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But <span class="alt-label">an</span><span class="alt-text">(=the)</span> angel of the Lord through the night opened the doors of the prison, and having led them out, said,

<span class="v-num">20</span> "Go, and standing in the temple, speak to the people all the words of this life."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And having heard, they entered into the temple about dawn, and taught. But the high priest came, and those with him, and called together the council and all the <span class="alt-label">elders</span><span class="alt-text">(=senate)</span> of the sons of Israel, and sent to the prison to have them brought.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But the officers who came did not find them in the prison; and returning, they reported,

<span class="v-num">23</span> saying, "We found the prison shut with all safety, and the guards standing outside before the doors; but having opened <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, we found no one inside."

<span class="v-num">24</span> Now when the priest and the captain of the temple and the chief priests heard these words, they were perplexed concerning them, <span class="added-text">(as to)</span> what this might become.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But a certain one came and reported to them, saying, "Behold, the men whom you put in prison are standing in the temple and teaching the people."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Then the captain went with the officers, and brought them, not with violence, for they feared the people, lest they should be stoned.[^3]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And having brought them, they set <span class="added-text">(them)</span> in the council; and the high priest asked them,

<span class="v-num">28</span> saying, "Did we not strictly command you not to teach in this name? And behold, you have filled Jerusalem with your teaching, and you intend to bring upon us the blood of this man."

<span class="v-num">29</span> But Peter and the apostles answered and said, "It is necessary to obey God rather than men.

<span class="v-num">30</span> The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom you killed, having hanged <span class="added-text">(him)</span> on a tree;

<span class="v-num">31</span> this one God has exalted with his right hand <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> a <span class="alt-label">Prince</span><span class="alt-text">(=Ruler)</span> and Savior, to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And we are his witnesses of these <span class="alt-label">words</span><span class="alt-text">(=things)</span>, and so also <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Holy Spirit, whom God has given to those obeying him."

<span class="v-num">33</span> But they, hearing <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, were cut to the <span class="alt-label">heart</span><span class="alt-text">(=enraged)</span>, and plotted to kill them.

<span class="v-num">34</span> Then a certain Pharisee in the council rose up, named Gamaliel, a teacher of the law, honored by all the people, and commanded to put the apostles outside for a little while,

<span class="v-num">35</span> and said to them, "Men of Israel, take heed to yourselves concerning these men, what you intend to do.

<span class="v-num">36</span> For before these days rose up Theudas, saying himself to be someone, to whom a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves; who was slain, and all, as many as obeyed him, were dispersed, and came to nothing.

<span class="v-num">37</span> After this man rose up Judas of Galilee, in the days of the census, and drew away a considerable people after him; he also perished, and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And now I say to you, withdraw from these men, and let them alone; because if this counsel or this work is of men, it will be overthrown;[^4]

<span class="v-num">39</span> but if it is of God, you cannot overthrow it; lest you even be found <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> fighting against God."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And they agreed with him; and having called the apostles, and beaten <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, they commanded <span class="added-text">(them)</span> not to speak in the name of Jesus, and let them go.

<span class="v-num">41</span> So then they departed from the presence of the council, rejoicing that they were counted worthy to suffer dishonor for his name.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And every day, in the temple and from house to house, they did not cease teaching and preaching the gospel <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Jesus Christ.

[^1]: κτῆμα(소유물)는 6:1의 χωρίον과 연결되어 부동산을 뜻함
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπισκιάσῃ)으로 목적절을 이끌며, 병자들을 거리에 데려다 놓은 '목적'이 베드로의 그림자라도 덮이게 하려는 데 있음을 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않은 기대되는 결과를 향한 의도를 표현한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μὴ λιθασθῶσιν)은 목적절로, 백성을 두려워한 이유(돌에 맞지 않으려는 목적)를 나타낸다. 강제로 끌고 가지 않은 행동의 배후 동기를 설명해준다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 아직 결과가 정해지지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. '만일 ~라면'이라는 열린 가정으로, 이 계획의 출처가 사람인지 하나님인지 판가름 나지 않은 상태에서 결과를 지켜보자는 뉘앙스를 담는다.
