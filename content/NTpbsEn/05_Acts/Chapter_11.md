<span class="v-num">1</span> Now the apostles and the brothers being throughout Judea heard that the Gentiles also received the word of God.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And when Peter went up to Jerusalem, those of the circumcision contended with him,

<span class="v-num">3</span> saying, "You went in to men having uncircumcision, and ate with them."

<span class="v-num">4</span> But Peter, having begun, explained <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to them in order, saying,

<span class="v-num">5</span> "I was in the city of Joppa praying, and I saw in a trance a vision, a certain vessel descending like a great sheet, being let down from heaven by four corners, and it came to me;

<span class="v-num">6</span> on which having fixed my eyes, I observed carefully, and I saw the four-footed animals of the earth, and the wild beasts, and the creeping things, and the birds of heaven.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And I heard a voice saying to me, 'Having risen, Peter, kill and eat.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> But I said, 'By no means, Lord; for nothing common or unclean has ever entered into my mouth.'

<span class="v-num">9</span> But a voice answered me the second time out of heaven, 'What God has cleansed, you must not call common.'[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And this happened three times, and again all was drawn up into heaven.

<span class="v-num">11</span> "And behold, immediately three men stood before the house in which I was, having been sent from Caesarea to me.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And the Spirit told me to go with them, doubting nothing. And these six brothers also went with me, and we entered into the man's house;

<span class="v-num">13</span> and he reported to us how he saw the angel standing in his house, and saying to him, 'Send men to Joppa, and call for Simon, who is called Peter,'

<span class="v-num">14</span> who will speak words to you, by which you will be saved, you and all your house.'"

<span class="v-num">15</span> And as I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell on them, just as also on us at the beginning.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, 'John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> If then God gave to them the same gift as also to us, having believed on the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I, that I could withstand God?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> And having heard these things, they were silent, and glorified God, saying, "Then indeed God has given to the Gentiles also the repentance unto life."[^3]

<span class="v-num">19</span> Now those who were scattered abroad because of the tribulation that arose over Stephen went as far as Phoenicia, and Cyprus, and Antioch, speaking the word to no one except Jews only.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But some of them were Cyprian and Cyrenian men, who, having entered into Antioch, spoke to the Hellenists, preaching the good news of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the hand of the Lord was with them; and a great number, having believed, turned to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the report concerning them was heard in the ears of the church that was in Jerusalem; and they sent forth Barnabas to go as far as Antioch;

<span class="v-num">23</span> who, having arrived, and having seen the grace of God, rejoiced, and exhorted them all, with purpose of heart, to remain with the Lord;

<span class="v-num">24</span> for he was a good man, and full of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit and faith; and a considerable crowd was added to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And Barnabas went out to Tarsus to seek out Saul,

<span class="v-num">26</span> and having found him, he brought him to Antioch. And it came to pass that they were gathered together with the church for a whole year, and taught a considerable crowd, and the disciples were first called Christians in Antioch.[^4]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Now in these days prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And one of them named Agabus stood up, and signified by the Spirit that there would be a great famine over all the world, which also happened in the days of Claudius Caesar.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And the disciples, according as any of them was prospering, each of them determined to send <span class="added-text">(something)</span> for a ministry to the brothers who were living in Judea;

<span class="v-num">30</span> which they also did, having sent <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul.

[^1]: κοινόω(속되다 하다)와 καθαρίζω(깨끗하게 하다)의 대비는 하나님이 이미 정하신 것을 사람이 판단할 수 없음을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἐβάπτισεν(과거, 완료된 사실)과 βαπτισθήσεσθε(미래, 약속)의 대조가 요한의 세례와 성령 세례의 차이를 부각시킨다.
[^3]: μετάνοιαν εἰς ζωήν(생명에 이르는 회개)은 회개가 단순한 태도 변화가 아니라 생명을 얻게 하는 하나님의 선물임을 나타낸다.
[^4]: Χριστιανούς(그리스도인)는 신약에서 이곳에 처음 등장하는 명칭으로, 외부인이 그리스도를 따르는 자들을 부르던 호칭이었다.
