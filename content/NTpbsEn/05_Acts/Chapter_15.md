# Acts 15

<span class="v-num">1</span> And some, having come down from Judea, were teaching the brothers, "Unless you are circumcised according to the custom of Moses, you cannot be saved."[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> A dissension and debate not small having arisen for Paul and Barnabas with them, they determined that Paul and Barnabas and some others of them should go up to the apostles and elders at Jerusalem concerning this <span class="alt-label">matter</span><span class="alt-text">(=question)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> They then, having been sent forward by the church, passed through Phoenicia and Samaria, relating in detail the conversion of the Gentiles, and they caused great joy to all the brothers.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And having arrived at Jerusalem, they were welcomed by the church and the apostles and the elders, and they reported as much as God had done with them.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But some of those from the sect of the Pharisees who had believed rose up, saying, "It is necessary to circumcise them and to command <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to keep the law of Moses."

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the apostles and the elders were gathered together to see concerning this matter.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And much debate having arisen, Peter stood up <span class="added-text">(and)</span> said to them, "Men, brothers, you know that from ancient days God chose among us, that through my mouth the Gentiles should hear the word of the gospel and believe.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And God, who knows the heart, testified to them, giving them the Holy Spirit, just as also to us,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and made no distinction at all between us and them, having purified their hearts by faith.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Now then, why do you test God, to put a yoke upon the neck of the disciples, which neither our fathers nor we were able to bear?

<span class="v-num">11</span> But we believe to be saved through the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, in the same manner as they also."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And all the multitude kept silent, and they were listening to Barnabas and Paul relating as many signs and wonders as God had done among the Gentiles through them.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And after they were silent, James answered, saying, "Men, brothers, listen to me.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Simeon has related how God first visited to take out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles a people for his name.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And with this the words of the prophets agree, just as it is written,

<span class="v-num">16</span> 'After these things I will return, and I will rebuild the tabernacle of David which has fallen; and I will rebuild its <span class="alt-label">ruins</span><span class="alt-text">(=what has been torn down)</span>, and I will set it up again;[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> so that the <span class="alt-label">rest</span><span class="alt-text">(=remnant)</span> of men may seek the Lord, and all the Gentiles upon whom my name has been called, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord who does all these things.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Known to God from eternity are all his works.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Therefore I judge not to trouble those from the Gentiles who are turning to God,

<span class="v-num">20</span> but to write to them to abstain from the pollutions of idols and from fornication and from what is strangled and from blood.

<span class="v-num">21</span> For Moses from ancient generations has, in every city, those proclaiming him, being read in the synagogues every Sabbath."

<span class="v-num">22</span> Then it seemed good to the apostles and the elders with the whole church, having chosen men from among them, to send <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to Antioch with Paul and Barnabas — Judas called Barsabas, and Silas, leading men among the brothers —

<span class="v-num">23</span> having written by their hand these things: "The apostles and the elders and the brothers, to the brothers from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles throughout Antioch and Syria and Cilicia, <span class="alt-label">greetings</span><span class="alt-text">(=rejoice)</span>.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Since we have heard that some from among us, having gone out, troubled you with words, unsettling your souls, saying <span class="added-text">(you must)</span> be circumcised and keep the law — to whom we gave no such command —

<span class="v-num">25</span> it seemed good to us, having become of one mind, having chosen men, to send <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to you with our beloved Barnabas and Paul,

<span class="v-num">26</span> men who have given up their lives for the sake of the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">27</span> We have therefore sent Judas and Silas, who themselves also will report the same things by word <span class="added-text">(of mouth)</span>.

<span class="v-num">28</span> For it seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us to lay upon you no greater burden than these necessary things:

<span class="v-num">29</span> to abstain from things sacrificed to idols and from blood and from what is strangled and from fornication; keeping yourselves from these, you will do well. Farewell."

<span class="v-num">30</span> So they, having been sent away, came to Antioch, and having gathered the multitude, they delivered the letter.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And having read <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they rejoiced over the encouragement.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And Judas and Silas, being themselves also prophets, encouraged the brothers with many words, and strengthened <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And having spent some time <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, they were sent away in peace from the brothers to the apostles.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But it seemed good to Silas to remain there.[^4]

<span class="v-num">35</span> And Paul and Barnabas stayed in Antioch, teaching and preaching, with many others also, the word of the Lord.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And after some days Paul said to Barnabas, "Let us indeed return and visit our brothers in every city in which we proclaimed the word of the Lord, <span class="added-text">(to see)</span> how they are."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And Barnabas wanted to take along with <span class="added-text">(them)</span> John, who was called Mark.

<span class="v-num">38</span> But Paul thought it right not to take along this one who had departed from them from Pamphylia and had not gone with them to the work.

<span class="v-num">39</span> So there arose a sharp <span class="alt-label">contention</span><span class="alt-text">(=dispute)</span>, so that they separated from each other, and Barnabas took Mark along and sailed away to Cyprus,

<span class="v-num">40</span> but Paul, having chosen Silas, went out, having been commended to the grace of God by the brothers.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And he passed through Syria and Cilicia, strengthening the churches.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(περιτέμνησθε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '만일 할례를 받지 않으면'이라는 뜻이다. 이는 실제 사실이 아니라 유대주의자들이 주장하는 가상의 조건을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 아모스 9:11-12(LXX)의 인용으로, 다윗의 무너진 장막을 다시 세우신다는 약속이 이방인 포용의 근거로 제시된다.
[^3]: 아모스 인용의 결론부로, 이방인들이 주의 이름으로 일컬음을 받는다는 표현이 이방인 구원 포함의 근거로 사용된다.
[^4]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 주요 사본(시내사본, 바티칸사본 등)에 이 구절이 없다는 사유로 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
