<span class="v-num">1</span> And having been saved, then they recognized that the island was called Melita.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the <span class="alt-label">natives</span><span class="alt-text">(=barbarians)</span> showed us no ordinary <span class="alt-label">kindness</span><span class="alt-text">(=humanity)</span>; for having kindled a fire, they received all of us, because of the rain that was <span class="alt-label">falling</span><span class="alt-text">(=impending)</span>, and because of the cold.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And Paul having gathered a bundle of dry sticks, and having put <span class="added-text">(them)</span> on the fire, a viper came out from the heat and fastened on his hand.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when the natives saw the creature hanging from his hand, they said to one another, "Certainly this man is a murderer, whom, though saved from the sea, <span class="alt-label">justice</span><span class="alt-text">(=Dike)</span> has not allowed to live."

<span class="v-num">5</span> He then, having shaken off the creature into the fire, suffered no harm.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But they were expecting that he was about to swell up or suddenly fall down dead; but after they had waited a long time, and saw nothing <span class="alt-label">amiss</span><span class="alt-text">(=unusual)</span> happening to him, they changed their minds and said that he was a god.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now in the <span class="added-text">(region)</span> around that place there were <span class="alt-label">lands</span><span class="alt-text">(=estates)</span> belonging to the chief man of the island, named Publius, who received us and entertained <span class="added-text">(us)</span> kindly three days.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And it happened that the father of Publius was lying <span class="added-text">(sick)</span>, afflicted with fevers and dysentery; to whom Paul, having entered, and having prayed, laid <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands on him, and healed him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> This then having happened, the rest also who had diseases in the island came and were healed;

<span class="v-num">10</span> who also honored us with many honors, and when we were putting to sea, they put on <span class="alt-label">board</span><span class="alt-text">(=supplied)</span> the things <span class="added-text">(needed)</span> for our need.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And after three months we set sail in a ship that had wintered in the island, an Alexandrian <span class="added-text">(ship)</span>, with the sign of the Twin <span class="alt-label">Brothers</span><span class="alt-text">(=Dioscuri)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And having landed at Syracuse, we remained three days;

<span class="v-num">13</span> from there, having gone around, we arrived at Rhegium, and after one day, a south wind having arisen, on the second day we came to Puteoli;

<span class="v-num">14</span> where, having found brothers, we were urged by them to stay seven days; and so we came to Rome.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And from there, the brothers, having heard about us, came out to meet us as far as Appius' <span class="alt-label">Market</span><span class="alt-text">(=Forum)</span> and Three Taverns; whom when Paul saw, having given thanks to God, he took courage.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And when we came to Rome, the centurion delivered the prisoners to the captain of the <span class="alt-label">guard</span><span class="alt-text">(=camp commander)</span>; but Paul was permitted to stay by himself, with the soldier guarding him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And it happened that after three days Paul called together those who were the chief men of the Jews; and when they had come together, he said to them, "Men, brothers, I, having done nothing against the people or the customs of our fathers, was delivered as a prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans,

<span class="v-num">18</span> who, having examined me, wanted to release <span class="added-text">(me)</span>, because there was no cause of death in me.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But when the Jews spoke against <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, I was compelled to appeal to Caesar, not as having anything to accuse my nation of.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For this reason then I called for you, to see <span class="added-text">(you)</span> and to speak with <span class="added-text">(you)</span>; for because of the hope of Israel I am bound with this chain."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they said to him, "We have neither received letters concerning you from Judea, nor has any of the brothers who came reported or spoken anything evil concerning you.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But we desire to hear from you what you think; for concerning this sect, it is known to us that it is spoken against everywhere."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And when they had set a day for him, more <span class="added-text">(people)</span> came to him at his lodging; to whom he expounded, testifying the kingdom of God, and persuading them concerning Jesus, both from the law of Moses and <span class="added-text">(from)</span> the prophets, from morning until evening.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And some were persuaded by the things said, but others did not believe.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And being at odds with one another, they departed, after Paul had spoken one word, "Well did the Holy Spirit speak through Isaiah the prophet to our fathers,

<span class="v-num">26</span> saying, 'Go to this people and say, "Hearing you will hear, and will not at all understand; and seeing you will see, and will not at all perceive;"

<span class="v-num">27</span> for the heart of this people has grown dull, and with their ears they have heard <span class="alt-label">heavily</span><span class="alt-text">(=dully)</span>, and their eyes they have closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with <span class="added-text">(their)</span> heart, and should turn, and I should heal them.'"[^1]

<span class="v-num">28</span> "Let it be known to you therefore, that the salvation of God has been sent to the Gentiles, and they will hear it."

<span class="v-num">29</span> And when he had said these things, the Jews departed, having much discussion among themselves.[^2]

<span class="v-num">30</span> And Paul remained two whole years in his own rented house, and received all who came in to him,

<span class="v-num">31</span> preaching the kingdom of God, and teaching the things concerning the Lord Jesus Christ, with all boldness, unhindered.
