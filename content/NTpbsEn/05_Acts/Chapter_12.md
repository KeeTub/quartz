<span class="v-num">1</span> Now at that time Herod the king put forth <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands to mistreat some of <span class="added-text">(those)</span> from the church.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And he killed James the brother of John with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sword.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And seeing that it was pleasing to the Jews, he proceeded to seize Peter also — and it was <span class="added-text">(the)</span> days of unleavened <span class="added-text">(bread)</span>.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Whom having also seized, he put <span class="added-text">(him)</span> into prison, delivering <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to four squads of four soldiers each to guard him, intending after the Passover to bring him up to the people.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> So then Peter was being kept in the prison, but prayer was being made fervently by the church to God on his behalf.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And when Herod was about to bring him forth, on that night Peter was sleeping between two soldiers, bound with two chains, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> guards before the door were keeping the prison.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And behold, an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord stood by, and a light shone in the cell; and striking the side of Peter, he raised him up, saying, "Rise quickly." And his chains fell off from <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the angel said to him, "Gird yourself, and bind on your sandals." And he did so. And he says to him, "Put on your garment, and follow me."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And having gone out, he followed him; and he did not know that what was happening through the angel was true, but was thinking he was seeing a vision.[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And having passed through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first guard and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> second, they came to the iron gate that leads into the city, which opened to them by itself; and going out they went forward one street, and immediately the angel departed from him.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And Peter, having come to himself, said, "Now I know truly that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord sent forth his angel and delivered me out of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hand of Herod and all the expectation of the people of the Jews."[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And having perceived <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, he came to the house of Mary the mother of John who was called Mark, where many were gathered together and praying.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And Peter having knocked at the door of the gate, a servant girl named Rhoda came to answer.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And recognizing the voice of Peter, from joy she did not open the gate, but running in she reported that Peter was standing before the gate.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But they said to her, "You are mad." But she kept insisting that it was so. And they said, "It is his angel."

<span class="v-num">16</span> But Peter continued knocking; and having opened, they saw him, and were amazed.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And motioning to them with <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand to be silent, he related to them how the Lord had brought him out of the prison. And he said, "Report these things to James and to the brothers." And going out, he went to another place.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And when day came, there was no small disturbance among the soldiers as to what had become of Peter.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And Herod, having sought him and not found <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, having examined the guards, commanded <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to be led away (=to execution); and going down from Judea to Caesarea, he stayed there.[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> And Herod was enraged with the Tyrians and Sidonians; but they came to him with one accord, and having persuaded Blastus who was over the king's bedchamber, they asked for peace, because their country was fed from the royal <span class="added-text">(land)</span>.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And on an appointed day, Herod, having put on royal clothing, and having sat on the judgment seat, delivered an oration to them.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the people were shouting, "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> voice of a god, and not of a man!"

<span class="v-num">23</span> And immediately an angel of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord struck him, because he did not give the glory to God; and having become eaten by worms, he expired.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But the word of God grew and multiplied.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And Barnabas and Saul returned from Jerusalem, having fulfilled the ministry, taking along with <span class="added-text">(them)</span> John who was called Mark also.

[^1]: φυλάσσειν은 목적 부정사구문으로, 넘겨준 목적(지키게 하려고)을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἦν...γινομένη는 미완료 우언법으로 교회의 간절한 기도가 지속되었음을 강조한다.
[^3]: ᾔδει·ἐδόκει의 미완료는 베드로가 그 순간 계속 몰랐고 계속 그렇게 여겼음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: γενόμενος ἐν ἑαυτῷ는 관용구로 '제정신이 들다'는 뜻이며, 그 전까지 환상으로 여겼던 상태에서 현실을 자각함을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἀπαχθῆναι는 단순히 '끌려가다'이나 문맥상 처형으로 끌려감을 뜻하는 완곡어법이다.
