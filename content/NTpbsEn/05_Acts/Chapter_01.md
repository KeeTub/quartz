# Acts 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> I made the first account concerning all things, O Theophilus, which Jesus began both to do and to teach,

<span class="v-num">2</span> until the day in which, having commanded the apostles through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit whom he chose, he was taken up.

<span class="v-num">3</span> To whom also he presented himself alive after his suffering, by many convincing proofs, appearing to them through forty days, and speaking the things concerning the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And meeting together with <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, he commanded them not to depart from Jerusalem, but to wait for the promise of the Father, "which you heard from me;

<span class="v-num">5</span> for John indeed baptized with water, but you shall be baptized <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit not many days from now."[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> So they, having come together, asked him, saying, "Lord, will you at this time restore the kingdom to Israel?"

<span class="v-num">7</span> He said to them, "It is not for you to know <span class="added-text">(the)</span> times or <span class="added-text">(the)</span> seasons which the Father put in his own authority;[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> but you shall receive power, the Holy Spirit having come upon you, and you shall be witnesses to me both in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria and to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">end</span><span class="alt-text">(=uttermost part)</span> of the earth."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And having said these things, as they were watching, he was taken up, and a cloud received him out of their sight.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And as they were gazing into heaven while he was going, behold, two men stood by them in white clothing,

<span class="v-num">11</span> who also said, "Men <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Galilee, why do you stand looking into heaven? This Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will come in this way (=manner), just as you saw him going into heaven."[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then they returned to Jerusalem from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mount called Olivet, which is near Jerusalem, a Sabbath day's journey away.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And when they entered, they went up into the upper room where they were staying, Peter and James and John and Andrew, Philip and Thomas, Bartholomew and Matthew, James <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Alphaeus and Simon the Zealot, and Judas <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of James.

<span class="v-num">14</span> All these were continuing with one accord in prayer and supplication, with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> women and Mary the mother of Jesus, and with his brothers.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And in these days Peter, having stood up in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of the disciples, said <span class="added-text">(the crowd of <span class="alt-label">names</span><span class="alt-text">(=persons)</span></span> gathered together being about a hundred twenty):[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> "Men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, this scripture had to be fulfilled, which the Holy Spirit spoke before through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouth of David concerning Judas, who became a guide to those who arrested Jesus;

<span class="v-num">17</span> for he was numbered with us and obtained <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">portion</span><span class="alt-text">(=lot)</span> of this ministry.

<span class="v-num">18</span> This man indeed acquired a field with the reward of unrighteousness, and having fallen headlong, burst open in the middle, and all his intestines gushed out,

<span class="v-num">19</span> And it became known to all those dwelling <span class="added-text">(in)</span> Jerusalem, so that that field was called in their own language Akeldama, that is, Field of Blood.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For it is written in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> book of Psalms, 'Let his dwelling place be made desolate, and let there be no one dwelling in it,' and, 'Let another take his <span class="alt-label">office</span><span class="alt-text">(=position of oversight)</span>.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> Therefore it is necessary, of the men who accompanied us in all the time in which the Lord Jesus went in and went out among us,

<span class="v-num">22</span> beginning from the baptism of John until the day when he was taken up from us, one of these <span class="added-text">(men)</span> to become a witness of his resurrection with us."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And they put forward two, Joseph called Barsabas, who was surnamed Justus, and Matthias.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And having prayed, they said, "You, Lord, knower of the hearts of all, show which one of these two you have chosen

<span class="v-num">25</span> to take the <span class="alt-label">portion</span><span class="alt-text">(=lot)</span> of this ministry and apostleship, from which Judas fell away, to go to his own place."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And they gave their lots, and the lot fell on Matthias, and he was numbered with the eleven apostles.

[^1]: ἐβάπτισεν(아오리스트, 완료된 과거사건)과 βαπτισθήσεσθε(미래수동, 아직 이루어지지 않은 사건)의 시제 대조가 물세례와 성령세례의 질적 차이를 드러낸다.
[^2]: χρόνους(연대기적 시간의 흐름)와 καιροὺς(정해진 결정적 시점)는 구분되는 개념으로, 하나님의 주권적 계획 전체를 가리킨다.
[^3]: ἐλεύσεται(미래시제)는 승천의 방식과 동일한 재림을 확정된 미래사건으로 약속한다.
[^4]: ὄχλος ὀνομάτων(이름들의 무리)은 히브리적 관용구로 '사람들의 수'를 가리키는 표현이다.
[^5]: 시편 69:25과 109:8의 인용으로, 배신자에게 임할 심판과 그 직분의 계승을 함께 예언한 것이다.
