<span class="v-num">1</span> Now in these days, as the disciples were multiplying, there arose a murmuring of the Hellenists against the Hebrews, because their widows were being overlooked in the daily <span class="alt-label">ministration</span><span class="alt-text">(=distribution)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the twelve, having called together the multitude of the disciples, said, "It is not <span class="alt-label">pleasing</span><span class="alt-text">(=fitting)</span> for us, having left the word of God, to serve tables.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Therefore, brothers, look <span class="alt-label">out</span><span class="alt-text">(=select)</span> from among you seven men, being witnessed <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=well reported of)</span>, full of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit and wisdom, whom we will appoint over this need;

<span class="v-num">4</span> but we will devote ourselves to prayer and to the ministry of the word."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the word <span class="alt-label">pleased</span><span class="alt-text">(=was pleasing)</span> before all the multitude; and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and Philip, and Prochorus and Nicanor, and Timon and Parmenas, and Nicolas, a proselyte of Antioch,

<span class="v-num">6</span> whom they set before the apostles; and having prayed, they laid <span class="added-text">(their)</span> hands on them.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And the word of God increased, and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly, and a great crowd of the priests were obedient to the faith.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Stephen, full of faith and power, did great wonders and signs among the people.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But some of those from the synagogue called <span class="added-text">(that)</span> of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Libertines, and <span class="added-text">(of the)</span> Cyrenians and Alexandrians, and of those from Cilicia and Asia, rose up, disputing with Stephen;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and they were not able to withstand the wisdom and the spirit by which he spoke.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Then they suborned men, saying, "We have heard him speaking blasphemous words against Moses and God."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And they stirred up the people and the elders and the scribes, and having set upon <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, they seized him, and brought <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to the council,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and they set up false witnesses, saying, "This man does not cease speaking blasphemous words against this holy place and the law.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For we have heard him saying, 'This Jesus the Nazarene will destroy this place, and will change the customs which Moses delivered to us.'"

<span class="v-num">15</span> And all who sat in the council, having gazed intently at him, saw his face as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of an angel.[^2]

[^1]: 헬라파(헬레니스트)는 헬라어를 사용하는 디아스포라 출신 유대인을, 히브리인은 아람어를 사용하는 팔레스타인 출신 유대인을 가리키는 것으로 초대교회 내 문화적 구분을 보여준다.
[^2]: 모세의 얼굴이 하나님과의 만남 후 빛났던 출애굽기 34장을 연상시키며, 스데반이 하나님의 임재 가운데 있었음을 암시한다.
