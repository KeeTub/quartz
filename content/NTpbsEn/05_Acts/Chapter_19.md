# Acts 19

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now it came to pass, while Apollos was at Corinth, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Paul, having passed through the upper parts, came to Ephesus; and having found some disciples,

<span class="v-num">2</span> he said to them, "Did you receive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, having believed?" And they said to him, "But we did not even hear whether <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit is."[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he said to them, "Into what then were you baptized?" And they said, "Into John's baptism."

<span class="v-num">4</span> But Paul said, "John indeed baptized <span class="added-text">(with the)</span> baptism of repentance, saying to the people that they should believe on the one coming after him, that is, on Christ Jesus."[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And having heard <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, they were baptized into the name of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And Paul having laid hands on them, the Holy Spirit came upon them, and they spoke with tongues and prophesied.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And they were all about twelve men.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And having entered into the synagogue, he spoke boldly for three months, reasoning and persuading the things concerning the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But when some were hardened and did not obey, speaking evil of the Way before the multitude, having departed from them, he separated the disciples, reasoning daily in the school of one Tyrannus.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And this continued for two years, so that all those dwelling in Asia heard the word of the Lord Jesus, both Jews and Greeks.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And God worked no <span class="alt-label">ordinary</span><span class="alt-text">(=extraordinary)</span> miracles through the hands of Paul,

<span class="v-num">12</span> so that even handkerchiefs or aprons were carried from his skin to the sick, and the diseases departed from them, and the evil spirits went out of them.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But certain ones of the itinerant Jewish exorcists took upon themselves to name the name of the Lord Jesus over those having the evil spirits, saying, "We command you by Jesus whom Paul preaches."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And there were certain seven sons of Sceva, a Jewish chief priest, who did this.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But the evil spirit answered and said, "Jesus I know, and Paul I understand; but who are you?"

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the man in whom the evil spirit was leaped on them, and having overpowered them, prevailed against them, so that they fled out of that house naked and wounded.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And this became known to all, both Jews and Greeks, dwelling at Ephesus; and fear fell upon them all, and the name of the Lord Jesus was magnified.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And many of those having believed came confessing and declaring their deeds.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And a considerable number of those who practiced magic, having brought together their books, burned <span class="added-text">(them)</span> before all; and they reckoned up the value of them, and found <span class="added-text">(it to be)</span> fifty thousand <span class="added-text">(pieces)</span> of silver.

<span class="v-num">20</span> So mightily the word of the Lord grew and prevailed.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Now when these things were fulfilled, Paul purposed in the <span class="alt-label">spirit</span><span class="alt-text">(=his spirit)</span>, having passed through Macedonia and Achaia, to go to Jerusalem, saying, "After I have been there, I must also see Rome."[^4]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And having sent into Macedonia two of those ministering to him, Timothy and Erastus, he himself stayed a while in Asia.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And there arose at that time no small disturbance concerning the Way.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For a certain man named Demetrius, a silversmith, who made silver shrines of Artemis, provided no small business for the craftsmen;

<span class="v-num">25</span> whom having gathered together, with the workmen of similar occupation, he said, "Men, you know that from this business is our <span class="alt-label">prosperity</span><span class="alt-text">(=wealth)</span>.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And you see and hear that not only at Ephesus, but almost throughout all Asia, this Paul has persuaded and turned away a considerable crowd, saying that they are no gods which are made with hands.

<span class="v-num">27</span> So not only is this our trade in danger of coming into disrepute, but also the temple of the great goddess Artemis <span class="added-text">(is in danger)</span> of being counted as nothing, and her majesty, whom all Asia and the world worships, is about to be <span class="alt-label">destroyed</span><span class="alt-text">(=deposed)</span>."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And having heard <span class="added-text">(this)</span> and having become full of wrath, they cried out, saying, "Great <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Artemis of the Ephesians!"

<span class="v-num">29</span> And the whole city was filled with confusion; and they rushed with one accord into the theater, having seized Gaius and Aristarchus, Macedonians, Paul's fellow travelers.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But when Paul wanted to enter in to the people, the disciples did not allow him.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And some of the Asiarchs also, being his friends, having sent to him, urged <span class="added-text">(him)</span> not to give himself into the theater.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Some indeed cried out one thing, and some another; for the assembly was confused, and the majority did not know for what reason they had come together.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And out of the crowd they brought forward Alexander, the Jews having put him forward; and Alexander, having motioned with the hand, wanted to make a defense to the people.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But when they recognized that he was a Jew, one voice arose from all, crying out for about two hours, "Great <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Artemis of the Ephesians!"

<span class="v-num">35</span> And having quieted the crowd, the town clerk said, "Men of Ephesus, for who is there among men who does not know that the city of the Ephesians is temple-keeper of the great goddess Artemis, and of the <span class="added-text">(image)</span> fallen from heaven?

<span class="v-num">36</span> Since then these things cannot be <span class="alt-label">denied</span><span class="alt-text">(=are undeniable)</span>, it is fitting for you to be calm, and to do nothing rash.

<span class="v-num">37</span> For you have brought these men, who are neither robbers of temples, nor blasphemers of your goddess.

<span class="v-num">38</span> If then Demetrius and the craftsmen with him have a matter against anyone, the <span class="alt-label">courts</span><span class="alt-text">(=court days)</span> are open, and there are proconsuls; let them accuse one another.

<span class="v-num">39</span> But if you seek anything concerning other matters, it shall be settled in the lawful assembly.

<span class="v-num">40</span> For indeed we are in danger of being accused of riot concerning today's <span class="added-text">(events)</span>, there being no cause by which we shall be able to give account of this commotion."

<span class="v-num">41</span> And having said these things, he dismissed the assembly.

[^1]: πιστεύσαντες는 부정과거 분사로 '믿었을 때에'라는 선행 동작을 나타내며 믿음이 성령 받음보다 먼저였음을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα πιστεύσωσι는 목적을 나타내는 ἵνα+가정법 구문으로, 요한의 세례 선포 목적이 그리스도를 믿게 하려는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^3]: ἐλάλουν, προεφήτευον은 미완료형으로 방언과 예언이 계속 이어졌음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἐν τῷ πνεύματι는 성령이 아니라 바울 자신의 심령(결심)을 가리키는 것으로 보는 것이 문맥상 자연스럽다.
