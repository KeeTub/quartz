# Acts 14

<span class="v-num">1</span> It came to pass in Iconium, that they entered together into the synagogue of the Jews, and spoke in such a way that a great multitude both of Jews and of Greeks believed.

<span class="v-num">2</span> But the unbelieving Jews stirred up and made evil-disposed the <span class="alt-label">souls</span><span class="alt-text">(=minds)</span> of the Gentiles against the brothers.

<span class="v-num">3</span> So then they stayed a considerable time, speaking boldly <span class="added-text">(relying)</span> on the Lord, who bore witness to the word of his grace, and granted signs and wonders to be done through their hands.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But the multitude of the city was divided; and some were with the Jews, and some with the apostles.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But when there was an <span class="alt-label">assault</span><span class="alt-text">(=attempt)</span> of both the Gentiles and the Jews with their rulers, to insult and to stone them,

<span class="v-num">6</span> they, becoming aware <span class="added-text">(of it)</span>, fled for refuge to the cities of Lycaonia, Lystra and Derbe, and the surrounding region,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and there they were preaching the gospel.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And a certain man in Lystra, unable in his feet, sat, being lame from his mother's womb, who had never walked.

<span class="v-num">9</span> This one was hearing Paul speaking; who, having looked intently at him, and seen that he had faith to be <span class="alt-label">saved</span><span class="alt-text">(=healed)</span>,[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> said with a loud voice, "Stand up straight on your feet." And he leaped and walked.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But the crowds, having seen what Paul did, lifted up their voice in Lycaonian, saying, "The gods, having become like men, have come down to us,"

<span class="v-num">12</span> and they called Barnabas Zeus, and Paul Hermes, because he was the leader in <span class="alt-label">speaking</span><span class="alt-text">(=of the word)</span>.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And the priest of Zeus, who was before their city, brought bulls and garlands to the gates, and wished, with the crowds, to sacrifice.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But the apostles Barnabas and Paul, having heard <span class="added-text">(of it)</span>, tore their garments and rushed in among the crowd, crying out

<span class="v-num">15</span> and saying, "Men, why do you do these things? We also are men of like nature with you, preaching the gospel to you, <span class="added-text">(to turn)</span> from these vain things to the living God, who made the heaven and the earth and the sea and all things in them;[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> who in the generations that have passed allowed all the nations to go their <span class="added-text">(own)</span> ways;

<span class="v-num">17</span> and yet indeed he did not leave himself without witness, doing good, giving us rains from heaven and fruitful seasons, filling our hearts with food and gladness."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And saying these things, they scarcely restrained the crowds from sacrificing to them.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Jews came from Antioch and Iconium, and having persuaded the crowds, and having stoned Paul, they dragged him out of the city, supposing him to be dead.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But as the disciples surrounded him, he rose up and entered into the city; and on the next day he went out with Barnabas to Derbe.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And having preached the gospel to that city, and having made many disciples, they returned to Lystra, and Iconium, and Antioch,

<span class="v-num">22</span> strengthening the souls of the disciples, exhorting <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to continue in the faith, and <span class="added-text">(saying)</span> "that through many afflictions we must enter into the kingdom of God."[^3]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having appointed elders for them in every church, having prayed with fastings, they committed them to the Lord in whom they had believed.[^4]

<span class="v-num">24</span> And having passed through Pisidia, they came into Pamphylia;

<span class="v-num">25</span> and having spoken the word in Perga, they went down to Attalia;

<span class="v-num">26</span> and from there they sailed away to Antioch, from where they had been committed to the grace of God for the work which they fulfilled.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And having arrived, and having gathered the church together, they reported all that God had done with them, and that he had opened to the Gentiles a door of faith.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And they stayed there no little time with the disciples.

[^1]: τοῦ σωθῆναι는 관사 부정사로 '구원받기에 (이르는)' 목적·결과의 뉘앙스를 나타낸다.
[^2]: ματαίων(헛된 것들)은 우상들을 가리키는 관용적 표현으로, 살아계신 하나님과 대조된다.
[^3]: δεῖ(반드시 ~해야 한다)는 하나님 나라 입성이 환난을 통과하는 필연적 과정임을 나타내는 신학적 진술이다.
[^4]: χειροτονήσαντες는 원래 '손을 들어 뽑다'는 뜻으로, 교회의 참여 아래 장로를 세운 것을 나타낸다.
