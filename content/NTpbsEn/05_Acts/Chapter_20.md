<span class="v-num">1</span> After the uproar ceased, Paul, having called the disciples to him and having greeted <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, went out to go to Macedonia.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having passed through those parts, and having exhorted them with much <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=discourse)</span>, he came to Greece;

<span class="v-num">3</span> And having spent three months, a plot against him having arisen by the Jews as he was about to <span class="alt-label">sail</span><span class="alt-text">(=set out)</span> to Syria, there arose a resolution to return through Macedonia.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And there accompanied him as far as Asia, Sopater of Berea; and of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Thessalonians, Aristarchus and Secundus; and Gaius of Derbe, and Timothy; and <span class="added-text">(of the)</span> Asians, Tychicus and Trophimus.

<span class="v-num">5</span> These, having gone ahead, were waiting for us in Troas.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But we sailed away from Philippi after the days of unleavened <span class="added-text">(bread)</span>, and came to them in Troas within five days, where we stayed seven days.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And on the first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of the <span class="alt-label">week</span><span class="alt-text">(=Sabbaths)</span>, when the disciples were gathered together to break bread, Paul discoursed with them, being about to depart on the next day, and he prolonged the <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=discourse)</span> until midnight.[^1]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And there were many lamps in the upper room where they were gathered.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And a certain young man named Eutychus, sitting in the window, being carried away by deep sleep as Paul discoursed still longer, having been overcome by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sleep, fell down from the third story, and was taken up dead.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But Paul, having gone down, fell upon him, and having embraced <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, said, "Do not be <span class="alt-label">troubled</span><span class="alt-text">(=make an uproar)</span>, for his <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=soul)</span> is in him."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And having gone up, and having broken bread, and having eaten, and having talked a considerable while until dawn, he departed thus.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And they brought the young man alive, and were not a little comforted.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But we, having gone ahead to the ship, sailed to Assos, intending from there to take Paul on board; for so he had arranged, intending himself to go on foot.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And when he met with us at Assos, we took him on board and came to Mitylene.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And having sailed from there, on the following <span class="added-text">(day)</span> we arrived opposite Chios; and the next <span class="added-text">(day)</span> we touched at Samos; and having stayed at Trogyllium, on the <span class="added-text">(day)</span> following we came to Miletus.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For Paul had decided to sail past Ephesus, so that it might not happen for him to spend time in Asia; for he was hurrying, if it were possible for him, to come to Jerusalem <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> the day of Pentecost.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But from Miletus, having sent to Ephesus, he called <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=summoned)</span> the elders of the church.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And when they had come to him, he said to them, "You know, from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first day on which I set foot in Asia, how I was with you the whole time,

<span class="v-num">19</span> serving the Lord with all humility, and with many tears and trials, which happened to me by the plots of the Jews;

<span class="v-num">20</span> how I kept back nothing of the things that were profitable, so as not to announce <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to you and to teach you publicly and from house to house,

<span class="v-num">21</span> testifying both to Jews and to Greeks repentance toward God, and faith toward our Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And now behold, I, bound in the <span class="added-text">(my)</span> spirit, am going to Jerusalem, not knowing the things that will meet me there,[^2]

<span class="v-num">23</span> except that the Holy Spirit testifies in every city, saying that bonds and afflictions await me.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But I make account of nothing, nor do I hold my life dear to myself, so as to finish my course with joy, and the ministry which I received from the Lord Jesus, to testify <span class="added-text">(to)</span> the gospel of the grace of God.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And now behold, I know that you all, among whom I went about preaching the kingdom of God, will see my face no more.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Therefore I testify to you on this present day, that I am clean from the blood of all <span class="added-text">(men)</span>;

<span class="v-num">27</span> for I did not keep back from announcing to you the whole <span class="alt-label">counsel</span><span class="alt-text">(=plan)</span> of God.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Take heed therefore to yourselves, and to all the flock, in which the Holy Spirit has made you overseers, to shepherd the church of God, which he purchased through his own blood.[^3]

<span class="v-num">29</span> For I know this, that after my departure grievous wolves will come in among you, not sparing the flock;

<span class="v-num">30</span> and from among your own selves men will arise, speaking perverse things, to draw away the disciples after them.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Therefore watch, remembering that for three years, night and day, I did not cease admonishing each one with tears.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And now, brothers, I commend you to God, and to the word of his grace, which is able to build <span class="added-text">(you)</span> up, and to give you an inheritance among all those who are sanctified.

<span class="v-num">33</span> I coveted no one's silver or gold or clothing.

<span class="v-num">34</span> You yourselves know that these hands ministered to my necessities, and to those who were with me.

<span class="v-num">35</span> In all things I showed you, that thus laboring, you must help the weak, and remember the words of the Lord Jesus, that he himself said, 'It is more blessed to give than to receive.'"[^4]

<span class="v-num">36</span> And having said these things, he knelt down <span class="added-text">(his knees)</span>, and prayed with them all.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And there was much weeping <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> all; and they fell on Paul's neck and kissed him,

<span class="v-num">38</span> grieving especially at the word which he had spoken, that they would see his face no more. And they accompanied him to the ship.

[^1]: 떡을 떼다(κλάσαι ἄρτον)는 단순 식사가 아니라 주의 만찬(성찬) 또는 그와 결부된 공동 식사를 가리키는 초대교회의 관용 표현이다.
[^2]: τῷ πνεύματι는 관사 있는 여격으로 '성령 안에서' 또는 '자기 자신의 영으로'(내적 확신·강권) 두 가지로 해석 가능하나, 문맥상 바울의 결연한 내적 결심을 가리키는 것으로 봄이 자연스럽다.
[^3]: 감독(ἐπισκόπους)과 목양하다(ποιμαίνειν)는 17절의 장로(πρεσβυτέρους)와 동일 직분을 가리키며, 초대교회에서 장로·감독·목자가 하나의 직분이었음을 보여준다.
[^4]: 이 말씀은 사복음서에는 기록되지 않은 예수의 말씀(이른바 agraphon)으로, 바울이 구전으로 전해 받은 주의 말씀을 인용한 것이다.
