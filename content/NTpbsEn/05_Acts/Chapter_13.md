<span class="v-num">1</span> Now there were in Antioch, according to the church that was <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, certain prophets and teachers: Barnabas, and Symeon called Niger, and Lucius the Cyrenian, and Manaen, the foster-brother of Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And as they were ministering to the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, "Set apart for me now both Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them."

<span class="v-num">3</span> Then, having fasted and prayed, and having laid hands on them, they sent <span class="added-text">(them)</span> away.

<span class="v-num">4</span> These, then, having been sent out by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia, and from there sailed away to Cyprus.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And having come to Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the synagogues of the Jews; and they had John also as <span class="added-text">(their)</span> assistant.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And having gone through the island as far as Paphos, they found a certain magician, a Jewish false prophet, whose name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> Bar-Jesus,

<span class="v-num">7</span> who was with the proconsul Sergius Paulus, a man of understanding. This one, having called for Barnabas and Saul, sought earnestly to hear the word of God;

<span class="v-num">8</span> but Elymas the magician <span class="added-text">(for so his name is translated)</span> withstood them, seeking to turn the proconsul away from the faith.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But Saul, who is also <span class="added-text">(called)</span> Paul, having been filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and having fixed his eyes upon him,[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> said, "O <span class="added-text">(you)</span> full of all deceit and all recklessness, son of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> devil, enemy of all righteousness, will you not cease perverting the straight ways of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord?

<span class="v-num">11</span> And now, behold, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hand of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> upon you, and you shall be blind, not seeing the sun for a time." And immediately a mist and darkness fell upon him, and going about he sought <span class="added-text">(someone to)</span> lead him by the hand.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then the proconsul, having seen what had happened, believed, being astonished at the teaching of the Lord.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Now those around Paul, having set sail from Paphos, came to Perga of Pamphylia; but John, having departed from them, returned to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But they, having passed through from Perga, arrived at Antioch of Pisidia, and having entered into the synagogue on the day of the sabbaths, they sat down.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And after the reading of the law and the prophets, the rulers of the synagogue sent to them, saying, "Men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, if there is a word of exhortation in you for the people, speak."

<span class="v-num">16</span> And Paul, having stood up and having motioned with his hand, said, "Men, Israelites, and you who fear God, listen.

<span class="v-num">17</span> The God of this people Israel chose our fathers, and exalted the people during <span class="added-text">(their)</span> sojourn in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land of Egypt, and with an uplifted arm led them out of it.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And for about a period of forty years he bore with <span class="alt-label">them</span><span class="alt-text">(=put up with their manners)</span> in the wilderness.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And having destroyed seven nations in the land of Canaan, he gave their land to them as an inheritance.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And after these things, for about four hundred and fifty years, he gave <span class="added-text">(them)</span> judges until Samuel the prophet.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And from then they asked for a king, and God gave them Saul the son of Kish, a man of the tribe of Benjamin, for forty years.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And having removed him, he raised up David to them for a king, of whom also he said, testifying, 'I have found David the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Jesse, a man according to my heart, who will do all my will.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">23</span> From this man's seed, according to promise, God raised up to Israel a Savior, Jesus,

<span class="v-num">24</span> John having first proclaimed, before the face of his coming, a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And as John was fulfilling <span class="added-text">(his)</span> course, he said, 'Whom do you suppose me to be? I am not <span class="added-text">(he)</span>, but behold, one comes after me, of whom I am not worthy to loose the sandal of <span class="added-text">(his)</span> feet.'

<span class="v-num">26</span> Men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, sons of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> race of Abraham, and those among you who fear God, to you the word of this salvation was sent.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For those dwelling in Jerusalem, and their rulers, not knowing this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and <span class="added-text">(not knowing)</span> the voices of the prophets that are read every sabbath, having judged <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, fulfilled <span class="added-text">(them)</span>;

<span class="v-num">28</span> and having found no cause of death, they asked Pilate that he be put to death.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And when they had finished all the things written concerning him, having taken <span class="added-text">(him)</span> down from the tree, they laid <span class="added-text">(him)</span> in a tomb.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But God raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead,

<span class="v-num">31</span> who was seen for many days by those who came up with him from Galilee to Jerusalem, who are his witnesses to the people.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And we bring you good news of the promise made to the fathers,

<span class="v-num">33</span> that God has fulfilled this to us their children, having raised up Jesus; as also it is written in the second psalm, 'You are my Son, today I have begotten you.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">34</span> And that he raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, no more about to return to corruption, thus he has said, 'I will give you the holy and sure <span class="added-text">(mercies)</span> of David.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">35</span> Therefore also in another <span class="added-text">(psalm)</span> he says, 'You will not give your Holy One to see corruption.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">36</span> For David, having served his own generation by the will of God, fell asleep, and was added to his fathers, and saw corruption.

<span class="v-num">37</span> But he whom God raised up did not see corruption.

<span class="v-num">38</span> Be it known to you therefore, men <span class="added-text">(and)</span> brothers, that through this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you;

<span class="v-num">39</span> and from all <span class="added-text">(the things)</span> which you could not be justified by the law of Moses, in this <span class="added-text">(one)</span> everyone who believes is justified.

<span class="v-num">40</span> Watch, therefore, lest what is spoken in the prophets come upon you:

<span class="v-num">41</span> 'Behold, you despisers, and marvel, and perish; for I work a work in your days, a work which you will by no means believe, though one declares it to you.'"[^6]

<span class="v-num">42</span> And as the Jews were going out of the synagogue, the Gentiles begged that these words be spoken to them on the next sabbath.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And when the synagogue had broken up, many of the Jews and of the devout proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas, who, speaking to them, persuaded them to continue in the grace of God.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And on the coming sabbath almost all the city was gathered together to hear the word of God.

<span class="v-num">45</span> But the Jews, having seen the crowds, were filled with envy, and spoke against the things spoken by Paul, contradicting and blaspheming.

<span class="v-num">46</span> But Paul and Barnabas, speaking boldly, said, "It was necessary that the word of God be spoken first to you; but since you thrust it away, and judge yourselves not worthy of eternal life, behold, we turn to the Gentiles.

<span class="v-num">47</span> For so the Lord has commanded us, 'I have set you for a light of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles, that you should be for salvation to the end of the earth.'"[^7]

<span class="v-num">48</span> And when the Gentiles heard, they rejoiced, and glorified the word of the Lord; and as many as were appointed to eternal life believed.[^8]

<span class="v-num">49</span> And the word of the Lord was spread abroad through all the region.

<span class="v-num">50</span> But the Jews stirred up the devout and honorable women, and the chief men of the city, and raised up persecution against Paul and Barnabas, and cast them out of their borders.

<span class="v-num">51</span> But they, having shaken off the dust of their feet against them, went to Iconium.

<span class="v-num">52</span> And the disciples were filled with joy and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.

[^1]: 이 절에서 처음으로 '사울'이 '바울'로 불리며, 이후 본문에서 이방 선교의 사도로서 새 이름이 정착됨을 보여준다.
[^2]: 삼상 13:14와 시 89:20을 결합 인용하여 다윗이 하나님의 마음에 맞는 왕으로 세워졌음을 증언한다.
[^3]: 시편 2:7 인용으로 예수의 부활을 하나님 아들 됨의 공적 선포로 제시한다.
[^4]: 사 55:3 인용으로 다윗 언약의 확실한 성취가 부활에 있음을 증언한다.
[^5]: 시 16:10 인용으로 그리스도의 부활이 썩지 않음을 예언한 구절임을 밝힌다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἐκδιηγῆται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건절로, '누군가 설명해 줄지라도'라는 양보적 뉘앙스를 담아 믿기 어려운 정도를 강조한다.
[^7]: 사 49:6 인용이며 τοῦ εἶναί σε는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문으로, 이방인 구원을 향한 하나님의 뜻을 명시한다.
[^8]: '정해진'(τεταγμένοι)은 완료수동분사로, 믿음이 하나님의 예정하신 은혜에 근거함을 함의한다.
