<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things, Paul having departed from Athens, came to Corinth.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having found a certain Jew named Aquila, a Pontian by birth, having recently come from Italy, with Priscilla his wife, because Claudius had commanded all the Jews to depart from Rome, he came to them;

<span class="v-num">3</span> And because he was of the same trade, he stayed with them and worked; for they were tentmakers by trade.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And he reasoned in the synagogue every Sabbath, and persuaded Jews and Greeks.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And when Silas and Timothy came down from Macedonia, Paul was <span class="alt-label">pressed</span><span class="alt-text">(=compelled)</span> in <span class="added-text">(his)</span> spirit, testifying to the Jews <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Jesus <span class="added-text">(was)</span> the Christ.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But as they opposed and blasphemed, having shaken out his garments, he said to them, "Your blood <span class="added-text">(be)</span> upon your <span class="added-text">(own)</span> head; I <span class="added-text">(am)</span> clean; from now <span class="added-text">(on)</span> I will go to the Gentiles."[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having departed from there, he went into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house of a certain <span class="added-text">(man)</span> named Justus, one who worshiped God, whose house was adjoining the synagogue.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Crispus the ruler of the synagogue believed in the Lord with all his house; and many of the Corinthians hearing believed and were baptized.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the Lord said to Paul through a vision in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> night, "Do not be afraid, but speak and do not be silent;

<span class="v-num">10</span> because I am with you, and no one will set upon you to harm you; because I have much people in this city."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And he stayed a year and six months, teaching among them the word of God.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And when Gallio was <span class="alt-label">proconsul</span><span class="alt-text">(=governor)</span> of Achaia, the Jews rose up with one accord against Paul, and brought him to the judgment seat,

<span class="v-num">13</span> saying, "This <span class="added-text">(man)</span> persuades men to worship God contrary to the law."

<span class="v-num">14</span> But as Paul was about to open <span class="added-text">(his)</span> mouth, Gallio said to the Jews, "If indeed it were some unjust deed or wicked crime, O Jews, I would reasonably bear with you;

<span class="v-num">15</span> but if it is a question concerning words and names and <span class="added-text">(your own)</span> law, you yourselves shall see <span class="added-text">(to it)</span>; for I do not wish to be a judge of these matters."

<span class="v-num">16</span> And he drove them from the judgment seat.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Then all the Greeks, having seized Sosthenes the ruler of the synagogue, beat <span class="added-text">(him)</span> before the judgment seat; and Gallio cared nothing for these things.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But Paul, having stayed on many more days, having taken leave of the brothers, sailed away to Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila, having shaved <span class="added-text">(his)</span> head in Cenchrea; for he had a vow.[^3]

<span class="v-num">19</span> And he came to Ephesus, and left them there; but he himself, having entered into the synagogue, reasoned with the Jews.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And when they asked <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to stay a longer time with them, he did not consent;

<span class="v-num">21</span> but took leave of them, saying, "I must by all means keep the feast that is coming at Jerusalem; but I will return again to you, God willing." And he set sail from Ephesus;

<span class="v-num">22</span> and having landed at Caesarea, and having gone up and greeted the church, he went down to Antioch.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having spent some time <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, he departed, going through the region of Galatia and Phrygia in order, strengthening all the disciples.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Now a certain Jew named Apollos, an Alexandrian by birth, an eloquent man, came to Ephesus, being mighty in the scriptures.

<span class="v-num">25</span> This man had been instructed in the way of the Lord; and being fervent in spirit, he spoke and taught accurately the things concerning the Lord, knowing only the baptism of John;

<span class="v-num">26</span> And he began to speak boldly in the synagogue. And when Aquila and Priscilla heard him, they took him aside, and explained to him the way of God more accurately.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And when he was wishing to pass into Achaia, the brothers, having encouraged <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, wrote to the disciples to receive him; who, having arrived, greatly <span class="alt-label">helped</span><span class="alt-text">(=contributed much to)</span> those who had believed, through grace;

<span class="v-num">28</span> for he powerfully refuted the Jews publicly, showing through the scriptures that Jesus was the Christ.

[^1]: διελέγετο는 미완료 시제로 안식일마다 반복된 강론 행위를 나타낸다.
[^2]: "피가 머리로 돌아간다"는 구약(겔 33:4 등)에서 유래한 관용구로 책임 전가를 뜻한다.
