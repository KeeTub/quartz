<span class="v-num">1</span> Now Peter and John went up together into the temple at the ninth hour of prayer.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And a certain man, being lame from his mother's womb, was being carried; whom they laid daily at the door of the temple called Beautiful, to ask alms of those entering into the temple.

<span class="v-num">3</span> who, seeing Peter and John about to go into the temple, asked to receive alms.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But Peter, having looked intently at him with John, said, "Look at us."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And he gave attention to them, expecting to receive something from them.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But Peter said, "Silver and gold I do not have; but what I have, this I give you. In the name of Jesus Christ the Nazarene, rise up and walk."

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having grasped him by the right hand, he raised <span class="added-text">(him)</span> up; and immediately his feet and ankle bones were made firm,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and leaping up, he stood and walked, and entered with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And all the people saw him walking and praising God;

<span class="v-num">10</span> and they recognized him, that this was the one who sat for alms at the Beautiful Gate of the temple; and they were filled with wonder and amazement at what had happened to him.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And as the lame <span class="added-text">(man)</span> who was healed held on to Peter and John, all the people ran together to them in the porch that is called Solomon's, greatly amazed.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But Peter, seeing <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, answered to the people, "Men of Israel, why do you marvel at this, or why do you look intently at us, as <span class="added-text">(though)</span> by our own power or godliness we had made him walk?

<span class="v-num">13</span> The God of Abraham and Isaac and Jacob, the God of our fathers, glorified his servant Jesus, whom you delivered up, and denied him in the presence of Pilate, when he had decided to release <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But you denied the Holy and Righteous One, and asked that a man, a murderer, be granted to you,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and you killed the <span class="alt-label">Author</span><span class="alt-text">(=Prince)</span> of life, whom God raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, of which we are witnesses.[^1]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And by faith in his name, this man whom you see and know, his name has made firm; and the faith that <span class="added-text">(comes)</span> through him has given him this perfect soundness before you all.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And now, brothers, I know that you did <span class="added-text">(it)</span> according to ignorance, as also your rulers <span class="added-text">(did)</span>.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But God has thus fulfilled the things which he foretold through the mouth of all his prophets, that his Christ should suffer.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Repent therefore, and turn again, that your sins may be blotted out, so that times of refreshing may come from the presence of the Lord,[^2]

<span class="v-num">20</span> and he may send Jesus Christ, who was preached to you before,

<span class="v-num">21</span> whom heaven must receive until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> times of restoration of all things, which God spoke through the mouth of all his holy prophets since the age <span class="added-text">(began)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">22</span> For Moses indeed said to the fathers, 'A prophet the Lord your God will raise up to you from your brothers, like me; him you shall hear in all things, whatever he shall say to you.[^4]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And it shall be, every soul that will not hear that prophet shall be utterly destroyed from among the people.'

<span class="v-num">24</span> And all the prophets from Samuel and those following, as many as have spoken, also foretold these days.

<span class="v-num">25</span> You are the sons of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying to Abraham, 'And in your seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">26</span> To you first, God, having raised up his servant <span class="added-text">(Jesus)</span>, sent him blessing you, in turning away each one from your wickedness."

---

[^1]: ἀρχηγός는 단순한 창시자가 아니라 '길을 여는 주관자'라는 의미로, 생명의 근원 되신 그리스도를 가리킨다.
[^2]: εἰς τὸ ἐξαλειφθῆναι는 εἰς τό+부정사 목적구문으로 회개·돌이킴의 목적(죄가 지워짐)을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἀποκατάστασις(회복)는 종말론적 만물 회복을 가리키는 핵심 용어로, 선지자들의 예언 성취라는 문맥에서 이해해야 한다.
[^4]: 신 18:15 인용으로, 모세와 같은 선지자로 오실 그리스도를 가리킨다.
[^5]: 창 22:18 언약 인용으로, 아브라함 언약의 성취가 그리스도를 통해 이루어짐을 보여준다.
