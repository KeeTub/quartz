<span class="v-num">1</span> And when it came to pass that we, having been parted from them, set sail, having run a straight course we came to Cos, and the <span class="added-text">(day)</span> following to Rhodes, and from there to Patara.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having found a ship crossing over to Phoenicia, having gone aboard we set sail.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And having sighted Cyprus, and having left it on the left <span class="added-text">(hand)</span>, we sailed to Syria, and came down to Tyre; for there the ship was unloading its cargo.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And having found out the disciples, we stayed there seven days; who said to Paul through the Spirit not to go up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But when it came to pass that we had completed the days, having gone forth we journeyed on, all of them accompanying us with wives and children until <span class="added-text">(we were)</span> outside the city, and having knelt down on the shore we prayed.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And having bid farewell to one another, we went up into the ship, and they returned to their own <span class="added-text">(homes)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> But we, having finished the voyage from Tyre, arrived at Ptolemais, and having greeted the brothers we stayed one day with them.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And on the next day those around Paul went forth <span class="added-text">(and)</span> came to Caesarea; and having entered into the house of Philip the evangelist, who was of the seven, we stayed with him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Now this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> had four virgin daughters who prophesied.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And as we stayed <span class="added-text">(there)</span> many days, a certain prophet named Agabus came down from Judea.

<span class="v-num">11</span> and having come to us, and having taken Paul's belt, and having bound his own hands and feet, he said, "Thus says the Holy Spirit, 'The man whose belt this is, thus will the Jews at Jerusalem bind, and will deliver <span class="added-text">(him)</span> into the hands of the Gentiles.'"[^1]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And when we heard these things, we, and those of that place, begged him not to go up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But Paul answered, "What are you doing, weeping and breaking my heart? For I am ready not only to be bound, but also to die at Jerusalem for the name of the Lord Jesus."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And since he would not be persuaded, we were silent, saying, "Let the will of the Lord be done."

<span class="v-num">15</span> And after these days, having packed up, we went up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And some of the disciples from Caesarea went with us also, bringing <span class="added-text">(us)</span> to one with whom we should lodge, a certain Mnason of Cyprus, an early disciple.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And when we had come to Jerusalem, the brothers received us gladly.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And on the following <span class="added-text">(day)</span> Paul went in with us to James, and all the elders were present.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And having greeted them, he related one by one each of the things God had done among the Gentiles through his ministry.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And when they heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span> they glorified the Lord; and they said to him, "You see, brother, how many myriads there are of the Jews who have believed; and they are all zealous for the law.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they have been informed concerning you, that you teach apostasy from Moses to all the Jews who are among the Gentiles, saying that they should not circumcise <span class="added-text">(their)</span> children, nor walk according to the customs.

<span class="v-num">22</span> What then is it? A multitude must certainly come together; for they will hear that you have come.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Therefore do this that we say to you: We have four men having a vow on themselves;

<span class="v-num">24</span> take these and purify yourself with them, and pay their expenses, that they may shave <span class="added-text">(their)</span> heads; and all will know that there is nothing in what they were informed concerning you, but <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you yourself also walk orderly, keeping the law.[^2]

<span class="v-num">25</span> But concerning the Gentiles who have believed, we have written, having judged <span class="added-text">(that)</span> they should observe no such thing, except that they keep themselves from what is sacrificed to idols, and blood, and what is strangled, and fornication."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Then Paul, having taken the men, on the next day, having been purified with them, went into the temple, declaring the fulfillment of the days of purification, until the offering was offered for each one of them.

<span class="v-num">27</span> But when the seven days were about to be completed, the Jews from Asia, having seen him in the temple, stirred up all the crowd, and laid hands on him,

<span class="v-num">28</span> crying out, "Men of Israel, help! This is the man who teaches all everywhere against the people and the law and this place; and moreover he also brought Greeks into the temple, and has defiled this holy place."[^3]

<span class="v-num">29</span> For they had previously seen Trophimus the Ephesian with him in the city, whom they supposed that Paul had brought into the temple.[^4]

<span class="v-num">30</span> And the whole city was moved, and there was a running together of the people; and having laid hold of Paul, they dragged him out of the temple; and immediately the doors were shut.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And as they were seeking to kill him, a report went up to the <span class="alt-label">chiliarch</span><span class="alt-text">(=commander)</span> of the cohort, that all Jerusalem was in confusion;

<span class="v-num">32</span> who at once, having taken soldiers and centurions, ran down to them. And they, having seen the chiliarch and the soldiers, stopped beating Paul.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Then the chiliarch, having come near, took hold of him, and commanded <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to be bound with two chains; and he inquired who he might be, and what he had done.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And some were shouting one thing, some another, in the crowd; and since he could not know the certainty because of the uproar, he commanded him to be brought into the barracks.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And when he came upon the stairs, it came about that he was carried by the soldiers because of the violence of the crowd.

<span class="v-num">36</span> For the multitude of the people were following, crying out, "Away with him!"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And as Paul was about to be brought into the barracks, he says to the chiliarch, "Is it permitted for me to say something to you?" And he said, "Do you know Greek?

<span class="v-num">38</span> Are you then not the Egyptian who before these days stirred up a revolt and led out into the wilderness the four thousand men of the assassins?"

[^1]: 아가보의 띠를 묶는 행동은 구약 선지자들의 상징적 예언 행위(겔 4장 등)와 같은 전통을 따른다.
[^2]: ἵνα ξυρήσωνται는 목적을 나타내는 ἵνα+가정법 구문으로 '머리를 밀게 하려' 함을 뜻한다.
[^3]: κεκοίνωκε는 완료시제로 그 더럽힘의 결과가 지속됨을 강조하여 고발자들의 확신을 드러낸다.
[^4]: ἐνόμιζον은 미완료시제로 그들의 잘못된 추측이 지속되었음을 나타낸다(실제로는 사실이 아니었음).
[^5]: Ἑβραΐδι διαλέκτῳ(히브리 방언)는 당시 실제로는 아람어를 가리키는 관용적 표현으로 보는 것이 일반적이다.
