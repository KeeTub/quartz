<span class="v-num">1</span> And as they were speaking to the people, the priests and the captain of the temple and the Sadducees came upon them,

<span class="v-num">2</span> being greatly troubled because they taught the people and proclaimed in Jesus the resurrection <span class="added-text">(from the)</span> dead;

<span class="v-num">3</span> and they laid hands on them, and put <span class="added-text">(them)</span> in custody until the next day, for it was already evening.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But many of those who heard the word believed; and the number of the men became about five thousand.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And it came to pass on the next day, that their rulers and elders and scribes were gathered together in Jerusalem,

<span class="v-num">6</span> and Annas the high priest, and Caiaphas, and John, and Alexander, and as many as were of the high priest's kindred.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And having set them in the midst, they inquired, "By what power or by what name did you do this?"

<span class="v-num">8</span> Then Peter, filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, said to them, "Rulers of the people, and elders of Israel,

<span class="v-num">9</span> if we this day are examined concerning a good deed done to an infirm man, by what means this man has been made whole,

<span class="v-num">10</span> let it be known to all of you, and to all the people of Israel, that in the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom you crucified, whom God raised from the dead, in this <span class="added-text">(name)</span> this man stands here before you whole.

<span class="v-num">11</span> This is the stone which was despised by you <span class="added-text">(the)</span> builders, which has become the head of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> corner.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And there is salvation in no one else, for neither is there another name under heaven given among men, by which we must be saved."

<span class="v-num">13</span> Now beholding the boldness of Peter and John, and having perceived that they were unlearned and untrained men, they marveled, and they recognized them, that they had been with Jesus;

<span class="v-num">14</span> And seeing the man who had been healed standing with them, they had nothing to say against <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But having commanded them to go outside the council, they conferred among themselves,

<span class="v-num">16</span> saying, "What shall we do to these men? For indeed that a known sign has been done through them <span class="added-text">(is)</span> evident to all who dwell in Jerusalem, and we cannot deny <span class="added-text">(it)</span>;

<span class="v-num">17</span> but so that it may spread no further among the people, let us threaten them with a threat, to speak no more to anyone in this name."[^1]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And having called them, they commanded them not to speak at all nor teach in the name of Jesus.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Peter and John answered and said to them, "Whether it is right in the sight of God to listen to you more than to God, judge <span class="added-text">(for yourselves)</span>.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For we cannot but speak the things which we saw and heard."

<span class="v-num">21</span> So when they had further threatened them, they let them go, finding no way to punish them, because of the people, since all glorified God for what had happened.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For the man on whom this sign of healing had been performed was more than forty years old.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having been released, they went to their own <span class="added-text">(people)</span>, and reported all that the chief priests and the elders had said to them.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And when they heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they lifted up <span class="added-text">(their)</span> voice to God with one accord, and said, "Master, you <span class="added-text">(are the)</span> God who made the heaven and the earth and the sea, and all that is in them,

<span class="v-num">25</span> who through the mouth of your servant David have said, 'Why did the nations rage, and the peoples plot vain things?

<span class="v-num">26</span> The kings of the earth took their stand, and the rulers were gathered together, against the Lord, and against his Christ.'"

<span class="v-num">27</span> For truly against your holy servant Jesus, whom you anointed, both Herod and Pontius Pilate, with the Gentiles and the peoples of Israel, were gathered together,

<span class="v-num">28</span> to do whatever your hand and your counsel predetermined to come to pass.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And now, Lord, look upon their threats, and grant to your servants to speak your word with all boldness,

<span class="v-num">30</span> "by your stretching out <span class="added-text">(your)</span> hand to heal, and <span class="added-text">(by)</span> signs and wonders being done through the name of your holy servant Jesus."

<span class="v-num">31</span> And when they had prayed, the place in which they were gathered together was shaken, and they were all filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and spoke the word of God with boldness.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Now the multitude of those who believed were of one heart and one soul, and not even one said that any of the things he possessed was his own, but they had all things in common.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And with great power the apostles gave testimony of the resurrection of the Lord Jesus, and great grace was upon them all.

<span class="v-num">34</span> For neither was there anyone needy among them, for as many as were possessors of lands or houses sold <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and brought the prices of the things that were sold,

<span class="v-num">35</span> and laid <span class="added-text">(them)</span> at the feet of the apostles; and distribution was made to each, as anyone had need.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And Joses, who was called Barnabas by the apostles <span class="added-text">(which is, being translated, Son of <span class="alt-label">Encouragement</span><span class="alt-text">(=Consolation)</span></span>), a Levite, a Cypriot by birth,

<span class="v-num">37</span> having a field, sold <span class="added-text">(it)</span> and brought the money, and laid <span class="added-text">(it)</span> at the feet of the apostles.
