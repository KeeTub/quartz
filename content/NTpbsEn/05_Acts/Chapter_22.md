# Acts 22

<span class="v-num">1</span> "Men, brothers and fathers, hear my defense to you now."

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having heard that he addressed them in the Hebrew dialect, they kept silence the more. And he said,

<span class="v-num">3</span> "I am a Jewish man, born in Tarsus of Cilicia, but brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, trained according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> strictness of the ancestral law, being a zealot for God, as you all are today;[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> who persecuted this Way to the death, binding and delivering to prisons both men and women,

<span class="v-num">5</span> as also the high priest testifies to me, and all the council of elders; from whom also having received letters to the brothers, I was going to Damascus, to bring also the ones being there, having been bound, to Jerusalem, that they might be punished.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And it happened to me, as I was going and drawing near to Damascus about midday, suddenly out of the heaven a <span class="alt-label">sufficient</span><span class="alt-text">(=great)</span> light shone around me;

<span class="v-num">7</span> and I fell to the ground, and heard a voice saying to me, 'Saul, Saul, why do you persecute me?'

<span class="v-num">8</span> And I answered, 'Who are you, Lord?' And he said to me, 'I am Jesus the Nazarene, whom you persecute.'

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the ones being with me saw the light indeed, and became afraid; but they did not <span class="alt-label">hear</span><span class="alt-text">(=understand)</span> the voice of the one speaking to me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> And I said, 'What shall I do, Lord?' And the Lord said to me, 'Having risen, go into Damascus; and there it will be told you concerning all things which have been appointed for you to do.'

<span class="v-num">11</span> And as I could not see because of the glory of that light, being led by the hand by the ones being with me, I came into Damascus.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And a certain Ananias, a devout man according to the law, well testified of by all the Jews dwelling <span class="added-text">(there)</span>,

<span class="v-num">13</span> having come to me and stood by, said to me, 'Brother Saul, look <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=receive sight)</span>.' And I, in that very hour, looked up at him.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he said, 'The God of our fathers has chosen you beforehand to know his will, and to see the Righteous One, and to hear a voice out of his mouth;

<span class="v-num">15</span> for you will be a witness for him to all men of what you have seen and heard.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And now why do you <span class="alt-label">delay</span><span class="alt-text">(=hesitate)</span>? Having risen, be baptized, and wash away your sins, calling upon the name of the Lord.'

<span class="v-num">17</span> And it happened to me, having returned to Jerusalem, and as I was praying in the temple, that I came to be in a trance,

<span class="v-num">18</span> and to see him saying to me, 'Hurry and go out quickly from Jerusalem; because they will not receive your testimony concerning me.'

<span class="v-num">19</span> And I said, 'Lord, they themselves know that I was imprisoning and beating in every synagogue those believing on you;

<span class="v-num">20</span> and when the blood of Stephen your witness was being shed, I myself was also standing by and consenting to his killing, and guarding the garments of those killing him.'

<span class="v-num">21</span> And he said to me, 'Go, because I will send you far away to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles.'"

<span class="v-num">22</span> And they were listening to him until this word, and they raised their voice, saying, "Take such a man away from the earth; for it is not fitting for him to live."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And as they were crying out, and throwing off their garments, and throwing dust into the air,

<span class="v-num">24</span> the <span class="alt-label">chiliarch</span><span class="alt-text">(=commander)</span> commanded him to be brought into the barracks, saying that he should be examined by <span class="alt-label">scourging</span><span class="alt-text">(=whips)</span>, that he might know for what reason they were shouting so against him.[^4]

<span class="v-num">25</span> And as they stretched him out with the <span class="alt-label">thongs</span><span class="alt-text">(=straps)</span>, Paul said to the centurion standing <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, "Is it lawful for you to scourge a man <span class="added-text">(who is)</span> a Roman and uncondemned?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> And the centurion, having heard <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, went and reported to the chiliarch, saying, "Watch what you are about to do; for this man is a Roman."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And the chiliarch came and said to him, "Tell me, are you a Roman?" And he said, "Yes."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And the chiliarch answered, "I acquired this citizenship with a large <span class="alt-label">sum</span><span class="alt-text">(=great price)</span>." And Paul said, "But I was even born <span class="added-text">(a Roman)</span>."

<span class="v-num">29</span> Then immediately those who were about to examine him withdrew from him; and the chiliarch also was afraid, having realized that he was a Roman, and that he had bound him.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And on the next day, wanting to know the <span class="alt-label">certainty</span><span class="alt-text">(=exact matter)</span> of why he was accused by the Jews, he loosed him from the bonds, and commanded the chief priests and all their council to come; and having brought Paul down, he set <span class="added-text">(him)</span> before them.

[^1]: πεπαιδευμένος는 완료 수동 분사로, 철저한 훈련이 이미 이루어져 그 결과가 지속됨을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(τιμωρηθῶσιν)은 목적절로, 결박하여 끌고 온 행동의 의도(형벌을 받게 하려는 목적)를 나타낸다.
[^3]: 여기서 ἀκούω가 대격 φωνὴν을 취해 '음성의 내용을 이해하지 못함'을 뜻하니, 행 9:7에서 속격을 취해 단순히 '소리를 들음'을 나타낸 것과 구별된다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιγνῷ)이 천부장이 채찍질을 명한 목적을 나타낸다.
