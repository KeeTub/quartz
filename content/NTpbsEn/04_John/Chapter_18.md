<span class="v-num">1</span> Having said these things, Jesus went out with his disciples across the <span class="added-text">(Kedron/Cedron)</span> <span class="alt-label">brook</span><span class="alt-text">(=torrent)</span>, where there was a garden, into which he and his disciples entered.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Judas also, the one betraying him, knew the place, because Jesus often gathered there with his disciples.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Judas therefore, having taken the <span class="alt-label">band</span><span class="alt-text">(=cohort)</span> and officers from the chief priests and Pharisees, came there with lanterns and torches and weapons.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Jesus therefore, knowing all the things coming upon him, went out and said to them, "Whom do you seek?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> They answered him, "Jesus the Nazarene." Jesus said to them, "I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>." And Judas also, the one betraying him, was standing with them.[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> When therefore he said to them, "I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>," they went back and fell to the ground.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Again therefore he asked them, "Whom do you seek?" And they said, "Jesus the Nazarene."

<span class="v-num">8</span> Jesus answered, "I told you that I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>; if therefore you seek me, let these <span class="added-text">(men)</span> go <span class="added-text">(their way)</span>,"

<span class="v-num">9</span> <span class="added-text">(this was)</span> so that the word might be fulfilled which he spoke, "Of those whom you have given me, I lost not one of them."[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Simon Peter therefore, having a sword, drew it and struck the high priest's <span class="alt-label">servant</span><span class="alt-text">(=slave)</span> and cut off his right ear; and the <span class="alt-label">servant's</span><span class="alt-text">(=slave's)</span> name was Malchus.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Jesus therefore said to Peter, "Put your sword into the <span class="alt-label">sheath</span><span class="alt-text">(=scabbard)</span>. The cup which the Father has given me, shall I not drink it?"

<span class="v-num">12</span> The <span class="alt-label">band</span><span class="alt-text">(=cohort)</span> therefore and the <span class="alt-label">chiliarch</span><span class="alt-text">(=commander)</span> and the officers of the Jews seized Jesus and bound him,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and led him away to Annas first; for he was father-in-law of Caiaphas, who was high priest of that year.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Now Caiaphas was the one who had advised the Jews that it was expedient <span class="added-text">(that)</span> one man should die <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=on behalf of)</span> the people.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And Simon Peter followed Jesus, and <span class="added-text">(so did)</span> another disciple. Now that disciple was known to the high priest, and entered with Jesus into the <span class="alt-label">courtyard</span><span class="alt-text">(=palace)</span> of the high priest;

<span class="v-num">16</span> but Peter stood at the door outside. The other disciple therefore, who was known to the high priest, went out and spoke to the doorkeeper, and brought Peter in.

<span class="v-num">17</span> The servant <span class="alt-label">girl</span><span class="alt-text">(=maid)</span> who kept the door then said to Peter, "Are you not also <span class="added-text">(one)</span> of this man's disciples?" He said, "I am not."

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now the <span class="alt-label">servants</span><span class="alt-text">(=slaves)</span> and the officers were standing, having made a fire of coals, because it was cold, and they were warming themselves; and Peter was standing with them and warming himself.

<span class="v-num">19</span> The high priest therefore asked Jesus about his disciples, and about his teaching.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Jesus answered him, "I have spoken openly to the world; I always taught in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> synagogue and in the temple, where the Jews always come together, and in secret I spoke nothing.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Why do you ask me? Ask those who have heard <span class="added-text">(me)</span>, what I said to them; behold, these know what I said."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And when he had said these things, one of the officers standing by gave Jesus a <span class="alt-label">slap</span><span class="alt-text">(=blow)</span>, saying, "Do you answer the high priest <span class="alt-label">so</span><span class="alt-text">(=like this)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> Jesus answered him, "If I have spoken <span class="alt-label">wrongly</span><span class="alt-text">(=evil)</span>, bear witness concerning the <span class="alt-label">wrong</span><span class="alt-text">(=evil)</span>; but if well, why do you strike me?"

<span class="v-num">24</span> Annas sent him bound to Caiaphas the high priest.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Now Simon Peter was standing and warming himself. They said therefore to him, "Are you not also <span class="added-text">(one)</span> of his disciples?" He denied <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and said, "I am not."

<span class="v-num">26</span> One of the <span class="alt-label">servants</span><span class="alt-text">(=slaves)</span> of the high priest, being a relative of him whose ear Peter cut off, said, "Did I not see you in the garden with him?"

<span class="v-num">27</span> Peter therefore denied <span class="added-text">(it)</span> again, and immediately a rooster crowed.

<span class="v-num">28</span> They led Jesus therefore from Caiaphas into the <span class="alt-label">praetorium</span><span class="alt-text">(=governor's residence)</span>, and it was early <span class="added-text">(morning)</span>; and they themselves did not enter into the praetorium, so that they might not be defiled, but might eat the passover.[^3]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Pilate therefore went out to them and said, "What accusation do you bring against this man?"

<span class="v-num">30</span> They answered and said to him, "If this man were not an evildoer, we would not have delivered him up to you."

<span class="v-num">31</span> Pilate therefore said to them, "Take him yourselves, and judge him according to your law." The Jews therefore said to him, "It is not lawful for us to put anyone to death,"

<span class="v-num">32</span> <span class="added-text">(this was)</span> so that the word of Jesus might be fulfilled, which he spoke, signifying by what <span class="alt-label">kind</span><span class="alt-text">(=manner)</span> of death he was about to die.[^4]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Pilate therefore entered again into the praetorium, and called Jesus, and said to him, "Are you the king of the Jews?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> Jesus answered him, "Do you say this <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> yourself, or did others tell you concerning me?"

<span class="v-num">35</span> Pilate answered, "Am I a Jew? Your own nation and the chief priests delivered you up to me. What have you done?"

<span class="v-num">36</span> Jesus answered, "My kingdom is not of this world; if my kingdom were of this world, my <span class="alt-label">officers</span><span class="alt-text">(=servants)</span> would have <span class="alt-label">fought</span><span class="alt-text">(=been fighting)</span>, that I might not be delivered up to the Jews; but now my kingdom is not from here."[^5]

<span class="v-num">37</span> Pilate therefore said to him, "Are you not then a king?" Jesus answered, "You say that I am a king. For this I have been born, and for this I have come into the world, that I might bear witness to the truth. Everyone who is of the truth hears my voice."[^6]

<span class="v-num">38</span> Pilate said to him, "What is truth?" And having said this, he went out again to the Jews, and said to them, "I find no <span class="alt-label">fault</span><span class="alt-text">(=guilt)</span> in him.

<span class="v-num">39</span> But you have a custom that I should release <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> you one <span class="added-text">(prisoner)</span> at the passover; do you desire therefore <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I release <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> you the king of the Jews?"[^7]

<span class="v-num">40</span> They cried out therefore again, all <span class="added-text">(of them)</span>, saying, "Not this man, but Barabbas!" Now Barabbas was a <span class="alt-label">robber</span><span class="alt-text">(=insurrectionist)</span>.

[^1]: 에고 에이미(나는 ~이다)는 출애굽기 3:14의 하나님의 자기 계시를 반영하는 요한 특유의 표현으로, 이 말씀에 군인들이 뒤로 물러나 땅에 엎드러지는 6절의 반응과 연결된다.
[^2]: ἵνα πληρωθῇ는 목적을 나타내는 절로, 예수께서 앞서(요한복음 17:12) 하신 말씀이 이루어짐을 가리킨다.
[^3]: 유대인들이 이방인의 관정에 들어가면 부정하게 되어 유월절 식사에 참여할 수 없다고 여겨 들어가지 않았다.
[^4]: 이는 예수께서 자신이 십자가에 들려 죽으실 것을 미리 말씀하신 것(요한복음 12:32-33)이 이루어짐을 가리키는 목적절이다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(παραδοθῶ)은 목적절로, '내가 넘겨지지 않도록'이라는 부하들의 싸움의 목적을 나타낸다. 실제로는 싸우지 않았으므로 이 목적은 이루어지지 않은 가상의 상황을 전제한다.
[^6]: ἵνα μαρτυρήσω τῇ ἀληθείᾳ는 예수께서 세상에 오신 목적을 나타내는 절로, 그의 사명이 진리 증언에 있음을 보여준다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπολύσω)이 συνήθεια(관례)의 내용을 설명하는 목적절/동격절로 쓰여 '~하는 관례'라는 뜻을 이룬다. 즉 관례가 지향하는 목적·내용을 나타낸다.
