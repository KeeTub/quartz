<span class="v-num">1</span> Now on the first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of the <span class="alt-label">Sabbaths</span><span class="alt-text">(=week)</span>, Mary Magdalene came early, while it was still dark, to the tomb, and saw the stone taken away from the tomb.

<span class="v-num">2</span> So she ran and came to Simon Peter and to the other disciple whom Jesus loved, and said to them, "They took the Lord out of the tomb, and we do not know where they laid him."

<span class="v-num">3</span> So Peter went out, and the other disciple, and they were going to the tomb.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And the two were running together, and the other disciple ran ahead faster than Peter, and came first to the tomb,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and having stooped down <span class="added-text">(to look)</span>, he saw the linen cloths lying <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, yet he did not go in.

<span class="v-num">6</span> So Simon Peter also came, following him, and went into the tomb, and saw the linen cloths lying <span class="added-text">(there)</span>,

<span class="v-num">7</span> and the napkin, that was on his head, not lying with the linen cloths, but folded up in a place by itself.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Then therefore the other disciple also went in, who had come first to the tomb, and he saw, and believed;

<span class="v-num">9</span> for they did not yet know the Scripture, that he must rise again from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> So the disciples went away again to their own <span class="added-text">(homes)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But Mary stood at the tomb weeping outside. So as she was weeping, she stooped down <span class="added-text">(to look)</span> into the tomb,

<span class="v-num">12</span> and saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head, and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had lain.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And they said to her, "Woman, why do you weep?" She said to them, "Because they took away my Lord, and I do not know where they laid him."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And having said these things, she turned around, and saw Jesus standing, and did not know that it was Jesus.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Jesus said to her, "Woman, why do you weep? Whom do you seek?" She, supposing that he was the gardener, said to him, "Sir, if you have carried him away, tell me where you have laid him, and I will take him away."

<span class="v-num">16</span> Jesus said to her, "Mary." Having turned, she said to him, "Rabboni," <span class="added-text">(which means, Teacher)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Jesus said to her, "Do not <span class="alt-label">touch</span><span class="alt-text">(=hold onto)</span> me, for I have not yet ascended to my Father; but go to my brothers, and say to them, 'I ascend to my Father and your Father, and <span class="added-text">(to)</span> my God and your God.'"[^3]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Mary Magdalene came, announcing to the disciples that she had seen the Lord, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he had said these things to her.

<span class="v-num">19</span> So when it was evening, on that day, the first <span class="added-text">(day)</span> of the <span class="alt-label">Sabbaths</span><span class="alt-text">(=week)</span>, and the doors having been shut, where the disciples were gathered for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst, and said to them, "Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And having said this, he showed them <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands and <span class="added-text">(his)</span> side. So the disciples rejoiced, having seen the Lord.

<span class="v-num">21</span> So Jesus said to them again, "Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you; as the Father has sent me, I also send you."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And having said this, he breathed on <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and said to them, "Receive <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.[^4]

<span class="v-num">23</span> If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven them; if you retain <span class="added-text">(the sins)</span> of any, they are retained."[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> But Thomas, one of the twelve, called <span class="alt-label">Didymus</span><span class="alt-text">(=Twin)</span>, was not with them when Jesus came.

<span class="v-num">25</span> So the other disciples said to him, "We have seen the Lord." But he said to them, "Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails, and put my finger into the mark of the nails, and put my hand into his side, I will not believe."[^6]

<span class="v-num">26</span> And after eight days, again his disciples were inside, and Thomas with them. Jesus came, the doors having been shut, and stood in the midst, and said, "Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you."

<span class="v-num">27</span> Then he said to Thomas, "Bring your finger here, and see my hands; and bring your hand, and put <span class="added-text">(it)</span> into my side; and do not be unbelieving, but believing."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And Thomas answered and said to him, "My Lord and my God."[^7]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Jesus said to him, "Because you have seen me, Thomas, you have believed; blessed <span class="added-text">(are)</span> those who have not seen, and <span class="added-text">(yet)</span> have believed."

<span class="v-num">30</span> Many other signs indeed also Jesus did in the presence of his disciples, which are not written in this book;

<span class="v-num">31</span> but these have been written, that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in his name.[^8]

[^1]: δεῖ(반드시 ~해야 한다)는 하나님의 구속사적 필연성을 나타내는 신약 특유의 표현으로, 부활이 우연이 아니라 성경의 계획임을 강조한다.
[^2]: 랍오니(Ραββουνί)는 아람어 존칭으로 단순한 '선생'보다 더 친밀하고 경외에 찬 표현이며, 마리아가 부활하신 주를 인식한 순간의 감격을 담고 있다.
[^3]: ἅπτου는 현재 명령형으로 '이미 하고 있는 행동을 멈추라'는 뜻이니, 마리아가 이미 그를 붙잡고 있었음을 암시하며 '더 이상 붙잡지 말라'는 의미다.
[^4]: ἐνεφύσησε(숨을 불어넣다)는 창세기 2:7 칠십인역에서 하나님이 아담에게 생기를 불어넣으신 장면을 그대로 반영하니, 예수께서 새 창조의 생명을 제자들에게 주심을 암시한다.
[^5]: ἀφίενται와 κεκράτηνται는 완료/현재 수동태로서, 제자들의 선포가 이미 하늘에서 확정된 하나님의 심판을 땅에서 드러내는 것임을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(부정어 μή와 함께)으로 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 조건을 제시하는 3류 조건문이다. 도마가 자신의 직접적 확인 없이는 믿지 않겠다는 강한 의심과 조건을 강조한다.
[^7]: ὁ Κύριός μου καὶ ὁ Θεός μου에서 관사(ὁ)가 각각 붙어 있어 도마가 예수를 단순한 존칭이 아니라 온전한 신적 존재로 고백하는 표현이다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(πιστεύσητε, ἔχητε)이 이 책 기록의 목적을 나타내니, 믿음과 생명이 요한복음 전체의 저술 목적임을 명시한다.
