<span class="v-num">1</span> And on the third day there was a <span class="alt-label">wedding</span><span class="alt-text">(=marriage feast)</span> in Cana of Galilee, and the mother of Jesus was there.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Jesus also, and his disciples, were called to the wedding.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And when <span class="added-text">(the)</span> wine had <span class="alt-label">failed</span><span class="alt-text">(=run out)</span>, the mother of Jesus said to him, "They have no wine."

<span class="v-num">4</span> Jesus said to her, "What <span class="added-text">(is it)</span> to me and <span class="added-text">(to)</span> <span class="alt-label">you</span><span class="alt-text">(=What have I to do with you)</span>, woman? My hour has not yet come."

<span class="v-num">5</span> His mother said to the servants, "Whatever he says to you, do <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now there were six stone waterpots lying there according to the purification of the Jews, each holding two or three <span class="alt-label">metretes</span><span class="alt-text">(=measures)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Jesus said to them, "Fill the waterpots with water." And they filled them up to the top.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And he said to them, "Draw out now, and carry <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the master of the feast." And they carried <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And as the master of the feast tasted the water that had become wine, and did not know where it was from—but the servants who had drawn the water knew—the master of the feast called the bridegroom,

<span class="v-num">10</span> and said to him, "Every man sets out the good wine first, and when they have drunk <span class="alt-label">freely</span><span class="alt-text">(=become drunk)</span>, then the lesser <span class="added-text">(wine)</span>; you have kept the good wine until now."

<span class="v-num">11</span> This beginning of the signs Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, and manifested his glory; and his disciples believed in him.

<span class="v-num">12</span> After this he went down to Capernaum, he and his mother, and his brothers, and his disciples; and there they stayed not many days.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And the Passover of the Jews was near, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he found in the temple those selling oxen and sheep and doves, and the money-changers sitting.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And having made a whip out of cords, he cast all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the coins of the money-changers, and overturned the tables;

<span class="v-num">16</span> and to those selling the doves he said, "Take these away from here; do not make my Father's house a house of merchandise."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And his disciples remembered that it is written, 'The zeal of your house has <span class="alt-label">consumed</span><span class="alt-text">(=will consume)</span> me.'

<span class="v-num">18</span> The Jews therefore answered and said to him, "What sign do you show us, that you do these things?"

<span class="v-num">19</span> Jesus answered and said to them, "Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up."

<span class="v-num">20</span> The Jews therefore said, "This temple was built in forty-six years, and will you raise it up in three days?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> But that one spoke concerning the temple of his body.

<span class="v-num">22</span> When therefore he was raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, his disciples remembered that he said this to them; and they believed the scripture, and the word which Jesus spoke.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Now as he was in Jerusalem at the Passover, at the feast, many believed in his name, beholding his signs which he was doing.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But Jesus himself did not entrust himself to them, because he knew all <span class="added-text">(men)</span>;

<span class="v-num">25</span> and because he had no need that anyone should testify concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man.[^1]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(μαρτυρήσῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, '누가 증언할 필요'의 내용을 나타낸다. 예수님이 사람의 증언이 필요치 않으셨던 목적(이유)을 설명하는 구문이다.
