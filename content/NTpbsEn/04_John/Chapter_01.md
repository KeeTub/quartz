<span class="v-num">1</span> In <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning was the Word, and the Word was <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> God, and the Word was God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> This one was in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> God.

<span class="v-num">3</span> All things came into being through him, and without him not even one thing came into being that has come into being.

<span class="v-num">4</span> In him was life, and the life was the light of men,

<span class="v-num">5</span> And the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness did not <span class="alt-label">overcome</span><span class="alt-text">(=comprehend)</span> it.

<span class="v-num">6</span> There was a man sent from God, whose name <span class="added-text">(was)</span> John.

<span class="v-num">7</span> This one came for testimony, that he might testify concerning the light, that all might believe through him.[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> That one was not the light, but <span class="added-text">(came)</span> that he might testify concerning the light.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> <span class="added-text">(This)</span> was the true light, which enlightens every man coming into the world.

<span class="v-num">10</span> He was in the world, and the world came into being through him, and the world did not know him.

<span class="v-num">11</span> He came to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> own <del class="deleted-text">[things/home]</del>, and <span class="added-text">(his)</span> own <span class="added-text">(people)</span> did not receive him.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But as many as received him, to them he gave <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right/authority to become children of God, to those believing in his name,

<span class="v-num">13</span> who were born, not of bloods, nor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, nor of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of a <span class="alt-label">man</span><span class="alt-text">(=husband)</span>, but of God.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And the Word became flesh, and <span class="alt-label">dwelt</span><span class="alt-text">(=tabernacled)</span> among us, and we beheld his glory, glory as of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> only begotten from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, full of grace and truth.[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> John testified concerning him, and cried out, saying, "This was he of whom I said, 'He who comes after me has come before me, for he was before me.'"

<span class="v-num">16</span> And of his fullness we all received, and grace <span class="alt-label">upon</span><span class="alt-text">(=instead of)</span> grace;

<span class="v-num">17</span> For the law was given through Moses; grace and truth came through Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">18</span> No one has ever seen God; the only begotten Son, who is in the bosom of the Father, he has <span class="alt-label">declared</span><span class="alt-text">(=explained)</span> <span class="added-text">(him)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent from Jerusalem priests and Levites, that they might ask him, "Who are you?"[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he confessed, and did not deny, and confessed, "I am not the Christ."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they asked him, "What then? Are you Elijah?" And he says, "I am not." "Are you the Prophet?" And he answered, "No."

<span class="v-num">22</span> They said to him therefore, "Who are you? that we may give an answer to those who sent us. What do you say concerning yourself?"[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> He said, "I <span class="added-text">(am the)</span> voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Make straight the way of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord,' as Isaiah the prophet said."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And the ones sent were from the Pharisees.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And they asked him, and said to him, "Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ, nor Elijah, nor the Prophet?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> John answered them, saying, "I baptize with water, but in the midst of you stands <span class="added-text">(one)</span> whom you do not know,

<span class="v-num">27</span> He it is who comes after me, who has come before me, of whom I am not worthy that I should loose the strap of his sandal."[^8]

<span class="v-num">28</span> These things happened in Bethabara beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing.

<span class="v-num">29</span> The next day John sees Jesus coming to him, and says, "Behold, the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world.[^9]

<span class="v-num">30</span> This is he of whom I said, 'After me comes a man who has come before me, for he was before me.'

<span class="v-num">31</span> And I did not know him; but that he might be revealed to Israel, for this reason I came baptizing with water."[^10]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And John testified, saying, "I have beheld the Spirit descending as a dove from heaven, and it remained upon him.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And I did not know him; but he who sent me to baptize with water, that one said to me, 'Upon whomever you see the Spirit descending and remaining upon him, this is he who baptizes with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit.'

<span class="v-num">34</span> And I have seen, and have testified that this is the Son of God."

<span class="v-num">35</span> Again the next day John was standing, and two of his disciples;

<span class="v-num">36</span> and looking at Jesus walking, he says, "Behold, the Lamb of God."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And the two disciples heard him speaking, and followed Jesus.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And Jesus, having turned, and having seen them following,

<span class="v-num">39</span> says to them, "What do you seek?" And they said to him, "Rabbi" <span class="added-text">(which is translated, being interpreted, Teacher)</span>, "where do you dwell?"

<span class="v-num">40</span> He says to them, "Come and see." They went and saw where he dwelt, and remained with him that day; and it was about the tenth hour.

<span class="v-num">41</span> Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who heard from John, and followed him.

<span class="v-num">42</span> He first finds his own brother Simon, and says to him, "We have found the Messiah" <span class="added-text">(which is, being translated, the Christ)</span>;[^11]

<span class="v-num">43</span> and he brought him to Jesus. And Jesus, having looked at him, said, "You are Simon the son of <span class="alt-label">Jonah</span><span class="alt-text">(=John)</span>; you shall be called Cephas," which is translated <span class="added-text">(as)</span> Peter.

<span class="v-num">44</span> The next day Jesus wanted to go out into Galilee; and he finds Philip, and says to him, "Follow me."

<span class="v-num">45</span> Now Philip was from Bethsaida, of the city of Andrew and Peter.

<span class="v-num">46</span> Philip finds Nathanael, and says to him, "Him of whom Moses wrote in the law, and the prophets, we have found, Jesus the son of Joseph, who is from Nazareth."

<span class="v-num">47</span> And Nathanael said to him, "Can any good thing be from Nazareth?" Philip says to him, "Come and see."

<span class="v-num">48</span> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and says concerning him, "Behold, truly an Israelite, in whom is no deceit."

<span class="v-num">49</span> Nathanael says to him, "From where do you know me?" Jesus answered and said to him, "Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you."

<span class="v-num">50</span> Nathanael answered and says to him, "Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the King of Israel."

<span class="v-num">51</span> Jesus answered and said to him, "Because I said to you, 'I saw you under the fig tree,' do you believe? You shall see greater things than these."

<span class="v-num">52</span> And he says to him, "Truly, truly, I say to you, from now on you shall see the heaven opened, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of Man."

[^1]: 관사 없는 Θεός(하나님)는 말씀의 신적 본질을 질적으로 나타내며, 앞의 관사 있는 τὸν Θεόν은 성부를 인격적으로 가리켜 구별된다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절이 두 번 이어져 요한의 사명(증언)과 그 궁극 목적(믿음)을 순서대로 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μαρτυρήσῃ)은 목적절로, 세례 요한이 온 목적이 '빛에 관하여 증언하기 위함'임을 나타낸다. 그가 빛 자체가 아니라 빛을 증거하는 사명자였음을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^4]: μονογενής(독생자)는 '유일하게 낳은 자'라는 뜻으로 하나님과 아들 사이의 독보적 관계를 강조한다.
[^5]: 다수사본(TR)은 ὁ μονογενὴς υἱός(독생하신 아들)로 읽어 그리스도의 인격적 아들 됨을 강조하며, 일부 사본의 μονογενὴς θεός(독생하신 하나님)와 구별된다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐρωτήσωσιν)은 제사장들과 레위인들을 보낸 목적을 나타낸다 - '그에게 묻기 위하여'라는 의도를 표현한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(δῶμεν)이 이끄는 목적절로, '우리를 보낸 이들에게 대답하기 위해서'라는 뒤이은 질문의 의도·목적을 밝힌다. 아직 이루어지지 않은 의도된 결과를 나타내는 전형적 용법이다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(λύσω)이 ἄξιος를 풀이하는 절로, '~할 자격/가치'의 내용을 나타내는 목적/결과적 용법이다. 세례 요한이 그리스도 앞에서 자신의 극도의 겸손을 표현하는 구문이다.
[^9]: '하나님의 어린양'은 출애굽기 유월절 어린양과 이사야 53장의 속죄양 예언을 배경으로 한 표현이다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(φανερωθῇ)은 목적절로, 세례 요한이 물로 세례를 베푼 목적이 '그리스도를 이스라엘에게 나타내기 위함'임을 밝힌다. 요한의 사역 전체가 자신을 위한 것이 아니라 그리스도를 드러내려는 목적임을 강조한다.
[^11]: 메시아는 히브리어로 '기름부음 받은 자'라는 뜻으로 그리스어 그리스도(Χριστός)와 같은 의미이다.
