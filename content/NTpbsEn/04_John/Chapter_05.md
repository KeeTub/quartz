<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Now there is in Jerusalem, at the sheep <span class="added-text">(gate)</span>, a pool, called in Hebrew Bethesda, having five porches.

<span class="v-num">3</span> In these lay a great multitude of the sick, blind, lame, withered, waiting for the moving of the water.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For an angel went down at a certain season into the pool, and troubled the water; whoever then first, after the troubling of the water, stepped in, was made <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healed)</span> of whatever disease he had.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And a certain man was there, having been in <span class="added-text">(his)</span> infirmity thirty-eight years.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Jesus, having seen him lying <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, and having known that he already had a long time, said to him, "Do you want to become <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healthy)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">7</span> The sick <span class="added-text">(man)</span> answered him, <span class="alt-label">"Sir</span><span class="alt-text">(=Lord)</span>, I have no man, that when the water is troubled, he may put me into the pool; but while I am coming, another steps down before me."[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Jesus said to him, "Rise, take up your <span class="alt-label">bed</span><span class="alt-text">(=mat)</span>, and walk."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And immediately the man became <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healthy)</span>, and took up his <span class="alt-label">bed</span><span class="alt-text">(=mat)</span> and walked. Now it was <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Sabbath on that day.

<span class="v-num">10</span> So the Jews said to the <span class="added-text">(one who)</span> had been healed, "It is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Sabbath; it is not lawful for you to take up the <span class="alt-label">bed</span><span class="alt-text">(=mat)</span>."

<span class="v-num">11</span> He answered them, "The one who made me <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healthy)</span>, he said to me, 'Take up your <span class="alt-label">bed</span><span class="alt-text">(=mat)</span>, and walk.'"

<span class="v-num">12</span> So they asked him, "Who is the man who said to you, 'Take up your <span class="alt-label">bed</span><span class="alt-text">(=mat)</span>, and walk'?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> But the <span class="added-text">(one who)</span> had been healed did not know who he was; for Jesus had <span class="alt-label">withdrawn</span><span class="alt-text">(=moved away)</span>, a crowd being in the place.

<span class="v-num">14</span> After these things Jesus found him in the temple, and said to him, "See, you have become <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healthy)</span>; sin no more, lest something worse happen to you."[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> The man went away, and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had made him <span class="alt-label">well</span><span class="alt-text">(=healthy)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And because of this the Jews persecuted Jesus, and sought to kill him, because he did these things on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Sabbath.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But Jesus answered them, "My Father works until now, and I <span class="added-text">(too)</span> work."

<span class="v-num">18</span> So because of this the Jews sought all the more to kill him, because he not only broke the Sabbath, but also called God his own Father, making himself equal with God.

<span class="v-num">19</span> So Jesus answered and said to them, "Truly, truly, I say to you, the Son can do nothing of himself, unless he sees the Father doing <span class="added-text">(something)</span>; for whatever things that <span class="added-text">(one)</span> does, these the Son also does likewise.[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For the Father loves the Son, and shows him all things which he himself does; and greater works than these he will show him, that you may marvel.[^5]

<span class="v-num">21</span> For as the Father raises the dead and gives <span class="added-text">(them)</span> life, so also the Son gives life to whomever he wills.

<span class="v-num">22</span> For neither does the Father judge anyone, but has given all judgment to the Son;

<span class="v-num">23</span> that all may honor the Son, just as they honor the Father. The one not honoring the Son does not honor the Father who sent him.[^6]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, that the one hearing my word, and believing the one who sent me, has eternal life; and does not come into judgment, but has passed from death into life.[^7]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hour is coming, and now is, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and the ones hearing will live.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For as the Father has life in himself, so he has given to the Son also to have life in himself;

<span class="v-num">27</span> and he has given him authority to execute judgment also, because he is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of Man.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Do not marvel <span class="added-text">(at)</span> this; for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hour is coming, in which all who are in the tombs will hear his voice,

<span class="v-num">29</span> and will come forth, the ones having done good things, into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> resurrection of life; but the ones having practiced evil things, into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> resurrection of judgment.

<span class="v-num">30</span> I can do nothing of myself. As I hear, I judge; and my judgment is <span class="alt-label">just</span><span class="alt-text">(=righteous)</span>; because I do not seek my own will, but the will of the Father who sent me.

<span class="v-num">31</span> If I bear witness concerning myself, my <span class="alt-label">witness</span><span class="alt-text">(=testimony)</span> is not true.[^8]

<span class="v-num">32</span> There is another who bears witness concerning me, and I know that the <span class="alt-label">witness</span><span class="alt-text">(=testimony)</span> which he witnesses concerning me is true.

<span class="v-num">33</span> You have sent to John, and he has borne witness to the truth;

<span class="v-num">34</span> But I do not receive <span class="alt-label">witness</span><span class="alt-text">(=testimony)</span> from man, but I say these things that you may be saved.[^9]

<span class="v-num">35</span> He was the lamp burning and shining, and you were willing to rejoice for an hour in his light.

<span class="v-num">36</span> But I have the <span class="alt-label">witness</span><span class="alt-text">(=testimony)</span> greater than <span class="added-text">(that of)</span> John; for the works which the Father gave me to <span class="alt-label">complete</span><span class="alt-text">(=finish)</span>, these very works which I do, bear witness concerning me, that the Father has sent me.[^10]

<span class="v-num">37</span> And the Father who sent me, he has borne witness concerning me. You have neither heard his voice at any time, nor seen his form.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And you do not have his word abiding in you, because the one whom he sent, this one you do not believe.

<span class="v-num">39</span> You search the scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and these are the ones bearing witness concerning me;[^11]

<span class="v-num">40</span> But you are not willing to come to me, that you may have life.[^12]

<span class="v-num">41</span> I do not receive glory from men;

<span class="v-num">42</span> But I have known you, that you do not have the love of God in yourselves.

<span class="v-num">43</span> I have come in the name of my Father, and you do not receive me; if another comes in his own name, him you will receive.[^13]

<span class="v-num">44</span> How can you believe, receiving glory from one another, and the glory that is from the only God you do not seek?

<span class="v-num">45</span> Do not think that I will accuse you to the Father; there is one accusing you, Moses, in whom you have hoped.

<span class="v-num">46</span> For if you believed Moses, you would believe me; for he wrote concerning me.[^14]

<span class="v-num">47</span> But if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe my words?"

[^1]: 이 절은 후대 다수 사본에는 없어 현대 역본 상당수가 생략하나, TR(Scrivener)에는 포함되어 있으므로 본문에 둔다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(βάλλῃ)으로 '나를 넣어줄' 목적을 나타내는 목적절이며, 앞의 ἄνθρωπον οὐκ ἔχω(사람이 없다)의 의도/용도를 설명한다. 즉 '~하기 위해 넣어 줄 사람이 없다'는 의미로, 아직 이루어지지 않은 소망 섞인 필요를 표현한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)은 부정 목적절로, '더 심한 일이 생기지 않도록' 하려는 의도를 나타낸다. 죄를 그치라는 명령의 목적/결과를 설명한다.
[^4]: ἐὰν μή τι βλέπῃ는 3인칭 단수 가정법을 사용한 부정 조건절로, '보지 않는 한'이라는 반복적·일반적 원칙을 나타낸다. 아들이 아버지를 보는 것이 항상 행동의 전제 조건임을 강조하는 현재 가정법 구문이다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(θαυμάζητε)은 목적절로 '너희가 놀라게 하려고'라는 의도를 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(τιμῶσι)은 목적절이다.
[^7]: μεταβέβηκεν(완료형)은 이미 완료되어 지속되는 상태를 나타내어 '이미 옮겨졌다'는 확정된 결과를 강조한다.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(μαρτυρῶ)의 3급 조건문으로 가상의 상황을 전제하여 논증한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(σωθῆτε)은 목적절이다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(τελειώσω)은 목적절로 '내가 그것들을 완성하게 하려고'라는 뜻이다.
[^11]: 여기서 '성경'은 구약을 가리키며, 그리스도를 증언하는 것으로 지시된다.
[^12]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχητε)은 목적절이다.
[^13]: ἐὰν+가정법(ἔλθῃ)의 3급 조건문으로 미래의 가능성을 가정한다.
[^14]: εἰ+미완료(ἐπιστεύετε)는 현재 사실에 반하는 조건문으로, 실제로 그들이 모세를 믿지 않음을 전제한다.
