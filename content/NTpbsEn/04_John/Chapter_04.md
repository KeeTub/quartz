# John 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> So when the Lord knew that the Pharisees had heard that Jesus was making and baptizing more disciples than John,

<span class="v-num">2</span> <span class="added-text">(Although Jesus himself was not baptizing, but his disciples (were)</span>,)

<span class="v-num">3</span> He left Judea, and went away again into Galilee.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And it was necessary for him to pass through Samaria.

<span class="v-num">5</span> So he came to a city of Samaria called Sychar, near the plot <span class="added-text">(of land)</span> which Jacob gave to Joseph his son.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And there was Jacob's well there. Jesus therefore, having been wearied from the journey, sat thus by the well. It was about the sixth hour.

<span class="v-num">7</span> A woman came out of Samaria to draw water. Jesus said to her, "Give me <span class="added-text">(something)</span> to drink."

<span class="v-num">8</span> For his disciples had gone away into the city, that they might buy food.[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> The Samaritan woman therefore said to him, "How <span class="added-text">(is it that)</span> you, being a Jew, ask <span class="added-text">(a)</span> drink from me, being a Samaritan woman?" For Jews do not associate with Samaritans.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Jesus answered and said to her, "If you knew the gift of God, and who it is who says to you, 'Give me <span class="added-text">(something)</span> to drink,' you would have asked him, and he would have given you living water."

<span class="v-num">11</span> The woman said to him, "Sir, you have nothing to draw with, and the well is deep. From where then do you have the living water?

<span class="v-num">12</span> Are you greater than our father Jacob, who gave us the well, and he himself drank of it, and his sons, and his cattle?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> Jesus answered and said to her, "Everyone who drinks of this water will thirst again,

<span class="v-num">14</span> but whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never thirst forever, but the water that I will give him will become in him a spring of water welling up into eternal life."[^2]

<span class="v-num">15</span> The woman said to him, "Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come here to draw."[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Jesus said to her, "Go, call your husband, and come here."

<span class="v-num">17</span> The woman answered and said, "I do not have a husband." Jesus said to her, "You have rightly said, 'I do not have a husband,'

<span class="v-num">18</span> for you have had five husbands, and the one you now have is not your husband. This you have said truly."

<span class="v-num">19</span> The woman said to him, "Sir, I perceive that you are a prophet.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Our fathers worshiped on this mountain, and you say that in Jerusalem is the place where it is necessary to worship."

<span class="v-num">21</span> Jesus said to her, "Woman, believe me, an hour is coming when neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem will you worship the Father.

<span class="v-num">22</span> You worship what you do not know; we worship what we know, for salvation is from the Jews.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But an hour is coming, and now is, when the true worshipers will worship the Father in spirit and truth, for indeed the Father seeks such <span class="added-text">(people)</span> to worship him.[^4]

<span class="v-num">24</span> God is Spirit, and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth."

<span class="v-num">25</span> The woman said to him, "I know that Messiah is coming" <span class="added-text">(who is called Christ)</span>; "when he comes, he will declare to us all things."

<span class="v-num">26</span> Jesus said to her, "I who speak to you am he."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And at this his disciples came, and marveled that he was speaking with a woman; yet no one said, "What do you seek?" or, "Why do you speak with her?"

<span class="v-num">28</span> The woman then left her water jar, and went away into the city, and said to the people,

<span class="v-num">29</span> "Come, see a man who told me all things that I did. Is this not the Christ?"

<span class="v-num">30</span> They went out of the city, and were coming to him.

<span class="v-num">31</span> In the meanwhile the disciples were asking him, saying, "Rabbi, eat."

<span class="v-num">32</span> But he said to them, "I have food to eat that you do not know <span class="added-text">(of)</span>."

<span class="v-num">33</span> The disciples therefore said to one another, "Has someone brought him <span class="added-text">(something)</span> to eat?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> Jesus said to them, "My food is that I do the will of him who sent me, and finish his work.[^5]

<span class="v-num">35</span> Do you not say, 'There are yet four months, and the harvest comes'? Behold, I say to you, lift up your eyes and look at the fields, that they are already white for harvest.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And he who reaps receives wages, and gathers fruit for eternal life, that both he who sows and he who reaps may rejoice together.[^6]

<span class="v-num">37</span> For in this the saying is true, that one is the sower, and another is the reaper.

<span class="v-num">38</span> I sent you to reap that for which you have not labored; others have labored, and you have entered into their labor."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And out of that city many of the Samaritans believed in him because of the word of the woman who testified, "He told me all things that I did."

<span class="v-num">40</span> So when the Samaritans came to him, they asked him to stay with them, and he stayed there two days.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And many more believed because of his word,

<span class="v-num">42</span> and they said to the woman, "No longer do we believe because of your speaking, for we ourselves have heard, and know that this is truly the Savior of the world, the Christ."

<span class="v-num">43</span> And after the two days he went out from there, and went away into Galilee.

<span class="v-num">44</span> For Jesus himself testified that a prophet has no honor in his own homeland.

<span class="v-num">45</span> So when he came into Galilee, the Galileans received him, having seen all the things he did in Jerusalem at the feast, for they themselves also went to the feast.

<span class="v-num">46</span> Jesus therefore came again into Cana of Galilee, where he made the water wine. And there was a certain <span class="alt-label">nobleman</span><span class="alt-text">(=royal official)</span>, whose son was sick at Capernaum.

<span class="v-num">47</span> This one, having heard that Jesus had come out of Judea into Galilee, went away to him, and was asking him that he would come down and heal his son, for he was about to die.[^7]

<span class="v-num">48</span> Jesus therefore said to him, "Unless you see signs and wonders, you will not believe at all."[^8]

<span class="v-num">49</span> The nobleman said to him, "Sir, come down before my child dies."

<span class="v-num">50</span> Jesus said to him, "Go your way; your son lives." And the man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him, and went his way.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And as he was already going down, his servants met him, and told him, saying, "Your child lives."

<span class="v-num">52</span> So he inquired of them the hour in which he got better. And they said to him, "Yesterday at the seventh hour the fever left him."

<span class="v-num">53</span> So the father knew that it was at that hour in which Jesus said to him, "Your son lives." And he himself believed, and his whole household.

<span class="v-num">54</span> This again <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> second sign Jesus did, having come out of Judea into Galilee.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀγοράσωσι)은 제자들이 도시에 들어간 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: '솟아나는 샘'은 생명수의 내적·지속적 근원됨을 강조하는 표현으로, 요한복음 전체의 생명 신학과 연결된다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(διψῶ, ἔρχωμαι)이 여자가 청하는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: '영과 진리로'는 특정 성전 장소가 아니라 하나님의 본성(영)에 상응하는 참된 예배의 성격을 가리킨다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῶ, τελειώσω)은 '나의 양식'이 무엇인지를 설명하는 동격적 목적절이다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(χαίρῃ)이 뿌리는 자와 거두는 자가 함께 기뻐하는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(καταβῇ, ἰάσηται)은 요청의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, '내려오셔서 고쳐주시기를' 청한 간구의 목적을 표현한다.
[^8]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법(ἴδητε)은 아직 이루어지지 않은 미래 조건을 나타내는 3급 조건문이다.
