<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things Jesus went away across the sea of Galilee, <span class="added-text">(that is,)</span> of Tiberias.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And a great crowd followed him, because they saw his signs which he was doing on the ones being sick.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And Jesus went up into the mountain, and there he sat with his disciples.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And the Passover, the feast of the Jews, was near.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Then Jesus, having lifted up <span class="added-text">(his)</span> eyes, and having seen that a great crowd was coming to him, said to Philip, "From where shall we buy loaves, that these may eat?"[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But this he said testing him, for he himself knew what he was about to do.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Philip answered him, "Two hundred denarii worth of loaves are not sufficient for them, that each of them may take a little."[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, said to him,

<span class="v-num">9</span> "There is one lad here, who has five barley loaves and two fish; but what are these for so <span class="alt-label">many</span><span class="alt-text">(=this many people)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> But Jesus said, "Make the people sit down." Now there was much grass in the place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And Jesus took the loaves, and having given thanks, distributed <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to the disciples, and the disciples to the ones reclining; likewise also from the fish, as much as they wanted.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And when they were filled, he said to his disciples, "Gather the fragments that remain, that nothing may be lost."[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> So they gathered <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and filled twelve baskets with fragments from the five barley loaves, which remained over to the ones who had eaten.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Then the men, having seen the sign which Jesus did, said, "This is truly the prophet who is coming into the world."

<span class="v-num">15</span> Jesus therefore, knowing that they were about to come and seize him, that they might make him king, withdrew again to the mountain, himself alone.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And when evening came, his disciples went down to the sea,

<span class="v-num">17</span> and having gotten into the boat, they were going across the sea to Capernaum. And it was already dark, and Jesus had not come to them,

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the sea was rising because a great wind was blowing.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Having rowed then about twenty-five or thirty stadia, they saw Jesus walking on the sea, and coming near the boat; and they were afraid.

<span class="v-num">20</span> But he said to them, "<span class="added-text">(It is)</span> I; do not be afraid."

<span class="v-num">21</span> They wanted then to take him into the boat, and immediately the boat was at the land to which they were going.

<span class="v-num">22</span> The next day, the crowd that was standing on the other side of the sea, having seen that there was no other small boat there except that one which his disciples had entered, and that Jesus had not gone with his disciples into the small boat, but <span class="added-text">(that)</span> his disciples had gone away alone,

<span class="v-num">23</span> <span class="added-text">(But)</span> other small boats came from Tiberias near the place where they had eaten the bread, after the Lord had given thanks;

<span class="v-num">24</span> When therefore the crowd saw that Jesus was not there, nor his disciples, they themselves got into the boats, and came to Capernaum, seeking Jesus.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And having found him on the other side of the sea, they said to him, "Rabbi, when did you come here?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> Jesus answered them and said, "Truly, truly I say to you, you seek me, not because you saw signs, but because you ate from the loaves and were filled.

<span class="v-num">27</span> Work not <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the food that perishes, but <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the food that remains to eternal life, which the Son of man will give you; for this one the Father, God, has sealed."[^5]

<span class="v-num">28</span> They said therefore to him, "What shall we do, that we may work the works of God?"[^6]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Jesus answered and said to them, "This is the work of God, that you believe in him whom that one sent."[^7]

<span class="v-num">30</span> They said therefore to him, "What sign then do you do, that we may see and believe you? What do you work?[^8]

<span class="v-num">31</span> Our fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, as it is written, 'He gave them bread out of heaven to eat.'"[^9]

<span class="v-num">32</span> Jesus therefore said to them, "Truly, truly I say to you, Moses has not given you the bread out of heaven; but my Father gives you the true bread out of heaven.

<span class="v-num">33</span> For the bread of God is the one coming down out of heaven, and giving life to the world."

<span class="v-num">34</span> They said therefore to him, "Lord, always give us this bread."

<span class="v-num">35</span> But Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life; the one coming to me shall never hunger, and the one believing in me shall never thirst.[^10]

<span class="v-num">36</span> But I said to you that you have also seen me, and you do not believe.

<span class="v-num">37</span> All that the Father gives me will come to me; and the one coming to me I will never cast out.[^11]

<span class="v-num">38</span> For I have come down from heaven, not that I may do my own will, but the will of him who sent me.[^12]

<span class="v-num">39</span> And this is the will of the Father who sent me, that of all which he has given me I should lose nothing of it, but should raise it up at the last day.[^13]

<span class="v-num">40</span> For this is the will of him who sent me, that everyone who beholds the Son and believes in him should have eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day."[^14]

<span class="v-num">41</span> The Jews therefore murmured concerning him, because he said, "I am the bread that came down out of heaven."

<span class="v-num">42</span> And they said, "Is this not Jesus the son of Joseph, whose father and mother we know? How then does he say, 'I have come down out of heaven'?"

<span class="v-num">43</span> Jesus therefore answered and said to them, "Do not murmur among one another.

<span class="v-num">44</span> No one can come to me, unless the Father who sent me draws him; and I will raise him up at the last day.[^15]

<span class="v-num">45</span> It is written in the prophets, 'And they shall all be taught of God.' Everyone therefore who has heard from the Father, and has learned, comes to me.[^16]

<span class="v-num">46</span> Not that anyone has seen the Father, except the one who is from God; this one has seen the Father.

<span class="v-num">47</span> Truly, truly I say to you, the one believing in me has eternal life.

<span class="v-num">48</span> I am the bread of life.

<span class="v-num">49</span> Your fathers ate the manna in the wilderness, and they died.

<span class="v-num">50</span> This is the bread that comes down out of heaven, that one may eat of it and not die.[^17]

<span class="v-num">51</span> I am the living bread that came down out of heaven; if anyone eats of this bread, he will live forever. And indeed the bread which I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."[^18]

<span class="v-num">52</span> The Jews therefore quarreled with one another, saying, "How can this man give us <span class="added-text">(his)</span> flesh to eat?"

<span class="v-num">53</span> Jesus therefore said to them, "Truly, truly I say to you, unless you eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, you have no life in yourselves.[^19]

<span class="v-num">54</span> The one eating my flesh, and drinking my blood, has eternal life; and I will raise him up at the last day.[^20]

<span class="v-num">55</span> For my flesh is truly food, and my blood is truly drink.

<span class="v-num">56</span> The one eating my flesh, and drinking my blood, abides in me, and I in him.

<span class="v-num">57</span> As the living Father sent me, and I live because of the Father, so also the one eating me, that one also will live because of me.

<span class="v-num">58</span> This is the bread that came down out of heaven; not as your fathers ate, and died; the one eating this bread will live forever."

<span class="v-num">59</span> These things he said in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> synagogue, teaching in Capernaum.

<span class="v-num">60</span> Many therefore of his disciples, having heard, said, "This word is hard; who can hear it?"

<span class="v-num">61</span> But Jesus, knowing in himself that his disciples were murmuring about this, said to them, "Does this <span class="alt-label">offend</span><span class="alt-text">(=cause to stumble)</span> you?

<span class="v-num">62</span> What then if you should behold the Son of man ascending where he was before?[^21]

<span class="v-num">63</span> The Spirit is the one giving life; the flesh profits nothing; the words which I have spoken to you are spirit, and are life.[^22]

<span class="v-num">64</span> But there are some of you who do not believe." For Jesus knew from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning who were the ones not believing, and who was the one about to betray him.

<span class="v-num">65</span> And he said, "Because of this I have said to you, that no one can come to me, unless it has been given to him from <span class="alt-label">my</span><span class="alt-text">(=the)</span> Father."[^23]

<span class="v-num">66</span> From this <span class="added-text">(time)</span> many of his disciples went away to the things <span class="alt-label">behind</span><span class="alt-text">(=turned back)</span>, and no longer walked with him.

<span class="v-num">67</span> Jesus therefore said to the twelve, "Do you also want to go away?"

<span class="v-num">68</span> Simon Peter therefore answered him, "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have <span class="added-text">(the)</span> words of eternal life;

<span class="v-num">69</span> and we have believed and have come to know that you are the Christ, the Son of the living God."

<span class="v-num">70</span> Jesus answered them, "Did I not choose you, the twelve, and one of you is a devil?"

<span class="v-num">71</span> But he spoke of Judas Iscariot, <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Simon; for this one, being one of the twelve, was about to betray him.

[^1]: ἵνα φάγωσιν은 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절로, 예수께서 빌립을 시험하시려는 질문의 의도를 담고 있다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(λάβῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, '각 사람이 조금씩 받도록 하기에도'라는 결과의 목적을 나타낸다. 200데나리온으로도 그 목적을 이루기에 부족하다는 뉘앙스이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μή τι ἀπόληται)은 '아무것도 버려지지 않도록'이라는 목적절로, 조각을 모으라는 명령의 의도를 밝혀준다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιήσωσιν)은 무리가 예수를 억지로 붙잡으려는 '목적'을 나타낸다: 그들이 왕으로 삼으려는 의도가 아직 실현되지 않은 목표임을 보여준다.
[^5]: ἐσφράγισεν(인치다)은 인장을 찍어 소유와 신원을 확증하는 행위로, 아버지께서 아들의 정체성과 권위를 공적으로 인증하셨음을 뜻한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐργαζώμεθα)이 목적절을 이끌어 '행하기 위하여'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 하나님의 일을 행하는 것이 그들 질문의 목표임을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(πιστεύσητε)은 여기서 '하나님의 일'의 내용을 설명하는 동격적 용법이지만 문법적으로 목적절 구문을 취한다. 하나님이 원하시는 목적/의도가 '그가 보내신 이를 믿는 것'임을 강조한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδωμεν, πιστεύσωμέν)은 목적절로, 표적을 요구하는 '보고 믿기 위함'이라는 의도를 나타낸다. 아직 이루어지지 않은 일에 대한 의지적 목적을 강조한다.
[^9]: 시편 78:24 등 광야 만나 전통을 인용한 것으로, 유대인들이 모세의 표적과 예수를 비교하려는 의도가 담겨 있다.
[^10]: ἐγώ εἰμι(나는 ~이다)는 출애굽기 3:14의 하나님 자기계시 어법을 반영하는 요한복음 특유의 표현으로, 예수의 신적 정체성을 드러낸다.
[^11]: οὐ μὴ ἐκβάλω는 부정어 이중 사용(οὐ μή)과 부정과거 가정법이 결합된 강한 미래 부정으로, '결코 결코 내쫓지 아니하리라'는 절대적 확신을 나타낸다.
[^12]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῶ) 목적절로, 그리스도께서 하늘에서 내려오신 목적을 부정(οὐχ)과 대조(ἀλλά)로 설명한다. 자기 뜻이 아닌 보내신 이의 뜻을 행함이 그 목적임을 강조한다.
[^13]: ἵνα...μὴ ἀπολέσω...ἀλλὰ ἀναστήσω는 아버지의 뜻의 내용을 밝히는 목적절로, 성도 보존과 부활의 확실성을 함께 제시한다.
[^14]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχῃ)은 아버지의 뜻의 내용/목적을 나타냄: 아들을 보고 믿는 자가 영생을 '얻게 하는 것'이 바로 그 뜻임을 강조한다.
[^15]: ἑλκύσῃ(이끌다)는 강제적 끌어당김을 뜻하는 동사로, 구원이 전적으로 아버지의 이끄심에 의존함을 강조한다. ἐὰν μή는 부정조건으로 그 필연성을 나타낸다.
[^16]: 이사야 54:13을 인용한 것으로, 종말론적 새 언약 시대에 하나님께서 친히 백성을 가르치실 것이라는 약속이 예수 안에서 성취됨을 보여준다.
[^17]: ἵνα+가정법(φάγῃ, ἀποθάνῃ) 목적절로, 하늘에서 내려온 빵의 목적이 '누구든지 먹고 죽지 않게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다. 이 빵의 존재 이유와 기능을 설명하는 구문이다.
[^18]: ἐάν+가정법(φάγῃ)은 '누구든지 먹으면'이라는 미래에 열려 있는 조건절로, 조건이 충족되는 모든 경우에 결과(영생)가 보편적으로 성립함을 강조한다.
[^19]: ἐὰν μὴ φάγητε...καὶ πίητε는 부정조건문으로 생명 소유의 필수 전제를 강하게 규정한다.
[^20]: τρώγων(먹다, 씹어 먹다)은 φάγω보다 더 생생하고 육체적인 먹는 행위를 나타내는 동사로, 그리스도와의 연합의 실제성을 강조한다.
[^21]: ἐάν+가정법(θεωρῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. '만약 ~을 보게 된다면'이라는 뜻으로, 실제로 일어날 수 있는 상황을 가정하여 청중의 반응을 묻는 수사적 질문의 전제를 이룬다.
[^22]: πνεῦμα와 σάρξ의 대조는 물질적 이해(식인적 오해)를 넘어, 예수의 말씀이 성령을 통해 영적으로 이해되어야 함을 밝힌다.
[^23]: ἐὰν μὴ ᾖ δεδομένον은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 3형 조건절로, '아버지께서 허락하지 않으시면'이라는 뜻이다. 사람이 예수께 나아오는 것은 전적으로 아버지의 주권적 은혜에 달려 있음을 강조한다.
