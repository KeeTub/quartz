<span class="v-num">1</span> Jesus therefore, six days before the Passover, came to Bethany, where was Lazarus who had died, whom he raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.

<span class="v-num">2</span> They therefore made him a supper there, and Martha served, but Lazarus was one of those reclining with him.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Mary therefore, having taken a pound of ointment of <span class="alt-label">pure</span><span class="alt-text">(=genuine)</span>, precious nard, anointed the feet of Jesus and wiped his feet with her hair, and the house was filled with the fragrance of the ointment.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Then one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, Simon's <span class="added-text">(son)</span>, who was about to betray him, said,

<span class="v-num">5</span> "Why was this ointment not sold for three hundred denarii and given to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> poor?"

<span class="v-num">6</span> But he said this, not because he cared about the poor, but because he was a thief, and having the money box, he used to carry away <span class="added-text">(what)</span> was put in <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Jesus therefore said, "Let her alone; she has kept it for the day of my burial.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For the poor you have always with yourselves, but me you do not have always."

<span class="v-num">9</span> A great crowd of the Jews therefore knew that he was there, and they came not on account of Jesus only, but that they might also see Lazarus, whom he raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But the chief priests took counsel that they might kill Lazarus also,[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> because on account of him many of the Jews were going away and were believing in Jesus.

<span class="v-num">12</span> On the next day, the great crowd that had come to the feast, having heard that Jesus was coming to Jerusalem,

<span class="v-num">13</span> took the branches of the palm trees and went out to meet him, and cried out, "Hosanna! Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> he who comes in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, the King of Israel!"

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus, having found a young donkey, sat on it, as it is written,

<span class="v-num">15</span> "Fear not, daughter of Zion; behold, your King comes, sitting on a colt of a donkey."[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> His disciples did not understand these things at first, but when Jesus was glorified, then they remembered that these things were written about him, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> they had done these things to him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> The crowd therefore who was with him when he called Lazarus out of the tomb and raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, bore witness.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For this reason also the crowd went to meet him, because they heard that he had done this sign.

<span class="v-num">19</span> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, "You see that you are gaining nothing; behold, the world has gone after him."

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now there were certain Greeks among those who were going up to worship at the feast,[^4]

<span class="v-num">21</span> these therefore came to Philip, who was from Bethsaida of Galilee, and asked him, saying, "Sir, we desire to see Jesus."

<span class="v-num">22</span> Philip came and told Andrew, and again Andrew and Philip told Jesus.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And Jesus answered them, saying, "The hour has come that the Son of Man should be glorified.[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, unless the grain of wheat, falling into the ground, dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit.[^6]

<span class="v-num">25</span> He who loves his life will lose it, and he who hates his life in this world will keep it to life eternal.

<span class="v-num">26</span> If anyone serves me, let him follow me, and where I am, there my servant will be also; and if anyone serves me, the Father will honor him.[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Now my soul is troubled, and what shall I say? 'Father, save me from this hour'? But for this reason I came to this hour.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Father, glorify your name." Then a voice came from heaven, "I have both glorified <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and will glorify <span class="added-text">(it)</span> again."

<span class="v-num">29</span> The crowd therefore who stood by and heard it said that it had thundered; others said, "An angel has spoken to him."

<span class="v-num">30</span> Jesus answered and said, "This voice has not come for my sake, but for your sake.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Now is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judgment of this world; now the ruler of this world will be cast out.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And I, if I be lifted up from the earth, will draw all <span class="added-text">(people)</span> to myself."[^8]

<span class="v-num">33</span> But he said this, signifying by what death he was about to die.

<span class="v-num">34</span> The crowd answered him, "We have heard from the law that the Christ remains forever; and how do you say, 'The Son of Man must be lifted up'? Who is this Son of Man?"

<span class="v-num">35</span> Jesus therefore said to them, "Yet a little while <span class="added-text">(the)</span> light is with you. Walk while you have the light, lest darkness overtake you; and he who walks in darkness does not know where he goes.[^9]

<span class="v-num">36</span> While you have the light, believe in the light, that you may become sons of light." Jesus spoke these things, and having gone away, hid himself from them.[^10]

<span class="v-num">37</span> But though he had done so many signs before them, they did not believe in him,

<span class="v-num">38</span> that the word of Isaiah the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spoke, "Lord, who has believed our report? And to whom has the arm of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord been revealed?"[^11]

<span class="v-num">39</span> For this reason they could not believe, because Isaiah said again,

<span class="v-num">40</span> "He has blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart, lest they should see with <span class="added-text">(their)</span> eyes, and understand with <span class="added-text">(their)</span> heart, and be converted, and I should heal them."[^12]

<span class="v-num">41</span> Isaiah said these things when he saw his glory, and spoke concerning him.

<span class="v-num">42</span> Nevertheless, even among the rulers many believed in him, but because of the Pharisees they did not confess <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, lest they should be put out of the synagogue,[^13]

<span class="v-num">43</span> for they loved the glory of men more than the glory of God.

<span class="v-num">44</span> But Jesus cried out and said, "He who believes in me, believes not in me, but in him who sent me;

<span class="v-num">45</span> and he who sees me sees him who sent me.

<span class="v-num">46</span> I have come <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a light into the world, that whoever believes in me should not remain in darkness.[^14]

<span class="v-num">47</span> And if anyone hears my words and does not believe, I do not judge him; for I did not come that I should judge the world, but that I should save the world.[^15]

<span class="v-num">48</span> He who rejects me and does not receive my words has <span class="added-text">(one)</span> who judges him; the word which I spoke, that will judge him on the last day.

<span class="v-num">49</span> For I have not spoken from myself, but the Father who sent me, he has given me a command, what I should say and what I should speak;

<span class="v-num">50</span> and I know that his command is eternal life. What I speak therefore, just as the Father has said to me, so I speak."

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδωσιν)은 목적절로, 무리가 온 이유가 나사로를 '보기 위함'이었음을 나타낸다. 예수님뿐 아니라 부활의 기적을 직접 확인하려는 의도가 강조된다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀποκτείνωσιν)은 목적절로, 대제사장들이 '의논한' 목적(나사로도 죽이려는 의도)을 나타낸다. 아직 실행되지 않은 의도된 목표를 가리킨다.
[^3]: 스가랴 9:9의 인용으로, 왕이신 메시아의 겸손한 등장을 예고한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(προσκυνήσωσιν)은 목적절로, 그들이 명절에 올라온 목적이 예배하기 위함이었음을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(δοξασθῇ)이 목적절을 이끌며, '때'(ὥρα)의 내용을 설명하는 동격적 용법으로 쓰였다. 인자가 영광 받는 것이 바로 그 '때'가 이르러야 할 목적/내용임을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἐὰν μὴ ἀποθάνῃ는 아직 실현되지 않은 3급 조건문으로, 죽음이 열매 맺음의 필수 전제임을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(διακονῇ)은 '누구든지 섬기면'이라는 미래 가능성의 조건절로, 아직 결정되지 않은 열린 조건을 나타낸다. 섬김이라는 조건이 충족될 때 따름과 존귀함이라는 결과가 뒤따름을 강조한다.
[^8]: ἐὰν ὑψωθῶ는 미래에 실현될 것으로 예상되는 3급 조건문으로, 십자가의 들림을 가리킨다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(καταλάβῃ) 목적절로, '어둠이 덮치지 않도록'이라는 부정적 목적을 나타낸다. 빛이 있는 동안 걸으라는 명령의 이유(목적)를 제시한다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(γένησθε)은 목적절로, 빛을 믿는 목적이 '빛의 아들이 되는 것'임을 나타낸다. 아직 이루어지지 않았지만 믿음의 결과로 기대되는 목표를 가리킨다.
[^11]: 이사야 53:1의 인용으로 ἵνα는 목적을 나타내며, 이스라엘의 불신이 예언의 성취임을 보여준다.
[^12]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδωσι, νοήσωσι, ἐπιστραφῶσι, ἰάσωμαι)이 이끄는 목적절로, 하나님이 눈멀게 하시고 마음을 완악케 하신 목적/결과를 나타낸다. 부정적 목적(보지 못하고 깨닫지 못하게 하려 함)을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^13]: ἵνα+가정법(γένωνται)은 부정 목적절로, '출교당하지 않기 위하여'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 믿음을 고백하지 못한 이유가 바리새인들로 인한 출교에 대한 두려움임을 강조한다.
[^14]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 예수의 오심의 목적이 믿는 자를 어둠에서 건지는 데 있음을 밝힌다.
[^15]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀκούσῃ, πιστεύσῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가상적 상황을 나타내는 조건절로 '누구든지 ~할지라도'의 뉘앙스이다. 두 ἵνα+가정법(κρίνω, σώσω)은 예수님이 오신 목적을 밝히는 목적절로, 심판이 아닌 구원이 그 목적임을 강조한다.
