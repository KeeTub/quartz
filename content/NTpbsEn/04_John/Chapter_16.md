# John 16

<span class="v-num">1</span> "These things I have spoken to you, that you may not be made to stumble.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> They will make you outcasts from the <span class="alt-label">synagogue</span><span class="alt-text">(=excommunicated)</span>; but an hour comes that everyone killing you will think he offers service to God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And these things they will do to you, because they have not known the Father nor me.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But these things I have spoken to you, that when the hour comes, you may remember them, that I told you <span class="added-text">(of them)</span>. But these things I did not say to you from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning, because I was with you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> But now I go to Him who sent me, and none of you asks me, 'Where do you go?'

<span class="v-num">6</span> But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow has filled your heart.

<span class="v-num">7</span> But I tell you the truth: it is profitable for you that I go away. For if I do not go away, the Comforter will not come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you.[^4]

<span class="v-num">8</span> And having come, that one will convict the world concerning sin, and concerning righteousness, and concerning judgment.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Concerning sin, indeed, because they do not believe in me;

<span class="v-num">10</span> And concerning righteousness, because I go to my Father, and you no longer see me;

<span class="v-num">11</span> And concerning judgment, because the ruler of this world has been judged.

<span class="v-num">12</span> I still have many things to say to you, but you cannot bear <span class="added-text">(them)</span> now.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But when that one comes, the Spirit of truth, he will guide you into all the truth; for he will not speak from himself, but whatever he hears he will speak, and he will announce to you the things coming.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> That one will glorify me, because he will receive from mine, and will announce <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to you.

<span class="v-num">15</span> All things that the Father has are mine; therefore I said that he will receive from mine, and will announce <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to you.

<span class="v-num">16</span> A little while, and you do not see me; and again a little while, and you will see me, because I go to the Father."

<span class="v-num">17</span> Some of his disciples then said to one another, "What is this that he says to us, 'A little while, and you do not see me; and again a little while, and you will see me'; and, 'because I go to the Father'?"

<span class="v-num">18</span> They said therefore, "What is this that he says, 'a little while'? We do not know what he is saying."

<span class="v-num">19</span> Jesus therefore knew that they wished to ask him, and said to them, "Concerning this do you inquire with one another, that I said, 'A little while, and you do not see me; and again a little while, and you will see me'?

<span class="v-num">20</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, that you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice; and you will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will become joy.

<span class="v-num">21</span> The woman, when she gives birth, has sorrow, because her hour has come; but when she has borne the child, she no longer remembers the anguish, because of the joy that a man has been born into the world.

<span class="v-num">22</span> So you also now indeed have sorrow; but again I will see you, and your heart will rejoice, and no one takes your joy from you.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And in that day you will ask me nothing. Truly, truly, I say to you, that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he will give you.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Until now you have asked nothing in my name; ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be made full.[^6]

<span class="v-num">25</span> These things I have spoken to you in <span class="alt-label">figures</span><span class="alt-text">(=parables)</span>; but an hour comes when I will no longer speak to you in figures, but I will announce to you plainly concerning the Father.

<span class="v-num">26</span> In that day you will ask in my name; and I do not say to you that I will ask the Father concerning you;

<span class="v-num">27</span> For the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God.[^7]

<span class="v-num">28</span> I came out from the Father, and have come into the world; again, I leave the world, and go to the Father."

<span class="v-num">29</span> His disciples said to him, "Behold, now you speak plainly, and speak no <span class="alt-label">figure</span><span class="alt-text">(=parable)</span>.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Now we know that you know all things, and have no need that anyone should ask you. By this we believe that you came out from God."[^8]

<span class="v-num">31</span> Jesus answered them, "Do you believe now?

<span class="v-num">32</span> Behold, an hour comes, and now has come, that you will be scattered each to his own <span class="added-text">(place)</span>, and will leave me alone; and I am not alone, because the Father is with me.[^9]

<span class="v-num">33</span> These things I have spoken to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation; but be of good cheer, I have overcome the world."[^10]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(σκανδαλισθῆτε) 목적절로, 예수님이 미리 말씀하신 목적이 제자들의 실족 방지에 있음을 나타낸다. 미래의 시험 앞에서 믿음을 지키게 하려는 의도적 목적을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(δόξῃ)은 '때(ὥρα)'의 내용을 설명하는 동격적 목적절로, 그 때가 어떤 일이 일어날 때인지를 구체적으로 풀어준다. 즉 죽이는 자들이 하나님을 섬긴다고 착각하게 될 것이라는 미래의 확실한 상황을 가리킨다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μνημονεύητε) 목적절로, 예수께서 미리 말씀하신 목적이 '때가 이르렀을 때 기억하게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다. 미래에 일어날 일에 대비해 지금 말해두는 배려의 뉘앙스가 담겨 있다.
[^4]: ἵνα ἐγὼ ἀπέλθω는 목적을 나타내는 부정사적 절이며, ἐὰν μὴ ἀπέλθω는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건문이다.
[^5]: '진리의 영'은 성령의 인격과 사역, 곧 진리를 계시하는 사역을 강조하는 칭호이다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ πεπληρωμένη, 완료 수동 가정법 우언법)이 이끄는 목적절로, 구하고 받는 행위의 궁극적 목적을 나타낸다. '너희 기쁨이 충만하게 되기 위하여'라는 뜻으로, 기도 응답이 지향하는 결과를 강조한다.
[^7]: πεφιλήκατε는 완료시제로 제자들이 예수를 사랑해 온 지속적 상태를 강조한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐρωτᾷ)이 χρείαν ἔχεις의 목적절로 쓰여 '누군가 물어볼 필요'라는 의미를 이룬다. 여기서는 결과적 용법에 가까워 '~할 필요'라는 내용절로 자연스럽게 옮겨진다.
[^9]: ἵνα σκορπισθῆτε는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 절로, 제자들의 흩어짐이 하나님의 섭리 안에 있음을 시사한다.
[^10]: ἵνα+현재가정법(ἔχητε)은 목적절로, 예수님이 이 말씀을 하신 목적이 제자들이 그 안에서 평강을 '누리게' 하려는 것임을 나타낸다. 이는 단회적 사건이 아니라 지속적으로 누리는 평강을 뜻한다.
