<span class="v-num">1</span> Now there was a certain <span class="added-text">(man)</span> sick, Lazarus, from Bethany, of the village of Mary and Martha her sister.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And it was Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment and wiped his feet with her hair, whose brother Lazarus was sick.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Therefore the sisters sent to him, saying, "Lord, behold, he whom you love is sick."

<span class="v-num">4</span> Hearing <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, Jesus said, "This sickness is not <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=unto)</span> death, but for the glory of God, that the Son of God may be glorified through it."[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus.

<span class="v-num">6</span> When therefore he heard that he was sick, then indeed he remained in the place where he was two days.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then after this he said to the disciples, "Let us go into Judea again."

<span class="v-num">8</span> The disciples said to him, "Rabbi, now the Jews were seeking to stone you, and <span class="added-text">(do)</span> you go there again?"

<span class="v-num">9</span> Jesus answered, "Are there not twelve hours of the day? If anyone walks in the day, he does not stumble, because he sees the light of this world.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> But if anyone walks in the night, he stumbles, because the light is not in him."[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> He said these things, and after this he said to them, "Lazarus our friend has fallen asleep, but I go that I may awaken him."[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Then his disciples said, "Lord, if he has fallen asleep, he will recover."

<span class="v-num">13</span> But Jesus had spoken concerning his death, but they thought that he spoke concerning the resting of sleep.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Then therefore Jesus said to them plainly, "Lazarus has died,"

<span class="v-num">15</span> and I rejoice for your sake that I was not there, that you may believe. But let us go to him."[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Then Thomas, who is called <span class="alt-label">Didymus</span><span class="alt-text">(=the Twin)</span>, said to his fellow disciples, "Let us also go, that we may die with him."[^6]

<span class="v-num">17</span> Jesus therefore, having come, found him having been in the tomb already four days.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now Bethany was near Jerusalem, about fifteen stadia <span class="added-text">(off)</span>;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and many of the Jews had come to Martha and Mary, that they might console them concerning their brother.[^7]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Martha therefore, when she heard that Jesus was coming, went to meet him, but Mary was sitting in the house.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Martha therefore said to Jesus, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."

<span class="v-num">22</span> But even now I know that whatever you ask God, God will give you."

<span class="v-num">23</span> Jesus said to her, "Your brother will rise again."

<span class="v-num">24</span> Martha said to him, "I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day."

<span class="v-num">25</span> Jesus said to her, "I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me, though he may die, he will live,"

<span class="v-num">26</span> and everyone living and believing in me shall never die to the <span class="alt-label">age</span><span class="alt-text">(=forever)</span>. Do you believe this?"

<span class="v-num">27</span> She said to him, "Yes, Lord, I have believed that you are the Christ, the Son of God, the one coming into the world."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And having said these things, she went away and called Mary her sister secretly, saying, "The teacher is here and is calling you."

<span class="v-num">29</span> She, when she heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, rose quickly and came to him.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Now Jesus had not yet come into the village, but was in the place where Martha met him.

<span class="v-num">31</span> The Jews therefore who were with her in the house and consoling her, seeing Mary, that she rose up quickly and went out, followed her, saying, "She is going to the tomb, that she may weep there."[^8]

<span class="v-num">32</span> Mary therefore, when she came where Jesus was, seeing him, fell at his feet, saying to him, "Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died."

<span class="v-num">33</span> Jesus therefore, when he saw her weeping, and the Jews who came with her weeping, groaned in the spirit and troubled himself,

<span class="v-num">34</span> and said, "Where have you laid him?" They said to him, "Lord, come and see."

<span class="v-num">35</span> Jesus <span class="alt-label">wept</span><span class="alt-text">(=shed tears)</span>.

<span class="v-num">36</span> The Jews therefore said, "Behold how he loved him."

<span class="v-num">37</span> But some of them said, "Could not this man, who opened the eyes of the blind <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, have made <span class="added-text">(it)</span> that this man also should not die?"[^9]

<span class="v-num">38</span> Jesus therefore, again groaning in himself, came to the tomb. Now it was a cave, and a stone was lying against it.

<span class="v-num">39</span> Jesus said, "Take away the stone." Martha, the sister of the dead <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, said to him, "Lord, by now he stinks, for he is <span class="added-text">(in his)</span> fourth <span class="added-text">(day)</span>."

<span class="v-num">40</span> Jesus said to her, "Did I not say to you that if you believe, you will see the glory of God?"[^10]

<span class="v-num">41</span> Then they took away the stone, where the dead <span class="added-text">(man)</span> was lying. And Jesus lifted up his eyes <span class="alt-label">above</span><span class="alt-text">(=upward)</span>, and said, "Father, I thank you that you heard me."

<span class="v-num">42</span> But I knew that you always hear me, but because of the crowd standing around I said <span class="added-text">(this)</span>, that they may believe that you sent me."[^11]

<span class="v-num">43</span> And having said these things, he cried out with a loud voice, "Lazarus, come forth."

<span class="v-num">44</span> And the dead <span class="added-text">(man)</span> came out, bound <span class="added-text">(in)</span> his feet and hands with wrappings, and his face was wrapped about with a cloth. Jesus said to them, "Loose him, and let <span class="added-text">(him)</span> go."

<span class="v-num">45</span> Many therefore of the Jews who came to Mary, and beheld what Jesus did, believed in him.

<span class="v-num">46</span> But some of them went away to the Pharisees, and told them what Jesus did.

<span class="v-num">47</span> Then the chief priests and the Pharisees gathered a council, and said, "What are we doing? For this man does many signs."

<span class="v-num">48</span> If we let him alone like this, all will believe in him, and the Romans will come and take away both our place and <span class="added-text">(our)</span> nation."[^12]

<span class="v-num">49</span> But a certain one of them, Caiaphas, being high priest that year, said to them, "You know nothing,"

<span class="v-num">50</span> nor do you consider that it is expedient for us that one man should die for the people, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> the whole nation should not perish."[^13]

<span class="v-num">51</span> But this he did not say of himself, but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus would die for the nation,

<span class="v-num">52</span> and not for the nation only, but that he might also gather together into one the children of God who were scattered abroad.[^14]

<span class="v-num">53</span> From that day therefore they took counsel that they might kill him.[^15]

<span class="v-num">54</span> Jesus therefore no longer walked openly among the Jews, but went away from there into the region near the wilderness, into a city called Ephraim, and there he stayed with his disciples.

<span class="v-num">55</span> Now the Passover of the Jews was near, and many went up to Jerusalem out of the country before the Passover, that they might purify themselves.[^16]

<span class="v-num">56</span> They sought therefore Jesus, and spoke among one another, standing in the temple, "What do you think? That he will not come to the feast at all?"

<span class="v-num">57</span> Now both the chief priests and the Pharisees had given a command, that if anyone knew where he was, he should report <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, so that they might seize him.[^17]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법은 목적을 나타냄 — 하나님 아들이 영광 받으려는 목적절.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(περιπατῇ)은 '누구든지 낮에 다니면'이라는 일반적 조건절로, 특정인이 아닌 누구에게나 적용되는 보편적 사실을 가정한다. 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성이 아니라 반복적·일반적 진리를 나타내는 용법이다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법의 3급 조건문 — 낮과 밤의 대조를 통한 일반 원리 진술.
[^4]: ἵνα ἐξυπνίσω — 나사로를 깨우려는 목적 부정사적 목적절.
[^5]: ἵνα πιστεύσητε — 제자들의 믿음을 위한 목적절.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀποθάνωμεν)은 목적절로, '가서 그와 함께 죽기 위하여'라는 뜻을 나타낸다. 도마가 예수와 함께 가려는 목적/의도를 강조한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(παραμυθήσωνται)은 목적절로, 유대인들이 찾아온 목적이 '위로하기 위함'임을 나타낸다. 방문의 의도를 분명히 밝히는 구문이다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(κλαύσῃ)은 목적절로, 마리아가 무덤에 가는 '목적'이 곡하기 위함임을 나타낸다. 유대인들의 추측 속에 담긴 의도를 설명하는 구문이다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(μὴ ἀποθάνῃ)은 ποιῆσαι의 목적절로, '이 사람도 죽지 않게 하다'라는 의도된 결과를 나타낸다. 즉 예수가 나사로의 죽음도 막을 수 있었을 것이라는 안타까움 섞인 반문의 핵심 내용이다.
[^10]: ἐάν πιστεύσῃς — 3급 조건문으로 믿음의 결과를 조건적으로 제시함.
[^11]: ἵνα πιστεύσωσιν — 무리로 하여금 믿게 하려는 목적절.
[^12]: ἐάν+가정법의 3급 조건문 — 미래에 실현 가능한 정치적 위협을 상정함.
[^13]: ἵνα εἷς ἄνθρωπος ἀποθάνῃ — 백성을 위한 대속적 죽음의 목적절, 요한이 신학적 의미로 재해석함.
[^14]: ἵνα...συναγάγῃ — 흩어진 하나님의 자녀를 하나로 모으려는 목적절.
[^15]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀποκτείνωσιν)이 이끄는 목적절로, '의논하다(συνεβουλεύσαντο)'의 목적/내용을 나타낸다. 그를 죽이려는 의도가 그날부터 시작된 계획된 목적임을 강조한다.
[^16]: ἵνα ἁγνίσωσιν — 자신을 정결케 하려는 목적절.
[^17]: ἵνα+가정법(γνῷ, μηνύσῃ)은 명령의 목적/내용절이며, 그 안의 ἐάν τις γνῷ는 '누구든지 알게 되면'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 즉 불특정 다수를 향해 실제로 일어날 수 있는 상황을 전제로 한 지시문이다.
