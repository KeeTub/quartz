<span class="v-num">1</span> Now there was a man from the Pharisees, Nicodemus <span class="added-text">(was)</span> his name, a ruler of the Jews.

<span class="v-num">2</span> This man came to Jesus by night, and said to him, "Rabbi, we know that you have come <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a teacher from God; for no one is able to do these signs which you do, unless God is with him."[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Jesus answered and said to him, "Truly, truly I say to you, unless one is born from <span class="alt-label">above</span><span class="alt-text">(=again)</span>, he is not able to see the kingdom of God."[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Nicodemus said to him, "How is a man able to be born, being old? He is not able to enter into his mother's womb a second <span class="added-text">(time)</span> and be born, is he?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> Jesus answered, "Truly, truly I say to you, unless one is born of water and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, he is not able to enter into the kingdom of God.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> That which has been born of the flesh is flesh, and that which has been born of the Spirit is spirit.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born from above.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear its sound, but you do not know where it comes from and where it goes; so is everyone who has been born of the Spirit."[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Nicodemus answered and said to him, "How can these things be?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> Jesus answered and said to him, "Are you the teacher of Israel, and do not know these things?

<span class="v-num">11</span> Truly, truly I say to you, we speak what we know, and we testify what we have seen; and you do not receive our testimony.

<span class="v-num">12</span> If I told you earthly things, and you do not believe, how will you believe if I tell you heavenly things?[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And no one has ascended into heaven, except he who descended from heaven, the Son of man who is in heaven;[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, so must the Son of man be lifted up,

<span class="v-num">15</span> so that everyone who believes in him would not perish, but would have eternal life.[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, so that everyone who believes in him would not perish, but would have eternal life.[^8]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For God did not send his Son into the world so that he would judge the world, but so that the world would be saved through him.

<span class="v-num">18</span> The one believing in him is not judged; but the one not believing has already been judged, because he has not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And this is the judgment, that the light has come into the world, and men loved the darkness rather than the light, for their deeds were evil.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For everyone who does evil hates the light, and does not come to the light, so that his deeds would not be exposed.

<span class="v-num">21</span> But the one doing the truth comes to the light, so that his deeds would be made manifest, that they have been worked in God."

<span class="v-num">22</span> After these things Jesus and his disciples came into the Judean land, and there he stayed with them and baptized.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And John also was baptizing in Aenon near Salim, because there was much water there; and they came and were baptized.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For John had not yet been thrown into prison.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Then a dispute arose from the disciples of John with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jews about purification.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And they came to John and said to him, "Rabbi, he who was with you beyond the Jordan, to whom you have testified, behold, this one baptizes, and all are coming to him."

<span class="v-num">27</span> John answered and said, "A man is not able to receive anything, unless it has been given to him from heaven.[^9]

<span class="v-num">28</span> You yourselves testify for me that I said, 'I am not the Christ,' but that 'I have been sent before him.'

<span class="v-num">29</span> The one having the bride is the bridegroom; but the friend of the bridegroom, who stands and hears him, rejoices greatly because of the bridegroom's voice. Therefore this joy of mine has been fulfilled.

<span class="v-num">30</span> He must increase, but I must decrease.

<span class="v-num">31</span> He who comes from above is above all. He who is of the earth is of the earth, and speaks of the earth; he who comes from heaven is above all,

<span class="v-num">32</span> And what he has seen and heard, this he testifies; and no one receives his testimony.

<span class="v-num">33</span> The one who has received his testimony has set his seal that God is true.

<span class="v-num">34</span> For he whom God has sent speaks the words of God; for God does not give the Spirit by measure.

<span class="v-num">35</span> The Father loves the Son, and has given all things into his hand.

<span class="v-num">36</span> The one who believes in the Son has eternal life; but the one who does not obey the Son will not see life, but the wrath of God remains on him."

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ)은 미래 가능성을 열어둔 조건절로, '하나님이 함께 하지 않으시면'이라는 실제로는 부정되는(실제로는 함께 하신다는) 논리적 전제를 제시한다. 니고데모는 이 조건절을 통해 예수의 표적이 하나님 임재의 증거임을 논증하고 있다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(γεννηθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 3류 조건문으로, '누구든지 거듭나지 않는다면'이라는 보편적 필요조건을 강조한다. 거듭남은 하나님 나라를 보기 위한 예외 없는 전제 조건임을 못박는 표현이다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(γεννηθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 조건을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '누구든지 물과 성령으로 태어나지 않는 한'이라는 필수 전제 조건을 제시한다. 이는 일반적 진리이자 예외 없는 규칙임을 강조하는 어법이다.
[^4]: πνεῦμα는 '바람'과 '영'을 동시에 뜻하는 중의어로 이 구절의 핵심 언어유희다.
[^5]: ἐὰν εἴπω(가정법)는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '내가 하늘의 일들을 말한다면(그런 일이 있다면)'이라는 뜻이다. 땅의 일도 못 믿는데 하물며 하늘의 일을 말하면 어떻게 믿겠느냐는 논증을 이끈다.
[^6]: "하늘에 있는"(ὁ ὢν ἐν τῷ οὐρανῷ)은 TR에 포함된 구절로 그리스도의 편재성을 암시하나 일부 사본에는 없다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπόληται, ἔχῃ)으로 목적을 나타냄.
[^8]: μονογενῆ(독생자)는 유일하게 나신 분이라는 뜻으로 하나님과 아들의 유일무이한 관계를 강조한다.
[^9]: ἐάν+가정법(ᾖ δεδομένον, 완료수동 우언적 표현) 조건절로, 하늘의 허락이라는 조건이 충족되지 않는 한 인간의 소유·성취가 불가능함을 강조한다. 아직 실현 여부가 불확정한 일반적 진리를 나타내는 3류 조건문이다.
