<span class="v-num">1</span> After these things Jesus manifested himself again to the disciples at the sea of Tiberias; and he manifested <span class="added-text">(himself)</span> thus:[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> There were together Simon Peter, and Thomas called Didymus, and Nathanael from Cana of Galilee, and the <span class="added-text">(sons)</span> of Zebedee, and two others of his disciples.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Simon Peter said to them, "I am going fishing." They said to him, "We also are coming with you." They went out and got into the boat immediately, and in that night they caught nothing.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But when morning had already come, Jesus stood on the shore; yet the disciples did not know that it was Jesus.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Jesus therefore said to them, "Children, do you have any <span class="alt-label">fish</span><span class="alt-text">(=relish)</span>?" They answered him, "No."

<span class="v-num">6</span> But he said to them, "Cast the net into the right side<span class="added-text">(s)</span> of the boat, and you will find." They cast <span class="added-text">(it)</span> therefore, and they were no longer able to draw it <span class="added-text">(in)</span> from the multitude of the fish.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Therefore that disciple whom Jesus loved said to Peter, "It is the Lord." Simon Peter therefore, having heard that it was the Lord, girded <span class="added-text">(on)</span> his outer garment, for he was naked, and threw himself into the sea.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But the other disciples came in the little boat, for they were not far from the land, but about two hundred cubits <span class="added-text">(off)</span>, dragging the net of fish.

<span class="v-num">9</span> When therefore they got out onto the land, they saw a fire of coals lying <span class="added-text">(there)</span>, and fish laid on <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and bread.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Jesus said to them, "Bring some of the fish which you have now caught."

<span class="v-num">11</span> Simon Peter went up, and drew the net to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> land, full of large fish, a hundred <span class="added-text">(and)</span> fifty-three; and though there were so many, the net was not torn.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Jesus said to them, "Come, dine." But none of the disciples dared to question him, "Who are you?" knowing that it was the Lord.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Jesus came therefore, and took the bread and gave <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to them, and the fish likewise.

<span class="v-num">14</span> This was now <span class="added-text">(the)</span> third <span class="added-text">(time)</span> Jesus was manifested to his disciples, having been raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead.

<span class="v-num">15</span> So when they had dined, Jesus said to Simon Peter, "Simon <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Jonah, do you love me more than these?" He said to him, "Yes, Lord; you know that I <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=am fond of)</span> you." He said to him, "Feed my lambs."

<span class="v-num">16</span> He said to him again a second <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, "Simon <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Jonah, do you love me?" He said to him, "Yes, Lord; you know that I <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=am fond of)</span> you." He said to him, "Shepherd my sheep."

<span class="v-num">17</span> He said to him the third <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, "Simon <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Jonah, do you <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=are you fond of)</span> me?" Peter was grieved because he said to him the third <span class="added-text">(time)</span>, "Do you <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=are you fond of)</span> me?" and he said to him, "Lord, you know all things; you know that I <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=am fond of)</span> you." Jesus said to him, "Feed my sheep.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, when you were younger, you girded yourself, and walked where you wished; but when you shall grow old, you will stretch out your hands, and another will gird you, and carry <span class="added-text">(you)</span> where you do not wish."

<span class="v-num">19</span> But this he said, signifying by what death he would glorify God. And having said this he said to him, "Follow me."

<span class="v-num">20</span> But Peter, having turned, saw the disciple whom Jesus loved following, who also had reclined at the supper on his breast and said, "Lord, who is the one betraying you?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> Peter, having seen this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, said to Jesus, "Lord, but what <span class="added-text">(about)</span> this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">22</span> Jesus said to him, "If I want him to remain until I come, what <span class="added-text">(is that)</span> to you? You follow me."[^2]

<span class="v-num">23</span> This word therefore went out to the brothers, "That that disciple does not die." Yet Jesus did not say to him that he does not die, but, "If I want him to remain until I come, what <span class="added-text">(is that)</span> to you?"[^3]

<span class="v-num">24</span> This is the disciple who testifies concerning these things, and <span class="added-text">(who)</span> wrote these things; and we know that his testimony is true.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And there are also many other things which Jesus did, which if they should be written one by one, I <span class="alt-label">suppose</span><span class="alt-text">(=think)</span> not even the world itself could contain the books that would be written. Amen.[^4]

[^1]: ἐφανέρωσεν(아오리스트)이 반복되어 부활 현현 사건의 확실성을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(θέλω)은 아직 실현되지 않은 가상의 조건을 나타내는 3급 조건절로, '설령 ~하고자 할지라도'라는 양보적 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 예수님의 뜻은 베드로가 관여할 바가 아님을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(θέλω)은 '만일 내가 원한다면'이라는 아직 확정되지 않은 가상의 조건을 나타낸다. 예수님의 뜻은 베드로가 참견할 사안이 아님을 강조하는 수사적 조건절이다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(γράφηται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 낱낱이 기록된다면'이라는 가상의 상황을 가정한다. 예수님의 행적이 얼마나 many한지를 과장법으로 강조하는 수사적 표현이다.
