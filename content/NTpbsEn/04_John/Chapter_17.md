# John 17

<span class="v-num">1</span> Jesus spoke these things, and lifted up his eyes to heaven, and said, "Father, the hour has come; glorify your Son, that <span class="added-text">(your)</span> Son also may glorify you,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> as you gave him authority over all flesh, that <span class="added-text">(to)</span> all whom you have given him, he may give them eternal life.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And this is eternal life, that they may know you the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> I glorified you on the earth; I completed the work which you gave me to do;[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And now, Father, glorify me with yourself, with the glory which I had with you before the world was.[^4]

<span class="v-num">6</span> I manifested your name to the men whom you gave me out of the world; they were yours, and you gave them to me, and they have kept your word.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Now they have known that all things whatever you have given me are from you;

<span class="v-num">8</span> for the words which you gave me I have given them; and they received <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and knew truly that I came out from you, and believed that you sent me.

<span class="v-num">9</span> I ask concerning them; I do not ask concerning the world, but concerning those whom you have given me, for they are yours.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And all my things are yours, and your things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> mine; and I have been glorified in them.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And I am no longer in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to you. Holy Father, keep them in your name, whom you have given me, that they may be one, as we <span class="added-text">(are)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> While I was with them in the world, I kept them in your name; those whom you gave me I have guarded, and none of them perished, except the son of perdition, that the scripture might be fulfilled.[^6]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But now I come to you, and I speak these things in the world, that they may have my joy fulfilled in themselves.

<span class="v-num">14</span> I have given them your word; and the world hated them, because they are not of the world, as I am not of the world.

<span class="v-num">15</span> I do not ask that you should take them out of the world, but that you should keep them from the evil <span class="added-text">(one)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> They are not of the world, as I am not of the world.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Sanctify them in your truth; your word is truth.[^7]

<span class="v-num">18</span> As you sent me into the world, I also sent them into the world;

<span class="v-num">19</span> and for them I sanctify myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth.[^8]

<span class="v-num">20</span> And I do not ask for these only, but also for those who will believe in me through their word;

<span class="v-num">21</span> that they all may be one; as you, Father, <span class="added-text">(are)</span> in me, and I in you, that they also may be one in us, that the world may believe that you sent me.[^9]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And I have given them the glory which you have given me, that they may be one, as we are one;

<span class="v-num">23</span> I in them, and you in me, that they may be perfected into one, and that the world may know that you sent me, and loved them, as you loved me.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Father, those whom you have given me, I will that where I am, they also may be with me; that they may see my glory, which you have given me, for you loved me before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world.[^10]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Righteous Father, the world also did not know you, but I knew you, and these knew that you sent me;

<span class="v-num">26</span> and I made known to them your name, and will make <span class="added-text">(it)</span> known, that the love with which you loved me may be in them, and I in them."[^11]

[^1]: ἐλήλυθεν은 완료시제로 '때가 이미 이르러 현재까지 그 결과가 지속됨'을 나타내며, ἵνα+가정법은 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(γινώσκωσι)이 '영원한 생명'의 내용을 설명하는 절로, 여기서는 목적이 아니라 동격/설명적 용법으로 쓰였다. 즉 '영원한 생명이란 곧 하나님과 예수 그리스도를 아는 것'이라는 정의를 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιήσω)은 아버지께서 주신 일의 목적/내용을 밝히는 목적절로, '내가 행하도록' 하시려고 맡기신 일임을 강조한다.
[^4]: πρὸ τοῦ τὸν κόσμον εἶναι는 그리스도의 선재(先在)를 명시하는 표현이다.
[^5]: οὐκ ἔτι εἰμὶ는 현재시제로 임박한 떠남을 생생하게 표현한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῇ) 목적절: 멸망의 아들이 멸망한 것이 성경 성취라는 하나님의 뜻과 목적을 나타낸다. 단순한 결과가 아니라 예언 성취라는 신적 목적을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^7]: ἁγιάζω는 세상으로부터 구별되어 하나님께 드려짐을 뜻하며, 여기서는 말씀(진리)이 그 수단이다.
[^8]: 동일한 ἁγιάζω 동사가 그리스도 자신에게 적용되어, 그의 자기 봉헌이 제자들의 거룩함의 근거가 됨을 보여준다.
[^9]: 아버지-아들-신자의 상호내주가 반복되는 키아즘적 구조로 연합의 신비를 강조한다.
[^10]: θέλω ἵνα는 부정사 대신 가정법 목적절을 취하는 구어적 구문으로, 강한 소원을 표현한다.
[^11]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ) 목적절로, 아버지의 이름을 알게 하신 목적이 무엇인지 밝힌다. 하나님의 사랑이 제자들 안에 거하고 그리스도께서도 그들 안에 거하시게 하려는 궁극적 목적을 나타낸다.
