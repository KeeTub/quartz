# John 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now before the feast of the Passover, Jesus knowing that his hour had come, that he should depart out of this world to the Father, having loved his own who were in the world, he loved them to the <span class="alt-label">end</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the uttermost)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And supper having come, the devil already having put into the heart of Judas Iscariot, <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Simon, that he should betray him,[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Jesus knowing that the Father had given all things into his hands, and that he came forth from God and was going to God,

<span class="v-num">4</span> he rose from the supper, and laid aside <span class="added-text">(his)</span> garments, and having taken a towel, girded himself.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Then he poured water into the basin, and began to wash the feet of the disciples, and to wipe <span class="added-text">(them)</span> with the towel with which he was girded.

<span class="v-num">6</span> So he came to Simon Peter, and that one said to him, "Lord, do you wash my feet?"

<span class="v-num">7</span> Jesus answered and said to him, "What I do, you do not know now, but you will know after these things."

<span class="v-num">8</span> Peter said to him, "You shall never wash my feet, forever." Jesus answered him, "If I do not wash you, you have no part with me."[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, not my feet only, but also <span class="added-text">(my)</span> hands and <span class="added-text">(my)</span> head."

<span class="v-num">10</span> Jesus said to him, "The one having bathed has no need except to wash the feet, but is wholly clean; and you are clean, but not all."

<span class="v-num">11</span> For he knew the one betraying him; because of this he said, "Not all of you are clean."

<span class="v-num">12</span> So when he had washed their feet, and had taken his garments, and reclined again, he said to them, "Do you know what I have done to you?

<span class="v-num">13</span> You call me, 'The teacher,' and 'The lord,' and you say well, for I am <span class="added-text">(so)</span>.

<span class="v-num">14</span> If then I, the lord and the teacher, washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.

<span class="v-num">15</span> For I gave you a <span class="alt-label">pattern</span><span class="alt-text">(=example)</span>, that just as I did to you, you also should do.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, a servant is not greater than his lord, nor an <span class="alt-label">apostle</span><span class="alt-text">(=one sent)</span> greater than the one who sent him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> If you know these things, blessed are you if you do them.[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> I do not speak concerning all of you; I know whom I chose; but that the scripture might be fulfilled, 'The one eating bread with me lifted up his heel against me.'[^6]

<span class="v-num">19</span> From now I tell you before it comes to pass, that when it comes to pass, you may believe that I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>.[^7]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, the one receiving whomever I send receives me; and the one receiving me receives the one who sent me."[^8]

<span class="v-num">21</span> Jesus, having said these things, was troubled in <span class="added-text">(his)</span> spirit, and testified and said, "Truly, truly, I say to you, that one of you will betray me."

<span class="v-num">22</span> So the disciples looked at one another, being at a loss concerning whom he spoke.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Now one of his disciples, whom Jesus loved, was reclining in the bosom of Jesus;

<span class="v-num">24</span> Simon Peter therefore beckoned to this one to inquire who it might be of whom he spoke.

<span class="v-num">25</span> That one, having leaned back on the breast of Jesus, said to him, "Lord, who is it?"

<span class="v-num">26</span> Jesus answered, "That is he to whom I, having dipped the piece of bread, shall give it." And having dipped the piece of bread, he gave <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to Judas Iscariot, <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Simon.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And after the piece of bread, then Satan entered into that one. Jesus therefore said to him, "What you do, do more quickly."

<span class="v-num">28</span> But this no one of those reclining <span class="added-text">(at table)</span> knew, for what <span class="added-text">(purpose)</span> he said it to him.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For some thought, since Judas had the money box, that Jesus said to him, "Buy what things we have need of for the feast," or that he should give something to the poor.[^9]

<span class="v-num">30</span> So having taken the piece of bread, that one went out immediately; and it was night when he went out.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Jesus said, "Now the Son of man is glorified, and God is glorified in him.

<span class="v-num">32</span> If God is glorified in him, God will also glorify him in himself, and will glorify him at <span class="alt-label">once</span><span class="alt-text">(=immediately)</span>.

<span class="v-num">33</span> Little children, yet a little while I am with you. You will seek me, and just as I said to the Jews, that where I go, you cannot come, so now I say to you also.

<span class="v-num">34</span> A new commandment I give to you, that you love one another; just as I loved you, that you also love one another.[^10]

<span class="v-num">35</span> By this all will know that you are my disciples, if you have love among one another."[^11]

<span class="v-num">36</span> Simon Peter said to him, "Lord, where are you going?" Jesus answered him, "Where I go, you cannot follow me now, but you will follow me afterward."

<span class="v-num">37</span> Peter said to him, "Lord, why can I not follow you now? I will lay down my life for you."

<span class="v-num">38</span> Jesus answered him, "Will you lay down your life for me? Truly, truly, I say to you, the rooster will not crow until you deny me three times."

[^1]: ἵνα절은 '때'(ὥρα)가 무엇을 위한 때인지 풀어 설명하는 동격적 목적절이다.
[^2]: ἵνα αὐτὸν παραδῷ는 마귀가 유다의 마음에 넣은 생각의 내용(목적)을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법(μὴ νίψω)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '내가 씻기지 않는다면'이라는 실제 발생 여부가 열려 있는 조건을 나타낸다. 예수의 씻김이 제자됨의 필수 조건임을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^4]: ἵνα...ποιῆτε는 본보기를 준 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법(ποιῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 실천 가능성을 나타내는 조건이다.
[^6]: 시편 41:9(LXX 40:10)의 인용으로, ἵνα ἡ γραφὴ πληρωθῇ는 유다의 배신이 성경 성취를 위한 것임을 밝힌다.
[^7]: ἐγώ εἰμι는 단순한 자기 확인을 넘어 출애굽기 3:14의 하나님의 이름을 반영하는 표현으로 이해된다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(τινα πέμψω)은 '내가 누구를 보내든지'라는 미확정 대상을 가리키는 조건절로, 보냄받은 자를 영접하는 것이 곧 보내신 이를 영접하는 것과 동일시됨을 강조한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(δῷ)은 목적절로, '가난한 자들에게 무엇을 주기 위해'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 여기서는 예수님의 말씀 내용을 제자들이 추측한 목적으로 이해했음을 보여준다.
[^10]: 두 개의 ἵνα절이 나란히 서로 사랑하라는 계명의 내용과 목적을 반복하여 강조한다.
[^11]: ἐὰν+가정법(ἔχητε)은 제자됨의 표지가 아직 실현될 사랑의 실천에 달려 있음을 나타낸다.
