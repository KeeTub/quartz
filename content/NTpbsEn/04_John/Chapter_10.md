<span class="v-num">1</span> Truly truly I say to you, the one not entering through the door into the <span class="alt-label">fold</span><span class="alt-text">(=pen)</span> of the sheep, but climbing up from another place, that one is a thief and a robber;

<span class="v-num">2</span> But the one entering through the door is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> shepherd of the sheep.

<span class="v-num">3</span> To him the doorkeeper opens <span class="added-text">(the door)</span>, and the sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name, and leads them out.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And when he has brought out his own sheep, he goes before them; and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice.

<span class="v-num">5</span> But a stranger they will not follow at all, but will flee from him; because they do not know the voice of strangers.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Jesus spoke this <span class="alt-label">parable</span><span class="alt-text">(=proverb)</span> to them; but those <span class="added-text">(people)</span> did not know what it was that he was speaking to them.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Jesus therefore said to them again, "Truly truly I say to you, that I am the door of the sheep.[^1]

<span class="v-num">8</span> All who came before me are thieves and robbers; but the sheep did not hear them.

<span class="v-num">9</span> I am the door; if anyone enters through me, he will be saved, and will go in and go out, and will find <span class="alt-label">pasture</span><span class="alt-text">(=food)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> The thief does not come except that he may steal and kill and destroy; I came that they may have life, and may have <span class="added-text">(it)</span> abundantly.[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> I am the good shepherd; the good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But the hired servant, and not being a shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming, and leaves the sheep, and flees; and the wolf seizes them, and scatters the sheep.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But the hired servant flees because he is a hired servant, and does not care about the sheep.

<span class="v-num">14</span> I am the good shepherd; and I know my own, and am known by my own.

<span class="v-num">15</span> As the Father knows me, I also know the Father; and I lay down my life for the sheep.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold; those also I must bring, and they will hear my voice; and there will become one flock, one shepherd.[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For this reason the Father loves me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again.[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have authority to lay it down, and I have authority to take it again; this commandment I received from my Father."[^6]

<span class="v-num">19</span> There arose therefore again a division among the Jews because of these words.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And many of them said, "He has a demon and is mad; why do you listen to him?"

<span class="v-num">21</span> Others said, "These sayings are not those of one possessed by a demon; can a demon open the eyes of the blind?"

<span class="v-num">22</span> Now the feast of dedication took place in Jerusalem, and it was winter;[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> and Jesus was walking in the temple, in the porch of Solomon.

<span class="v-num">24</span> The Jews therefore surrounded him, and said to him, "How long will you hold our <span class="alt-label">soul</span><span class="alt-text">(=mind)</span> in <span class="alt-label">suspense</span><span class="alt-text">(=doubt)</span>? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly."

<span class="v-num">25</span> Jesus answered them, "I told you, and you do not believe. The works which I do in my Father's name, these bear witness concerning me;

<span class="v-num">26</span> but you do not believe, for you are not of my sheep, as I told you.

<span class="v-num">27</span> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me,

<span class="v-num">28</span> and I give them eternal life; and they will never perish forever, and no one will snatch them out of my hand.

<span class="v-num">29</span> My Father, who has given <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to me, is greater than all; and no one is able to snatch <span class="added-text">(them)</span> out of my Father's hand.

<span class="v-num">30</span> I and the Father are one."[^8]

<span class="v-num">31</span> The Jews therefore took up stones again, that they might stone him.[^9]

<span class="v-num">32</span> Jesus answered them, "Many good works I have shown you from my Father; for which of these works do you stone me?"

<span class="v-num">33</span> The Jews answered him, saying, "For a good work we do not stone you, but for blasphemy, and because you, being a man, make yourself God."

<span class="v-num">34</span> Jesus answered them, "Is it not written in your law, 'I said, you are gods'?[^10]

<span class="v-num">35</span> If he called those gods, to whom the word of God came, and the scripture cannot be broken,

<span class="v-num">36</span> do you say of him whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world, 'You blaspheme,' because I said, 'I am <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God'?

<span class="v-num">37</span> If I do not do the works of my Father, do not believe me;

<span class="v-num">38</span> but if I do, even though you do not believe me, believe the works; that you may know and believe that the Father is in me, and I in him."[^11]

<span class="v-num">39</span> They sought therefore again to seize him; and he went out of their hand.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And he went away again beyond the Jordan, to the place where John was first baptizing; and he stayed there.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And many came to him, and said, "John indeed did no sign; but all things that John said about this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> were true."

<span class="v-num">42</span> And many believed there in him.

[^1]: '나는 ~이다'(ἐγώ εἰμι)는 출애굽기 3:14의 하나님 자기계시 표현과 연결되는 절대적 자기선언이다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(εἰσέλθῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '누구든지 들어가기만 하면'이라는 열린 초대의 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 문(예수)을 통해 들어가는 자는 누구나 예외 없이 구원과 자유(들어가고 나감)와 공급(꼴)을 누린다는 보편적 약속을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 도둑의 목적(훔치고 죽이고 멸망시킴)과 예수의 목적(생명을 얻게 함)이 대조된다.
[^4]: '우리'(αὐλή)가 아니라 '무리'(ποίμνη)이니, 유대인과 이방인이 여러 제도가 아니라 하나의 무리로 통합됨을 뜻한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(λάβω)은 목적절로, 목숨을 버리는 행위의 궁극적 목적이 '다시 얻음'에 있음을 나타낸다. 예수님의 자발적 죽음이 부활을 향한 의도적 행위였음을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐξουσίαν(권세)은 목숨을 버리고 다시 얻는 두 가지 능동적 권한을 가리키며 예수의 자발적 죽음과 부활을 강조한다.
[^7]: τὰ ἐγκαίνια는 마카베오 시대 성전 재봉헌을 기념하는 수전절(하누카)을 가리킨다.
[^8]: ἕν(하나)은 중성으로 위격의 동일함이 아니라 본질과 뜻의 하나 됨을 가리킨다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(λιθάσωσιν)은 목적절로, 돌을 든 행동의 의도(그를 돌로 치려는 목적)를 나타낸다.
[^10]: 시편 82:6의 인용으로, 이스라엘 재판관들을 '신들'이라 부른 구약 용례를 근거로 예수께서 자신의 신성 주장을 변호하신다.
[^11]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 그 일들을 믿는 목적이 아버지와 아들의 상호 내주를 깨달아 아는 데 있음을 보인다.
