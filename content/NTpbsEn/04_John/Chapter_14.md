# John 14

<span class="v-num">1</span> "Let not your heart be troubled: believe in God, believe also in me.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> In my Father's house are many <span class="alt-label">mansions</span><span class="alt-text">(=dwelling places)</span>: if <span class="added-text">(it were)</span> not <span class="added-text">(so)</span>, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you to myself; that where I am, <span class="added-text">(there)</span> you may be also.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And where I go you know, and the way you know."

<span class="v-num">5</span> Thomas said to him, "Lord, we do not know where you are going; and how can we know the way?"

<span class="v-num">6</span> Jesus said to him, "I am the way and the truth and the life; no one comes to the Father, except through me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> If you had known me, you would have known my Father also: and from now <span class="added-text">(on)</span> you know him, and have seen him."

<span class="v-num">8</span> Philip said to him, "Lord, show us the Father, and it is enough for us."

<span class="v-num">9</span> Jesus said to him, "Have I been so long time with you, and you have not known me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father; and how do you say, 'Show us the Father?'

<span class="v-num">10</span> Do you not believe that I am in the Father, and the Father is in me? The words that I speak to you, I do not speak from myself: but the Father who dwells in me, he does the works.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me: or else believe me for the sake of the works themselves.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Truly, truly, I say to you, he who believes in me, the works that I do, he will do also; and greater <span class="added-text">(works)</span> than these he will do; because I go to my Father.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And whatever you ask in my name, that I will do; that the Father may be glorified in the Son.[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> If you ask anything in my name, I will do <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">15</span> "If you love me, keep my commandments.[^6]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another <span class="alt-label">Comforter</span><span class="alt-text">(=Advocate)</span>, that he may abide with you forever,[^7]

<span class="v-num">17</span> The Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it does not see him, nor knows him: but you know him; for he dwells with you, and will be in you.

<span class="v-num">18</span> I will not leave you <span class="added-text">(as)</span> orphans: I <span class="added-text">(will)</span> come to you.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Yet a little <span class="added-text">(while)</span>, and the world sees me no more; but you see me: because I live, you will live also.

<span class="v-num">20</span> In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you.

<span class="v-num">21</span> He who has my commandments and keeps them, he it is who loves me: and he who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him."

<span class="v-num">22</span> Judas said to him, not Iscariot, "Lord, how is it that you will manifest yourself to us, and not to the world?"

<span class="v-num">23</span> Jesus answered and said to him, "If anyone loves me, he will keep my word: and my Father will love him, and we will come to him, and make <span class="added-text">(our)</span> dwelling with him.[^8]

<span class="v-num">24</span> He who does not love me does not keep my words: and the word which you hear is not mine, but the Father's who sent me."

<span class="v-num">25</span> "These things I have spoken to you, while dwelling with you.

<span class="v-num">26</span> But the <span class="alt-label">Comforter</span><span class="alt-text">(=Advocate)</span>, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, and bring to your remembrance all things that I said to you.[^9]

<span class="v-num">27</span> Peace I leave with you, my peace I give to you: not as the world gives, <span class="added-text">(do)</span> I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

<span class="v-num">28</span> You heard that I said to you, 'I go away, and I come <span class="added-text">(again)</span> to you.' If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I said, 'I go to the Father:' for my Father is greater than I.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And now I have told you before it comes to pass, that when it comes to pass, you may believe.[^10]

<span class="v-num">30</span> "I will no longer speak much with you: for the ruler of this world comes, and he has nothing in me:

<span class="v-num">31</span> But that the world may know that I love the Father; and as the Father commanded me, so I do. Arise, let us go from here."[^11]

[^1]: πιστεύετε가 목적어-동사, 동사-목적어 순서로 교차 배열되어(=키아즘) 하나님께 대한 믿음과 그리스도께 대한 믿음이 동등함을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(ἦτε)으로 목적을 나타내며, 재림의 궁극 목적이 신자와의 영원한 연합에 있음을 보인다.
[^3]: 길·진리·생명 세 관사 명사가 하나의 인격(ἐγώ εἰμι)에 귀속되어, 그리스도만이 아버지께 이르는 유일한 통로임을 선언한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(δοξασθῇ)으로 기도 응답의 궁극 목적이 아버지의 영광임을 밝힌다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(αἰτήσητε)의 3급 조건문으로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἐάν+현재가정법(ἀγαπᾶτε)은 '너희가 사랑한다면'이라는 실현 가능한 조건절로, 사랑이 전제되면 계명 준수가 뒤따른다는 논리적 인과관계를 나타낸다. 단순 가정이 아니라 실제 있을 수 있는 상황을 가리키며, 사랑과 순종이 분리될 수 없음을 강조한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(μένῃ)은 목적절로, 보혜사를 보내시는 목적이 '영원토록 함께 계심'에 있음을 나타낸다. 아버지께서 보혜사를 주시는 궁극적 의도를 강조하는 구문이다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀγαπᾷ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 제3형 조건절이다. '누구든지 나를 사랑한다면'이라는 열린 조건으로, 사랑의 실천이 말씀 순종과 하나님의 임재로 이어짐을 강조한다.
[^9]: 성령을 '보혜사'로 다시 부르며 그의 사역을 가르침과 기억나게 함으로 규정하여, 그리스도의 부재 중 사도적 계시의 통로가 되심을 보인다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(πιστεύσητε)으로, 미리 말씀하신 목적이 사건 성취 시 믿음을 견고케 함에 있음을 밝힌다.
[^11]: ἵνα+가정법(γνῷ)으로, 십자가로 향하시는 순종의 궁극 목적이 세상으로 하여금 아버지 사랑을 알게 함에 있음을 보인다.
