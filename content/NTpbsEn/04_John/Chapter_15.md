# John 15

<span class="v-num">1</span> I am the true vine, and my Father is the <span class="alt-label">husbandman</span><span class="alt-text">(=vinedresser)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Every branch in me not bearing fruit, he takes it away; and every <span class="added-text">(branch)</span> bearing fruit, he <span class="alt-label">prunes</span><span class="alt-text">(=cleanses)</span> it, so that it may bear more fruit.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> Already you are clean, because of the word which I have spoken to you.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, unless it abides in the vine, so neither <span class="added-text">(can)</span> you, unless you abide in me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> I am the vine, you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the branches. The one abiding in me, and I in him, this one bears much fruit; for apart from me you can do nothing.

<span class="v-num">6</span> If anyone does not abide in me, he is cast out as a branch, and is withered; and they gather them and cast <span class="added-text">(them)</span> into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fire, and they are burned.[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> If you abide in me, and my words abide in you, whatever you desire you shall ask, and it shall be done for you.[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> In this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit; and <span class="added-text">(so)</span> you shall become my disciples.[^6]

<span class="v-num">9</span> As the Father has loved me, I also have loved you; abide in my love.

<span class="v-num">10</span> If you keep my commandments, you shall abide in my love; as I have kept my Father's commandments, and abide in his love.[^7]

<span class="v-num">11</span> These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may abide in you, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> your joy may be fulfilled.[^8]

<span class="v-num">12</span> This is my commandment, that you love one another, as I have loved you.[^9]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Greater love than this no one has, that one lay down his life for his friends.[^10]

<span class="v-num">14</span> You are my friends, if you do whatever I command you.[^11]

<span class="v-num">15</span> No longer do I call you servants, for the servant does not know what his lord does; but I have called you friends, for all things which I heard from my Father I have made known to you.

<span class="v-num">16</span> You did not choose me, but I chose you, and <span class="alt-label">appointed</span><span class="alt-text">(=set)</span> you, that you should go and bear fruit, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> your fruit should remain; that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he may give you.[^12]

<span class="v-num">17</span> These things I command you, that you love one another.[^13]

<span class="v-num">18</span> If the world hates you, know that it has hated me <span class="alt-label">before</span><span class="alt-text">(=first, before)</span> you.

<span class="v-num">19</span> If you were of the world, the world would love its own; but because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Remember the word that I said to you, 'A servant is not greater than his lord.' If they persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they kept my word, they will keep yours also.[^14]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But all these things they will do to you because of my name, for they do not know him who sent me.

<span class="v-num">22</span> If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin; but now they have no <span class="alt-label">excuse</span><span class="alt-text">(=pretext)</span> for their sin.

<span class="v-num">23</span> He who hates me hates my Father also.

<span class="v-num">24</span> If I had not done among them the works which no other man has done, they would not have sin; but now they have both seen and hated both me and my Father.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But <span class="added-text">(this happened)</span> that the word might be fulfilled which is written in their law, 'They hated me without <span class="alt-label">cause</span><span class="alt-text">(=for nothing)</span>.'[^15]

<span class="v-num">26</span> But when the <span class="alt-label">Comforter</span><span class="alt-text">(=Advocate)</span>, whom I will send to you from the Father, the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, comes, he will testify concerning me;[^16]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And you also testify, because you have been with me from the beginning.

[^1]: 포도나무는 구약에서 이스라엘을 가리키는 표상(시 80편, 사 5장)으로, '참된' 포도나무는 이 상징의 완전한 성취가 그리스도이심을 드러낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법은 손질(전정)의 목적이 더 풍성한 결실에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἐὰν μὴ+가정법은 '~하지 않으면'이라는 미래의 가능성 있는 조건을 나타낸다. 가지가 포도나무에 붙어 있어야 열매 맺듯, 성도도 그리스도 안에 거해야 함을 조건적으로 강조하는 구문이다.
[^4]: ἐὰν μή τις μείνῃ(가정법)는 '누구든지 내 안에 머물지 않으면'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3类 조건절이다. 실제 일어날 수 있는 상황을 가정하여 경고의 의미를 담고 있다.
[^5]: ἐὰν+가정법 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건문이다.
[^6]: ἵνα절이 열매 맺음을 아버지의 영광과 제자됨의 목적으로 제시한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(τηρήσητε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3종 조건문으로, '지키면(조건 성립 시)'이라는 뜻이며 순종이 사랑 안에 거함의 전제 조건임을 강조한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(μείνῃ, πληρωθῇ)이 이끄는 목적절로, 예수님이 이 말씀을 하신 목적(내 기쁨이 너희 안에 머물고 너희 기쁨이 충만해지는 것)을 나타낸다.
[^9]: ἵνα절이 명령의 내용(서로 사랑함)을 목적절 형태로 규정한다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(θῇ)이 ἀγάπην의 내용을 설명하는 목적절/동격절로 쓰여 '~하는 것'이라는 사랑의 실질적 내용을 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않은 자기희생의 가능성을 가리키는 뉘앙스이다.
[^11]: ἐάν+가정법(ποιῆτε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 행한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 친구됨의 증거가 순종에 있음을 강조하는 조건문이다.
[^12]: 세 개의 ἵνα절이 사명(가서 열매 맺음), 지속(열매가 항상 있음), 응답(기도 응답)의 목적을 순차적으로 제시한다.
[^13]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀγαπᾶτε)은 목적절로, 명령의 의도·목표를 나타낸다. 즉 계명을 주신 목적이 '서로 사랑하는 것' 자체에 있음을 강조한다.
[^14]: 이는 요 13:16에서 예수께서 이미 말씀하신 것을 상기시키는 자기 인용이다.
[^15]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῇ) 목적절로, 그들의 미움이 성경 말씀을 성취하려는 하나님의 목적 안에 있었음을 보여준다. 사람의 죄악된 행동조차 하나님의 예정된 뜻을 이루는 데 사용됨을 강조하는 구문이다.
[^16]: παράκλητος는 '곁에 부름 받은 자'라는 뜻으로 변호자·위로자·돕는 자의 역할을 함축하며, ἐκπορεύεται의 현재형은 성령의 지속적 나오심(발출)을 나타낸다.
