# Mark 13

<span class="v-num">1</span> And as he was going out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, "Teacher, look, what wonderful stones and what wonderful buildings!"

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Jesus, answering, said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? There shall not be left stone upon stone, that shall not be thrown down."

<span class="v-num">3</span> And as he sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,

<span class="v-num">4</span> "Tell us, when will these things be? And what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the sign when all these things are about to be accomplished?"

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Jesus, answering them, began to say, "Watch, lest anyone deceive you.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For many will come in my name, saying, 'I am <span class="added-text">(he)</span>'; and they will deceive many.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And when you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be troubled; for <span class="added-text">(this)</span> must happen, but the end <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not yet.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; and there will be earthquakes in various places, and there will be famines and troubles. These <span class="added-text">(are the)</span> beginning of birth pains.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> But watch yourselves. For they will deliver you up to councils, and you will be beaten in synagogues, and you will stand before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony to them.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And the gospel must first be preached to all the nations.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And when they lead you <span class="added-text">(away)</span>, delivering <span class="added-text">(you)</span> up, do not be anxious beforehand what you should say, nor premeditate; but whatever is given to you in that hour, speak this; for it is not you who speak, but the Holy Spirit.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And brother will deliver brother to death, and father <span class="added-text">(his)</span> child; and children will rise up against parents, and put them to death.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And you will be hated by all because of my name; but the one who endures to the end, this one will be saved.

<span class="v-num">14</span> "But when you see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where it ought not — <span class="added-text">(let the reader understand)</span> — then let those in Judea flee to the mountains,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and let the one on the housetop not go down into the house, nor enter to take anything out of his house,

<span class="v-num">16</span> and let the one who is in the field not turn back to take his garment.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing in those days!

<span class="v-num">18</span> And pray that your flight may not be in winter.[^4]

<span class="v-num">19</span> For those days will be tribulation, such as has not been from the beginning of the creation which God created, until now, and never will be.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And unless the Lord had shortened the days, no flesh would be saved; but for the sake of the elect, whom he chose, he shortened the days.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And then if anyone says to you, 'Look, here <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the Christ!' or 'Look, there!' do not believe <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">22</span> For false christs and false prophets will arise, and will give signs and wonders, to deceive, if possible, even the elect.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But you, watch! Behold, I have told you all things beforehand.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light,

<span class="v-num">25</span> and the stars of heaven will be falling, and the powers that are in the heavens will be shaken.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And then they will see the Son of Man coming in clouds with great power and glory.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And then he will send his angels, and gather together his elect from the four winds, from the end of the earth to the end of heaven.

<span class="v-num">28</span> "Now learn the parable from the fig tree: when its branch already becomes tender and puts forth leaves, you know that summer is near,

<span class="v-num">29</span> so also you, when you see these things happening, know that <span class="alt-label">he</span><span class="alt-text">(=it)</span> is near, at the doors.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Truly I say to you, that this generation shall not pass away, until all these things happen.

<span class="v-num">31</span> Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words shall not pass away.

<span class="v-num">32</span> "But concerning that day and hour, no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but the Father <span class="added-text">(only)</span>.[^6]

<span class="v-num">33</span> "Watch, be alert and pray, for you do not know when the time is.

<span class="v-num">34</span> <span class="added-text">(It is)</span> as a man going on a journey, having left his house, and given authority to his servants, and to each his work, and commanded the doorkeeper to watch.[^7]

<span class="v-num">35</span> Watch therefore; for you do not know when the master of the house comes, in the evening, or at midnight, or at cockcrow, or in the morning,

<span class="v-num">36</span> lest, coming suddenly, he find you sleeping.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And what I say to you, I say to all: Watch."

[^1]: ἐγώ εἰμι는 자기 자신을 그리스도(메시아)로 자칭하는 표현으로, 거짓 그리스도들의 참람됨을 드러낸다.
[^2]: 해산의 고통은 앞으로 점점 심해질 종말의 고난을 비유하는 표현이다.
[^3]: ὃ ἐὰν δοθῇ는 '무엇이든지 주어지는 것'이라는 뜻으로, 미래에 실현될 불특정한 상황을 가정하는 관계사+ἐάν+가정법 구문이다. 성령께서 그 순간에 주실 말씀이 무엇이든 그대로 말하라는 신뢰의 약속을 담고 있다.
[^4]: ἵνα μὴ γένηται는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 절로, 도망하는 일이 겨울에 일어나지 않기를 구하는 기도의 내용을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '누군가 그렇게 말한다면'이라는 가상의 상황을 가정한다. 실제로 일어날 수 있는 미래 사건에 대한 경고성 조건절이다.
[^6]: 관사가 붙은 '아들'은 예수 자신을 가리키며, 성육신 중 스스로 제한하신 지식의 범위를 보여주는 구절이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(γρηγορῇ)은 명령(ἐνετείλατο)의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, '깨어 있으라'는 지속적 명령의 취지를 담고 있다. 이는 단순 사실이 아니라 종이 이루어야 할 목표된 상태를 강조한다.
