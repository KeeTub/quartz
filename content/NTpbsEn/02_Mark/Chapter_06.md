<span class="v-num">1</span> He went out from there and came into his own <span class="alt-label">hometown</span><span class="alt-text">(=native place)</span>, and his disciples followed him.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Sabbath having come, he began to teach in the synagogue, and many hearing were astonished, saying, "From where <span class="added-text">(do)</span> these things <span class="added-text">(come)</span> to this <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the wisdom given to him, that such works of power come about through his hands?

<span class="v-num">3</span> Is not this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> the carpenter, the son of Mary, and brother of James and Joses and Judas and Simon? And are not his sisters here with us?" And they were <span class="alt-label">offended</span><span class="alt-text">(=stumbled)</span> in him.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Jesus said to them, "A prophet is not without honor, except in his own hometown, and among his own relatives, and in his own house."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And he could not do any work of power there, except that having laid his hands on a few sick people, he healed <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And he marveled because of their unbelief. And he went around the villages in a circuit, teaching.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And he called to <span class="added-text">(himself)</span> the twelve, and began to send them out two <span class="added-text">(by)</span> two, and gave them authority over the unclean spirits;

<span class="v-num">8</span> and he commanded them that they should take nothing for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> road, except a staff only—no bread, no bag, no copper <span class="added-text">(money)</span> in the belt—[^1]

<span class="v-num">9</span> but <span class="added-text">(to go)</span> shod with sandals, and "Do not put on two tunics."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And he said to them, "Wherever you enter into a house, remain there until you depart from there.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And whoever will not receive you, nor hear you, going out from there, shake off the dust under your feet, for a testimony to them. Truly I say to you, it will be more tolerable for Sodom or Gomorrah in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day of judgment than for that city."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And having gone out, they preached that they should repent.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And they cast out many demons, and anointed with oil many <span class="added-text">(who were)</span> sick, and healed <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And King Herod heard, <span class="added-text">(for his name had become known,)</span> and said, "John the Baptist has risen from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, and because of this these works of power operate in him."

<span class="v-num">15</span> Others said, "He is Elijah." And others said, "He is a prophet, or as one of the prophets."

<span class="v-num">16</span> But Herod having heard, said, "He whom I beheaded—John—this is he; he has risen from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead."

<span class="v-num">17</span> For Herod himself had sent and seized John, and bound him in the prison, because of Herodias, his brother Philip's wife, because he had married her.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For John had said to Herod, "It is not lawful for you to have your brother's wife."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And Herodias held <span class="added-text">(a grudge)</span> against him, and wanted to kill him; but she could not.

<span class="v-num">20</span> For Herod feared John, knowing him <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> a righteous and holy man, and he protected him; and having heard him, he did many things, and heard him gladly.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And a convenient day having come, when Herod on his birthday made a feast for his nobles and the commanders and the chief <span class="added-text">(men)</span> of Galilee,

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the daughter of Herodias herself having come in, and having danced, and having pleased Herod and those reclining with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, the king said to the girl, "Ask me whatever you wish, and I will give <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to you."[^4]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And he swore to her, "Whatever you ask me, I will give to you, up to half of my kingdom."[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> And she having gone out, said to her mother, "What shall I ask?" And she said, "The head of John the Baptist."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And having come in immediately with haste to the king, she asked, saying, "I want that you give me at once, on a platter, the head of John the Baptist."[^6]

<span class="v-num">26</span> And the king, having become deeply grieved, because of his oaths and those reclining with <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, did not want to refuse her.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And immediately the king, having sent a soldier of the guard, commanded <span class="added-text">(his)</span> head to be brought. And he having gone, beheaded him in the prison,

<span class="v-num">28</span> and brought his head on a platter, and gave it to the girl; and the girl gave it to her mother.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And his disciples having heard, came, and took up his corpse, and laid it in a tomb.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And the apostles gathered themselves to Jesus, and reported to him all things, both as many as they had done, and as many as they had taught.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And he said to them, "Come yourselves alone into a desolate place, and rest a little." For there were many coming and going, and they did not even have opportunity to eat.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And they went away in the boat into a desolate place alone.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And the crowds saw them going, and many recognized <span class="alt-label">him</span><span class="alt-text">(=them)</span>, and ran together there on foot from all the cities, and got ahead of them, and came together to him.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And having come out, Jesus saw a great crowd, and was moved with compassion for them, because they were as sheep not having a shepherd; and he began to teach them many things.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And it now being a late hour, his disciples having come to him, said, "The place is desolate, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hour <span class="added-text">(is)</span> already late.

<span class="v-num">36</span> Send them away, that having gone into the surrounding fields and villages, they may buy for themselves bread<span class="added-text">(s)</span>; for they do not have anything to eat."[^7]

<span class="v-num">37</span> But he answering said to them, "You give them <span class="added-text">(something)</span> to eat." And they said to him, "Shall we go and buy two hundred denarii <span class="added-text">(worth)</span> of bread<span class="added-text">(s)</span>, and give them to eat?"

<span class="v-num">38</span> But he said to them, "How many loaves do you have? Go and see." And having found out, they said, "Five, and two fish."

<span class="v-num">39</span> And he commanded them to make all recline <span class="added-text">(in)</span> groups <span class="added-text">(upon)</span> the green grass.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And they sat down in <span class="alt-label">rows</span><span class="alt-text">(=groups)</span>, by hundreds and by fifties.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And having taken the five loaves and the two fish, having looked up into heaven, he blessed, and broke the loaves, and gave <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to his disciples that they might set <span class="added-text">(them)</span> before them; and the two fish he divided among all.[^8]

<span class="v-num">42</span> And they all ate, and were filled.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And they took up twelve baskets full of fragments, and also of the fish.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And those who ate the loaves were about five thousand men.

<span class="v-num">45</span> And immediately he compelled his disciples to embark into the boat, and to go before <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to the other side, to Bethsaida, while he himself sent away the crowd.

<span class="v-num">46</span> And having taken leave of them, he departed into the mountain to pray.

<span class="v-num">47</span> And evening having come, the boat was in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of the sea, and he alone <span class="added-text">(was)</span> on the land.

<span class="v-num">48</span> And he saw them being distressed in rowing; for the wind was contrary to them. And about <span class="added-text">(the)</span> fourth watch of the night he came to them, walking upon the sea, and wanted to pass by them.

<span class="v-num">49</span> But they, having seen him walking upon the sea, thought it was a ghost, and cried out;

<span class="v-num">50</span> for they all saw him, and were troubled. And immediately he spoke with them, and said to them, "Take courage; it is I, do not be afraid."

<span class="v-num">51</span> And he went up to them into the boat, and the wind ceased; and they were exceedingly astonished in themselves beyond measure, and marveled.

<span class="v-num">52</span> For they had not understood concerning the loaves; for their heart was hardened.

<span class="v-num">53</span> And having crossed over, they came to the land of Gennesaret, and moored <span class="added-text">(there)</span>.

<span class="v-num">54</span> And they having come out of the boat, immediately having recognized him,

<span class="v-num">55</span> having run about through that whole surrounding region, they began to carry about on pallets those who were sick, wherever they heard he was.

<span class="v-num">56</span> And wherever he entered into villages, or cities, or fields, they laid the sick in the marketplaces, and begged him that they might touch even the hem of his garment; and as many as touched him were <span class="alt-label">healed</span><span class="alt-text">(=saved)</span>.[^9]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(αἴρωσιν)은 명령의 내용(무엇을 명하셨는지)을 목적절 형태로 풀어 설명한다. 헬라어에서는 명령이나 요청의 내용을 ἵνα절로 표현하는 경우가 많으며, 여기서는 '아무것도 가지지 말라'는 지시 사항 자체를 나타낸다.
[^2]: ὅπου ἐὰν εἰσέλθητε는 '어디든지 들어가면'이라는 뜻으로, 장소를 특정하지 않고 어떤 곳이든 해당되는 미실현 조건을 가정법으로 나타낸다. 특정 상황이 아니라 일반적이고 반복 가능한 상황을 전제하는 조건절이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(μετανοήσωσι)은 목적절로, 제자들이 나가서 선포한 행동의 목적('회개하게 하려고')을 나타낸다. 단순한 내용 전달이 아니라 그 선포가 지향하는 결과를 강조한다.
[^4]: ὃ ἐὰν θέλῃς는 '무엇이든 네가 원하는 것'이라는 부정관계+가정법 구문으로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 폭넓게 열어두는 표현이다. 왕의 무조건적이고 파격적인 약속의 뉘앙스를 살려 설교에 활용할 수 있다.
[^5]: ὃ ἐάν+가정법은 '무엇이든지'라는 불특정 관계절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 조건을 나타낸다. 헤롯이 딸의 요구 내용을 모르는 상태에서 한 무조건적 서약임을 보여준다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(δῷς)이 θέλω의 목적어절 역할을 하여 '~해 주기를 원한다'는 의도를 나타낸다. 고전 헬라어의 부정사 목적어 자리를 신약에서는 ἵνα절이 대신하는 전형적 용법이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀγοράσωσιν)은 '보내는' 목적을 나타내는 목적절로, 무리를 보내는 이유가 스스로 먹을 것을 사게 하려는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(παραθῶσιν)은 목적절로, 제자들에게 빵을 주신 목적이 '무리 앞에 나눠주게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다. 예수님은 직접 나누지 않으시고 제자들을 통해 분배하게 하시는 사역의 위임 구조를 보여준다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(ἅψωνται)은 간청의 목적절로, '옷단이라도 만지게 해달라'는 사람들이 바라는 목적/내용을 나타낸다. 예수께 대한 믿음이 간절한 요청으로 표현된 것이다.
