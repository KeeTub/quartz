# Mark 11

<span class="v-num">1</span> And when they drew near to Jerusalem, to Bethphage and Bethany, toward the Mount of Olives, he sent two of his disciples,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and said to them, "Go into the village that is opposite you, and immediately entering into it you will find a colt tied, on which no one of men has sat; having loosed it, bring <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And if anyone says to you, 'Why are you doing this?' say, 'The Lord has need of it,' and immediately he will send it here."[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And they went away, and found the colt tied at the door outside on the street corner, and they loosed it.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And some of the ones standing there said to them, "What are you doing, loosing the colt?"

<span class="v-num">6</span> But they told them just as Jesus had commanded, and they allowed them.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And they brought the colt to Jesus, and put their garments on it, and he sat on it.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And many spread their garments on the road; and others were cutting <span class="alt-label">branches</span><span class="alt-text">(=leafy branches)</span> from the trees, and spreading <span class="added-text">(them)</span> on the road.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And the ones going before and the ones following cried out, saying, "Hosanna, blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> he who comes in the name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the coming kingdom of our father David, in the name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord: Hosanna in the highest."

<span class="v-num">11</span> And Jesus entered into Jerusalem, and into the temple; and having looked around at everything, the hour already being late, he went out to Bethany with the twelve.

<span class="v-num">12</span> And on the next day, as they went out from Bethany, he was hungry.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And seeing a fig tree from afar, having leaves, he came, if perhaps he would find anything on it; and having come to it, he found nothing except leaves, for it was not the time of figs.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus answering said to it, "Let no one eat fruit from you ever again into the age." And his disciples heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And they came to Jerusalem; and Jesus, having entered into the temple, began to cast out the ones selling and buying in the temple, and overturned the tables of the money-changers, and the seats of the ones selling the doves;

<span class="v-num">16</span> and he did not allow that anyone should carry a vessel through the temple.[^3]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And he taught, saying to them, "Is it not written, 'My house shall be called a house of prayer for all the nations'? But you have made it a <span class="alt-label">den</span><span class="alt-text">(=cave)</span> of robbers."[^4]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the scribes and the chief priests heard, and they were seeking how they might destroy him; for they feared him, because all the crowd was astonished at his teaching.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And when evening came, he went out of the city.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And passing by in the morning, they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And Peter, remembering, said to him, "Rabbi, behold, the fig tree which you cursed has withered."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And Jesus answering said to them, "Have <span class="alt-label">faith</span><span class="alt-text">(=the faith)</span> of God.

<span class="v-num">23</span> For truly I say to you, that whoever says to this mountain, 'Be taken up, and be cast into the sea,' and does not doubt in his heart, but believes that what he says comes to pass, it will be to him whatever he says.[^5]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Therefore I say to you, all things whatever you ask, praying, believe that you receive <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and it will be to you.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And whenever you stand praying, forgive if you have anything against anyone, so that your Father who is in the heavens may also forgive you your trespasses.[^6]

<span class="v-num">26</span> But if you do not forgive, neither will your Father who is in the heavens forgive your trespasses."[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And they came again to Jerusalem; and as he was walking in the temple, the chief priests and the scribes and the elders came to him,

<span class="v-num">28</span> and said to him, "By what authority do you do these things? And who gave you this authority, that you should do these things?"[^8]

<span class="v-num">29</span> But Jesus answering said to them, "I also will ask you one word, and answer me, and I will tell you by what authority I do these things.

<span class="v-num">30</span> The baptism of John, was it from heaven, or from men? Answer me."

<span class="v-num">31</span> And they reasoned among themselves, saying, "If we say, 'From heaven,' he will say, 'Why then did you not believe him?'[^9]

<span class="v-num">32</span> but if we say, 'From men,'" — they feared the people; for all held John, that he really was a prophet.[^10]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And answering they said to Jesus, "We do not know." And Jesus answering said to them, "Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things."

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(τις εἴπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 상황을 가정하는 3급 조건문으로, 만일의 상황에 대한 지시임을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 호산나 외침은 시편 118:25-26을 인용한 것으로 메시아를 향한 환영의 표현이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(διενέγκῃ)이 이끄는 목적절로, '아무도 성전을 통해 그릇을 나르지 못하게 하려는' 금지의 목적을 나타낸다. 예수님의 허락하지 않으심의 의도를 설명한다.
[^4]: ‘기도하는 집’은 사56:7 인용이며 ‘강도의 동굴’은 렘7:11 인용으로, 성전 본래 목적과 당대 타락상을 대조한다.
[^5]: ὃς ἂν εἴπῃ의 부정관계+가정법 구문은 아직 실현되지 않은 상황을 전제하는 조건적 표현으로, 의심 없는 믿음의 결과로 응답됨을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀφῇ)은 용서의 목적을 나타내며, 우리의 용서와 하늘 아버지의 용서가 상응함을 보여준다.
[^7]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 사본상의 근거 부족(마 6:15와의 동화 가능성)으로 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῇς)은 목적절로, '이런 일들을 행하도록'이라는 권세 수여의 목적/결과를 나타낸다. 즉 권세를 준 목적이 바로 이 행위를 하게 하려는 것이었음을 강조한다.
[^9]: ἐὰν εἴπωμεν(가정법)은 아직 실현되지 않은 대답의 결과를 미리 헤아리는 3급 조건문으로, 그들의 곤경을 드러낸다.
[^10]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 우리가 말한다면'이라는 뜻이다. 종교 지도자들이 실제로 그렇게 말할 것을 두려워하며 미리 계산하는 상황을 보여준다.
