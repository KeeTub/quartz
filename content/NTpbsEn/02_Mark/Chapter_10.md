<span class="v-num">1</span> And from there having risen, he came into the borders of Judea through the <span class="added-text">(region)</span> beyond the Jordan; and crowds came together to him again, and as he was accustomed, he taught them again.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the Pharisees having come, asked him, testing him, whether it is lawful for a man to put away <span class="added-text">(his)</span> wife.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he answering said to them, "What did Moses command you?"

<span class="v-num">4</span> And they said, "Moses permitted <span class="added-text">(a man)</span> to write a certificate of divorce, and to put <span class="added-text">(her)</span> away."

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Jesus answering said to them, "Because of your hardness of heart he wrote you this commandment.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning of creation, God made them male and female.

<span class="v-num">7</span> "'For this cause a man shall leave his father and mother, and shall be joined to his wife,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and the two shall become one flesh'; so that they are no longer two, but one flesh.

<span class="v-num">9</span> What therefore God has joined together, let not man separate."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And in the house his disciples asked him again concerning the same <span class="added-text">(matter)</span>.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And he said to them, "Whoever puts away his wife and marries another, commits adultery against her;[^1]

<span class="v-num">12</span> and if a woman puts away her husband and is married to another, she commits adultery."[^2]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And they brought young children to him, that he might touch them; but the disciples rebuked those bringing <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.[^3]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Jesus seeing <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, was indignant, and said to them, "Permit the young children to come to me, and forbid them not; for of such is the kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">15</span> "Truly I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, may not enter into it at all."[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And having taken them in his arms, putting <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hands on them, he blessed them.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And as he was going out into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> way, one having run to <span class="added-text">(him)</span> and having knelt to him, asked him, "Good Teacher, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?"[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Jesus said to him, "Why do you call me good? No one <span class="added-text">(is)</span> good, except one, God.

<span class="v-num">19</span> You know the commandments: 'Do not commit adultery, do not murder, do not steal, do not bear false witness, do not defraud, honor your father and mother.'"

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he answering said to him, "Teacher, all these I have kept from my youth."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And Jesus having looked at him, loved him, and said to him, "One thing you lack: go, sell as much as you have, and give to the poor, and you will have treasure in heaven; and come, follow me, having taken up the cross."

<span class="v-num">22</span> But he, having become gloomy at the word, went away sorrowful; for he was one having many possessions.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And Jesus having looked around, said to his disciples, "How difficultly will those having riches enter into the kingdom of God!"

<span class="v-num">24</span> And the disciples were astonished at his words. But Jesus answering again said to them, "Children, how difficult it is for those trusting in riches to enter into the kingdom of God!

<span class="v-num">25</span> It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And they were exceedingly astonished, saying to themselves, "And who can be saved?"

<span class="v-num">27</span> And Jesus having looked at them said, "With men <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> impossible, but not with God; for all things are possible with God."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And Peter began to say to him, "Behold, we have left all things, and have followed you."

<span class="v-num">29</span> And Jesus answering said, "Truly I say to you, there is no one who has left house, or brothers, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or fields, for my sake and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> gospel's,

<span class="v-num">30</span> but he shall receive a hundredfold now in this time, houses and brothers and sisters and mothers and children and fields, with persecutions, and in the age to come, eternal life.[^6]

<span class="v-num">31</span> But many <span class="added-text">(who are)</span> first shall be last, and the last, first."

<span class="v-num">32</span> And they were on the way, going up to Jerusalem; and Jesus was going before them, and they were astonished, and as they followed, they were afraid. And having taken the twelve again, he began to tell them the things about to happen to him:

<span class="v-num">33</span> "Behold, we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man shall be delivered to the chief priests and to the scribes, and they shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him to the Gentiles;

<span class="v-num">34</span> and they shall mock him, and shall scourge him, and shall spit on him, and shall kill him; and on the third day he shall rise again."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And James and John, the sons of Zebedee, came to him, saying, "Teacher, we want you to do for us whatever we ask."[^7]

<span class="v-num">36</span> And he said to them, "What do you want me to do for you?"

<span class="v-num">37</span> And they said to him, "Grant to us that one may sit on your right hand and one on your left, in your glory."[^8]

<span class="v-num">38</span> And Jesus said to them, "You do not know what you ask. Can you drink the cup that I drink, and be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with?"

<span class="v-num">39</span> And they said to him, "We can." And Jesus said to them, "You shall indeed drink the cup that I drink; and with the baptism that I am baptized with, you shall be baptized;

<span class="v-num">40</span> but to sit on my right hand and on my left, is not mine to give, but <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> for those for whom it has been prepared."

<span class="v-num">41</span> And the ten having heard, began to be indignant concerning James and John.

<span class="v-num">42</span> But Jesus having called them, said to them, "You know that those who are recognized to rule over the Gentiles lord it over them; and their great ones exercise authority over them.

<span class="v-num">43</span> But it shall not be so among you; but whoever wants to become great among you, shall be your servant;[^9]

<span class="v-num">44</span> and whoever of you wants to become first, shall be servant of all.

<span class="v-num">45</span> For even the Son of man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life a ransom for many."

<span class="v-num">46</span> And they came to Jericho; and as he was going out from Jericho, with his disciples and a considerable crowd, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of Timaeus, blind Bartimaeus, sat by the way begging.

<span class="v-num">47</span> And having heard that it was Jesus the Nazarene, he began to cry out and say, "<span class="added-text">(You)</span> son of David, Jesus, have mercy on me!"

<span class="v-num">48</span> And many rebuked him, that he should be silent; but he cried out much more, "<span class="added-text">(You)</span> son of David, have mercy on me!"[^10]

<span class="v-num">49</span> And Jesus having stood still, commanded him to be called; and they called the blind man, saying to him, "Take courage, rise, he calls you."

<span class="v-num">50</span> And he, having thrown away his garment, rose <span class="added-text">(and)</span> came to Jesus.

<span class="v-num">51</span> And Jesus answering said to him, "What do you want me to do for you?" And the blind man said to him, "Rabboni, that I may see again."[^11]

<span class="v-num">52</span> And Jesus said to him, "Go your way; your faith has saved you." And immediately he saw again, and followed Jesus in the way.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀπολύσῃ, γαμήσῃ)은 '누구든지 ~하면'이라는 불특정 대상을 향한 일반 조건절로, 현실에서 일어날 수 있는 미래적 가능성을 나타낸다. 특정 개인이 아닌 그런 행동을 하는 모든 사람에게 적용되는 보편적 원칙을 제시한다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀπολύσῃ, γαμηθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. '만일 ~한다면'이라는 가정으로, 이혼 후 재혼이라는 구체적 상황을 전제하여 간음죄를 선언하는 논리 구조다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἅψηται)은 사람들이 아이들을 데려온 목적을 나타내는 목적절로, '만져 주시기를 (바라며)'라는 의도를 표현한다.
[^4]: ὃς ἐὰν+가정법(δέξηται)은 '누구든지 ~하지 않으면'이라는 불특정 대상을 가리키는 조건절로, 아직 결정되지 않은 일반적 가능성을 나타낸다. 어린아이처럼 받아들이는 태도가 하나님 나라에 들어가는 필수 조건임을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(κληρονομήσω)은 '무엇을 해야 하는가'의 목적을 나타내는 목적절로, 영생을 얻기 위한 행위의 목표를 묻는 구문이다. 이는 행위로 영생을 '얻으려는' 인간적 사고방식을 드러내며, 이후 예수님의 답변(은혜의 구원)과 대조를 이룬다.
[^6]: ἐὰν μὴ λάβῃ는 3류 조건문으로, 아직 이루어지지 않았지만 실현 가능한 미래적 상황을 가정한다. 여기서는 부정을 겹쳐 '받지 못할 자가 없다'는 강한 긍정을 표현하는 수사적 조건절이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιήσῃς)은 '해 주시기를'이라는 목적절이며, 그 안의 ὃ ἐὰν αἰτήσωμεν(가정법)은 '무엇이든지 구하는 것'이라는 불특정 관계 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 요구 내용을 포괄적으로 나타낸다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(καθίσωμεν)이 δός의 목적을 나타내는 목적절로, '~하도록, ~하게'라는 의도를 표현한다. 제자들이 구하는 요청의 내용(영광 중 좌우편에 앉는 것)이 실현되기를 바라는 목적을 담고 있다.
[^9]: ὃς ἐὰν θέλῃ(가정법)는 '누구든지 원하면'이라는 불특정 조건절로, 아직 실현되지 않은 일반적 가능성을 나타낸다. 미래에 누구에게든 적용될 수 있는 열린 조건임을 강조한다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(σιωπήσῃ)은 '잠잠하게 하려고'라는 목적절로, 많은 사람이 꾸짖은 의도를 나타낸다. 즉 꾸짖음의 목적이 그를 침묵시키는 데 있었음을 강조한다.
[^11]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀναβλέψω)이 소경이 원하는 내용을 목적절로 표현함. '보게 해주세요'라는 간절한 소원의 목적을 나타내는 구문으로, 단순 명사절처럼 '~하는 것'을 의미.
