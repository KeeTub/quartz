# Mark 7

<span class="v-num">1</span> And the Pharisees, and some of the scribes, having come from Jerusalem, gathered to him.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And having seen some of his disciples eating bread with defiled, that is, unwashed, hands, they found fault <span class="added-text">(with them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> <span class="added-text">(For the Pharisees, and all the Jews, holding the tradition of the elders, do not eat unless they wash (their)</span> hands with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">fist</span><span class="alt-text">(=diligently)</span>,[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> and <span class="added-text">(coming)</span> from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> marketplace, unless they wash, they do not eat; and there are many other things which they received to hold, washings of cups, and pitchers, and brasen vessels, and couches.)[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Then the Pharisees and the scribes questioned him, "Why do your disciples not walk according to the tradition of the elders, but eat bread with unwashed hands?"

<span class="v-num">6</span> But he answered and said to them, "Isaiah prophesied well concerning you hypocrites, as it is written, 'This people honors me with the lips, but their heart is far away from me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But in vain do they worship me, teaching <span class="added-text">(as their)</span> teachings the commandments of men.'"

<span class="v-num">8</span> For having left the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men, washings of pitchers and cups; and you do many other such similar things."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he said to them, "Well do you set aside the commandment of God, that you may keep your tradition.[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For Moses said, 'Honor your father and your mother,' and, 'The one speaking evil of father or mother, let him die the death,'[^5]

<span class="v-num">11</span> but you say, 'If a man says to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> father or mother, "<span class="added-text">(Whatever)</span> you would have been profited from me is Corban"' <span class="added-text">(that is, a gift)</span>,[^6]

<span class="v-num">12</span> then you no longer permit him to do anything for his father or his mother,

<span class="v-num">13</span> making void the word of God by your tradition which you have handed down; and you do many such similar things."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And having called all the crowd <span class="added-text">(to him)</span>, he said to them, "Hear me, all of you, and understand.

<span class="v-num">15</span> There is nothing outside the man entering into him which can defile him, but the things coming out of him, those are the things defiling the man.

<span class="v-num">16</span> "If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear."[^7]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And when he entered into a house away from the crowd, his disciples questioned him about the parable.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And he said to them, "Are you also thus without understanding? Do you not perceive that whatever from outside enters into the man cannot defile him,

<span class="v-num">19</span> because it does not enter into his heart, but into the belly, and goes out into the latrine, purging all the foods?"

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he said, "That which comes out of the man, that defiles the man.

<span class="v-num">21</span> For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed the evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,

<span class="v-num">22</span> thefts, covetousness, wickednesses, deceit, licentiousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness.

<span class="v-num">23</span> All these evil things proceed from within, and defile the man."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And having risen up from there, he went away into the borders of Tyre and Sidon; and having entered into a house, he wanted no one to know <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, but he was not able to be hidden.

<span class="v-num">25</span> For a woman, whose little daughter had an unclean spirit, having heard about him, came and fell down at his feet.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Now the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoenician by birth; and she asked him that he would cast the demon out of her daughter.[^8]

<span class="v-num">27</span> But Jesus said to her, "Let the children first be filled, for it is not good to take the children's bread and throw <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to the little dogs."

<span class="v-num">28</span> But she answered and said to him, "Yes, Lord; yet even the little dogs under the table eat from the children's crumbs."

<span class="v-num">29</span> And he said to her, "Because of this word, go your way; the demon has gone out of your daughter."

<span class="v-num">30</span> And having gone away to her house, she found the demon gone out, and her daughter laid upon the bed.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And again, having gone out from the borders of Tyre and Sidon, he came to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the borders of Decapolis.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And they brought to him one deaf and having an impediment in his speech; and they begged him that he would lay <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand on him.[^9]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And having taken him aside from the crowd privately, he put his fingers into his ears, and having spit, touched his tongue,

<span class="v-num">34</span> and having looked up to heaven, he sighed, and said to him, "Ephphatha," that is, "Be opened."

<span class="v-num">35</span> And immediately his ears were opened, and the bond of his tongue was loosed, and he spoke plainly.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And he commanded them that they should tell no one; but the more he commanded them, so much the more abundantly they proclaimed <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.[^10]

<span class="v-num">37</span> And they were astonished beyond measure, saying, "He has done all things well; he even makes the deaf to hear, and the mute to speak."

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(νίψωνται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 반복적/일반적 조건을 나타내는 3류 조건문으로, '만일 ~하지 않으면'이라는 관습적 조건을 표현한다. 바리새인들의 손 씻는 정결 관습이 예외 없이 지켜지는 규칙임을 강조한다.
[^2]: ἐὰν μὴ βαπτίσωνται는 가정법을 사용한 부정 조건절로, '만약 씻지 않으면'이라는 뜻으로 반복적이고 일반적인 관습적 조건(장에서 돌아올 때마다 해당되는 상황)을 나타낸다. 실현 여부가 매번 결정되는 습관적 규례를 표현한다.
[^3]: 이사야 29:13 칠십인역 인용으로, 외식하는 자들의 형식적 신앙을 책망한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(τηρήσητε)은 목적절로, 하나님의 계명을 저버리는 행위의 의도된 목적(자신들의 전통을 지키기 위함)을 나타낸다. 예수님은 이를 반어적으로 지적하시며 그들의 우선순위가 전도된 것을 책망하신다.
[^5]: 출애굽기 20:12(신명기 5:16)과 출애굽기 21:17 인용으로, 하나님의 계명의 권위를 강조한다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 상황을 가정하는 3류 조건문으로, '누구든지 이렇게 말한다면'이라는 일반적 가능성을 나타낸다. 예수님은 이런 서원(고르반) 관습이 실제로 일어날 수 있는 사례로 지적하며 그 위선을 책망하신다.
[^7]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 사본상의 증거 부족과 4:9, 4:23의 유사 어구의 필사자 삽입 가능성으로 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐκβάλλῃ)이 ἠρώτα(간구하다)의 내용을 나타내는 목적절로, '~하기를' 구하는 청원의 목적을 표현한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐπιθῇ)이 παρακαλοῦσιν(간구하다)의 내용을 이루는 목적절로, '안수해 주시기를' 간구하는 청원의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(εἴπωσιν)이 이끄는 목적절로, '아무에게도 말하지 말라'는 명령의 내용(목적)을 나타낸다. 직접명령이 아니라 명령의 취지를 간접적으로 전달하는 구문이다.
