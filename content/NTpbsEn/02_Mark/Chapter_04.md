<span class="v-num">1</span> And again he began to teach beside the sea, and a great crowd was gathered to him, so that he, having gone up into the boat, sat in the sea, and all the crowd was on the land toward the sea.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And he taught them many things in parables, and said to them in his teaching,

<span class="v-num">3</span> "Listen. Behold, the sower went out to sow."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And it happened, in the sowing, that some fell beside the road, and the birds of the heaven came and devoured it.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell on the rocky <span class="added-text">(ground)</span>, where it did not have much earth, and immediately it sprang up, because it had no depth of earth.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And when the sun rose it was scorched, and because it had no root it withered.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell into the thorns, and the thorns grew up and choked it, and it gave no fruit.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And other <span class="added-text">(seed)</span> fell into the good ground, and gave fruit, coming up and growing, and bore, one thirty<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>, and one sixty<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>, and one a hundred<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he said to them, "He who has ears to hear, let him hear."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And when he was alone, those around him with the twelve asked him about the parable.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And he said to them, "To you it has been given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God, but to those outside, all things come in parables,

<span class="v-num">12</span> so that seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand; lest they should turn, and their sins be forgiven them."[^1]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he said to them, "Do you not know this parable? And how will you know all the parables?

<span class="v-num">14</span> The sower sows the word.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And these are the ones beside the road, where the word is sown, and when they hear, immediately Satan comes and takes away the word sown in their hearts.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And likewise these are the ones sown on the rocky <span class="added-text">(places)</span>, who, when they hear the word, immediately receive it with joy,

<span class="v-num">17</span> and have no root in themselves, but are <span class="added-text">(only)</span> for a time; then, when affliction or persecution arises because of the word, immediately they fall away.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And these are the ones sown into the thorns; these are the ones hearing the word,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and the cares of this age, and the deceit of riches, and the desires for the rest of things, entering in, choke the word, and it becomes unfruitful.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And these are the ones sown on the good ground, who hear the word and receive it, and bear fruit, one thirty<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>, and one sixty<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>, and one a hundred<span class="added-text">(fold)</span>."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And he said to them, "Does the lamp come, that it may be put under the <span class="added-text">(grain)</span> basket, or under the bed? <span class="added-text">(Is it)</span> not that it may be put on the lampstand?[^2]

<span class="v-num">22</span> For there is nothing hidden that shall not be made known; nor was anything made secret, but that it should come to light.[^3]

<span class="v-num">23</span> "If anyone has ears to hear, let him hear."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And he said to them, "Take heed what you hear. With the measure you measure, it will be measured to you, and <span class="added-text">(more)</span> will be added to you who hear."

<span class="v-num">25</span> "For whoever has, to him it shall be given; and whoever does not have, even what he has shall be taken from him."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And he said, "Thus is the kingdom of God, as if a man should cast the seed on the ground,[^4]

<span class="v-num">27</span> and should sleep and rise night and day, and the seed sprout and grow, he himself does not know how.

<span class="v-num">28</span> For the earth bears fruit of itself; first the blade, then the ear, then the full grain in the ear.

<span class="v-num">29</span> But when the fruit is ripe, immediately he sends forth the sickle, because the harvest has come."

<span class="v-num">30</span> And he said, "To what shall we liken the kingdom of God? Or with what parable shall we picture it?

<span class="v-num">31</span> <span class="added-text">(It is)</span> like a grain of mustard seed, which, when it is sown on the ground, is smaller than all the seeds that are on the earth;

<span class="v-num">32</span> but when it is sown, it comes up, and becomes greater than all the herbs, and makes great branches, so that the birds of the heaven are able to nest under its shadow."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And with many such parables he spoke the word to them, as they were able to hear <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">34</span> But without a parable he did not speak to them; but privately to his own disciples he explained all things.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And on that day, evening having come, he said to them, "Let us go across to the other side."

<span class="v-num">36</span> And having left the crowd, they took him along, as he was, in the boat. And other small boats also were with him.

<span class="v-num">37</span> And a great storm of wind arose, and the waves beat into the boat, so that it was now filling <span class="added-text">(with water)</span>.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he himself was in the stern, sleeping on the pillow; and they woke him, and said to him, "Teacher, is it no concern to you that we are perishing?"

<span class="v-num">39</span> And having awoken, he rebuked the wind, and said to the sea, "Be silent, be muzzled." And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.[^5]

<span class="v-num">40</span> And he said to them, "Why are you so fearful? How is it you have no faith?"

<span class="v-num">41</span> And they feared a great fear, and said to one another, "Who then is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?"[^6]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 밖에 있는 자들에게 비유가 오히려 완악하게 하는 결과(목적)를 낳음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(τεθῇ, ἐπιτεθῇ)으로 등불을 가져오는 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다. 수사의문문으로 '등불 아래 두려고?'가 아니라 '등잔대 위에 두려는' 목적이 자명함을 강조한다.
[^3]: ὃ ἐὰν μὴ φανερωθῇ는 ἐάν+가정법으로 '드러나지 않을' 아직 실현되지 않은 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이며, 뒤의 ἵνα εἰς φανερὸν ἔλθῃ는 감추어진 목적—결국 드러나기 위함—을 밝히는 목적절이다.
[^4]: ἐάν+가정법(βάλῃ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 일반적 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만약 ~한다면'의 뉘앙스를 지닌다. 여기서는 ὡς와 함께 쓰여 하나님 나라를 씨 뿌리는 행위에 비유하는 예화의 조건을 이끈다.
[^5]: πεφίμωσο는 완료 명령형으로, 단순히 '조용히 하라'가 아니라 '(계속) 재갈 물린 상태로 있으라'는 완결된 상태의 명령을 나타낸다.
[^6]: τίς ἄρα(그러므로 누구이기에)는 놀람 속에서 결론을 이끌어내는 수사적 의문으로, 예수의 정체에 대한 제자들의 경외를 강조한다.
