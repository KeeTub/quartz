<span class="v-num">1</span> And he began to speak to them in parables, "A man planted a vineyard, and set a fence around <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, and dug a <span class="alt-label">winepress</span><span class="alt-text">(=vat)</span>, and built a tower, and let it out to farmers, and went to a far country."

<span class="v-num">2</span> And at the season he sent a servant to the farmers, that he might receive from the farmers of the fruit of the vineyard.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> But they, having taken him, beat <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, and sent <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away empty.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And again he sent to them another servant; and that one, having stoned <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, they wounded in the head, and sent <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away having been dishonored.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And again he sent another; and that one they killed; and many others, some beating, and some killing.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Therefore, still having one beloved son of his <span class="added-text">(own)</span>, he sent him also to them last, saying, 'They will respect my son.'

<span class="v-num">7</span> But those farmers said among themselves, 'This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and the inheritance will be ours.'

<span class="v-num">8</span> And having taken him, they killed <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, and cast <span class="added-text">(him)</span> out of the vineyard.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Therefore what will the lord of the vineyard do? He will come and destroy the farmers, and will give the vineyard to others.

<span class="v-num">10</span> "Have you not even read this <span class="alt-label">scripture</span><span class="alt-text">(=passage)</span>? 'The stone which the builders rejected, this one became <span class="added-text">(the)</span> head of the corner.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> This came about from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, and it is marvelous in our eyes.'"[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And they sought to seize him, and feared the crowd; for they knew that he spoke the parable against them; and having left him, they went away.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And they sent to him some of the Pharisees and of the Herodians, that they might catch him in a word.[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And they, having come, said to him, "Teacher, we know that you are true, and you do not care about anyone; for you do not look at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> face of men, but teach the way of God <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=according to)</span> truth. Is it lawful to give <span class="alt-label">tribute</span><span class="alt-text">(=tax)</span> to Caesar, or not?

<span class="v-num">15</span> Shall we give, or shall we not give?" But he, knowing their hypocrisy, said to them, "Why do you test me? Bring me a denarius, that I may see <span class="added-text">(it)</span>."[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And they brought <span class="added-text">(it)</span>. And he said to them, "Whose <span class="added-text">(is)</span> this image and inscription?" And they said to him, "Caesar's."

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus, answering, said to them, "Render the things of Caesar to Caesar, and the things of God to God." And they marveled at him.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And Sadducees came to him, who say there is no resurrection; and they asked him, saying,

<span class="v-num">19</span> "Teacher, Moses wrote for us, that if anyone's brother dies, and leaves a wife, and leaves no children, that his brother should take his wife, and raise up seed for his brother.[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> There were seven brothers; and the first took a wife, and dying, left no seed.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And the second took her, and died, and neither did he leave seed; and the third likewise.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the seven took her, and left no seed. Last of all the woman also died.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Therefore in the resurrection, when they rise, whose wife will she be? For the seven had her as <span class="added-text">(their)</span> wife."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And Jesus, answering, said to them, "Is it not because of this that you are <span class="alt-label">deceived</span><span class="alt-text">(=err)</span>, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God?"

<span class="v-num">25</span> For when they rise from the dead, they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are like the angels who are in the heavens.

<span class="v-num">26</span> But concerning the dead, that they are raised, have you not read in the book of Moses, at the bush, how God spoke to him, saying, 'I <span class="added-text">(am)</span> the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob?'[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> He is not <span class="added-text">(the)</span> God of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, but <span class="added-text">(the)</span> God of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living; therefore you are greatly <span class="alt-label">deceived</span><span class="alt-text">(=err)</span>."

<span class="v-num">28</span> And one of the scribes, having come near, having heard them debating, knowing that he had answered them well, asked him, "Which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first commandment of all?"

<span class="v-num">29</span> And Jesus answered him, "The first of all the commandments <span class="added-text">(is)</span>, 'Hear, O Israel; the Lord our God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> one Lord.[^8]

<span class="v-num">30</span> and you shall love the Lord your God <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all your heart, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all your soul, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all your mind, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all your strength.' This <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> first commandment.[^9]

<span class="v-num">31</span> And <span class="added-text">(the)</span> second, like this, <span class="added-text">(is)</span>, 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.' There is no other commandment greater than these."[^10]

<span class="v-num">32</span> And the scribe said to him, "Well <span class="added-text">(said)</span>, Teacher, you have said <span class="added-text">(it)</span> <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=according to)</span> truth, that he is one God, and there is no other besides him.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And to love him <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all the heart, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all the understanding, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all the soul, and <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> all the strength, and to love the neighbor as oneself, is more than all the whole burnt offerings and sacrifices."

<span class="v-num">34</span> And Jesus, seeing him, that he answered wisely, said to him, "You are not far from the kingdom of God." And no one dared to question him any more.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And Jesus, answering, said, teaching in the temple, "How do the scribes say that the Christ is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of David?"

<span class="v-num">36</span> For David himself said in the Holy Spirit, 'The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at my right hand, until I put your enemies <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a footstool of your feet.'[^11]

<span class="v-num">37</span> David himself therefore calls him Lord; and how is he his son?" And the great crowd heard him gladly.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he said to them in his teaching, "Beware of the scribes, who desire to walk about in long robes, and <span class="added-text">(love)</span> greetings in the marketplaces,"

<span class="v-num">39</span> and the first <span class="alt-label">seats</span><span class="alt-text">(=chief seats)</span> in the synagogues, and the first places at the feasts;

<span class="v-num">40</span> who devour the houses of widows, and for a pretense pray at length; these will receive greater judgment."

<span class="v-num">41</span> And Jesus, having sat down opposite the treasury, watched how the crowd cast money into the treasury. And many rich <span class="added-text">(people)</span> cast in much;

<span class="v-num">42</span> and a certain poor widow came and cast in two <span class="alt-label">lepta</span><span class="alt-text">(=mites)</span>, which is a <span class="alt-label">quadrans</span><span class="alt-text">(=kodrantes)</span>.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And having called his disciples to <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, he said to them, "Truly I say to you, that this poor widow has cast in more than all those casting into the treasury."

<span class="v-num">44</span> For they all cast in out of their abundance; but she, out of her poverty, cast in all that she had, her whole living."

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(λάβῃ)은 종을 보낸 목적을 나타낸다: '열매를 받기 위하여'. 주인의 의도된 목적이 아직 실현되지 않은 상태에서 기대되는 결과를 표현한다.
[^2]: 시편 118:22의 인용으로, 버림받은 돌이 오히려 모퉁이의 머릿돌이 된다는 메시아 예언.
[^3]: 시편 118:23 인용이 앞 절에 이어짐.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀγρεύσωσι)으로 사람을 보낸 목적을 나타냄: 말로 예수를 옭아매려는 의도적 계략을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδω)은 목적절로, '보기 위하여'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 데나리온을 가져오게 하신 목적이 직접 확인하시려는 데 있음을 강조한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(λάβῃ, ἐξαναστήσῃ)으로 계대결혼법의 목적을 나타냄(신 25:5 규례).
[^7]: 출애굽기 3:6의 인용으로, 하나님이 현재 시제('나는 ~이다')로 말씀하신 것이 부활의 근거로 제시됨.
[^8]: 신명기 6:4의 쉐마 인용.
[^9]: 신명기 6:5 인용.
[^10]: 레위기 19:18 인용.
[^11]: 시편 110:1 인용으로, 그리스도가 다윗의 자손이자 다윗의 주가 됨을 논증함.
