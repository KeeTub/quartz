<span class="v-num">1</span> And he entered again into the synagogue, and there was a man there having his hand withered,

<span class="v-num">2</span> And they were watching him <span class="added-text">(to see)</span> whether he would heal him on the Sabbath, so that they might accuse him.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And he said to the man having his hand withered, "Rise up into the midst."

<span class="v-num">4</span> And he said to them, "Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good, or to do evil? To save <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=a soul)</span>, or to kill?" But they were silent.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And having looked around at them with anger, being grieved at the hardness of their heart, he said to the man, "Stretch out your hand." And he stretched <span class="added-text">(it)</span> out, and his hand was restored, healthy as the other.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And the Pharisees, having gone out, immediately took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And Jesus withdrew with his disciples to the <span class="alt-label">sea</span><span class="alt-text">(=lake)</span>; and a great multitude from Galilee followed him, and from Judea,

<span class="v-num">8</span> and from Jerusalem, and from Idumea, and beyond the Jordan; and those around Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, having heard what things he was doing, came to him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he told his disciples that a small boat should be <span class="alt-label">ready</span><span class="alt-text">(=wait)</span> for him, because of the crowd, so that they might not press upon him.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For he healed many, so that as many as had plagues fell upon him, that they might touch him;[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> And the unclean spirits, whenever they saw him, fell down before him, and cried out, saying, "You are the Son of God."[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And he strictly <span class="alt-label">rebuked</span><span class="alt-text">(=warned)</span> them many times, so that they should not make him known.[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he went up into the mountain, and called to <span class="added-text">(him)</span> those whom he himself willed; and they went to him.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And he <span class="alt-label">appointed</span><span class="alt-text">(=made)</span> twelve, that they might be with him, and that he might send them to preach,[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> and to have authority to heal diseases, and to cast out demons;

<span class="v-num">16</span> and to Simon he <span class="alt-label">gave</span><span class="alt-text">(=attached)</span> the name Peter;

<span class="v-num">17</span> and James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and to them he gave the names Boanerges, which is, Sons of Thunder;

<span class="v-num">18</span> and Andrew, and Philip, and Bartholomew, and Matthew, and Thomas, and James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Alphaeus, and Thaddaeus, and Simon the Canaanite,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and Judas Iscariot, who also <span class="alt-label">betrayed</span><span class="alt-text">(=delivered up)</span> him.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And they came into a house; and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> crowd gathered together again, so that they were not able even to eat bread.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And when his own <span class="added-text">(people)</span> heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, they went out to seize him; for they said, "He is out of his mind."

<span class="v-num">22</span> And the scribes who came down from Jerusalem said, "He has Beelzebub," and, "By the ruler of the demons he casts out demons."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And having called them to him, he spoke to them in parables, "How can Satan cast out Satan?

<span class="v-num">24</span> And if a kingdom is divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand;[^7]

<span class="v-num">25</span> and if a house is divided against itself, that house cannot stand.[^8]

<span class="v-num">26</span> And if Satan has risen up against himself and is divided, he cannot stand, but has an end.

<span class="v-num">27</span> No one can enter into the house of the strong <span class="added-text">(man)</span> and plunder his goods, unless he first binds the strong <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, and then he will plunder his house.[^9]

<span class="v-num">28</span> Truly I say to you, that all sins will be forgiven to the sons of men, and whatever blasphemies they may blaspheme;

<span class="v-num">29</span> but whoever blasphemes against the Holy Spirit does not have forgiveness forever, but is guilty of eternal <span class="alt-label">judgment</span><span class="alt-text">(=condemnation)</span>."[^10]

<span class="v-num">30</span> because they said, "He has an unclean spirit."

<span class="v-num">31</span> Therefore his brothers and mother came, and standing outside, they sent to him, calling him.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And a crowd was sitting around him; and they said to him, "Behold, your mother and your brothers are outside seeking you."

<span class="v-num">33</span> And he answered them, saying, "Who is my mother, or my brothers?"

<span class="v-num">34</span> And having looked around in a circle at those sitting around him, he said, "Behold, my mother and my brothers.

<span class="v-num">35</span> For whoever does the will of God, this one is my brother and my sister and mother."[^11]

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 그를 지켜본 목적이 고발함에 있었음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(προσκαρτερῇ, θλίβωσιν)이 이끄는 두 개의 목적절로, 예수께서 배를 대기시키라 명하신 의도(무리에게 밀리지 않기 위함)를 나타낸다. 아직 일어나지 않은 의도된 결과를 표현하는 전형적 목적절 구문이다.
[^3]: ἵνα αὐτοῦ ἅψωνται는 목적절로, 병자들이 몰려든 목적(그를 만지려고)을 가정법으로 나타낸다. 병 고침을 위해 예수님께 접촉하려는 간절한 의도를 강조한다.
[^4]: ὁ υἱὸς τοῦ Θεοῦ에서 관사 ὁ가 있어 '하나님의 아들'이 유일하고 특정한 존재임을 귀신들조차 인정함을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιήσωσι) 목적절로, 예수께서 엄히 경계하신 목적이 '자신을 나타내지 못하게 하려는' 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^6]: 두 개의 ἵνα 목적절이 열두 제자를 세우신 이중 목적, 곧 함께 있음과 파송하여 선포함을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(μερισθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절이다. '만일 나뉜다면'이라는 가정 하에 필연적 결과(설 수 없음)를 이끌어내는 논리적 화법이다.
[^8]: ἐάν+가정법(μερισθῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 전제하는 조건절로, '만일 ~한다면'이라는 가정적 상황을 설정한다. 집이 스스로 분열되는 가상의 경우를 상정하여 그 필연적 결과(설 수 없음)를 논증하는 구조다.
[^9]: ἐὰν μὴ...δήσῃ는 가정법을 사용한 조건절로, '먼저 강한 자를 묶지 않는 한'이라는 미실현 전제 조건을 나타낸다. 이 조건이 충족되어야만 비로소 집을 강탈하는 것이 가능해진다는 논리적 필수 전제를 강조한다.
[^10]: TR은 '영원한 심판'(αἰωνίου κρίσεως)으로 읽으나 일부 사본은 '영원한 죄'(αἰωνίου ἁμαρτήματος)로 읽는다. TR 본문을 따라 '심판'으로 번역하였다.
[^11]: θέλημα(뜻)는 하나님의 의지·계획을 가리키며, 이를 행함이 혈연을 초월한 새로운 가족 공동체의 기준이 됨을 보여준다.
