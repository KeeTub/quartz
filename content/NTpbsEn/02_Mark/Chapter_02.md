<span class="v-num">1</span> And again after <span class="added-text">(some)</span> days he entered into Capernaum, and it was heard that he was in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> house.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And immediately many were gathered together, so that there was no longer room, not even at the door, and he was speaking the word to them.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And they came to him, bringing a paralytic, being carried by four <span class="added-text">(men)</span>.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And not being able to come near to him because of the crowd, they uncovered the roof where he was, and having dug through, they let down the bed on which the paralytic was lying.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Jesus, having seen their faith, said to the paralytic, "Child, your sins have been forgiven you."[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But some of the scribes were sitting there, reasoning in their hearts,

<span class="v-num">7</span> "Why does this <span class="added-text">(man)</span> speak blasphemies thus? Who is able to forgive sins, except one, God?"

<span class="v-num">8</span> And Jesus, having immediately known in his spirit that they were reasoning thus within themselves, said to them, "Why do you reason these things in your hearts?

<span class="v-num">9</span> Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, 'Your sins have been forgiven,' or to say, 'Rise, and take up your bed, and walk'?

<span class="v-num">10</span> But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on the earth to forgive sins <span class="added-text">(he says to the paralytic)</span>,[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> I say to you, rise, and take up your bed, and go to your house."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And immediately he rose, and having taken up the bed, went out before all, so that all were amazed, and glorified God, saying, "We never saw <span class="added-text">(it)</span> thus."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he went out again beside the sea, and all the crowd came to him, and he was teaching them.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And passing by, he saw Levi the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Alphaeus sitting at the tax office, and said to him, "Follow me." And having risen, he followed him.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And it came to pass, as he was reclining in his house, that many tax collectors and sinners also reclined with Jesus and his disciples, for there were many, and they followed him.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the scribes and the Pharisees, having seen him eating with the tax collectors and sinners, said to his disciples, "Why does he eat and drink with the tax collectors and sinners?"

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus, having heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said to them, "The ones being strong have no need of a physician, but the ones having illness <span class="added-text">(do)</span>. I did not come to call <span class="added-text">(the)</span> righteous, but sinners to repentance."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And the disciples of John and the <span class="added-text">(disciples)</span> of the Pharisees were fasting, and they came and said to him, "Why do the disciples of John and the <span class="added-text">(disciples)</span> of the Pharisees fast, but your disciples do not fast?"

<span class="v-num">19</span> And Jesus said to them, "Can the sons of the bridal chamber fast while the bridegroom is with them? As long as they have the bridegroom with them, they cannot fast.[^3]

<span class="v-num">20</span> But days will come when the bridegroom will be taken away from them, and then they will fast in those days.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And no one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment; otherwise the new patch (=fullness) pulls away from the old, and a worse tear results.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And no one puts new wine into old wineskins; otherwise the new wine bursts the skins, and the wine is spilled out, and the skins are ruined. But new wine must be put into new wineskins."

<span class="v-num">23</span> And it came to pass <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he passed through the grainfields on the sabbath, and his disciples began to make <span class="added-text">(their)</span> way, plucking the ears <span class="added-text">(of grain)</span>.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And the Pharisees said to him, "Behold, why do they do on the sabbath what is not lawful?"

<span class="v-num">25</span> And he said to them, "Have you never read what David did, when he had need and was hungry, he and those with him

<span class="v-num">26</span> how he entered into the house of God in <span class="added-text">(the time of)</span> Abiathar the high priest, and ate the loaves of <span class="alt-label">presentation</span><span class="alt-text">(=showbread)</span>, which it is not lawful to eat except for the priests, and gave also to those being with him?"

<span class="v-num">27</span> And he said to them, "The sabbath was made for man, <span class="added-text">(and)</span> not man for the sabbath.

<span class="v-num">28</span> So then the Son of Man is Lord also of the sabbath."

[^1]: ἀφέωνταί는 완료수동태로 이미 이루어져 지속되는 용서의 상태를 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(εἰδῆτε)은 목적절로서 '너희로 알게 하려 함'이라는 인자의 사죄권 계시 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: '혼인집 자녀들(υἱοὶ τοῦ νυμφῶνος)'은 신랑의 결혼 잔치에 참석한 하객들을 가리키는 셈어적 관용 표현이다.
