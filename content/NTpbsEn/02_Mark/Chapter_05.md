<span class="v-num">1</span> And they came to the other side of the sea, into the region of the Gadarenes.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And as he went out of the boat, immediately a man <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> an unclean spirit met him out of the tombs,

<span class="v-num">3</span> who had his dwelling among the tombs, and no one could bind him even with chains,

<span class="v-num">4</span> because he had often been bound with fetters and chains, and the chains had been torn apart by him, and the fetters broken in pieces, and no one had strength to subdue him;

<span class="v-num">5</span> And always, night and day, he was in the mountains and in the tombs, crying out and cutting himself with stones.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But seeing Jesus from afar, he ran and worshiped him,

<span class="v-num">7</span> And crying out with a loud voice, he said, "What <span class="added-text">(is there)</span> to me and to you, Jesus, Son of the Most High God? I implore you by God, do not torment me,"

<span class="v-num">8</span> <span class="added-text">(For he was saying to him, "Come out of the man, (you)</span> unclean spirit.")

<span class="v-num">9</span> And he asked him, "What <span class="added-text">(is)</span> your name?" And he answered, saying, "Legion <span class="added-text">(is)</span> my name, for we are many."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And he begged him much, that he would not send them out of the region.[^1]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now there was there, near the mountains, a great herd of pigs feeding;

<span class="v-num">12</span> And all the demons begged him, saying, "Send us into the pigs, that we may enter into them."[^2]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And immediately Jesus permitted them. And the unclean spirits went out and entered into the pigs; and the herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea <span class="added-text">(they were about two thousand)</span>, and they were drowned in the sea.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And those feeding the pigs fled, and reported <span class="added-text">(it)</span> in the city and in the fields. And they went out to see what it was that had happened;

<span class="v-num">15</span> And they came to Jesus, and saw the one who had been demon-possessed sitting, and clothed, and in his right mind, the one who had had the legion; and they were afraid.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And those who had seen <span class="added-text">(it)</span> told them how it had happened to the demon-possessed man, and about the pigs.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And they began to beg him to depart from their region.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And as he was getting into the boat, the man who had been demon-possessed begged him that he might be with him.[^3]

<span class="v-num">19</span> But Jesus did not permit him, but said to him, "Go to your house, to your own <span class="added-text">(people)</span>, and report to them how much the Lord has done for you, and <span class="added-text">(how he)</span> had mercy on you."

<span class="v-num">20</span> And he departed and began to proclaim in Decapolis how much Jesus had done for him; and all marveled.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And when Jesus had crossed again in the boat to the other side, a great crowd gathered to him, and he was by the sea.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And behold, one of the rulers of the synagogue came, named Jairus, and seeing him, he fell at his feet;

<span class="v-num">23</span> and begged him greatly, saying, "My little daughter is at the point of death; <span class="added-text">(I beg you)</span> that you come and lay <span class="added-text">(your)</span> hands on her, so that she may be <span class="alt-label">healed</span><span class="alt-text">(=saved)</span>, and she will live."[^4]

<span class="v-num">24</span> And he went with him; and a great crowd followed him, and pressed upon him.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And a certain woman, having had a flow of blood twelve years,

<span class="v-num">26</span> and having suffered many things from many physicians, and having spent all that she had, and gaining no benefit, but rather having grown worse,

<span class="v-num">27</span> having heard about Jesus, came in the crowd behind, and touched his garment;

<span class="v-num">28</span> for she said, "If I touch even his garments, I will be <span class="alt-label">healed</span><span class="alt-text">(=saved)</span>."[^5]

<span class="v-num">29</span> And immediately the fountain of her blood was dried up; and she knew in her body that she was healed from the affliction.

<span class="v-num">30</span> And immediately Jesus, perceiving in himself the power that had gone out of him, turned around in the crowd, and said, "Who touched my garments?"

<span class="v-num">31</span> And his disciples said to him, "You see the crowd pressing on you, and <span class="added-text">(yet)</span> you say, 'Who touched me?'"

<span class="v-num">32</span> And he looked around to see her who had done this.

<span class="v-num">33</span> But the woman, fearing and trembling, knowing what had happened to her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And he said to her, "Daughter, your faith has <span class="alt-label">healed</span><span class="alt-text">(=saved)</span> you; go in peace, and be whole from your affliction."

<span class="v-num">35</span> While he was still speaking, they came from the ruler of the synagogue's house, saying, "Your daughter has died; why trouble the teacher any further?"

<span class="v-num">36</span> But Jesus, immediately hearing the word being spoken, said to the ruler of the synagogue, "Do not be afraid, only believe."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And he did not permit anyone to follow with him, except Peter and James and John the brother of James.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he came to the house of the ruler of the synagogue, and saw a tumult, <span class="added-text">(people)</span> weeping and wailing greatly.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And having entered, he said to them, "Why do you make a tumult and weep? The child has not died, but sleeps."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And they laughed him to scorn. But he, having put them all out, took the father of the child and the mother and those with him, and entered where the child was lying.

<span class="v-num">41</span> And taking the hand of the child, he said to her, "Talitha, cumi;" which is, being translated, "Little girl, <span class="added-text">(I say to you)</span>, arise."

<span class="v-num">42</span> And immediately the girl arose and walked, for she was twelve years old; and they were amazed with great amazement.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And he strictly ordered them that no one should know this; and he said that something should be given to her to eat.[^6]
