# Mark 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> <span class="added-text">(The)</span> beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God.

<span class="v-num">2</span> As it is written in the prophets, "Behold, I send my messenger before your face, who will prepare your way before you.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> A voice of one crying in the wilderness, 'Prepare the way of the Lord; make his paths straight.'"

<span class="v-num">4</span> John came, baptizing in the wilderness and preaching a baptism of repentance <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=unto)</span> forgiveness of sins.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And all the Judean country and the Jerusalemites went out to him, and were all baptized by him in the Jordan river, confessing their sins.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now John was clothed <span class="added-text">(with)</span> camel's hair, and <span class="added-text">(had)</span> a leather belt around his waist, and ate locusts and wild honey.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And he preached, saying, "<span class="added-text">(The)</span> one stronger than me comes after me, of whom I am not worthy, having stooped down, to loose the strap of his sandals.

<span class="v-num">8</span> I indeed baptized you <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> water, but he will baptize you <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=with)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit."

<span class="v-num">9</span> And it came to pass in those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee, and was baptized by John into the Jordan.

<span class="v-num">10</span> And immediately, coming up from the water, he saw the heavens being torn open, and the Spirit like a dove descending upon him.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And a voice came from the heavens, "You are my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased."

<span class="v-num">12</span> And immediately the Spirit drove him out into the wilderness.[^2]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And he was there in the wilderness forty days, being tempted by Satan, and was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered to him.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Now after John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and saying, "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God has drawn near; repent, and believe in the gospel."

<span class="v-num">16</span> And walking beside the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea; for they were fishermen.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And Jesus said to them, "Come after me, and I will make you become fishers of men."

<span class="v-num">18</span> And immediately, having left their nets, they followed him.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And having gone on a little from there, he saw James the <span class="added-text">(son)</span> of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the boat mending the nets.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And immediately he called them; and having left their father Zebedee in the boat with the hired servants, they went away after him.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they went into Capernaum; and immediately on the Sabbath, having entered into the synagogue, he taught.[^3]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And they were astonished at his teaching; for he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit, and he cried out,

<span class="v-num">24</span> saying, "Ah! What <span class="added-text">(is there)</span> to us and to you, Jesus <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know you who you are, the Holy One of God."

<span class="v-num">25</span> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be silent, and come out of him."

<span class="v-num">26</span> And the unclean spirit, having convulsed him, and having cried with a loud voice, came out of him.

<span class="v-num">27</span> And they were all amazed, so as to question among themselves, saying, "What is this? What <span class="added-text">(is)</span> this new teaching, that with authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him?"

<span class="v-num">28</span> And his fame went out immediately into all the surrounding region of Galilee.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And immediately, having come out of the synagogue, they went into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John.

<span class="v-num">30</span> Now Simon's mother-in-law lay sick of a fever, and immediately they told him about her.

<span class="v-num">31</span> And having come, he raised her up, having taken her by the hand; and the fever left her immediately, and she served them.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And evening having come, when the sun set, they brought to him all who were ill and those who were demon-possessed.

<span class="v-num">33</span> And the whole city was gathered together at the door.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And he healed many who were ill with various diseases, and cast out many demons; and he did not permit the demons to speak, because they knew him.

<span class="v-num">35</span> And in the morning, having risen very early while still dark, he went out, and departed into a desolate place, and there he prayed.

<span class="v-num">36</span> And Simon and those with him pursued after him;

<span class="v-num">37</span> and having found him, they said to him, "All are seeking you."

<span class="v-num">38</span> And he said to them, "Let us go into the neighboring towns, that I may preach there also; for this <span class="added-text">(purpose)</span> I have come out."[^4]

<span class="v-num">39</span> And he was preaching in their synagogues throughout all Galilee, and casting out the demons.

<span class="v-num">40</span> And a leper came to him, begging him and kneeling <span class="added-text">(before)</span> him, and saying to him, "If you are willing, you are able to make me clean."[^5]

<span class="v-num">41</span> And Jesus, moved with compassion, having stretched out <span class="added-text">(his)</span> hand, touched him, and said to him, "I am willing, be cleansed."

<span class="v-num">42</span> And when he had spoken, immediately the leprosy departed from him, and he was cleansed.

<span class="v-num">43</span> And having sternly warned him, immediately he sent him away,

<span class="v-num">44</span> and said to him, "See <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you say nothing to anyone; but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, for a testimony to them."

<span class="v-num">45</span> But he, having gone out, began to proclaim <span class="added-text">(it)</span> much and to spread the <span class="alt-label">report</span><span class="alt-text">(=word)</span> abroad, so that he could no longer openly enter into a city; but he was outside in desolate places, and they came to him from every quarter.

[^1]: 말라기 3:1과 이사야 40:3을 결합한 인용으로, 마가는 이를 선지자들의 글로 소개한다.
[^2]: ἐκβάλλει는 역사적 현재형이나 개역체 관례에 따라 본문은 과거형으로 옮겼다.
[^3]: εἰσπορεύονται는 역사적 현재형이나 개역체 관례에 따라 과거형으로 옮겼다.
[^4]: ἵνα κηρύξω(가정법)는 목적을 나타내는 ἵνα절로, '전파하기 위하여'라는 뜻이다.
[^5]: ἐὰν θέλῃς는 3급 조건문으로, 아직 이루어지지 않은 가능성을 나타낸다.
