<span class="v-num">1</span> And immediately at dawn, the chief priests, having made a council with the elders and scribes, and the whole Sanhedrin, having bound Jesus, led <span class="added-text">(him)</span> away and delivered <span class="added-text">(him)</span> to Pilate.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And Pilate asked him, "Are you the king of the Jews?" And he, answering, said to him, "You say <span class="added-text">(so)</span>."

<span class="v-num">3</span> And the chief priests accused him of many things,

<span class="v-num">4</span> And Pilate again asked him, saying, "Do you answer nothing? Behold, how many things they testify against you."

<span class="v-num">5</span> But Jesus no longer answered anything, so that Pilate marveled.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> feast he used to release to them one prisoner, whomever they requested.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And there was one called Barabbas, bound with the fellow rebels, who had committed murder in the rebellion.

<span class="v-num">8</span> And the crowd, having cried out, began to ask <span class="added-text">(him to do)</span> just as he always did for them.

<span class="v-num">9</span> And Pilate answered them, saying, "Do you want <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I release to you the king of the Jews?"

<span class="v-num">10</span> For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him up because of envy.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But the chief priests stirred up the crowd, that he should rather release Barabbas to them.[^1]

<span class="v-num">12</span> And Pilate, answering again, said to them, "What then do you want <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I do <span class="added-text">(with)</span> the one you call king of the Jews?"

<span class="v-num">13</span> And they cried out again, "Crucify him."

<span class="v-num">14</span> And Pilate said to them, "Why, what evil has he done?" But they cried out even more, "Crucify him."

<span class="v-num">15</span> And Pilate, wanting to do <span class="added-text">(what was)</span> sufficient <span class="added-text">(for)</span> the crowd, released Barabbas to them; and he delivered Jesus, having scourged <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, that he might be crucified.[^2]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And the soldiers led him away inside the courtyard, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> praetorium, and they called together the whole cohort.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And they clothed him in purple, and they put on him, having woven <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, a crown of thorns.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And they began to greet him, "Hail, king of the Jews."

<span class="v-num">19</span> And they struck his head with a reed, and spat on him, and, bending the knees, worshiped him.

<span class="v-num">20</span> And when they had mocked him, they stripped him of the purple, and put his own garments on him; and they led him out, that they might crucify him.[^3]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And they compelled a certain Simon <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Cyrene, passing by, coming from the field, the father of Alexander and Rufus, that he might carry his cross.[^4]

<span class="v-num">22</span> And they brought him to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> place Golgotha, which is, being translated, Place of a Skull.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And they gave him wine mixed with myrrh to drink; but he did not take <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.

<span class="v-num">24</span> And having crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots for them, who should take what.

<span class="v-num">25</span> And it was <span class="added-text">(the)</span> third hour, and they crucified him.

<span class="v-num">26</span> And the inscription of his charge was written, "The King of the Jews."

<span class="v-num">27</span> And with him they crucified two robbers, one on <span class="added-text">(his)</span> right and one on his left.

<span class="v-num">28</span> And the scripture was fulfilled which says, "And he was reckoned with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> lawless."[^5]

<span class="v-num">29</span> And those passing by blasphemed him, shaking their heads and saying, "Aha, the one destroying the temple and building <span class="added-text">(it)</span> in three days,

<span class="v-num">30</span> save yourself, and come down from the cross."

<span class="v-num">31</span> Likewise also the chief priests, mocking among themselves with the scribes, said, "He saved others, himself he cannot save.

<span class="v-num">32</span> Let the Christ, the king of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe." And those crucified with him also reproached him.[^6]

<span class="v-num">33</span> And when <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sixth hour had come, darkness came over the whole land until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ninth hour.

<span class="v-num">34</span> And at the ninth hour Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying, "Eloi, Eloi, lama sabachthani?" which is, being translated, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?"

<span class="v-num">35</span> And some of those standing by, having heard <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, said, "Behold, he is calling Elijah."

<span class="v-num">36</span> And one ran and, having filled a sponge full of vinegar, and having put <span class="added-text">(it)</span> on a reed, gave him to drink, saying, "Let <span class="added-text">(him)</span> be, let us see if Elijah comes to take him down."

<span class="v-num">37</span> And Jesus, having let out a loud voice, breathed his last.

<span class="v-num">38</span> And the veil of the temple was torn in two, from top to bottom.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And the centurion, standing by opposite him, having seen that he thus cried out <span class="added-text">(and)</span> breathed his last, said, "Truly this man was <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son of God."

<span class="v-num">40</span> And there were also women looking on from afar, among whom was also Mary Magdalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salome,

<span class="v-num">41</span> who also, when he was in Galilee, followed him and ministered to him, and many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem.

<span class="v-num">42</span> And when evening had already come, since it was <span class="added-text">(the day of)</span> preparation, that is, the day before the Sabbath,

<span class="v-num">43</span> Joseph of Arimathea came, a prominent council member, who himself was also waiting for the kingdom of God; having taken courage, he went in to Pilate and asked for the body of Jesus.

<span class="v-num">44</span> And Pilate marveled if he had already died; and having called the centurion, he asked him if he had died long ago.

<span class="v-num">45</span> And having learned <span class="added-text">(this)</span> from the centurion, he granted the body to Joseph.

<span class="v-num">46</span> And having bought fine linen, and having taken him down, he wrapped <span class="added-text">(him)</span> in the linen, and laid him in a tomb which had been hewn out of rock; and he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb.

<span class="v-num">47</span> And Mary Magdalene and Mary the <span class="added-text">(mother)</span> of Joses looked on where he was laid.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀπολύσῃ)으로 대제사장들이 무리를 선동한 '목적'을 나타낸다. 즉 무리를 충동질한 이유가 바라바 석방을 요구하게 하려는 데 있었음을 보여준다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(σταυρωθῇ)은 빌라도가 예수를 넘겨준 목적을 나타낸다. '십자가에 못박히게 하려고'라는 의도된 결과를 강조하는 목적절이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(σταυρώσωσιν)은 목적절로, '십자가에 못박기 위하여'라는 끌고 나간 행동의 의도·목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἄρῃ)은 목적절로, 시몬을 강제로 붙든 목적이 '그의 십자가를 지게 하려는 것'임을 나타낸다. 즉 병사들의 행동 의도를 분명히 밝히는 구문이다.
[^5]: 이 구절은 TR(공인본문) 전통에 포함되나 대다수 현대 비평본(NA/UBS)은 사본상 근거 부족(주요 고대 사본에 부재)으로 제외한다. 이 프로젝트는 TR 원문을 기준으로 삼는 원칙에 따라 그대로 유지한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ἴδωμεν, πιστεύσωμεν) 목적절로, '보고 믿기 위하여'라는 의도를 나타낸다. 조롱조의 도전이지만 형식상 목적을 표현하는 구문이다.
