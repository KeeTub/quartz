# 3 John 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> The elder to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beloved Gaius, whom I love in truth.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Beloved, concerning all things I pray <span class="added-text">(that)</span> you prosper and be in health, even as your soul prospers.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For I rejoiced greatly, <span class="added-text">(when the)</span> brothers came and testified of your truth, even as you walk in truth.

<span class="v-num">4</span> I have no greater joy than this, that I hear my children walking in truth.[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Beloved, you do a faithful thing whatever you <span class="alt-label">work</span><span class="alt-text">(=do)</span> for the brothers and for the strangers,[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> who testified of your love before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> church, whom you will do well, having sent <span class="added-text">(them)</span> forward worthily of God.

<span class="v-num">7</span> For they went out for the sake of the name, taking nothing from the Gentiles.

<span class="v-num">8</span> We therefore ought to receive such <span class="added-text">(ones)</span>, that we may become fellow workers with the truth.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> I wrote to the church, but Diotrephes, the one loving to be first among them, does not receive us.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Because of this, if I come, I will remind <span class="added-text">(them)</span> of his works which he does, prating against us with evil words; and not being content with these, neither does he himself receive the brothers, and he forbids those wanting <span class="added-text">(to)</span>, and casts <span class="added-text">(them)</span> out of the church.[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Beloved, do not imitate the evil, but the good. The one doing good is of God; but the one doing evil has not seen God.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Demetrius has been testified of by all, and by the truth itself; and we also testify, and you know that our testimony is true.

<span class="v-num">13</span> I had many things to write, but I do not wish to write to you with ink and <span class="alt-label">pen</span><span class="alt-text">(=reed pen)</span>;

<span class="v-num">14</span> But I hope to see you soon, and we will speak mouth to mouth.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to you. The friends greet you. Greet the friends by name.

[^1]: ἵνα+직설법이 아닌 가정법(ἀκούω)이 쓰였으나 목적이 아니라 '기쁨'의 내용을 풀어주는 요한 특유의 부연(설명)절이다.
[^2]: ὃ ἐὰν+가정법은 '무엇이든지'라는 뜻의 부정관계문으로, 대상을 특정하지 않고 미래에 실현될 가능성 있는 행위 전체를 포괄하는 조건절이다. 형제와 나그네에게 행하는 어떤 종류의 섬김이든 신실함의 증거가 됨을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(γινώμεθα): 이러한 자들을 영접하는 목적이 진리의 동역자가 되는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἐὰν+가정법(ἔλθω): 아직 실현되지 않은 방문의 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건문이다.
