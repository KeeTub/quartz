# Ephesians 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children,

<span class="v-num">2</span> And walk in love, just as Christ also loved us, and gave himself up for us <span class="added-text">(as)</span> an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweet-smelling <span class="alt-label">fragrance</span><span class="alt-text">(=savor)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> But sexual immorality and all uncleanness or covetousness, let it not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints,

<span class="v-num">4</span> and shameful behavior, and foolish talking or coarse jesting, which are not fitting, but rather thanksgiving.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For this you know, that every fornicator, or unclean <span class="added-text">(person)</span>, or covetous <span class="added-text">(person)</span>, who is an idolater, has no inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Let no one deceive you with empty words, for because of these things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Therefore do not become partakers with them.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For you were once darkness, but now <span class="added-text">(you are)</span> light in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord; walk as children of light,[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> for the fruit of the Spirit <span class="added-text">(is)</span> in all goodness and righteousness and truth,

<span class="v-num">10</span> proving what is well-pleasing to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And do not participate in the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather even expose <span class="added-text">(them)</span>.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For the things done by them in secret, it is shameful even to speak of.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But all things being exposed by the light are made manifest, for everything that is made manifest is light.

<span class="v-num">14</span> Therefore he says, "Awake, you who sleep, and arise from the dead, and Christ will shine upon you."[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> See then how carefully you walk, not as unwise, but as wise,

<span class="v-num">16</span> redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Because of this do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And do not be drunk with wine, in which is dissipation, but be filled with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit,[^5]

<span class="v-num">19</span> speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody in your heart to the Lord,

<span class="v-num">20</span> giving thanks always for all things in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God, even the Father,

<span class="v-num">21</span> submitting yourselves to one another in the fear of God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Wives, submit to your own husbands, as to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">23</span> because the husband is the head of the wife, as also Christ is the head of the church, and he is the savior of the body.[^6]

<span class="v-num">24</span> But as the church is subject to Christ, so also let the wives <span class="added-text">(be subject)</span> to their own husbands in everything.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Husbands, love your own wives, just as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself up for her,

<span class="v-num">26</span> that he might sanctify her, having cleansed <span class="added-text">(her)</span> with the washing of water by the word,[^7]

<span class="v-num">27</span> that he might present her to himself <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such things, but that she should be holy and without blemish.[^8]

<span class="v-num">28</span> So husbands ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. He who loves his own wife loves himself.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For no one ever hated his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, just as the Lord also <span class="added-text">(does)</span> the church.

<span class="v-num">30</span> For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.

<span class="v-num">31</span> "For this reason a man will leave his father and mother, and will be joined to his wife, and the two will become one flesh."[^9]

<span class="v-num">32</span> This mystery is great; but I speak concerning Christ and concerning the church.[^10]

<span class="v-num">33</span> Nevertheless let each one of you in particular so love his own wife as himself, and let the wife <span class="added-text">(see)</span> that she reverences her husband.[^11]

[^1]: 예물과 제물, 향기로운 냄새라는 표현은 구약 제사법(레위기 1:9 등)의 언어를 그대로 가져온 것이다.
[^2]: 탐욕자를 우상숭배자와 동일시함은 탐욕이 하나님 대신 재물을 섬기는 것임을 보여준다.
[^3]: ἦτε(임퍼펙트)는 과거의 지속적 상태를, νῦν δέ는 현재의 새로운 신분 전환을 대조한다.
[^4]: 이 인용은 이사야 60:1 등을 바울이 자유롭게 종합·인용한 것으로 보인다.
[^5]: πληροῦσθε는 현재수동명령으로 일회적 사건이 아니라 지속적으로 채움을 받으라는 뜻이다.
[^6]: 머리(κεφαλή)는 단순 권위가 아니라 그리스도가 교회의 구원자이심과 결부된 근원·생명 공급의 개념을 포함한다.
[^7]: ἵνα ἁγιάσῃ는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 절로, 그리스도의 구속 행위의 목적이 성도의 거룩함에 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: 두 ἵνα절(παραστήσῃ, ᾖ)이 나란히 목적을 이루는데, 전자는 결과적 상태를, 후자는 그 상태의 본질(거룩·흠없음)을 규정한다.
[^9]: 창세기 2:24의 직접 인용으로 결혼 제도의 근본 원리를 제시한다.
[^10]: μυστήριον은 이전에 감추어졌다가 이제 계시된 하나님의 경륜을 가리키며, 여기서는 그리스도와 교회의 연합을 뜻한다.
[^11]: ἵνα+가정법(φοβῆται)은 여기서 목적절이 아니라 명령의 뜻으로 쓰인 독립적 용법으로, 남편에 대한 아내의 마땅한 태도(존경)를 명하는 표현이다. 문법적으로는 목적절 형태를 빌려 완곡한 명령을 나타낸다.
