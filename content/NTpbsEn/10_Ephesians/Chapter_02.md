<span class="v-num">1</span> And you <span class="added-text">(were)</span> dead in your trespasses and sins,

<span class="v-num">2</span> in which you once walked according to the age of this world, according to the ruler of the authority of the air, the spirit now working in the sons of disobedience,

<span class="v-num">3</span> among whom we all also once lived in the lusts of our flesh, doing the desires of the flesh and of the thoughts, and we were children of wrath by nature, as also the rest;

<span class="v-num">4</span> But God, being rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us,

<span class="v-num">5</span> even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ <span class="added-text">((by)</span> grace you are <span class="added-text">(having been)</span> saved);

<span class="v-num">6</span> and raised <span class="added-text">(us)</span> up together, and made <span class="added-text">(us)</span> sit together in the heavenlies in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">7</span> so that He might show in the ages to come the exceeding riches of His grace in kindness toward us in Christ Jesus.[^1]

<span class="v-num">8</span> For by grace you are <span class="added-text">(having been)</span> saved through faith, and this <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not of yourselves, <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> the gift of God,

<span class="v-num">9</span> not of works, so that no one may boast.[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand, so that we should walk in them.[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Therefore remember, that you <span class="added-text">(were)</span> once <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles in the flesh, who are called Uncircumcision by the so-called Circumcision in the flesh made by hands—

<span class="v-num">12</span> that you were at that time without Christ, alienated from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers to the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without God in the world.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But now in Christ Jesus, you who were once far off have become near by the blood of Christ.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For He Himself is our peace, who made both one, and broke down the middle wall of partition,

<span class="v-num">15</span> having abolished in His flesh the enmity, the law of commandments in ordinances, so that He might create the two in Himself into one new man, making peace,[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> and might reconcile both in one body to God through the cross, having killed the enmity by it,[^5]

<span class="v-num">17</span> And having come, He preached peace to you who were far off, and to those who were near.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For through Him we both have <span class="added-text">(our)</span> access in one Spirit to the Father.

<span class="v-num">19</span> So then you are no longer strangers and foreigners, but fellow citizens with the saints, and members of the household of God,

<span class="v-num">20</span> having been built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ Himself being the chief cornerstone,

<span class="v-num">21</span> in whom every building, being fitted together, grows into a holy temple in the Lord,

<span class="v-num">22</span> in whom you also are being built together into a dwelling place of God in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐνδείξηται)으로 하나님의 궁극적 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^2]: ἵνα μή+가정법(καυχήσηται)으로 자랑을 막으려는 부정적 목적절이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(περιπατήσωμεν)으로 선행의 목적을 밝히는 절이다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(κτίσῃ)으로 새사람을 지으시려는 목적을 나타내며 16절까지 이어진다.
[^5]: 15절의 ἵνα절에 이어지는 가정법(ἀποκαταλλάξῃ)으로 동일한 목적절 구조 안에 있다.
