# Ephesians 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> Children, obey your parents in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, for this is right.

<span class="v-num">2</span> "Honor your father and mother," which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> first commandment with a promise,

<span class="v-num">3</span> "that it may be well with you, and you may be long-lived on the earth."[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And fathers, do not provoke your children to anger, but bring them up in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> discipline and admonition of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Slaves, obey your masters according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as to Christ,

<span class="v-num">6</span> not according to eye-service as men-pleasers, but as slaves of Christ, doing the will of God from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> soul,

<span class="v-num">7</span> serving with good will as to the Lord, and not to men,

<span class="v-num">8</span> knowing that whatever good thing each one may do, this he will receive back from the Lord, whether slave or free.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And masters, do the same things to them, giving up threatening, knowing that your own Master also is in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens, and there is no respect of persons with him.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Finally, my brothers, be strengthened in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, and in the might of his strength.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Put on the full armor of God, so that you may be able to stand against the schemes of the devil.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For our wrestling is not against blood and flesh, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the world-rulers of the darkness of this age, against the spiritual <span class="added-text">(hosts)</span> of evil in the heavenly <span class="added-text">(places)</span>.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Because of this, take up the full armor of God, so that you may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done everything, to stand.[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Stand, therefore, having girded your loins with truth, and having put on the breastplate of righteousness,

<span class="v-num">15</span> and having shod the feet with the readiness of the gospel of peace;

<span class="v-num">16</span> above all, taking up the shield of faith, with which you will be able to quench all the fiery darts of the evil one;

<span class="v-num">17</span> And receive the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of God;

<span class="v-num">18</span> praying through all prayer and supplication at every time in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, and watching to this same end in all perseverance and supplication concerning all the saints,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and for me, that a word may be given to me in the opening of my mouth, with boldness to make known the mystery of the gospel,[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> for which I am an ambassador in a chain, that in it I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> But that you also may know the things concerning me, what I am doing, Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, will make known to you all things,[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> whom I sent to you for this same purpose, that you may know the things concerning us, and <span class="added-text">(that)</span> he may comfort your hearts.[^8]

<span class="v-num">23</span> Peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> to the brothers, and love with faith, from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">24</span> Grace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all the ones loving our Lord Jesus Christ in incorruption. Amen. <span class="added-text">(This was)</span> written to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Ephesians from Rome through Tychicus.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법으로 앞 계명이 주어진 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἐάν+가정법으로 각자의 행위에 따른 미래의 보응을 나타내는 조건절이다.
[^3]: πρὸς τό+부정사 구문으로 갑주를 입는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법으로 갑주를 취하는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+희구법(δοθείη)으로 바울 자신을 위한 기도 제목의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법으로 사신 노릇의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법으로 두기고를 보내는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^8]: ἵνα+가정법(γνῶτε, παρακαλέσῃ) 목적절로, 두기고를 보낸 목적(소식을 알게 하고 위로하게 함)을 나타낸다.
