# Ephesians 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> For this reason I, Paul, the prisoner of Christ Jesus for you Gentiles—

<span class="v-num">2</span> if indeed you have heard of the stewardship of the grace of God given to me <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> you,

<span class="v-num">3</span> that according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> revelation he made known to me the mystery, as I wrote before in brief,

<span class="v-num">4</span> in regard to which, reading <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, you are able to understand my understanding in the mystery of Christ,

<span class="v-num">5</span> which in other generations was not made known to the sons of men, as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit;

<span class="v-num">6</span> that the Gentiles are fellow heirs and <span class="added-text">(a)</span> fellow body and fellow partakers of his promise in Christ, through the gospel,

<span class="v-num">7</span> of which I became a minister according to the gift of the grace of God given to me according to the working of his power.

<span class="v-num">8</span> To me, the very least of all the saints, this grace was given, to proclaim among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and to enlighten all as to what is the <span class="alt-label">fellowship</span><span class="alt-text">(=administration)</span> of the mystery which has been hidden from the ages in God who created all things through Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">10</span> so that now to the rulers and to the authorities in the heavenly places might be made known through the church the manifold wisdom of God,[^1]

<span class="v-num">11</span> according to the eternal purpose which he made in Christ Jesus our Lord,

<span class="v-num">12</span> in whom we have the boldness and the access with confidence through faith <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=of)</span> him.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore I ask <span class="added-text">(you)</span> not to lose heart at my tribulations for you, which is your glory.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For this reason I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">15</span> from whom every family in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens and on earth is named,

<span class="v-num">16</span> that he might grant to you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with power through his Spirit <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=into)</span> the inner man,[^2]

<span class="v-num">17</span> <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Christ may dwell through faith in your hearts;

<span class="v-num">18</span> rooted and grounded in love, that you may be fully able to grasp with all the saints what is the breadth and length and depth and height,[^3]

<span class="v-num">19</span> and to know the love of Christ which surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled unto all the fullness of God.[^4]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now to him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us,

<span class="v-num">21</span> to him <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory in the church in Christ Jesus, to all the generations of the age of the ages. Amen.

---

[^1]: ἵνα+수동태 가정법(γνωρισθῇ)으로 목적절을 이루니, 교회를 통해 천상의 권세들에게 하나님의 지혜가 알려지는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법(δῴη)으로 목적절을 이루니, 하나님이 성령을 통해 속사람을 강건케 하시는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἐξισχύσητε)으로 목적절을 이루니, 사랑에 뿌리박힌 결과로 모든 성도와 함께 그리스도 사랑의 차원을 깨닫게 되는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(πληρωθῆτε)으로 목적절을 이루니, 그리스도의 사랑을 앎으로써 하나님의 충만하심에 이르는 목적을 나타낸다.
