<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, to the saints who are in Ephesus and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faithful in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">2</span> Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Blessed <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly <span class="added-text">(places)</span> in Christ,[^1]

<span class="v-num">4</span> just as he chose us in him before <span class="added-text">(the)</span> foundation of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, that we should be holy and blameless before him in love,[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> having predestined us to adoption through Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,

<span class="v-num">6</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> praise of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of his grace, in which he freely gave us grace in the beloved <span class="added-text">(One)</span>,

<span class="v-num">7</span> in whom we have the redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of trespasses, according to the riches of his grace,

<span class="v-num">8</span> which he made to abound toward us in all wisdom and prudence,

<span class="v-num">9</span> having made known to us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he purposed in himself,[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> administration of the fullness of the times, to sum up all things in Christ, both which <span class="added-text">(are)</span> in the heavens and which <span class="added-text">(are)</span> upon the earth—in him,[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> in him, in whom also we obtained <span class="added-text">(an)</span> inheritance, having been predestined according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> purpose of him who works all things according to the counsel of his will,

<span class="v-num">12</span> that we should be to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> praise of his glory, we who first hoped in Christ.[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> in whom you also, having heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation, in whom also, having believed, you were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> who is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> down payment of our inheritance, unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> redemption of the possession, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> praise of his glory.[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> For this reason, I also, having heard of the faith among you in the Lord Jesus, and the love toward all the saints,

<span class="v-num">16</span> do not cease giving thanks for you, making mention <span class="added-text">(of you)</span> in my prayers,

<span class="v-num">17</span> that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give to you <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirit of wisdom and revelation in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of him,[^8]

<span class="v-num">18</span> the eyes of your understanding having been enlightened, so that you may know what is the hope of his calling, and what <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,[^9]

<span class="v-num">19</span> and what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the exceeding greatness of his power toward us who believe, according to the working of the might of his strength,

<span class="v-num">20</span> which he worked in Christ, having raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, and set <span class="added-text">(him)</span> at his right hand in the heavenly <span class="added-text">(places)</span>,[^10]

<span class="v-num">21</span> far above all rule and authority and power and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age, but also in the <span class="added-text">(age)</span> to come,

<span class="v-num">22</span> and put all things under his feet, and gave him <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> head over all things to the church,

<span class="v-num">23</span> which is his body, the fullness of him who fills all in all.[^11]

[^1]: εὐλογήσας는 부정과거 분사로 하나님이 이미 완료하신 축복 행위를 나타내며, 그리스도 안에서 단번에 주어진 영적 복을 강조한다.
[^2]: εἶναι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사로 택하심의 목적이 거룩함과 흠 없음에 있음을 보여준다.
[^3]: μυστήριον(비밀)은 이전에 감추어졌다가 이제 그리스도 안에서 계시된 하나님의 구원 계획을 가리킨다.
[^4]: ἀνακεφαλαιώσασθαι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사로 만물을 그리스도 안에서 통일하시려는 하나님의 경륜을 표현한다.
[^5]: εἰς τὸ εἶναι는 εἰς τό+부정사 목적구문으로 택하심의 궁극 목적이 하나님의 영광 찬송에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἀκούσαντες와 πιστεύσαντες는 부정과거 분사로 듣고 믿은 후 인치심을 받은 구원의 순서를 보여준다.
[^7]: ἀρραβών(보증금)은 장차 온전히 받을 기업을 미리 보증하는 성령의 역할을 가리키는 상업 용어다.
[^8]: ἵνα+희구법 δῴη는 바울의 간구를 나타내는 목적/소원절로, 하나님이 주시기를 바라는 기도의 내용을 표현한다.
[^9]: εἰς τὸ εἰδέναι는 목적 부정사구문으로 마음의 눈이 밝아지는 목적이 온전히 아는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^10]: ἐγείρας와 ἐκάθισεν은 부정과거로 그리스도의 부활과 승귀가 이미 성취된 역사적 사건임을 강조한다.
[^11]: πλήρωμα(충만)는 그리스도의 몸인 교회가 그의 충만하심을 나타내는 그릇임을 의미하는 핵심 신학 용어다.
