# Colossians 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> If then you were raised together with Christ, seek the things above, where Christ is, sitting at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of God.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Think on the things above, not the things on the earth.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For you died, and your life has been hidden with Christ in God.

<span class="v-num">4</span> When Christ, our life, is manifested, then you also will be manifested with him in glory.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> Put to death therefore your members which are on the earth: fornication, uncleanness, passion, evil desire, and covetousness, which is idolatry,

<span class="v-num">6</span> On account of which things the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience,

<span class="v-num">7</span> among whom you also walked once, when you lived in them.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But now you also, put off all these: anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, shameful speech out of your mouth.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Do not lie to one another, having put off the old man with his deeds,

<span class="v-num">10</span> and having put on the new <span class="added-text">(man)</span>, the one being renewed unto full knowledge according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> image of the one who created him,[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> where there is not Greek and Jew, circumcision and uncircumcision, barbarian, Scythian, slave, free, but Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> all things and in all.[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Put on therefore, as <span class="added-text">(the)</span> elect of God, holy and beloved, hearts of compassion, kindness, humility, meekness, longsuffering,

<span class="v-num">13</span> bearing with one another, and forgiving each other, if anyone has a complaint against anyone; just as Christ also forgave you, so also you <span class="added-text">(do)</span>.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> And above all these things <span class="added-text">(put on)</span> love, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> bond of perfectness.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, unto which also you were called in one body; and become thankful.[^6]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly, in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing yourselves, with psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your heart to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And whatever you do in word or in deed, <span class="added-text">(do)</span> all in the name of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father through him.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Wives, be subject to your own husbands, as was fitting in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Husbands, love your wives, and do not be bitter against them.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Children, obey your parents in all things, for this is well-pleasing to the Lord.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Fathers, do not provoke your children, lest they be discouraged.[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> Servants, obey in all things your masters according to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, not with eyeservice, as men-pleasers, but in singleness of heart, fearing God.

<span class="v-num">23</span> And whatever you do, work from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> soul, as to the Lord and not to men,[^8]

<span class="v-num">24</span> knowing that from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But the one doing wrong will receive what he did wrong, and there is no respect of persons.

[^1]: ζητεῖτε는 현재 명령형으로 위엣 것을 지속적으로 찾으라는 뜻이다.
[^2]: φανερωθήσεσθε는 미래 수동태로 그리스도 재림 때 성도가 영광 중에 나타날 것을 가리킨다.
[^3]: ἀνακαινούμενον은 현재 수동분사로 지속적으로 새로워지는 과정을 나타낸다.
[^4]: τὰ πάντα καὶ ἐν πᾶσι Χριστός는 그리스도께서 새 사람 안에서 모든 인간적 차별을 초월하는 전부이심을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἐάν+가정법(ἔχῃ)은 '누구든지 불만이 있거든'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 조건으로 제시한다. 특정 개인을 지목하지 않고 일반적·반복적 상황을 가정하는 표현이다.
[^6]: βρα브εύω는 본래 경기의 심판이 판정을 내리는 것을 가리키는 말로, 평화가 마음의 결정을 주관하게 하라는 뜻이다.
[^7]: ἵνα μὴ ἀθυμῶσιν은 목적을 나타내는 부정법 목적절로 자녀를 격분시키지 말아야 할 이유를 밝힌다.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(ποιῆτε)은 '무엇을 하든지'라는 불특정 조건절로, 특정 행위가 아니라 장차 행할 모든 일반적 경우를 가리킨다. 어떤 일이든 그 내용에 관계없이 주께 하듯 최선을 다하라는 권면이다.
