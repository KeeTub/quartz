# Colossians 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> For I want you to know how great a struggle I have concerning you and those in Laodicea, and as many as have not seen my face in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh,

<span class="v-num">2</span> that their hearts may be comforted, having been united together in love, and unto all <span class="added-text">(the)</span> riches of the full assurance of understanding, unto <span class="added-text">(the)</span> full knowledge of the mystery of God and Father and of Christ,[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> in whom are all the treasures of wisdom and of knowledge hidden.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But this I say, that no one may delude you with persuasive speech.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> For even if I am absent in the flesh, yet I am with you in the spirit, rejoicing and seeing your order, and the firmness of your faith <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> Christ.

<span class="v-num">6</span> As therefore you received Christ Jesus the Lord, walk in him,

<span class="v-num">7</span> having been rooted and being built up in him, and being confirmed in the faith, as you were taught, abounding in it with thanksgiving.

<span class="v-num">8</span> See to it that no one will be the one carrying you off as spoil through philosophy and empty deceit, according to the tradition of men, according to the <span class="alt-label">elements</span><span class="alt-text">(=elementary principles)</span> of the world, and not according to Christ.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For in him dwells all the fulness of the <span class="alt-label">Godhead</span><span class="alt-text">(=deity)</span> bodily,[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> and you are <span class="alt-label">complete</span><span class="alt-text">(=filled)</span> in him, who is the head of all rule and authority;

<span class="v-num">11</span> in whom also you were circumcised with <span class="added-text">(a)</span> circumcision not made with hands, in the putting off of the body of the sins of the flesh, in the circumcision of Christ,[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> having been buried together with him in baptism, in whom also you were raised together through faith <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=of)</span> the working of God, who raised him from the dead.

<span class="v-num">13</span> And you, being dead in the trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, he made alive together with him, having forgiven us all the trespasses;

<span class="v-num">14</span> having wiped out the handwriting in <span class="alt-label">decrees</span><span class="alt-text">(=ordinances)</span> against us, which was contrary to us, and he has taken it out of the midst, having nailed it to the cross;[^5]

<span class="v-num">15</span> having stripped the rulers and the authorities, he made a show of them openly, triumphing over them in <span class="alt-label">it</span><span class="alt-text">(=the cross)</span>.[^6]

<span class="v-num">16</span> Therefore let no one judge you in food or in drink, or in respect of a feast or a new moon or sabbaths,

<span class="v-num">17</span> which are a shadow of the things to come, but the body <span class="added-text">(is)</span> of Christ.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Let no one defraud you of your <span class="alt-label">prize</span><span class="alt-text">(=reward)</span>, <span class="alt-label">delighting</span><span class="alt-text">(=wishing)</span> in humility and worship of angels, intruding into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> things which he has not seen, vainly puffed up by the mind of his flesh,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and not holding the head, from whom all the body, being supplied and knit together through the joints and <span class="alt-label">ligaments</span><span class="alt-text">(=sinews)</span>, grows with the growth of God.

<span class="v-num">20</span> If therefore you died with Christ from the <span class="alt-label">elements</span><span class="alt-text">(=elementary principles)</span> of the world, why, as though living in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world, are you subject to <span class="alt-label">decrees</span><span class="alt-text">(=ordinances)</span>,

<span class="v-num">21</span> "Do not handle, nor taste, nor touch,"

<span class="v-num">22</span> which are all to perish with the using, according to the commandments and teachings of men?

<span class="v-num">23</span> which things indeed have a <span class="alt-label">word</span><span class="alt-text">(=appearance)</span> of wisdom in self-made <span class="alt-label">worship</span><span class="alt-text">(=will-worship)</span> and humility and severity to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> body, not in any honor to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> satisfying of the flesh.

---

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 위안과 사랑 안의 연합, 온전한 지식에 이르는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 그럴듯한 말에 속지 않게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^3]: σωματικῶς(육체로)는 그리스도의 성육신 안에 신성이 관념이 아니라 실제로 거하심을 강조한다.
[^4]: 손으로 하지 아니한 할례는 육체적 의식이 아니라 그리스도 안에서 이루어진 영적 실체를 가리킨다.
[^5]: χειρόγραφον(빚 증서)은 율법의 요구를 지고 있던 우리의 채무 문서를 가리키는 법적 은유이다.
[^6]: θριαμβεύσας는 로마 장군의 개선행진 이미지를 빌려, 십자가로 권세들을 정복해 끌고 가시는 승리를 묘사한다.
