# Colossians 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, and Timothy the brother,

<span class="v-num">2</span> to the saints and faithful brothers in Christ who are in Colossae: Grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">3</span> We give thanks to the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, praying always concerning you,

<span class="v-num">4</span> having heard of your faith in Christ Jesus, and the love <span class="added-text">(which is)</span> toward all the saints,

<span class="v-num">5</span> because of the hope laid up for you in the heavens, which you heard before in the word of the truth of the gospel,

<span class="v-num">6</span> which has come to you, as also in all the world, and is bearing fruit and growing, as also in you, from the day you heard and fully knew the grace of God in truth,

<span class="v-num">7</span> just as you learned from Epaphras our beloved fellow servant, who is a faithful minister of Christ on your <span class="alt-label">behalf</span><span class="alt-text">(=for you)</span>,

<span class="v-num">8</span> who also declared to us your love in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For this reason we also, from the day we heard, do not cease praying for you and asking that you may be filled with the full knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding,[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> so that you may walk worthily of the Lord unto all pleasing, bearing fruit in every good work and growing into the <span class="added-text">(full)</span> knowledge of God,[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> being strengthened with all power according to the might of his glory, unto all endurance and longsuffering with joy,

<span class="v-num">12</span> giving thanks to the Father who has made us fit for the portion of the inheritance of the saints in the light,

<span class="v-num">13</span> who delivered us out of the power of darkness, and transferred <span class="added-text">(us)</span> into the kingdom of the Son of his love,

<span class="v-num">14</span> in whom we have the redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins,

<span class="v-num">15</span> who is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> image of the invisible God, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstborn of all creation,[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> because in him all things were created, the things in the heavens and the things on the earth, the visible and the invisible, whether thrones, or <span class="alt-label">dominions</span><span class="alt-text">(=lordships)</span>, or principalities, or powers; all things have been created through him and unto him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> And he is before all things, and in him all things hold <span class="alt-label">together</span><span class="alt-text">(=consist)</span>.

<span class="v-num">18</span> And he is the head of the body, the church; who is the beginning, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstborn from the dead, so that he himself might become preeminent in all things,[^4]

<span class="v-num">19</span> because in him all the fullness was pleased to dwell,[^5]

<span class="v-num">20</span> and through him to reconcile all things to himself, having made peace through the blood of his cross, through him, whether the things on the earth or the things in the heavens.

<span class="v-num">21</span> And you, who were once alienated and enemies in <span class="alt-label">mind</span><span class="alt-text">(=understanding)</span> by <span class="added-text">(your)</span> wicked works,

<span class="v-num">22</span> yet now he has reconciled <span class="added-text">(you)</span> in the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and blameless and unreproveable before him,[^6]

<span class="v-num">23</span> if indeed you continue in the faith, grounded and steadfast, and not moved away from the hope of the gospel which you heard, which was preached in all the creation under heaven, of which I, Paul, became a minister.[^7]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Now I rejoice in my sufferings for you, and fill up <span class="added-text">(in)</span> my flesh what is lacking of the afflictions of Christ, on behalf of his body, which is the church,

<span class="v-num">25</span> of which I became a minister, according to the stewardship of God given to me for you, to fulfill the word of God,[^8]

<span class="v-num">26</span> the mystery which has been hidden from the ages and from the generations, but now has been revealed to his saints,

<span class="v-num">27</span> to whom God willed to make known what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles, which is Christ in you, the hope of glory,

<span class="v-num">28</span> whom we proclaim, admonishing every man and teaching every man in all wisdom, so that we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus,[^9]

<span class="v-num">29</span> for which I also labor, striving according to his working, which works in me in power.

[^1]: ἵνα πληρωθῆτε는 목적절로 하나님의 뜻을 아는 것이 기도의 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^2]: περιπατῆσαι는 목적 부정사로 9절의 기도 내용이 실현될 삶의 목표를 제시한다.
[^3]: πρωτότοκος는 시간적 선재와 만물에 대한 주권적 우선권을 함께 나타내는 신학적 칭호다.
[^4]: ἵνα γένηται는 목적절로 그리스도께서 만물 가운데 으뜸 되심이 부활의 목적임을 밝힌다.
[^5]: πλήρωμα는 하나님의 신성의 충만을 가리키는 핵심 신학 용어로, 그리스도 안에 거하시는 것으로 표현된다.
[^6]: παραστῆσαι는 목적 부정사로 화목의 궁극 목적이 성도를 흠 없이 세우는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^7]: εἴγε ἐπιμένετε는 조건절로 화목의 결과가 신자의 지속적인 믿음에 달려 있음을 표현한다.
[^8]: πληρῶσαι는 목적 부정사로 바울에게 맡겨진 청지기직의 목적이 하나님 말씀의 성취임을 나타낸다.
[^9]: ἵνα παραστήσωμεν은 목적절로 전파와 가르침의 궁극 목표가 성도를 온전하게 세우는 것임을 나타낸다.
