# Hebrews 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> For every high priest taken from among men is appointed on behalf of men in the things pertaining to God, in order that he might offer both gifts and sacrifices for sins,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> being able to deal gently with the ignorant and erring, since he himself also is beset with weakness;

<span class="v-num">3</span> And because of this he is obligated, as concerning the people, so also concerning himself, to offer for sins.

<span class="v-num">4</span> And no one takes this honor to himself, but the one being called by God, just as Aaron also <span class="added-text">(was)</span>.

<span class="v-num">5</span> So also <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Christ did not glorify himself to become <span class="added-text">(a)</span> high priest, but <span class="added-text">(it was)</span> the one having spoken to him, "You are my son, today I have begotten you."[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> as he says also in another <span class="added-text">(place)</span>, "You <span class="added-text">(are)</span> a priest forever according to the order of Melchisedec."[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Who in the days of his flesh, having offered up prayers and supplications with strong crying and tears to the one being able to save him from death, and having been heard because of his godly fear,

<span class="v-num">8</span> though being a son, he learned obedience from the things which he suffered,

<span class="v-num">9</span> and having been made perfect, he became to all those obeying him <span class="added-text">(the)</span> source of eternal salvation,

<span class="v-num">10</span> having been called by God <span class="added-text">(a)</span> high priest according to the order of Melchisedec.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Concerning whom we have much to say, and hard to explain, since you have become dull of hearing.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For indeed, though you ought to be teachers by this time, you have need again for someone to teach you the elementary principles of the oracles of God, and you have become <span class="added-text">(ones)</span> having need of milk, and not of solid food.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For everyone partaking of milk <span class="added-text">(is)</span> unskilled in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of righteousness, for he is a babe;

<span class="v-num">14</span> But solid food belongs to the full-grown (=mature), those who because of habitual use have their senses trained for discernment of both good and evil.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로, 대제사장이 세움받는 목적(제사를 드림)을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 시편 2:7의 인용으로, 그리스도의 대제사장 됨이 하나님의 선언에 근거함을 보인다.
[^3]: 시편 110:4의 인용으로, 멜기세덱 계열 제사장직의 근거가 된다.
