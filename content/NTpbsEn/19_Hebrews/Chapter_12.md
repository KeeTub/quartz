# Hebrews 12

<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore, we also having so great a cloud of witnesses surrounding us, having put <span class="alt-label">off</span><span class="alt-text">(=laid aside)</span> every weight and the easily entangling sin, let us run with endurance the race set before us,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of faith, who, <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=instead of)</span> the joy set before him, endured <span class="added-text">(the)</span> cross, having despised <span class="added-text">(the)</span> shame, and sat down at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of the throne of God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> For consider him who endured such <span class="alt-label">contradiction</span><span class="alt-text">(=hostility)</span> <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> sinners against himself, that you may not grow weary, fainting in your souls.[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> Not yet unto blood have you resisted, striving against sin,

<span class="v-num">5</span> and you have forgotten the exhortation which speaks to you as to sons, "My son, do not despise <span class="added-text">(the)</span> chastening of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, nor faint when rebuked by him,[^4]

<span class="v-num">6</span> for whom <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord loves, he chastens, and scourges every son whom he receives."

<span class="v-num">7</span> If you endure chastening, God deals with you as with sons; for what son is there whom <span class="added-text">(his)</span> father does not chasten?

<span class="v-num">8</span> But if you are without chastening, of which all have become partakers, then you are <span class="alt-label">illegitimate</span><span class="alt-text">(=bastards)</span>, and not sons.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Furthermore, we had fathers of our flesh who disciplined <span class="added-text">(us)</span>, and we respected <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; shall we not much more be subject to the Father of spirits, and live?

<span class="v-num">10</span> For they indeed for a few days disciplined <span class="added-text">(us)</span> as seemed <span class="added-text">(good)</span> to them, but he for <span class="added-text">(our)</span> profit, that we might partake of his holiness.[^5]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now all chastening for the present does not seem to be of joy, but of grief; but afterward it yields <span class="added-text">(the)</span> peaceful fruit of righteousness to those who have been trained by it.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore "lift up the hands that hang down, and the <span class="alt-label">feeble</span><span class="alt-text">(=weakened)</span> knees,"[^6]

<span class="v-num">13</span> and "make straight paths for your feet," that what is lame may not be turned aside, but rather be healed.[^7]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Pursue peace with all, and holiness, without which no one will see the Lord,

<span class="v-num">15</span> looking carefully lest anyone fall short of the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness springing up cause trouble, and by this many be defiled;

<span class="v-num">16</span> lest there be any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau, who for one morsel of food sold his birthright.

<span class="v-num">17</span> For you know that afterward, when he desired to inherit the blessing, he was rejected, for he found no place for repentance, though he sought it <span class="added-text">(diligently)</span> with tears.

<span class="v-num">18</span> For you have not come to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mountain that could be touched, and that burned with fire, and to blackness, and darkness, and tempest,

<span class="v-num">19</span> and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> sound of a trumpet, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of words, which <span class="added-text">(voice)</span> those who heard entreated that <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word should not be spoken to them anymore;

<span class="v-num">20</span> for they could not endure what was commanded, "And if even a beast touches the mountain, it shall be stoned, or shot through with an arrow;"[^8]

<span class="v-num">21</span> and so terrible was the sight, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> Moses said, "I am exceedingly afraid and trembling;"[^9]

<span class="v-num">22</span> But you have come to Mount Zion, and to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> city of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God, the heavenly Jerusalem, and to an innumerable company of angels,

<span class="v-num">23</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> general assembly and church of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> firstborn who are <span class="alt-label">registered</span><span class="alt-text">(=enrolled)</span> in heaven, and to God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judge of all, and to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> spirits of just men made perfect,[^10]

<span class="v-num">24</span> and to Jesus <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mediator of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> new covenant, and to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of sprinkling that speaks better things than <span class="added-text">(that of)</span> Abel.[^11]

<span class="v-num">25</span> See that you do not refuse him who speaks. For if they did not escape who refused him who warned <span class="added-text">(them)</span> on earth, much more shall we not <span class="added-text">(escape)</span>, if we turn away from him who <span class="added-text">(warns)</span> from heaven,

<span class="v-num">26</span> whose voice then shook the earth, but now he has promised, saying, "Yet once more I shake not only the earth, but also the heaven."[^12]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And this <span class="added-text">(phrase)</span>, "Yet once more," signifies the <span class="alt-label">removal</span><span class="alt-text">(=transition)</span> of those things that are being shaken, as of things that have been made, that the things which cannot be shaken may remain.[^13]

<span class="v-num">28</span> Therefore, receiving a kingdom that cannot be shaken, let us have grace, by which we may serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear.

<span class="v-num">29</span> For indeed "our God is a consuming fire."[^14]

[^1]: 현재분사 ἔχοντες·περικείμενον과 청유형 가정법 τρέχωμεν이 결합되어 지속적 결단을 촉구한다.
[^2]: ἀρχηγόν(창시자)과 τελειωτήν(완성하시는 이)은 믿음의 시작과 끝을 예수께 돌리는 표현이다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 '지치지 않도록' 함이 목적임을 나타낸다.
[^4]: 잠언 3:11-12(LXX) 인용.
[^5]: εἰς τό+부정사 목적구문으로 '그의 거룩함에 참여케 하려는' 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: 이사야 35:3 암시.
[^7]: 잠언 4:26(LXX) 인용이며 ἵνα+가정법으로 목적을 나타낸다.
[^8]: 출애굽기 19:12-13 인용.
[^9]: 신명기 9:19 암시.
[^10]: 완료분사 τετελειωμένων은 이미 완성된 상태를 나타내어 구원 얻은 의인들의 완전함을 강조한다.
[^11]: παρά+대격은 비교의 뉘앙스로 '아벨보다'를 의미한다.
[^12]: 학개 2:6 인용.
[^13]: ἵνα+가정법 μείνῃ로 '남아 있게 하려는' 목적을 나타낸다.
[^14]: 신명기 4:24 인용.
