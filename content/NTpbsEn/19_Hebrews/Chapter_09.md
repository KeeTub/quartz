<span class="v-num">1</span> Then indeed the first tabernacle also had regulations of service, and the worldly <span class="alt-label">sanctuary</span><span class="alt-text">(=holy place)</span>.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For a tabernacle was prepared, the first, in which <span class="added-text">(were)</span> the lampstand and the table and the setting forth of the <span class="alt-label">loaves</span><span class="alt-text">(=bread)</span>, which is called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> holy <span class="added-text">(place)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And after the second veil, <span class="added-text">(there was)</span> the tabernacle called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> holy of holies,

<span class="v-num">4</span> having a golden <span class="alt-label">censer</span><span class="alt-text">(=incense altar)</span>, and the ark of the covenant covered all around with gold, in which <span class="added-text">(was)</span> a golden jar having the manna, and Aaron's rod that budded, and the tablets of the covenant;

<span class="v-num">5</span> And above it <span class="added-text">(were the)</span> cherubim of glory overshadowing the mercy <span class="alt-label">seat</span><span class="alt-text">(=propitiatory)</span>; concerning which it is not <span class="added-text">(possible)</span> now to speak in <span class="alt-label">detail</span><span class="alt-text">(=part by part)</span>.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Now these things having been thus prepared, the priests enter continually into the first tabernacle, performing the services;

<span class="v-num">7</span> but into the second <span class="added-text">(only the)</span> high priest <span class="added-text">(alone)</span>, once a year, not without blood, which he offers for himself and for the <span class="alt-label">errors</span><span class="alt-text">(=sins of ignorance)</span> of the people;

<span class="v-num">8</span> The Holy Spirit signifying this, that the way of the holy <span class="alt-label">places</span><span class="alt-text">(=holiest place)</span> has not yet been made manifest, while the first tabernacle still <span class="alt-label">has</span><span class="alt-text">(=stands)</span> standing;

<span class="v-num">9</span> which is a <span class="alt-label">symbol</span><span class="alt-text">(=parable)</span> for the present <span class="alt-label">time</span><span class="alt-text">(=season)</span>, according to which both gifts and sacrifices are offered, not being able to perfect the one <span class="alt-label">serving</span><span class="alt-text">(=worshiper)</span> according to conscience,[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> <span class="added-text">(being)</span> only <span class="added-text">(regulations)</span> concerning foods and drinks and various <span class="alt-label">washings</span><span class="alt-text">(=baptisms)</span>, and <span class="added-text">(regulations)</span> of the flesh, imposed until <span class="added-text">(the)</span> time of <span class="alt-label">reformation</span><span class="alt-text">(=correction)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> But Christ, having arrived <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a high priest of the good things to come, through the greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands, that is, not of this creation,

<span class="v-num">12</span> nor through the blood of goats and calves, but through his own blood, entered once for all into the holy places, having obtained eternal redemption.[^3]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For if the blood of bulls and goats, and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> ashes of a heifer sprinkling the <span class="alt-label">defiled</span><span class="alt-text">(=those who have been defiled)</span>, sanctifies to the purity of the flesh,

<span class="v-num">14</span> how much more shall the blood of Christ, who through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> eternal Spirit offered himself without blemish to God, cleanse your conscience from dead works, to serve <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God?[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> And for this reason he is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mediator of a new covenant, so that, death having taken place for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> redemption of the transgressions under the first covenant, those who have been called might receive the promise of the eternal inheritance.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> For where a <span class="alt-label">covenant</span><span class="alt-text">(=testament)</span> is, there must of necessity be the death of the one who made it.[^6]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For a <span class="alt-label">covenant</span><span class="alt-text">(=testament)</span> is <span class="alt-label">confirmed</span><span class="alt-text">(=valid)</span> <span class="alt-label">over</span><span class="alt-text">(=in the case of)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, since it is never in force while the one who made it lives.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Whence not even the first <span class="added-text">(covenant)</span> was <span class="alt-label">inaugurated</span><span class="alt-text">(=dedicated)</span> without blood.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For when every commandment had been spoken by Moses according to the law to all the people, he took the blood of calves and goats, with water and scarlet wool and hyssop, and sprinkled both the book itself and all the people,

<span class="v-num">20</span> saying, "This <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the blood of the covenant which God commanded <span class="added-text">(to)</span> you."

<span class="v-num">21</span> And likewise he sprinkled with blood both the tabernacle and all the vessels of the ministry.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And according to the law almost all things are cleansed with blood, and without shedding of blood there is no <span class="alt-label">forgiveness</span><span class="alt-text">(=remission)</span>.[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> It was necessary therefore that the <span class="alt-label">copies</span><span class="alt-text">(=patterns)</span> of the things in the heavens be purified with these, but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.

<span class="v-num">24</span> For Christ did not enter into holy places made with hands, <span class="added-text">(which are)</span> copies of the true, but into heaven itself, now to appear in the <span class="alt-label">presence</span><span class="alt-text">(=face)</span> of God for us;[^8]

<span class="v-num">25</span> nor <span class="added-text">(was it)</span> that he should offer himself often, as the high priest enters into the holy places every year with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of <span class="alt-label">another</span><span class="alt-text">(=not his own)</span>;[^9]

<span class="v-num">26</span> since then he would have needed to suffer often since the foundation of the world; but now once, at the <span class="alt-label">end</span><span class="alt-text">(=consummation)</span> of the ages, he has been manifested for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> putting away of sin through the sacrifice of himself.[^10]

<span class="v-num">27</span> And as it is <span class="alt-label">appointed</span><span class="alt-text">(=laid up)</span> for men to die once, and after this <span class="added-text">(the)</span> judgment;

<span class="v-num">28</span> so Christ also, having been offered once to bear the sins of many, will appear a second time, without sin, to those who eagerly await him, unto salvation.[^11]

[^1]: παραβολή는 첫 언약의 제사 제도가 실체가 아니라 장차 올 것의 '비유·모형'임을 가리키며, 양심의 완전함은 짐승 제사로 이룰 수 없음을 강조한다.
[^2]: διόρθωσις(개혁)는 그리스도로 말미암은 새 언약의 도래를 가리키는 시간적 한계 표시이다.
[^3]: αἰωνίαν λύτρωσιν(영원한 구속)은 매년 반복되는 짐승 제사의 구속과 대조되어 그리스도의 속죄의 단번성·영원성을 강조한다.
[^4]: πνεύματος αἰωνίου(영원한 영)는 그리스도의 자기 헌신이 시간적 제사 제도를 초월하는 영원한 성격임을 밝힌다.
[^5]: ὅπως+가정법(λάβωσιν)은 그리스도의 죽음의 목적, 곧 부르심 받은 자들이 영원한 기업의 약속을 받게 함을 나타낸다.
[^6]: διαθήκη는 '언약'과 '유언'의 이중 의미를 지니며, 여기서는 유언의 법적 효력(유언자의 죽음이 전제됨)을 논거로 삼는다.
[^7]: αἱματεκχυσία(피 흘림)와 ἄφεσις(사함)의 연결은 구약 제사 전체가 그리스도의 십자가 대속을 예표함을 압축적으로 보여준다.
[^8]: ἀντίτυπα(모형)는 지상 성소가 하늘 실체의 사본에 불과함을 나타내며, 그리스도의 하늘 성소 입성의 우월성을 뒷받침한다.
[^9]: ἵνα+가정법(προσφέρῃ)은 그리스도께서 대제사장처럼 자주 자신을 드릴 필요가 없었음을 부정하는 목적절이다.
[^10]: ἔδει(미완료)는 실현되지 않은 가정적 필연을 나타내며, 반복 제사가 필요했을 것이나 실제로는 단번의 제사로 충분함을 대조한다.
[^11]: 27-28절은 '한 번 죽음-심판'과 '한 번 드려짐-재림'이 대응하는 병행 구조로, 그리스도의 초림과 재림을 인간의 죽음과 심판에 유비시킨다.
