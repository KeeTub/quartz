<span class="v-num">1</span> In many parts and in many ways, of old, God, having spoken to the fathers <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> the prophets,

<span class="v-num">2</span> in these last days spoke to us <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Son, whom He appointed heir of all things, through whom also He made the <span class="alt-label">ages</span><span class="alt-text">(=worlds)</span>,[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> who being <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">brightness</span><span class="alt-text">(=radiance)</span> of <span class="added-text">(His)</span> glory and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> exact <span class="alt-label">image</span><span class="alt-text">(=character)</span> of His <span class="alt-label">substance</span><span class="alt-text">(=being)</span>, and <span class="alt-label">upholding</span><span class="alt-text">(=bearing)</span> all things by the word of His power, having made <span class="added-text">(by)</span> Himself purification of our sins, sat down on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of the Majesty on high,[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> having become so much <span class="alt-label">better</span><span class="alt-text">(=greater)</span> than the angels, by how much He has inherited a more excellent name <span class="alt-label">than</span><span class="alt-text">(=beyond)</span> them.

<span class="v-num">5</span> "For to which of the angels did He ever say, 'You are My Son, today I have begotten You'? And again, 'I will be to Him a Father, and He will be to Me a Son'?"[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And again, when He brings the firstborn into the world, He says, "And let all <span class="added-text">(the)</span> angels of God worship Him."[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> And <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> the angels He says, "He who makes His angels <span class="alt-label">spirits</span><span class="alt-text">(=winds)</span>, and His ministers a flame of fire."[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> but <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=to)</span> the Son, "Your throne, O God, <span class="added-text">(is)</span> forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the age of the age)</span>; a scepter of righteousness <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the scepter of Your kingdom.[^6]

<span class="v-num">9</span> You have loved righteousness and hated lawlessness; therefore God, Your God, has anointed You with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> oil of gladness above Your companions."

<span class="v-num">10</span> And, "You, Lord, <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=according to)</span> the beginning laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the works of Your hands;[^7]

<span class="v-num">11</span> they will perish, but You remain; and they will all grow old like a garment,

<span class="v-num">12</span> and like a mantle You will fold them up, and they will be changed; but You are the same, and Your years will not fail."

<span class="v-num">13</span> But to which of the angels has He ever said, "Sit at My right hand, until I make Your enemies <span class="added-text">(the)</span> footstool of Your feet"?[^8]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=into)</span> service on account of those about to inherit salvation?

[^1]: ἐν υἱῷ에 관사가 없어 '아들'이라는 신분·성격 자체(그가 아들 되신 분이라는 특성)를 강조하는 표현이다.
[^2]: ἀπαύγασμα(광채)와 χαρακτήρ(본체의 형상)는 아들이 하나님의 본질을 그대로 드러내며 아버지와 동일한 본체이심을 나타내는 표현이다.
[^3]: 시편 2:7과 사무엘하 7:14의 인용으로 아들의 신적 정체성을 증거한다.
[^4]: 신명기 32:43(칠십인역) 또는 시편 97:7의 인용으로, 만물이 아들께 경배함을 명한다.
[^5]: 시편 104:4의 인용으로 천사들의 피조물 됨과 변화하는 본성을 아들의 불변하심과 대조시킨다.
[^6]: 시편 45편을 아들에게 적용하며 그를 '하나님'이라 부름으로써 아들의 신성을 명백히 증거한다.
[^7]: 시편 102:25-27의 인용으로 아들의 창조주 되심을 증거한다.
[^8]: 시편 110:1의 인용으로 아들의 높아지심과 통치자 되심을 증거한다.
