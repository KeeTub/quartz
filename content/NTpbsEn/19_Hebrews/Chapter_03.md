# Hebrews 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> <span class="alt-label">Therefore</span><span class="alt-text">(=Whence)</span>, holy brothers, partakers of <span class="added-text">(a/the)</span> heavenly calling, consider the apostle and high priest of our confession, Christ Jesus,[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> <span class="added-text">(who is)</span> faithful to the one having made him, as Moses also <span class="added-text">(was faithful)</span> in all his house.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For this one has been counted worthy of more glory than Moses, by as much as the one having built the house has more honor than the house.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For every house is built by someone, but the one having built all things <span class="added-text">(is)</span> God.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And Moses <span class="added-text">(was)</span> faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony of the things that would be spoken;

<span class="v-num">6</span> but Christ <span class="added-text">(was faithful)</span> as a son over his <span class="added-text">(own)</span> house, whose house we are, if indeed we hold fast the boldness and the boast of the hope firm until the end.[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Therefore, as the Holy Spirit says, 'Today if you hear his voice,[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> do not harden your hearts, as in the provocation, according to the day of the trial in the wilderness,

<span class="v-num">9</span> where your fathers tested me, tried me, and saw my works forty years.

<span class="v-num">10</span> Therefore I was angry with that generation, and said, 'They always go astray in <span class="added-text">(their)</span> heart, and they have not known my ways;'

<span class="v-num">11</span> as I swore in my wrath, 'They shall not enter into my rest.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Watch, brothers, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God;

<span class="v-num">13</span> but encourage <span class="alt-label">yourselves</span><span class="alt-text">(=one another)</span> every day, while it is called 'today,' lest any of you be hardened by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> deceit of sin;[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> for we have become partakers of Christ, if indeed we hold fast the beginning of <span class="added-text">(our)</span> confidence firm until the end;[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> while it is said, 'Today if you hear his voice, do not harden your hearts, as in the provocation.'[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> For who, having heard, provoked? Was it not all who came out of Egypt through Moses?

<span class="v-num">17</span> And with whom was he angry forty years? Was it not with those who sinned, whose <span class="alt-label">bodies</span><span class="alt-text">(=limbs)</span> fell in the wilderness?

<span class="v-num">18</span> And to whom did he swear that they would not enter into his rest, except to those who disobeyed?

<span class="v-num">19</span> And we see that they were not able to enter because of unbelief.

[^1]: τὸν ἀπόστολον καὶ ἀρχιερέα가 관사 하나(τὸν) 아래 묶여 있어 사도직과 대제사장직이 한 인격(그리스도)에게 속함을 보여준다.
[^2]: ἐάνπερ+가정법(κατάσχωμεν)은 아직 실현되지 않은 조건으로, 신자됨의 확증이 끝까지 견디는 데 달려 있음을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἐὰν+가정법(ἀκούσητε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능한 조건을 나타낸다. '만일 오늘 그의 음성을 듣거든'이라는 뜻으로, 지금 이 순간의 반응이 결정적임을 강조하는 권면적 조건절이다.
[^4]: 시편 95편(70인역 94편)의 인용으로, 광야 세대의 불신앙과 안식 상실을 경고의 근거로 삼는다.
[^5]: ἵνα μὴ σκληρυνθῇ는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 날마다 권면해야 할 이유(완악해지지 않도록)를 제시한다.
[^6]: 6절과 같은 ἐάνπερ+가정법 구문이 반복되어, 그리스도와의 동참이 끝까지 견디는 믿음에 달려 있음을 다시 강조한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀκούσητε)은 '만약 듣거든'이라는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, 오늘이라는 시점에 반복적으로 적용될 수 있는 조건을 강조한다. 설교자는 이를 '지금 이 순간에도 유효한 초청과 경고'로 적용할 수 있다.
