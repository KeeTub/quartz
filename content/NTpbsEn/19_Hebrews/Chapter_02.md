# Hebrews 2

<span class="v-num">1</span> Because of this, it is necessary <span class="added-text">(for)</span> us to give more earnest heed to the things heard, lest at any time we drift away.

<span class="v-num">2</span> For if the word spoken through angels became <span class="alt-label">firm</span><span class="alt-text">(=valid)</span>, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompense,

<span class="v-num">3</span> How shall we escape, having neglected so great a salvation? which, having taken <span class="added-text">(its)</span> beginning to be spoken through the Lord, was confirmed to us by those who heard <span class="added-text">(him)</span>,

<span class="v-num">4</span> God also bearing witness together with <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, both by signs and wonders, and by various <span class="alt-label">miracles</span><span class="alt-text">(=powers)</span>, and by distributions of the Holy Spirit, according to his own will.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For he did not subject to angels the world to <span class="alt-label">come</span><span class="alt-text">(=the coming world)</span>, concerning which we are speaking.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But someone testified somewhere, saying, "What is man, that you are mindful of him, or <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of man, that you visit him?

<span class="v-num">7</span> You made him a little lower than the angels; you crowned him with glory and honor, and set him over the works of your hands;

<span class="v-num">8</span> You put all things in subjection under his feet." For in subjecting all things to him, he left nothing not subject to him. But now we do not yet see all things subjected to him.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honor, so that by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> grace of God he might taste death for <span class="alt-label">everyone</span><span class="alt-text">(=every man)</span>.[^1]

<span class="v-num">10</span> For it was fitting for him, for whom <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all things and through whom <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all things, in bringing many sons to glory, to make the <span class="alt-label">author</span><span class="alt-text">(=captain)</span> of their salvation perfect through sufferings.[^2]

<span class="v-num">11</span> For both he who sanctifies and those who are being sanctified <span class="added-text">(are)</span> all of one; for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brothers,

<span class="v-num">12</span> saying, "I will declare your name to my brothers, in the midst of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> church I will sing praise to you."

<span class="v-num">13</span> And again, "I will put my trust in him." And again, "Behold, I and the children whom God has given me."

<span class="v-num">14</span> Since therefore the children have shared in flesh and blood, he himself likewise took part <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> the same, in order that through death he might destroy him who had the power of death, that is, the devil,[^3]

<span class="v-num">15</span> and might deliver those who through fear of death were subject to bondage all their lifetime.[^4]

<span class="v-num">16</span> For surely he did not take hold of angels, but he took hold of the seed of Abraham.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Therefore, he had to be made like his brothers in all things, that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, to make propitiation for the sins of the people.[^5]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For in that he himself has suffered, having been tempted, he is able to help those who are being tempted.

[^1]: '하나님의 은혜로'(χάριτι Θεοῦ)는 그리스도의 죽음이 하나님의 은혜의 발로임을 강조하는 핵심 어구다(일부 사본은 '하나님을 떠나'χωρὶς Θεοῦ로 읽음).
[^2]: '창시자'(ἀρχηγός)는 길을 여는 선구자이자 주관자라는 의미로, 그리스도께서 구원의 길을 여신 분임을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(καταργήσῃ)은 그리스도의 죽음의 목적을 나타내는 목적절이다.
[^4]: 앞 절의 ἵνα에 이어지는 가정법(ἀπαλλάξῃ)으로 동일한 목적절이 계속된다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(γένηται)과 εἰς τό+부정사(ἱλάσκεσθαι)가 이중으로 목적을 표현하여 대제사장직의 목적(자비·신실·속죄)을 강조한다.
