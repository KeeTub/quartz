<span class="v-num">1</span> Let brotherly <span class="alt-label">love</span><span class="alt-text">(=Philadelphia)</span> continue.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Do not forget hospitality; for through this some, without knowing <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, entertained angels.

<span class="v-num">3</span> Remember the prisoners, as bound with <span class="added-text">(them)</span>; those who are mistreated, as <span class="added-text">(yourselves)</span> also being in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> body.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Marriage <span class="added-text">(is)</span> honorable among all, and the bed undefiled; but fornicators and adulterers God will judge.

<span class="v-num">5</span> <span class="added-text">(Let your)</span> way of life <span class="added-text">(be)</span> without love of money; being content with the things present; for he himself has said, 'I will never leave you, nor will I ever forsake you.'[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> So that we boldly say, 'The Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> my helper, and I will not fear what man will do to me.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Remember your leaders, who spoke to you the word of God; considering the outcome of <span class="added-text">(their)</span> conduct, imitate <span class="added-text">(their)</span> faith.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the same yesterday and today, and forever.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Do not be carried about by various and strange teachings; for <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> good for the heart to be established by grace, not by foods, by which those who walked <span class="added-text">(in them)</span> were not benefited.

<span class="v-num">10</span> We have an altar, from which those serving the tabernacle have no <span class="alt-label">authority</span><span class="alt-text">(=right)</span> to eat.

<span class="v-num">11</span> For the bodies of those animals whose blood is brought into the holy <span class="added-text">(place)</span> for sin through the high priest, are burned outside the camp.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people through his own blood, suffered outside the gate.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Therefore let us go out to him outside the camp, bearing his reproach.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For here we have no continuing city, but we seek the <span class="added-text">(city)</span> to come.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Through him then let us offer up a sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of lips confessing to his name.[^5]

<span class="v-num">16</span> But do not forget to do good and to share; for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Obey your leaders, and submit <span class="added-text">(to them)</span>; for they watch on behalf of your souls, as those who will give account, that they may do this with joy, and not with groaning; for this <span class="added-text">(would be)</span> unprofitable for you.[^6]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Pray for us; for we are persuaded that we have a good conscience, desiring to conduct ourselves well in all things.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And I especially urge <span class="added-text">(you)</span> to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner.[^7]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now the God of peace, who brought up from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead the great shepherd of the sheep, our Lord Jesus, by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> blood of the eternal covenant,[^8]

<span class="v-num">21</span> equip you in every good work to do his will, working in you what <span class="added-text">(is)</span> well pleasing before him, through Jesus Christ; to whom <span class="added-text">(be)</span> glory forever and ever. Amen.[^9]

<span class="v-num">22</span> But I urge you, brothers, bear with the word of exhortation; for indeed I have written to you <span class="alt-label">through</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> few <span class="added-text">(words)</span>.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Know that <span class="added-text">(our)</span> brother Timothy has been released, with whom, if he comes soon, I will see you.[^10]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Greet all your leaders, and all the saints. Those from Italy greet you.

<span class="v-num">25</span> Grace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with all of you. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The letter)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Hebrews was written from Italy <span class="alt-label">through</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> Timothy.

[^1]: 신 31:6, 수 1:5의 구약 인용으로 하나님의 신실하심을 근거로 제시한다.
[^2]: 시편 118:6 인용으로 성도의 담대함의 근거를 제시한다.
[^3]: 관사 있는 술어 ὁ αὐτός가 그리스도의 신적 불변성을 강조한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ἁγιάσῃ) 목적절로 그리스도의 고난의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: 호세아 14:2 등 구약 제사 언어를 차용해 찬송을 영적 제사로 규정한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ποιῶσι) 목적절로, 지도자들이 순종을 받는 '목적'이 기쁨으로 사역하게 하려는 데 있음을 보여준다. 성도의 순종이 지도자의 사역 태도(기쁨 vs 탄식)를 좌우한다는 실천적 권면이다.
[^7]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀποκατασταθῶ) 목적절로 저자의 바람을 나타낸다.
[^8]: ἀναγαγὼν(분사)은 그리스도의 부활을 목자 은유로 표현한다.
[^9]: εἰς τὸ ποιῆσαι는 목적을 나타내는 부정사 구문(가정법 아님)이다.
[^10]: ἐάν+가정법(ἔρχηται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '그가 만약 속히 오면'이라는 불확실한 미래 상황을 전제한다. 디모데의 도착 시점에 따라 저자의 방문 여부가 결정됨을 보여준다.
