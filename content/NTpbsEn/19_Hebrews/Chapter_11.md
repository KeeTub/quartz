<span class="v-num">1</span> Now faith is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">substance</span><span class="alt-text">(=assurance)</span> of things hoped for, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> evidence of things not seen.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> For in this the elders were borne witness <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=obtained a good testimony)</span>.

<span class="v-num">3</span> By faith we understand that the worlds were framed by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of God, so that the things being seen have not come into being from things <span class="alt-label">appearing</span><span class="alt-text">(=visible)</span>.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> By faith Abel offered to God a more <span class="alt-label">excellent</span><span class="alt-text">(=greater)</span> sacrifice than Cain, through which he was borne witness to be righteous, God bearing witness <span class="added-text">(in respect)</span> of his gifts; and through it, having died, he still speaks.

<span class="v-num">5</span> By faith Enoch was translated so as not to see death, and he was not found, because God translated him; for before his translation he was borne witness to have pleased God.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But without faith <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> impossible to please <span class="added-text">(him)</span>; for the one coming to God must believe that he is, and that he becomes a rewarder to the ones seeking him out.

<span class="v-num">7</span> By faith Noah, having been divinely warned concerning things not yet seen, having feared, prepared an ark for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> salvation of his house; through which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness according to faith.

<span class="v-num">8</span> By faith Abraham, being called, obeyed to go out into the place which he was about to receive for an inheritance, and he went out, not knowing where he is <span class="alt-label">going</span><span class="alt-text">(=was going)</span>.

<span class="v-num">9</span> By faith he sojourned in the land of promise, as <span class="added-text">(in)</span> a foreign <span class="added-text">(land)</span>, having dwelt in tents with Isaac and Jacob, the fellow heirs of the same promise;

<span class="v-num">10</span> For he was waiting for the city having the foundations, whose designer and builder <span class="added-text">(is)</span> God.

<span class="v-num">11</span> By faith also Sarah herself received power for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> conception of seed, and bore a child past the time of age, since she considered him faithful who had promised.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Therefore also from one, and him as good as dead, were born <span class="added-text">(descendants)</span> as <span class="added-text">(many as)</span> the stars of heaven in multitude, and as innumerable as the sand which <span class="added-text">(is)</span> by the <span class="alt-label">seashore</span><span class="alt-text">(=lip of the sea)</span>.

<span class="v-num">13</span> These all died according to faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them from afar, and having been persuaded <span class="added-text">(of them)</span>, and having embraced <span class="added-text">(them)</span>, and having confessed that they are strangers and <span class="alt-label">sojourners</span><span class="alt-text">(=pilgrims)</span> on the earth.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For the ones saying such things make <span class="alt-label">plain</span><span class="alt-text">(=show)</span> that they are seeking a <span class="alt-label">homeland</span><span class="alt-text">(=fatherland)</span>.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And if indeed they had been mindful of that <span class="added-text">(country)</span> from which they came out, they would have had opportunity to return;

<span class="v-num">16</span> But now they desire a better <span class="added-text">(country)</span>, that is, a heavenly <span class="added-text">(one)</span>. Therefore God is not ashamed of them, to be called their God; for he has prepared for them a city.

<span class="v-num">17</span> By faith Abraham, being tried, offered up Isaac, and he who had received the promises offered up <span class="added-text">(his)</span> only begotten <span class="added-text">(son)</span>,

<span class="v-num">18</span> "To whom it was said, 'In Isaac shall your seed be called,'"

<span class="v-num">19</span> Considering that God <span class="added-text">(was)</span> able to raise <span class="added-text">(him)</span> even from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, from where he also received him back in a <span class="alt-label">figure</span><span class="alt-text">(=parable)</span>.

<span class="v-num">20</span> By faith Isaac blessed Jacob and Esau concerning things to come.

<span class="v-num">21</span> By faith Jacob, dying, blessed each of the sons of Joseph, and worshipped, <span class="added-text">(leaning)</span> on the top of his staff.

<span class="v-num">22</span> By faith Joseph, dying, made mention of the <span class="alt-label">departure</span><span class="alt-text">(=exodus)</span> of the sons of Israel, and gave instruction concerning his bones.

<span class="v-num">23</span> By faith Moses, having been born, was hidden three months by his parents, because they saw <span class="added-text">(that)</span> the child <span class="added-text">(was)</span> beautiful; and they did not fear the <span class="alt-label">ordinance</span><span class="alt-text">(=decree)</span> of the king.

<span class="v-num">24</span> By faith Moses, having become <span class="alt-label">great</span><span class="alt-text">(=grown up)</span>, refused to be called <span class="added-text">(the)</span> son of Pharaoh's daughter,

<span class="v-num">25</span> rather choosing to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to have <span class="added-text">(the)</span> temporary enjoyment of sin;

<span class="v-num">26</span> esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt; for he was looking away to the reward.

<span class="v-num">27</span> By faith he left Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king; for he endured, as seeing the invisible <span class="added-text">(One)</span>.

<span class="v-num">28</span> By faith he <span class="alt-label">kept</span><span class="alt-text">(=performed)</span> the passover, and the sprinkling of blood, so that the one destroying the firstborn might not touch them.[^4]

<span class="v-num">29</span> By faith they passed through the Red Sea as through dry <span class="added-text">(land)</span>; which the Egyptians, having attempted, were swallowed up.

<span class="v-num">30</span> By faith the walls of Jericho fell down, having been encircled for seven days.

<span class="v-num">31</span> By faith Rahab the harlot did not perish with the ones having disobeyed, having received the spies with peace.

<span class="v-num">32</span> And what more shall I say? For time will fail me telling of Gideon, Barak and Samson and Jephthah, David and Samuel and the prophets;

<span class="v-num">33</span> who through faith subdued kingdoms, worked righteousness, obtained promises, stopped <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mouths of lions,

<span class="v-num">34</span> quenched <span class="added-text">(the)</span> power of fire, escaped <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">edge</span><span class="alt-text">(=mouths)</span> of the sword, were made strong out of weakness, became mighty in war, turned to flight armies of <span class="alt-label">foreigners</span><span class="alt-text">(=aliens)</span>.

<span class="v-num">35</span> Women received their dead by resurrection; but others were <span class="alt-label">tortured</span><span class="alt-text">(=beaten to death)</span>, not accepting the <span class="alt-label">deliverance</span><span class="alt-text">(=release)</span>, so that they might obtain a better resurrection;[^5]

<span class="v-num">36</span> and others received trial of mockings and scourgings, and further, of bonds and imprisonment;

<span class="v-num">37</span> they were stoned, they were sawn asunder, they were tempted, they died by slaughter of the sword; they went about in sheepskins, in goatskins, being destitute, afflicted, mistreated,

<span class="v-num">38</span> of whom the world was not worthy; wandering in deserts, and mountains, and caves, and the holes of the earth.

<span class="v-num">39</span> And these all, having obtained a good testimony through faith, did not receive the promise,

<span class="v-num">40</span> God having foreseen <span class="added-text">(something)</span> better concerning us, so that they should not be made perfect apart from us.[^6]

[^1]: ὑπόστασις는 '실상, 확신'을 뜻하며, 믿음이 아직 보이지 않는 소망에 실체를 부여함을 나타낸다.
[^2]: εἰς τό+부정사는 결과·설명 구문으로, 창조가 보이는 것에서 나온 것이 아님을 밝힌다.
[^3]: τοῦ μὴ ἰδεῖν은 목적 부정사구문으로, 옮겨진 목적이 죽음을 보지 않게 함임을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(θίγῃ)은 목적절로, 멸하는 자가 장자를 해하지 못하게 하려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(τύχωσιν)은 더 나은 부활을 얻으려는 목적을 나타낸다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(τελειωθῶσι)은 목적절로, 구약 성도의 온전함이 신약 성도와 함께 이루어짐을 나타낸다.
