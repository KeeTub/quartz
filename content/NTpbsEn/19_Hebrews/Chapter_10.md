# Hebrews 10

<span class="v-num">1</span> For the law, having a shadow of the good things to come, not the image itself of the things, can never, by the same sacrifices which they offer continually year by year, make perfect those who draw near.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Otherwise, would they not have ceased being offered, because the worshipers, having been cleansed once, would no longer have had any consciousness of sins?

<span class="v-num">3</span> But in them <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> a reminder of sins year by year;

<span class="v-num">4</span> for <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> impossible <span class="added-text">(for the)</span> blood of bulls and goats to take away sins.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Therefore, coming into the world, he says, "Sacrifice and offering you did not desire, but a body you prepared for me;[^2]

<span class="v-num">6</span> in burnt offerings and <span class="added-text">(sacrifices)</span> for sin you did not delight."

<span class="v-num">7</span> Then I said, "Behold, I come - in the scroll of the book it is written of me - to do, O God, your will."

<span class="v-num">8</span> Saying above, "Sacrifice and offering and burnt offerings and <span class="added-text">(sacrifices)</span> for sin you did not desire, nor did you delight <span class="added-text">(in them)</span>," which are offered according to the law,

<span class="v-num">9</span> then he has said, 'Behold, I come to do, O God, your will.' He takes away the first, that he may establish the second;[^3]

<span class="v-num">10</span> By which will we have been sanctified, <span class="added-text">(through)</span> the offering of the body of Jesus Christ once for all.

<span class="v-num">11</span> And every priest indeed stands daily ministering, and offering often the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins;

<span class="v-num">12</span> but this one, having offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down at <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hand of God,

<span class="v-num">13</span> waiting from now on until his enemies are made a footstool of his feet.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For by one offering he has perfected forever those who are being sanctified.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And the Holy Spirit also testifies to us; for after having said before,

<span class="v-num">16</span> "This is the covenant that I will make with them after those days, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord: giving my laws upon their hearts, and upon their minds I will write them;[^4]

<span class="v-num">17</span> and their sins and their lawless deeds I will remember no more."

<span class="v-num">18</span> Now where there is forgiveness of these, there is no longer an offering for sin.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Therefore, brothers, having boldness to enter the holy <span class="added-text">(places)</span> by the blood of Jesus,

<span class="v-num">20</span> which he opened for us <span class="added-text">(as)</span> a new and living way, through the veil, that is, his flesh,

<span class="v-num">21</span> and <span class="added-text">(having)</span> a great priest over the house of God,

<span class="v-num">22</span> let us draw near with a true heart in full assurance of faith, having our hearts sprinkled from an evil conscience, and <span class="added-text">(our)</span> body washed with pure water.

<span class="v-num">23</span> Let us hold fast the confession of <span class="added-text">(our)</span> hope without wavering, for he who promised is faithful;

<span class="v-num">24</span> and let us consider one another to stir up love and good works,

<span class="v-num">25</span> not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as is the custom of some, but exhorting <span class="added-text">(one another)</span>; and so much the more as you see the day approaching.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For if we sin willfully after receiving the knowledge of the truth, there no longer remains a sacrifice for sins,[^5]

<span class="v-num">27</span> but a certain fearful expectation of judgment, and a fierceness of fire about to consume the adversaries.

<span class="v-num">28</span> Anyone who has rejected <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law of Moses dies without mercy on <span class="added-text">(the testimony of)</span> two or three witnesses;

<span class="v-num">29</span> Of how much worse punishment, do you suppose, will he be deemed worthy who has trampled underfoot the Son of God, and has considered the blood of the covenant, by which he was sanctified, a common thing, and has insulted the Spirit of grace?

<span class="v-num">30</span> For we know him who said, "Vengeance <span class="added-text">(belongs)</span> to me, I will repay, says <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord;" and again, "The Lord will judge his people."[^6]

<span class="v-num">31</span> <span class="added-text">(It is)</span> fearful to fall into <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hands of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living God.

<span class="v-num">32</span> But remember the former days, in which, after being enlightened, you endured a great struggle of sufferings;

<span class="v-num">33</span> partly, being made a spectacle both by reproaches and afflictions; and partly, becoming companions of those who were so treated;

<span class="v-num">34</span> for you also sympathized with my chains, and accepted with joy the plundering of your possessions, knowing that you have for yourselves a better and abiding possession in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens.

<span class="v-num">35</span> Therefore do not cast away your boldness, which has a great reward.

<span class="v-num">36</span> For you have need of endurance, so that, having done the will of God, you may receive the promise.[^7]

<span class="v-num">37</span> For yet a little while, <span class="added-text">(how)</span> very little, "he who is coming will come, and will not delay.[^8]

<span class="v-num">38</span> But the righteous will live by faith; and if he draws back, my soul has no pleasure in him."[^9]

<span class="v-num">39</span> But we are not of those who draw back to destruction, but of those who have faith to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> preserving of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> soul.

[^1]: προσφέρουσιν(현재)이 해마다 반복되는 행위임을 나타내어 율법 제사의 근본적 불완전성을 강조한다.
[^2]: 시편 40:6-8(칠십인역)의 인용으로 그리스도의 성육신을 예표한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(στήσῃ)은 목적절로, 첫 언약 폐지의 목적이 둘째 언약을 세우려는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: 예레미야 31:33-34의 새 언약 예언 인용으로, 율법의 내면화를 강조한다.
[^5]: ἁμαρτανόντων(현재분사)은 일회적 실수가 아니라 지속적·고의적 범죄를 가리켜, 배교의 심각성을 부각한다.
[^6]: 신명기 32:35 및 시편 135:14(칠십인역)의 인용으로, 하나님의 심판의 확실성을 뒷받침한다.
[^7]: ἵνα+κομίσησθε(가정법)는 목적절로, 인내의 목적이 약속을 받는 데 있음을 나타낸다.
[^8]: 하박국 2:3-4(칠십인역)의 인용으로, 재림의 확실성과 임박성을 강조한다.
[^9]: ἐὰν+가정법(ὑποστείληται)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래의 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '만일 뒤로 물러간다면'이라는 가정을 표현한다. 실제로 그런 일이 일어날지는 열려 있으나, 그렇게 될 경우의 결과(하나님의 기뻐하지 않으심)를 강조한다.
