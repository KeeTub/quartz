# 1 Thessalonians 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> But concerning the times and the seasons, brothers, you have no need <span class="added-text">(for it)</span> to be written to you,

<span class="v-num">2</span> for you yourselves know accurately that the day of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord comes thus, as a thief in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> night.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> For when they say, "Peace and safety," then sudden destruction comes upon them, as the birth pang <span class="added-text">(comes)</span> upon her having in <span class="alt-label">womb</span><span class="alt-text">(=the pregnant woman)</span>, and they shall not escape.[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> But you, brothers, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you as a thief.[^3]

<span class="v-num">5</span> You are all sons of light and sons of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day; we are not of night nor of darkness.

<span class="v-num">6</span> So then let us not sleep as also the rest <span class="added-text">(do)</span>, but let us watch and be sober.[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For those sleeping sleep at night, and those getting drunk are drunk at night.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But we, being of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> day, let us be sober, having put on <span class="added-text">(the)</span> breastplate of faith and love, and <span class="added-text">(for)</span> a helmet, <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hope of salvation.[^5]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For God did not appoint us to wrath, but to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> obtaining of salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">10</span> who died for us, that whether we watch or sleep, we should live together with him.[^6]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Therefore encourage one another, and build up each one the other, just as also you do.

<span class="v-num">12</span> But we ask you, brothers, to know the ones laboring among you, and presiding over you in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, and admonishing you,

<span class="v-num">13</span> and to esteem them exceedingly highly in love, because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But we exhort you, brothers, admonish the disorderly, encourage the fainthearted, support the weak, be longsuffering toward all.

<span class="v-num">15</span> See that no one renders to anyone evil for evil, but always pursue the good, both toward one another and toward all.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Always rejoice.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Pray without ceasing.

<span class="v-num">18</span> In everything give thanks, for this <span class="added-text">(is the)</span> will of God in Christ Jesus toward you.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Do not quench the Spirit.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Do not despise prophecies.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Test all things; hold fast the good.

<span class="v-num">22</span> Abstain from every form of evil.

<span class="v-num">23</span> But may the God of peace himself sanctify you wholly; and may your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.[^7]

<span class="v-num">24</span> Faithful <span class="added-text">(is)</span> he who calls you, who will also do <span class="added-text">(it)</span>.[^8]

<span class="v-num">25</span> Brothers, pray concerning us.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Greet all the brothers with a holy kiss.

<span class="v-num">27</span> I adjure you by the Lord, <span class="added-text">(that)</span> this letter be read to all the holy brothers.

<span class="v-num">28</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you. Amen. <span class="added-text">(The)</span> first <span class="added-text">(letter)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Thessalonians was written from Athens.

[^1]: 현재형 ἔρχεται는 미래에 확실히 일어날 일을 생생하게 서술하는 용법이나 본문은 개역체 서사 흐름에 맞춰 자연스럽게 옮겼다.
[^2]: 평안과 안전이라는 말은 렘 6:14, 겔 13:10 등 거짓 평강 선포에 대한 구약적 배경을 반영한다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(καταλάβῃ)으로 결과적 목적을 나타내며, 어둠에 있지 않음이 그 날에 사로잡히지 않는 이유가 됨을 보인다.
[^4]: 권고적 가정법(καθεύδωμεν, γρηγορῶμεν, νήφωμεν)으로 공동체를 향한 권면을 나타낸다.
[^5]: 흉갑·투구의 군사적 은유는 엡 6장의 전신갑주 사상과 연결되며 믿음·사랑·소망이라는 신앙의 삼요소를 무장으로 표현한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(ζήσωμεν)이 그리스도의 죽으심의 목적을 나타내며, 깨어 있음과 잠듦은 문맥상 생사(生死)의 상태를 가리킨다.
[^7]: ἁγιάσαι·τηρηθείη는 모두 희구법으로 기원(祈願)의 어조를 나타내며, 영·혼·몸의 전인적 보전을 구하는 축도의 성격을 지닌다.
[^8]: πιστὸς는 하나님의 성품을 가리키는 핵심어로, 앞 절의 기원이 인간의 노력이 아닌 부르시는 이의 신실하심에 근거함을 강조한다.
