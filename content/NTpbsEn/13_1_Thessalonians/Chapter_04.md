<span class="v-num">1</span> Now then, brothers, we ask you and exhort <span class="added-text">(you)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus, just as you received from us how it is necessary <span class="added-text">(for)</span> you to walk and to please God, that you may abound more.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> For you know what commands we gave you through the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For this is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> will of God, your sanctification, that you abstain from fornication;[^2]

<span class="v-num">4</span> that each of you know <span class="added-text">(how)</span> to possess his own vessel in sanctification and honor,

<span class="v-num">5</span> not in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> passion of lust, just as also the Gentiles who do not know God;

<span class="v-num">6</span> that no one transgress and take advantage of his brother in this <span class="alt-label">matter</span><span class="alt-text">(=affair)</span>, because the Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> an avenger concerning all these things, just as we also told you before and solemnly testified.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> For God did not call us <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> uncleanness, but in sanctification.

<span class="v-num">8</span> Therefore the one rejecting <span class="added-text">(this)</span> does not reject man, but God, who also gave His Holy Spirit <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=into)</span> us.

<span class="v-num">9</span> But concerning brotherly love you have no need for <span class="added-text">(anyone)</span> to write to you; for you yourselves are taught by God to love one another;[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> for indeed you do this <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> all the brothers in the whole <span class="added-text">(of)</span> Macedonia. But we exhort you, brothers, to abound more,

<span class="v-num">11</span> and to be <span class="alt-label">ambitious</span><span class="alt-text">(=strive)</span> to live quietly, and to attend to your own affairs, and to work with your own hands, just as we commanded you;

<span class="v-num">12</span> so that you may walk decently toward those outside, and have need of nothing.[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> But I do not want you to be ignorant, brothers, concerning those who are asleep, so that you do not grieve, just as also the rest who have no hope.[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> For if we believe that Jesus died and rose again, so also God will bring with him those who have fallen asleep <span class="alt-label">through</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> Jesus.[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> For this we say to you by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> word of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, that we who are living, who remain until the coming of the Lord, will not at all precede those who have fallen asleep.[^8]

<span class="v-num">16</span> For the Lord himself will descend from heaven with a <span class="alt-label">shout</span><span class="alt-text">(=command)</span>, with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> voice of an archangel, and with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> trumpet of God, and the dead in Christ will rise first;[^9]

<span class="v-num">17</span> then we who are living, who remain, will be caught up together with them in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> clouds to meet the Lord in the air, and so we will always be with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.[^10]

<span class="v-num">18</span> So then, <span class="alt-label">comfort</span><span class="alt-text">(=exhort)</span> one another with these words.

[^1]: ἵνα περισσεύητε는 가정법 목적절로, 그리스도인의 행실이 더욱 풍성해지는 것을 목표로 제시한다.
[^2]: ἁγιασμὸς(거룩함)는 여기서 단회적 사건이 아니라 지속적 삶의 상태로서 하나님의 뜻과 직접 동일시된다.
[^3]: ἔκδικος(보응자)는 하나님이 성적 부정과 형제 침해에 대해 친히 심판하시는 재판장이심을 강조한다.
[^4]: θεοδίδακτοί(하나님께 배운)는 신약에 유일하게 쓰인 조어로, 성령의 내적 가르침을 통해 형제 사랑이 이미 심겨졌음을 뜻한다.
[^5]: ἵνα περιπατῆτε는 앞선 권면들의 목적을 밝히는 절로, 외인 앞에서의 신뢰성 있는 삶을 지향한다.
[^6]: ἵνα+가정법(λυπῆσθε) 목적절로, 바울이 잠자는 자들에 대해 알려주는 목적(소망 없는 자들처럼 슬퍼하지 않게 하려는 것)을 나타낸다.
[^7]: διὰ τοῦ Ἰησοῦ가 '잠든 자들'에 걸리는지 '데려오다'에 걸리는지 어순상 애매하나, 전통적으로 전자로 읽어 '예수 안에서 잠든 자들'로 옮긴다.
[^8]: ἐν λόγῳ Κυρίου(주의 말씀으로)는 바울이 자신의 사견이 아니라 주께 받은 계시로서 이 가르침을 전함을 밝힌다.
[^9]: καταβήσεται(내려가다)의 미래시제는 주의 재림이 확실한 미래 사건임을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἁρπαγησόμεθα(이끌려 올라가다)는 수동태 미래형으로 '휴거' 교리의 근거가 되는 핵심 동사이다.
