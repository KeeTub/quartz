# 1 Thessalonians 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore no longer enduring <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, we thought <span class="added-text">(it)</span> good to be left in Athens alone,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and we sent Timothy our brother and <span class="added-text">(a)</span> minister of God and our fellow worker in the gospel of Christ, to establish you and to encourage you concerning your faith,[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> that no one be <span class="alt-label">shaken</span><span class="alt-text">(=troubled)</span> in these afflictions; for you yourselves know that we are appointed to this.

<span class="v-num">4</span> For indeed when we were with you, we were telling you beforehand that we were about to be afflicted, just as also it came to pass, and you know.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Because of this, I also no longer enduring <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, sent to know your faith, lest somehow the tempter had tempted you, and our labor should become in vain.

<span class="v-num">6</span> But now Timothy having come to us from you, and having brought us good news of your faith and love, and that you have good remembrance of us always, longing to see us, just as we also <span class="added-text">(long to see)</span> you,

<span class="v-num">7</span> because of this we were comforted, brothers, over you, in all our affliction and distress, through your faith;

<span class="v-num">8</span> for now we live, if you stand fast in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord.[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> For what thanksgiving can we render to God concerning you, for all the joy with which we rejoice because of you before our God,

<span class="v-num">10</span> night and day praying exceedingly to see your face, and to <span class="alt-label">perfect</span><span class="alt-text">(=complete)</span> what is lacking in your faith?

<span class="v-num">11</span> Now may our God and Father Himself, and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct our way to you;[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> and may the Lord make you increase and abound in love toward one another and toward all, just as we also <span class="added-text">(do)</span> toward you,[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> to establish your hearts blameless in holiness before our God and Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all His saints.[^5]

[^1]: εἰς τὸ στηρίξαι는 목적 부정사구문으로 디모데를 보낸 목적을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ἐὰν+가정법(στήκητε)은 아직 실현될 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3급 조건문이다.
[^3]: κατευθύναι는 희구법으로 소원·기도의 뜻을 담고 있다.
[^4]: πλεονάσαι, περισσεύσαι는 희구법으로 축복의 기도를 나타낸다.
[^5]: εἰς τὸ στηρίξαι는 목적 부정사구문으로 앞선 축복 기도의 목적을 나타낸다.
