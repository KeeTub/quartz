# 1 Thessalonians 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul and Silvanus and Timothy, to the church of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Thessalonians in God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ: grace to you and peace from God our Father and <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">2</span> We give thanks to God always concerning all of you, making mention of you <span class="alt-label">at</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> our prayers,

<span class="v-num">3</span> remembering without ceasing your work of faith, and labor of love, and patience of hope of our Lord Jesus Christ, before our God and Father,

<span class="v-num">4</span> knowing, brothers loved by God, your <span class="alt-label">election</span><span class="alt-text">(=choosing)</span>;[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> because our gospel did not come <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=unto)</span> you in word only, but also in power, and in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit, and in much full assurance, just as you know what kind <span class="added-text">(of men)</span> we became among you for your sake.

<span class="v-num">6</span> And you became imitators of us and of the Lord, having received the word in much affliction with joy of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Holy Spirit,

<span class="v-num">7</span> so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and <span class="added-text">(in)</span> Achaia.

<span class="v-num">8</span> For from you the word of the Lord has sounded forth, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but also in every place your faith toward God has gone out, so that we have no need to say anything.

<span class="v-num">9</span> For they themselves report concerning us what kind of entrance we had <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> you, and how you turned to God from idols, to serve <span class="added-text">(the)</span> living and true God,

<span class="v-num">10</span> and to wait for His Son from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> heavens, whom He raised from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead, Jesus, the one delivering us from the coming wrath.[^2]

[^1]: ἐκλογή(택하심)는 인간의 결단이 아니라 하나님 편에서의 선택 행위를 가리키는 용어로, 이하 5절의 복음 사역의 능력 있는 임함이 그 증거로 제시된다.
[^2]: ῥυόμενον(건지시는)은 현재분사로서 예수의 구원 사역이 일회적 과거 사건이 아니라 지속적·현재적 성격을 지님을 나타낸다.
