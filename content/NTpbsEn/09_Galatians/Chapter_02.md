<span class="v-num">1</span> Then after fourteen years I went up again to Jerusalem with Barnabas, taking Titus along also.

<span class="v-num">2</span> And I went up according to <span class="added-text">(a)</span> revelation, and I laid before them the gospel which I preach among the Gentiles, but privately to those who were of repute, lest somehow I should be running or had run in vain.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> But not even Titus, who was with me, being a Greek, was compelled to be circumcised.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But <span class="added-text">(this was)</span> because of the false brothers secretly brought in, who slipped in to spy out our freedom which we have in Christ Jesus, in order that they might enslave us—[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> to whom we did not yield in subjection even for an hour, so that the truth of the gospel might remain <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=for)</span> you.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> But from those seeming to be something—whatever they were then makes no difference to me; God does not accept <span class="added-text">(the)</span> <span class="alt-label">face</span><span class="alt-text">(=person)</span> of man—for those of repute added nothing to me,[^4]

<span class="v-num">7</span> but on the contrary, having seen that I had been entrusted <span class="added-text">(with)</span> the gospel of the uncircumcision, just as Peter <span class="added-text">(with that)</span> of the circumcision,[^5]

<span class="v-num">8</span> <span class="added-text">(for)</span> he who worked in Peter for <span class="added-text">(the)</span> apostleship of the circumcision worked also in me for the Gentiles);

<span class="v-num">9</span> and having known the grace given to me, James and Cephas and John, those seeming to be pillars, gave to me and Barnabas <span class="added-text">(the)</span> right hands of fellowship, that we <span class="added-text">(should go)</span> to the Gentiles, and they to the circumcision;[^6]

<span class="v-num">10</span> only that we should remember the poor, which very thing I also was eager to do.[^7]

<span class="v-num">11</span> But when Peter came to Antioch, I opposed him to <span class="added-text">(his)</span> face, because he had been condemned.[^8]

<span class="v-num">12</span> For before certain <span class="added-text">(men)</span> came from James, he was eating with the Gentiles; but when they came, he began to draw back and separate himself, fearing those of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> circumcision.[^9]

<span class="v-num">13</span> And the rest of the Jews also joined <span class="added-text">(him)</span> in hypocrisy along with him, so that even Barnabas was carried away together <span class="added-text">(with them)</span> by their hypocrisy.

<span class="v-num">14</span> But when I saw that they were not walking <span class="alt-label">straight</span><span class="alt-text">(=uprightly)</span> toward the truth of the gospel, I said to Peter before <span class="added-text">(them)</span> all, "If you, being a Jew, live like <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles and not like <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Jews, why do you compel the Gentiles to live like <span class="alt-label">Jews</span><span class="alt-text">(=Judaize)</span>?"

<span class="v-num">15</span> We <span class="added-text">(are)</span> Jews by nature, and not sinners of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Gentiles,

<span class="v-num">16</span> knowing that a man is not justified by works of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, <span class="alt-label">but</span><span class="alt-text">(=except)</span> through faith <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> Jesus Christ, we also believed <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=into)</span> Christ Jesus, that we might be justified by faith <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> Christ, and not by works of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law; because by works of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law no <span class="alt-label">flesh</span><span class="alt-text">(=no one)</span> will be justified.[^10]

<span class="v-num">17</span> But if, seeking to be justified in Christ, we ourselves also were found sinners, is Christ then a minister of sin? May it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=God forbid)</span>![^11]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For if I build again those things which I destroyed, I <span class="alt-label">demonstrate</span><span class="alt-text">(=prove)</span> myself <span class="added-text">(to be)</span> a transgressor.

<span class="v-num">19</span> For I through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law died to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, that I might live to God.[^12]

<span class="v-num">20</span> I have been crucified with Christ; and I live, no longer I, but Christ lives in me; and the <span class="added-text">(life)</span> which I now live in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, I live by faith <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> the Son of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.[^13]

<span class="v-num">21</span> I do not set <span class="alt-label">aside</span><span class="alt-text">(=nullify)</span> the grace of God; for if righteousness <span class="added-text">(comes)</span> through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law, then Christ died in <span class="alt-label">vain</span><span class="alt-text">(=for nothing)</span>.[^14]

[^1]: μή πως... τρέχω ἢ ἔδραμον은 부정적 목적(우려)을 나타내는 가정법 구문으로, 바울이 자신의 사역이 헛되지 않기를 염려하는 뉘앙스를 담는다.
[^2]: ἵνα καταδουλώσωνται는 거짓 형제들이 몰래 들어온 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^3]: ἵνα διαμείνῃ는 복음의 진리를 보존하려는 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^4]: πρόσωπον ... λαμβάνει는 히브리어 관용구를 반영한 표현으로 '사람을 외모로 판단하다, 편애하다'라는 뜻이다.
[^5]: τῆς ἀκροβυστίας와 τῆς περιτομῆς는 신체적 상태가 아니라 이방인과 유대인이라는 두 선교 대상 집단을 가리키는 관사적 용법이다.
[^6]: δεξιὰς ἔδωκαν은 고대 근동에서 동역 관계를 확증하는 악수의 관용구이며, ἵνα절은 사역 분담의 목적을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἵνα μνημονεύωμεν은 가난한 자를 기억하라는 요청의 내용(목적)을 나타내는 가정법절이다.
[^8]: κατεγνωσμένος ἦν은 완료분사+미완료형으로 이루어진 대과거적 표현으로, 베드로의 책망받을 상태가 바울이 도착하기 전부터 이미 성립되어 있었음을 나타낸다.
[^9]: συνήσθιεν, ὑπέστελλε, ἀφώριζεν은 모두 미완료 시제로 베드로의 반복적·지속적 행동 변화를 생생하게 묘사한다.
[^10]: ἐὰν μὴ διὰ πίστεως는 율법의 행위로 의롭게 됨을 전면 부정하고 오직 그리스도의 믿음을 통한 의롭게 됨만을 인정하는 예외적 조건 표현이며, ἵνα δικαιωθῶμεν은 그 목적을 나타내는 가정법절이다.
[^11]: μὴ γένοιτο는 '결코 그럴 수 없다'는 강한 부정을 나타내는 관용적 기원법 표현이다.
[^12]: ἵνα ζήσω는 율법에 대해 죽음으로써 하나님께 대해 사는 목적을 나타내는 가정법절이다.
[^13]: τῇ τοῦ υἱοῦ τοῦ Θεοῦ (믿음)은 목적격적 소유격(그리스도를 믿는 믿음)과 주격적 소유격(그리스도 자신의 신실하심)으로 모두 해석 가능한 핵심 신학 구문이다.
[^14]: εἰ διὰ νόμου δικαιοσύνη는 사실과 반대되는 상황을 전제하는 조건문으로, 율법으로는 의를 얻을 수 없다는 결론을 강조한다.
