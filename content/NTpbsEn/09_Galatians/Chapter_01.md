<span class="v-num">1</span> Paul, an apostle - not from men, nor through man, but through Jesus Christ and God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father, the one having raised him from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> dead,

<span class="v-num">2</span> and all the brothers with me, to the churches of Galatia:

<span class="v-num">3</span> Grace to you and peace from God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father and our Lord Jesus Christ,

<span class="v-num">4</span> who gave himself for our sins, so that he might deliver us out of this present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father,[^1]

<span class="v-num">5</span> to whom <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory forever and ever. Amen.

<span class="v-num">6</span> I marvel that you are so quickly turning away from the one who called you in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> grace of Christ, to a different gospel,[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> which is not another, except there are some who are troubling you and wanting to distort the gospel of Christ.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But even if we, or an angel from heaven, should preach a gospel to you contrary to what we preached to you, let him be accursed.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> As we have said before, and now I say again, if anyone preaches a gospel to you contrary to what you received, let him be accursed.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For am I now persuading men, or God? Or am I seeking to please men? For if I were still pleasing men, I would not be a servant of Christ.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But I make known to you, brothers, the gospel that was preached by me, that it is not according to man.

<span class="v-num">12</span> For neither did I receive it from man, nor was I taught <span class="added-text">(it)</span>, but <span class="added-text">(it came)</span> through <span class="added-text">(the)</span> revelation of Jesus Christ.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For you heard of my former manner of life in Judaism, that I was <span class="alt-label">persecuting</span><span class="alt-text">(=pursuing)</span> the church of God beyond measure, and was destroying it,[^4]

<span class="v-num">14</span> and I was advancing in Judaism beyond many of my own <span class="alt-label">age</span><span class="alt-text">(=contemporaries)</span> among my <span class="added-text">(own)</span> people, being more exceedingly zealous for the traditions of my fathers.

<span class="v-num">15</span> But when God, who set me apart from my mother's womb and called <span class="added-text">(me)</span> through his grace, was pleased

<span class="v-num">16</span> to reveal his Son in me, that I might preach him among the Gentiles, immediately I did not confer with flesh and blood,[^5]

<span class="v-num">17</span> nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia, and returned again to Damascus.

<span class="v-num">18</span> Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to <span class="alt-label">visit</span><span class="alt-text">(=make acquaintance with)</span> Peter, and stayed with him fifteen days.

<span class="v-num">19</span> But I saw none other of the apostles, except James the Lord's brother.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now the things that I write to you, behold, before God, <span class="added-text">(I say)</span> that I do not lie.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cilicia.

<span class="v-num">22</span> And I was unknown by face to the churches of Judea that were in Christ,

<span class="v-num">23</span> but they were only hearing, "The one who once <span class="alt-label">persecuted</span><span class="alt-text">(=pursued)</span> us now preaches the faith which he once destroyed."

<span class="v-num">24</span> And they glorified God in me.

[^1]: ὅπως+가정법은 목적절로, 하나님이 자기 아들을 주신 목적이 우리를 악한 세대에서 건져내려 하심에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: μετατί�εσθε는 현재 중간태로 갈라디아인들이 지금 한창 진행 중인 이탈을 스스로 행하고 있음을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἐάν+가정법은 아직 일어나지 않은 가상의 상황을 나타내는 3급 조건문으로, 바울 자신이나 천사라도 예외가 될 수 없음을 강조한다.
[^4]: διώκω는 본래 '뒤쫓다'는 뜻이지만 여기서는 교회를 대적하여 '박해하다'는 뜻으로 쓰였다.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법은 하나님이 자기 아들을 바울 안에 나타내신 목적이 이방인에게 복음을 전하게 하려 하심에 있음을 나타낸다.
