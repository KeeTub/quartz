# Galatians 5

<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore stand fast in the freedom with which Christ set us free, and do not be entangled again with <span class="added-text">(the)</span> yoke of slavery.

<span class="v-num">2</span> Behold, I Paul say to you, that if you are circumcised, Christ will profit you nothing.[^1]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I testify again to every man being circumcised, that he is a debtor to do <span class="added-text">(the)</span> whole law.

<span class="v-num">4</span> You were <span class="alt-label">severed</span><span class="alt-text">(=annulled)</span> from Christ, you who are justified by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law; you have fallen from grace.[^2]

<span class="v-num">5</span> For we, by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, out of (=by) faith, eagerly await <span class="added-text">(the)</span> hope of righteousness.

<span class="v-num">6</span> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision, but faith working through love.[^3]

<span class="v-num">7</span> You were running well; who hindered you from <span class="alt-label">obeying</span><span class="alt-text">(=being persuaded by)</span> the truth?

<span class="v-num">8</span> This persuasion <span class="added-text">(is)</span> not from the one calling you.

<span class="v-num">9</span> A little leaven leavens the whole lump.

<span class="v-num">10</span> I have confidence in you in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, that you will think nothing else; but the one troubling you shall bear the judgment, whoever he may be.

<span class="v-num">11</span> But I, brothers, if I still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? Then the stumbling block of the cross has been abolished.

<span class="v-num">12</span> I would that those who <span class="alt-label">unsettle</span><span class="alt-text">(=trouble)</span> you would even cut themselves off.

<span class="v-num">13</span> For you were called to freedom, brothers; only <span class="added-text">(do)</span> not <span class="added-text">(turn)</span> the freedom into an <span class="alt-label">occasion</span><span class="alt-text">(=opportunity)</span> for the flesh, but through love serve one another.

<span class="v-num">14</span> For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, in this: 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">15</span> But if you bite and devour one another, watch out lest you be consumed by one another.

<span class="v-num">16</span> But I say, walk by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, and you shall not fulfill <span class="added-text">(the)</span> desire of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh.[^5]

<span class="v-num">17</span> For the flesh desires against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh; and these are opposed to one another, so that you may not do the things that you would.[^6]

<span class="v-num">18</span> But if you are led by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, you are not under <span class="added-text">(the)</span> law.

<span class="v-num">19</span> Now the works of the flesh are evident, which are: adultery, fornication, uncleanness, licentiousness,

<span class="v-num">20</span> idolatry, sorcery, hatreds, strife, jealousies, wraths, <span class="alt-label">factions</span><span class="alt-text">(=selfish ambitions)</span>, divisions, <span class="alt-label">heresies</span><span class="alt-text">(=sects)</span>,

<span class="v-num">21</span> envyings, murders, drunkenness, revelings, and things like these; of which I forewarn you, just as I also foretold, that those practicing such things shall not inherit <span class="added-text">(the)</span> kingdom of God.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faith,[^7]

<span class="v-num">23</span> gentleness, self-control; against such things there is no law.

<span class="v-num">24</span> But those who are of Christ have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.[^8]

<span class="v-num">25</span> If we live by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, let us also walk by <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit.

<span class="v-num">26</span> Let us not become vainglorious, provoking one another, envying one another.

[^1]: ἐάν+가정법(περιτέμνησθε)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '혹시라도 할례를 받는다면'이라는 열려있는 가정을 뜻한다. 이는 실제로 일부 성도들이 할례를 받으려는 유혹에 있었음을 암시하며, 그 결과의 심각성(그리스도의 무익함)을 경고하는 강력한 조건문이다.
[^2]: κατηργήθητε는 부정과거 수동태로 이미 완결된 단절을 나타냄 - 율법으로 의롭다 함을 구하는 순간 그리스도와의 관계가 이미 끊어졌음을 강조한다.
[^3]: ἐνεργουμένη는 중간태/수동태로 사랑을 통해 '역사하는(또는 역사되는)' 믿음을 가리키며, 믿음과 사랑의 유기적 결합을 강조한다.
[^4]: 레위기 19:18의 인용으로 율법 전체의 요약을 사랑의 계명으로 제시한다.
[^5]: οὐ μὴ+부정과거 가정법은 강한 미래 부정을 나타내어 '결코 이루지 아니하리라'는 확신을 표현한다.
[^6]: ἵνα μὴ+가정법 목적절로, 육신과 성령의 대적 관계가 신자의 뜻대로 행함을 막는 목적(결과)을 나타낸다.
[^7]: ἔργα(복수, 육신의 일들)와 달리 καρπός(단수, 성령의 열매)는 성령의 역사가 여러 조각이 아닌 하나의 통일된 인격적 열매임을 보여준다.
[^8]: ἐσταύρωσαν은 부정과거로 그리스도인의 정체성 안에서 이미 완결된 사건임을 나타낸다—육신의 십자가 못박힘은 계속 반복할 과제가 아니라 이미 이루어진 사실이다.
