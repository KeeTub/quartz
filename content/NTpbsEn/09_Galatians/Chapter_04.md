# Galatians 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> Now I say, for as much time <span class="added-text">(as)</span> the heir is a child, he differs nothing from a slave, though being lord of all,

<span class="v-num">2</span> but he is under guardians and stewards until the time appointed of the father.

<span class="v-num">3</span> So we also, when we were children, were held in bondage under the elements of the world;

<span class="v-num">4</span> but when the fullness of the time came, God sent forth his Son, having become <span class="added-text">(born)</span> of a woman, having become <span class="added-text">(born)</span> under the law,

<span class="v-num">5</span> that he might redeem those under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> And because you are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, "Abba, Father."

<span class="v-num">7</span> So you are no longer a slave, but a son; and if a son, also an heir of God through Christ.

<span class="v-num">8</span> But at that time, indeed, not knowing God, you served <span class="added-text">(as slaves to)</span> those who by nature are not gods;

<span class="v-num">9</span> But now, having known God, or rather having been known by God, how do you turn again to the weak and beggarly elements, to which you desire to be enslaved again from the start?[^2]

<span class="v-num">10</span> You observe days, and months, and seasons, and years.

<span class="v-num">11</span> I fear for you, lest somehow I have labored for you in vain.

<span class="v-num">12</span> Become as I <span class="added-text">(am)</span>, for I also <span class="added-text">(have become)</span> as you <span class="added-text">(are)</span>, brothers, I beg of you. You have wronged me in nothing.

<span class="v-num">13</span> But you know that through weakness of the flesh I preached the gospel to you at the first,

<span class="v-num">14</span> and my trial which was in my flesh you did not despise nor spit <span class="alt-label">out</span><span class="alt-text">(=reject)</span>, but you received me as an angel of God, as Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Where then was your blessedness? For I testify to you that, if it had been possible, you would have plucked out your own eyes and given <span class="added-text">(them)</span> to me.[^3]

<span class="v-num">16</span> So have I become your enemy, telling you the truth?

<span class="v-num">17</span> They zealously seek you, not rightly, but they desire to exclude you, that you might be zealous for them.[^4]

<span class="v-num">18</span> But it is good to be zealously sought in a good thing always, and not only when I am present with you,

<span class="v-num">19</span> my little children, for whom I am again in <span class="alt-label">labor</span><span class="alt-text">(=travail)</span>, until Christ be formed in you,

<span class="v-num">20</span> But I would wish to be present with you now, and to change my <span class="alt-label">voice</span><span class="alt-text">(=tone)</span>, for I am perplexed concerning you.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Tell me, you who desire to be under the law, do you not hear the law?

<span class="v-num">22</span> For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one from the maidservant, and one from the free woman.[^5]

<span class="v-num">23</span> But the one from the maidservant was born according to the flesh, but the one from the free woman was through the promise.

<span class="v-num">24</span> These things are <span class="alt-label">allegorized</span><span class="alt-text">(=are an allegory)</span>; for these are the two covenants: the one from Mount Sinai, bearing <span class="added-text">(children)</span> into bondage, which is Hagar.[^6]

<span class="v-num">25</span> For this Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia, and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, and is in bondage with her children.

<span class="v-num">26</span> But the Jerusalem above is free, which is <span class="added-text">(the)</span> mother of us all.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For it is written, "Rejoice, <span class="added-text">(you)</span> barren woman who does not bear; break forth and cry, you who are not in travail; for many <span class="added-text">(are)</span> the children of the desolate rather than of her who has the husband."[^7]

<span class="v-num">28</span> Now we, brothers, according to Isaac, are children of promise.

<span class="v-num">29</span> But just as then the one born according to the flesh persecuted the one born according to the Spirit, so also now.

<span class="v-num">30</span> But what does the scripture say? "Cast out the maidservant and her son, for the son of the maidservant shall by no means inherit with the son of the free woman."[^8]

<span class="v-num">31</span> So then, brothers, we are not children of a maidservant, but of the free woman.

[^1]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절이 두 번 반복되어 그리스도의 오심의 이중 목적(속량과 양자됨)을 강조한다.
[^2]: γνόντες(능동, 너희가 앎)와 γνωσθέντες(수동, 하나님께 알려짐)의 대조는 구원이 인간의 인식이 아니라 하나님의 은혜에서 비롯됨을 강조한다.
[^3]: εἰ+평서법과 ἂν이 결합된 현실과 반대되는(과거) 조건문으로, 실제로는 불가능했던 극진한 사랑을 회상하며 표현한다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법 목적절로 거짓 교사들의 숨은 의도를 드러낸다.
[^5]: 창세기 16장과 21장의 이스마엘·이삭 출생 기사를 인용한다.
[^6]: ἀλληγορούμενα는 역사적 사실을 부정하지 않으면서 그 안에 담긴 언약적 의미를 풀어내는 바울의 해석 방법을 가리킨다.
[^7]: 이사야 54:1을 인용하여 자유로운 예루살렘(약속의 자녀들)의 풍성함을 예언적으로 뒷받침한다.
[^8]: 창세기 21:10을 인용하여 율법주의자들을 향한 결론적 경고로 삼는다.
