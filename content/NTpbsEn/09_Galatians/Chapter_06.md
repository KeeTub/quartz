# Galatians 6

<span class="v-num">1</span> Brothers, if even a man is overtaken in some trespass, you the spiritual ones, restore such a one in a spirit of gentleness, watching yourself, lest you also be tempted.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Bear one another's burdens, and thus fulfill the law of Christ.

<span class="v-num">3</span> For if anyone thinks himself to be something, being nothing, he deceives himself.

<span class="v-num">4</span> But let each one examine his own work, and then he will have <span class="added-text">(his)</span> boasting in regard to himself alone, and not in regard to the other.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For each one shall bear his own load.

<span class="v-num">6</span> Let the one being taught the word share with the one teaching, in all good things.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Do not be deceived, God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, this he will also reap.[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> For the one sowing to his own flesh will reap corruption from the flesh; but the one sowing to the Spirit will reap eternal life from the Spirit.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> And let us not grow weary in doing good; for in due season we will reap, if we do not faint.

<span class="v-num">10</span> So then, as we have <span class="alt-label">opportunity</span><span class="alt-text">(=time)</span>, let us work the good toward all, but especially toward those of the household of faith.

<span class="v-num">11</span> <span class="alt-label">See</span><span class="alt-text">(=Look)</span> with how large letters I have written to you with my own hand.

<span class="v-num">12</span> As many as desire to make a good showing in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh, these compel you to be circumcised, only that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> For not even the ones being circumcised themselves keep the law, but they desire you to be circumcised, that they may boast in your flesh.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> But may it not be <span class="added-text">(that)</span> I boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through whom <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.[^6]

<span class="v-num">15</span> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation.[^7]

<span class="v-num">16</span> And as many as will walk by this <span class="alt-label">rule</span><span class="alt-text">(=canon)</span>, peace <span class="added-text">(be)</span> upon them and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.[^8]

<span class="v-num">17</span> From now on, let no one trouble me, for I bear in my body the marks of the Lord Jesus.

<span class="v-num">18</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with your spirit, brothers. Amen. <span class="added-text">(Written)</span> to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Galatians from Rome.

[^1]: ἐὰν+προληφθῇ(가정법)는 아직 실현되지 않은 가상적 상황을 전제하는 3급 조건문으로, 실제 발생 여부보다 가능성에 대한 권면임을 나타낸다.
[^2]: ὃ ἐὰν σπείρῃ는 '무엇을 심든지'라는 뜻의 부정관계대명사+ἐάν+가정법 구문으로, 아직 실현되지 않은 일반적 가능성을 나타내는 조건절이다. 심는 대로 거둔다는 보편적 원리를 강조한다.
[^3]: σάρξ(육체)와 πνεῦμα(성령)의 대조는 인간의 본성적 욕망을 따르는 삶과 성령을 따르는 삶이 맺는 열매가 근본적으로 다름을 보여준다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(διώκωνται) 목적절로, 할례를 강요하는 실제 동기(십자가 박해를 피하려는 목적)를 밝힌다. 겉으로 내세운 명분이 아니라 속셈을 폭로하는 뉘앙스.
[^5]: ἵνα+가정법(καυχήσωνται)은 목적절로, 할례를 강요하는 그들의 숨은 의도(목적)를 드러낸다. 즉 '너희 육체로 자랑하려는' 것이 할례 강요의 실제 목적임을 밝히는 구문이다.
[^6]: ἐσταύρωται는 완료시제로, 과거의 십자가 사건이 낳은 단절된 관계가 현재까지 지속됨을 나타낸다.
[^7]: καινὴ κτίσις(새로운 창조)는 그리스도 안에서 이루어지는 존재론적 변화를 가리키며, 할례·무할례라는 외적 구분을 무의미하게 만든다.
[^8]: '하나님의 이스라엘'이 교회 전체를 가리키는지 혈통적 이스라엘을 가리키는지는 해석이 갈리는 핵심 구절이다.
