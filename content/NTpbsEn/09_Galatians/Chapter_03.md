<span class="v-num">1</span> O foolish Galatians, who bewitched you not to obey the truth, before whose eyes Jesus Christ was previously <span class="alt-label">written</span><span class="alt-text">(=portrayed)</span> among you as crucified?

<span class="v-num">2</span> This only I want to learn from you: did you receive the Spirit from works of law, or from hearing of faith?

<span class="v-num">3</span> Are you so foolish? Having begun in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, are you now being <span class="alt-label">perfected</span><span class="alt-text">(=completed)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh?

<span class="v-num">4</span> Did you suffer so many things in vain? If indeed also in vain.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Therefore the one supplying to you the Spirit, and working <span class="alt-label">miracles</span><span class="alt-text">(=works of power)</span> among you, <span class="added-text">(does he do it)</span> from works of law or from hearing of faith?

<span class="v-num">6</span> Just as Abraham believed God, and it was reckoned to him <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=as)</span> righteousness.[^1]

<span class="v-num">7</span> Know therefore that those of faith, these are sons of Abraham.

<span class="v-num">8</span> The scripture, having foreseen that God justifies the nations <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> faith, preached the gospel beforehand to Abraham, <span class="added-text">(saying,)</span> 'In you all the nations shall be blessed.'[^2]

<span class="v-num">9</span> So then, those of faith are blessed together with the believing Abraham.

<span class="v-num">10</span> For as many as are of works of law are under a curse; for it is written, 'Cursed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> everyone who does not <span class="alt-label">continue</span><span class="alt-text">(=remain)</span> in all the things having been written in the book of the law, to do them.'[^3]

<span class="v-num">11</span> But that no one is justified <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> law before God <span class="added-text">(is)</span> evident, because 'the righteous shall live <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> faith.'[^4]

<span class="v-num">12</span> But the law is not of faith, but 'The man who has done them shall live in them.'[^5]

<span class="v-num">13</span> Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, having become a curse <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=on behalf of)</span> us; for it is written, 'Cursed <span class="added-text">(is)</span> everyone hanging on a <span class="alt-label">tree</span><span class="alt-text">(=cross)</span>.'[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> in order that the blessing of Abraham might come to the nations in Christ Jesus, in order that we might receive the promise of the Spirit through faith.[^7]

<span class="v-num">15</span> Brothers, I speak according to man. Yet a man's covenant, having been confirmed, no one annuls or adds <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=supplements)</span>.

<span class="v-num">16</span> Now to Abraham the promises were spoken, and to his seed. He does not say, 'And to seeds,' as <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> many, but as of one, 'And to your seed,' which is Christ.[^8]

<span class="v-num">17</span> But this I say, a covenant previously confirmed by God <span class="alt-label">unto</span><span class="alt-text">(=regarding)</span> Christ, the law having come into <span class="alt-label">being</span><span class="alt-text">(=which came)</span> four hundred and thirty years after, does not annul, so as to make <span class="alt-label">void</span><span class="alt-text">(=nullify)</span> the promise.[^9]

<span class="v-num">18</span> For if the inheritance <span class="added-text">(is)</span> from law, <span class="added-text">(it is)</span> no longer from promise; but God has granted <span class="added-text">(it)</span> to Abraham through promise.

<span class="v-num">19</span> What then <span class="added-text">(is)</span> the law? It was added for the sake of transgressions, until the seed should come to whom it has been promised, having been ordained through angels, in the hand of a mediator.

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now the mediator is not of one, but God is one.

<span class="v-num">21</span> <span class="added-text">(Is)</span> the law then against the promises of God? Let it not <span class="alt-label">be</span><span class="alt-text">(=Certainly not)</span>! For if a law had been given which was able to make alive, righteousness would indeed have been from law.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But the scripture shut <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=confined)</span> all things under sin, in order that the promise <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> faith of Jesus Christ might be given to those who believe.[^10]

<span class="v-num">23</span> But before the faith came, we were <span class="alt-label">guarded</span><span class="alt-text">(=kept)</span> under law, shut up together unto the faith about to be revealed.

<span class="v-num">24</span> So that the law has become our <span class="alt-label">tutor</span><span class="alt-text">(=schoolmaster)</span> unto Christ, in order that we might be justified <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> faith.[^11]

<span class="v-num">25</span> But <span class="added-text">(the)</span> faith having come, we are no longer under a <span class="alt-label">tutor</span><span class="alt-text">(=schoolmaster)</span>.

<span class="v-num">26</span> For you are all sons of God through faith in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For as many <span class="added-text">(of you)</span> as were baptized into Christ have put on Christ.

<span class="v-num">28</span> There is neither Jew nor Greek; there is neither slave nor free; there is not male and female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">29</span> And if you <span class="added-text">(are)</span> of Christ, then you are Abraham's seed, and heirs according to promise.

[^1]: 창세기 15:6의 인용으로, ἐλογίσθη(여겨졌다)는 회계 용어로서 믿음이 의로 '전가'되었음을 나타낸다.
[^2]: 창세기 12:3, 18:18의 인용으로, 이방인 구원이 아브라함 언약 안에 이미 예고되었음을 보여준다.
[^3]: 신명기 27:26의 인용으로, 율법 전체를 온전히 지키지 못하는 자에 대한 저주를 명시한다.
[^4]: 하박국 2:4의 인용으로, 바울 신학에서 이신칭의의 핵심 근거 구절이다.
[^5]: 레위기 18:5의 인용으로, 율법의 원리는 믿음이 아니라 행함임을 대조한다.
[^6]: 신명기 21:23의 인용으로, 그리스도의 십자가 죽음이 율법의 저주를 대신 짊어지신 것으로 해석된다.
[^7]: 두 개의 ἵνα 목적절이 병렬되어 그리스도의 속량의 이중 목적(이방인의 복, 성령의 약속)을 보여준다.
[^8]: σπέρμα(씨)의 단수형을 근거로 아브라함 언약의 궁극적 성취가 그리스도 한 분께 있음을 논증한다.
[^9]: εἰς τό+부정사 구문으로, 율법이 결코 약속을 무효화하는 목적/결과를 가질 수 없음을 나타낸다.
[^10]: ἵνα 목적절로, 율법 아래 죄에 갇힘의 궁극 목적이 믿는 자에게 약속을 주기 위함임을 밝힌다.
[^11]: ἵνα 목적절로, 율법의 몽학선생 역할의 궁극 목적이 믿음으로 말미암은 칭의임을 밝힌다.
