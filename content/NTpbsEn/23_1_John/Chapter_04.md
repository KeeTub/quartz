# 1 John 4

<span class="v-num">1</span> Beloved, do not believe every spirit, but <span class="alt-label">test</span><span class="alt-text">(=discern)</span> the spirits, whether they are from God, because many false prophets have gone out into the world.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> By this you know the Spirit of God: every spirit that confesses Jesus Christ having come in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh is from God.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And every spirit that does not confess Jesus Christ having come in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> flesh is not from God, and this is the <span class="added-text">(spirit)</span> of the antichrist, which you have heard that it comes, and now it is already in the world.

<span class="v-num">4</span> You are of God, little children, and you have overcome them, because greater is he who is in you than he who is in the world.

<span class="v-num">5</span> They are of the world; therefore they speak <span class="alt-label">of</span><span class="alt-text">(=from)</span> the world, and the world listens to them.

<span class="v-num">6</span> We are of God; the one who knows God listens to us; the one who is not of God does not listen to us. By this we know the spirit of truth and the spirit of error.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Beloved, let us love one another, because love is from God, and everyone who loves has been born of God and knows God.

<span class="v-num">8</span> The one who does not love has not known God, because God is love.[^3]

<span class="v-num">9</span> By this the love of God was manifested <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=among)</span> us, that God has sent his only <span class="alt-label">begotten</span><span class="alt-text">(=unique)</span> Son into the world, that we might live through him.[^4]

<span class="v-num">10</span> In this is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us and sent his Son <span class="added-text">(as the)</span> <span class="alt-label">propitiation</span><span class="alt-text">(=atoning sacrifice)</span> <span class="alt-label">for</span><span class="alt-text">(=concerning)</span> our sins.[^5]

<span class="v-num">11</span> Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.

<span class="v-num">12</span> No one has ever seen God; if we love one another, God abides in us, and his love has been <span class="alt-label">perfected</span><span class="alt-text">(=made complete)</span> in us.[^6]

<span class="v-num">13</span> By this we know that we abide in him, and he in us, because he has given us of his Spirit.

<span class="v-num">14</span> And we have seen and testify that the Father has sent the Son <span class="added-text">(as)</span> Savior of the world.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Whoever confesses that Jesus is the Son of God, God abides in him, and he in God.

<span class="v-num">16</span> And we have known and believed the love which God has <span class="alt-label">in</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> us. God is love, and the one who abides in love abides in God, and God in him.

<span class="v-num">17</span> By this love has been <span class="alt-label">perfected</span><span class="alt-text">(=made complete)</span> with us, that we may have <span class="alt-label">boldness</span><span class="alt-text">(=confidence)</span> in the day of judgment, because as he is, so are we also in this world.[^7]

<span class="v-num">18</span> Fear is not in love, but <span class="alt-label">perfect</span><span class="alt-text">(=complete)</span> love casts out fear, because fear has <span class="alt-label">punishment</span><span class="alt-text">(=torment)</span>. But the one who fears has not been perfected in love.[^8]

<span class="v-num">19</span> We love <span class="added-text">(him)</span>, because he first loved us.

<span class="v-num">20</span> If anyone says, "I love God," and hates his brother, he is a liar; for the one who does not love his brother whom he has seen, how can he love God whom he has not seen?[^9]

<span class="v-num">21</span> And we have this commandment from him, that the one who loves God should love his brother also.[^10]

[^1]: δοκιμάζετε는 금이나 물건의 진위를 시험하듯 영을 분별하라는 뜻으로, 단순한 의심이 아니라 적극적 검증을 요구한다.
[^2]: ἐληλυθότα(완료분사)는 그리스도의 성육신이 과거 일회적 사건일 뿐 아니라 지금도 지속되는 영구한 사실임을 강조한다.
[^3]: ὁ Θεὸς ἀγάπη ἐστίν에서 ἀγάπη에 관사가 없는 것은 '하나님이 곧 사랑'이라는 등식이 아니라 '하나님은 사랑이라는 속성을 지니셨다'는 서술을 뜻한다.
[^4]: μονογενῆ는 단지 '유일하게 태어난' 것이 아니라 아버지와의 유일무이한 관계 안에 있는 독생자 됨을 가리킨다. ἵνα절은 하나님이 아들을 보내신 목적을 나타낸다.
[^5]: ἱλασμὸν은 단순히 죄를 덮는 것이 아니라 하나님의 진노를 그치게 하는 화목의 제물 됨을 뜻한다.
[^6]: ἐάν+가정법(ἀγαπῶμεν)은 아직 미확정된 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3종 조건문으로, '만일 우리가 서로 사랑한다면(그리고 그럴 가능성이 있다면)'이라는 뜻이다. 이는 단순 가정이 아니라 성도의 삶에서 반복적으로 실현되어야 할 조건으로 제시된다.
[^7]: τετελείωται(완료)는 사랑의 완성이 이미 이루어진 상태임을, ἵνα절은 그 완성의 목적이 심판 날의 담대함에 있음을 보여준다.
[^8]: τελεία ἀγάπη는 결함 없이 성숙하게 완성된 사랑을 가리키며, 그런 사랑은 형벌에 대한 두려움과 공존할 수 없다.
[^9]: ἐάν+가정법(εἴπῃ, μισῇ)은 아직 실현되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, '누구든지 ~라고 말하면서 ~하다면'이라는 가상의 상황을 전제한다. 실제 삶에서 언제든 일어날 수 있는 반복적 사례를 가리키는 뉘앙스이다.
[^10]: ἵνα+가정법(ἀγαπᾷ)은 그 계명의 내용을 설명하는 목적절/내용절로, '하나님을 사랑하는 자가 형제도 사랑해야 한다'는 계명의 실질적 내용을 나타낸다.
