# 1 John 1

<span class="v-num">1</span> That which was from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning, which we have heard, which we have seen with our eyes, which we looked upon, and our hands touched, concerning the word of life—[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> And the life was manifested, and we have seen, and testify, and declare to you the eternal life, which was <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=toward)</span> the Father, and was manifested to us—[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> That which we have seen and heard we declare to you also, that you also may have fellowship with us; and <span class="added-text">(indeed)</span> our fellowship <span class="added-text">(is)</span> with the Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ.[^3]

<span class="v-num">4</span> And these things we write to you, that our joy may be fulfilled.[^4]

<span class="v-num">5</span> And this is the <span class="alt-label">promise</span><span class="alt-text">(=message)</span> which we have heard from him and declare to you, that God is light, and in him there is no darkness at all.[^5]

<span class="v-num">6</span> If we say that we have fellowship with him, and walk in the darkness, we lie, and do not <span class="alt-label">practice</span><span class="alt-text">(=do)</span> the truth;[^6]

<span class="v-num">7</span> But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanses us from all sin.[^7]

<span class="v-num">8</span> If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.[^8]

<span class="v-num">9</span> If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just, to forgive us <span class="alt-label">the</span><span class="alt-text">(=our)</span> sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.[^9]

<span class="v-num">10</span> If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.[^10]

---

[^1]: ἦν(미완료)은 태초부터 이미 계속되어 온 존재를 나타내어 그리스도의 선재성을 함의한다.
[^2]: πρὸς τὸν πατέρα는 단순한 위치가 아니라 아버지와의 인격적 대면·교통의 관계를 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα+가정법(ἔχητε)은 목적절로, 사도의 전함의 목적이 독자의 교제 참여에 있음을 나타낸다.
[^4]: ἵνα+가정법(ᾖ πεπληρωμένη, 완료 수동태 우언법)은 기록의 목적이 기쁨의 충만함에 있음을 강조한다.
[^5]: ἐπαγγελία는 본래 '약속'이나 여기서는 그리스도로부터 들어 전하는 '소식·선포'의 의미로 쓰였다.
[^6]: ἐὰν+가정법(περιπατῶμεν)은 아직 실현 여부가 정해지지 않은 가정으로, 말과 행위의 불일치를 경계한다.
[^7]: ἐὰν+가정법(περιπατῶμεν)의 조건이 성취될 때 교제와 정결케 하심이 뒤따름을 보인다.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 죄 없음을 주장하는 태도 자체를 자기기만으로 규정한다.
[^9]: ἐὰν+가정법의 조건에 이어 ἵνα+가정법(ἀφῇ, καθαρίσῃ)의 목적절이 하나님의 신실하심에 근거한 용서와 정결을 보여준다.
[^10]: ἐὰν+가정법(εἴπωμεν)은 죄를 부인하는 것이 곧 하나님의 증언을 거짓으로 만드는 것임을 지적한다.
