# 1 John 3

<span class="v-num">1</span> Behold what manner of love the Father has given us, that we should be called children of God! Because of this the world does not know us, because it did not know him.[^1]

<span class="v-num">2</span> Beloved, now we are children of God, and it has not yet been revealed what we shall be. But we know that if he is revealed, we shall be like him, because we shall see him just as he is.[^2]

<span class="v-num">3</span> And everyone having this hope on him purifies himself, just as that one is pure.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Everyone practicing sin also practices lawlessness, and sin is lawlessness.

<span class="v-num">5</span> And you know that that one was revealed in order that he might take away our sins, and in him there is no sin.[^3]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Everyone abiding in him does not sin. Everyone sinning has not seen him, nor has known him.

<span class="v-num">7</span> Little children, let no one deceive you. The one practicing righteousness is righteous, just as that one is righteous.

<span class="v-num">8</span> The one practicing sin is of the devil, because the devil sins from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning. For this the Son of God was revealed, in order that he might destroy the works of the devil.[^4]

<span class="v-num">9</span> Everyone having been born of God does not practice sin, because his seed abides in him, and he is not able to sin, because he has been born of God.

<span class="v-num">10</span> In this the children of God and the children of the devil are manifest. Everyone not practicing righteousness is not of God, and <span class="added-text">(neither is)</span> the one not loving his brother.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Because this is the message which you heard from <span class="added-text">(the)</span> beginning, that we should love one another.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> Not as Cain <span class="added-text">(was)</span>, who was of the evil one, and slew his brother. And for what reason did he slay him? Because his works were evil, but his brother's <span class="added-text">(works were)</span> righteous.

<span class="v-num">13</span> Do not marvel, my brothers, if the world hates you.

<span class="v-num">14</span> We know that we have passed out of death into life, because we love the brothers. The one not loving his brother abides in death.

<span class="v-num">15</span> Everyone hating his brother is a murderer, and you know that no murderer has eternal life abiding in him.

<span class="v-num">16</span> In this we have known love, because that one laid down his life for us, and we ought to lay down <span class="added-text">(our)</span> lives for the brothers.

<span class="v-num">17</span> But whoever has the world's goods, and sees his brother having need, and shuts up his <span class="alt-label">affections</span><span class="alt-text">(=compassion)</span> from him, how does the love of God abide in him?

<span class="v-num">18</span> My little children, let us not love in word nor in tongue, but in deed and truth.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And in this we know that we are of the truth, and shall <span class="alt-label">persuade</span><span class="alt-text">(=set at rest)</span> our hearts before him,

<span class="v-num">20</span> because if our heart condemns us, God is greater than our heart, and knows all things.[^6]

<span class="v-num">21</span> Beloved, if our heart does not condemn us, we have confidence toward God,[^7]

<span class="v-num">22</span> and whatever we ask, we receive from him, because we keep his commandments and do the things pleasing before him.[^8]

<span class="v-num">23</span> And this is his commandment, that we should believe in the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, just as he gave commandment to us.[^9]

<span class="v-num">24</span> And the one keeping his commandments abides in him, and he in him. And in this we know that he abides in us, <span class="alt-label">from</span><span class="alt-text">(=by)</span> the Spirit which he gave us.

[^1]: ἵνα κληθῶμεν은 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절로, 하나님이 사랑을 주신 목적이 곧 우리가 그의 자녀라 불리는 데 있음을 보여준다.
[^2]: ἐὰν φανερωθῇ는 아직 실현되지 않은 미래를 가리키는 3급 조건문으로, 그리스도의 재림 나타나심이 확정적이나 그 시점이 열려있음을 나타낸다.
[^3]: ἵνα ἄρῃ는 그리스도가 나타나신 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^4]: ἵνα λύσῃ는 하나님의 아들이 나타나신 목적을 나타내는 가정법 목적절이다.
[^5]: ἵνα ἀγαπῶμεν은 소식(ἀγγελία)의 내용을 나타내는 가정법 목적절로 쓰였다.
[^6]: ἐὰν καταγινώσκῃ는 아직 실현 여부가 열려있는 3급 조건문으로, 마음의 정죄가 실제로 있을 수 있는 가능성을 전제한다.
[^7]: ἐάν+가정법(καταγινώσκῃ)은 아직 결정되지 않은 미래 가능성을 나타내는 3류 조건절로, '만일 우리 마음이 우리를 책망하지 않는다면'이라는 조건이 충족될 때 하나님 앞에서 담대함을 얻는다는 결과가 뒤따름을 보여준다.
[^8]: ἐὰν+가정법(αἰτῶμεν)은 '무엇이든지 구하면'이라는 뜻의 미래 가능성을 나타내는 조건절로, 아직 정해지지 않은 다양한 기도 제목을 포괄적으로 지시한다. 관계대명사 ὃ와 결합해 '무엇을 구하든지'라는 불특정 조건의 의미를 강조한다.
[^9]: ἵνα πιστεύσωμεν...καὶ ἀγαπῶμεν은 계명(ἐντολή)의 내용을 나타내는 가정법 목적절로, 믿음과 사랑이 함께 하나님의 계명의 실질임을 보여준다.
