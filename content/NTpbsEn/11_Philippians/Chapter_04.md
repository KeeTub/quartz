<span class="v-num">1</span> Therefore, my beloved and longed-for brothers, my joy and crown, thus stand firm in the Lord, beloved.

<span class="v-num">2</span> I exhort Euodia, and I exhort Syntyche, to think the same thing in the Lord.

<span class="v-num">3</span> And I ask you also, true <span class="alt-label">yokefellow</span><span class="alt-text">(=companion)</span>, help these women, who labored together with me in the gospel, with Clement also, and the rest of my fellow workers, whose names <span class="added-text">(are)</span> in the book of life.

<span class="v-num">4</span> Rejoice in the Lord always; again I will say, rejoice.

<span class="v-num">5</span> Let your <span class="alt-label">gentleness</span><span class="alt-text">(=forbearance)</span> be known to all men. The Lord <span class="added-text">(is)</span> near.[^1]

<span class="v-num">6</span> Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.

<span class="v-num">7</span> And the peace of God, which surpasses all <span class="alt-label">understanding</span><span class="alt-text">(=mind)</span>, shall guard your hearts and your <span class="alt-label">thoughts</span><span class="alt-text">(=minds)</span> in Christ Jesus.[^2]

<span class="v-num">8</span> Finally, brothers, whatever things are true, whatever things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> reverent, whatever things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> righteous, whatever things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> pure, whatever things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> lovely, whatever things <span class="added-text">(are)</span> of good report, if <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> any virtue and if <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> any praise, think on these things.

<span class="v-num">9</span> The things which you both learned and received and heard and saw in me, practice these things; and the God of peace will be with you.

<span class="v-num">10</span> But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly, that now at last you have revived <span class="added-text">(your)</span> thinking for me; for <span class="alt-label">which</span><span class="alt-text">(=on which)</span> you were also thinking, but you lacked opportunity.

<span class="v-num">11</span> Not that I speak according <span class="alt-label">to</span><span class="alt-text">(=because of)</span> want; for I have learned, in whatever <span class="added-text">(state)</span> I am, to be content.[^3]

<span class="v-num">12</span> But I know how to be abased, I know also how to abound; in everything and in all things I have learned the <span class="alt-label">secret</span><span class="alt-text">(=am initiated)</span> both to be full and to be hungry, both to abound and to suffer need.[^4]

<span class="v-num">13</span> I am able <span class="added-text">(to do)</span> all things in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Christ strengthening me.[^5]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Nevertheless you did well, sharing together <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=in)</span> my affliction.

<span class="v-num">15</span> And you yourselves also know, Philippians, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no church shared with me <span class="alt-label">concerning</span><span class="alt-text">(=in the matter of)</span> giving and receiving, except you only.

<span class="v-num">16</span> For even in Thessalonica you sent to my need both once and twice.

<span class="v-num">17</span> Not that I seek the gift, but I seek the fruit that abounds to your account.

<span class="v-num">18</span> But I have all, and I abound; I am full, having received from Epaphroditus the things from you, a sweet-smelling fragrance, an acceptable sacrifice, well-pleasing to God.

<span class="v-num">19</span> And my God shall fill all your need according to His riches in glory, in Christ Jesus.[^6]

<span class="v-num">20</span> Now to <span class="added-text">(our)</span> God and Father <span class="added-text">(be)</span> the glory forever and <span class="alt-label">ever</span><span class="alt-text">(=to the ages of the ages)</span>. Amen.

<span class="v-num">21</span> Greet every saint in Christ Jesus. The brothers with me greet you.

<span class="v-num">22</span> All the saints greet you, but especially those of Caesar's household.

<span class="v-num">23</span> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(be)</span> with you all. Amen. <span class="added-text">(This was)</span> written to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Philippians from Rome <span class="alt-label">by</span><span class="alt-text">(=through)</span> Epaphroditus.

[^1]: ἐγγύς는 공간적 근접뿐 아니라 종말론적 임박(주의 재림)을 함께 함축하는 표현이다.
[^2]: ἡ εἰρήνη τοῦ Θεοῦ에 관사가 있어 막연한 평화가 아니라 특정한, 하나님께서 주시는 바로 그 평화를 가리킨다.
[^3]: αὐτάρκης는 스토아 철학의 자족 개념을 바울이 차용하되, 그 근거를 자기 자신이 아니라 그리스도(13절)께 둔다.
[^4]: μεμύημαι는 본래 밀의종교의 입문 용어이나, 바울은 이를 삶의 모든 형편 속에서 얻은 영적 비결을 가리키는 말로 사용한다.
[^5]: ἐνδυναμοῦντί는 현재분사로서 그리스도께서 지속적으로, 매 순간 힘을 주고 계심을 나타낸다.
[^6]: πληρώσει는 미래시제로서 하나님의 채우심이 장차 반드시 이루어질 확실한 약속임을 나타낸다.
