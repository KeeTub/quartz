<span class="v-num">1</span> If therefore <span class="added-text">(there is)</span> any comfort in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Spirit, if any affection and compassion,

<span class="v-num">4</span> Let each <span class="added-text">(of you)</span> not look <span class="added-text">(only)</span> to <span class="added-text">(your)</span> own <span class="added-text">(things)</span>, but each also to the <span class="added-text">(things)</span> of others.

<span class="v-num">5</span> For let this be <span class="alt-label">thought</span><span class="alt-text">(=minded)</span> in you which <span class="added-text">(was)</span> also in Christ Jesus,

<span class="v-num">6</span> who, <span class="alt-label">being</span><span class="alt-text">(=existing)</span> in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> form of God, did not think <span class="added-text">(it)</span> <span class="alt-label">robbery</span><span class="alt-text">(=a thing to be grasped)</span> to be equal with God,[^2]

<span class="v-num">7</span> but emptied himself, taking <span class="added-text">(the)</span> form of a servant, having become in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> likeness of men;[^3]

<span class="v-num">8</span> and being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, having become obedient to <span class="added-text">(the point of)</span> death, even <span class="added-text">(the)</span> death of <span class="added-text">(the)</span> cross.

<span class="v-num">9</span> Therefore God also highly exalted him, and graciously gave him <span class="added-text">(the)</span> name <span class="added-text">(that is)</span> above every name;

<span class="v-num">10</span> that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of <span class="added-text">(those in)</span> heaven and <span class="added-text">(those on)</span> earth and <span class="added-text">(those)</span> under the earth;[^4]

<span class="v-num">11</span> and every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ <span class="added-text">(is)</span> Lord, to <span class="added-text">(the)</span> glory of God <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Father.[^5]

<span class="v-num">12</span> So then, my beloved, as you have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling;

<span class="v-num">13</span> for it is God who works in you both to will and to work for <span class="added-text">(his)</span> good pleasure.[^6]

<span class="v-num">14</span> Do all things without murmurings and disputings,

<span class="v-num">15</span> that you may become blameless and pure, blameless children of God in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> midst of a crooked and perverted generation, among whom you <span class="alt-label">shine</span><span class="alt-text">(=appear)</span> as lights in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> world,[^7]

<span class="v-num">17</span> But even if I am being poured out upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I rejoice, and rejoice <span class="alt-label">with</span><span class="alt-text">(=congratulate)</span> you all;

<span class="v-num">19</span> But I hope in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord Jesus to send Timothy to you shortly, that I also may be <span class="alt-label">encouraged</span><span class="alt-text">(=of good soul)</span>, knowing the <span class="added-text">(things)</span> concerning you;[^8]

<span class="v-num">20</span> For I have no one likeminded, who will genuinely care for the <span class="added-text">(things)</span> concerning you;

<span class="v-num">21</span> For all seek their own <span class="added-text">(things)</span>, not the <span class="added-text">(things)</span> of Christ Jesus.

<span class="v-num">22</span> But you know his proven <span class="alt-label">character</span><span class="alt-text">(=the proof of him)</span>, that as a child <span class="added-text">(serves)</span> a father, he served with me for the gospel.

<span class="v-num">23</span> This <span class="added-text">(one)</span> then I hope to send, as soon as I <span class="alt-label">see</span><span class="alt-text">(=see clearly)</span> how it will go with me;

<span class="v-num">24</span> But I am confident in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord, that I myself also will come shortly.

<span class="v-num">25</span> But I considered it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother and fellow worker and fellow soldier, but your messenger and minister of my need;

<span class="v-num">26</span> since he was longing for you all, and was distressed, because you heard that he was sick.

<span class="v-num">27</span> For indeed he was sick, near to death; but God had mercy on him, and not on him only, but also on me, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.[^9]

<span class="v-num">28</span> I sent him therefore the more diligently, that, seeing him again, you may rejoice, and I may be less sorrowful.[^10]

<span class="v-num">29</span> Receive him therefore in <span class="added-text">(the)</span> Lord with all joy, and hold such ones in honor;

<span class="v-num">30</span> because for the work of Christ he came near to death, having gambled his <span class="alt-label">life</span><span class="alt-text">(=risking his life)</span>, that he might fill <span class="alt-label">up</span><span class="alt-text">(=fully supply)</span> your lack of service toward me.[^11]
